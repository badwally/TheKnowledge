## FAQs about Klaviyo

Overview

### What problems does Klaviyo solve?

Klaviyo solves fragmented e-commerce marketing by combining customer data, segmentation, automated email and SMS flows, and campaign analytics in one platform. It helps marketing teams, e-commerce managers, and agencies reduce manual campaign work, recover abandoned carts, personalize outreach by behavior, and improve conversion tracking across Shopify and other connected tools.

Answer based on 292 reviews

Overview

### Which roles and teams benefit most from Klaviyo?

Klaviyo is most used by marketing teams, ecommerce managers, and business owners who need to automate email and SMS campaigns, segment audiences, and track revenue performance. Executives such as CEOs, founders, and COOs use it to monitor growth, while operations and developers support integrations, customer data flows, and campaign execution.

Answer based on 505 reviews

Overview

### What company size and industries is Klaviyo built for?

Klaviyo is built for small B2C businesses, with 92% of reviewers from small companies, and it is used most in Retail at 20%. It also serves midsize and enterprise teams, while reviewer adoption is concentrated in Marketing and Advertising at 16% and Apparel & Fashion at 9%.

Answer based on 528 reviews

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Klaviyo?

Klaviyo includes core email marketing features like email automation, flow setup, email templates, A/B testing, and campaign analytics. Differentiating features include detailed segmentation, ecommerce integrations such as Shopify, behavior-based triggers for abandoned cart and post-purchase flows, plus SMS marketing for cross-channel messaging.

Answer based on 232 reviews

Pricing

### How much does it cost and what fees or limitations are included?

Klaviyo offers a free plan for up to 250 active profiles, while paid Email plans start at $20/month and Email + SMS starts at 35/month for 251–500 profiles. Pricing scales by active profiles, which reviewers say can become expensive or confusing as lists grow, though many find the free tier and month-to-month value reasonable.

Answer based on 161 reviews

Integrations

### Which third-party tools and platforms does Klaviyo integrate with?

Klaviyo integrates with over 90 third-party tools, led by ecommerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, WordPress, and Adobe Commerce. It also connects with marketing and support tools including Yotpo, Google Analytics 360, and Gorgias, covering store data, customer service, reviews, and performance tracking.

Answer based on 205 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### How easy is it to onboard and train a team on Klaviyo?

Klaviyo is fairly quick to onboard for marketers handling basic campaigns, especially with Shopify, thanks to live online sessions, webinars, documentation, and videos. Nontechnical users often learn newsletters and templates quickly, while admins and teams setting up flows, segments, analytics, or integrations usually need more time and guided training.

Answer based on 63 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Klaviyo offer, and how do users rate the experience?

Klaviyo offers email/help desk, chat, a knowledge base, and FAQ/forum resources. Users often describe support as helpful, knowledgeable, and quick through chat or email, with strong self-service content. Still, some report slower queues, inconsistent guidance, and frustration when complex issues take longer to resolve.

Answer based on 193 reviews

Email automation

### How well does Klaviyo handle email automation?

Klaviyo handles email automation well for ecommerce marketing, especially for triggered flows, segmentation, and behavior-based campaigns. Marketing teams like welcome series, abandoned cart emails, and purchase follow-ups, while some small businesses mention a learning curve, rising costs, limited non-retail fit, and occasional deliverability concerns.

Answer based on 232 reviews
