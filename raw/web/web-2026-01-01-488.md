A reserve study for an HOA (homeowners association) is specifically tailored to evaluate the common property and shared amenities within a community. It provides a financial plan for future repairs and replacements, helping HOAs to maintain their properties and avoid unexpected costs.

Industry standards recommend that a reserve study be conducted every 3-5 years. Regular updates ensure that the financial plan stays current with any changes in the community, such as new assets, wear and tear, or inflation.

A comprehensive reserve study requires details about the community's common areas, including current conditions, repair histories, and estimated life expectancies. It also considers financial details like current reserve fund balances and projected future costs.
