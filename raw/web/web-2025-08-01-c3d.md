Pieter Levels is one of the most successful indie hackers today. This interview with @John Collison is a deep dive into how he's generating over $3M in annual revenue all by himself.
In the new age of AI, this will be the future of entrepreneurship. Here are the key insights:
1. Solve your own problems. He built Nomad List because he needed a tool to find places to live as a digital nomad. This "scratch your own itch" philosophy directly led to Nomad List hitting $700,000 ARR.His job board, Remote OK, then scaled to $3.4 million in annual revenue, becoming the primary driver of his multi-million dollar portfolio.
2. Launch projects constantly. To date, Pieter's launched over 70 projects, focusing on rapid iteration and market discovery. Many might fail, but you only need a few to hit some sort of product market fit to create a profitable, self-sustaining business.
3. Automate as much as possible without hiring. If indiehackers are bogged down by admin, operations or managing people, they have less time to be launching as much and getting as many shots on goal.
E.g. for NomadList, users can find places to remote work and join communities/chat groups. But the content moderation was very hard to do by one person. GPTs made it easy to successfully follow rules of moderation in a neutral manner for rooms of 40k people.
4. Create software enabled by new technology. A pivotal moment for Pieter was the emergence of accessible payment infrastructure. He vividly recalls Stripe launching in Holland in 2014, realizing, "Oh shit, now I can start making money!" This directly unlocked monetization for his projects and allowed for rapid revenue growth.
AI is now creating similar opportunities for him, where PhotoAI and InteriorAI earn him 1.7M and 420k every year.
5. Consistency is king for building an audience and a business. @levelsio
has tweeted approximately 125,000 times over 10 years, averaging 40 tweets per day.
This sheer volume of building in public, sharing insights, and actively engaging with his audience cultivated a loyal community that converts into customers.
6. AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization might be the future. He saw referrals jump from 4 to 20% in the last month month across many of his products.
While venture-backed founders often face intense "go big or bust" pressure, he has powerfully demonstrated the indie hacker path: building sustainable, profitable businesses entirely on his own terms.
With one person (or very small teams), anyone can build a successful company by consistently building their brand and shipping.
