# WA ConnectLive

With our robust WA ConnectLive1 API, you can manage association finances efficiently and securely using one intuitive digital tool. The technology works seamlessly and integrates with industry software programs including AppFolio, Buildium, C3, Caliber by Frontsteps, CINC, Enumerate, eUnify, Smartwebs, SPI, Vantaca and VMS.

Dennis Abbott, CMCA, AMS, PCAM President, Carolinas FirstService ResidentialWe have over 200 accounts at Western Alliance Bank. Thanks to the efficiencies provided by the bank, we can complete all our reconciliations accurately by the second business day of the month. This allows us to get our financials to our clients much, much sooner, which they love.


1. The ability to integrate with WA ConnectLive may depend on the accounting software used. Services and integration tools may differ by software.

2. Funds deposited through the Lockbox will follow Western Alliance Bank’s funds availability policy as outlined in the Deposit Account Agreement Disclosure.

3. Must have a checking account and be enrolled in Positive Pay. An updated Treasury Management Services agreement is required. Charges apply for the Integrated Payables service.
