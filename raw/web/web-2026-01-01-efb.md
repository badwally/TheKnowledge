## What is cyan™?

**All-in-one association management**

cyan™ is a cloud-based digital platform designed exclusively for FirstService Residential and is used by community managers and heart-of-house staff to provide community association management services to our clients through a suite of integrated tools. Integration with the HODA™ platform makes cyan a one-of-a-kind technology available only to FirstService Residential associates and customers.
*cyan enhances experiences and relationships within our communities to simplify the lives of our residents, board members, and associates – ultimately driving trust and client success.*
