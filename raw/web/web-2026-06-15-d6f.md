## What is Postscript?

Postscript is an SMS marketing and sales platform designed specifically for Shopify brands. The software provides SMS capabilities to help brands grow their subscriber lists, engage customers through personalized messaging, drive repeat purchases and loyalty, and boost revenue. Key features include tools for list growth, campaign creation and management, compliance monitoring, deliverability optimization, and deep Shopify integration. Postscript aims to make SMS the number one revenue channel for ecommerce brands by offering a full suite of conversational commerce solutions tailored to each stage of the customer journey.

## FAQs about Postscript

Overview

### What company size and specific industries is Postscript built for?

Postscript is designed for Shopify merchants, ecommerce brands, and SMS marketers, making it a fit for businesses selling online and teams focused on text message marketing. The description does not specify a company size, so its target audience is best understood by industry and use case rather than small, mid-market, or enterprise segments.

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Postscript?

Postscript offers 2-way messaging, SMS messaging, mass texting, and scheduled messaging for customer communication. It includes marketing automation, automated responses, campaign planning, and campaign scheduling for multi-campaign execution. Additional features include contact management, customer segmentation, compliance management, reporting/analytics, and tracking for activity, engagement, interactions, and open rates.

Pricing

### How much does it cost and what fees or limitations are included?

Postscript starts at $100 per month for the Growth plan, which includes live chat and email support plus unlimited opt-in keywords. The Professional plan costs $500 per month and adds API access, enhanced analytics, and priority live chat/email support. A free version and free trial are also available.

Integrations

### Which third-party tools and platforms does Postscript integrate with?

Postscript integrates with over 50 third-party tools and platforms, including Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Cordial, Listrak, Gorgias, Help Scout, Kustomer, Front, and Gladly. Other catalog integrations include AfterShip, Builder.io, Black Crow AI, Elevar, Justuno, Growave, Littledata, Loop, LoyaltyLion, and Malomo.

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Postscript offer?

Postscript provides email/help desk and chat support. No reviewer feedback about support experience is available in the catalog data, so user sentiment on response time, helpfulness, or frustration cannot be stated here.
