Whether you manage a 50-unit community or multiple high-rise properties, managing a Stratas comes with a list of responsibilities. From managing payments, tracking keys, or handling package deliveries, staying on top of the many moving parts is no small feat. That’s where having the right tools, like our condo management software and our partner integrations, comes in.

Integrating the right tools with our software can make your day easier by connecting additional technologies that help property managers automate tasks, enhance security, and create a seamless experience for both staff and residents. Here are six powerful integrations that can elevate your property management and simplify your workload.

### 1. Accounting Software: Keep Your Accounting in Check

Finances are essential to condo management, and having your accounting tools synced with BuildingLink ensures smooth financial operations. BuildingLink integrates with sixteen partners to track account balances, transaction history, move-ins/move-outs, and more. No more manually entering data in multiple systems or spreadsheets; just seamless financial management at your fingertips.

### 2. KeyLink: Simplify Key Management

Ever had a resident or vendor request a key, only to spend precious time tracking it down? With KeyLink, BuildingLink’s electronic key management system, you can securely store, track, and manage access to unit and common area keys. A digital log keeps everything documented so you know exactly who took what key and when; no more misplaced keys or confusion at the front desk.

### 3. Package Management: Say Goodbye to Lobby Clutter

With online shopping at an all-time high, package deliveries can quickly pile up. Integrating package management tools like BuildingLink’s package tracking system helps staff log and notify residents instantly when their package arrives. Instead of sorting through a mountain of boxes, residents can simply pick up their deliveries hassle-free, and your lobby stays clean and organized.

BuildingLink also integrates with leading package locker solutions like Parcel Pending, Package Concierge, Luxer One, and Snaile. These integrations allow for secure, automated delivery storage, ensuring residents can retrieve packages at their convenience while reducing staff workload.

### 4. IoT Sensor Technology: Smarter Maintenance Management

Proactive maintenance is key to preventing costly repairs and improving building efficiency. BuildingLink integrates with Aware Buildings, an advanced IoT sensor platform that helps property managers monitor environmental conditions like temperature, humidity, leaks, and air quality in real time. These smart sensors detect issues early, allowing staff to address problems before they escalate—reducing costs, improving energy efficiency, and enhancing resident comfort.

### 5. Building Access Systems: Enhance Building Security

Managing building access is essential for security and efficiency. With BuildingLink’s building access system integrations, you can seamlessly connect with top providers like ButterflyMX, Brivo, and HID to automate entry, manage key fobs, and track visitor access—all from one platform. These integrations simplify security, reduce manual data entry, and enhance the resident experience. With BuildingLink, you’ll always have a clear record of who is coming and going, adding an extra layer of protection for your building.

### 6. Smart Home Automation: Increase Resident Comfort and Efficiency

BuildingLink’s smart home automation integrations offer residents the ability to verbally control lights, blinds, doors, and even thermostats, all through the BuildingLink App. Integrating BuildingLink with Google Home or Alexa is smart and convenient and enhances the resident experience.

## Making Life Easier for Property Managers

By choosing the right integrations, you’re not just making things easier for yourself, you’re improving the experience for residents and staff alike. The result?
