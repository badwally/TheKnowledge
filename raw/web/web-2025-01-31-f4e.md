# Market place

Integration with third-party applications

UpperBee can be integrated with various online software solutions to fully meet the expectations of the most demanding users.

## Business Accounting

Tired of having to reenter your invoices in various accounting systems, your properties and your business accounting softwares? With the integration of UpperBee Manager and Xero®, an enterprise online accounting software, you get the best of both worlds! That means you can automate everyday business tasks, get up-to-date financials and run your business - from wherever you are.

**xero.com**

## Business Accounting

Integrate QuickBooks® with UpperBee Manager to seamlessly unify your business and client property accounting systems, enabling a powerful and efficient accounting experience.

**quickbooks.intuit.com**

## Payment Solution

Streamline rent and condominium fee collections while simplifying payments to vendors and stakeholders with UpperBee's payment solution. Maximize productivity with the only online payment platform fully integrated into the accounting system of all your properties managed with UpperBee.

**upperbee.com**

Online Virtual Meetings

Need to hold an owners' meeting but can't do it in person? No problem—Meeting by UpperBee has you covered. Available as a stand-alone solution or integrated with the UpperBee all-in-one online condo management platform, Meeting enables video conferences for groups of over 1,000 participants. With Meeting, you can efficiently manage proxies, quorum, and votes.

**upperbee.com/meeting**

## Inspections and Follow-ups

TrackandFix is a powerful web-based tool for PC and mobile platforms, designed to save time and money by streamlining issue identification, organization, assignment, and resolution across multiple projects in real time. Enhance inspection and after-sales activities with improved communication, faster corrective work, and increased customer satisfaction.

Integrated with BIM360 Field® and Procore®, TrackandFix allows delivery and after-sales teams to assign corrective tasks in real time during inspections, eliminating the need for manual report entry.

**trackandfix.com**

## Pre-rental Screening

Users can seamlessly access Trustii's pre-rental screening tool through UpperBee. Trustii’s innovative technology, including bank authentication, enables property managers to validate applicants' identities and assess their financial reliability in minutes. The fully automated process delivers comprehensive results quickly and efficiently.

Trustii reports integrate directly with UpperBee, enabling automatic e-lease generation using pre-entered information.

**trustii.com**

## Smart Lockers

Parcel locker solutions for condominiums and apartments that seamlessly integrate with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental property management systems, providing a comprehensive solution for the entire building. From day one, accept deliveries from any carrier for all owners and tenants.

**snailelockers.com**


## Smart Lockers

Integrate Expedibox® with UpperBee Condo or UpperBee Rental to streamline real-time package delivery. Expedibox® and UpperBee offer a turnkey solution for efficient package management.

**expedibox.com**


## Smart Lockers

Integrate BlueBox with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental to simplify package management in your buildings. BlueBox and UpperBee, a secure and efficient solution for receiving and managing deliveries.

**bluebits.ca**
