## FAQs about Attentive

Overview

### What problems does Attentive solve?

Attentive solves low customer engagement, weak list growth, and manual SMS execution by helping marketers launch compliant campaigns, automated journeys, and sign-up forms quickly. Ecommerce teams, retention marketers, and agencies use it to improve deliverability, segment audiences, recover carts, track performance, and coordinate SMS with email and customer support tools.

Answer based on 82 reviews

Overview

### Which roles and teams benefit most from Attentive?

Attentive is most used by marketing teams, ecommerce leaders, and business owners who need to grow revenue through SMS and digital campaigns. Marketing managers and specialists use it for audience segmentation, automation, and promotions, while executives and operations leaders rely on it to improve customer engagement, conversions, and retention.

Answer based on 108 reviews

Overview

### What company size and industries is Attentive built for?

Attentive is built mainly for small businesses in retail and e-commerce, with 82% of reviewers from small companies and Apparel & Fashion leading at 22%. It also serves Retail at 21% and Consumer Goods at 20%, aligning with its stated focus on retail and e-commerce, food and beverage, and media and entertainment.

Answer based on 108 reviews

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Attentive?

Attentive includes core SMS marketing features like mass texting, scheduled messaging, and automated responses, plus sign-up forms for SMS and email capture. Reviewers also focus on segmentation, A/B testing, campaign analytics dashboards, and third-party integrations such as email service providers and customer service platforms for broader customer messaging workflows.

Answer based on 54 reviews

Integrations

### Which third-party tools and platforms does Attentive integrate with?

Attentive integrates with Klaviyo, Gorgias, and WooCommerce, with reviewer mentions also referencing Shopify and Magento workflows. Its catalog shows more than 50 integrations overall, including Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, AfterShip, ActiveCampaign, and AWeber, covering ecommerce, customer support, and email marketing use cases.

Answer based on 18 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### What training and onboarding options does Attentive offer?

Attentive provides live online sessions, webinars, documentation, and videos for team training. Live online sessions and webinars support guided instruction, while documentation and videos give teams self-paced reference material for setup and feature review.

Answer based on 4 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Attentive offer, and how do users rate the experience?

Attentive offers email/help desk, FAQ/forum, a knowledge base, and phone support. Users generally describe support as fast, accessible, and hands-on, with helpful account managers and proactive guidance during onboarding and ongoing use. Some report technical issues or gaps in customer service tools, but support responses are usually timely.

Answer based on 53 reviews

Features and Usability

### How good is Attentive for campaign reporting?

Attentive gives marketing teams a clear view of campaign performance, but reporting depth still feels limited for many users. The dashboard helps with high-level analytics, auditing, and board-ready updates, while some brands and agencies want more granular filters, combined cost-and-revenue reporting, in-platform exports, and less manual spreadsheet work.

Answer based on 30 reviews

Features and Usability

### Is Attentive easy to use for SMS marketing?

Attentive makes SMS marketing easy to learn and run, especially for beginners, small teams, and agency users. The interface feels intuitive for building campaigns, journeys, segments, and pop-ups, and setup is often straightforward. Some users still find certain areas clunky, and non-Shopify integrations can require extra steps or outside help.

Answer based on 20 reviews
