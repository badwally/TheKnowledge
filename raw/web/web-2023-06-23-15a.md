The NRC's Construction Research Centre develops sustainable, durable and climate-resilient solutions for Canada's buildings and infrastructure through scientific expertise and industry collaboration.

Our work focuses on durability and service life prediction for both traditional and emerging building envelope materials. We also support the construction sector with performance benchmarking and the development of diagnostic tools that aim to reduce environmental impacts and extend building lifespan.

## Our capabilities

Our multidisciplinary team works with industry, government and academic partners to evaluate and de-risk innovative and standardized construction products. We help develop cost-effective building materials that meet performance expectations and service life targets.

Our services support a range of applied research projects and industry collaborations across 4 key areas:

- Bio-based and recycled building envelope materials
- Performance assessment for service life
- Advanced diagnostic methods and condition monitoring
- Practitioner's tools for resilient design, including datasets, methodologies, guidelines and technical reports

## Areas of expertise

We provide:

- Durability assessment, long-term performance evaluation and service life prediction
- Material characterization to identify degradation mechanisms
- Short-term performance testing, physico-chemical characterization and analysis of materials to establish structure–property relationships
- Support for developing sustainable materials, including bio-based and recycled alternatives
- Development of in-situ, non-destructive tools for building condition assessment and preventive maintenance
- Product performance validation under real-world and extreme conditions
- Testing of high-performance glazing systems
- Thermal performance simulations and experimental measurements of high-performance building envelope and glazing systems
- Moisture ingress evaluation and hygrothermal performance testing
- Research on mold growth in building envelope materials
- Research to inform construction codes, standards and guidelines for climate loads, climate resilient retrofit strategies, low-carbon design and life-cycle carbon management

## Why work with us

When you work with our team, you gain access to extensive expertise and support from a network of industry and academic partners. You will also benefit from access to our specialized facilities that support the development of solutions to mitigate premature failure, reduce long-term maintenance costs, and enhance resiliency and life-cycle carbon performance for buildings.

## Equipment

### Spectroscopy


- Fourier transform infrared spectrometer (FT-IR)
- Raman spectrometer
- Single-sided nuclear magnetic resonance (SS-NMR) spectrometer

### Thermal analysis


- Differential scanning calorimeter (DSC)
- Modulated differential scanning calorimeter (MDSC)
- High-resolution thermogravimetric analyzer (TGA)
- Thermal mechanical analyzer (TMA)
- Heat flow meter

### Surface analysis


- Contact angle goniometer
- Force tensiometer
- Hydrostatic head tester

### Mechano-rheological analysis


- Dynamic mechanical analyzer (DMA)
- Dynamic shear rheometer (DSR)

### Microscopy


- Optical microscope
- Infrared and Raman microscopes

### Weathering, conditioning and accelerated aging facilities


- Convection ovens for thermal aging
- Environmental chambers for hygrothermal aging and freeze-thaw cycling
- Xenon arc weatherometers and fluorescent tester for thermal, hydrolytic and ultraviolet aging
