## Funding plans for every state and every workflow.

Pick preset plans for guardrailed speed, build custom targets when you need control, or allocate by component method - then compare and publish.

### Preset funding plans

- Full Funding - Stay at/above fully funded each year.
- Baseline - Keep the balance positive with minimums.
- Threshold - Never drop below a floor you set.

### Custom funding plans

- Target - Hit a specific balance or % by a year.
- Threshold (custom) - Define the yearly floor.
- Custom - Control yearly contributions and rates.

### Component method

- Sequential - Fund one component at a time by RUL.
- Cyclic Percentage - Distribute in repeating %
- Pro Rata - Allocate proportional to total need.

## Frequently asked questions

### What exactly is the Reserve Builder workflow?

The iOS/Android app captures geotagged photos and on-site notes and links them to the right component (or your photo gallery for later allocation). Everything lands in the study—no double entry.

### How does Field Mode save time in the field?

A simple flow: project details & visit scheduling → choose RS level/SIRS → add or tweak components → auto-generate funding plans → publish/download reports.

### Is Portfolio Intelligence the same as the dashboard?

No. The dashboard shows project/visit status; **Portfolio Intelligence (Enterprise)** rolls up **upcoming component projects** across communities, grouped by location, date, or category to spot bundling opportunities—built for engineering teams that execute the work.

### Do you provide a master library of components and costs?

We don’t ship defaults. Create your own **Component Templates** (UL, RUL, cost, even per-state pricing rules) or **import them via CSV**; they auto-populate during report assembly.

### How flexible are Funding Plans?

Choose **preset plans** (Full, Baseline, Threshold), **custom plans** (Target, your own Threshold, fully Custom), or use **Component Method** strategies (Sequential, Cyclic %, Pro Rata, optional Underfunding Shift). Some modes are fully editable; others are auto-produced with guardrails for accuracy.

### What does One-click Update change?

Update past studies in one action: **Basic** (project year), **Lifespan** (RUL), **Price** (costs), or **Full** (lifespans + prices). New plans are calculated automatically and remain editable.

### Can we replicate our existing PDF report format?

Yes—**White-label Reports (Enterprise).** We remove PropFusion branding and can replicate your current PDF layout; our team completes the migration in **3–4 weeks** post-signup (one-off fee).

### How do Procurement Automation and Bulk RFPs work?

**Enterprise** identifies bundle opportunities, groups similar upcoming projects, and assembles structured **RFP files** (category/time/location).

### Do you provide leads? How are they routed?

Yes. The **Lead Generator** forwards **~300–500 HOA inquiries/year** from across the U.S., routed by the states you choose—helping firms scale without extra marketing spend.
