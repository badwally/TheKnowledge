# Depreciation Reports & Reserve Fund Studies

RJC Engineers can help you plan the maintenance and replacement of your building’s components to reduce or eliminate future surprises.

Building materials have life expectancies that can be as short as two years or can exceed 50 years. RJC's Depreciation Reports and Reserve Fund Studies assist with strategic planning approaches towards building maintenance and renewals.

By implementing a strategic approach, the future costs associated with these necessary activities can be proactively budgeted for without undue financial impact to building owners.
