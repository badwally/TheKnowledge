# The Living Reserve Study® Expands Internationally with Humberts Dubai and SmartProperty® Partnership

San Diego, CA – SmartProperty® is bringing The Living Reserve Study® to the UAE through its collaboration with Humberts Dubai. As the first international firm to adopt SmartProperty’s advanced asset and financial planning platform, Humberts Dubai is setting a new benchmark for accuracy, transparency, and strategic financial planning in the Jointly Owned Property (JOP) sector.

“With over 180 years of history in the real estate industry, Humberts is proud to be a trusted leader in the UK and now in Dubai. Operating 10 offices across the UK and a flagship office in Dubai, UAE, we offer a comprehensive range of services, including Brokerage, Property Management, Valuations, Reserve Studies, and Advisory. Our collaboration with SmartProperty is a natural extension of our commitment to bringing the best in technology and expertise to the UAE market, ensuring that our clients benefit from the highest standards in asset and financial planning”, said Waqar Hasan CPM PCAM, Director of Humberts UAE.

This collaboration reflects SmartProperty’s mission to modernize reserve fund studies with Humberts Dubai’s vision to leverage advanced technology and specialized expertise to enhance property management and investment value. As the demand for more advanced financial and asset management grows, both Humberts Dubai and SmartProperty continue to lead the way with innovative solutions and expert leadership.

**About SmartProperty**

SmartProperty specializes in financial and asset management for community associations, providing innovative solutions that help streamline operations, enhance transparency, and optimize financial performance. By leveraging advanced technology, SmartProperty empowers homeowners’ associations (HOAs), condo associations, club and resorts, as well as property management firms with the tools they need to keep their fees as reasonably low as possible while keeping property values high. With a focus on automation, compliance, and real-time financial insights, SmartProperty is redefining the way community associations manage their assets and financial health.
