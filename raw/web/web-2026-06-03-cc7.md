Introducing Atlas

SmartProperty Atlas brings transparency, precision, and confidence to every capital planning decision your community makes.

The Problem With Reserve Studies Today

Two-thirds of boards don’t trust their reserve study—and they’re right not to. Traditional studies rely on averages-of-averages, not your community’s reality. Built on generic inputs, the results are expensive guesses, and your community pays for them in surprise special assessments.

Introducing SmartProperty Atlas™

Rather than replacing human judgment, Atlas AI agents strengthens it. Your reserve analyst still leads the study. Atlas gives them better information to work with and makes their conclusions more defensible.

Atlas includes a proprietary data model: PRISM (Property Replacement Intelligence & Specification Model). Built from SmartProperty's cost database — billions in replacement values across thousands of communities — PRISM is what makes Atlas intelligent.

Atlas factors in local climate, construction quality, usage frequency, and property type — not the generic benchmarks most studies rely on.

When a board member asks "why does the pool deck cost this much?" Atlas provides the explanation. Every cost projection traces back to the real data behind it.

Atlas is a recursive learner. Every reserve study it completes feeds back into PRISM — refining cost models, sharpening predictions, and improving accuracy. The more it processes, the better it gets.

You're a volunteer. You don't have time to be a finance professional. But you're responsible for millions of dollars in assets. Atlas gives you the confidence to say "yes" or "no" to capital projects — with data you can actually explain to your community.

High-expectation boards want answers, not spreadsheets. Atlas arms your team with benchmarked, defensible reserve data so you walk into every board meeting as a trusted strategic advisor — not someone defending a number you didn't create.

Fannie Mae's new reserve funding requirements take effect January 2027. Associations that can't demonstrate adequate reserve planning risk losing access to conventional financing — affecting every unit owner's ability to buy or refinance.

SmartProperty Atlas is the only platform purpose-built to document and defend your reserve position with the kind of data-backed evidence Fannie Mae compliance demands.
