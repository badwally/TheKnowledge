# From Zero to $10K

The Solo Founder's AI Playbook, Build a Real SaaS Company in 60 Days with $1,000

A step-by-step playbook for solo founders who want to go from idea to $10K in revenue in 60 days using AI coding assistants, growth hacking, and a $1,000 budget. Covers validation, infrastructure, AI-powered development, launch strategy, aggressive outreach, and scaling, with practical prompts, templates, and real-world examples tailored for young and first-time entrepreneurs.

Start Reading## Table of Contents

### The $1,000 Thesis

Why starting a software company in 2026 costs less than a used laptop, and how AI, free infrastructure, and open distribution channels changed the game forever.

### Finding a Problem Worth Solving (Days 1–2)

AI-powered problem validation in 48 hours: mining complaints, mapping competitors, defining your buyer, and testing willingness to pay before writing a single line of code.

### Day 1 Infrastructure: Domain, Email, and Digital Identity (Day 3)

Register your domain, set up business email, configure DNS and email authentication, the complete digital foundation in one afternoon for under $20.

### Building Your Product Website with AI (Days 3–5)

Build a professional, SEO-optimized product website before your product exists using AI coding assistants, the spec-first approach, and modern frameworks.

### Deploying and Automating with Git + Cloudflare (Day 5)

Ship your website in 30 minutes with GitHub, Cloudflare Pages, and automatic deployments, zero DevOps, zero server management.

### Organic Growth Before Product: The Unfair Advantage (Days 5–7)

Build your digital footprint before writing product code: social profiles, 25+ marketplace listings, press releases, and community seeding that compound while you build.

### Building the MVP with AI (Week 2)

One focused week turning your idea into a working product using AI coding assistants, from spec document to authentication, payments, and core features.

### Launch Week: Getting Your First Users (Week 3)

The coordinated launch playbook: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, social media blitz, and converting your first visitors into your first paying customers.

### The Aggressive Outreach Sprint (Weeks 3–4)

Two weeks of focused outreach: cold email, LinkedIn DMs, community engagement, partnership pitches, and converting trial users into revenue.

### Scaling to $10K: Growth Hacking for Solo Founders (Weeks 5–8)

Systematize what works: content engines, referral loops, strategic partnerships, upselling, and the metrics that matter on the road to $10K MRR.

### The Solo Founder's Tech Stack (Reference)

The complete, opinionated tech stack reference: every tool, service, and platform a solo founder needs, categorized by function, with free-tier details and alternatives.

### Mindset: Why Most People Never Launch

The psychology of shipping: overcoming imposter syndrome, perfectionism, and fear of failure, plus building while young, sustainable hustle, and knowing when to quit.

### Appendices: Calendar, Prompts, Budget, and Legal

The 60-day calendar, AI prompt templates for every stage, the $1,000 budget tracker, and legal basics every solo founder needs to know.
