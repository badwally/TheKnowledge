## FAQs about Listrak

Overview

### Which roles and teams benefit most from Listrak?

Listrak is most used by marketing leaders, email and digital marketing specialists, and ecommerce teams that need to manage customer messaging and revenue growth. Directors and VPs use it to guide campaign strategy and reporting, while specialists and ecommerce managers execute segmentation, automation, personalized promotions, and lifecycle marketing across channels.

Answer based on 22 reviews

Overview

### What company size and specific industries is Listrak built for?

Listrak is designed for leading retailers and brands, making it a fit for companies in the retail and branded consumer goods sectors. The target audience appears to be established organizations rather than very small businesses, with a focus on businesses that manage customer engagement and marketing at scale.

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Listrak?

Listrak offers marketing automation, email marketing, SMS marketing, and multi-channel communication across email, text, push notifications, and chat. It also includes customer segmentation, personalization, campaign scheduling, A/B testing, real-time analytics, workflow automation, and eCommerce integration for tracking engagement and conversions.

Getting Started and Support

### What training and onboarding options does Listrak offer?

Listrak provides in-person training, live online sessions, webinars, and documentation to help teams get started. In-person and live online options support guided instruction, webinars offer scheduled presentations, and documentation gives written reference material for self-paced review and feature lookup.

Answer based on 5 reviews

### What customer support options does Listrak offer?

Listrak provides Email/Help Desk, FAQ/Forum, Knowledge Base, Phone Support, 24/7 Live Rep, and Chat. Users describe support as personal and reachable, with helpful live chat and knowledgeable account guidance, though setup can be a bit difficult and some situations benefit from direct contact with a specific rep.
