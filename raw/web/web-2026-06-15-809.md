## FAQs about Yotpo

Overview

### What problems does Yotpo solve?

Yotpo helps ecommerce teams solve low review volume, weak product-page trust, and manual post-purchase follow-up by automating review requests, moderation, incentives, and display. Marketing, ecommerce, and customer service teams benefit most by turning customer photos, ratings, and feedback into social proof, repeat purchases, SEO visibility, and faster product improvement.

Answer based on 331 reviews

Overview

### Which roles and teams benefit most from Yotpo?

Yotpo is most used by ecommerce owners, founders, and executives who need to strengthen customer trust, retention, and revenue growth. Marketing teams use it to manage reviews, loyalty programs, SMS campaigns, and social proof, while brand, social, and web teams support campaign execution, storefront optimization, and customer engagement.

Answer based on 359 reviews

Overview

### What company size and industries is Yotpo built for?

Yotpo serves small eCommerce brands and retailers, with 93% of reviewers from small businesses and Retail leading at 30%. It also fits Consumer Goods at 12% and Apparel & Fashion at 10%, with limited use among midsize businesses at 5% and enterprises at 2%.

Answer based on 376 reviews

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Yotpo?

Yotpo includes core review management features like ratings/reviews, review request emails, review monitoring, moderation, and customizable widgets for product pages. Differentiating features include UGC collection with photo reviews, social sharing and automated publishing, Google Shopping and SEO support, loyalty rewards, and eCommerce integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce.

Answer based on 243 reviews

Pricing

### How much does it cost and what fees or limitations are included?

Yotpo starts at $199/month for the Pro plan, with Premium at 1198/month; both charge SMS and email usage separately. Pro includes loyalty, reviews, SMS and email, and up to 500 orders monthly; Premium raises that to 1,000 orders plus onboarding and support. Reviewers often call pricing expensive for small businesses, though some report no hidden fees.

Answer based on 128 reviews

Integrations

### Which third-party tools and platforms does Yotpo integrate with?

Yotpo integrates with Shopify and HubSpot Marketing Hub, with reviewer-confirmed use centered on Shopify. It also supports over 50 integrations across ecommerce, marketing, and support tools, including BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce, Commerce Cloud, Gorgias, Instagram, and AdRoll, covering storefront, customer service, and campaign workflows.

Answer based on 155 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### What training and onboarding options does Yotpo offer?

Yotpo provides in person training, live online sessions, webinars, and documentation to help teams get started. In person training supports direct instruction, live online sessions and webinars offer guided learning remotely, and documentation gives written reference material for self-paced review and setup.

Answer based on 4 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Yotpo offer, and how do users rate the experience?

Yotpo offers email/help desk, FAQ/forum, a knowledge base, phone support, 24/7 live representatives, and chat. Users often describe support as responsive, helpful, and effective during setup and troubleshooting, though some report inconsistent experiences, slower ticket resolution, and occasional difficulty getting complex issues fully resolved.

Answer based on 138 reviews

Customer reviews

### How well does Yotpo handle customer reviews?

Yotpo makes review collection and display easy for ecommerce teams, especially Shopify merchants, with automation, moderation, and flexible widgets. Many businesses value the custom review requests, photo reviews, and Google integration, while some teams want better search, filtering, timing controls, and lower pricing for advanced review features.

Answer based on 243 reviews
