Accelerate your due diligence with high-precision automated reporting. Transform thousands of pages of complex strata and condo filings into clear, actionable insights in minutes. Your initial Eli Report is free — start your professional analysis today.

### How Condo Document Review Software Helps You

#### Decoding Governing Documents and Bylaws

The foundational step in property evaluation is a rigorous review of the CC&Rs and bylaws. These documents dictate the operational standards of the community and directly affect a resident’s daily experience.

Our **automated review system** extracts and categorizes critical lifestyle restrictions, including:

**Lifestyle Alignment:**Ensuring the property’s governing rules match the buyer’s specific requirements.**Pet and Rental Policies:**Instant identification of occupancy and ownership constraints.**Operational Rules:**Clear presentation of noise, parking, and renovation protocols.

### Screening of Historical Meeting Minutes

HOA and strata board minutes provide the only true record of a community’s internal health. However, these documents are often voluminous and difficult to parse manually.

Eli Report utilizes **intelligent data extraction** to identify:

**Recurring Maintenance:**Flagging persistent building issues that may indicate systemic failure.**Community Dynamics:**Identifying resident disputes or litigation that could impact future value.**Financial Red Flags:**Highlighting discussions regarding significant upcoming expenditures or mismanagement.

### Technical Summarization of Inspection & Engineering Reports

Professional inspection reports are essential for assessing structural integrity but can be overly technical for the average stakeholder.

Our platform processes these technical filings to provide a concise summary of:

**Known Deficiencies:**Identifying existing water damage, foundation concerns, or aging mechanical systems.**Capital Projects:**Forecasting major renewals, such as roofing, or envelope work, that require significant investment.**Risk Mitigation:**Helping buyers anticipate maintenance costs before they become liabilities.

### Comparing Budgets to Other Buildings of the Same Type and Age

A building’s budget is most meaningful when viewed in context. We offer **automated budget analysis** that compares a property’s financials against similar buildings of the same age and type.

This data-driven comparison ensures buyers understand:

**Management Efficiency:**Whether the Strata is effectively allocating funds for repairs and maintenance.**Financial Stability:**Identifying discrepancies or funding gaps relative to industry standards.**Operational Health:**Confirming the building is well-maintained and financially sound.

### Evaluating Insurance Policy Terms

Inadequate insurance can lead to devastating personal liability for owners. Our platform scrutinizes policy terms and compares them against regional benchmarks.

**Coverage Gap Identification:**Determining if the property is adequately protected against property damage and liability.**Deductible Benchmarking:**Comparing insurance terms with other communities to assess relative risk.**Liability Awareness:**Ensuring buyers are fully informed of their potential financial exposure.

### Forecasting Special Assessments Based on Reserve Studies and Budgets

Special levies are often the most significant financial shock to a new owner. We provide a **predictive assessment forecast** based on reserve studies and current budget contributions.

**Financial Planning:**Projecting the timing and amount of potential assessments.**Negotiation Power:**Equipping buyers with the data needed to negotiate a fair purchase price based on future costs.**Long-term Security:**Eliminating “unpleasant surprises” through rigorous reserve fund analysis.
