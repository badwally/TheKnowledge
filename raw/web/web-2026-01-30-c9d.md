## FAQ

Concierge Plus is a smart, AI-enabled platform built for HOAs, condos, and multi-family communities that streamlines communications, amenities, packages, service requests, and more.

Designed for property managers, board members, building owners, and property management companies that want to streamline and enhance workflows while delivering a seamless resident experience.

We take your data security seriously and use industry-leading practices to protect your information:

**Private & Secure Infrastructure**: All our systems run on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in a private cloud network managed by AWS Certified Professionals. Only authorized systems can access your data, and every access is tracked and monitored.**Reliable & Backed Up**: We use enterprise-grade database technology that automatically creates six copies of your data across three separate data centers in North America. We also take daily backups and keep them for 35 days. This means your data is protected even in the unlikely event of a system failure.**Fast Recovery**: Our system is designed to recover quickly from any issues, with minimal data loss (typically less than a minute of data).**Encryption & Privacy**: Sensitive information is encrypted, and all passwords are securely encrypted and salted. We never store credit card numbers.**Complete Audit Trail**: Nearly every action in Concierge Plus is logged, so administrators can see who made changes and when, providing full accountability and transparency.

Concierge Plus runs on secure private cloud platforms to ensure your data stays safe.

**Document Storage**: Files you upload (like PDFs, Word documents, and other files) are stored on AWS servers.**Publicly Shared Files**: If you choose to share files publicly, they may be distributed through content delivery networks around the world. This ensures fast access for anyone viewing them, no matter where they’re located.**Industry Standards**: AWS maintains strict privacy and data protection standards. You can learn more about their security practices at https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/data-privacy/
