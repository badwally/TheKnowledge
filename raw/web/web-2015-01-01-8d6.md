# Turn asset intelligence into confident strategies with a capital planning software

Get full visibility into asset conditions, budgets, and project pipelines. Facilio gives you one platform to forecast CAPEX, control spend, and prove ROI on every capital decision.

Request demo## Why legacy CMMS tools fall short of capital planning goals

Legacy CMMS platforms focus only on maintenance logs, leaving asset condition and cost data trapped in silos, making it hard to plan ahead.

Without linking operational and capital spend, it’s impossible to see how maintenance decisions impact long-term investment needs.

Teams still rely on manual planning outside the CMMS, leading to version chaos, missed updates, and unreliable forecasts.

Legacy systems lack approval trails and cost histories, making compliance reviews time-consuming and error-prone.
