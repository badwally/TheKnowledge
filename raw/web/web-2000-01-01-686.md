Brookstone Engineering delivers licensed Reserve Fund Studies under the Alberta Condominium Property Act — transparent pricing, on-site inspections, and 30-year capital projections.

Brookstone Engineering provides Reserve Fund Studies that satisfy Section 38 of the Alberta Condominium Property Act and Regulation 168/2000. Each study is sealed by a licensed Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) registered with APEGA and delivers a comprehensive component inventory, condition-based remaining-life assessment, and a 30-year cash-flow projection of contributions, expenditures, and ending balances. Boards receive multiple funding scenarios — Full Funding, Threshold, and Cash-Flow — with transparent assumptions for inflation, interest, and contribution increases so directors can make defensible decisions. The deliverable includes a written report, schedule of planned actions, and an executive summary suitable for AGMs and lender or insurer review. Pricing is fixed in advance with no hourly billing.
