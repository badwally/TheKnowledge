# The Living Reserve Study®

Helping communities and clubs protect, maintain, and plan for the future of their property.

Trusted by Industry Leaders

## Reserve Component Inventory Control

## Manage your entire reserve study inventory—clearly and completely


Track every component, cost, and detail in one place. Map locations, attach docs, link financials, and generate reports—accessible anytime, even on mobile.

### Log full asset details

Store specs, suppliers, files, photos, and historical notes in one place.

### Visualize with precision

Map components to exact locations and organize by property zones.

### Tie to financials

Connect each asset to GL codes, replacement schedules, and cost forecasts.

### Stay mobile and report-ready

View on the go, surface key notes in reports, and export complete inventories anytime.

### Ditch the Spreadsheets

The Living Reserve Study® is a living document that can be updated in real-time to guarantee accuracy and success because it never goes out of date.

### Manage Your Projects

Track what needs to be done, how much it will cost, and when it needs to be completed, over a forecasted length of time.

### Fully Integrated

No need to cross reference between your reserve study, your spreadsheets and your financials. It’s all connected and at your fingertips!

## Capital Project Tracking Tools

Track every project from bid to closeout—linked to your reserve plan, budget, and board-ready reports.

### Connect the dots
