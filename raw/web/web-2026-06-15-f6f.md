## What is Simon AI?

Simon AI empowers marketing teams with the data, tools, and support to deliver 1:1 personalization across every customer touchpoint. Simon AI combines a composable customer data platform with agentic AI to help marketers start with a goal—like reducing churn or driving repeat purchases—and let intelligent agents handle the rest. By uncovering hidden signals, activating 100x more customer and contextual data, and automating execution across engagement channels, Simon AI enables even small teams to perform like much larger ones. Leading brands like ASOS, SeatGeek, and others trust Simon to turn complex data into faster launches, personalization at massive scale, and revenue-generating performance.

## FAQs about Simon AI

Overview

### What company size and specific industries is Simon AI built for?

Simon AI is designed for businesses of all sizes, from small companies to enterprise teams, that need to use customer data for marketing testing. It fits organizations across industries that run campaigns in multiple channels and want to create, conduct, and evaluate marketing tests.

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Simon AI?

Simon AI offers campaign management, multi-channel marketing, and drip campaigns for coordinated outreach. It includes customer profiles, customer segmentation, and behavioral analytics for audience targeting, plus personalization and dynamic content. Testing and measurement features include A/B testing, multivariate testing, ROI tracking, and website visitor tracking.

Integrations

### Which third-party tools and platforms does Simon AI integrate with?

Simon AI integrates with 9 third-party tools, including Shopify, Snowflake, Oracle Commerce, Braze, Iterable, Attentive, Movable Ink, Lob, and Lobbipad. These catalog integrations span commerce, marketing automation, customer messaging, data, and direct mail workflows.

Getting Started and Support

### What training and onboarding options does Simon AI offer?

Simon AI provides in person training, live online sessions, and documentation to help teams get started. In person training supports direct, guided instruction, live online sessions allow remote walkthroughs, and documentation gives written reference material for self-paced review and setup.

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Simon AI offer?

Simon AI provides email/help desk support, a FAQ/forum, and a knowledge base. These channels give users written guidance and self-service help for common setup and usage questions. No reviewer feedback about support experience is available here, so no claims can be made about response speed or helpfulness.
