BuildingLink Canada was established in 2012 by Toronto based entrepreneur Nicholas Gill and BuildingLink LLC founders Jerry and Zach Kestenbaum. Having pioneered one of the first cloud based residential property management solutions in Canada Gill was an ideal partner for BuildingLink as they looked to build on their success in the U.S. market. The combination of Gill’s local market expertise and BuildingLink’s proven platform has been a recipe for success since inception.

“We are very grateful for all the support our Canadian customers have given us over the last decade and are super excited about the next decade of BuildingLink as a prop-tech leader in Canada”.

Nicholas Gill

Director of Marketing and Sales

BuildingLink Canada
