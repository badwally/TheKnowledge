## What is Black Crow AI?

Black Crow AI is a Shopify tool that uses machine learning to predict shopping behavior patterns to efficiently acquire and retain more customers across digital marketing channels.

The platform enables eCommerce brands to manage customer data, personalize customer relationships across marketing channels, create email and SMS lists through more opt-ins, and more. Key features include privacy-friendly tracking, customer tracking, and user recognition.

Key Benefits:
\- Grow email and SMS lists through more onsite opt-ins.
\- Increased abandonment email/SMS flows.
\- Better personalization and customer-journey identification.

## FAQs about Black Crow AI

Overview

### What company size and specific industries is Black Crow AI built for?

Black Crow AI is designed for Shopify-powered, non-headless direct-to-consumer brands. It targets retail and ecommerce businesses that sell directly to customers through Shopify, rather than companies in unrelated industries or brands using headless commerce setups.

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Black Crow AI?

Black Crow AI offers AI and machine learning, predictive analytics, and real-time analytics for behavior and customer tracking. Core marketing features include audience targeting, campaign management, attribution modeling, conversion tracking, and personalization, along with dashboards, reporting, workflow automation, and third-party integrations.

Integrations

### Which third-party tools and platforms does Black Crow AI integrate with?

Black Crow AI integrates with 3 third-party platforms: Attentive, Klaviyo, and Shopify Plus. These catalog integrations cover marketing automation and ecommerce connections, giving Black Crow AI direct links to tools commonly used for customer messaging and online store operations.

Getting Started and Support

### What training and onboarding options does Black Crow AI offer?

Black Crow AI provides live online training, webinars, and documentation to help teams get started. Live online sessions support guided setup and direct instruction, webinars cover product topics in a group format, and documentation offers written material for self-paced reference and feature review.

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Black Crow AI offer?

Black Crow AI provides Email/Help Desk, a Knowledge Base, and Phone Support. No reviewer feedback about support is available in the catalog data, so support quality, response times, and common frustrations cannot be assessed from user comments.
