## Compare Orita to Competitors

Hellodialog is a Dutch e-mail marketing tool for small and medium-sized businesses. The platform allows users to create, send, and analyze e-mail campaigns, featuring a drag & drop editor, segmentation options, A/B testing, and e-mail automation. The services comply with AVG regulations. It was founded in 2001 and is based in Haarlem, Netherlands.

Flexmail is an email marketing platform that provides tools for email campaigns. The company offers services including email campaign management, marketing automation, customer relationship management (CRM), and analytics to analyze customer engagement. Flexmail's platform allows the creation of targeted emails, management of subscriber data, and analysis of campaign performance. It was founded in 2000 and is based in Genk, Belgium.

Easymailing is an email marketing platform that specializes in sending and analyzing email campaigns. The company offers a suite of tools for designing professional templates, managing subscribers, ensuring GDPR compliance, and generating detailed reports to optimize marketing efforts. Easymailing provides support services to assist customers throughout their email marketing processes. It was founded in 2020 and is based in Madrid, Spain.

Copernica is a company that offers marketing automation and email marketing solutions across digital platforms. Their offerings include a marketing automation platform that supports the creation, management, and optimization of multi-channel campaigns with segmentation and personalization features. They also provide cloud-based SMTP services and on-premise Mail Transfer Agent solutions for email delivery and management. It was founded in 2001 and is based in Amsterdam, Netherlands.

EmailOctopus is an email marketing platform that provides tools for businesses to manage and grow their email subscriber lists and campaigns. The company includes a drag and drop builder, list segmentation, targeting, automation, and analytics. EmailOctopus serves various sectors including ecommerce, real estate, charities, and small businesses. It was founded in 2015 and is based in London, England.

Automizy is a company that focuses on email marketing automation within the digital marketing sector. Its offerings include tools for growing contact lists, building and automating campaigns, and analyzing performance. The company provides services for businesses looking to improve their email marketing through automation and analytics. It was founded in 2014 and is based in Budapest, Hungary.
