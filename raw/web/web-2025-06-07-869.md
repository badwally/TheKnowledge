| Abstract: |
This study addresses the condition assessment of a reinforced concrete (RC) bridge pier after an earthquake based on seismic responses. This study proposes a physics-informed neural network (PINN) for solving a nonlinear multi-degree-of-freedom (MDOF) structural identification problem to localize and quantify damages. This study adopts a stacked bilinear rotational spring model. Governing equations, Newmark-β method, and hysteresis information are incorporated into a loss function of a deep-layered neural network. The method was validated using real seismic responses, which were obtained by full-scale shaking table experiments. The method successfully estimated the distributions of elastic stiffnesses and ductility factors (DFs) along the pier height in different deterioration cases. The distributions revealed the local decrease and increase of the parameters corresponding to the observed concrete and rebar damages. |
