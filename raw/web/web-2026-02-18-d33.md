VOYAGER CONDO, CO-OP & HOA

# Optimize condo operations with one platform

Consolidate accounting, operations and maintenance on a single platform with real-time analytics. Add CondoCafe and provide online services to resident owners for a seamless experience.

## Features

### Save time and cut costs

Ensure accuracy and drive efficiency with an integrated database and automated workflows. Track association fees, ownership changes, maintenance items and violations with Voyager Condo.

### Tailor to your needs

Customize views, menus and reports to suit your organization’s needs. Streamline compliance with regional regulations and accounting updates. Enhance services with CondoCafe for online payments and paperless certificates.

### Support owners and renters

Easily manage associations and rental units with streamlined workflows. If an owner requests that you manage a rental or series of rentals, simply add the unit(s) to a rental pool property to automate the entire process.

### Keep board members informed

Maintain critical board member and meeting records, along with sign-in sheets. Scan and attach meeting minutes to the integrated meeting record while board members enjoy secure 24/7 access to essential reports through CondoCafe.

### Expand the possibilities

Build a comprehensive condo, co-op and HOA management solution by adding Yardi Payment Processing, Yardi Procure to Pay and Yardi Aspire.

## FAQ

**What is Yardi Voyager for Condo?**

Yardi Voyager for Condo is a comprehensive property management software designed to streamline operations for condominiums, co-ops, and homeowner associations (HOAs). It integrates accounting, operations and maintenance management into a single platform.

**Who can benefit from using Yardi Voyager for Condo?**

This software is ideal for property managers, board members and owners of condominiums, co-ops, and HOAs looking to optimize their management processes and reduce costs.

**What features does Yardi Voyager for Condo offer?**

Yardi Voyager for Condo offers integrated accounting and financial management, maintenance and operations management, real-time analytics and reporting, customizable screens, menus and reports, as well as online services for payments and document processing through CondoCafe.

**Does Yardi Voyager for Condo support online payments?**

Yes, by integrating CondoCafe, users can offer online payment options and paperless certificate processing.

**What kind of support is available for Yardi Voyager for Condo users?**

Yardi offers comprehensive support, including live chat, phone support and email assistance. Users also have access to a dedicated account manager during the setup process.

**Can Yardi Voyager for Condo be customized to fit specific needs?**

Yes, the software allows for extensive customization of screens, menus and reports to meet the unique needs of different organizations.

**What additional modules can be integrated with Yardi Voyager for Condo?**

Users can enhance their management solution by adding modules like Yardi Payment Processing, Yardi Procure to Pay and Yardi Aspire.

**How secure is Yardi Voyager for Condo?**

Yardi Voyager for Condo ensures data security with robust access controls and secure 24/7 access to essential reports for board members through CondoCafe.
