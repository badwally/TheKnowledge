**OuterSignal** has **acquired lifecycle marketing startup Monocle** as the company moves to build a more comprehensive AI platform for ecommerce brands seeking to unify customer intelligence, personalization and automated engagement across the full shopping journey.

Terms of the acquisition were not disclosed.

The deal reflects a broader shift underway in ecommerce software, where vendors are moving beyond standalone AI tools toward integrated platforms designed to manage both customer data and real-time engagement from a centralized operating layer.

OuterSignal has focused on helping brands develop richer customer intelligence through AI agents that analyze publicly available and behavioral data to build more granular audience segmentation and targeting models. Monocle complements that strategy with autonomous lifecycle marketing tools that dynamically adjust messaging, timing and promotional offers across email, SMS and web channels.

Combined, the companies are positioning themselves around what they describe as a “full-stack agentic personalization platform” — an increasingly competitive category centered on AI systems capable of independently interpreting customer behavior and executing marketing decisions without manual workflow configuration.

OuterSignal co-founder and CEO **Zach Zelner** said generative AI is making large-scale one-to-one personalization more achievable, but only if platforms can pair deep customer understanding with autonomous execution capabilities.

That approach reflects growing pressure on ecommerce brands to move beyond fragmented marketing stacks that separate analytics, customer data, engagement automation and customer support into disconnected systems.

As acquisition costs rise and traditional digital advertising performance becomes less predictable, retailers and direct-to-consumer brands are increasingly looking for AI infrastructure capable of improving conversion efficiency, retention and customer lifetime value through more adaptive engagement models.

Monocle’s platform was built to replace rules-based marketing flows with AI-driven customer journeys that respond to individual purchase intent, discount sensitivity and engagement timing in real time. The company’s technology is designed to reduce reliance on static campaign logic and manual segmentation processes commonly used in ecommerce marketing.

Monocle co-founder and CEO **Noam Szpiro** said the acquisition creates a more complete personalization system by combining upstream customer intelligence with downstream engagement automation.

The transaction also highlights how “agentic AI” is becoming a defining theme across commerce technology markets. Rather than functioning as passive recommendation engines or support tools, agentic systems are increasingly being designed to autonomously make and execute operational decisions based on evolving behavioral signals.

That positioning has drawn growing investor and enterprise interest as companies search for ways to operationalize AI across revenue-generating workflows rather than limiting deployments to isolated productivity features.

OuterSignal said existing Monocle customers will continue using the platform without disruption while integration between the two systems expands over time.

The combined platform will target ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands looking to consolidate personalization, segmentation and customer engagement capabilities into a single AI-driven infrastructure layer.

OuterSignal says its broader platform already supports precision audience targeting, personalized content generation and identification of high-value customer segments for brands operating across ecommerce and digital advertising channels.

The acquisition comes amid accelerating consolidation across ecommerce software as AI-native startups and established marketing technology providers compete to become the primary orchestration layer for customer engagement, behavioral analytics and commerce automation.
