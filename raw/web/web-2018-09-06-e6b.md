# Condominium Law

Our condominium law team represents over 400 existing condominium corporations in Nova Scotia and continues to grow their services throughout Atlantic Canada. With extensive experience in this area since 1982, our lawyers advise on all areas relevant to condominium boards and owners, such as:

- Declarations, By-laws and Common Element Rules interpretation and amendments
- Arbitration
- Contracts
- Governance
- Human Rights Issues
- Labour Issues
- Litigation
- Municipal considerations
- Arrears collections, liens and foreclosures
- Tenant evictions

Our team has extensive experience with the creation and registration of new condominium corporations. Our lawyers advise those interested in developing property within the condominium model in all areas including:

- Bare land condominiums
- Conversion condominiums
- Townhouse condominiums
- High rise condominiums
- Commercial condominiums
- The registration process
- Financing
- Creative solutions for land use

Among our team of lawyers are a Past and Current president of the Canada Condominium Institute – NS Chapter and National CCI Board, which is a national organization for all condominium stakeholders.
