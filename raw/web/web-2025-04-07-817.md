# SmartProperty & Strategic Club Solutions Announce Strategic Partnership

SmartProperty® is thrilled to announce a **strategic partnership with Strategic Club Solutions** to bring The Living Reserve Study to more Large Scale Clubs as a powerful new way to transform how clubs and communities manage assets, financial planning, and operational strategies.

This collaboration brings together SmartProperty’s industry-leading financial and asset management solutions—including our innovative Living Reserve Study®—with Strategic Club Solutions’ expertise in operational and strategic planning. Together, we are delivering a seamless, data-driven approach to club management that ensures long-term financial health and operational success.

## Key Features of Navigator Include:

**Living Reserve Study®**– A dynamic financial planning tool that evolves with your club’s needs by combining SmartProperty’s reserve study insights with Strategic Club Solutions’ strategic expertise.**Document Management & Storage**– A secure, organized, and accessible repository for all essential club documents.**Real-Time Scenario Planning**– Enables clubs to forecast and adjust financial and operational strategies in real time.**Project Management**– Streamlines oversight from inception to completion, ensuring projects stay on schedule and within budget.**Decision Support System**– Enhances decision-making with a robust system supported by comprehensive data analytics.**Thought Capital Dashboard**– Visualizes strategic financial data and capital planning in an intuitive, easily accessible dashboard.**Operational Integration**– Seamlessly aligns asset management with strategic planning for enhanced execution.

*“This partnership with Strategic Club Solutions is an exciting step forward in providing clubs with superior financial planning and asset management tools,”* said Damian J. Esparza, Founder of SmartProperty®. *“By integrating our Living Reserve Study® with SCS’s strategic expertise, we are equipping decision-makers with the insights and technology needed to optimize their operations and long-term success.”*

Ryan Doerr, President and CEO of Strategic Club Solutions, adds, *“…this is a game-changer for clubs and communities. By partnering with SmartProperty, we are bringing together the best in financial planning and strategic management to create a comprehensive solution that empowers clubs to make data-driven decisions with confidence.”*

Together, SmartProperty and Strategic Club Solutions are redefining club and community management.
