## The Problem with Traditional Reserve Studies

Associations pay thousands for reserve studies that become outdated immediately. Consultants visit once, estimate useful lives, and deliver a static PDF. By year two, the numbers no longer reflect reality.

## Component Inventory

Every major asset tracked with acquisition date, cost, useful life, and replacement schedule. The foundation of accurate reserve planning.

## Funding Analysis

Multi-method funding calculations that show exactly where you stand and what it takes to reach your target funding level.

#### Funding Methods

- Full Funding (100% of future obligations)
- Baseline Funding (cash flow method)
- Threshold Funding (minimum balance)
- Statutory Minimum (state requirements)
- Custom funding targets

#### Analysis Outputs

- Percent funded calculation
- Fully funded balance target
- Annual contribution requirements
- Special assessment scenarios
- Funding gap analysis

**Percent Funded:**The industry-standard measure of reserve health. CommunityPay calculates your percent funded in real-time as fund balances and component data change—not once every three years.

## 30-Year Cash Flow Projections

See exactly when major expenses hit and whether current funding keeps pace. Adjust assumptions and see the impact instantly.

| Projection Element | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| Annual Expenditures | Projected replacement costs by year based on remaining useful life and inflation. |
| Reserve Contributions | Required annual funding to meet target by method (full, baseline, threshold). |
| Ending Fund Balance | Year-end reserve balance after contributions and expenditures. |
| Percent Funded Trend | How funding level changes over the 30-year horizon. |
| Deficit Years | Years where projected balance goes negative without action. |

## What-If Scenario Modeling

Model different funding strategies before committing. See the impact of contribution changes, special assessments, or deferred replacements.

## Living Document Updates

Unlike static PDFs, CommunityPay reserve studies update automatically when relevant data changes in your system.

#### Automatic Triggers

- Reserve fund balance changes
- Component replaced (expenditure posted)
- New component added to registry
- Contribution rate changed
- Useful life adjustment

#### What Updates

- Percent funded recalculates
- 30-year projections refresh
- Funding recommendations adjust
- Deficit warnings update
- Board reports regenerate

**Replace a Roof:**Post the expense to the ledger. Component registry updates. Useful life resets. Projections recalculate. Percent funded adjusts. All automatic.

## State Compliance

Many states mandate reserve studies and specific disclosure requirements. CommunityPay templates meet statutory requirements by jurisdiction.

**Level 3 Studies:**CommunityPay generates Level 3 reserve studies (no on-site inspection) based on your system data. For states requiring physical inspections or structural assessments, pair with a qualified reserve specialist for initial on-site evaluation. Level 3 studies typically fulfill requirements for ongoing maintenance and annual updates thereafter.

## Board-Ready Reports

Professional reports that communicate reserve status clearly. Executive summaries for board meetings. Detailed appendices for deep dives.

#### Report Sections

- Executive summary with key metrics
- Component inventory with photos
- Funding analysis by method
- 30-year cash flow projection
- Expenditure schedule
- Contribution recommendations

#### Output Formats

- PDF for distribution
- Board presentation slides
- Owner summary (simplified)
- Lender/insurance format
- State-specific disclosure
- Export to Excel for analysis

## System Integration

Reserve studies pull from and write to other CommunityPay modules. No duplicate data entry. No manual reconciliation.
