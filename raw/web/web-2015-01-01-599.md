## Built-in compliance and enterprise-grade security

### Get 360° visibility with configurable reports and dashboards

Facilio's EAM software gives facility leaders a unified view across asset performance, OPEX/CAPEX, SLA tracking, and compliance metrics.

- Create interactive dashboards to monitor asset costs, maintenance status, and open work orders
- Track SLAs, repair frequency, and vendor performance across buildings or service types
- Share tailored reports instantly with finance, operations, or compliance stakeholders

### Make decisions backed by MTTR, MTBF, and lifecycle costs

Go beyond basic logs. Facilio tracks critical asset KPIs that help teams extend asset life and avoid overspending.

- Monitor mean time to repair (MTTR) and failure trends to improve preventive strategies
- Capture asset lifecycle costs—across parts, labor, and downtime—to guide future capital allocation
- Spot underperforming assets through downtime, repeat failures, and excessive service needs

### Digitize every asset and access key data in seconds

With Facilio, every asset is assigned a unique QR or barcode ID, making tracking, requests, and service reviews seamless.

- Scan asset codes to raise service requests or pull up full maintenance history instantly
- View preventive maintenance schedules, overdue tasks, and audit logs at a glance
- Eliminate silos—everything is connected in one platform, accessible on desktop or mobile

### Maintain a clean, complete record for every asset

Facilio captures the entire service lifecycle for each asset to support audits, troubleshooting, and smarter planning.

- View service requests, completed work orders, checklists, and technician notes in one place
- Link inventory usage, vendor actions, and downtime reports to the asset timeline
- Strengthen compliance with easy-to-pull, timestamped maintenance logs

### Address issues before they become emergencies

Facilio automates triggers and alerts based on asset conditions, usage patterns, and maintenance thresholds.

- Identify high-risk assets based on breakdown history and missed PMs
- Set up workflows to automatically escalate maintenance tasks tied to performance metrics
- Use energy usage reports and work order trends to surface operational inefficiencies
