Facility Manager

# Master your maintenance oversight

Facility Manager ensures properties remain in top condition by automating maintenance schedules. Effortlessly track technicians, assign work orders and guarantee timely responses to maintenance requests.

## Features

### Automate scheduling & tracking

Automate your facility maintenance schedules to keep properties operating in peak condition. Assign work orders, track technicians and ensure timely responses to requests. Facility Manager auto-calculates purchase orders and work orders and posts payables and charges, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks. Easily track requests, alerts and exceptions.

### Plan ahead & save money

Avoid costly breakdowns and service interruptions with planned and preventative maintenance oversight that includes code compliance checks, warranty maintenance and regular inspections. Automate all types of maintenance scheduling to avoid equipment breakdowns, save money and keep your properties in optimal condition.

### Complete inspections

Satisfy all types of inspection requirements, including annual, regulatory and due diligence. Easy-to-follow workflows with flexible templates ensure that inspections cover every detail, every time. Facility Manager eliminates paperwork and rekeying of information, giving users immediate access to inspection history reports stored in Yardi Voyager.

### Empower mobile management

Stay connected and efficient with the Facility Manager mobile app. Empower technicians to update work orders, perform inspections and manage maintenance tasks directly from the field. Automatic syncing provides the latest information for quick and confident decision-making. Stay connected and efficient, even without data connection, with support for both Apple and Android devices.

## FAQ

**What is Yardi Facility Manager?**

Yardi Facility Manager is a comprehensive solution designed to optimize facility and maintenance management for commercial real estate companies. It automates maintenance tasks, improves tenant satisfaction and enhances asset value through automated preventive maintenance, property inspections, word order assignments and tracking.

**How does Facility Manager handle work order management?**

Facility Manager streamlines work order management by automating the assignment, tracking and completion of work orders. It ensures prompt attention to maintenance requests and allows tenants to submit work orders online, improving communication and efficiency.

**What is preventive maintenance and how does Facility Manager support it?**

Preventive maintenance involves regular inspections and maintenance tasks to prevent equipment failures and extend the lifespan of your assets. Facility Manager automates preventive maintenance scheduling, ensuring that all necessary tasks are performed on time, reducing downtime and maintenance costs.

**Is Facility Manager considered a CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System)?**

Yes, Facility Manager functions as a CMMS by providing tools to manage maintenance activities, track equipment history and generate work orders. It helps in maintaining compliance with regulatory standards and optimizing maintenance operations.

**How does Facility Manager improve tenant satisfaction?**

Facility Manager enhances tenant satisfaction by providing a user-friendly platform for submitting maintenance requests, tracking word order status and receiving updates. It improves tenant communication and increases convenience. Facility Manager also integrates with CommercialCafe, allowing tenants to access a self-service portal to pay rent, communicate directly with property managers and submit and receive documents.

**Can Facility Manager be accessed on a mobile device?**

Absolutely! Facility Manager offers a convenient mobile app that allows technicians to manage work orders, perform inspections and update maintenance tasks on the go. All information is synced in real-time, even in areas with no data connection.

**How does Facility Manager help to reduce operational costs?**

By automating maintenance tasks, optimizing preventive maintenance schedules and improving resource allocation, Facility Manager helps reduce operational costs. It minimizes equipment downtime, extends asset life and reduces the need for emergency repairs.

**What kind of reporting and analytics does Facility Manager provide?**

Facility Manager offers robust reporting and analytics tools that provide insights into maintenance activities, equipment performance, labor reports, preventive maintenance forecasts and more.

**How does VendorCafe integrate with Facility Manager?**

VendorCafe works with Facility Manager to enhance vendor communication. Vendors can log in to VendorCafe to view invoice statuses, payment details, and other updates, making the vendor payment process transparent and efficient.
