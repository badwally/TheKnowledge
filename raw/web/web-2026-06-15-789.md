## FAQs about Drip

Overview

### What problems does Drip solve?

Drip solves manual, one-size-fits-all email marketing by letting teams build visual automations, segment contacts with tags and behavior data, and trigger timely messages for cart abandonment, lead nurturing, win-back campaigns, and transactional emails. Marketing teams, ecommerce operators, small businesses, and B2B demand generation teams benefit most.

Answer based on 119 reviews

Overview

### Which roles and teams benefit most from Drip?

Drip is most used by business owners, founders, and executives who need scalable email marketing and customer engagement without a large team. Marketing managers and email specialists use it to automate campaigns, segment audiences, and improve conversions, while web developers support ecommerce integrations, tracking, and personalized customer journeys.

Answer based on 184 reviews

Overview

### What company size and industries is Drip built for?

Drip is for ecommerce SMBs, and reviewer data shows it is used primarily by small businesses at 95%, especially in Marketing and Advertising at 19%. It also appears with Information Technology and Services firms at 10% and Computer Software companies at 7%, fitting code-free email subscriber capture and segmented email campaigns.

Answer based on 188 reviews

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Drip?

Drip includes core email marketing features like broadcast emails, email templates, and subscriber management, plus differentiators such as a visual workflow builder, behavior-based automation, tagging and segmentation, and event-triggered campaigns. Reviewers also highlight integrations with Shopify and Facebook Custom Audiences, along with conversion tracking and campaign analytics.

Answer based on 89 reviews

Pricing

### How much does it cost and what fees or limitations are included?

Drip starts at $39/month for its Email Marketing plan, with pricing tied to contact list size, and a free trial is available. Reviewers often call the entry price reasonable and easy to justify, but many say costs rise sharply above 5,000 subscribers, making it less appealing for small businesses or growing lists.

Answer based on 36 reviews

Integrations

### Which third-party tools and platforms does Drip integrate with?

Drip integrates with MemberMouse and Make, both mentioned by reviewers, and supports over 90 integrations overall. Official catalog connections include BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce, Calendly, ClickFunnels, Acuity Scheduling, and Close. These integrations cover ecommerce, scheduling, sales, and marketing workflows across connected third-party platforms.

Answer based on 27 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### How easy is it to onboard and train a team on Drip?

Drip offers live online sessions, webinars, documentation, and videos, which help teams get started quickly on basic email tasks. Beginners and marketers can often launch lists and campaigns fast, while admins or users handling workflows, tags, segmentation, and funnels usually need more time because the feature set brings a clear learning curve.

Answer based on 20 reviews

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Drip offer, and how do users rate the experience?

Drip offers email/help desk, FAQ/forum, a knowledge base, phone support, and chat. Users often describe support as helpful, friendly, and effective during setup, with clear documentation and training. However, experiences are mixed: some report quick chat or same-day replies, while others cite inconsistent quality and multi-day delays.

Answer based on 91 reviews

User interface

### Is Drip easy to use for email marketing and automation?

Drip offers an interface that many marketing teams find easy to learn for email campaigns, workflows, and segmentation. Small businesses often like the visual builder, clean layout, and quick setup, though some users find dashboards, analytics, and automation screens less intuitive, especially on mobile or during more complex workflow management.

Answer based on 30 reviews
