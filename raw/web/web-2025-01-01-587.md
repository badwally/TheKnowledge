# Our Vision

Our vision is to build the physical ledger for the built world, driving financial health and human connection.

## Damian J. Esparza is the Founder and CEO of SmartProperty


With over two decades of experience in the community association industry, a Boston College graduate, Damian is principal shareholder of Barrera & Company and worked at Banc of America Securities and SG Cowen. At SmartProperty, he pioneered the Living Reserve Study™ bringing the reserve study to life, and transforming static reserve studies into dynamic, real-time financial roadmaps for community associations.

His work focuses on transparency, control, and informed decision-making to optimize asset management. Featured in Forbes and other publications, Damian is dedicated to improving community association well-being and preventing failures like the Surfside Condo collapse. He is a proud husband and father to three boys.

## Our Values

### Respect

Respect is the foundation of our collaboration. It means valuing the diverse perspectives, backgrounds, and expertise each person brings to the table.

We practice active listening and maintain a culture of psychological safety where every voice can be heard. By treating our colleagues, partners, and competitors with dignity, we foster an environment of trust that allows us to solve complex problems together.

### Stewardship & Excellence

We view ourselves as caretakers of our company’s mission, our resources, and—most importantly—each other. This value is rooted in the human connection; we don’t just deliver results, we take care of the people behind the work.

Excellence is not a static goal but a continuous commitment to leaving things better than we found them, ensuring that our growth is sustainable and our community remains supported.

### Meeting Customers Where They Are

Success is defined by our ability to understand and solve our customers' specific challenges. We believe that to serve our partners effectively, we must seek first to understand before being understood. Instead of leading with our own agenda, we listen deeply to their unique needs and pain points.

By meeting them where they are—both technically and emotionally—we tailor our approach to provide value that is immediately relevant to their journey and helps them achieve their goals.

### Stay Curious

We encourage curiosity and the courageous pursuit of new ideas, even when they challenge the status quo.

We recognize that meaningful breakthrough innovation often come from experimentation and a willingness to rethink established norms. By fostering a "builder" mindset, we stay ahead of the curve and continuously redefine what is possible in our industry.

### Do It Now

In a fast-moving world, speed is a competitive advantage. We fight complacency by taking immediate action and refusing to defer today’s opportunities to tomorrow.

We embrace the philosophy that "perfect is the enemy of good," prioritizing momentum and iterative progress over over-analysis. We move fast, learn quickly, and keep the needle moving forward every single day.

### Flexibility

Our strength lies in our ability to pivot and adapt. We recognize that plans can change and markets can shift, so we remain agile in our thinking and our operations.

Flexibility means being open to new information, staying resilient in the face of obstacles, and finding creative ways to achieve our goals even when the path forward isn't linear.

### High Agency & Autonomy

We empower our people to be the CEOs of their own roles. High agency means taking extreme ownership of outcomes and finding ways to get things done without waiting for a roadmap.

We trust our team members with the autonomy to make decisions, take risks, and lead their projects, fostering a culture of self-starters who drive the company forward through personal initiative.
