## Our comprehensive condo financial services

#### Reserve Fund Investment Planning Tool

Secure the future of your condo community with our Reserve Fund Investment Planning Tool. Our custom 10-year investment plans are developed in collaboration with your board and property manager to maximize returns while supporting long-term financial stability.

This forward-looking strategy is essential to protecting your community’s financial well-being. Investing in your future is a smart choice, and partnering with a trusted financial institution helps you make the most of your reserves.

#### Custom rates for financial freedom

Enjoy custom rates that deliver the perfect blend of top market rates, ensuring your interest-earning potential is maximized while providing the flexibility to access funds during unforeseen circumstances.

#### Innovative lending solutions

Our consultative lending approach helps safeguard your residents and facilities while supporting long-term financial sustainability as your needs evolve. Count on us as your financial partner, delivering solutions that help your condo community adapt and thrive through changing circumstances.

#### Resident benefits for community prosperity

Empower your residents with FirstOntario membership benefits, including investment bonuses, lending discounts, and educational financial literacy seminars.
