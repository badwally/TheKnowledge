###### Condo Registry Search

# CAO Condo Registry

This is a free searchable database of condo corporations in Ontario based on those corporation’s filings with the Condo Authority. Use this registry to find your condo’s address for service, number of voting units, board director names, management company and more. The Condo Registry can only be used for personal purposes.
