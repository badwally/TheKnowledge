## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a reserve study cost?

It depends on your community's size, location, and what level of service you need. Request a proposal and we'll send a custom quote within 12 hours. We're transparent about pricing — no hidden costs after the quote.

### Is ReservePulse really free with every study?

The Free tier is included with every engagement at no additional charge. Premium features are available as an annual upgrade.

### What's the difference between Free and Premium?

Free covers study viewing, AI summaries, health score, alerts, and 2 basic scenarios. Premium unlocks unlimited AI scenarios, board meeting reports, living study editing, project tracking, and self-service updates.

### Can I buy ReservePulse without ordering a study from PropFusion?

Yes. Standalone access is available if you already have a recent reserve study from another provider.

### Is manager access really free?

Yes. Portfolio-level access for community association managers costs nothing. We want managers to have visibility — it's better for their communities and better for us.

### Which service level should our association choose?

If you've never had a study or need to start fresh, choose a Full Reserve Study. If you have an existing study due for renewal, an Update is the right call. Still unsure? The free consultation is there for exactly this question.

### Do you offer discounts for multiple communities?

We work with managers and management companies overseeing multiple associations. Request a portfolio proposal and we'll provide pricing that reflects the scope.
