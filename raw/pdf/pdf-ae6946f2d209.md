PLEASE READ
Thank you for purchasing the DIY Pump & Chiller Plumbing Plan!
I have experimented & built several DIY Cold Plunges, and this plan is what I have in
my existing plunge. It is the simplest and most effective way that I have come up with
to connect the key mechanical components (pump & chiller) to the rest of my DIY
Cold Plunge Plumbing System.
Of course, with all of the plumbing fittings and parts available, you could modify this
plan with different configurations. If you feel comfortable enough to do that, feel free
- you can use this template as a starting point to build off of. Otherwise, this step-by-
step will help you achieve the exact setup I am using.

Instagram - @DIYCOLDPLUNGE

DANNER SUPREME AQUA-MAG PUMP
I own and have used both the Danner Model 12 (1200 GPH) and the Danner Model
9.5 (950 GHP). The Model 12 is what I use for my current DIY Cold Plunge setup.
Penguin Chillers recommends the Model 9.5 for the 1/2 HP HE. I opted for the pump
with a higher flow rate and have had no issues. You will need to make your own
decision on what is best for you regarding pump size.
These pumps will be 3/4” threaded on the inside of the INLET (alt view 1)
and 3/4” threaded on the outside OUTLET (alt view 2).

PENGUIN HE CHILLER
I own and have built cold plunges with an Active Aqua 1/4HP Chiller and a Penguin
1/2HP HE Chiller. The durability, design, power and lower chilling temperature
are all reasons why I recommend the Penguin over other brands. For this build I
specifically recommend the 1/2HP HE but the setup for the 1 HP HE will be nearly
identical.
This chiller will have a 1” (female) threaded INLET (alt view 1)
and a 1” (female) threaded OUTLET (alt view 2).
