Genetic Screening Summary Report
CLINICAL SUMMARY: Genetic Report with COMT and CLL Protocol
This document summarizes key pharmacogenomic findings and a tailored adjunctive protocol for a patient diagnosed
with early-stage CLL and carrying stress-sensitive polymorphisms.
Prepared for clinical use. Includes ICD-10 reference codes and integrative therapeutic options aligned with patient
genomics.
I. Genetic Polymorphism Summary (Key Findings)
- COMT rs4680 Val/Val: High dopamine degradation in PFC; associated with reduced executive function, increased
stress sensitivity.
- SLC6A4 L(G)/S: Serotonin transporter variant; reduced SSRI efficacy, increased cortisol response to stress.
- MTHFR C677T (T/T) & A1298C (A/A): Compound homozygous; impaired folate metabolism, neurotransmitter
synthesis.
- DRD2 C/DEL: Reduced D2 receptor expression; risk of poor antipsychotic response and opioid sensitivity.
These polymorphisms may increase vulnerability to stress-related inflammation and cognitive fatigue in CLL patients.
II. ICD-10 Diagnostic Codes for Contextual Use
- C91.10 - Chronic lymphocytic leukemia of B-cell type not having achieved remission
- Z13.79 - Encounter for other screening for genetic and chromosomal anomalies
- R41.840 - Attention and concentration deficit
- F32.89 - Other specified depressive episodes (when subthreshold symptoms exist)
III. Clinical Protocol Overview (For Adjunctive Use)
1. Peptide Therapy:
- SS-31: 3mg SC daily (4-6 weeks), then 2-3×/week. Improves mitochondrial function, reduces inflammatory cytokines.
- MOTS-c: 5-10mg SC 2-3×/week. Activates AMPK, supports stress resilience and glucose metabolism.
2. Neuromodulation:
- Transcendental Meditation (TM) or HRV biofeedback: 20 minutes, 2×/day.
- Cognitive support stack (AM): L-Tyrosine 300-500mg + Rhodiola Rosea 100-150mg.
- Evening: Magnesium Threonate 1-2g + L-Theanine 200mg.
