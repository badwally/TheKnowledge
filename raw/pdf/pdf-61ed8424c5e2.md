DMC xCD-1– Research & Advanced Development Motivations
Provide network efficiency, reliability and experience benefits for ALL media applications
Built on a flexible Multisource media format that:
• Improves network performance & reliability → improved QoE
• Enables efficient use of multipath, decentralized and distributed network/compute topologies
• Enables improved network storage, network utilization, and delivery capacity
• Provides advantages over routing, eliminating need for cross-layer coordination and state-management
• Improves network-related QoS impacting QoE:
– Peering congestion
– Path outages
– Slow links
– Effectively offers ZERO switching latency between CDN/Cache paths
Our research in this area began in 2014 – with earliest thinking/ideas dating back to 2010
