CEG Cloud Strategy
Reduced draft for Enterprise Capability launch meeting
06202020 - Dolby Confidential
Streaming has yet to close the quality and cost gap with traditional media. Internet delivery is
complicated and inconsistent – even on the highest performing networks – and consumer
Quality of Experience (QoE) and network Quality of Service (QoS) suffer even as demand for
streaming grows exponentially. Dolby is in a position to chart the course to a future of high-
performance, reliable, hyper-connected and more efficient entertainment experiences. Building
on our deep domain expertise in media and perceptual sciences and systems and the breadth
of our relationships in the media value chain, we are positioned to develop a new paradigm for
media processing and delivery that will improve quality, consistency, and scalability while also
lowering the cost of serving connected entertainment experiences for the streaming services of
today and tomorrow. Our emerging platform product – CEG Cloud – will provide streaming
services with essential cloud-based tools for media/information coding, delivery, playback, and
measurement designed to improve QoS and QoE and create predictable changes to the ways
consumers interact with entertainment.
CEG Cloud is a cloud-native media platform consisting of software components that rely on
Dolby’s unique expertise in human perception, audio and video processing, communications
networks, and connected devices. Streaming services integrate our CEG Cloud tools into their
existing cloud workflows to improve QoE and QoS by intelligently transformation, delivery,
playback, and measurement of experiences. Available as-a-service, CEG Cloud will develop a
material new revenue stream for Dolby while also supporting existing business initiatives like
Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, Codec Licensing, and emerging efforts like Dolby.io. We will create
value for our licensees by exposing cloud capabilities to support device testing, management,
and analytics. We will also make our platform available to our Pro Partners for integration into
their tools. Our cloud-native approach provides Dolby with immediate and future agility and
efficiency, and will create downstream opportunities as the business grows. Probable
opportunities include data products and services, consumer personalization services (made
available via streaming customers), and a potentially large platform opportunity for workflow
tool development, orchestration, and monetization made possible by our position as a trusted
aggregator of devices, content, content services, and media technologies.
To win we must build our cloud platform intelligently, develop new technical and go-to-market
muscles, and establish an expansive data strategy (inclusive of world-class data protection
methods). We aim to have our first prototype customer integrate an audio-focused lightweight
version of our system by October 2020, meet a broad base of potential customers to define
2021-2022 roadmap requirements, and bring a commercial customer (VOD OTT Streaming
Service) to the platform by end of March 2021. To support the eventual scale of the business
(e.g. 250M concurrent sessions at a global level), we must identify and develop new enterprise
capabilities to support a cloud-native SAAS business model. We established an Enterprise team
drawn from CEG, CP, Legal, IT, and Support to lead this capability development project.

Guiding Policies
Guiding policy #1: The customer is our first priority
• We will enter the market in late FY20 with a Phase One trial of a system that includes a limited feature set
for use by one OTT SVOD service. To support the launch efforts, we will establish a Launch Team drawn
from CEG Business leadership, product management, engineering, and CP field engineering.
• We will use the Phase One trial to dig deeply into the challenges and inefficiencies experienced by our
lighthouse partner in the areas of cloud-based media processing and delivery.
• In addition, we will target 25 additional deep-dive meetings with prospective customers across at least
three segments and at least two geographies to map these challenges more broadly and understand the
market’s willingness to pay for our potential value .
• As a team, we will develop a disciplined practice of workflow and meeting documentation with specific,
assigned roles for each meeting and clear follow-up requirements. This will help us build an actionable
knowledge base that can be used internally.
• We will develop a 1-3 year roadmap for CEG Cloud in the early 2021 timeframe, following the trial.
• We will launch our first commercial (i.e. paying) customer on a platform by end of March 2021.
Guiding Policy #2: Our product approach is coordinated with other Dolby experiences and commercial initiatives
to derive and deliver value to these programs.
• We will work closely with the Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, Dolby.io, and OPaL teams to identify overlapping
needs, requirements, and opportunities and map those to the external market. This will augment our
externally-facing approach to requirements gathering, inform our Market Requirements Document, and
allow for more efficient allocation of product and engineering resources across CEG.
Guiding Policy #3: Our product is cloud-native.
• We will develop a lightweight, highly scalable, modular architecture for service design and delivery.
• We will explore partnership and M&A to improve our cloud software capabilities.
• We will work across lower-level cloud infrastructures like AWS and Google Cloud.
• We will explore development of a dedicated cloud software engineering function or team.
Guiding Policy #4: We will measure our product’s performance against an authoritative set of Quality metrics
that Dolby develops in concert with the industry and based on our core expertise in the science of perception.
• We will work across research and engineering to define – likely with an external partner – an industry
recognized standard for perceptual and experiential quality: QoE, In Dolby.
• We will develop internal systems to measure system performance against these metrics. This will likely
include exposing the underlying data feed and algorithm to early customers.
Guiding Policy #5 : Data is critical to our long-term success and the immediate success of our customers.
• We will place the highest priority on data protection and data privacy.
• We will use data to drive decision making internally and during customer engagements. We will establish
the necessary infrastructure, tools, and training to do so.
• We will provide detailed requirements for our data infrastructure to the ATG data platform project to
support the Phase One and Phase Two developments and inform our longer term technical and
commercial data platform capabilities.
• We should explore development of a dedicated data science and engineering function or team.
• Over time, we expect to bring data products and services to market derived from use of our platform. This
long-term ambition will shape decisions at each stage of development.

Guiding Policy #6: We will be very clear about the things we need to own to realize our vision, and disciplined in
engaging externally around the things we do not.
• We will establish a Build-Partner-Buy-Ignore framework with Corporate Development, Engineering, and
CEG Cloud leadership, updating on a quarterly basis.
Guiding Policy #7: We will prioritize feature development based on disciplined analysis of expected value in the
marketplace.
• We will develop a system with FP&A and Corp Dev to help model value of new features or products.
Guiding Policy #8: We will engage our research teams to create a roadmap that aligns with our cloud-first
business objectives over the long term.
• We will establish a set of cross-functional efforts to identify and explore ways to develop and deploy new
experiential features in the cloud, including industry reference metrics for audio and video quality.
• We will support the continuous refinement, execution and meaningful resource investments across near-,
mid- and long-term research activities focused on defensibility in the form of know-how and new IP –
including continued work to advance the existing xCD-1 research and development of a flexible multi-
source network-centric media representation.
o The output is quarterly review and prioritization with the CEG Cloud business teams and
appropriate stakeholders to continually tune.
• We will also establish a process or framework for moving ATG research projects into CEG productization.
This includes XCD-1 and Data Analytics.
Guiding Policy #9: We will use market data to prioritize our roadmap for device/platform support.
• Roku, Android (and Android TV), Apple, and smart TV platforms from LG (WebOS) and Samsung (Tizen)
represent a majority share of high-value streaming traffic and are therefore critical to our early success.
We will scope the development roadmap for each and map the business development plan to define the
engagement strategy and workstreams for each in early FY21.
Guiding Policy #10: We will develop and deploy the enterprise capabilities – internal systems, practices, sales
channels, legal frameworks, and business support and analysis tools – to support our cloud vision and the
associated go-to-market, rather than simply re-purposing existing approaches if they do not fit.
• We will establish an Enterprise Capability team from Legal, CP, Operations, Engineering, CEG Cloud
Leadership, ATG to define the requirements and assign workstreams for the development of the
necessary enterprise systems for the initial and ongoing CEG Cloud business development. This group will
meet at monthly and will be supported by Program Management.
Guiding Policy #11: We will develop a broader strategy for Dolby in Data and Analytics, working closely with ATG
and internal product, legal, and IT leadership.
• By End of Q1FY21 we will have established a Data and Analytics strategy team, established a charter, and
set roadmap objectives for the remainder of the fiscal year. We expect this team to identify both a
technical and commercial path for business development using data derived from use of CEG Cloud.
