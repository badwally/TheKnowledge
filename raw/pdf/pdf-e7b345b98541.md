Re: Extension and Amendment of the LIMITED USE LICENSE AGREEMENT for Dolby Imaging
Professional (the “Agreement”) between Curiosity Stream Inc. (“COMPANY”) and Dolby
Laboratories Licensing Corporation (“Dolby”). Agreement ID AGR1549060, effective
December 18, 2020.
To Whom it May Concern:
Dolby and Company desire to amend and extend the Purpose (defined terms used in this letter
and not otherwise defined have the respective meanings given to them in the Agreement) of
the Agreement and also amend the Agreement as follows:
1. Purpose. Company would like to work with Dolby to use the Deliverables and certain
services provided by Dolby to evaluate the Dolby Media Cloud (“DMC”) service. Under
this DMC service evaluation (the “Evaluation”), a limited group of Company customers
will receive software deliverables, participate in the Evaluation, and Company will
provide data regarding the results of the Evaluation to Dolby along with comparable
results from the use of Company’s system without DMC integration (a “Control
Session”) to enable evaluation of the relative performance of DMC (the “Purpose”). The
parties anticipate that the Evaluation will last 6 weeks.
2. Additional Deliverables. Dolby will provide the deliverables identified on Appendix A to
this letter, and such deliverables will be included as “Deliverables” under the
Agreement.
3. Integration Services. Dolby will provide reasonable technical support services, that in
Dolby’s judgement are appropriate for the Evaluation, to integrate Company’s materials
and systems with DMC for the Evaluation. Such services will include:
a. Integration of Dolby’s DMC Android ReactNative SDK with the Company’s
Android Mobile Application.
b. Integration of Dolby’s DMC Android Native SDK with the Company’s Android TV
Application.

c. Integration and testing of a log data delivery system to support active monitoring
and tuning of the Evaluation (as detailed in Section 4 below).
d. Content Delivery Network (CDN) Integration. This will include integration with
the CDN used by Company.
Company will cooperate with Dolby for Dolby’s successful provision of the foregoing
services. Company will permit Dolby to test and approve in advance, any materials to be
provided to Company customers participating in the Evaluation, and the terms between
Company and Company customers regarding such materials. Company will use the same
CDN(s) for both the Evaluation and Control Session). Company will only provide data to
the extent appropriately authorized and, in any event, Company will not provide to
Dolby any personal information that could be linked to or otherwise reveal the identity
of any Company customer or individual consumer end user participating in the
Evaluation. Company may not use the DMC and services provided by Dolby for any
purpose other than the Purpose.
4. Data Reports. The Evaluation will require Company’s use of its data processing and
analytics pipeline, including third party data processing services, to evaluate system
behavior, performance, and value relative to current solutions. Company will work with
Dolby to generate reports of usage for sessions related to the scope of this Evaluation as
well as other data reasonably requested by Dolby. This data will include all Evaluation
and Control Session data for Dolby’s data analysis and management of the Evaluation.
Company will, as requested by Dolby, publish processed log files to an Amazon Web
Services (“AWS”) account designated by Dolby on a daily basis during the term of the
Evaluation.
5. AWS. The Evaluation will require access to certain data processing on Dolby’s AWS site.
Dolby will work with Company to provide such access to support the Evaluation. Such
access to AWS will be provided at Dolby’s expense.
6. Meetings and Data. The parties will meet regularly to discuss both (a) progress of the
Evaluation, and (b) the appropriate number of Company customers necessary to
participate in the Evaluation to generate the data Dolby reasonably believes necessary
for a successful Evaluation. At a minimum there will be weekly check-in calls of 30
minutes each. The parties will meet at the conclusion of the Evaluation to review data
resulting from the Evaluation. All data generated from the Evaluation will be Licensor
Confidential Information, and Dolby will have the right to use or disclose such data for
any purposes.
7. Agreement. Except as specifically modified and amended by this letter, the Agreement
will remain in full force and effect. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between
the terms of this Agreement and this letter, the terms of this letter shall prevail.
