FRAMING PLAN 2.0
WITH MECHANICAL ENCLOSURE SPACE
Thanks for purchasing the DIY Cold Plunge Plans v2.0! Please note - this plan
is for the 100 gallon Rubbermaid Stock Tank (I got mine at Tractor Supply).
You will now be able to build a frame for your tank that includes a mechanical
area designed for a Penguin 1/2 HP HE Chiller, Pump, Ozone & Filtration System.
Please double check measurements of your equipment before moving forward.
My YouTube Channel will be a great resource in addition to this PDF. Make sure
to follow me there, on TikTok & Instagram - @DIYCOLDPLUNGE
I would also recommend joining my private group on Facebook, we have
thousands of people learning and experimenting with different set-ups.
THEDIYCOLDPLUNGE.COM

BEAM FOR MOUNTING FILTRATION SYSTEM
The purpose of this beam is to mount your ozone + filtration system
(check my YouTube channel for what that looks like).
The height of this board may vary based on your setup.
I attached mine 3.25” from the top framing and it works great.

UPRIGHT
Assemble six “L” shaped uprights using 22” FRAMING LUMBER cuts. You
should have those plus three single 22” boards when this step is completed.
THEDIYCOLDPLUNGE.COM

ATTACHING UPRIGHTS TO BASE
Attach the 22” uprights as displayed in the illustration below.
Please note the location of the middle “L” uprights (magnified for detail).
The single 22” uprights should be centered between each “L” post.
THEDIYCOLDPLUNGE.COM

ATTACHING THE UPPER FRAME
Starting with the outer four posts,
attach the upper frame to the lower frame and uprights.
THEDIYCOLDPLUNGE.COM

YOU DID IT!
If you aren’t plumbing a drain for your plunge, go ahead and attach the last
22” upright, centered between the two “L” posts like on the opposite side.
The last frame should be attached AFTER you plumb the drain out the back
side of your plunge. It should be attached as a reinforcement for your PVC.
For that reason I cannot provide an exact measurement of where to attach it.
Next steps are wrapping, plumbing, finishing the exterior, insulating
and of course enjoying your custom built cold plunge. Great job!
