ABSTRACT
2018 will stand out as the year that the first generation of UHD services started to scale, with over 50 commercial services worldwide, more
than 125 live TV channels with 4K resolution and thousands of hours of VOD programming. With the move to UHD, the consumer video
experience keeps getting better. But it is not just the video quality that is getting better, it is also the services supported by UHD. These
include features, such as high dynamic range imaging and next-generation audio, which together deliver a new and improved consumer
experience. When it comes to audio, next-generation codecs such as Dolby AC-4 are enabling a slew of enhanced audio experiences (i.e.,
more immersive and personalized) that consumers have shown they want.
For anyone who is involved in the creation or delivery of media and entertainment experiences for broadcast or streaming, it is important to
understand what these new audio features are and how they will be rolled out. This paper outlines the market and industry drivers that are
creating the demand for next-generation audio codecs. It also describes how these codecs are being staged in both the network and in the
end devices that deliver these experiences to the consumer, wherever they are viewing from.
As with any new technology, it is important to understand how the transition from today’s services will be orchestrated. This white paper
will present Harmonic’s ongoing technology advancements for the native implementation of the Dolby AC-4 codec in its market-leading
portfolio of media processing solutions and services. It will also describe the features, operational benefits and exciting new consumer
experiences that Dolby AC-4 brings to next-generation audio entertainment services.
Finally, this paper examines how partnerships between companies such as Harmonic and Dolby are helping to fuel the transition from
existing broadcast and OTT services, to future experiences enabled by these new technologies.

1. INTRODUCTION
Driven in part by the Olympics and the World Cup, 2018 was a big year for next-generation TV concepts and features. Specifically, the first
generation of UHD TV services were rolled out, with over 50 commercial services worldwide, 125 live 4K UHD TV channels and thousands
of hours of VOD content. The industry has continued to raise the bar for the modern-day viewing experiences across platforms. Service
providers are continuing to experiment with new features and functionalities that will surprise and delight consumers.
The demand for ever-higher picture quality, with more and better pixels, goes hand in hand with the quest for an equally compelling set of
features and functionalities for audio. With the advent of audio codecs such as Dolby AC-4, service providers now have the tools they need
to deliver these audio services and to make entertainment more engaging.
Consumers want better audio. A study by Pearl Group in the U.S., found that viewers are looking for a better experience that includes
improvements in both video and audio. For audio, this means improved intelligibility, use of descriptive audio and being able to have a great
experience on any device.
At the same time, user behavior is changing. Not all content is viewed in the home. Viewers are no longer chained to their TVs, and there is
an ever-growing variety of devices being used to watch content.
Dolby AC-4 is a powerful new audio emission format that meets today’s content delivery requirements and supports video service
innovations. Dolby AC-4 next-generation audio (NGA) transforms broadcast, providing viewers with a greatly improved quality of
experience, which includes personalized, immersive and more lifelike entertainment. Dolby AC-4 enables NGA experiences to be delivered
and paired with the enhanced visual experience that 4K UHD with HDR delivers.
The codec is only part of the story, however. To reap the benefits provided by NGA, the entire ecosystem and the new and modern
workflows for delivering video and audio need to be ready to support these features. That is why companies like Harmonic play acritical
role in the deployment of next-generation audio. By supporting the integration of Dolby AC-4 across broadcast and streaming for both
hardware-based and cloud-based workflows, Harmonic is helping drive ecosystem innovation.
NGA revolutionizes audio quality for end users. Dolby AC-4, as delivered by Harmonic, is the way that broadcasters and streaming providers
can leverage these revolutionary changes for delivering new and improved user experiences.

3. MARKET AND INDUSTRY MOMENTUM
While unseen to end users, technical standards are critical to the ultimate success of new video services. Standards are the pillar of
technology, as they ensure the interoperability and economies of scale that make mass market adoption possible. To this end, global
standards bodies such as DVB and ATSC have developed standards that will govern the rollout of next-generation TV, including audio.
Standards were created to ensure smooth deployment and sustainable future for broadcast TV services delivered across a wide variety of
workflows and to multiple consumer devices. These new standards have to be extensible, scalable and ready for an unknown future.
Dolby AC-4 is the audio codec of choice for many national broadcast standards. One of the key drivers behind the strong market momentum
around Dolby AC-4 is its acceptance as part of the main transmission standards in many regions and countries throughout the world. To date,
Dolby AC-4 has been adopted by standards bodies in 11 countries, including the U.S., Canada, Italy, the NorDig countries and the UK (Freesat).
The acceptance from regulatory bodies in these countries, provides assurance that the growth of NGA as delivered by Dolby AC-4 will continue.
Dolby AC-4 is also supported by many global standards bodies covering multiple elements of the broadcast and OTT ecosystem. For

develop a framework to facilitate nationwide deployment of ATSC 3.0 services (including best practices), to test consumer devices and to
collect real-time consumer input through surveys and focus groups. As part of this trial, Dolby AC-4 will be used to deliver next-generation

Harmonic will provide participating stations in Phoenix with its Electra X ATSC 3.0 media processor. This solution integrates Harmonic’s

4. IMPLEMENTATION OF THE DOLBY AC-4 NGA CODEC NATIVELY IN MEDIA PROCESSING SOLUTIONS AND SERVICES
To ensure that Dolby AC-4 is seamlessly incorporated into next-generation service workflows, it is critical that it be integrated into the
media processing platforms that deliver these new experiences to the home. To this end, Harmonic has partnered with Dolby Laboratories
to integrate AC-4 NGA into its market-leading portfolio of media processing solutions and services. In 2015, the two companies made
history when they worked to deliver the first live on-air transmission of Dolby AC-4.
Harmonic offers solutions for video delivery via the widest possible deployment types, from hardware appliances to cloud services (SaaS).
Through Harmonic’s video encoding, audio encoding and ancillary data processing solutions, service providers can deliver a complete end
user entertainment experience.
As far as audio encoding is concerned, Harmonic recently added Dolby AC-4 native encoding to its solutions. This complements the existing
support for encoding in MPEG 1 Layer2, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, AAC, and HE-AAC. Pass-through of pre-compressed audio,
including Dolby AC-4, is also supported.
There are several advantages for embedding Dolby AC-4 encoding within the video encoder.
The first advantage is density. With the integration, the combined solution requires less resources and less equipment (in case of
appliances), or less CPU (when using a software solution). By embedding, the interfaces are mutualized for all processing requirements. This
creates economies on space, power and cooling. If a software solution is used, this saves on required resource allocation.

5. CONTENT DELIVERY MECHANISMS FOR DOLBY AC-4
Dolby AC-4 can be used with a variety of video distribution architectures. All distribution workflows can benefit from the improvements
offered by Dolby AC-4 to help deliver programing that will keep viewers engaged. These delivery architectures include over the air
broadcast (OTA), over the top (OTT) received through internet connections, satellite, IPTV and cable.
To achieve this level of flexibility, the Dolby AC-4 syntax can be adapted to the different transmission mechanism, whether it is a transport
stream, or file based. The codec has been standardized for most container formats used today:
• Like Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus, Dolby AC-4 can be carried over AES3, using formatting as per SMPTE ST-337. This extends to
carriage over SDI (Serial Digital Interface) and its derivatives, such as those defined in SMPTE ST-2022-6.

6.1 End-User Benefits
• Flexibility / Agility
Dolby AC-4 provides flexibility and agility like no other audio codec before. First, it enables rendering on multiple devices, providing
consistent and premium speaker playback across all screens from stereo TV, to home theatre, to mobile device, to headphones. This
agility is achieved with a single stream that saves data and ensures always-perfect synchronization. Multiple presentations can be
carried in a single bitstream, enabling numerous versions of the audio experience — such as different languages or commentaries to be
delivered — but using less data.
Finally, Dolby AC-4 produces an optimized output, for an enhanced experience on any device – whether it be a TV, laptop, tablet, phone
– or potentially even a watch.
• Accessibility
Harmonic and Dolby recognize the fact that users want the freedom to view television in their chosen way and, in this respect, Dolby
AC-4’s accessibility is another key advantage. Dolby AC-4 has integrated a feature called audio description that allows a voice to
describe the video scene. It is possible for the end user to adjust the relative levels of this descriptive voice and the main audio of the
video content to ensure their own chosen balance between the audio description and the video content atmosphere.
Dialog in the content can also be enhanced. Depending on the listening conditions, the user may decide to boost the dialogs above
background sounds, for better comprehension.
Dolby AC-4 enables the delivery of enticing new user experiences, where the content creator can enhance audio way beyond the
traditional stereo or 5.1 mix.

• Personalization
Harmonic and Dolby recognize that users have their own unique preferences and that they appreciate customized video experiences.
Dolby AC-4 provides audiences with the power to choose between different audio experiences, such as alternative languages, or
director’s commentaries. They can even select their favorite sports team announcer. Sports fans can change the audio balance to focus

Immersiveness is a key element of audio, and Dolby AC-4 enables a more lifelike experience in this respect, providing three-

compatible for playback on any device from stereo TV to sound bar, home theater, PC, and headphones
6.2 Operational benefits
In addition to end user benefits, Dolby AC-4 brings a number of advantages to operators, such as:
• Operational simplification
Dolby AC-4 brings simplicity and cost savings to the operator, whether for stereo or 5.1 content.
Ad insertions are greatly simplified, thanks to the alignment of audio frames to video frames. If operators need to insert different
content at a specific video frame, the audio insertion can be done at the same frame, with no need to re-encode audio at splices and in
head-end turnarounds.
Even audio loudness is taken care of with Dolby AC-4, thanks to automated loudness and dynamic range management.
Naturally, Dolby AC-4 provides better compression efficiency, which can translate to more programs in less space. More details about
this aspect will be described in the next section.

7. RENDERING ON CONSUMER DEVICES
The needs and expectations of TV audiences are changing. At the same time, the number of devices used within each household has
reached the highest point ever. Dolby has a long history of working closely with TV and other consumer playback device manufacturers to
enable new experiences. Dolby AC-4 continues to follow this model, and the resulting collaborations will bring innovative experiences to
consumers and enable viewing devices to better meet the needs of all audiences.
Millions of Dolby AC-4 decoders have already been deployed in a wide range of consumer products. All of the major TV manufacturers
support Dolby AC-4 in selected models, and there are an increasing number of models shipping with Dolby AC-4 support. TV and set-
top box manufacturers that support Dolby AC-4 include Bang & Olufsen, Hisense, Huawei, TPVision, Vizio, LG, Panasonic, Sagemcom,
Samsung, Sharp, Skyworth, Sony and TCL. The market share of devices with Dolby AC-4 will continue to grow, and it is estimated that by
2020, the majority of all new UHD TVs in Europe and the U.S. will support Dolby AC-4.
As the NGA codec for delivering immersive audio, Dolby AC-4 will continue to support the delivery of Dolby Atmos®. For TVs, soundbars
and home theater systems featuring Dolby Atmos®, Dolby AC-4 decoders will support conversion to Dolby Atmos® bitstreams that are
supported in these devices. This means that a consumer that purchased even one of the first Dolby Atmos® home theater systems can get
an exceptional immersive experience via Dolby AC-4.
In addition, an increasing number of mobile phones are shipping with Dolby AC-4 as a standard codec. This includes devices from Samsung,
Huawei and Sony, amongst others. Broadcasters and operators also have the option to include Dolby AC-4 decoding software directly into their
apps, allowing them to quickly reach millions of consumers with the efficiency and new experiences that Dolby AC-4 delivers.
8. CONCLUSION
Next-generation video services need enhanced audio experiences, and Dolby AC-4 has been developed to satisfy these requirements.
Whether the delivery pipe is broadcast, IPTV or OTT, more consumer-friendly experiences can be now delivered at lower bit rates.
Dolby AC-4 delivers advantages to consumers and to service providers. For consumers the benefits of consistent loudness levels, accessibility
features, and immersive and personalized content remove the pain points of existing platforms and increase their engagement with the
content. For service providers the greater coding efficiency, flexibility and enhanced ad insertion support make it simpler to monetize content.
This will only get easier to access as more organizations join those in the 11 existing countries that have included Dolby AC-4 in their local
standards. Services such as KQED and NRJ have led the way in successful real-world deployments. Thanks to the native implementation of
Dolby AC-4 in Harmonic’s market-leading portfolio of media processing solutions and services, it is now an easy move to make.
The ecosystem to support NGA is growing rapidly. Millions of devices are already deployed featuring Dolby AC-4, including many models of
UHD TVs and mobile devices. And while Dolby AC-4 brings great benefits to stereo and 5.1 content, the amount of content mixed in Dolby
Atmos®, or created in a personalized format, is continuing to grow. Nevertheless, none of this will be successful unless this great content can
be encoded efficiently and reliably. That is where the partnership between Harmonic and Dolby is completing the ecosystem.
Harmonic is a pioneer in media encoding and processing solutions, and this paper has provided an overview of its ongoing technological
advancements regarding the native implementation of the Dolby AC-4 codec. Dolby AC-4 is enabled across Harmonic’s wide range of
deployment models, from appliances to SaaS. By integrating Dolby AC-4 with its video encoding solutions, Harmonic enables compelling
NGA features to be delivered simpler and easier.
