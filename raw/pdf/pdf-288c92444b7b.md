Cyclical Analysis: 1.3% cycles are suggesting slight preference for upside (~1.3:1),
whereas 5% cycles are suggesting significantly more room to the downside (~2.5:1).
When the cycles are mixed, I tend to want to wait for a better point of entry in which
the short term R:R is aligned with the long-term R:R before joining in the direction of
the latter. At present, a short-term pop to ~6140 is the expectation in the case of
continuation, while ~5985 is the target for a new down cycle having begun.
