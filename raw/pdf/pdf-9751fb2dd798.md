Recap and Plan: last week ES expanded on the range from the prior (holiday) week, ultimately closing below it. The 20 DMA (and 50 DMA in confluence) acted as
resistance, and support was attempted around the 100 DMA (in confluence with the yearly SPX/ES opens), but in the end the NFP beat to the upside sent rate cut
expectations lower along with ES. The POC for the week is near the midpoint, which is in between the SPX/ES yearly opens. The 75% extensions to either side held the
range, and price ended near the latter, in confluence with the 150 DMA. My weekly pivot will start off there, at 5860. I will move it to the the 100 DMA, and then weekly
mid, if/when tagged. My expected range will be 5730 (200 DMA and -100% ext) to 6180 (100% ext and essentially ATHs). Next week, we have CPI and PPI, which
should take center stage. Chop until then could be expected as the market awaits data; however fear seems to be kicking in, and pre-emptive positioning may also be
expected. If to the downside, and if CPI shifts the narrative, an explosive move higher may follow.
Zooming out, the prior year left many HVNs, and we just found the latest at ~5850. The next is at (creatively) 5750, coinciding with the 200 DMA, which held the most
extreme downside of 2024. As that’s only 100 pts away, it’s appearing more likely to be tested early this year. Below there, another HVN at (you guessed it) 5650 guards
the POC of 2024 at 5450. Apparently 5550 doesn’t get to be a level of interest, suggesting that if we were to drift below 5600, that return to 5500 may also occur swiftly.
So far this year (this contract, really) has been quite volatile, and doesn’t seem to be slowing down. Some stuffing of sellers may be required for things to settle down,
and that hasn’t happened since ES has lost the 20 DMA. Returning above ES~6035 in the coming sessions would lead to that scenario.
