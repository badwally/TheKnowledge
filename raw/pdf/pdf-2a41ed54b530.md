DRAFT JD
Senior Director of Business Strategy and Development
CEG CLOUD
The Senior Director of Business Strategy and Development will lead the planning, creation, and
execution of the multi-year development plan for the Dolby CEG CLOUD platform.
A combination of business strategy, business development, product management, product
marketing, and technology strategy, this role serves as an operating officer for CEG CLOUD,
collaborating closely with internal stakeholders as the individual accountable for CEG Cloud’s
successful market development and introduction. Drawing on demonstrated experience in
media cloud services, content delivery, technology startups, and OTT content service
enablement, this individual will partner closely with the CTO and product leaders to set the
course for CEG CLOUD and help lead the program to success.
- Establish the business strategy for the Dolby CEG CLOUD platform in partnership with
business unit leadership, CTO, and technology leadership.
- Develop and manage CEG Cloud product and business development teams.
- Lead business planning process, including market analysis and segmentation, product
milestones, definition of the partner ecosystem.
- Responsible for development of platform and product requirements. Identify the
internal workstreams and investments required to establish the Minimum Viable
Product for CEG CLOUD, in partnership with the CTO and technology leadership.
- Develop and manage pipeline of internal and external proof-of-concept projects in
service to the development of the platform strategy. Establish framework for target
identification. Serve as interface with sales organization as CEG CLOUD moves from
Build to Scale phases.
- Define product pricing strategy and associated revenue plan for delivery and
presentation to internal stakeholders.
- Define staged sales strategy; accountable individual for revenue.
- Manage alignment and graduated integration of CEG CLOUD into ongoing Dolby product
development efforts within the Consumer Entertainment and/or Voice business groups.
- Lead evangelism for the CEG CLOUD program internally and externally.
Success in the first year is based on:
1) Delivery of a multi-phased business, product and operating plan for CEG CLOUD.
2) Execution of (TBD) proof-of-concept implementations with ‘beta’ partners, inclusive of
synthesizing iterative learnings into the business, product, and operating plans.
3) Development of a business development and marketing capacity within the scope of the
CEG CLOUD team, inclusive of talent acquisition and leadership.
4) Leading CEG CLOUD to first revenue.
This role reports to the business group VP.

Role is based in SF and will require 20-40% domestic and international travel to drive
engagements and evangelism, as well as coordination of distributed internal teams.
