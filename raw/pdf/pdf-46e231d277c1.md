CuriosityStream Beta Test Proposal
DRAFT
09302021
Overview:
Early tests of Dolby’s xCD-1 research tools on CuriosityStream traffic showed positive impacts
on Quality of Service and Quality of Experience KPIs. The last full test yielded a 19% increase in
average session length for streams delivered using Dolby’s unique multi-source, multi-path
delivery optimization technology. Dolby has since moved xCD-1 from research toward
productization. Today, xCD-1 is an essential part of Dolby Media Cloud (DMC), our system for
the optimization of QoS and QoE for streams of any format delivered to any device via multi-
CDN delivery workflows. CuriosityStream has been an essential partner to Dolby during the
evolution of xCD-1.
For the next round of testing, we propose use of the DMC service infrastructure to execute a
progression of tests designed to detail the specific behavior and impact of DMC stream-
enhancement on sessions delivered to Android mobile and living room applications.
By extending the tests to the DMC platform, CuriosityStream will benefit in the following ways:
1. Continued improvement to QoS and QoE for enhanced Android TVs, directly benefitting
their users with improved playback stability and performance;
2. Expanded insight into specific delivery and playback behavior through detailed
hypothesis development, testing, and analysis, in collaboration with an expanded team
of research, engineering, and development experts from Dolby;
3. Direct influence into the ongoing development of DMC, shaping the system to meet
CuriosityStream’s specific needs in advance of general product availability in 2022;
4. Access to expanded development resources from Dolby to support the ongoing test
