3. SENSE’S SCOPE OF WORK
3.1. Condominium Act Requirements
As per the Condominium Act R.S., c. 85 last revised in 2022 by NS Bill No. 106 and Condominium
Regulations under Section 33 and 46 of the Condominium Act, reserve funds are to be used solely for
the major repair and replacement of the common elements and assets of the Corporation.
The Reserve Fund Study must contain:
 a physical analysis including an inventory and assessment of each common element item that is
expected to require major repair or replacement within at least 20 years of the date of the study;
and
 a financial analysis which recommends a funding plan to adequately offset the anticipated
reserve fund expenditures.
Studies must be updated every five years, with site inspections, to confirm that funding levels are
adequate to provide for future anticipated repairs/replacements.
Our intent in preparing this Study was to:
 meet the requirements of the Condominium Act and associated regulations;
 make the report easy to understand, and be a useful document to assist in managing the
building; and
 include a sensible plan for managing costs to maintain, repair, and renew the property over both
the short and long term.

4. PROJECTED RESERVE FUND EXPENDITURES
Using the information gathered, we created an inventory of the common elements of the Corporation and
anticipated the year and cost for their major repair or replacement.
The estimated timing of repair/replacement is based on typical service lives for similar components,
adjusting for current conditions and past performance history. We assume good maintenance practices
are followed. Actual timing can vary depending on many factors, including the frequency or intensity of
future building maintenance. Under strict maintenance regimens, the timing of repairs and renewal
projects could possibly extend beyond what is presented in this report. Conversely, if essential and
timely maintenance is not carried out, it may be necessary to undertake repairs and renewal projects
years sooner than what is presented in this report.
