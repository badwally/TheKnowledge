Dolby Media Cloud
Business Plan
Overview for Enterprise Team
10 Mar 2021

What is Dolby Media Cloud?
• Dolby Media Cloud is a cloud-based service that easily integrates with
existing content services.
• It will improve consumer engagement and quality of experience in
objectively measurable ways and thereby reduce subscriber churn
and the cost associated with addressing quality of experience
problems.
• This is achieved through optimizing delivery based on properties of
the content, network conditions, understanding of the playback
devices and playback environment while taking viewers’ physiologies
and preferences into account.

Success in 2021-2022
• Dolby Media Cloud will enter the market in June 2021 with a v1 SaaS system focused on improving the
quality of content delivery through use of xCD.
• We will scale to 1-3 paying customers by end of CY2021 (one of which is a ‘tier-one’ service) and support for a class of 3-5
connected device platforms.
• By end of FY21 we will have an operational system capable of supporting more than 50m simultaneous streams with 24/7
support capability and 99% uptime
• We will show a positive operating margin on a per-user-session basis in FY22.
• Our system will be capable of ”experiential decisioning” –data-driven use of Dolby processing functions on a per-session
basis –by mid CY22, with the expectation that these component functions will be monetized as part of the platform.
• Services will integrate our capabilities within their endpoint playback applications and their enterprise data services.
• We will explore monetization strategies that align with the way our customers measure business success –i.e. engagement increases
measured A/B via our data platform.
• To support the function and expand the capabilities of DMC, we will develop an ecosystem of technology providers including
online video platforms, device operating and middleware systems, content delivery networks, media player software, data
processing, and AI applications.

Our Opportunity
• We address a global opportunity for on-demand, live, and real-time video
experiences that exceeds $16BN.
• This is inclusive of ~$12BN in media delivery, with the remainder allocated to media
processing, media platforms, media software (middleware and players), and data an
analytics.
• This opportunity aligns with the transition of all major media distributors
to Direct-to-Consumer offerings.
• Most media services do not have the immediate capability to build and deploy the
type of experience-aware delivery optimization and management technology that
serves as the foundation of Dolby Media Cloud.
• As the market for D2C matures, the opportunity will expand as data and
machine learning functions become more essential to media services.

Business Model
• Dolby Media Cloud is a Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) offering, monetized based on
tiered bandwidth commitments.
• Target is ARR of $1M/customer
• Focus on per-stream profitability based on a comprehensive variable cost model
• P&L driven requires integration of usage analytics
• New customers will incur an annual platform fee of ~$100K.
• This includes training, on-boarding, the device SDK (~for the first (X) platforms), system use, and minimal
analytics engine.
• Quarterly upfront commitments for stream utilization.
• $/minute delivered based on use-or-lose tiered commits with defined $/min overage
• The Dolby Analytics engine will serve as the audited agent for collection of usage data.
• We aim to structure the business model and associated contracts such that we recognize most revenue
up front
• Support contracts to match industry standard
• Silver/Gold/Platinum
• Integration, 24/7 support, etc

Growth Strategies
• Monetize existing Dolby streaming service relationships
• We believe we have an opportunity to transition “enablement” relationships to “vendor” relationships by delivering a
platform service that builds on Dolby’s established credibility in audio and video coding and ecosystem development. We will
align our initial investment in sales and marketing to capitalize on the transition of these existing relationships, thereby
driving strong sales efficiency.
• Establish indirect channels to capture “fat tail” in 2023
• We will engage with platform providers such as Brightcove, Kaltura, Synamedia, et al to address streaming providers outside
current (or future) account management. These are experienced video SaaS and PaaS providers with scaled up sales
functions capable of addressing a longer tail of the streaming service market (including regional broadcasters).
• Extend Product Leadership
• The Dolby Media Cloud is suited for expansion across products, solutions, industries, and use-cases. We will continue to
invest in the decisioning systems and the underlying platform services (e.g.the data pipeline) in order to deliver and
measure more expansive experiential processing. This represents a long-term opportunity for SAM expansion.
• Increase Revenue from Existing Customers:
• We plan to continue to increase sales within our existing customer base through increased usage of our platform and the
cross-selling of additional products and solutions. This requires integration at the service and support level.
• Augment our Platform Offering through Partnerships and Opportunistic M&A:
• We plan to increase the breadth of partnerships with our technology partners, further allowing us to provide the most
comprehensive video solutions to our customers. Additionally, we intend to continue to explore potential transactions that
could enhance our capabilities or increase the scope of our technology footprint.
