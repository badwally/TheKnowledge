Recap and Plan: last week ES started off somewhat bearish, but after tagging the prior week’s low decided that it had other plans. On Tuesday (the
election) ES shot straight up, mostly in the initial hours of Globex. I caught much of this, following my pivots, but unfortunately turned bearish a bit too
early at the ~100% extension of the prior week. Flat would have been better than short with that level of bullishness and with a bullish catalyst.
Anyways, this week I will start my pivot out near last week’s close at 6020. Once/if tagged, I will move it to the 25% extension and then to the
midpoint. That is to say, I will stay in shorts if sellers continue to show strength; otherwise, I will flip long at lower (but pre-determined) levels. The
volume profile last week shows a distinct LVN between ~5800 to 5900, centered on the 20 DMA. The six week profile shows excess, with a double
distribution around 5900 and 5800. Support should be found around ~5900, representing the midpoint of last week, and also the upper of the two
relatively equal areas of high volume. For weekly range I expect ~5560 to 6200 (±100%)… that’s quite wide, and given the range expansion that we
just saw, some consolidation within a smaller range may be more likely than this way in which I typically set my expected range. The major focus this
week will likely be on the US CPI (Wednesday) and on whether the Trump trade continues, begins to consolidate, or mean reverts.
