Dolby Audio Improves Consumers Experience
Third party research into sound quality shows Dolby Audio enhances consumers experience
and public perception of Dolby’s brand is favorable and trustworthy.
In A/B testing, when playing Dolby audio content, consumers perceive the audio and picture
quality to be higher. And when they know the content they are viewing is Dolby their
perception of the quality is even higher.
In a recent study, in collaboration with an OTT, comparing Dolby Audio and Standard Audio,
80% of the users found that the audio quality was improved when listening to Dolby Audio
content*
*From Aug 15-29, 2016, a key OTT in China conducted a study among 100
participants. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION © 2015 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 1

A/B Testing Research Results
December 22, 2016
CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION © 2015 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 2

Research Shows that Consumers Value Dolby
• In a blind test* Users prefer Dolby Audio over standard audio:
– Dolby Audio performs better than Standard Audio in any environment, and
significantly better among those who used headphones
– Consumers spend more time watching Dolby Audio content
– Consumers perceive a better picture quality when watching Dolby audio
content
*Blind test, between Dolby 5.1 Surround Sound, Dolby Audio, and Standard Audio content, for more than
400 respondents from Sept 30 to Nov 22, 2016. Research performed by PSB. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION © 2015 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 3

Dolby Audio Products Improve Users Experience
Users viewing content in an environment with moderate or a lot of background noise:
• 79% of users watching Dolby Audio content reported that the overall sound quality was
excellent
• Users watching Dolby Audio polled an average of 10 points higher than standard audio
users on the ability to hear details, the richness of sound and the loudness
• Users watching Dolby Audio with headphones polled even higher, in almost all key sound
metrics
CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION © 2015 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 5

Consumers Spent more time watching Dolby Content
• Users who experienced Dolby Audio watched the content 18% longer (30 seconds
longer) than those with standard audio
Consumers Perceive a Better Picture Quality when watching Dolby Audio
• The picture color perception was “much better” with 7 points improvement compared
to Standard Audio

Methodology
• From September 30 to November 22, PSB recruited participants, aged 16 to 29, from across the US who own an iPhone
or Android device and regularly stream and watch video content on their mobile device.
• Participants downloaded the mobile viewing app developed by the Intelygenz team.
• After each time they watched content on the mobile app, respondents were prompted to answer a short survey.
• In the analysis of the data, we removed data from respondents who said they had streaming issues during viewing and
those who said they did not enjoy the storyline to ensure we are focused on those who had an uninterrupted audio
experience and comparing experiences across similar samples.

The Dolby Brand Ranks Favorably in India
In a recent Dolby brand tracking study in India:
• 94% of those surveyed are aware of Dolby, and of those aware 87% view the brand
favorably and 90% would recommend
Of those aware
• 86% actively look for Dolby when shopping among ingredient brands
• 87% are more likely to purchase a smartphone that has Dolby sound or Audio
• 92% think that audio quality would improve if it included Dolby technology
Source: Dolby Global Brand Tracker, conducted by Penn Schoen Berland in fall 2015;
10 countries, 4,201 entertainment enthusiast consumers. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION © 2015 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 16
