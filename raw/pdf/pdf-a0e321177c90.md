Customer Experience #1: Ease of Use and Consistent Loudness
• “As a Fire TV stick customer, I am annoyed by inconsistent dialog and audio levels because I
have to constantly adjust the volume.”
• Solution:
– Dolby Digital Plus is an end-to-end solution that leverages bitstream metadata to provide consistent
loudness, clear dialog, and appropriate dynamic range across all living room endpoints (TV speakers,
home theater systems, headphones, etc)
– Netflix and AIV are converting 100% of their titles (including stereo) to Dolby Digital Plus by Fall
2016 to address loudness variability problems associated with non-Dolby content

Customer Experience #3: Improved HDMI Compatibility
• Problem: When I connect my FireTV stick to my TV, I get no surround sound on my HDMI
connected home theater system
– Over 90% of connected TVs support only Dolby Digital over HDMI ARC, even though the home
theater is Dolby Digital Plus capable. Therefore, the TV will be forced to send stereo.
• Solution:
– FireTV stick transcodes Dolby Digital Plus to Dolby Digital to deliver surround sound to connected
home theater system via HDMI ARC
