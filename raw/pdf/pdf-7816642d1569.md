Dolby AC-4 and Analytics
Objective: Develop a set of empirical measures suitable for tracking the progress of AC-4
deployments within services and across regions. In addition, develop criteria for identification
and measurement of the impact of Dolby AC-4 (inclusive of the range of experiences and
enhancements associated with AC-4 features) on specific metrics associated with content
service user behavior.
Output: A portfolio of measurement tools will allow Dolby generally and CEG specifically to:
1. Accurately report out to partners and the market-at-large on AC-4 availability among
device OEMs. This will allow operators to define the serviceable market for AC-4 audio
experiences (SAM) and serve as a critical input into calculations on the ROI for AC-4
workflow implementations.
2. Accurately report out to partners and the market at large on the utilization of AC-4 by
services and broadcasters.
3. Measure the impact of Dolby Audio on service launches, often as Dolby scales across
devices. (e.g. NPS relative to Atmos @ t+5 audience)
4. Architect quantifiable business arguments for AC4 adoption and implementation based
on the estimated impact of measured changes to user behavior on key operator internal
business metrics (e.g. Session Frequency, Session Length, NPS, etc).
5. Identify current or prototype features that merit increased attention in GTM and
product planning.
