Thank you for purchasing the DIY Filtration & Sanitation System plan!
Please note that this plan is for plunges that use SPA (flexible PVC) Tubing. If
you have yours set up with vinyl tubing, please see the other PDF included in your
download.

WHOLE HOUSE FILTER
There are several options when it comes to choosing the filter for your cold plunge build. I recommend
the GE Whole House Filter pictured below. It has a built in shutoff and bypass valve which makes
controlling the water flow easy. With it, you will be able to change the filter without shutting off your
pump or draining your plunge.
I also prefer a filter with a clear housing to see how much debris is in the filter at any given time. If those
factors aren’t important to your build, there are many other options available. Make sure the one you
choose is a 10” water filter and has threaded 3/4” inlet and outlet holes (standard in most). There are
many more available with a quick search on Amazon, Google, Home Depot, etc.

3/4” THREADED VENTURI INJECTOR
This system sanitizes water by injecting ozone into the water flow. It does this with a Venturi Injector. This
fitting has 3/4” male threads on both sides, a 1/4” tubing coupling on top and is clearly marked (alt View 2)
for which way the water should flow.
I use, recommend and sell the Ambohr Spa-124 Ozone Generator. The generator comes with the tubing,
venturi and check valve you need to complete this system. You can get one at DIYCOLDPLUNGE.COM

STEP 1
Use PTFE THREAD SEAL TAPE to attach two 3/4” SOCKET X FIP PVC COUPLINGS
to both ends of the 3/4” THREADED VENTURI INJECTOR.
In my own experience and seeing many questions online, this is the part of this
system that is most prone to small leaks. Make sure to check out videos on YouTube
for best practices and use plenty of tape for this step.
Do that, and you should be just fine!
