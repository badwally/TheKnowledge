• Massive negative gamma at current price:
• Dealers are short gamma at current price, meaning they must buy into
rallies and sell into drops, reinforcing volatility.
• A spike in volume at the upper call strike (2-3 strikes above):
• Buyers are coming in aggressively at this level, either closing their short
calls (if they’re part of the Iron Condor) or buying outright in anticipation of a
breakout.

• Gamma is at its highest intraday, making dealer hedging most aggressive.
Probable Impact on Price Action Into the Close
1. Dealer Forced Buyback → Gamma Squeeze:
• If the spike in call volume represents closing of short calls, dealers must
unwind their hedges (which were previously shorting futures to offset the calls).
• This means they must buy back SPX futures, creating strong upside
momentum.
2. Short Gamma at Current Price Magnifies the Move:
• Since dealers are already short gamma, their delta exposure shifts rapidly
as SPX rises.
• They are forced to buy more futures as price moves up, accelerating the
rally.
3. Potential Breakout Toward the Upper Call Strike:
• If SPX starts moving up toward the upper call strike, dealers must keep
buying to maintain neutrality.
• This can create a fast-moving rally into the close, often called a gamma
squeeze.
4. Potential for a Blow-Off Top & Retrace into the Close:
• If dealers finish covering, buying pressure may evaporate in the final
minutes.
• If momentum traders chase, SPX may overshoot before fading into
settlement.

• However, if large expiration hedging flows remain, pinning at the call strike
may occur instead.
Summary:
• Most Likely Outcome:
• A rapid upward move (“gamma squeeze”) toward the upper call strike.
• Momentum acceleration as dealer hedging fuels the rally.
• Potential for a blow-off top or pinning at the upper strike into the close.
• Key Trading Implications:
• If you’re already long, ride the move but be prepared to take profits near
the strike.
• If you’re flat, entering long at the breakout can work but requires tight risk
management.
• If price overshoots and stalls near the upper strike, watch for signs of
exhaustion or reversal in the final 1-2 minutes.
This is a classic dealer hedging-driven price action setup, where dealer
positioning and gamma dynamics create a self-reinforcing move into expiration.
