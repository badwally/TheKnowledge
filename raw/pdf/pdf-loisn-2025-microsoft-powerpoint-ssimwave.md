Sales Process - Phases
• Intro & Qualification phase (Sales qualified leads):
-MQL (Marketing qualified) are assigned passed to sales for validation
-Discovery call - focus on customer needs - Show capabilities
-Qualification: Key questions: Fit? Do we have solution? Is there a driver?
• Trial:
-Tight trial plan with clear goals and next steps - aligned with discovery phase
• Evaluation & Decision: (Solves the issue, fit, can we integrate and run, what is needed)
-Value based hands on dialogue and support of internal approval process
- If real value and business impact, decision should flow
• Procurement: Value based approach makes this step “simpler” and less price-driven
