Potential Speaker Bar Interfaces
• HDMI:
– Single IN proprietary connection labeled for PayTV Operator Set-top Box (Atmos PCM input) – other devices
connected will result in no Audio or Video.
– Single OUT for TV (for routing Video / Dolby Vision), supporting HDMI ARC (limited to Stereo PCM)
• SPDIF:
– Single IN for external device (e.g., Blu-Ray Player / Game Console, and limited to Stereo PCM)
• Left / Right Analog Audio
– Potential single pair (limited to Stereo PCM) – Speaker Bar OEMs may end up using SPDIF or L/R Audio
• Bluetooth:
– Available for mobile devices (limited to Stereo PCM)
• Other:
– None expected except relevant proprietary RF interface to Subwoofer
