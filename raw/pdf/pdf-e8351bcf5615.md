Session 3
Stress Physiology & Exercise/Pacing
• Inflammation ! brain inflammation
• Neuroinflammation ! stress physiology on body/brain, further
increasing neuroinflammation
• That’s a looped, self reinforcing mechanism!
• Vagal Nerve (CN-X) is negatively impacted by COVID. The
Vagus is a regulator of Fight/Flight responses, and it
decreases inflammation in organs/body
• When the Vagus is impacted, you have high stress responses
in the body and perpetual inflammation
• How do you break the cycle?

The Vagus Nerve
“The Wanderer”
• It is the longest cranial nerve
in our body, and it regulates all
organs, acting as a pacemaker
for the organs
• has stretch receptors in the
ear, lungs, diaphragm
• It has anti-inflammatory
effects on all organs
• The Vagus is exquisitely
sensitive to high inflammation
as caused by COVID
• There is evidence of Vagus
dysfunction after COVID

Many Long COVID symptoms
(tachycardia, lung, gastro, adrenal
symptoms) are mediated by CN X

TVNS
transcutaneous vagal nerve stimulation
• Low frequency current applied to one ear
• In 2020 the FDA approved Gamma core, a TVNS device
for treatment of acute COVID for pulmonary
exacerbations in hospitalized patients
• Several trials completed and more underway evaluating
TVNS for Long COVID
• Start with 5 minutes, maximum 20 minutes daily

Why Exercise Intolerance?
• Exercise temporarily increases inflammation and oxidative
stress
• In a healthy uninflamed body, this is a Hormetic or “good”
stress that builds muscle and cardiovascular strength
through activation of SIRT genes
• Overexercising can result in cell damage and excess
inflammation
• In an inflamed body, even mild-mod exercise can be the
straw that breaks the camels back and increases cell
damage

Exercise intolerance
& Pacing
• Exercise intolerance + fatigue are the 2
most common symptoms of Long COVID
• Adopt “pacing”- incorporate periods of rest
during your day after activity: ex: after
cooking a meal, rest for 20 minutes
• DO NOT push through the fatigue--you
will crash
• Give yourself permission to be where you
are in your healing journey
• “No pain, no gain” is a harmful mindset in
Long COVID - unlearn it
• Build strength slowly before
cardiovascular exercise

POTS protocol
