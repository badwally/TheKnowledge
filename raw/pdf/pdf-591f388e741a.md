For COMT Val/Val, MTHFR Compound Homozygosity (C677T T/T + A1298C A/A), and Early-Stage CLL
I. COMT Val/Val Therapeutic Strategy
COMT Val/Val genotype results in high COMT enzyme activity, rapidly clearing dopamine from the prefrontal cortex
(PFC). This can reduce executive function, working memory, and stress tolerance. The following strategies aim to
restore dopaminergic tone and improve cognitive performance while minimizing side effects.
1. Pharmacological Strategy:
- Dopaminergic Stimulants:
* Vyvanse (Lisdexamfetamine): Smooth release; boosts PFC dopamine.
- Risks: Anxiety, insomnia, appetite suppression.
* Methylphenidate: Effective but may wear off quickly in Val/Val.
- COMT Inhibitors:
* Tolcapone (central-acting): Improves PFC dopamine tone.
- Risks: Hepatotoxicity (monitor liver enzymes).
* Entacapone (peripheral): Less cognitive impact.
- L-Methylfolate (15mg/day): Essential due to compound homozygous MTHFR mutation.
* Supports BH4 cycle, critical for neurotransmitter synthesis.
* Risk: Overstimulation in rare cases.
- Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS):
* Increases local dopamine activity in left DLPFC.
* Considered for non-responders or medication-intolerant individuals.
2. Nootropic Strategy:
- L-Tyrosine or N-Acetyl-L-Tyrosine:
* Dopamine precursor; ideal under stress.
* Dose: 500-2000 mg AM on empty stomach.
- Rhodiola Rosea:
* Adaptogen; improves catecholamine efficiency under stress.
* Dose: 100-300 mg standardized extract.

- Uridine Monophosphate + CDP-Choline:
* Enhances dopamine receptor density and neuroplasticity.
* Doses: UMP 150-300 mg; CDP-Choline 250-500 mg.
- Sulbutiamine:
* Mild dopaminergic stimulant for task-specific drive.
* Dose: 200-400 mg; cycle use to avoid tolerance.
Cautions:
- Avoid combining multiple dopaminergics without supervision (e.g., Vyvanse + Sulbutiamine + Tyrosine).
- Monitor blood pressure, mood, and liver enzymes if using Tolcapone.
- Gradually titrate L-Methylfolate to avoid overstimulation.
This strategy should be overseen by a clinician familiar with dopaminergic modulation, particularly in individuals with
high COMT activity.

II. CLL-Focused Stress and Mitochondrial Resilience Protocol
This section provides a genetic and disease-informed resilience strategy for early-stage CLL, addressing high COMT
activity and poor stress regulation.
Objectives:
- Lower sympathetic tone (HPA axis)
- Control systemic inflammation (IL-6, TNF-alpha)
- Support mitochondrial function and immune tolerance
- Improve stress resilience and cognitive control
1. Peptides:
- SS-31: 3mg SC daily for 4-6 weeks; reduces ROS, improves ATP, lowers IL-6.
