Revenue f(Platforms, Players)
Player compatibility stands in the critical path to revenue for DMC
• Javascript players represent ~6BN viewing hour 2024 opportunity across HBO Max, Paramount +,
and PeacockTValone
• RokuPlayer unlocks a $3M opportunity with HBOMax in 2024
One-off SDK integrations present a significant threat to business viability as (Platform, Player)
combinations increase and we need to maintain backwards support
Business success will require deep collaboration between Engineering, Product Management,
and Business Strategy as we define an innovative path to an efficient solution
