The Dolby Experience Management Cloud (DxC) enables media companies of any size to create
and manage efficient delivery of personalized experiences at scale. DxC users receive guidance
about or automate the best potential action or most profitable action at any point in the
experience creation and delivery lifecycle. Using DxC, media companies can measure the
business value of sensational entertainment experiences across the entire delivery lifecycle
through the use of data analysis and machine learning. With DxC, media companies identify and
deliver the highest value experiences to a given user at the lowest possible aggregate cost.
DxC identifies the entire context of a entertainment experience, including technical content
attributes, network metadata, device capabilities, and user and environmental detail. By
providing this context on an individual or segment basis in near real-time across every data-
defined behavior and event along the lifecycle, DxC allows MediaCos to make decisions,
diagnose issues, and automate responses based on the next best action applied to a piece of
content, an audience segment, a target device, or a delivery network. The system analyzes
transactions in the context of quality of experience across time duration, content provisioning
decisions, and network and player events to quantify the holistic cost of a given experience.
To deliver this information, DxC either creates or aggregates the following categories of data
using a bi-directional data pipeline.
