Pat:
Edgar has passed on your message sent to the admin mailbox. I wish you had called me first to discuss this.
I am confused by your statement that "The information the Board has provided is confusing as it is presented as if Option 1 and Option 2 increase total monthly condo fees not just the Reserve Fund portion of the monthly
fee.” The memo before the first Town Hall spoke only of the capital portion and was at pains to be clear that this was what was being discussed. Did you have a chance to review the second memo that went out recently with
the detailed explanation of just that point, segregating the operating and capital portions of the monthly fee, stating assumptions about future operating costs, and then setting out for each year the monthly operating costs,
reserve contribution, and total condo fee?
In the town hall meeting we discussed only the capital contribution, and that is all that was addressed in the Reserve Fund study. However, some owners (including Ron, I think) asked for information about the impact on
monthly fees, so I did some arithmetic that you see in the table attached to the recent memo for discussion this week.
If by “The engineer is not recommending a Special Assessment" you imply that he is recommending against a special assessment, that is incorrect. It is the role of the engineer to certify that the chosen funding plan
complies with the requirements of the Act, and they confirm that any of the options presented will do that.
With respect to the ballot, as you know these decisions are not a “vote" - the Act requires a special assessment to be made with the consent of the required number of owners. Perhaps we should not have called it a ballot.
The form is clear, but if there is any doubt we can get confirmation from the $20K voters.
I have asked if the engineer can attend next week but have no response yet. I am not sure what you are suggesting the Board has done beyond its competence. We have simply presented the engineer’s report, which sets
out the capital requirements and the assumptions that they will make in certifying that the funding plan will meet those requirement.

Good morning.
My family has owned unit 402 since 1987.
I have provided legal advice to Summer Gardens since 1987.
I am concerned about this current matter arising from how to fund the Reserve Fund and am concerned it may be divisive as presented.
When a unit vote is required, it must be an informed vote, and the information presented to support the vote must be complete and clearly
explained.
The information the Board has provided is confusing as it is presented as if Option 1 and Option 2 increase total monthly condo fees not just the
Reserve Fund portion of the monthly fee.
In fact, the Reserve Fund Engineer is recommending an increase in monthly condo fees for only the Reserve Fund portion of the fee averaging
$200.00 per month for both Option 1 and Option 2 with Option 1 having a greater increase in year 1.

The Engineer is not recommending a Special Assessment.
Also, a vote for a $20,000.00 Special Assessment cannot be also counted in default as a vote for a $10,000.00 Special Assessment.
A Special Assessment requires owners owning 66 & 2/.3rds of the common elements to vote for the $20,000.00 Special Assessment or 66 &
2/3rds voting for the $10,000.00 Special Assessment – a vote cannot be counted twice.
In addition, I am requesting the Engineer who prepared the Reserve Fund Study be present at the May 14th meeting to answer questions about
the Reserve Fund Study and scope of work contemplated as the Board is not qualified to do so and should not be doing so.
I am requesting the Board send out my comments to the full Board and the unit owners and to confirm the vote will be conducted as I have set
out above to avoid the Board acting without the authority of the owners as required by the Condominium Act.
Thank you.
