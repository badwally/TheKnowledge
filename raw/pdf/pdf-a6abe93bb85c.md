THE BREAKER [UNICORN SETUP] | By Tyler on behalf of @Chroma_Trading
DEFINITION
In simple terms a breaker is a failed orderblock, the last closing candle that led
to liquidity being taken. Price should trade through this block without trouble,
giving its name as a ‘failed orderblock’
MEAN THRESHOLD
A critical aspect to be considered from the start is the mean [0.5 level], this has
significance as its average, a reaction will most likely be provided at this level.
SUPPORT RESISTANCE
Price will view an orderblock as a sense of support and resistance, however, it’s
important to note that a breaker is a variable inside a bigger picture, requiring
external factors to be fulfilled before it can be utilised

COUPLED
Similar to various other entry functions, breakers [br] are best when coupled with
an FVG, price should move and disregard a breaker seamlessly, thus creating a
failed orderblock, price should also be moving towards a draw of liquidity - a
reason for price to move in that direction.
A DOL can be derived from the H1 - D1 chart, such as smooth edges, FAIR VALUE [New
week opening gaps] and unmitigated imbalances
MEAN
Here is a look at the mean threshold of a breaker, and how it can help improve
entries.
Price is able to remain above the mean threshold, suggesting its strength - unable
to close inside the discount range.

TIME INFLUENCE
You would have probably missed this….take a look at a couple of examples above and
look at the time
Most happen following the 09:30 Opening NY time
[That is a unicorn (FVG inside of a breaker) one of the strongest algorithmic entry
positions ever, especially after you have taken out liquidity on opening price]
This dictates that following the 09:30 opening if price takes liquidity, the
probability for fulfilling an algorithmic entry function is far greater.
STOP LOSS PLACEMENT
Stop loss placement can vary, however, the best success will be placed underneath
the breaker, which price should find support.
Using inversion / FVG will also provide obvious areas for stop placement, below or
above depending on a short/long position.

EXAMPLES
Consistently after opening, price will move towards smooth edges using the unicorn
pattern, a breaker aligned with an FVG, one of the most reliable entry functions.
