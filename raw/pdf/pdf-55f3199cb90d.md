Cyclical Analysis: is pointing toward this move up being stretched by several
metrics, both in terms of directional probabilities, and in terms of expected moves. ES
is due for down cycles on both 1.3% and 5% scales. If it were to continue, 6110
would be the average (expected) move for continuation of ≥1.3% cycles, and ~6180
would be the expected move for continuation of ≥5% cycles. On the downside, if a
new ≥1.3% cycle were starting, the downside target would be ~5960 (confluence
with 20 DMA!), and the ≥5% target would be 5585. That is, the expected moves favor
downside by factors of ~1.6 and ~3.7. Continuation will push these even further in
favor of downside, until a new down cycle occurs.
