PELVIC RESTRUCTURING – “THE PELVIC CLOCK” EXERCISE
Goal of the Re-Patterning Exercise:
The goal of this exercise is to increase movement and blood flow to the pelvic musculature, as well as creating more
movement between the pelvis and spine articulation. Relief from back pain, and pelvic stiffness, are our main goals.
Proper Pelvic Exercise:
• Perform exercise lying on your back with your knees bend and feet flat on the ground.
• Picture a clock in front of your face that is displaying the time. Now picture that your pelvis is the clock and the
movements you are about to create are the hands of the clock, moving to different numbers.
• Slowly move your pelvis up to 12 o’clock (the location is at the top of your pelvis before it meets your spine).
• Slowly move your pelvis to 6 o’clock (by relaxing slowly to neutral, if you are tucking down to your tail bone, you
are going too far).
• Slowly let the weight of your legs pull you 2 inches to your right, bringing you to 3 o’clock (your legs should only
shift a couple of inches, and you should have your weight shifted to the right side of your pelvis).
• Slowly let the weight of your legs pull you 2 inches to your left, bringing you to 9 o’clock (your legs should only
shift a couple of inches, and you should have your weight shifted to the left side of your pelvis).
• After performing slow movements between 12 and 6 and 3 and 9 begin to slowly make your way through all the
numbers of the clock, spending time between numbers that seem to have tightness, limitation, or awkward
movement.
• NOTE: The movement created by the pelvic clock exercise is small precise movement. Exaggerated movement
does not create the specific heat and softening of connective tissues needed in the body.
What to Expect:
Your pelvis may feel sensitive or sore after performing the exercise; it may feel as though you have worked out. Slight
numbness and tingling may also be experienced as your bones and connective tissues are shifting, which slightly presses
on the nerves. The numbness should not continue after the first day or two. If numbness persists, please speak with me
about your experience. Some patients note soreness for a few days after performing this exercise for the first time.
Once again, you might experience slight discomfort and unease as your body is realigning into this new position that
works with gravity. This process only takes a week or two to work through. If you have any specific questions about
changes you are experiencing, please feel free to contact me. To improve your results in Alignment Therapy, it is
imperative that you eat healthy, drink plenty of water, and perform your retraining exercises. Once this area of your body
realigns, I will give you a new exercise for another segment of your body.
Frequency:
Perform this exercise daily, 5-10 minutes a day.
