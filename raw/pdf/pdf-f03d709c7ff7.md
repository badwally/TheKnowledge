The Corsican Highlights
Corsica is a stunning island to explore on two wheels, and this is
the favourite itinerary of many in the Marmot team. It offers all the
essential ingredients for a fabulous cycling holiday: mountains,
coastline, historic ports and towns, sunshine and warm scented
breezes. Add a splash of Corsican culture and this holiday really
does have it all! This flexible route road cycling holiday showcases
Corsica's highlights with far reaching sea views, peaceful roads,
beautiful climbs and of course excellent support from two Marmot
Highlights guides and support vans.
