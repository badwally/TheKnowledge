Vaughn:
Following up from my message this morning. For the benefit of the others, I a copy of that message.
I have reviewed the text of the report and have questions about almost every section relating to timing and
quantum. Has anyone at Sense done a line-by-line discussion with anybody at RE360 or Summer Gardens? It
seems that you are not aware of the projects we now have underway.
Most of the estimate amounts are beyond our knowledge, but there are a few that we do know about – like the
cost or decoration. We did extensive redecoration last year at a cost vastly below what is estimated for re-
decorations within a couple of years. And estimates of $7,500 for domestic hot water heaters do not seem
realistic.
You also include annual repair allowances, although we have, in recent years at least, been careful to include
anything short of major replacement as operating maintenance. Let’s ensure that the estimates match our
accounting practices.
I was not able to review the cash flows in Appendix C (and D & E, although I may be missing some pages?)
because we have the spreadsheets only in PDF in landscape. In that format they are too small to be read. In due
course, could you send them in Excel? In any case, I hope you will be able to explain them in the call on
Thursday.
However, it is clear that the very large expenditures proposed for the early years drive huge special
assessments. We would like to review both quantum and timing of some of these in the hope of flattening that
very jagged curve.
Each million dollars of special assessment represents $8,130 per unit for our 123 “equivalent units” (including a
few doubles – we have 118 “doors”). We can contemplate one substantial assessment, but not several of them.
Similarly, a $500,000 increase in annual contributions, as is proposed for 2026 in the fully-funded scenario at p.
C1, represents an increase of $339 per month per equivalent unit. There are then slight ups and downs until
2031, when it drops back to our present level.
The Board has not discussed this, but I think we would prefer a relatively level annual contribution, and can
look at the sort of assessment that would be necessary to make that work. The up-and-down contributions in
the Appendix pages (if I am reading them correctly) are not very desirable.
I look forward to our discussion. It is unfortunate that you will not be personally present. Is this intended to be
our “one call” as set out in your covering letter?
