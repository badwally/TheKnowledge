VDMS enhances the Quality of Experience for OTT services and applications
through integration of Dolby Audio
– The Verizon Digital Media Services platform now enables OTT content creators and providers to
deliver Dolby Audio content to the latest mobile and connected devices –
LOS ANGELES (April 24, 2017) – Verizon Digital Media Services and Dolby Laboratories announce
that encoding and playback of Dolby Audio will now be integrated into the Verizon Digital Media Services
platform. Dolby Audio will be available as an additional feature of the Uplynk Streaming Video service, augmenting
content with cinematic-like sound and providing viewers with the ultimate visual and audio experience on the latest
mobile and connected devices.
“The expectation for the highest quality audio and video experiences doesn’t go away when content is
streamed from the internet rather than delivered by a cable set top box or played from a Blu-ray player,” said Ted
Middleton, chief product officer for Verizon Digital Media Services. “Working with Dolby, we are ensuring that our

customers — leading media and entertainment content creators and distributors — now have the option to provide
their viewers with the ultimate home entertainment experience through integration of Dolby Audio support into our
digital media platform.”
With Dolby Audio integrated into the Uplynk Video Streaming service, consumers can now experience:
• The highest-quality immersive audio for Live, Episodic, and Movie content delivered to their living room,
portable, and mobile devices.
• Support for enhanced stereo, multichannel, and Dolby Atmos audio – the emerging standard for immersive
audio entertainment – across services and libraries using Uplynk Video Streaming for content management
and delivery.
• Compatibility with platforms including Roku4, PlayStaion, X-Box, Microsoft Windows 10 OS, and Apple
devices using iOS, tvOS and MacOS.
• Premium mobile listening, adding clear, realistic sound through headphones and speakers.
“Consumers around the world now expect the highest quality entertainment on all their devices,” said
Mathias Bendull, VP, Multi-Screen Services Audio, Dolby Laboratories. “We are pleased to work with Verizon
Digital Media Services, a proven leader in the digital media space, to integrate Dolby Audio into their end-to-end
OTT streaming video platform to support the next generation of immersive entertainment experiences that delight
these audience wherever they may go.”
Dolby Audio delivers immersive and personalized audio for streaming, on-demand, and downloaded
content across a variety of consumer electronics, including Smart TV’s, game consoles, home theaters, smartphones
and connected devices. Dolby Audio’s support for flexible content creation, management, and delivery workflows
allows digital entertainment services to efficiently and effectively exceed the expectations for audio quality in the
content their audiences love. Services using the Dolby Audio capabilities of Uplynk may see increases in audience
engagement, including session frequency, length, and social sharing.
The Uplynk Streaming Video service from Verizon Digital Media Services enables content publishers to
quickly launch and grow their OTT service by removing complexities from the entire OTT video ecosystem. It uses
one streamlined workflow to simplify encoding, storage and playback. No matter whether streaming linear, live, or
VOD content, the Uplynk Streaming Video service supports many advanced capabilities, including closed-
captioning, unique server side ad technologies, blackout management, cross-platform analytics and more.
