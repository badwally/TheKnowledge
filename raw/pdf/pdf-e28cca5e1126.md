Advanced Anti-Aging & Performance Optimization Protocol
This protocol integrates advanced peptide therapy, metabolic regulation, mitochondrial
enhancement, and cognitive support.
It is designed to support anti-aging, soft tissue recovery, lean muscle retention, fat loss,
neuroprotection, and skin/hair health.
Daily Protocol Schedule
Morning (Fast or with Breakfast):
- SS-31: 3-5 mg SubQ (fasted)
- Retatrutide: 1.5 mg SubQ (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- NAD+: 100-250 mg SubQ (daily or 3-5x/week)
- TMG: 500 mg with food
- Methylated B-Complex (Methyl-CPG): 1 cap with food
- Creatine: 5g
- BPC-157: 250 mcg SubQ
- L. reuteri Yogurt: 1/2 cup
Evening (Pre-Bed):
- Sermorelin: 250-300 mcg SubQ
- Ipamorelin: 100-150 mcg SubQ
- BPC-157: 250 mcg SubQ
Meal Plan (2000 kcal, 150g Protein)

Breakfast Shake (~640 kcal, 55g protein):
- 1 banana
- 1 serving Ka'Chava
- 2 scoops Thorne Whey Protein
- 1/2 cup oat milk
- 1/2 cup blueberries
- 1 tbsp almond butter
- 1 tbsp cacao
Lunch (~660 kcal, 50g protein):
- 6 oz grilled chicken thigh
- 1 cup roasted broccoli w/ olive oil
- 1/2 cup cooked white rice
- 3 egg whites
Dinner (~700 kcal, 45g protein):
- 6 oz wild salmon
- 1 cup asparagus or spinach

- 1 tsp ghee or olive oil
Optional:
- 1/2 cup L. reuteri yogurt (~100 kcal, 5g protein)
Total Daily Macros:
- ~2000 kcal, 150g protein, ~120g carbs, ~90g fat
