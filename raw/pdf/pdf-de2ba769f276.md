SUMMER GARDENS – 1470 SUMMER STREET, HALIFAX 7
RESERVE FUND STUDY
8. RESERVE FUND STATUS CERTIFICATE
CORPORATION: Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130
ADDRESS: 1470 Summer Street, Halifax NS
DATE: June 11, 2025
OPENING RESERVE FUND BALANCE
The Reserve Fund opening balance on June 30, 2024, was $1,991,973 as per the most recent
audited Financial Statements.
ANNUAL CONTRIBUTIONS RECOMENDATION
The Board has received and reviewed the Reserve Fund Study dated June 10, 2025 prepared by
Sense Engineering Ltd. and selected the funding scenario presented in Appendix C which would
apply to all owners as set out in the Corporation’s Schedule of Contributions.
REPAIR, MAINTENANCE AND REPLACEMENT RECOMENDATIONS
The expected major repair and replacement budgets are presented in Appendix A based on our
physical analysis of the current conditions, past performance history and the typical service lives for
similar components.
OPINION REGARDING ADEQUACY
Based on our physical and financial analysis, the reserve fund will be adequate for the major repair
and replacement of the Corporation’s common elements and assets provided the recommended
contributions are made and this study is to be updated at least every 5 years as required by the
Nova Scotia Condominium Act R.S., c. 85 (last revised in 2022 by NS Bill No. 106).
I hereby certify that that this Reserve Fund Study has been prepared in accordance with Clause
78A(C) of the Nova Scotia Condominium Regulations and that, to the best of my knowledge, the
information provided in this study is accurate.
2025-06-11
Vaughn Keeble, P.Eng.
Regional Manager
Sense Engineering Ltd.
