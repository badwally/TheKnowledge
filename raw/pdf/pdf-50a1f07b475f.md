Toxin Overload
• Environmental toxins are on the rise-The EPA and CDC have announced the universal presence of “forever
chemicals” in human studies.
• Since 1999, Perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) has been present in human urine universally (surfactants, resins, plastics)
• Forever chemicals have a ½ life of 30 or more years, meaning it would take 60 years to clear from your body
• The CDC has found phthalates and glyphosates (Roundup) in the urine
• Agricultural toxins-(Roundup, Monsanto) petrochemicals are accumulating in human urine
• Infection and its resolution also create increased toxin loads on the body
• Toxins create inflammation and inflammation creates more toxins, creating a vicious cycle
• To counter these insults!Decrease your exposure to toxins and increase intake of antioxidants

Countering toxins
• Eat organics foods (esp. “dirty dozen”)
• Increase anti-oxidant consumption in the diet

green tea)

elimination via the skin.

bisphenols, use glass bottles.

cookware (Gotham steel, Wear Ever brands)

ones are convenient (Pure Blue, Zero Water, Brondel,

Nutrient deficits
Vitamin B2- needed for brain and nerve function. It recycles
Glutathione and helps clear histamine from the body. Should be
in mid range to upper range of normal
Ferritin – a marker of iron stores. Should be =100 ng/dl,
otherwise take an iron supplement to correct. Low ferritin
contributes to a hypoxic state in the body and promotes classic
inflammation. Iron is needed to clear histamine from the body
Vitamin D- goal >40 ideally 50-60, especially in Autoimmune
illness. Vitamin D lowers CRP, ESR, and classic pathways of
inflammation. Low vitamin D levels have been associated with
higher mortality from COVID and higher degree of Long COVID
Vitamin A- if low or borderline, take 8-10,000 units daily for 1
month. Vitamin A is essential for lymphocyte function
Zinc/Copper- both should be approximately 100 ng/dl, and on a
0.8-1 or 1/1 ratio
If zinc is much lower than copper, this will cause a picture of
anxiety/depression since zinc is a cofactor for Dopamine
production in the brain. Zinc is also needed for immune function

Tapering the protocol
-How to do it-
The 2 month rule
1- Stay on the protocol until your health returns to
baseline pre-COVID state, then continue the full protocol
for 2 months more
2- If you have had Long COVID for over a year, this could
take 6 months (your immune system has long memory)
3- Once you are ready, cut all doses by ½: for example,
take 500 mg of vitamin C instead of 1000 mg. If you don’t
slide back into Long COVID, you are ready for the taper.
You can try to taper the doses by ½ every 2 months
4 -If you flare up after cutting the dose, you are not yet
ready for the taper. Go back to previous dosing, stay on it
for another 2 months, then try again
