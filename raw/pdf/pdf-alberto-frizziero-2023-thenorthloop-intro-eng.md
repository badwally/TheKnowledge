THE NORTH LOOP
The best tour to explore Northern Dolomites
5 Days 7500 mt+ 215 km Moderate +
4 mountain refugio overnights + 1 guesthouse overnights
70% gravel 10% trail 20% tarmac
1° Day: individual arrival and welcome meeting + beers in the afternoon in
Tires (BZ). Overnight in Tires
2° Day: 1° cycling stage = 37km - 1630mt+
3° Day: 2° cycling stage = 47km - 2220mt+
4° Day: 3° cycling stage = 33km - 1100mt+
5° Day: 4° cycling stage = 57km - 1500mt+ (thermae option)
5° Day: 4° cycling stage = 60km - 2080mt+ (rifugio option)
6° Day: 5° cycling stage = 44km - 1120mt+ (optional extra night in Tires)

The North Loop is an amazing journey thinked and drawned for gravel bikes
that links together all the most famous peaks and valleys of North Western
Dolomites, in Trentino Alto-Adige region. The North Loop starts and ends in
Tires, a town placed at the feet of the impressive Catinaccio mountain range.
Your cycling adventure will start exploring the countryside at the foot of Sciliar
massif, passing in the presence of one of the best preserved medieval castle of
the region, and following ancient paths you'll reach historical towns, tasting the
unique South Tyrol atmosphere and the best coffee with a stop at the local
roastery. A climb to the Alpe di Siusi will let you ride in a real gravel bike
paradise facing the impressive shapes of Sassopiatto & Sassolungo mountains.
A visit of the beautiful Val Gardena - a stop at the local brewery is a must too -
will transport you to the glamor of a valley that has always been dedicated to
tourism, both in summer and in winter. Heading away from the mondanity you
will cycle north, exploring the Val di Funes in the Puez-Odle Natural Park and
then the Val Badia. You will enjoy the most iconic landscape of all Dolomites
cycling through epic mountain peaks with dramatic shapes.
The finest gravel roads and the best 360° views awaits for you on the Pralongia
plateau, turning point of the tour from where you’ll start cycling south heading
toward the legendary Pordoi Pass, under the stern look of the colossal Sella
group: this is a climb that belongs to the world famous Sellaronda cycling loop,
a ride that must be done once in a lifetime. Following the Avisio stream you will
cycle the Val di Fassa with it's towns and from here you will have two riding
options: enter the beautiful Val del Vajolet in the Sciliar-Catinaccio Natural
Park and reach for the overnight a super scenic mountain refugio or stop for the
night in the valley, just few steps away from the local thermae. PS: do not forget
flip-flops!
On the last stage you will climb till the turquoise waters of Carezza lake that
reflects the vertical spires of Latemar mountain. You will enjoy life laying down
on soft meadows and with a daisy in your mouth you will admire the mighty
serrated profile of the Catinaccio mountain before to takle a last and long
downhill that will take you back to the starting point in Tires.
