What are we doing?
The CEG Cloud will enable media companies of any size to create and manage efficient at-scale delivery of
personalized experiences to consumers using a wide array of connected devices
Once established, the system we envision will have three key benefit pillars focused on the consumer
Best Experience in all Personalized to suit each
Highest Quality from each device
Environments individual
We will do this with a cloud infrastructure that will identify and respond to the entire context of an
entertainment experience, including technical content attributes, network metadata, device capabilities, and
user and environmental detail.
Eventually, this cloud infrastructure will allow MediaCos to make decisions, diagnose issues, and automate
responses based on the next best action applied to a piece of content, an audience segment, a target device,
or a delivery network
CONFIDENTIAL © 2019 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 3

Four Broad Requirements for Business Success
1. Deliver measurable cost efficiencies to OTT service providers that make use of our system - first for audio,
then for video - through a cloud-native infrastructure that integrates into existing workflows
2. Improve the quality of experience for consumers enjoying both Dolby and non-Dolby content on Living
Room and mobile devices
3. Integrate XCD-1 as the system for experience delivery and QoE measurement
4. Implement a data-driven architecture designed to store and measure the impacts of content processing,
delivery, and playback decisions

Product Components
CEG Cloud Client SDK
• Integrates the receiving end of the network coding system, so that content sent using network coding can be properly unpacked for
processing by the player.
• Sends information about the device – what it is, how it’s setup, etc. – to a decision-making engine.
• Collects and streams experience and network metric data to the xCD-1 cloud database.
Dolby Broker
• Instructs partner CMS on the right asset to deliver based on detail about the requesting device
Cloud Content and Device Database
• Logs available versions of content and establishes proprietary repository of device capabilities
XCD-1 Service Dashboard (front-end) and Cloud Network Player Event Database (back-end)
• Provides near real-time visualization on system performance and behavior
• Built on ongoing work in ATG; potentially extensible to support other business areas (e.g. Mobile Video Services)
CONFIDENTIAL © 2019 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 9

Product Components
CEG Cloud Encoding Engine
• Determines the optimal encoding profiles and configurations and sends instructions to the Hybrik encoding engine
Hybrik Encoding Engine
• Orchestrates the pre-processing, content encoding, application of DRM, and network coding of assets, and then passes the finished
packages to the Origin server
Pre-processor
• Image and audio processing components that create pre-processed versions of content
• E.g. converting Atmos audio to two channels or virtualized sound suitable for playback on two-channel TVs; preparing SDR video by
upscaling it to Dolby Vision and then scaling it down for a particular HDR TV profile.
XCD-1 Encoder
• Applies network coding to finished, packaged assets
CONFIDENTIAL © 2019 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 10

Serving the Broader CEG Business
One guiding principle of CEG Cloud will be to add significant standalone value to the Consumer Entertainment
business while also complementing the existing device business.
At the highest level, this means that we will coordinate closely with partners in other business areas to identify
potential friction arising from our product and business strategy
It also means that we will actively seek out ways in which our emerging platform services can deliver value to
these businesses and architect to support these opportunities
• For example, through improving the OEM testing experience or delivering a scalable cloud-database that
improves the function and reach of our application licensing business (DAA, DVA)
CONFIDENTIAL © 2019 DOLBY LABORATORIES, INC. 15

Evolving the Enterprise: Core Capabilities
The CEG Cloud reflects and ongoing transition for Dolby to more multi-sided monetization of products and
services built on our unrivalled expertise in the Science of Perception
Bringing v1 to market will require significant, accelerated work over the summer to:
1. Define prospective business model(s) and customer segments
2. Identify and coordinate potential opportunities or challenges with other business areas
3. Establish contracts that reflect industry standards while protecting Dolby IP
4. Create systems for low-friction on-boarding and support of FY21 customers
5. Define a mature data-policy that reflects our Guiding Principle of a data-driven product and business
strategy and potentially interacts with consumer data in different regulatory environments
