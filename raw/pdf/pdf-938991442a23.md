Dolby Audio and Apple TV – FAQ
Q: What did Apple announce at the September 09, 2015 event related to Apple
TV?
A: Apple announced a new Apple TV model with more powerful hardware, a new OS
(tvOS), new UI and developer tools to create 3rd-party apps for the App store
• Powerful CPU/GPU (64-bit A8 chip) and onboard storage for apps.
• No more S/PDIF, HDMI 1.4 only
• Siri voice interface and touch-screen remote.
• OS: tvOS (based on iOS)
• Development tools use Xcode and rely on familiar low-level frameworks
• Universal search (includes iTunes, Netflix, Hulu, HBO, Showtime at launch,
more later)
• Bluetooth remote
• CEC to control volume of TV, AVRs, etc.
Q: When will the product be launched?
A: Apple says “coming soon”. We expect the product launch to happen prior to the
2015 holiday season.
Q: Did Apple discuss Dolby Audio during the Apple TV launch?
A: No. Dolby Digital Plus support is listed in Apple TV specifications. Dolby Digital
Plus support is primarily relevant to Apple’s iTunes content, which will roll out
when the product starts shipping.
Q: Will iTunes deploy Dolby Digital Plus content? If so, when?
A: We are confident of Dolby Digital Plus content in iTunes. We anticipate the
announcement of Dolby Digital Plus content on iTunes at the product launch time.
Q: Where can I find specifications for the new Apple TV product?
A: Apple TV specifications can found at this link: http://www.apple.com/tv/specs
Q: Does this Apple TV that was just announced support Dolby Digital Plus?
A: Yes. The new Apple TV decodes Dolby Digital Plus (5.1/7.1) content.
Q: What about Dolby Digital Plus bitstream pass through on the Apple TV
device?
A: Dolby Digital Plus bitstream pass through is not supported on the device. Apple
sees value in decoding Dolby Digital Plus:
• To achieve dynamic range compression for its night mode feature
• To mix system sounds to enhance interactive end user experience

• To deliver a premium audio experience that works seamlessly with Siri
requires decoding thereby allowing mixing voice and address echo
cancellation
Q: What output interfaces are supported on the new Apple TV device?
A: The device only has HDMI 1.4 and USB-C interfaces. The S/PDIF output has been
removed.
Q: What Dolby Digital Plus capabilities will be supported on the Apple TV
device?
A: Dolby Digital Plus 7.1ch decoding is supported. However, dual decoding
capabilities to enable Director’s commentary and Audio Description use cases are
not supported at this point. We will stay engaged with Apple on capabilities beyond
DD+ decoding.
Q: Why does the current Apple http://www.apple.com/tv/compare/ link show
“Dolby Digital 7.1” as opposed to Dolby Digital Plus under the Audio support?
A: This is incorrect. We are working with Apple to get this fixed to accurately reflect
Dolby Digital Plus Audio support for the new Apple TV device.

Q: Does the Apple TV support Dolby Atmos content?
A: No. Not at product launch. We are in discussions with Apple to implement Dolby
Atmos capability on the Apple TV.
Q: Has Apple updated the HLS specification to include support for Dolby
Digital Plus?
A: Apple authors and owns the HLS specification with IETF and updates it twice
every year in April and October. We expect the HLS specification will be updated to
include support for Dolby Digital Plus in mid-October.
Q: What specification will Apple TV use to support streaming and/or playback
of Dolby Digital Plus content?
A: Apple tells us that one of the key profiles supported will be H.264 Video and DD+
7.1.
Q: Did Apple announce any new content services?
A: No new content services have been announced. However, Apple expects tvOS SDK
and App developer API will lead to new players in content services to emerge.
Q: Does Apple TV support Dolby Digital Plus content “casting” via AirPlay?
A: We don’t know if Dolby Digital Plus support for Apple AirPlay is enabled. We are
in the process of procuring and testing the new hardware.

Q: Because Apple TV emphasizes tvOS and intends to tap third party
developers to build apps for the device, how is Dolby Digital Plus supported in
Apple’s documentation?
A: We are currently reviewing the Apple TV tvOS SDK and the associated
documentation. Dolby will work with Apple to ensure Dolby Digital Plus support at
the Apple API developer is accurate and eliminate discrepancies if any.
Q: What is Dolby’s plan to support Apple TV app developers?
A: In addition to ensuring accuracy of Apple TV app developer APIs and associated
documentation with respect to Dolby Digital Plus, we will continue to provide all the
Online Delivery Dolby Digital Plus Content Creation and Playback SDKs that
contains relevant reference Dolby Online Delivery test streams and tools to ensure
seamless Dolby Audio support on the device.
Q: When will Apple TV support Dolby Atmos for games?
Dolby aims to bring Atmos gaming experience to the new Apple TV device and
currently working to sell the feature inside Apple.
