Background
This updated 3 year rollout plan for Dolby AC-4 is more ambitious compared to the rollout of Dolby Digital
Plus. This is because we want to deploy faster and want to reach broader adoption of devices and content
categories. We see this as essential to achieving the 2023 Goal of “AC-4 everywhere.”
It is clear that adoption happens in stages. AC-4 has some momentum in Broadcast specifications and TV
deployment but we have not yet launched any AC-4 services. While launching that first service, it is essential
that we build with scaling in mind to ensure easy enablement of more content and services onto more devices.
It is imperative that we hit the window of opportunity for the specifications, services, and accounts that have
been selected in the 3 year rollout plan. If we miss the Year 1 goals, then the 2023 goal will be impacted with
uncertain consequences and it should NOT be assumed that we can just shift all of our year-to-year goals to
one year later with minimal impact.

Dolby AC-4 2023 Goal

Dolby AC-4 2023 Goal
By 2023, we will ensure that for consumers across the globe, every audio entertainment experience is
enriched and improved through AC-4
Sub goals:
By 2023:
• Dolby AC-4 is the codec of choice for Premium services and Next Generation Audio experiences
• Dolby AC-4 is the codec used in mainstream services
• Dolby AC-4 is deployed in more device types that Dolby Digital Plus
• Dolby AC-4 is deployed globally
• Dolby AC-4 is widely used in Over the Air broadcast and streaming / download services
• Dolby AC-4 is used to deliver many content types e.g:
– Movies, Episodic TV, Live Sports, Music, VR, …

3 Year Rollout Plan

AC-4 Summary – 3 Year Plan
Services
• DTT/FTA: Broadcast Trials (Europe / USA) - started 2015, and continue through 2018/2019
– Live Atmos of large events otherwise 2.0 and 5.1
• DTT/FTA: first service launches (Europe / USA) late 2019 and 2020
• PayTV, STB specification wins for 2018 services leading to first service launches in 2019
• Mobile: Lead with Atmos / DoVi. Deliver Atmos using Immersive Stereo starting in 2018
• 2018 (OTT) and 2019 (Broadcast) is when first Atmos services to living room happens
Products
• In place today
– Stereo and 5.1 playback products
– Atmos TV
– Encoding of 2.0, 5.1, 5.1.2 content
• Path to Mobile is DAX 3.0 and DAS, adding AC-4 capabilities in 2018
• Reaching mid/low end UHD TVs by 2019
• In 2019, add AC-4 for Soundbars/AVRs, SDK targeting 2020 devices in market
• Immersive encoding capabilities will be added in steps
– Next is IMS of Atmos content, more advanced personalized & immersive, then deliver objects to home

MSFT / Apple
Both are followers
• Prioritization against many other possible media features in any particular release
• We need to build AC-4 demand before it will make the cut
Once they decide to adopt, quick deployment into large device base (6-9 months)
Key Challenges
• HE-AAC is Good Enough, what common, compelling need does AC-4 fill better than HE-AAC?
– Atmos
• Living room: Still a niche, prove breadth with DD+JOC
• Mobile: has an unproven value
– Dialogue Enhancement
• Prove cost of deploying DE to Laptop / Portable devices is offset by consumer behavior
• There is no new/unique content category that is available only in AC-4 (sound/silence is ultimate value step)
• Solution needed for ad-supported services (haven’t solved for DD+ either)
Plan
• Prove in market to justify platform need – don’t expect deployment ahead of need

Summary of FY18 Goals – 1 of 2
Business
