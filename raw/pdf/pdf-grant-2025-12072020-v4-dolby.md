Dolby Media Cloud
Product Discovery Meeting Proposal
For over fifty years, Dolby has advanced the science of sight and sound, providing
content creators and distributors with tools to get the most out of their medium. In
today’s streaming era, getting the most out of the medium challenges even the most
advanced media engineering organizations. Massive complexity in the number and
configuration of consumer devices, an explosion in media formats, and an internet
that doesn’t always behave in predicable ways make it hard for streaming companies
to consistently and efficiently deliver the sensational entertainment experiences their
users expect. We think these challenges constrain the potential of the direct-to-
consumer streaming industry and limit the lifetime value of consumer relationships.
To help, we’ve embedded Dolby’s deep expertise in content processing, delivery
networks, and playback device capabilities into a system designed to consistently
deliver the best possible experience for a given session in the most efficient way
possible. Our system - named “Dolby Media Cloud” in pre-release – augments
existing streaming infrastructures to help services achieve higher Quality of Service
(QoS) and improve Quality of Experience (QoE) on any device. Dolby Media Cloud
will help streaming companies make better content resourcing and delivery
decisions, and provide rich data tools to understand the impact of these decisions on
user-behavior.
As we approach the next phase of our development efforts, we want to understand
the challenges you face related to device diversity, network complexity, content
formats, and QoE so we can build what you need to exceed audience expectations.
We propose a 90-minute discovery meeting with a few key internal stakeholders
focused on customer experience, video operations, player applications, and delivery
and data platforms. We will show you what Dolby Media Cloud does today in pre-
release and how it will evolve in 2021-2022. Then we will dive into your thoughts on
how Dolby might apply our unique understanding of content, device capabilities,
network behavior, and sensory science to improve QoS and QoE.
Discovery Meeting Agenda (90 Minutes)
• Introductions to Dolby Cloud leadership and your team (10mins)
• Overview of the Discovery Process: What is Dolby doing here? (5 mins)
• Overview of the Dolby Media Cloud (non-technical) (15 mins)
• Exploring your needs related to experience delivery
• Defining QoE (15 mins)
• Delivery and QoE (painpoints, device challenges, CDN strategy, etc) (15 mins)
• Role of Data in Experience Design and Delivery (15 mins)
• Exploring Innovation Opportunities in Experience Delivery (10 mins)
• Wrap (5 mins)
Small part presentation, large part open discussion, this is a chance to learn about
Dolby’s strategy and introduce your priorities for a roadmap directed toward
intelligent content processing and delivery.
