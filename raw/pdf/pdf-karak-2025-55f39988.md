DOLBY AUDIO MOBILE STRATEGY: FEBRUARY 2017 MOBILE WORLD CONGRESS
KEY TALKING POINTS FOR INTERNAL ALIGNMENT; NOT FOR PUBLICATION OR DISTRIBUTION FINAL 2/25/17
The Dolby Vision LG G6 and Netflix announcement at MWC 2017 is a major proof point that Dolby is bringing its end-to-end
ecosystem development approach to the mobile marketplace.
At MWC and moving forward, we have the opportunity to make this proof point relevant well beyond Mobile Device OEMs, by explaining how
Dolby’s approach can make a big difference to this industry’s ability to deliver great experiences on mobile. Dolby will help “raise the
experience bar” by working all the way through the Mobile industry, to make content better through our formats, to facilitate distribution to
mobile via content providers, and to optimize that content when consumed on smartphones and tablets. Dolby has a proven record of success
driving this approach in other CE ecosystems (cinema and home theater).
A focus on “raising the experience bar” will be a big shift for this industry, which is dominated by recurring cycles of device feature competition
(feature wars). This shift will be particularly relevant to distribution partners and OEMs, enabling them to utilize Dolby products and services to
deliver the necessary year over year differentiation with continuous experience innovation across their entire portfolio.
How to leverage the above developments in your audio discussions at MWC
Dolby already is an ecosystem builder in mobile audio entertainment, working with key ecosystem players to improve audio entertainment
experiences through the device OEMs who build mobile devices, and content providers who deliver content to these devices.
Over the long run, Dolby’s strategy is to drive a “virtuous cycle” between mobile device OEMs and content providers. As more content
providers create more Dolby content for mobile, that can drive demand by mobile device OEMs for Dolby technologies, which in turn, can drive
more content.
We are at an early stage in the evolution of this virtuous cycle in the mobile audio ecosystem.
However, to kick start this cycle, Dolby is offering a range of audio products with specific value propositions for key mobile ecosystem players.
Summary messaging and FAQs for these products for use at MWC follows.

FAQS
1. How do the Dolby Atmos (DAX2) and Dolby Audio (DS2) products relate to DAS?
We believe that providing both product offerings creates greater value to the mobile ecosystem, our customers, and Dolby. These to products are intended
for different customers within the mobile ecosystem and they both enhance the entertainment experience on mobile.
2. Does offering DAS mean we stop enabling content providers to deliver Dolby formats to mobile devices that have DAX2/DS?
No. Dolby continues to work with content providers to create and grow their Dolby libraries and to deliver them to Dolby-enabled iOS and Android devices
(devices with our playback solution integrated into the device).
The DAS product offering is a way to encourage content providers to create Dolby content and enables them to deliver this content to their subscribers
immediately across their Android and iOS mobile applications.
3. Should we actively demo Mobile Device OEMs with the DAS product offering?
No. The DAS product offering is targeted to content providers who wish to enhance the audio experience through their applications. The Dolby Audio and
Dolby Atmos product offering is targeted to mobile device OEMs who wish to enhance the audio experience on their devivces. When engaging mobile device
OEMs the focus should be about coveying the Dolby Atmos story and selling the Dolby Atmos/Dolby Audio product offering.
During in MWC, the mobile device team (Luis Polanco, Boris Hua, and Nick Kang) along with sales team will be engaging mobile device OEMs to highlight the
value of the Dolby Atmos and Dolby Audio product offiering along with new feature recently introduced. If you have any question on our mobile device
product offering please contact Luis Polanco, Borish Hua, or Nick Kang.
4. Will we target content providers to deliver content to DAX/DS devices?
Yes. We will target content providers primarily with our DAS offering. However, we will target a select few content providers (that want to deliver an
enhanced experience for their customers) with the DAX/DS value proposition, with the goal of having them deliver content in a Dolby format to DAX/DS
devices.
We will lead with the Dolby Atmos experience value proposition which can be delivered using DD+. We will be testing whether the Dolby Atmos value
proposition will outweigh the compression inefficiency drawback of DD+ (in comparison to HE-AAC) for content providers that want to deliver an enhanced
experience to at least a segment of their customers. Our belief is that the bandwith concerns are less prevalent in the China market.

5. How do we discuss the status of Netflix in audio?
We are working with content providers to deliver enhanced experiences to consumers. We do not have any announcements to make at the present time, but
Netflix has announced that they will be delivering Doilby Vision content to the LG G6. We believe that this demonstrates that content providers are
interested in raising the experience bar and we expect to have more to share in the future”.
