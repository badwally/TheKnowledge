Proposal to Create a Building Management Review Committee

Purpose

Modernize and streamline building management so the facility reflects the standards expected of an $800 multi-million dollar property in a growing metropolitan Halifax area.

Current Challenges

After 40 years, Summer Gardens is showing its age and has not kept pace with residents’ owner’s needs. The building lacks a clear operating approach beyond basic security and addressing a leaking garage. New issues continue to emerge without warning, including neglected air scrubber maintenance, peeling door veneers that make hallways look worn, a main entrance front door unusable for a month, excessive air conditioning for much of the year, cold hallways in the winter and common amenities that remain unusable while committees (hot tub) spend months studying solutions.

Work appears to be assigned informally rather than through a defined strategic long-term plan. This creates a do-it-yourself model that often leaves residents with little more than a phone number at the front desk. Even key contacts, including the building manager and board members, are not easily reachable by email.

To protect property values, Summer Gardens needs a new strategic management approach. A Management Review Committee can lead that effort and help drive meaningful change.

Management Review Priorities

Define and document the roles of everyone involved in Summer Gardens management.

Clarify internal and external roles and responsibilities including Real Estate 360

Review contracts.

Establish reporting mechanisms with continuity.

Standardize documentation processes.

Create a consistent review process.

Fraud prevention financial checks and balances

Review and document current procedures, including work orders, purchase orders, and scheduling, to identify opportunities for automation.

Recommend processes and changes that improve early detection of building issues, such as:

Balcony slippage

Ceiling issues caused by building movement

Ceiling fans detaching from units

Other owner unit recurring or emerging maintenance concerns

Envelope failure ie. seepage/seal failure preventative maintenance sealant replacement

Roof top membrane maintenance

Window replacement, air leaks, drafts

Water consumption management

Infrastructure pipe failure prevention. Enzyme treatment vs. harsh chemicals.  Vertical  plumbing/stacks and cast iron lines

Electrical review, infrared thermography

Develop requirements for a measurable maintenance schedule that is proactive rather than reactive.

Review the Reserve Fund and assess how to incorporate clearer risk ranges and funding availability into planning through input from owners and other sources.

Provide the Board with a full report and recommendations for implementing a modern management plan and educating residents on the new processes.

If we continue to use the “wait and see what happens” approach, that is not a neutral position.  It’s a decision and the data will prove that it’s the wrong one.

Please consider this proposal for this committee to be formed and move forward.
