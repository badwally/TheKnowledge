Proposal to Create a Building Management Review Committee

Purpose

Modernize and streamline building management so the facility reflects the standards expected of an $800 million property in a growing metropolitan area.

Current Challenges

After 40 years, Summer Gardens is showing its age and has not kept pace with residents’ needs. The building lacks a clear operating approach beyond basic security and addressing a leaking garage. New issues continue to emerge without warning, including neglected air scrubber maintenance, peeling door veneers that make hallways look worn, excessive air conditioning for much of the year, and amenities that remain unusable while committees spend months studying solutions.

Work appears to be assigned informally rather than through a defined plan, creating a do-it-yourself model that often leaves residents with little more than a phone number at the front desk. Even key contacts, including the building manager and board members, are not easily reachable by email.

To protect property values, Summer Gardens needs a new management approach. A management review committee can lead that effort and help drive meaningful change.

Management Review Priorities

Define and document the roles of everyone involved in Summer Gardens management.

Clarify internal and external roles and responsibilities.

Review contracts.

Establish reporting mechanisms.

Standardize documentation processes.

Create a consistent review process.

Review and document current procedures, including work orders, purchase orders, and scheduling, to identify opportunities for automation.

Recommend processes and changes that improve early detection of building issues, such as:

Balcony slippage

Ceiling issues caused by building movement

Ceiling fans detaching from units

Other recurring or emerging maintenance concerns

Develop requirements for a measurable maintenance schedule that is proactive rather than reactive.

Review the Reserve Fund and assess how to incorporate clearer risk ranges and funding availability into planning.

Provide the Board with a full report and recommendations for implementing a modern management plan and educating residents on the new processes.
