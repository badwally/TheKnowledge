## Design notes

- Podcast vs Voice distinction: `VoiceConverter.detect()` returns `False` for any URL (explicit guard: `if source.startswith(("http://", "https://")):  return False`). `PodcastConverter.detect()` returns `False` for local paths. The two are non-overlapping in the registry — no ordering dependency.
- RSS chain (INT-2 relationship): `RSSPoller` (M69) handles text podcast feeds and saves transcript-less web sources. The podcast converter is the transcription path for audio enclosures. A future extension to `RSSPoller` could detect `<enclosure>` tags in feeds and hand off audio URLs to `PodcastConverter` — that extension is not in scope for INT-3.
- No `feedparser` dep: stays stdlib-only, consistent with INT-2 RSS poller approach.
