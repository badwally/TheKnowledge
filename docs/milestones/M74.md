### AGT-13 — Per-domain skill auto-generation (`wiki skill-emit`)

`wiki skill-emit <domain>` generates `.claude/skills/wiki-<domain>/SKILL.md` from:
- Policy inclusion/exclusion criteria (`policy.yaml`)
- Top entities and concepts (from MOC wikilinks)
- Open threads (recent synthesis page titles)

Output is deterministic and capped at 295 lines. Safe to regenerate at any time — idempotent.

### DOC-5 — log.md rotation

log.md had grown to 19k+ lines. `wiki rotate-log [--keep-days N]` (default 90) moves entries older than N days to `log.archive.YYYY-Q.md` quarterly files. Archive files are machine-generated, documented in their header, and safe to query via `grep`. Scheduled weekly (Sunday 03:00 UTC) via `.knowledge/schedule.yaml`.
