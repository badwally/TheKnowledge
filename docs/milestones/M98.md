## Deliverable

`wiki abandon-stale-drafts [--min-age-days N] [--dry-run]`: auto-abandons
orphaned draft wiki pages that are older than N days (default 30) and have
no inbound wikilink citations from any other wiki page.

Qualification criteria:
1. `draft: true` in frontmatter
2. `draft_started_at` older than `min_age_days` days
3. No other wiki page contains `[[<type>/<slug>]]` pointing at the draft

Qualifying drafts are passed to `finalize(path, abandon=True)`, which
deletes the page and removes any `wiki_pages:` backlinks from raw sources.

## Implementation

**`src/gateway/ops/abandon_stale_drafts.py`** (new)
- `abandon_stale_drafts(min_age_days=30, dry_run=False) -> OperationResult`
- Builds an inbound-citation index over all wiki pages in a single pass
- Skips drafts missing `draft_started_at` (can't determine age safely)
- Skips aliased links `[[target|display]]` — inbound-index regex handles both forms
- Delegates actual deletion to `finalize(path, abandon=True)` for consistent backlink cleanup and log entries
