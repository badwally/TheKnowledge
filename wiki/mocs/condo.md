# condo — Map of Content

## Overview

The condo domain covers condominium and HOA management with an emphasis on integrated long-horizon proactive planning — the operational, governance, maintenance, and regulatory layer that sits above the software and capital-planning tools in condo-software and condo-capital-infra. The corpus was assembled around three themes: (1) preventive and predictive maintenance standards and cadences for 100+ unit buildings, (2) the jurisdictional regulatory regime driving inspection, reserve, and governance obligations, and (3) the post-Surfside legislative wave and its implications for board liability and software data requirements.
