# ai-and-agents — Map of Content

## Overview

The ai-and-agents domain covers AI capabilities, agentic systems architecture, multi-agent coordination, AI governance and risk, and the economic and societal implications of large-scale AI deployment. The corpus spans five areas: (1) foundation models, scaling, and training economics; (2) agentic AI architecture patterns — orchestration, memory, tool use, self-evolution; (3) multi-agent systems and coordination; (4) AI governance, safety, and existential risk; and (5) economic impacts — labor displacement, R&D acceleration, and inequality.
