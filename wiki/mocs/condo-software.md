# condo-software — Map of Content

## Overview

The condo-software domain covers the software ecosystem for community association management (CAM) — the operational platforms, reserve and capital planning tooling, payments infrastructure, and regulatory compliance layer for condominium and HOA management across the US and Canada. The corpus was assembled to support market analysis and acquisition targeting for Longspan (a capital-infra platform for condo associations).

The domain divides into four areas: (1) the CAM platform landscape, (2) the reserve fund banking and compliance infrastructure, (3) the regulatory regime by jurisdiction, and (4) the AI/agentic evolution underway in the space.
