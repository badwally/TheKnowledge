## Rationale

A mixed cluster of philosophy, spirituality, ethics, and human-psychology material: Hindu and Buddhist devotional texts (Hanuman Chalisa, invocation shlokas, Goenka discourse summaries), Leary's Psychedelic Prayers After the Tao Teh Ching, Ari Kohen's two papers on secular grounds for human rights, Adam Waytz on the moral circle, Patrick Teahan on therapy/relationships, the CIA manual of trickery and deception, an academic information-theory lecture (treated here as a foundational frame), Bohemian Grove ethnography, leadership/performance frameworks (Thrive scenarios, Via Optionality), and an ethnography of sympathetic exchange.

Likely splits later into spirituality vs. moral philosophy vs. psychology sub-domains.
