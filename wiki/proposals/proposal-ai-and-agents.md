## Rationale

All members concern artificial intelligence — foundation models, transformer architectures, multi-agent and agentic systems, model efficiency, evaluation, and AI policy/strategic-economics. The cluster spans seminal architecture papers (Attention Is All You Need), 2025–2026 agentic-AI surveys, multi-agent infrastructure papers (Pancake, Hyperagents, Autogenesis), efficiency/measurement work, and industry/policy state-of-AI reports.

This is a coherent technical-research domain that should support strong NotebookLM corpora and synthesis pages on agent design, multi-agent serving, and AI strategy.
