## Rationale

This cluster bundles two closely related strands. First, car-audio installation: speaker install guides (Morel, HB80RL, EZ80N, 634M), Pioneer service manual, ES300 amplifier integration, Andy Wehmeyer engineering documents, and Lexus service-manual references. Second, streaming/video-tech industry work — Netflix REDEF essays, Dolby Media Cloud business plans (Andrew Grant authored), CEG cloud strategy, SSIMWAVE Project Pixel and business deck, Datazoom white paper, and a Crackle TV deal document. Both relate to media production/consumption tech and the user's Dolby/streaming professional history.

Likely splits later into two domains (consumer audio vs. streaming-industry), but volume justifies a single first-pass cluster today.
