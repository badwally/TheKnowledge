## Rationale

Most members here are 'FallbackPDF__*' or 'PowerPoint' filenames where the converter produced no useful summary or claims, plus admin/transactional items (Eventbrite tickets, recycling/garbage/compost cart posters, the TikTok v. Garland court doc, an Italy train ticket, a Vespa LX 150 manual, a Verizon all-in-one, a city council report, etc.). They are clustered together so the discovery output is complete; the right next step is to re-extract content for fallback PDFs and either reassign them to one of the real proposals or split a new theme out (e.g., civic/admin) once signals emerge.

This cluster should NOT be promoted as-is; it exists to keep accounting whole.
