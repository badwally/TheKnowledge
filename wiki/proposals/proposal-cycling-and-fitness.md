## Rationale

A tightly thematic cluster around endurance cycling: training-physiology guides, Corsica self-guided tour materials, shop receipts for bike work, SRAM hose-shortening and eTap AXS manuals (mechanic reference), strength-and-conditioning content for cyclists, and a related kettlebell programming calendar that fits the broader fitness practice.
