## Rationale

The DIY cold-plunge build dominates this cluster — chiller manuals (multiple DE-series and DE2593/DE2525A/DE2501/DE429D), filtration/sanitation update, plumbing/pump diagrams, components list, V2 framing update, and full plunge protocols. Adjacent home-DIY builds (a laser-cut wok-table diagram, Parallam Plus PSL structural-lumber spec, and a 3D-printing roadmap) cluster naturally with these as physical-build/maker references.
