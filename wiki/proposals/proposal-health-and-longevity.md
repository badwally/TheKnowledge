## Rationale

This cluster collects personal health and longevity material: medical test orders and chart notes, peptide and autophagy protocols, fasting guides, Long-COVID symptom tracking and clinical research (guanfacine trial), brain-care scoring, methylene blue pharmacology, mobility/stretching routines, and the Aetna benefits summary. Sources span both primary clinical literature and self-experimentation protocols.

Protocol-heavy and citation-able — likely to anchor a strong synthesis page on personal-longevity stack as well as a Long-COVID sub-thread.
