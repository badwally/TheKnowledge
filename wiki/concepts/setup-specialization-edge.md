# Single-Setup Specialization as Edge

## Summary

Trader-development thesis that consistent profitability comes from selecting one or two setups, learning their behavior intimately, and only trading those — the resulting familiarity is what allows the trader to recognize early whether a trade is going his way and therefore to take smaller losses [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].

## Key claims

- "There are countless different setups and patterns that you can trade, but I learned that if I stick to mine and only trade those, thats where I have my edge" [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Mechanism: over time the trader grows familiar with the setup, which allows him to know much more quickly if the trade is going his way, resulting in smaller losses [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Identified by BobaTrader as one of the conditions for becoming consistently profitable, alongside cutting losses without bagholding and allowing green trades to continue [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
