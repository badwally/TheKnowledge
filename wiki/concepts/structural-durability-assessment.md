# Structural durability assessment

## Summary

Structural durability assessment is the engineering activity of estimating how a structure's performance will hold up over time under environmental loading. Its data inputs — coupled heat and moisture fields, material parameters, loading histories — are uncertain, which is what motivates Bayesian treatments.

## Key claims

- To assess the durability of structures, heat and moisture transport must be analyzed [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- Reliable estimation of heat and moisture distribution in a given structure requires including all available information about loading conditions and material parameters, and that information must be accompanied by a corresponding evaluation of its credibility [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- Bayesian inference is one route to combining these heterogeneous information sources into an estimate that supports durability assessment [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
