# Mast cell stabilization protocol (Kuon Long COVID)

## Summary

Mast-cell-stabilization layer added on top of the Long COVID basic supplement protocol in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Osher group medical visit, summarized as "QBC + Perilla" — quercetin, bromelain, vitamin C, plus perilla — with named intolerance fallbacks (astragalus, rutin, luteolin) and a B2/iron correction step when labs warrant [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- Mast cell support is captured as the formula "QBC + Perilla": quercetin, bromelain, vitamin C, plus perilla [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Quercetin 1500 mg daily in divided doses [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Bromelain 1200–2400 units between meals [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Vitamin C 1000 mg daily in divided doses [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Perilla 150 mg twice daily, with suggested brands Pure Encapsulations or Metagenics [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Zinc picolinate 30 mg twice daily, melatonin 3–6 mg at night, vitamin D3 5000 IU/day to high-normal range, NAC 1500–2700 mg/day in 2–3 divided doses, omega-3 3 g/day, low-salt/high-potassium anti-inflammatory diet, and optional famotidine 40 mg BID round out the layered stack [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- For patients intolerant to either quercetin or bromelain, the protocol substitutes any three of: astragalus 1 g twice daily, rutin 50 mg daily, luteolin 50 mg daily [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- If B2 and ferritin labs are low, correct with vitamin B2 100–200 mg daily (Now, Doctor's Best brands) and iron bis-glycinate 25 mg twice daily (Solgar) until ferritin reaches ≥100 [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Benadryl or Vistaril at bedtime are suggested optional add-ons to support sleep [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
