# FX Forward Contract

## Summary

An outright currency transaction for forward delivery — any settlement date longer than spot, i.e., three business days or more after the deal date — with the forward exchange rate calculated relative to the spot rate via forward points that reflect the interest-rate differential between the two currencies and the time to delivery [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].

## Key claims

- A forward is an outright transaction in which two parties exchange currencies for delivery on a date longer than spot — three business days or more after the deal date [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- The forward rate is calculated relative to the spot rate, with adjustments expressed as "forward points" added to or subtracted from spot [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Forward points may represent a premium or a discount to spot, with the direction set by the relative interest rates of the two currencies [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- "Pay and earn points" describes the convention by which forward points are added to or subtracted from spot depending on the direction of the trade and the relative interest rates [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- "Odd dates" are forward value dates that fall between the standard market dates and require interpolation to price [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- A "positive carry trade" is a forward-rate-based trade that produces income from the interest-rate differential during the life of the trade [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Currency futures are an exchange-traded analog to forward contracts and are covered alongside forwards in the Lehman manual, including a section on trading Eurodollar futures and yield-curve spreads [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Funding decisions and trade ideas in forward markets are formed by analyzing yield curves [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
