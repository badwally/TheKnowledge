# Transcutaneous Vagal Nerve Stimulation (TVNS)

## Summary

A vagal-toning intervention in which low-frequency current is applied to one ear; per Dr. Carla Kuon's 2023 Long COVID Part 3 group medical visit, the FDA approved Gamma core (a TVNS device) in 2020 for treatment of acute COVID for pulmonary exacerbations in hospitalized patients, and several trials have been completed with more underway evaluating TVNS for Long COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- Defined as low-frequency current applied to one ear [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- In 2020 the FDA approved Gamma core, a TVNS device, for treatment of acute COVID for pulmonary exacerbations in hospitalized patients [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Several trials have been completed and more are underway evaluating TVNS for Long COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Recommended dosing in Dr. Kuon's slide: start with 5 minutes; maximum 20 minutes daily [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Listed alongside diaphragmatic breath, ear acupuncture, meditation, guided imagery, singing, and ice-pack-to-forehead as ways to "rehab the Vagus after illness" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
