# Ontario Declarant Definition

## Summary

The Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 defines a "declarant" as the freehold or leasehold owner of the land described in the description who registers the declaration and description under the Act, including successors and assignees but explicitly excluding good-faith fair-market-value purchasers of units (and their successors) [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]]. The Act further defines "declarant affiliate" by reference to the Business Corporations Act's tests for subsidiaries, holding bodies, and affiliates — establishing a corporate-relationship-based extension of declarant obligations to related entities, whether or not the Condominium Act otherwise applies to those entities [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].

## Key claims

- "Declarant" means a person who owns the freehold or leasehold estate in the land described in the description and who registers a declaration and description under the Act, and includes a successor or assignee of that person but does not include a purchaser in good faith of a unit who pays fair market value or a successor or assignee of the purchaser [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- The good-faith-purchaser carve-out distinguishes the declarant role from ordinary unit-owner status, ensuring that subsequent owners do not inherit declarant obligations through resale [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- "Declarant affiliate" extends the declarant concept to a body corporate (with or without share capital) that is related to a declarant by being deemed (a) a subsidiary under subsection 1(2) of the Business Corporations Act, (b) a holding body under subsection 1(3) of the Business Corporations Act, or (c) affiliated under subsection 1(4) of the Business Corporations Act [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- The declarant-affiliate test applies whether or not the Condominium Act applies to the related body corporate, meaning the affiliate-status determination is purely a corporate-relationship inquiry under the Business Corporations Act and is not gated by condominium-statute applicability [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- "Declaration" means a declaration registered under section 2 and all amendments to the declaration [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- "Description" means a description registered under section 2 and all amendments to the description [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- The Table of Contents enumerates declarant-facing provisions including Requirements for declaration, Requirements for description, Disclosure statement and guide, Disclosure of budget, Rescission of agreement, Material changes in disclosure statement, Accountability for budget statement, Status certificate, Duty to register declaration and description, Interim occupancy, Money held in trust, First board of directors, Turn-over meeting, and Performance audit [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
