# Market Efficiency Paradigm (ICT)

## Summary

In ICT (Inner Circle Trader) teaching, the "market efficiency paradigm" describes how "smart money" interprets price and influences speculative, uninformed money — and it is operationalized through the Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA), framed as an AI that delivers prices and renders markets efficient by design rather than free [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].

## Key claims

- The market efficiency paradigm describes how "smart money" interprets price and influences speculative, uninformed money [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- Two ideas — the four defining elements of a trade setup and the four institutional order-flow reference points — are presented as the prerequisite for understanding the market efficiency paradigm [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- The Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA), also known as the "algo," is taught as an AI that delivers prices, and the framework holds that it is "very efficient for markets to be made this way" [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- The paradigm explicitly rejects the view that markets are "free": markets are described as manipulated, especially in the Forex market [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
