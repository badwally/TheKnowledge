# Energy and Carbon Assessment (BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E1.0b)

## Summary

The Energy and Carbon Assessment, as defined by BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB question E1.0b, is a baseline mandatory practice requiring that the energy efficiency and carbon emissions of systems managed by the owner or landlord be assessed within the last five years [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]]. It is the diagnostic input that feeds the Energy Management Plan (E2.0) and identifies the Energy Conservation Measures (ECMs) that the plan then schedules and budgets [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key claims

- E1.0b is a baseline practice and minimum requirement for BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB certification, applicable to Enclosed Shopping Centre, Universal, Light Industrial, Open-Air Retail, and Multi-Unit Residential buildings [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The assessment must cover all building components managed by the owner or landlord, with a clear distinction between systems that are owned vs. managed vs. maintained by the owner, landlord, or tenant [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The assessment must include energy utility history of at least 12 months of continuous data, typically the previous 24-36 months, for each energy source [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The assessment must include a greenhouse gas inventory or carbon emission history of at least 12 months of continuous data, typically the previous 24-36 months, for each carbon source [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The assessment must identify low- and no-cost energy conservation and/or carbon reduction measures, with high-level costing, simple payback, and anticipated savings; if no savings measures are identified, the assessment must state why [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The required documentation is a Limited Scope Energy and Carbon Assessment Report [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The most effective energy reduction strategies focus efforts on the end uses with the highest consumption; building operations and management teams should determine the largest end uses and consider sub-metering significant loads such as tenant process loads or mechanical equipment [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Where the owner or landlord only manages a subset of building systems (e.g., exterior parking lighting or common-area energy), the energy and carbon data and efficiency assessment apply only to those systems; if recently upgraded systems offer no further conservation opportunity, the assessment must state that as well [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- For systems managed by the tenant, the applicant (representing the owner or landlord) must outline those systems, but no energy or carbon assessment is required for them [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The suggested lead is in-house, with third-party support if required [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E1.0b References list points to ASHRAE Level I Audit (ASHRAE 211-2018) and to NRCan's benchmarking guidance and Energy Star greenhouse-gas emissions reference [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- E1.0b is new in BOMA BEST 4.0 (no adapted BB3 question) [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
