# Agency Channel GTM

## Summary

Agency Channel GTM at Orita treats marketing agencies-of-record as the high-leverage node in the ecommerce go-to-market graph: each agency is connected to multiple brands, and earning trust at the agency level compounds across that book of business [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]]. The Marketing Automation Plan formalizes this informal motion as Priority 4 of the customer-acquisition strategy, calling out structured referral incentives, co-branded case studies, partner onboarding, and deal registration as the formalization surfaces [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].

## Key claims

- Agencies are the high-leverage node because each agency relationship implies multiple downstream brand customers [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
- The Klaviyo agency ecosystem is already a meaningful driver of Orita's 5x YoY growth, alongside event marketing [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Formalization plan covers: structured referral incentive (rev share or credit), co-branded case studies, partner onboarding kit, and deal registration with attribution [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Formalizing the partner/referral channel is framed as compounding an already-organic growth engine, not creating it from scratch [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
