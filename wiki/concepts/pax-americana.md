# Pax Americana

## Summary

"Pax Americana" is the post-WWII U.S.-led world order that Zoltan Pozsar's August 24, 2022 Credit Suisse dispatch argues was the necessary geopolitical precondition for globalization and the lowflation regime; the dispatch's central claim is that the TRICKs alliance is now "poking holes in the Pax," putting the entire economic architecture downstream of it at risk [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].

## Key claims

- Pozsar argues the causal chain is: "if Pax Americana enabled globalization and globalization underwrote lowflation, the TRICKs trying to poke holes in the Pax means that inflation is a big risk" [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- The Pax depends on U.S. control of multiple instruments simultaneously — technologies (chips), commodities (gas), production (zero-Covid), and straits (chokepoints like the Taiwan Strait, the Strait of Hormuz, or the Bosporus Strait) [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar argues that the U.S. faces three simultaneous fronts (Ukraine, Taiwan, the question of South Korea) and that — like in 1956 when oil was prioritized over Hungary — the U.S. may have to decide between NATO's footprint, chips, or oil [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- The 1956 historical anchor: "the units of wars are battles, and… sometimes, even great powers choose their battles" — Pozsar uses the U.S.'s 1956 choice between supporting the Hungarian Revolution and supporting Egypt against Israel, Britain, and France as the reference case for the choice he expects today [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar invokes the question of whether "the TRICKs checkmate the U.S. on the Eurasian chessboard" as the core open risk to the Pax — a question to which he says "I have no idea. Neither do you. And neither does the Pentagon" [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
