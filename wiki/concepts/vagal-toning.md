# Vagal Toning

## Summary

A class of practices that stimulate the vagus nerve's outflow branches (in the inner ear, voice box, lungs, diaphragm, and other organs) via their stretch receptors; recommended by Dr. Carla Kuon in her 2023 Long COVID Part 3 group medical visit as the way to "break the cycle" of perpetual COVID-driven inflammation by rehabilitating CN-X after illness [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- Many ways exist to rehab the Vagus after illness; the underlying mechanism is that vagal outflow branches in the inner ear, voice box, lungs, diaphragm, and many organs have stretch receptors that can be activated [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Specific techniques include diaphragmatic breath techniques (notably the 4-7-8 breath), ear acupuncture (especially inner ear) under the "NADA" protocol, meditation, mindfulness, guided imagery, singing (especially lower tones like "OM"), TVNS (transcutaneous vagal nerve stimulation), and placing an ice pack to the forehead (where receptors exist) [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- The clinical recommendation is to "try several, adopt 1-2 daily" — framed as "Pilates for your Vagus" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Specific daily prescriptions include adopting a 10-minute daily mindfulness practice and using free guided-imagery apps such as CALM, INSIGHT TIMER, and Headspace [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Additional recommended resources include Belleruth Naparsteck guided imagery ($10, healthjourneys.com), the free Stasis.life and Osher guided imagery (osher.ucsf.edu/guided-imagery-meditation-resources), the free 4-7-8 breath video at drweil.com, and the Osher MBSR 8-week stress reduction class ($395, based on John Kabat Zin) [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Traditional Chinese Medicine acupuncture — especially ear acupuncture — is named as a helpful adjunct [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
