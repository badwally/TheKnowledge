# Eurussia

## Summary

"Eurussia" is Zoltan Pozsar's coinage in his August 24, 2022 Credit Suisse dispatch for the EU-Russia commercial-and-financial union, in which the EU paid euros for cheap Russian gas and Russia recycled earnings into G7 claims; Pozsar frames Eurussia as the second of the two "heavenly match" unions whose breakdown — alongside Chimerica's — drives the move from "The Great Moderation" to "The Great Reflation" [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].

## Key claims

- Coined by Pozsar as a parallel to Niall Ferguson's "Chimerica": "Niall Ferguson called the first one 'Chimerica'. I will call the other one 'Eurussia'" [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- The structural deal: "the EU paid euros for cheap Russian gas" while Germany got rich "selling expensive stuff produced with cheap gas" — current accounts swelled for both [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar describes the planned Nord Stream 2 expansion as a "vow renewal" between Russia and Germany whose ceremony was "called off abruptly and turned into divorce" after events involving NATO, Ukraine, and the European balance of power [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar notes that the divorce is "messy" and that the two sides don't talk anymore, citing Olaf Scholz's statement that partnership with Putin's Russia is "inconceivable" [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- The collapse of Eurussia removes one of the three pillars of the lowflation world (cheap Russian natural gas fueling German industry and Europe more broadly), and Pozsar argues that Russian piped natural gas was replaced with U.S. LNG for continental Europe [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Eurussia's breakdown pushes Russia into the Chussia alliance with China — the disenfranchised spouse from Eurussia finding the disenfranchised spouse from Chimerica [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
