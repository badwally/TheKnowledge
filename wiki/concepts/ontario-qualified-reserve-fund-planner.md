# Qualified Reserve Fund Planner (Ontario)

## Summary

Under Section 94(6) of the Ontario Condominium Act, 1998, a Reserve Fund Study must be conducted by a person of a prescribed class who is independent of the board and corporation. The current regulations recognize eight distinct professional designations as qualified Reserve Fund Planners [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]]. The CAO Best Practices Guide further specifies that RFS providers must not be the condominium board, a condominium manager, or any party with a direct or indirect interest in a contract or transaction to which a director or officer of the corporation is a party, nor a spouse or child of such a director or officer [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].

## Key claims

- A Reserve Fund Planner must be independent and properly qualified, with no affiliation with the board or corporation contrary to the regulations made under the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The current regulations recognize eight qualified classes of Reserve Fund Planner [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- (a) Members of the Appraisal Institute of Canada holding the designation of Accredited Appraiser Canadian Institute (AACI) [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- (b) Persons who hold a certificate of practice within the meaning of the Architects Act, 1990 [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- (c) Members of the Ontario Association of Certified Engineering Technicians and Technologists who are registered as certified engineering technologists under the Ontario Association of Certified Engineering Technicians and Technologists Act, 1998 [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- (d) Members of the Real Estate Institute of Canada holding the designation of certified reserve planner (CRP) [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- (e) Persons who hold a certificate of authorization within the meaning of the Professional Engineers Act, 1990 [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- (f) Graduates of Ryerson University (now Toronto Metropolitan University) with a Bachelor of Technology (Architectural Science) — Building Science Option or Architecture Option [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- (g) Members of the Canadian Institute of Quantity Surveyors holding the designation of professional quantity surveyor (PQS) [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- (h) Members of the Association of Architectural Technologists of Ontario holding the designation of architectural technologist, architecte-technologue, or registered building technologist under the Association of Architectural Technologists of Ontario Act, 1996 [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- Reserve Fund Studies are explicitly not conducted by the condominium board or by a condominium manager; they must be performed by qualified professionals with expertise in conducting reserve fund studies [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- A director, officer, or condominium manager of the condominium corporation is disqualified from conducting an RFS [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- A person who directly or indirectly has an interest in (a) a contract or transaction to which a director or officer of the corporation is a party in a capacity other than as director or officer, or (b) a proposed such contract or transaction, is disqualified from conducting an RFS [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The spouse or child of a director or officer of the condominium corporation, or the child of the spouse of a director or officer, is disqualified from conducting an RFS [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
