# Internal range liquidity

## Summary

Internal range liquidity is the price action occurring inside an established trading range — fair value gaps, liquidity voids, orderblocks, and market maker models — that fills in or is traded against when the range is likely to remain intact [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]]; the canonical MMXM PDF defines internal liquidity as the area inside a swing high and swing low (any time frame), and frames a break of swing as the transition from internal to external liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- When the current range is likely to remain, fair value gaps and liquidity voids will fill in [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Orderblocks inside the range will be traded off of [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Market maker models will form inside trading ranges [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, internal liquidity refers to the area inside of a swing high and swing low — at any time frame — and is contrasted with external liquidity outside that range [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, when price breaks the swing high or low it has moved from inside the range to outside the range — i.e., from internal liquidity to external liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, in an MMBM the trader is "adding into internal liquidity FVG's and orderblocks" and selling for profit at external liquidity, operationalizing the internal-vs-external distinction as an entry/exit rule [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
