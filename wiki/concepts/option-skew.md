# Option skew

## Summary

Option skew is the cross-strike pattern of implied volatility for options on a given underlying; Ambrus Capital reads recent SPX skew as showing dramatically increased call demand since 2018 — direct evidence of a new investor base's appetite for speculative leverage [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].

## Key claims

- Implied volatility plotted across moneyness — from puts on the left to calls on the right — forms the volatility smile, and the call-side of that curve has lifted noticeably for SPX 1-month options between 2019 and 2022 [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
- Demand for SPX call options has increased dramatically since 2018, manifesting as rising call-side implied volatility relative to the rest of the curve [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
- Ambrus Capital interprets the increasing call skew as direct evidence that the new investor base has an appetite for speculative leverage, expressed directly in the volatility smile [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
