# Moving-average change-of-direction rule

## Summary

A market-timing rule that generates Buy/Sell signals from changes in the direction (sign of the slope) of a moving average computed over k lagged prices; in Zakamulin's anatomy it is shown to be one-to-one equivalent to a corresponding price-minus-moving-average rule [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].

## Key claims

- The change-of-direction rule is one of the four main moving-average market-timing rules considered in Zakamulin (2016), alongside the momentum rule, the price-minus-moving-average rule, and the double-crossover method [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
- Zakamulin establishes a one-to-one equivalence between a moving-average-change-of-direction rule and a corresponding price-minus-moving-average rule [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
- Like every other moving-average rule in the anatomy framework, its trading indicator can equivalently be interpreted as a weighted moving average of price changes [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
- It can also be presented as a weighted average of momentum rules computed using different averaging periods [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
