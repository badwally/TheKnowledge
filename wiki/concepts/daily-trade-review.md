# Daily Trade Review

## Summary

A discipline Ari Kiev's "Trading to Win" (1998) requires of every reader: reviewing each day's trades to see how the trader may have veered from their commitment and what dropped out of their trading process — a daily-cadence audit of behavior against intent that supports the framework's commitment-concentration-recovery-preparation cycle [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].

## Key claims

- Trading to Win obliges the trader to review each day's trades [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- The purpose of review is to see how the trader may have veered from their commitment and what dropped out of their trading process [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Daily review is part of the framework's broader proactive-trading cycle — commitment, concentration, recovery, and preparation for the next day [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
