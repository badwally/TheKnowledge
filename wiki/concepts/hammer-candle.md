# Hammer candle

## Summary

In the Power of 3 framework, a hammer candle is a candle that pokes below old lows in a discount and immediately returns into the prior range, signalling a failure to displace lower and a likely upward expansion in the following candle [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].

## Key claims

- A hammer candle prints when price looks below old lows in a discount and immediately comes back into the range [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The hammer formation suggests that price is failing to displace lower, and by default may seek a higher (premium) target next [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- In the January 2023 weekly example, week 1 prints a hammer that closes near the open after attacking the December lows; this hammer becomes the framework anchor that suggests week 2 should be bullish and expand up [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- A bullish weekly consolidation/pullback candle (e.g. week 3 of January 2023) looks similar to the original hammer formation — closing near the high after taking a discount of the prior week's range — and supports the same anticipation of further expansion in the following week [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
