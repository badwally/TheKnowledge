# LLM Social Implementation

## Summary

The rapid, broad-based deployment of large language models — exemplified by ChatGPT's November 2022 launch — that places advanced AI in the hands of users with no programming experience and is treated by Japanese policy makers as a generational technological shift on par with the internal combustion engine, the semiconductor, and the Internet [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key claims

- ChatGPT, launched in November 2022 by OpenAI, made LLM-based chat services usable by anyone for free or at low cost via smartphones, with no programming experience required [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Yutaka Matsuo (University of Tokyo) judged that LLM social implementation "is likely to affect nearly every white-collar job" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Kazuto Ataka (Keio University SFC) characterized the moment as "an era of unlimited content creation through the use of machines" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Hiroaki Kitano (Sony Computer Science Laboratories) described the technology as "an invention equivalent to the internal combustion engine, the semiconductor, and the Internet ... happening before our eyes at an explosive rate" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Use cases proliferating in daily life and business include text correction and summarization, idea suggestion, scientific writing, programming, and image generation [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Expected effects include rapid productivity improvements, dramatic changes to the way people work, and lower language barriers [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- LLMs introduce specific risks: errors and biases from training data, and "plausible lies" — incorrect information presented in a format that is difficult to detect [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- A second category of LLM-era risk is the spread of highly sophisticated fake information [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Adjacent risks growing alongside LLM adoption include sophisticated fake content via image generation and speech synthesis, AI-based privacy violations, cyber attacks, and military use [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
