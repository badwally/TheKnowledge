# Florida Condominium Unit-Owner Written Inquiry Response Requirement

## Summary

Florida Statute §718.112(2)(a)2 imposes a written-response duty on the board of administration of a residential condominium association whenever a unit owner files a written inquiry by certified mail. The board has three permissible response paths (substantive answer, notice that a legal opinion has been requested, or notice that advice has been requested from the division), with corresponding deadlines, and the penalty for failure to respond is loss of the association's ability to recover attorney fees and costs in subsequent litigation, administrative proceedings, or arbitration arising from the inquiry [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].

## Key claims

- When a unit owner of a residential condominium files a written inquiry by certified mail with the board of administration, the board shall respond in writing to the unit owner within 30 days after receipt of the inquiry [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The board's response shall either give a substantive response to the inquirer, notify the inquirer that a legal opinion has been requested, or notify the inquirer that advice has been requested from the division [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- If the board requests advice from the division, the board shall, within 10 days after its receipt of the advice, provide in writing a substantive response to the inquirer [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- If a legal opinion is requested, the board shall, within 60 days after the receipt of the inquiry, provide in writing a substantive response to the inquiry [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The failure to provide a substantive response as required precludes the board from recovering attorney fees and costs in any subsequent litigation, administrative proceeding, or arbitration arising out of the inquiry [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The association may, through its board of administration, adopt reasonable rules and regulations regarding the frequency and manner of responding to unit owner inquiries [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- One permissible rule is that the association is only obligated to respond to one written inquiry per unit in any given 30-day period; additional inquiries must be responded to in the subsequent 30-day period(s) [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
