# First Citizens HOA Loan Products (Quick-Term, Traditional Term, Revolving LOC)

## Summary

First Citizens Bank's Community Association Banking Services group offers three named HOA lending product types — quick-term loans, traditional term loans, and revolving lines of credit — designed to accommodate a range of project scopes and budget sizes for HOA capital improvements [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]]. Product parameters published in First Citizens's April 14, 2026 board-facing guide differentiate the three on term length, loan size limits, documentation requirements, turnaround time, and repayment structure [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].

## Key claims

- Community association banks typically provide HOAs with multiple loan options that can accommodate a range of projects and budget sizes; First Citizens offers three [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Quick-term loans carry up to 10-year terms [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Quick-term loans have no minimum loan amounts [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Quick-term loans offer 3- to 5-day turnaround times when limited documentation is required [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Traditional term loans carry 5- to 15-year terms [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Traditional term loans are offered at fixed rates [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Traditional term loans have no maximum loan limits [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Revolving lines of credit are useful for short-term cash flow shortfalls at the community [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Revolving lines of credit carry limits on total dollar amounts [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Revolving lines of credit require that balances be repaid annually [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
