# Data Monopsony

## Summary

Data monopsony refers to market conditions in which a small number of dominant firms are the primary buyers of user-generated data, giving them outsized bargaining power over the price and terms on which information is supplied [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].

## Key claims

- Vincent, Prewitt and Li (2025) characterize the AI-builder ↔ information-producer market as a power-concentration problem: without coordination on the producer side, AI accelerates capital concentration with the buyers [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- The paper extends the monopsony framing beyond personal data to information goods (journalism, research outputs, creative work) that AI systems consume as training and grounding material [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- Atomized negotiations between individual information producers and AI builders are characterized as a structural failure mode that collective bargaining is meant to correct [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
