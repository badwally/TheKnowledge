# Functional Threshold Power (FTP)

## Summary

Functional Threshold Power (FTP) is the cycling power metric classically estimated from a 20-minute time-trial and used as a proxy for the strength of the [[concepts/aerobic-energy-system|aerobic system]] [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].

## Key claims

- FTP is described as the metric the 20-minute time-trial test classically measures, used to characterise the aerobic system [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
- Even over a 20-minute effort, the [[concepts/glycolytic-system|glycolytic system]] still makes a notable contribution, so an FTP estimate is not a pure aerobic measurement in practice [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
- FTP is part of the [[entities/trainingpeaks|TrainingPeaks]]-derived load-management vocabulary alongside [[concepts/training-stress-score|TSS]], IF, and Normalized Power [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
