# Availability-Misweighing Tendency

## Summary

Munger's eighteenth psychological tendency: the brain's drift toward working with what is easily available to it — overweighting vivid, recent, or easily recalled information and underweighting what is less salient — summarized by the great algorithm: "An idea or a fact is not worth more merely because it is easily available to you" [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].

## Key claims

- Munger frames the tendency with a song lyric: "When I'm not near the girl I love, I love the girl I'm near" — the mind overweighs what is easily available and underweighs what requires effort to retrieve [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].
- The main antidote is the use of procedures and checklists, which are "almost always helpful" [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].
- A second antidote follows Darwin's method: especially emphasize factors that don't produce reams of easily available numbers, instead of drifting mostly or entirely into factors that do produce such numbers [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].
- A third antidote: "find and hire some skeptical, articulate people with far-reaching minds to act as advocates for notions that are opposite to the incumbent notions" [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].
- Extra-vivid evidence, being more memorable and thus more available in cognition, should often consciously be underweighted while less vivid evidence should be overweighted [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].
- The tendency can be used constructively: vivid images can improve memory (the technique that enabled classical Greek and Roman orators to give long, organized speeches without notes) or persuade someone to reach a correct conclusion [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].
- Munger argues the early psychology professoriate fell victim to this tendency by preferring easy-to-control laboratory data over harder-to-study real-world phenomena, creating man-with-a-hammer distortions in the discipline itself [[sources/pdf-munger-2021-revised-psychology-of]].
