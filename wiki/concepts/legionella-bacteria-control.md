# Legionella Bacteria Control Management (BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB W5.2)

## Summary

Legionella Bacteria Control Management is the BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB question W5.2 within the Water focus area's Water Hazards topic, treating Legionella as a distinct, regulated water-system risk separate from the broader Mould and Water Damage Management baseline practice (W5.0) [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]]. For multi-unit residential buildings, Legionella control is positioned as a point-scoring water-hazard practice that complements the mandatory mould/water-damage baseline [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key claims

- W5.2 — Legionella Bacteria Control Management appears in the BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB Water focus area under the Water Hazards topic [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- W5.2 is distinct from W5.1 — Water Damage Response and from the W5.0 — Mould and Water Damage Management baseline practice, indicating that BOMA BEST 4.0 separates Legionella as its own water-hazard control discipline [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Legionella Bacteria Control Management sits in the same Water focus area that contains the W1.0b — Water Assessment baseline and the W3.2 — Data Monitoring point-scoring topic, situating Legionella control within a broader water-system measurement and management framework [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
