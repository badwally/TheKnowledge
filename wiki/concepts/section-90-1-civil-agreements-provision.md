# Section 90.1 Civil Agreements Provision (Competition Act)

## Summary

Section 90.1 of Canada's Competition Act is the civil reviewable-matters provision for agreements between competitors that fall outside the section 45 criminal prohibition [[sources/web-2024-06-27-57a]] [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]]. Agreements are reviewable where they prevent or lessen, or are likely to prevent or lessen, competition substantially [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].

## Key claims

- Section 90.1 is the default track for competitor collaborations that are not section 45 conspiracies and not section 91 mergers — joint ventures, strategic alliances, and similar arrangements [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- Subsection 90.1(9) creates an exception for agreements in respect of which the Minister of Finance has issued a certification for reasons of financial policy [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- Agreements between federal financial institutions exempted under subsection 49(2) are not assessed under section 45 but may still be reviewable under section 90.1 if likely to substantially lessen competition [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- Bid consortium agreements made known to the person who called for the bids may still be assessed under section 90.1 if the consortium itself results in a substantial lessening or prevention of competition among bidders [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- Agreements between competitors that fall under section 90.1 may also be examined under section 79 (abuse of dominance) where the parties are jointly dominant and the agreement has or facilitates exclusionary, predatory, or disciplinary conduct [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
