# Reinforcement Learning

## Summary

A learning paradigm in which agents learn through trial and error, treated by the Acharya et al. Agentic AI survey as one of the core technical foundations enabling goal-directed behavior, contextual adaptation, and autonomous decision-making [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].

## Key claims

- Reinforcement learning is central to many agentic systems because it equips AI models with the ability to learn through trial and error [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- RL combines with goal-oriented architectures and adaptive control mechanisms to form the technical foundations of Agentic AI [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- RL agents focus narrowly on optimizing cumulative rewards in a specific task environment [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Language-model-based agents extend pure RL by interpreting complex natural-language inputs and interacting with humans seamlessly during task execution [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- RL underlies adaptive control mechanisms that allow agents to learn from prior experience and maintain optimal performance under changing environmental conditions [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
