# 200 Day EMA as Long-Term Trend Reference

## Summary

The 200 day exponential moving average is the long-term-trend reference at the bottom of Scott Redler's layered moving-average stack; it sits below the 50 day intermediate reference and is consulted last, with case studies in the cannabis ETF MJ and the gold ETF GLD used to illustrate how the 200 day acts as the final structural support or resistance after the shorter MAs have been broken [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].

## Key claims

- The 200 day moving average is labeled the long-term trend reference in both traditional technical analysis and in Redler's own EMA-based stack [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- Redler's full layered stack is 8 EMA (very short-term), 21 EMA (short-term), 50 EMA (intermediate), 200 EMA (long-term) — the 200 day is the final reference once shorter MAs have been lost [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- The cannabis ETF MJ is cited as a 200 day case study in the eBook [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- The gold ETF GLD is cited as a 200 day case study in the eBook [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- The Portfolio Approach trend check explicitly requires SPY to be trending above the 8/21/50/200 day moving averages collectively, so the 200 day participates in the regime-confirmation filter even though only the 8 & 21 day trigger the regime exit [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
