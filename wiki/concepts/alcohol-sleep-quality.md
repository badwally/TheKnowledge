# Alcohol's Effect on Sleep Quality

## Summary

Single-variable claim derived from TraderEdge's Alex's one-month Whoop Band self-experiment: alcohol is the most damaging input to sleep quality the experiment surfaced, interfering with the sleep cycle and preventing deeper stages of sleep — and is therefore his top single recommendation for any health-improvement intervention [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key claims

- After wearing a Whoop Band for one month and optimizing sleep, Alex's most important observation was that "alcohol is the worst thing in the world for sleep quality" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- His stated conclusion: "if you wanna do one thing in life to improve your health just get rid of alcohol. It's useless and doesn't serve you at all" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Mechanistic claim: alcohol "can interfere with your sleep cycle and prevent you from reaching the deeper stages of sleep" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The corresponding recommendation in his five-habit sleep prescription is the strongest of any single item — to "eliminate alcohol consumption entirely" rather than moderate it [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
