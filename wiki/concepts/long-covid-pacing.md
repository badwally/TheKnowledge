# Long COVID Pacing

## Summary

The clinical practice of incorporating periods of rest into daily activities to prevent post-exertional crashes; per Dr. Carla Kuon's 2023 Long COVID Part 3 group medical visit, exercise intolerance and fatigue are the two most common Long COVID symptoms, "pushing through" causes crashes, and "no pain, no gain" is a harmful mindset that patients must unlearn [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- Exercise intolerance and fatigue are the two most common symptoms of Long COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- The pacing prescription: incorporate periods of rest during the day after activity — for example, after cooking a meal, rest for 20 minutes [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- "DO NOT push through the fatigue — you will crash" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- "Give yourself permission to be where you are in your healing journey" is offered as the framing for adherence to pacing [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- "'No pain, no gain' is a harmful mindset in Long COVID — unlearn it" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Build strength slowly before cardiovascular exercise [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- For patients with POTS, consider the modified Dallas POTS protocol from Children's Hospital of Philadelphia (URL referenced in the slide: dysautonomiainternational.org/pdf/CHOP_Modified_Dallas_POTS_Exercise_Program.pdf) [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
