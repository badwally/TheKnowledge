# Assume you are wrong (Command 1)

## Summary

"Assume you are wrong" is the first of Tom Hougaard's three commands for re-coding trader behaviour: at trade entry, the 95% behaviour is to assume the trade will work; the 5% behaviour is to assume it will not, which changes how stops, sizing, and the emotional charge of being wrong are handled before the position is even open [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].

## Key claims

- Trade entry is broken into 95% behaviour and 5% behaviour — Hougaard treats it as two distinct decision pathways at the same moment [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The command is structured around two pathways (Pathway One and Pathway Two) and a contrast between 95%-behaviour entry and 5%-behaviour entry [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- It is the first of three commands offered as the path to "re-coding" the trader from the default, loss-averse 95% wiring into the 5% [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The command pairs with the broader thesis that overcoming the reptile brain begins with accepting pain — assuming wrongness is a way of pre-accepting the pain of being wrong before the trade begins [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
