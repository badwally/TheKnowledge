# Trading roadblocks

## Summary

Trading roadblocks is Tom Hougaard's enumeration of five internal obstacles that keep traders inside the losing 95% — Fear, Hope, Guilt, Confidence, and Market Logic — presented as a final section of the book after the three commands and intended to name the residual psychological exposures the recoded trader still has to manage [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].

## Key claims

- Hougaard names five roadblocks: (a) Fear, (b) Hope, (c) Guilt, (d) Confidence, and (e) Market Logic [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The roadblocks chapter follows "Hope is not a strategy" and "The £100,000 day" in the book's structure, positioning these obstacles as failure modes that survive even after the three commands have been internalized [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The roadblock list pairs with Hougaard's diagnosis that traders, as a hive-minded group, hope when they should fear (a losing position they want returned) and fear when they should hope (a winning position they want to lock in early) [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- Confidence is listed alongside Fear as a roadblock — implying that overconfidence after winning trades is treated as a failure mode of comparable weight to fear after losing ones, rather than as a virtue [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
