# HOA Exempt Function Income (§528)

## Summary

Exempt function income is the central definitional construct of the §528 federal-tax regime for US homeowners associations: it is the income an association elects to exclude from gross income when it files Form 1120-H, in exchange for a flat tax rate on its remaining non-exempt taxable income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. Exempt function income consists of membership dues, fees, or assessments from owners in their capacity as owners — not as customers of the association's services [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. Critically for reserve-fund accounting and HOA money-flow infrastructure, interest earned on amounts in a sinking fund is expressly NOT exempt function income, meaning that reserve-fund interest income is taxable at the 30%/32% flat rate even when the underlying principal contributions (dues and assessments) were exempt [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. Other excluded income categories include payments from nonmembers, payments from members for special use of facilities apart from generally-available use, payments for work done on nonassociation property, and members' payments for transportation [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].

## Key claims

- Exempt function income consists of membership dues, fees, or assessments from (a) owners of condominium housing units, (b) owners of real property in the case of a residential real estate management association, or (c) owners of timeshare rights to use or timeshare ownership interests in the case of a timeshare association [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Exempt function income must come from the members as owners, not as customers, of the association's services [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Assessments or fees for a common activity qualify as exempt function income; charges for providing services do NOT qualify [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Examples of exempt function income include assessments made to pay principal, interest, and real estate taxes on association property; to maintain association property; and to clear snow from public areas and remove trash [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Income that is NOT exempt function income includes: amounts that are not includible in the organization's gross income other than under §528 (e.g., tax-exempt interest) [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Payments from nonmembers are NOT exempt function income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Payments from members for special use of the organization's facilities, apart from the use generally available to all members, are NOT exempt function income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Interest on amounts in a sinking fund is NOT exempt function income — reserve-fund interest is therefore taxable at the §528 flat rate when the association elects Form 1120-H treatment [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Payments for work done on nonassociation property are NOT exempt function income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Members' payments for transportation are NOT exempt function income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Treas. Reg. §1.528-9 governs the further enumeration of exempt and non-exempt function income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
