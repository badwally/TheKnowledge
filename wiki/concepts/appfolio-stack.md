# AppFolio Stack™

## Summary

AppFolio Stack™ is AppFolio's branded third-party integration framework, marketed as the surface through which AppFolio customers can customize key workflows within the AppFolio Property Manager platform by plugging in partner proptech products [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]]. SmartProperty is one of the named Stack partners, integrating asset management, reserve-study data, and capital project management into the AppFolio environment [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].

## Key claims

- AppFolio Stack™ is positioned as giving customers the power to customize their key workflows to match how their business operates, all within AppFolio's easy-to-use platform [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- The Stack is framed as a partnership program with "top proptech solutions on the market" [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- Marketed customer outcomes: give the customer's business a competitive edge, enhance productivity, and improve the resident experience [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- SmartProperty is one of the named Stack partners, integrating reserve-study data, replacement costs, financial health reporting, and capital project management into AppFolio Property Manager [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- The Stack's value proposition for management-company customers includes new revenue streams (asset management and capital project management services without additional staff hiring) and customer retention through expanded service offerings [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
