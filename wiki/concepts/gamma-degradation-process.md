# Gamma degradation process

## Summary

The Gamma (degradation) process is a stochastic process used to model monotonic, cumulative deterioration of engineered systems — bridges, components, structures — over time. It underpins reliability and maintenance models across the risksystems domain.

## Key claims

- The Gamma process is used to model resistance degradation of reinforced concrete structures, including bridges, under non-stationary degradation and increasing load frequency and intensity [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- A Gamma process-based approach can be applied to evaluate the time-dependent reliability of reinforced concrete structures [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
