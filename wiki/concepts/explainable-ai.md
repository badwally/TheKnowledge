# Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)

## Summary

Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) is a class of techniques used to interpret and attribute the predictions of machine-learning models. In the infrastructure-deterioration setting, XAI is applied to assess how features (such as flood exposure characteristics) contribute to model predictions of pavement performance change.

## Key claims

- XAI techniques can be applied to assess the impact of explanatory factors on pavement performance predictions from machine-learning models, providing per-feature attributions rather than black-box outputs [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- Two specific XAI methods used in the Peng et al. (2025) flood-pavement study are SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) and Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations (LIME) [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
