# Multipolar World Trade Reorganization

## Summary

Credit Suisse's 2023 thesis that international commerce, far from normalizing post-COVID, has reorganized according to political alliances — marking the dawn of a multipolar world and creating a new economic reality of elevated inflation and monetary policy that prioritizes inflation stability over growth [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].

## Key claims

- International commerce has reorganized according to political alliances rather than normalizing [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- The reorganization marks the dawn of a multipolar world [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Geopolitics has made a comeback as a key driver of the global economy [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Driver event: the West-Russia confrontation over Ukraine, which triggered an energy crisis and soaring food prices [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Macro consequence: a new economic reality with more elevated inflation and a monetary policy regime prioritizing inflation stability over growth [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Rate consequence: interest rates are at their highest in years while economic growth is slowing [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
