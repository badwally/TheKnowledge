# 4-7-8 Breath

## Summary

A diaphragmatic breath technique recommended by Dr. Carla Kuon in her 2023 Long COVID Part 3 group medical visit as a free vagal-toning practice — "Pilates for your vagus" — with an instructional video hosted at drweil.com [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- Listed under "diaphragmatic breath techniques" as a vagal-toning intervention that activates stretch receptors in the diaphragm, one of the vagus nerve's outflow branches [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Framed as "Pilates for your Vagus" in Dr. Kuon's recommendations slide [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Available free of charge via a video at https://www.drweil.com/videos-features/videos/breathing-exercises-4-7-8-breath [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
