# Quebec Pre-Sale Buyer Certificate

## Summary

The Quebec pre-sale buyer certificate (sometimes referred to as the syndicate certificate or *certificat du syndicat*) is the disclosure document the Bill 16 implementing regulation (Decree 991-2025) requires a divided co-ownership syndicate to issue for every unit sale on or after August 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].

## Key claims

- The certificate is mandatory for all unit sales of divided co-ownership units starting August 14, 2025 — no grace period [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The certificate must include the actual balance of the contingency fund and the self-insurance fund [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The certificate must disclose planned or completed repair work [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The certificate must disclose insurance coverage and any current insurance claims or disputes (including ongoing litigation) [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The certificate must be signed by an authorized representative of the syndicate [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The certificate must be based on the most recent maintenance logbook and contingency fund study [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
