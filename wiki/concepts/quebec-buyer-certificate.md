# Quebec Pre-Sale Buyer Certificate

## Summary

The Quebec pre-sale buyer certificate is the disclosure document the Bill 16 implementing regulation requires a divided co-ownership (copropriété divise) syndicate to provide, upon request of the selling co-owner, before the sale of a unit, with effect from August 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]]. The certificate consolidates fund balances, contingency-fund-study recommendations, three years of common-charge history, three years of surpluses or deficits, the current operating budget, major work completed or planned, ongoing litigation, and recent declaration amendments — giving prospective buyers a single document from which to assess the building's financial and technical situation [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].

## Key claims

- The buyer certificate is mandatory under the Bill 16 implementing regulation, with the obligation effective August 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The certificate must be provided by the co-ownership syndicate upon request of the selling co-owner, before the sale of a unit [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: the balances of the contingency and self-insurance funds at the time the certificate is issued [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: the contingency fund study's recommendation on the amount that should be in the fund at the beginning of the current fiscal year [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: the common charges (condo fees) required and paid over the past three years [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: any surpluses or deficits recorded in the financial statements for the last three fiscal years [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: the current year's operating budget [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: major work completed or planned [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: ongoing litigation [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: recent amendments to the declaration of co-ownership (within the last three years) [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Stated purpose: provide increased transparency, allowing prospective buyers to adequately assess the building's financial and technical situation [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
