# Nova Scotia Trustee Deposit Pending Investment (§8, §9)

## Summary

§§8 and 9 of the Nova Scotia Trustee Act govern the holding of trust moneys pending investment: §8 permits trustees to deposit trust moneys for a reasonable time in a trust account at a chartered Canadian bank or other approved depository, and §9 imposes a per-trust ledger-account discipline requirement on the trustee — either in the trustee's own books or in the bank or depository ledger — for the particular trust estate for which the moneys are held [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].

## Key claims

- §8 permits a trustee, pending the investment of any trust moneys, to deposit the same during such time as is reasonable in the circumstances in a trust account in the name of the trustee in any chartered bank of Canada [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- §8 alternatively permits deposit in any approved trust company, loan corporation, or other like depository that has by order of the Governor in Council been approved as such depository [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- §9 requires the trustee, where trust moneys are deposited under §8, to maintain a ledger account in the trustee's books for the particular trust estate for which the trust moneys are held [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- §9 alternatively requires the trustee to require the account in the bank or other depository ledger to be opened and kept in the name of the trustee for the particular trust estate for which the moneys are held [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- The combined §8/§9 regime imposes both a depositing-institution requirement (chartered Canadian bank or Governor-in-Council-approved depository) and a per-trust ledger discipline requirement [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- The §14(6) notice exemption explicitly extends to the temporary deposit on interest of the whole or any part of a fund in any solvent chartered bank of Canada, indicating that deposit pending investment is treated by the statute as a categorically lower-risk holding than long-term investment [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
