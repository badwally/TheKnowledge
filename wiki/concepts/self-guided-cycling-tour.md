# Self-guided cycling tour

## Summary

A self-guided cycling tour is a multi-day cycling holiday format in which an operator pre-arranges hotels, luggage transfers, route notes, GPS tracks, and remote support, but riders complete each day's route on their own without an accompanying guide — a pattern exemplified by [[entities/europe-active|Europe Active]]'s [[entities/cycling-corsica-self-guided-luxury|Cycling Corsica Self-Guided Luxury]] route on [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].

## Key claims

- The operator's pre-trip work covers route design, hotel curation across tiers, logistics, and equipment availability — Europe Active books 3-, 4-, and 5-star hotels and offers hybrid, electric, aluminium, and carbon-road bike rental as part of the same package [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Daily logistics are operator-handled even though the riding is not: luggage is transferred between hotels in private vehicles, and riders carry only day-equipment on the bike [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Navigation is delegated to a smartphone app — Europe Active's tour explicitly requires a smartphone to access route notes, maps, and GPS tracks [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Operator support during the holiday is remote rather than co-located: Europe Active runs a daily 08.00–20.00 local hotline as the in-trip support channel, and trips proceed from groups of 2 persons upward [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- The format off-loads day-of mechanical and medical readiness onto the rider — Europe Active's brochure includes a detailed pre-trip bike-revision checklist, expected on-bike repair material (tubes, sealant, pump, multi-tool, derailleur cable, hanger, chain oil), required cycling kit, and a personal first-aid kit, on top of the operator-provided guide kit [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Self-guided trips can still offer route flexibility: Europe Active's Corsica trip provides 2–3 route options per day, letting riders adjust distance and climbing without leaving the supported framework [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
