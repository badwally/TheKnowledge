# Front Squat

## Summary

The front squat is a squat variant performed with the load held in the front-rack position — paired with [[concepts/clean-and-press|clean & press]] inside the 30-minute density block on the C&P/FS days of [[entities/strongfirst|StrongFirst]]'s [[entities/dry-fighting-weight|Dry Fighting Weight]] and the [[entities/dfw-kettleballs-remix|DFW Kettleballs Remix]] [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key claims

- In the DFW Kettleballs Remix, the front squat is performed with paired [[concepts/kettlebell|kettlebells]] at the lifter's 5RM press weight, in the same session as the clean & press [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Clean & press and front squat sets are alternated, separated by rest, for a total of 30 minutes per session [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- The Remix's calendar treats C&P and FS as a paired prescription — both lifts share the same [[concepts/ladder-set-protocol|set ladder]] progression each week [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
