# Low Histamine Diet

## Summary

Food-elimination protocol — labeled "Modification #2" in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Long COVID Group Medical Visit handout — indicated for patients with frequent rashes, elevated plasma histamine or tryptase levels, or mast cell activation; reduces or eliminates the principal dietary sources of histamine and histamine-liberators, with optional DAO enzyme supplementation at meals to further degrade histamine intake [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].

## Key claims

- Indicated for frequent rashes, an elevated plasma histamine/tryptase level, or mast cell activation [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Foods to reduce or avoid: alcohol — especially fermented alcohols (wine, champagne, beer); smoked and cured meats; seafood; pickled foods; fermented foods, including yogurt and cheese; leftovers; canned fish or meat; berries — especially strawberries; nightshades, including tomatoes and potatoes; preservatives; and vinegar [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Use of DAO enzymes with meals is recommended as an option to reduce histamine content [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Patient handout reference: histaminintoleranz.ch/downloads/SIGHI-Leaflet_HistamineEliminationDiet.pdf [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- The intake bloodwork panel for entry into the group medical visits includes Histamine and Tryptase, providing the laboratory triggers that can indicate a patient should adopt this modification [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
