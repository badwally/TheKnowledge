# Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA)

## Summary

The Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA) is ICT's hypothesized model of how interbank price formation is delivered to the market; in the Month 2 mentorship it appears as one of three short-term-perspective tools alongside correlation analysis and time & price theory [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]]; the canonical MMXM PDF expands IPDA as "interbank price delivery array — ICT lingo" and operationalizes it as a 20/40/60-day look-back system used to identify higher-timeframe liquidity and PD Arrays that anchor MMXM model setups [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]]; and Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023) presents IPDA as the algorithm responsible for moving price in all markets, whose two primary targets are liquidity above/below old highs and lows and areas of inefficient price action [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- Per Ali Khan, ICT teaches that price is not moved by buying and selling pressure and that the direction of the market is not determined by which side has larger inflows of cash — "This is false" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, ICT holds that the financial system uses computer programming to automate markets with an algorithm, which is "much more efficient and reliable" than leaving the system at the mercy of random buying and selling [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Ali Khan identifies Michael J. Huddleston as "ICT — The engineer of the algorithm" and names the algorithm IPDA (The Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, IPDA's job is to "manipulate price in order to engineer liquidity into the market place, and to offer fair value," which allows smart money to capitalize on the movement of price [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, there are two main targets that IPDA will seek: (1) liquidity above/below old highs and old lows, and (2) areas of inefficient price action [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, understanding when and to where IPDA will manipulate price "can give unmatched levels of precision and an understanding in price action that prior to ICT, was never possible for us retail traders" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- IPDA is named in ICT Month 2 as one of three short-term-perspective analytical tools, alongside correlation analysis and time & price theory [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- The short-term perspective complements the big-picture (macro, interest rates, intermarket, seasonal) and intermediate (top-down analysis, COT data, sentiment) layers used to frame any trade [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- IPDA is therefore positioned as the operational, lowest-timeframe filter applied after higher-timeframe bias and intermediate confirmation are already established [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- The MMXM PDF expands the acronym as "interbank price delivery array" (an alternative to "algorithm") and labels it explicitly as ICT lingo [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF prescribes IPDA look-back periods of 20, 40, and 60 days, and instructs traders to look at the highs and lows formed within each period [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the IPDA workflow is: identify HTF Points of Interest (Quarterly, Monthly, Weekly, Daily) using the 20/40/60-day look-back, annotate the PD Arrays inside each major swing high or low from the analysis, and check whether price is in a premium or discount of those arrays [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the IPDA layer feeds directly into the MMXM model setup — defining the draw on liquidity that the MMSM or MMBM expansion will target [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per Ali Khan, the offering of fair value is based on IPDA's "market efficiency paradigm logic," whereby smart money will sell to the buyers and buy back from the sellers (and vice versa) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, time is the first component IPDA will refer to; once the time window is in operation, IPDA will then look to seek key levels (PD Arrays) in price — "It is always TIME then PRICE" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, news drivers within killzones are often the catalyst for IPDA to reprice to key levels by initiating buy/sell programs [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, during high-impact news events such as CPI, FOMC, or NFP, manual intervention can take place — normal IPDA pricing parameters can be manually overridden, usually at the central bank level [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
