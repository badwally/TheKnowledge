# HTF-to-LTF Liquidity Cascade

## Summary

HTF-to-LTF liquidity cascade is the ICT-style workflow of identifying buyside and sellside liquidity on the highest timeframes first — where they carry the most significance — and then drilling down through progressively lower timeframes to refine entries; "Lesson 1: Price and Liquidity" prescribes this cascade explicitly and pairs it with adjacent swing-high/low formations to surface the most significant liquidity pools [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].

## Key claims

- BSL and SSL exist on all timeframes of a chart [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- The larger the timeframe on which a liquidity level forms, the more significant that level becomes [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- The prescribed workflow is to start with a Higher Time Frame (HTF) and work down into the hourly, 15-minute, 5-minute, and 3-to-1-minute timeframes [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- The lesson instructs traders to combine the HTF-to-LTF cascade with adjacent swing-high and swing-low areas to find significant areas of liquidity [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
