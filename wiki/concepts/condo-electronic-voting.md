# Condo Electronic Voting and Notices

## Summary

Florida regulates electronic participation in condominium association governance through two distinct Florida Administrative Code rules: 61B-23.00211 governing Electronic Voting (last amended 3/21/2016) and 61B-23.0029 governing Electronic Transmission of Notices (last amended 7/27/2006) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].

## Key claims

- Florida treats electronic voting and electronic notice as separate regulated activities under separate FAC rules (61B-23.00211 and 61B-23.0029, respectively) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Electronic transmission of notices was codified earlier (2006) than electronic voting (2016), indicating Florida formalized owner communication before formalizing owner voting [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Election monitoring has its own rule (61B-23.00215, last amended 8/7/2005), which interacts with the electronic-voting rule when an association uses both [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
