# Quebec Syndicate of Co-Owners — Federal Income Tax Treatment

## Summary

Under the *Civil Code of Québec*, divided co-ownership of immovables (the Quebec analogue to the common-law condominium) is administered by a syndicate of co-owners — a legal person responsible for protecting the rights of the co-owners and managing and maintaining the common portions of the condominium [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. CRA Interpretation Bulletin IT-304R2 establishes that for federal income tax purposes, this syndicate is considered to be a *corporation*, and the bulletin's comments — including the NPO exemption analysis under paragraph 149(1)(*l*), the T2 filing obligation under paragraph 150(1)(*a*), and the T1044 obligation for NPO-qualifying syndicates — apply equally to syndicates established under the *Civil Code of Québec* [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].

## Key claims

- The condominium system of ownership is recognized in the *Civil Code of Québec* via articles dealing with the 'divided co-ownership of immovables', analogous to condominium legislation in effect in other provinces and territories [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- These provisions provide for the direct ownership of condominium units (called 'fractions') [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Each fraction includes a portion of the land and building that is the property of a specific co-owner and is reserved for the sole use of that co-owner — called *les parties privatives* — and an undivided interest in the common areas, called *les parties communes* [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- When a declaration of co-ownership is published, a syndicate is established as a legal person responsible for protecting the rights of the co-owners and managing and maintaining the common portions of the condominium [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- For federal income tax purposes, a Quebec syndicate of co-owners is considered to be a corporation, per IT-304R2 ¶ 9 [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- All comments in IT-304R2 — including those addressing legal structure, NPO qualification under paragraph 149(1)(*l*), tax-filing obligations under paragraph 150(1)(*a*), and the income/non-income characterization of fee surplus versus reserve-fund interest — apply equally to condominiums established under the *Civil Code of Québec*, with allowance for the terminological differences [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
