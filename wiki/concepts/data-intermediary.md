# Data Intermediary

## Summary

A data intermediary is an organisation that sits between data producers (e.g. individuals, firms) and data users (queriers, researchers, commercial buyers), brokering access under defined governance terms [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]]. In the Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council formulation, an independent data steward functions as an intermediary in corporate and contractual data-sharing arrangements to create a trusted environment for organisations that may not otherwise have a relationship of trust with each other [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].

## Key claims

- Data trusts, data cooperatives and corporate/contractual mechanisms can all be implemented in ways that introduce an intermediary actor (trustee, cooperative board, or independent steward) whose role is to mediate between the parties contributing data and those using it [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- The Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council report frames the independent data steward in corporate/contractual settings as the means by which mutual trust between organisations can be manufactured without prior relationships [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
