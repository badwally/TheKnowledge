# Probabilistic life-cycle multi-objective optimization

## Summary

Probabilistic life-cycle multi-objective optimization is the decision-making methodology proposed for managing infrastructure (specifically asphalt pavement in the 2026 Xin et al. review) by jointly optimizing across competing objectives — safety, total expected cost, and environmental effects — under explicit probabilistic models of aleatory and epistemic uncertainty across life-cycle phases.

## Key claims

- Rational decisions regarding asphalt pavement systems should be made using an integrated probabilistic life-cycle multi-objective optimization methodology [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The methodology jointly considers factors such as safety level, total expected cost, and environmental effects [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Probabilistic optimization methodologies for asphalt pavement are organized to account for different pavement life-cycle phases [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Key technical inputs to the optimization include distress/condition indicators, reliability, risk, life-cycle cost, sustainability, and resilience metrics [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
