# New York Lunch Stop Run

## Summary

The New York Lunch Stop Run is ICT's framing of the 12:00–13:00 ET lunch hour as a period when the market typically clears stops via a retracement-style sweep, providing an actionable liquidity event; ICT prefers waiting until 13:30 ET for cleaner price action and uses a break of the lunch consolidation low as a long trigger when bullish [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].

## Key claims

- 12–1pm ET is New York Lunch, and the market will typically clear stops during the lunch hour [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- ICT prefers to see a stop run during the lunch hour rather than a flat consolidation [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- ICT likes to wait until 1:30 ET for cleaner afternoon price action [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- A trader should only consider trading during lunchtime when there's a sell-stop raid during a retracement (rather than a slow consolidation) — i.e., when the market is trading fast and furiously [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- When bullish in the afternoon after a consolidation move, the trader should wait until price is at a discount and inside a previous FVG or break of structure with FVG before entering long [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- When bullish, ICT instructs identifying the lunch consolidation lows and waiting for a break of them as the long trigger [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- The afternoon-session question to ask is: what is the daily range trying to do — expand higher, expand lower, reverse after the morning, or consolidate ahead of a news event [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
