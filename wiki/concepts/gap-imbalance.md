# Gap Imbalance (G.I.)

## Summary

A Gap Imbalance (G.I.) is an area of price that is void of any price delivery including wick movement — a "real price gap"; per Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023), G.I. represents the most extreme form of inefficiency, distinguished from a standard FVG (where candle bodies don't overlap but wicks do) and from a Volume Imbalance (where wicks overlap but bodies don't) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- A Gap Imbalance (G.I.) occurs when an area of price is void of any price delivery including wick movement — "This is a real price gap" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- G.I. is the most extreme form of inefficiency in the FVG taxonomy: no body overlap and no wick overlap [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- G.I. is distinguished from a standard FVG (where wicks provide some two-way delivery in the gap zone) and from a Volume Imbalance (where wicks overlap but bodies do not) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
