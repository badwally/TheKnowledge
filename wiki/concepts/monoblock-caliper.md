# Monoblock Caliper

## Summary

A Monoblock caliper is a one-piece [[concepts/hydraulic-disc-brake|hydraulic disc brake]] caliper variant — distinguished in SRAM HRD service literature because pad-retraction reset is a Monoblock-only step [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].

## Key claims

- SRAM's HRD manual flags pad-retraction reset as applying to Monoblock calipers only, implying the procedure does not apply to two-piece calipers [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Caliper identification therefore precedes pad-retraction reset to determine whether the step applies [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
