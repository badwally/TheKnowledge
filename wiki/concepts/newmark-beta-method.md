# Newmark-β Method

## Summary

The Newmark-β method is a family of implicit time-integration schemes widely used in structural dynamics to solve the equations of motion for systems subjected to dynamic loading such as earthquakes.

## Key claims

- The Newmark-β time-integration method can be embedded as a physics constraint inside the loss function of a physics-informed neural network used to solve nonlinear multi-degree-of-freedom structural identification problems [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
- In Yamaguchi (2025), governing equations, the Newmark-β method, and hysteresis information are jointly incorporated into the PINN loss function to enforce dynamical consistency with seismic response measurements [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
