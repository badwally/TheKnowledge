# Human-AI Centaur Configuration

## Summary

An umbrella term used by Evans, Bratton, and Agüera y Arcas (2026) for composite actors that are neither purely human nor purely machine — one human directing many AI agents, one AI serving many humans, or many humans and many AIs collaborating in shifting configurations — positioned as the dominant unit of analysis once the path to more powerful AI is recognized as composing richer hybrid social systems rather than building a single colossal oracle [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key claims

- The authors describe "the era of human-AI centaurs": composite actors that are neither purely human nor purely machine [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Centaur actors take many forms and inhabit many roles, with each individual potentially moving in and out of diverse ensembles many times a day [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The three canonical configurations they enumerate are: one human directing many AI agents; one AI serving many humans; and many humans and many AIs collaborating in shifting configurations [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- If intelligence is inherently social, then the path to more powerful AI runs not through building a single colossal oracle but through composing richer social systems, and these systems will be hybrid [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Humans remain in the loop: agent institutions are populated by both humans and AI agents in different roles and configurations — "not 'either/or', but 'both/and'" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
