# DICO $250,000 Deposit Insurance Limit (per member institution)

## Summary

The Deposit Insurance Corporation of Ontario (DICO) insures deposits at Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires up to a $250,000 ceiling per member institution. This limit is structurally analogous to the CDIC per-institution ceiling for chartered-bank deposits, but is set at 2.5× CDIC's $100,000 figure. The DICO ceiling shapes Ontario condominium reserve-fund placement strategy under Condominium Act §115(5): a board placing reserve funds in DICO-member credit unions can hold up to $250,000 per institution while remaining fully insured, materially reducing the number of counterparty relationships required to fully insure a multi-million-dollar reserve fund relative to CDIC-only placement [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].

## Key claims

- DICO insures most deposits in credit unions and caisses populaires in Ontario up to $250,000 per member institution [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The $250,000 DICO ceiling is 2.5× the $100,000 CDIC per-member-institution ceiling [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- §115(5) of the Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 qualifies as eligible securities those instruments "issued by an institution located in Ontario insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation or the Deposit Insurance Corporation of Ontario," placing DICO and CDIC on equal §115(5) footing irrespective of their different ceilings [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- A board placing $1,000,000 of reserve funds at a single DICO-member credit union would carry $750,000 of uninsured exposure; a board placing the same balance at four CDIC-member institutions ($250,000 each) would carry $600,000 of uninsured exposure at the per-institution CDIC ceiling, illustrating why brokerage-channel CDIC stacking remains material even where DICO is an alternative [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
