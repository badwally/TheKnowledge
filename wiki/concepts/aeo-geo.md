# AEO / GEO (Answer Engine and Generative Engine Optimization)

## Summary

AEO/GEO is Orita's framing for AI-era discoverability — optimizing for answer engines and generative engines as the successor to traditional SEO, with the goal of being the surfaced answer when a buyer asks an AI assistant about a product category [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]]. In Orita's Marketing Automation Plan, AEO/GEO is designated Priority 1 for customer acquisition acceleration, framed as the highest-leverage investment available to the company [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].

## Key claims

- AEO/GEO targets surfaced answers in tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity in addition to traditional search [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- 89% of B2B buyers now use generative AI for vendor research, per Forrester [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Gartner projects search engine volume to drop 25% in 2026 [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- 60% of AI-cited sources are not in Google's top 10 search results, motivating off-Google citation strategy [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Citation authority — earned mentions across partner blogs, publications, and communities — is the central asset AEO/GEO is designed to compound at Orita [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- AEO/GEO intelligence is also a candidate productized offering for Orita's ecommerce customers in the 18–36 month roadmap [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
