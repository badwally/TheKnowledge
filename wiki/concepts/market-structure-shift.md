# Market Structure Shift

## Summary

In ICT trading, a market structure shift (MSS) is the confirmation event used as the entry trigger after price has taken out liquidity in a Power of 3 manipulation, signaling that the distribution phase is beginning in the direction of interest [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]]; the canonical MMXM PDF treats the MSS as the explicit start signal of an MMSM (down) or MMBM (up), which fires only after an initial "failure to displace" liquidity grab and a subsequent displacement [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- The Power of 3 entry rule is to take entries once liquidity has been taken AND there is a market structure shift in the direction of interest for price distribution [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- The Power of 3 framework requires no forecasting, just confirmation of market structure and market structure shifts after price has taken out liquidity [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMBM's MSS UP is created by an energetic displacement up out of the accumulation phase, where sellers were absorbed by longs in liquidity at an HTF discount of an array [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMSM's MSS DOWN is created by displacement down out of an HTF premium of an array, where sellers distribute their positions and shorts position for a bigger move [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF teaches that the initial impulse move at the start of an MMXM is a "failure to displace" that grabs liquidity, and that the next displacement is what provides the MSS signal that an MMXM is developing [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, after the MSS the trader uses market structure (FVGs and order blocks) to guide entry and exit, joining the trend alongside smart money as it expands toward the higher-timeframe draw on liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
