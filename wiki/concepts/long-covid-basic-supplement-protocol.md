# Long COVID basic supplement protocol (Kuon)

## Summary

The "basic protocol" prescribed in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Osher Long COVID group medical visit (Session 2): a fixed daily stack of quercetin, vitamin C, zinc, melatonin, vitamin D3, NAC, turmeric (or resveratrol), omega-3, plus a low-salt/high-potassium anti-inflammatory diet and optional famotidine, derived from Yanuck et al. 2020 [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- Quercetin 1500 mg daily in divided doses (liposomal preferred) [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Vitamin C 1000 mg daily in divided doses [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Zinc picolinate 30 mg twice daily [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Melatonin 3–6 mg at night, with the optimal dose noted as unknown [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Vitamin D3 5000 IU per day, increased as needed to reach the upper normal range [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- NAC 1500–2700 mg daily in 2–3 divided doses [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Turmeric 1000–2000 mg in divided doses, with resveratrol 1000 mg daily (with fat/food) listed as an alternative [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Omega-3 (fish oil or vegan algae oil) 3 grams daily in divided doses [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Low-salt (2.3 g) and high-potassium anti-inflammatory diet, except for renal impairment [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Optional famotidine 40 mg BID, with dose reduction in renal impairment [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Cited literature backbone: Yanuck et al., Evidence to Support a Phased Immuno-physiologic Approach to COVID-19, 2020, Journal of Integrative Medicine vol 19 [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
