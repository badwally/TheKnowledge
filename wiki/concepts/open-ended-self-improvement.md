# Open-Ended Self-Improvement

## Summary

A regime of AI improvement in which a system continually invents new, increasingly capable artifacts without converging on a fixed objective — and, in the strongest form, also improves the mechanisms by which novelty and progress are produced; Zhang et al. (2026) argue this is what hyperagents enable beyond the coding-only Darwin Gödel Machine [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].

## Key claims

- Open-endedness refers to the ability of a system to continually invent new, interesting, and increasingly complex artifacts, extending its own frontier of discovery without a fixed objective or predefined end (Stanley et al., 2017; Hughes et al., 2024) [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Recent work has leveraged foundation models as proxies for human interestingness and as engines for generating and evaluating novel behaviors across diverse domains, with sustained exploration producing diverse and increasingly capable artifacts in domains ranging from game-playing agents to scientific discovery and robotic control [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- In human scientific and technological progress, advances often build on prior advances not only by producing better artifacts but also by improving the tools and processes that generate future discoveries, leading to accelerating innovation (Good, 1966; Kwa et al., 2025) [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Inspired by this pattern, Zhang et al. (2026) focus on open-ended systems that improve not only the artifacts they generate but also the mechanisms by which novelty and progress are produced (Clune, 2019; Jiang et al., 2023) [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- The Darwin Gödel Machine is presented as an existence proof that open-ended self-improvement is achievable in coding; DGM-Hyperagents extends this to in principle any computable task by removing the fixed meta-level mechanism [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Ablations on DGM-H show that without self-improvement or without open-ended exploration there is little to no progress, indicating that both ingredients are individually necessary [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
