# Monthly open

## Summary

The monthly open is the reference level used in the Power of 3 framework to determine monthly bias: in a bullish month price should seek a discount under the monthly open before expanding up to take buyside liquidity; in a bearish month, a premium over the open before expanding down [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].

## Key claims

- The same OHLC / OLHC logic applies to monthly candles, but using the monthly open as the level at which to frame bias [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The trader starts with the monthly candle and works down to the daily candle when applying PO3 to multi-timeframe analysis [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- Bullish monthly OLHC example (January 2023, NQ): the monthly price opens, drops into a discount of the previous month's range, takes out December's lows (sellside liquidity), then returns to the open and expands up above it [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- In that example, price nearly takes out the December highs but does not — leading to the anticipation that February will attack those highs relatively quickly [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- A bearish monthly OHLC scenario is offered as homework using December 2022: identify where the monthly open is and where price reaches into a premium before expanding down [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- For a monthly bias the trader looks back at least 180 days and applies the same OHLC / OLHC logic [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
