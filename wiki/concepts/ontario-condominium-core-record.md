# Ontario Condominium "Core Record"

## Summary

Under Ontario Regulation 48/01 (General) made under the Condominium Act, 1998, "core record" is a defined term enumerating a closed list of ten record categories that a condominium corporation must maintain and produce in response to a request for records. The core-record framework is the substantive backbone of Ontario's owner-disclosure regime — a request-for-records procedure introduced through amendments tied to the Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].

## Key claims

- "Core record" means any of ten enumerated record types: (1) the corporation's declaration, by-laws, and rules; (2) §113 or §154(5) agreements entered into by or on behalf of the corporation; (3) the budget for the corporation's current fiscal year and all amendments; (4) the most recent board-approved financial statements under §66(3) of the Act; (5) the most recent auditor's report presented to the audit committee or board under §67(6) of the Act; (6) the current plan proposed by the board under §94(8) of the Act for future funding of the reserve fund; (7) records the corporation is required to maintain under §46.1(3) or §83(3) of the Act; (8) all periodic information certificates sent or required to be sent to owners under §26.3 of the Act within the 12-month period before receiving a request for records or a requester's response; (9) minutes of owner-meetings or board-meetings held after §51 of Schedule 1 to the Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 came into force and within the same 12-month window; and (10) any other record specified as a core record by a corporation by-law [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
- The 12-month look-back windows attached to clauses 8 and 9 mean core records are not a static archive but a rolling disclosure obligation indexed to when the corporation receives a request for records or a requester's response [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
- Clause 10 grants by-laws expansion authority to designate additional categories as core records; the statutory list is a floor rather than a ceiling [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
- The current plan proposed by the board under §94(8) for future funding of the reserve fund — clause 6 of the core record list — is the artifact corresponding to the Notice of Future Funding of the Reserve Fund (NOFF) disclosure regime [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
- The core-record list co-locates governance documents (declaration, by-laws, rules), financial artifacts (budget, financial statements, auditor's report), reserve-fund planning (§94(8) future-funding plan), records mandates under §§46.1(3) and §83(3), recurring information certificates under §26.3, and recent meeting minutes — defining the surface area of mandatory owner disclosure in a single closed list [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
