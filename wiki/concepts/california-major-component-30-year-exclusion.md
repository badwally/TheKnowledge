# California Major-Component 30-Year Exclusion Option

## Summary

Under California Civil Code §5570(b)(2), a major component with an estimated remaining useful life of more than 30 years may be included in a reserve study as a capital asset or disregarded from the reserve calculation, provided that the decision is revealed in the reserve study report and reported in the Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]]. The option ties directly to the 30-year forward-looking adequacy test at §5570(a) item (3): components beyond that horizon may be carved out of the calculation that drives the year-by-year sufficiency answer [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].

## Key claims

- Statutory anchor: Civil Code §5570(b)(2) [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Threshold: applies only to components with an estimated remaining useful life of more than 30 years [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Two permitted treatments: (a) include the component in the reserve study as a capital asset; or (b) disregard the component from the reserve calculation entirely [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Disclosure condition: the choice between (a) and (b) is conditional on the decision being revealed in the reserve study report and reported in the Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Definitional dependency: "Major component" carries the meaning used in Civil Code §5550, per §5570(b)(2) [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- ERUL definition: "Estimated remaining useful life" means the time reasonably calculated to remain before a major component will require replacement, per §5570(b)(1) [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Calculation-horizon alignment: the 30-year exclusion threshold matches the 30-year forward-look adequacy test at §5570(a) item (3), aligning the calculation scope with the horizon of the binding sufficiency question [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
