# SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP)

## Summary

SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) is an explainable-AI technique that attributes a machine-learning model's prediction to its input features using Shapley-value-based contributions. It is one of the two XAI methods used in the Peng et al. (2025) study of flood-driven pavement deterioration.

## Key claims

- SHAP is an Explainable AI (XAI) technique applied to assess the impact of input features on model predictions [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- SHAP was used together with LIME in the Peng et al. (2025) study to assess the impact of flooding on pavement performance [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
