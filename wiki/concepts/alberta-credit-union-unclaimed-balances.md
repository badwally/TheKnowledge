# Alberta Credit Union Unclaimed Balances Regime

## Summary

The Alberta *Credit Union Act* establishes the statutory mechanism by which inactive deposit accounts at Alberta credit unions are transferred annually to the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation (CUDGC), which then manages those balances — accruing monthly interest at a rate prescribed by the Alberta Minister of Finance — until a beneficiary makes a validated claim [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]]. This is the Alberta-specific counterpart to the federal Bank of Canada unclaimed balances regime, and is operationally relevant to condominium reserve funds at AB credit unions only in the edge case of long-dormant accounts held by dissolved associations or accounts that fall off active board oversight during management transitions.

## Key claims

- The Alberta *Credit Union Act* establishes the requirements for managing inactive deposit accounts transferred to CUDGC as unclaimed balances from Alberta credit unions [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Deposit principal and interest balances are transferred from Alberta credit unions to CUDGC once a year [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- CUDGC accrues monthly interest on transferred balances at a rate prescribed by the Alberta Minister of Finance [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Beneficiaries are entitled to both principal and accrued interest at the time of payment [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- The reporting threshold for the depositor-facing claims process is $100: depositors who believe they have an inactive account with a balance exceeding $100 should contact their credit union to determine whether the balance has been transferred to CUDGC [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Each January, CUDGC publishes notice in the Edmonton Journal and the Calgary Herald that the list of unclaimed balances has been updated on its website [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- All claims are subject to validation by CUDGC [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Claims may be filed by email to claims@cudgc.ab.ca or by mail to Claims Department, Suite 2000, 10104 – 103 Avenue NW, Edmonton, AB T5J 0H8 [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
