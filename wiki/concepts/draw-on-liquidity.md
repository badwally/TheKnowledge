# Draw on Liquidity (DOL)

## Summary

"Draw on Liquidity" (DOL) is the ICT-derived idea that price gravitates toward reference points where a high percentage of orders are resting, and that the trader's job is to identify those pools as targets rather than to read patterns; daytradingrauf organizes the framework into three layers — what liquidity is, market-structure liquidity, and external-to-internal range liquidity [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]]. The MMXM PDF treats DOL as the central narrative anchor of the Market Maker Models — the higher-timeframe target that the entire MMSM/MMBM expansion is built around — and enumerates the specific reference classes that count as draws [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- The market "tends to refer to areas with liquidity to pair orders" — and these areas, not patterns, are what the trader should be looking at [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- The DOL framework explicitly opposes pattern reading: "We do not look at patterns we look at areas where orders may reside to target them" [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Liquidity is operationalized through two reference-point classes: buy stops above old highs and sell stops below lows [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- The DOL framework breaks into three identification layers: (1) what liquidity is and where it rests, (2) market-structure liquidity (bull/bear flags, failure swings, trend line liquidity), and (3) external-to-internal range liquidity, which daytradingrauf calls "my favorite way to identify draws on liquidity because it is the most forgiving concept" [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- The framework is described as derived from ICT (Inner Circle Trader) teachings, with the author pointing readers to an "ICT LIQUIDITY RUN VIDEO" on his YouTube channel for a video explanation [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- The MMXM PDF frames DOL as the answer to "which way does price want to deliver?" — the higher-timeframe thesis that determines whether an MMSM or MMBM is in play [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF enumerates the specific reference classes that count as a draw on liquidity: OLD highs or lows, liquidity pools, equal highs or lows, OLD PD Arrays, and inefficiencies (imbalances such as SIBI or BISI, FVGs, voids, gaps) [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF prescribes that DOL be identified using IPDA look back periods of 20, 40, and 60 days and that the PD Arrays inside each major swing high or low from this analysis be annotated [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMXM model is valid only when DOL is paired with a low-resistance liquidity run — i.e., structure supports the direction toward the DOL and does not oppose it [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
