# Liquidity Void

## Summary

In ICT (Inner Circle Trader) price-action analysis, a "Liquidity Void" is a gap in price action created by a sharp run in price; the largest candles at the end of the run are described as "efficiently traded," and the gaps left behind tend to be filled later [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].

## Key claims

- After a sharp run in price, the largest candles that form at the last are efficiently traded [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- Sudden runs in price will leave gaps in price action that tend to fill later on; this is described as a void of market liquidity, or a Liquidity Void [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- In the ICT vocabulary for new traders, sharp runs in price are explicitly tagged as Liquidity Voids when learning to read order flow [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
