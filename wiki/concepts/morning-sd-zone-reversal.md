# Morning 15-Min Supply/Demand Zone Reversal

## Summary

Discretionary intraday reversal setup popularized in BobaTrader's 2022 daytrading guide as his "bread & butter" trade — taken roughly 90% of the time — built around plotting supply/demand zones on a 15-minute timeframe from the prior 2–5 sessions, then waiting at the open for price to enter a zone before executing options on the 5-minute chart [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].

## Key claims

- Setup is the trade BobaTrader looks for every day at the open and takes about 90% of the time [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Workflow: check watchlist, plot zones on the 15-minute timeframe by looking back 2–5 days for the best areas where there might be an imbalance, then wait to see how the market reacts at open [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Execution timeframe is the 5-minute chart; the trade is entered when price comes into the zone — calls in a demand zone, puts in a supply zone [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- A worked $SPY example from February 1 illustrates entering 2/4 $448 Calls on what ended up being the wick when $SPY bounced heavily inside a 15-minute demand zone, with the bottom of the zone marked by a red support line as the exit trigger if it began to break under that level [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- If the stock never enters the zone, the trade is never taken — "If the stock had never entered my zone, I wouldve never gotten into the trade" [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Favorite execution window is 9:30–11 a.m. EST, characterized as the period of highest market volume when zones are most likely to react [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Entries are not expected to be perfect — BobaTrader does not catch the exact bottom of a reversal, but zones are kept tight enough that the trade goes green once price leaves the zone [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Profit-taking is calibrated to a reversal rather than a breakout: reversal moves do not produce big-volume directional thrusts, so the playbook is to begin scaling out at +15–20% or on an immediate strong candle in the direction of the trade [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
