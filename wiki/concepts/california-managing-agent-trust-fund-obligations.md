# California Managing-Agent Trust-Fund Obligations (Civil Code § 5380)

## Summary

California Civil Code § 5380 imposes a trust-fund deposit and recordkeeping regime on any managing agent of a common interest development who accepts or receives funds belonging to the association. The default rule under § 5380(a) is that such funds must be deposited into a managing-agent-maintained trust fund account at a California bank, savings association, or credit union that is federally insured or insured by a guaranty corporation subject to California Financial Code § 14858, unless the funds are placed into an escrow account or an account under the association's direct control [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]]. An alternative pathway under § 5380(b) allows the board, by written request, to direct deposit into an FDIC-/NCUA-/guaranty-insured account, subject to enumerated conditions including a fiduciary account titling, principal protection, segregation, disclosure, and prohibition on any direct or indirect managing-agent benefit from interest [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

## Key claims

- The trust-fund deposit obligation is triggered when a managing agent accepts or receives funds belonging to a California common interest development association and does not place those funds into an escrow account or an account under the control of the association [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The trust fund account must be maintained by the managing agent in a California bank, savings association, or credit union [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The depository institution must be defined as a financial institution under California Financial Code § 31041 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The depository must be insured by the federal government or be a guaranty corporation subject to California Financial Code § 14858 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Funds must be maintained in the trust fund account until disbursed in accordance with written instructions from the association entitled to the funds [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Under the § 5380(b) board-directed alternative, the account must be in the name of the managing agent as trustee for the association or in the name of the association [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Under § 5380(b)(2), funds may only be deposited in accounts that protect the principal; in no event may those funds be invested in stocks or high-risk investment options [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The managing agent must disclose to the board the nature of the account, how interest will be calculated and paid, whether service charges will be paid to the depository and by whom, and any notice requirements or penalties for withdrawal of funds from the account [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- No interest earned on funds in the account may inure directly or indirectly to the benefit of the managing agent or the managing agent's employees [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The managing agent must maintain a separate record of the receipt and disposition of all funds described in § 5380, including any interest earned on the funds [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The prevailing party in an action to enforce § 5380 is entitled to recover reasonable legal fees and court costs [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
