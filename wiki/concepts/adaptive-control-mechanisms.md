# Adaptive Control Mechanisms

## Summary

The class of techniques — including learning-based controllers and environmental-adaptation routines — that allow agentic systems to maintain optimal performance even under changing conditions, identified by Acharya et al. as one of three core technical foundations of Agentic AI [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].

## Key claims

- Adaptive control mechanisms provide environmental adaptation, allowing agents to maintain optimal performance even under changing conditions [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- These mechanisms enable agents to learn to adapt based on prior experiences, conferring greater resilience and flexibility [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Together with reinforcement learning and goal-oriented architectures, adaptive control mechanisms form the technical foundations enabling goal-directed behavior, contextual adaptation, and autonomous decision-making [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- The combined foundations equip Agentic AI with structural and functional capabilities to manage complex, evolving tasks independently — a contrast with traditional AI that relies on rigidly defined parameters and instructions [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
