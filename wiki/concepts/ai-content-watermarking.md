# AI Content Watermarking and Detection

## Summary

AI content watermarking is a class of techniques for embedding detectable signals into AI-generated content — particularly audio-visual output — so that consumers and downstream systems can identify content as machine-produced [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].

## Key claims

- The ISED Voluntary Code of Conduct on Advanced Generative AI Systems requires developers of publicly available advanced generative AI systems to "develop and implement a reliable and freely available method to detect content generated by the system, with a near-term focus on audio-visual content (e.g., watermarking)" [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
- The code identifies generation of realistic images, video, and voice impersonation as risks that can "enable deception at a scale that can damage important institutions, including democratic and criminal justice systems," framing detection methods as a transparency obligation [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
- The detection-method requirement applies only to developers of systems made widely available for public use, not to developers of general-purpose systems that are not publicly available, and not to managers in either case [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
