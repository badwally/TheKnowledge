# Retail trader failure rate

## Summary

Retail trader failure rate is the empirical observation, reported by Tom Hougaard from over a decade as an insider at two London CFD brokerages, that roughly 95% of retail trading-account holders lose money — a rate Hougaard argues is systemic to trader psychology rather than to any particular broker, market, or instrument [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].

## Key claims

- 95% of those who attempt professional trading fail — "probably even more," per Hougaard's experience watching markets at two large London brokerages [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The 5% who win make all their money from the 95% who lose [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- At one of the largest financial-market brokers in the City of London, only around 4% of clients managed to keep their money — let alone make enough to replace their day job — over a 10-year window covering rallies, nose-dives, and a gold bull market from $250 to almost $2000 [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- Hougaard frames this as comparable to Harvard's 2020 admit rate: 1,950 admits out of 43,500 applications, a 4.5% success rate [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The failure rate is not broker-specific — Hougaard reports the same patterns across two different London brokerages with entirely different client groups, and characterizes it as systemic, happening everywhere inside all brokerages [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The cause is not broker malpractice ("the broking industry is highly regulated") and not the broker running stops by trading against client positions [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- Statistically, in a roughly random market, 95% should not lose a 50:50 bet; Hougaard's explanation is that traders are not being beaten by the market — they are beating themselves [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- After observing 100,000+ people and ~100 million trades, Hougaard reports that, as a group, traders are predictable because everyone is doing the same thing — week after week, month after month, hoping when they should fear and fearing when they should hope [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
