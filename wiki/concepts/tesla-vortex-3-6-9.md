# Tesla Vortex 3-6-9 Trading Framework

## Summary

A trading mapping, popularized by Hopiplaka in "Demystifying ICT," in which Nikola Tesla's 3-6-9 vortex numbers organize ICT concepts: the number 3 governs Power of 3 (PO3) dealing-range price partitions, the number 6 governs Goldbach/IPDA price markers, and the number 9 governs the 20-40-60 look back time period — yielding combined time-and-price coordinates [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].

## Key claims

- Hopiplaka opens the book with Tesla's quote — "If only you would know the magnificence of the 3, 6, and 9, you would have a key to the universe" — and uses the 3-6-9 vortex as the book's organizing principle [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- For PRICE: 3 maps to PO3 dealing ranges, and 6 maps to Goldbach/IPDA levels [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- For TIME: 9 maps to the look back period (the 20-40-60 day system) [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Combining 3-6-9 yields both price and time coordinates for a trade — Hopiplaka frames this as the framework's central deliverable [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Hopiplaka also uses "a tweaked version" of the Tesla Vortex for longer-term views (Weekly, Daily) where ICT says banks trade, integrating the resulting algorithm into a "Putting it all together" workflow with the Market Maker Sell Model and Optimal Trade Entry [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Hopiplaka explicitly disclaims that any match between the Tesla 3-6-9 mapping and ICT concepts is "pure coincidence," not a confirmed decoding of ICT's "enigma" or "IMP" [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Stupidity, fear, and greed — Einstein's "three great forces" — are invoked at the head of the PO3 chapter to motivate the centrality of the number three in market behavior [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
