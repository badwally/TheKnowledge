# Data Altruism

## Summary

Data altruism is the consensual contribution of personal data to altruistic causes — typically scientific research, public health, or pharmaceutical development — without expectation of direct financial return [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key claims

- Data altruism is cited as one of the primary motivations for individuals to join data cooperatives, alongside the desire for greater power and control over data sharing [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Cooperatives provide a collective and consensual structure through which altruistic contribution can be channelled, with members retaining the ability to revoke access [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- The term is associated with EU regulatory vocabulary, particularly the Data Governance Act's framing of voluntary data sharing [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
