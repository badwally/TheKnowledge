# Vantaca Action Items (Case-Based Workflow Model)

## Summary

"Action Items" is Vantaca's name for its case-based workflow model: every request entering the platform is tracked as a single case with complete history, with work moving through defined steps and clear ownership at each stage [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]]. The stated design intent is that when exceptions arise the process adapts without losing visibility, and that staff, boards, and residents all see the same story from start to finish — operationalizing Vantaca's broader positioning that "community management is connection" and that small breakdowns across people, roles, and partners turn into big problems at scale [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key claims

- Every request in Vantaca is tracked as a single case with complete history [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Work moves through defined steps, with clear ownership at each stage [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- When exceptions come up, the process adapts without losing visibility [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Staff, boards, and residents see the same story from start to finish — i.e., a unified case timeline shared across all three constituencies [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The model is presented as the alternative to "disconnected modules" — Vantaca markets its features as "connected by design" around coordinated workflows rather than as a module suite [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Customer Trever Midgely (HOA Strategies) attributes operational impact specifically to Vantaca's work-queue functionality, framing it as the proximate driver of right-job-to-right-desk routing and timely task completion [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Customer Michael LaPoint (Desert Vista Community Management) attributes Vantaca's competitive sales differentiation specifically to the workflow-build capability, framing it as a process that "simply doesn't exist in most other software" [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Customer Kathy Bollo (Aspire Community Management) attributes increased board-side transparency to Vantaca, including automatic follow-up that visibly signals service requests are not being ignored [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
