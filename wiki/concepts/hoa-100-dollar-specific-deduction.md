# HOA $100 Specific Deduction (§528)

## Summary

When a homeowners association computes its §528 taxable income on Form 1120-H, it is permitted a specific $100 deduction in addition to deductions directly connected with producing non-exempt gross income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. The $100 specific deduction is a fixed allowance against non-exempt taxable income and operates independently of the deductions otherwise traceable to the association's investment or non-member revenue [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. Two categories of deduction otherwise generally available to corporations are NOT allowed against §528 taxable income: the §172 net operating loss deduction, and deductions under Part VIII of subchapter B (the special deductions for corporations, including the dividends-received deduction) [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].

## Key claims

- Taxable income under §528 is the excess, if any, of gross income for the tax year (excluding exempt function income) over allowed deductions directly connected with producing any gross income except exempt function income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Allowed deductions include a specific $100 deduction [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- The §172 net operating loss deduction is NOT allowed against §528 taxable income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Deductions under Part VIII of subchapter B (special deductions for corporations) are NOT allowed against §528 taxable income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- If facilities are used (or personnel are employed) for both exempt and nonexempt purposes, Treas. Reg. §1.528-10 governs the cost-allocation rule for determining the portion of expenses deductible against non-exempt income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
