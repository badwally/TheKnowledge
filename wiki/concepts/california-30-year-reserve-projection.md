# California 30-Year Reserve Projection

## Summary

The California 30-year reserve projection is the forward-looking adequacy test embedded in the Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary prescribed by Civil Code §5570: the board, based upon the most recent reserve study and other information available to it, must answer yes or no on whether currently projected reserve account balances will be sufficient at the end of each year to meet the association's obligation for repair and/or replacement of major components during the next 30 years [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].

## Key claims

- Statutory anchor: Civil Code §5570(a) item (3) [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Question framing: "will currently projected reserve account balances be sufficient at the end of each year to meet the association's obligation for repair and/or replacement of major components during the next 30 years?" [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Evidence base: the answer is grounded in the most recent reserve study and other information available to the board of directors [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Test granularity: the test is year-by-year — balances must be sufficient at the end of each of the 30 years, not merely at the terminal year [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Major-component scope: the projection covers "major components" as defined at Civil Code §5550 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Exclusion of long-lived components: components with an estimated remaining useful life of more than 30 years may be included in the reserve study as a capital asset or disregarded from the reserve calculation, with disclosure of the decision [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Negative-answer disclosure: if the answer is no, the form requires disclosure of additional assessments or other contributions to reserves that would be necessary to ensure sufficient reserve funds will be available each year during the next 30 years that have not yet been approved [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Five-year carve-out within the 30-year horizon: item (7) of the form requires detailed numerical projections of required reserves, cash balances, and percent-funded figures for each of the next five budget years specifically — both with and without the approved funding plan [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
