# HTF Order Flow Confluence (Fade Authorization Rule)

## Summary

The HTF (higher-time-frame) order flow confluence rule, in daytradingrauf's DOL framework, is the discipline gate that authorizes the trader to fade market-structure liquidity patterns: trading the opposite of what the crowd does at bull/bear flags, failure swings, and trend lines is permitted only when higher-time-frame order flow supports the fade [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].

## Key claims

- The rule is stated as a hard precondition for fading market-structure patterns: "You must have HTF order flow to back up why you're 'fading' these patterns" [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- It applies specifically to the three enumerated market-structure forms: bull and bear flags, failure swings (double tops and bottoms), and trend line liquidity [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Without HTF confluence, the contrarian "do the opposite of the crowd" posture is not authorized — the rule is what prevents naive contrarianism [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
