# Transition from Developer Control

## Summary

Transition from developer control is the regulated handover of governance, financials, and common-element responsibility from a condominium's developer to the unit-owner-controlled board. In Florida, this transition is governed by Florida Administrative Code Rule 61B-23.003, which has been in its current codified form since at least 1/19/1997 [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].

## Key claims

- Florida codifies developer-control transition as a distinct administrative rule (FAC 61B-23.003), separate from elections, recalls, and ordinary board operations [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Florida also provides a parallel mechanic — recall of board members under 61B-23.0026 — that explicitly handles developer-appointed directors and class voting, indicating that recall and transition are treated as distinct but adjacent governance levers [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
