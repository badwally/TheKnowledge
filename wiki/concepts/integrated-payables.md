# Integrated Payables (HOA Treasury Service)

## Summary

Integrated Payables is a treasury management service that consolidates the disbursement side of an association's payment flow — typically routing vendor and contractor payments from the association's accounting platform through the bank's payment rails. The service is named by Western Alliance Bank as one of three treasury services exposed via its WA ConnectLive API platform to HOAs and their managing agents, alongside Lockbox and Positive Pay [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].

## Key claims

- Named by Western Alliance Bank as one of three treasury services available through the WA ConnectLive HOA banking platform, alongside Lockbox and Positive Pay [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Carries explicit per-use charges in the Western Alliance Bank context, distinguishing it from bundled or no-incremental-fee Lockbox and Positive Pay services [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Structurally a disbursement-side (payables) service, complementing Lockbox, which is a receivables-side (deposit) service [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Service availability is conditioned on the association's accounting software via the WA ConnectLive integration matrix; Western Alliance Bank's product page explicitly notes that services and integration tools may differ by software [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
