# Condo Board Fiduciary Duty

## Summary

In Florida, the fiduciary duty of a condominium association's Board of Administration and its committees is codified by Florida Administrative Code Rule 61B-23.001, which has been part of the regulatory framework since at least 4/14/1999 [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]]. This rule sits within Chapter 61B-23 and establishes the operational-governance baseline for board conduct, distinct from the structural-reserve obligations imposed by Florida's SIRS regime [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].

## Key claims

- Florida codifies condo board fiduciary duty by rule (FAC 61B-23.001), not just by statute, giving the Division of Condominiums a direct administrative enforcement hook [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- The rule covers both the Board of Administration and its Committees, extending fiduciary obligations beyond the directors themselves [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- The rule has been stable in this codified form since 4/14/1999, predating the post-Surfside SIRS reforms [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
