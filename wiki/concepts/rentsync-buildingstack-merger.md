## Summary

The Rentsync and Building Stack merger (December 2023) is a Canadian PropTech consolidation event in which Rentsync, a Toronto-based rental housing SaaS and marketplace company, merged with Building Stack, a Montreal-based property management software company for rental property operators [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]]. The merger is a landscape comp for Canadian PropTech M&A velocity in the 2023-2024 period, contextualizing the concurrent Vertica Capital condo roll-up thesis [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]].

## Key claims

- Rentsync is headquartered in Toronto; serves the rental housing industry; operates the Rentals.ca Network marketplace; Growth 500 company with 100+ employees as of 2023 [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]]
- Building Stack is headquartered in Montreal; serves rental property operators with a web and mobile platform covering the full tenant lifecycle [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]]
- Merger announced December 5, 2023; positions as a "list-to-lease" combined platform for rental housing professionals [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]]
- CEO of Rentsync is Max Steinman; COO of Building Stack is Pablo Menghini; CEO of Building Stack is Jonathan Margel [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]]
- Scope is rental housing and multifamily, not condo association management; no mention of reserve funds, condo boards, or owner governance [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]]
- Cited here as a Canadian PropTech consolidation comp, not as a direct condo-software landscape participant [[sources/web-2023-12-05-282]]
