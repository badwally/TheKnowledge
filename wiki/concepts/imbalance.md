# Imbalance

## Summary

An imbalance is the technical change of state in delivery of price that occurs when an energetic move in a particular direction signals the start of an expansion (displacement); imbalances are usually coupled with order blocks (OBs), can create Fair Value Gaps (FVGs), and become actionable specifically when they take out a previous market structure such as a swing high or swing low [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].

## Key claims

- An imbalance occurs when there is an energetic move in a particular direction that signals the start of an expansion — known as displacement [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- Imbalances are usually seen at expansions out of consolidation, reversal expansions, or during trends [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- Technically defined as the change of state in delivery of price, and usually coupled with OBs [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- Imbalances can create Fair Value Gaps (FVGs), which represent incomplete auctions where orders are waiting to be filled inside the gap [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- An imbalance becomes important specifically when it takes out a previous market structure — for the model in Lesson 3, that means imbalances that take out swing highs or swing lows [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- The setup works even better when the imbalance forms coming out of consolidation just after taking out liquidity [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- Imbalances are the areas where Lesson 3 wants traders to start or add to a position to join the new trend [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
