# GSE Condominium Property Insurance Deductible Cap ($50,000/unit)

## Summary

The GSE Condominium Property Insurance Deductible Cap is a $50,000-per-unit maximum deductible that Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac will accept on the master property insurance policy of a GSE-eligible condominium association, effective July 1 (year inferred 2026 from the publication date of the source CAI Advocacy explainer). The cap is part of a broader March 18, 2026 insurance restructuring (Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 + Freddie Mac Bulletin 2026-C) that simultaneously loosens replacement-cost documentation, drops the roof-replacement-cost requirement, removes the inflation guard, and increases coverage-sufficiency flexibility — but creates compliance challenges for communities currently carrying higher deductibles than the new cap [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].

## Key claims

- A new maximum deductible of $50,000 per unit will apply beginning July 1 [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The deductible cap is part of an insurance package that also eliminates strict replacement cost documentation requirements [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The same package removes the requirement to insure roofs at full replacement cost [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The same package introduces greater flexibility in how coverage sufficiency is determined [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- For condominium associations specifically, the inflation guard requirement is removed [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- These changes are framed by CAI as acknowledging the challenges associations face in today's insurance market while maintaining a baseline of protection [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The new deductible cap may create compliance challenges for communities currently carrying higher deductibles [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- At the unit level, owners must carry insurance — usually in the form of an H06 policy — when gaps exist in the master policy or when deductibles apply [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Unit-owner coverage must align with either interior exposure or the master policy deductible [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Loan servicers face new annual verification, coverage-reduction monitoring, and annual borrower reminder obligations, fully effective by January 1, 2027 [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- CAI directs boards to evaluate their insurance coverage and deductibles and prepare to provide this information in lender questionnaires for upcoming sales [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
