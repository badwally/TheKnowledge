# Neuroinflammation-Stress Loop

## Summary

A self-reinforcing mechanism, described by Dr. Carla Kuon in her 2023 Long COVID Part 3 group medical visit, in which body inflammation propagates into brain inflammation (neuroinflammation), which then amplifies stress physiology on the body and brain, which in turn further increases neuroinflammation — creating "a looped, self reinforcing mechanism" that vagal dysfunction worsens and that vagal-toning interventions are intended to break [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- Body inflammation drives brain inflammation (neuroinflammation) [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Neuroinflammation drives stress physiology on body and brain, which further increases neuroinflammation — "a looped, self reinforcing mechanism" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- The Vagus Nerve (CN-X), normally a regulator of fight/flight and a brake on organ inflammation, is negatively impacted by COVID, removing the loop's normal anti-inflammatory brake [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- "When the Vagus is impacted, you have high stress responses in the body and perpetual inflammation" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- The clinical question Dr. Kuon poses is: "How do you break the cycle?" — answered by vagal-toning practices that re-engage CN-X's anti-inflammatory effect [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
