# AI Literacy in Public Education

## Summary

A policy stance — articulated in Japan's April 2023 AI Whitepaper — treating AI literacy as a concrete skill set that must be embedded in public-education curricula in anticipation of an "AI-native" era in which active use of AI in everyday socio-economic activity is commonplace [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key claims

- AI is expected to bring significant impact on the education field, including diversification of learning methods and growing importance of nurturing AI-native talent [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Regulations are nonetheless deemed necessary for the use of AI in addressing classroom challenges [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- The Japanese government is recommended to position improvement of AI literacy in the public-education curriculum as a concrete skill set in anticipation of the AI-native era [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- The whitepaper calls for promptly establishing classroom-handling guidelines for AI, including the permissibility of using LLMs, to integrate AI-literacy improvement into curricula [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
