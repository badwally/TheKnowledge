# Extended gamma process

## Summary

The extended gamma process is a generalisation of the gamma process studied in the degradation and reliability literature; Al Masry, Mercier, and Verdier (2021) analyse its stochastic comparisons and ageing properties in the *Journal of Applied Probability*.

## Key claims

- The extended gamma process is the subject of stochastic-comparisons and ageing-properties analysis in Al Masry, Mercier, and Verdier's 2021 *Journal of Applied Probability* paper (Volume 58, Issue 1) [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
- Downstream work citing this analysis spans applications in optimal periodic inspection for multi-auxiliary component systems subject to load-sharing dependence, remaining-life prediction methods for special power plants, and material-performance evaluation for long-life structural design under random degradation from freeze–thaw cycles, indicating use of the extended gamma process framework in maintenance and reliability modelling contexts [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
