# Nonstationary gamma process

## Summary

A nonstationary gamma process is a stochastic process used to model monotone degradation in physical systems whose deterioration rate varies over time, and which is the underlying degradation model adopted by Wang et al. (2025) for their semi-Markov decision-process-based predictive maintenance policy verified on a coating system.

## Key claims

- Nonstationary gamma processes have extensive applications in depicting the degradation of many practical systems [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- Wang et al. (2025) adopt a nonstationary gamma process as the underlying degradation model in their predictive maintenance policy for systems undergoing periodic inspections and multiple types of maintenance actions [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- In the Wang et al. (2025) framework, periodic inspections fully reveal the degradation levels of the system governed by the nonstationary gamma process [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The effectiveness of the gamma-process-based maintenance optimization is demonstrated by a coating system case study [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
