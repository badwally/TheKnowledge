# Bayesian inference

## Summary

Bayesian inference is a statistical updating procedure in which different sources of information — such as observations, prior models, and expert judgement — are combined into a posterior distribution. In civil-engineering durability problems it is invoked to fuse heterogeneous information about loading conditions and material parameters with explicit treatment of their credibility.

## Key claims

- Bayesian inference is applied to combine different sources of information so as to provide a more accurate estimation of coupled heat and moisture fields in a structure [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The framing of Bayesian inference invoked here requires that each source of information be accompanied by a corresponding evaluation of its credibility, not merely a point value [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The procedure is demonstrated on the probabilistic description of a heterogeneous material in which the uncertainties consist of both particular values of individual material characteristics and their spatial fluctuations [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
