# Gamma stochastic process

## Summary

The Gamma stochastic process is a probabilistic model used to describe time-dependent deterioration in engineering structures, including bridge resistance. It has been suggested in the structural reliability literature as a way to capture the monotonic and uncertain nature of degradation processes, in contrast to deterministic or fully correlated deterioration assumptions.

## Key claims

- The Gamma stochastic process has been suggested in the literature to describe the probabilistic behavior of structural time-dependent resistance [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- Wang, Feng, Zhang, and Zou (2018) adopt the Gamma stochastic process in their method for updating the resistance of service-proven bridges, treating it as a realistic alternative to deterministic or fully correlated deterioration models [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- Parametric examples in the 2018 paper show that the choice of a gamma-process-based deterioration model materially affects the updated estimate of bridge resistance given historical service information [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
