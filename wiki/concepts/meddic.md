# MEDDIC

## Summary

MEDDIC is a sales qualification framework applied to deal records during pursuit, with the explicit goal of confirming that critical buyer-side roles — notably the economic buyer — have been identified before a deal is treated as close-ready [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]]. The canonical failure mode the framework guards against is a rep reporting a deal as about to close without ever having spoken to the person who signs the check [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].

## Key claims

- MEDDIC is described as a "sales qualification framework" that gates deal-stage progression on the identification of specific buyer roles [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
- The roles called out by name in the framework's operational application include economic buyer, champion, and blocker — each modeled as a contact-side attribute of a deal [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
- In HubSpot, MEDDIC can be implemented via association labels on the deal↔contact association combined with segments and deal tags that surface missing roles visually on the pipeline [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
- The hardest part of running MEDDIC is not the tool setup but adoption — getting the sales team to actually apply the methodology in practice [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
