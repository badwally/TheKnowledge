# Bridge resistance deterioration

## Summary

Bridge resistance deterioration is the time-dependent reduction in structural capacity of in-service bridges driven by environmental and anthropogenic loading. Probability-based modelling of this deterioration supports condition evaluation and the optimisation of maintenance and repair decisions for aging bridges.

## Key claims

- Environmental and anthropogenic factors to which in-service bridges are subjected are responsible for the reduction of bridge performance and ultimately for service risk and an increased probability of substantial economic losses [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- Probability-based estimation of bridge resistance is an essential indicator for bridge condition evaluation and for optimisation of bridge maintenance/repair decisions [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- In analyses updating bridge resistance with past service information, deterioration models have historically been assumed as either deterministic or fully correlated; Wang, Feng, Zhang, and Zou (2018) argue this may differ significantly from the realistic case and motivates a stochastic-process treatment [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
