# Miter gates

## Summary

Miter gates are a class of civil infrastructure asset whose degradation modelling is treated as an application target for hybrid modelling combined with recursive Bayesian state estimation in the 2025 IMAC Article 5 by Zhao, Zeng, Todd, and Hu.

## Key claims

- Miter gates are the application target of 'Degradation Model Correction of Miter Gates Through Synthesis of Hybrid Modeling with Recursive Bayesian State Estimation,' published as Article 5 in *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3* (DOI 10.13052/97887-438-0148-1_5) [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The IMAC 2025 paper frames miter gates as a context in which existing degradation models can be corrected using a combination of hybrid modelling and recursive Bayesian state estimation [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
