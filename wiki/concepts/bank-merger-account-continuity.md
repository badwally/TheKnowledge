# Bank merger account continuity

## Summary

Bank merger account continuity is the operational pattern in which a US chartered depository institution explicitly commits — at the time of an announced merger and subsequent rebrand — to preserve through the transition: customer account numbers, routing numbers, signature cards, supporting documents, lockbox addresses, online banking tools, third-party software integrations, debit/credit cards, loan payments, CDs, FDIC coverage, and supplemental IntraFi deposit services [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. The Pacific Premier Bank–Umpqua Bank merger of August 31, 2025 and the subsequent rebrand to Columbia Bank is the source-of-record instance for this continuity pattern in the HOA banking segment, with each listed item explicitly committed to continue uninterrupted by the August 28, 2025 Community Financials client communication [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key claims

- Account and routing numbers remain unchanged for customers through a merger/rebrand under the continuity pattern [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Signature cards, supporting documents, and lockbox addresses remain valid through a merger/rebrand; the bank name simply updates over time [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Online banking tools and third-party software integrations (notably Smartwebs API integrations in the HOA segment) continue without interruption through the transition [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The banking team currently serving the customer remains in place through the transition [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Pre-merger debit and credit cards continue to work without changes; replacements are issued by direct bank notification if needed [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Loan payments, CDs, and FDIC coverage remain unaffected through the transition [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- IntraFi CDARS and ICS deposit services continue as usual through the transition [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The Pacific Premier–Umpqua–Columbia Bank transition is the source-of-record instance of the continuity pattern in the HOA banking segment [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
