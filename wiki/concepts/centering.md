# Centering

## Summary

Psychological technique whereby a trader learns to distance himself from the sequence of emotional responses and negative interpretations of stressful trades that perpetuate a circle of failure; the common element across all forms of centering — meditation, visualization, progressive muscle relaxation — is the narrowing of awareness to a single focus, which reduces automatic thinking and increases concentration and involvement in the present [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].

## Key claims

- Centering enables the trader to commit to a specific trading objective and maintain sufficient discipline to follow his trading strategy rather than be disturbed by fears of losing [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Fear of failure traps many traders in a narrow trading range, making them reluctant to expand their profitability by consciously relinquishing defensive habits and developing a proactive approach [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- By recognizing the automatic nature of negative thinking, the centered trader can turn off self-doubt and self-criticism and adjust behavior to respond to events in a disciplined way in line with his trading strategy [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Centering leads to increased control over the autonomic nervous system, enabling the trader to stretch beyond conventional limits so he can be guided by trading objectives and tape action with a minimum of preconceived notions, fears, and beliefs [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Dramatically demonstrated in archery, where any tension from trying to control anxiety is reflected in the bow and arrow at the moment of release — the best shots occur when the arrow is released effortlessly [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- In sports, manifests as heightened awareness and increased receptivity: unspoken communication between quarterbacks and wide receivers, and the passing skills of Bob Cousy, Larry Bird, and Isaiah Thomas [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- The centered trader is able to break endless loops of destructive trading, which occur when buying is linked to losses or selling is linked to the need to recover losses [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- The centered trader can separate a losing trade from his trading style and continue to follow his trading strategy despite carrying a continuing loss [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- The centered trader is not afraid to climb on board a trade after it has begun rather than assume it has reached its limit, and does not have to be first or certain of the outcome before trading [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- The centered trader has an exit strategy and is always proactive in relationship to the market — he marks profits and losses, stops rationalizing losses, keeps expanding activity as he succeeds, and is willing to cut losses rather than ride them [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Critical in producing the centered state is eliminating all thoughts but the thought of the object being focused on and blocking out awareness of extraneous thoughts or activity [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- By focusing on a visual image, the brain enters the alpha state — slowed electrical rhythms in the visual centers reflecting decreased attention to the external environment, a slowing of time, and the capacity to perceive minute and precise details [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Can be produced through progressive muscle relaxation (systematically tightening and releasing muscle groups while breathing deeply), praying, reading a story, humming, dance, or physical exercise — all activities that temporarily bypass intellectual activity and shut down external stimuli input [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Consciously shifting the focus and rhythm of ordinary habitual actions returns automatic defenses to full awareness, increasing the capacity to observe automatic negative thoughts and associated feelings [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
