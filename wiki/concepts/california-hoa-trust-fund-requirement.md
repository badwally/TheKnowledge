# California HOA Trust Fund Requirement (Unused Dues, Fees, and Assessments)

## Summary

Under California Revenue and Taxation Code Section 23701t, amounts received by a tax-exempt California homeowners' association as membership dues, fees, and assessments — to the extent not used for association purposes during the year — must be transferred and held in trust to provide for the management, maintenance, and care of association property [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]]. This statutory trust requirement is one of the six operational-test conditions for §23701t exemption and operates as California's structural anchor for the reserve-fund-trust treatment of HOA capital reserves [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].

## Key claims

- Amounts received as membership dues, fees, and assessments that are not used for association purposes during the year must be transferred and held in trust [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- The trust must be used to provide for the management, maintenance, and care of association property [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- The trust requirement is enumerated as one of the six operational-test conditions a California HOA must satisfy to qualify for §23701t tax-exempt status [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- The trust requirement applies generally to all eligible §23701t organization types (condominium management associations, residential real estate management associations, cooperative housing corporations, time-share associations, and mutual water companies) [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- The trust requirement is articulated alongside, and is distinct from, the no-private-inurement rule that prohibits net earnings from benefiting private individuals other than in the normal course of association activities [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
