# Hybrid modelling synthesized with recursive Bayesian state estimation

## Summary

A degradation model correction approach, introduced in the 2025 IMAC Article 5 by Zhao, Zeng, Todd, and Hu, that synthesizes hybrid modelling with recursive Bayesian state estimation as a strategy for correcting degradation models of miter gates.

## Key claims

- The synthesis of hybrid modelling with recursive Bayesian state estimation is the methodological framing of Article 5, 'Degradation Model Correction of Miter Gates Through Synthesis of Hybrid Modeling with Recursive Bayesian State Estimation,' published in *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3* (DOI 10.13052/97887-438-0148-1_5) [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The approach is presented as a means of degradation model correction, applied to miter gates [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The paper situates this method within the wider Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification literature surveyed by the 43rd IMAC proceedings [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
