# Offset-Distribution

## Summary

In the canonical MMXM framework, "offset-distribution" is the structural terminus of an MMXM expansion — the price level at the higher-timeframe draw on liquidity where smart money exits its position and where opposite-side traders have their stops resting [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]]. In an MMBM this is above old highs, where longs distribute into the buyside liquidity at shorts' stops; in an MMSM the symmetric concept applies at old lows, where shorts cover into the sellside liquidity at longs' stops [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- Per the MMXM PDF, in an MMBM, longs exit above old highs — "where shorts are positioned with their stops" — and the MMXM PDF labels this terminus "offset-distribution" [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the offset-distribution event "signals the terminus for our model's current move up" in an MMBM, after which price may base and continue the trend or reverse back down for an MMSM [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, an MMSM move down terminates at offset-distribution where sellers are absorbed by buyers in liquidity — at old lows where old longs are positioned with their stops [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF includes a clarifying note that in the draw-on-liquidity zone the terminus "should state offset-distribution" — but flags that the same zone can become re-accumulation if an MMBM sets up in the discount of the current array or an older array lower [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Offset-distribution is the structural counterpart to the "draw on liquidity" target — it is where the move toward DOL terminates by filling stops on the opposite side of the trade [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, after offset-distribution, price either bases and continues the trend or reverses into the opposite MMXM model — the choice between continuation and reversal is what the next round of HTF analysis must answer [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
