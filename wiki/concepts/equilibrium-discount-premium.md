# Equilibrium, Discount, and Premium

## Summary

Framework for identifying entry zones within a dealing range by dividing it at the 0.5 Fibonacci level — the equilibrium point where neither buyers nor sellers have an advantage — into a discount zone (below 0.5) and a premium zone (above 0.5), with the principle that longs should be entered in the discount and shorts in the premium [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].

## Key claims

- Equilibrium is the state at which opposing forces are at a balance with one another; for price, this is the fair value where there is no advantage for either buyers or sellers [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- The 0.5 Fibonacci retracement level marks the equilibrium point of any dealing range, regardless of timeframe [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- Below the 0.5 level is discount; above the 0.5 level is premium [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- To go long, a trader wants price to retrace to the 0.50 fib area or below, entering inside a fair value gap (FVG) or near an order block (OB) in the discount zone, expecting higher prices [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- To go short, a trader wants price to retrace to the 0.50 fib area or above, entering inside an FVG or near an OB in the premium zone, expecting lower prices [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- Letting price pull back into a discount or premium zone mitigates risk while potentially increasing reward, skewing the trade outcome in the trader's favor when price action has been correctly read [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- The concept is fractal — applicable to any timeframe — with higher timeframes offering better odds that the setup works [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- Finding equilibrium requires a swing low/high and a swing high/low with a market structure shift (MSS) between them; the Fibonacci tool is drawn from the displacement origin to the displacement endpoint [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
- The trader's job is to determine the narrative for price (direction and targets), then determine where to add positions and exit using equilibrium as the filter [[sources/pdf-f2565310670c]].
