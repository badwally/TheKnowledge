# Liquidity vs. Support and Resistance

## Summary

A core editorial argument of "Lesson 1: Price and Liquidity" is that classical support and resistance is the wrong mental model for retail traders to operate from: what looks like support or resistance is in most cases an accumulation of buyside or sellside liquidity that smart-money flow is engineered to engage by trapping retail breakout and short-the-rip traders before reversing toward the opposing pool [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].

## Key claims

- The lesson explicitly tells traders not to make the mistake of treating buyside/sellside liquidity as synonymous with support and resistance, even though the two may align at times [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- Adjacent swing highs and lows create the illusion of support and resistance when they are in fact areas of buyside and sellside liquidity [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- The lesson's worked downtrend example shows that when retail "resistance" breaks to the upside, the typical interpretation is that the trend is reversing — but the lesson labels this reading WRONG and reframes the move as a classic trap of retail traders into believing the trend is reversing [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- When the retail-resistance level breaks, it triggers stop losses of weak retail and eager traders trying to short the move up, forcing them to buy back at higher prices from smart money that is selling those shares short [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- Once the BSL is purged, algorithms begin to deliver price back down toward SSL — "what we see as retail support" — and once that level breaks, stop losses force long-positioned weak/eager traders to sell at lower prices, allowing the smart money that shorted above to cover from those shares [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
- The lesson positions the smart-money short region above retail resistance as a "premium" — flagged as a concept developed further later in the curriculum — distinguishing it from the everyday support/resistance vocabulary of retail charting [[sources/pdf-704c439723d0]].
