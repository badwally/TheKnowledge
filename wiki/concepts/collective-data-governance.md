# Collective Data Governance

## Summary

Collective data governance is the umbrella class of third-party intermediary mechanisms — data trusts, decentralised autonomous organisations (DAOs), data cooperatives, data unions, and trusted data intermediaries — that aggregate data-subject or data-producer interests for collective negotiation, stewardship, or licensing [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].

## Key claims

- Vincent, Prewitt and Li (2025) extend the collective-governance umbrella to a producer-side reading: trusted data intermediary organizations represent guilds or syndicates of information producers in collective bargaining with AI builders [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- The paper argues the collective layer is what creates the market frictions and aligned incentives needed for a pro-social, sustainable AI economy [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- Coalition-building requires both technical infrastructure (federated data management tools, explainable data value estimations) and regulatory support for the intermediary form [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
