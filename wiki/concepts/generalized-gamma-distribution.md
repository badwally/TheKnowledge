# Generalized-gamma distribution

## Summary

The Generalized-gamma distribution is a parametric survival model that, alongside the Weibull distribution, is identified as a notable exception fit for individual machine components in mobile-work-equipment prognostics — even where non-parametric estimators are preferred for the overall component population.

## Key claims

- The Generalized-gamma distribution is a parametric model usable for estimating survival curves and hazard rates in survival analysis [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- In the prognostic modeling of machine components from mobile work equipment, notable exceptions to the non-parametric preference can be found that are amenable to a Generalized-gamma model [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
