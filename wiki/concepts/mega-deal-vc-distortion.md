# Mega-Deal VC Distortion

## Summary

The phenomenon whereby a small number of very large venture capital transactions inflate headline investment totals, obscuring stagnation or decline in the underlying market for typical deals; per SVB's H2 2025 "State of the Markets," unprecedented fundings like the $40B OpenAI deal push US VC investment totals toward all-time highs, but deals under $100M remained stubbornly low — making top-line VC statistics misleading as a measure of ecosystem health [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].

## Key claims

- SVB frames the distortion explicitly: "Mega-deals push VC toward all-time highs; smaller deals trend flat" [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- The $40B OpenAI deal is cited as the exemplar of unprecedented fundings that inflate aggregate VC investment totals [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- When looking behind the headline numbers, investment activity for deals under $100M "remained stubbornly low" [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- US Series A tech deal count in H1 2025 was 729, the first growth in investment activity since 2021 but still the lowest level in over a decade [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- The number of Series A financings is "still the lowest in over a decade," even as the moderate uptick off 2024 lows is fueled by a robust cohort of seed companies seeking to raise Series A [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Full-year 2025 Series A outlook is 1,450 deals (2024 actual: 1,345), with 729 completed in H1, tracking expectations [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
