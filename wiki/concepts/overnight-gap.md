# Overnight gap

## Summary

An overnight gap is a significant discontinuity between one session's closing price and the next session's opening price, typically driven by news, earnings, economic indicators, or geopolitical events; Zarattini and Stamatoudis (2024) operationalize a tradable upside gap as a ≥6% open above the prior close with a $2 minimum opening price and ≥200,000 pre-market shares, identifying 9,794 such events on U.S. NYSE/Nasdaq stocks from 2016 to 2023 [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].

## Key claims

- An overnight gap is a significant discontinuity in price from one trading session to the next, often driven by substantial news events, earnings reports, economic indicators, or geopolitical developments, leading to heightened volatility and uncertainty [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- A gap up — opening significantly higher than the previous close — typically reflects positive market sentiment or favorable news; a gap down indicates negative sentiment or adverse news [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Oppenheimer and Schlarbaum (1981) categorize gaps into breakaway, runaway, and exhaustion gaps, each possessing distinct predictive qualities; breakaway gaps often signify the beginning of a new trend, while exhaustion gaps may indicate the end of a trend [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Bulkowski (2005) further expands on this work, providing comprehensive analyses of chart patterns including gaps, and confirms that certain types of gaps can serve as reliable indicators of future price movements [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Zarattini and Stamatoudis (2024) define a tradable gap as satisfying three criteria: an opening price at least 6% higher than the previous closing price, a minimum opening price of $2, and at least 200,000 shares traded in pre-market [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Applying these criteria to U.S. NYSE/Nasdaq stocks from January 1, 2016 to December 31, 2023 — approximately 7,000 stocks, free from survivorship bias, with CRSP daily data and 1-minute IQFeed and Polygon intraday data — identifies 9,794 gap events [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- The intraday data used in this study is not adjusted for stock splits or dividends, ensuring the database remains unaffected by retrospective price adjustments [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
