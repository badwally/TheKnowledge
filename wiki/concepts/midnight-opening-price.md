# Midnight Opening Price

## Summary

The Midnight Opening Price is ICT's daily-bias anchor — the open of the daily candle at 00:00 ET — used alongside the 8:30 ET opening price to gauge whether the day is bullish, bearish, or extremely bearish, and to define the price zone where a bullish trader should accumulate [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].

## Key claims

- ICT instructs traders to mark out the opening price of the candle at midnight as a daily reference [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- When bullish, ICT teaches buying at or close to the opening price at midnight [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- If price did not rally above the midnight opening price or above the 8:30 ET opening price and the bias is bearish, the day is "extremely bearish" — a heavy market unlikely to rally, with no reason to go long [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- The midnight open is a key marker for the New York Open setup, paired with the 8:30 ET open in the Judas swing + FVG + order-block configuration [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- An advanced application is buying during the manipulation phase below the midnight open if the trader knows the market is likely to go down to go up [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
