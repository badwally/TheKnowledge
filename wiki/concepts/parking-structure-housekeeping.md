# Parking Structure Housekeeping (PCI MNL-136-04)

## Summary

PCI MNL-136-04 defines parking structure housekeeping as the cluster of maintenance tasks that enhance the aesthetic appeal and functional performance of the facility, performed by the owner or operator on a scheduled basis as set out in the manual's Table A (Housekeeping Schedule) [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].

## Key claims

- Housekeeping tasks listed by PCI include general cleaning (trash removal, drain cleaning, sweeping, window cleaning); annual high-pressure floor wash and oil-stain removal; cleaning of expansion joints and control joints; periodic painting touch-up; landscaping upkeep; door and hardware operation checks; re-painting of parking stripes and graphics; lighting-fixture cleaning and lamp replacement; elevator cleaning and maintenance; sign cleaning and repair; graffiti removal; snow and ice control; security system checks; parking equipment and revenue control system checks; and janitorial services [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Sweeping should remove debris and sand from drains, expansion joints, and control joints, and cleaning should include removal of litter from beam ledges and member joints [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Cleaning and sweeping are tell-tale signs of the amount of attention a parking facility receives; lack of attention is an invitation to loiter and may reduce safety of use [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- When parking striping is changed, existing striping should be removed completely by shot or water blasting rather than painted over, to prevent two sets of striping from creating user confusion [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Highly visible parking striping promotes centering of parked cars in the parking space, minimizing overlap [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Lighting fixtures should be cleaned annually, immediately following the spring wash down, because dirt accumulation on lenses or clear covers reduces fixture effectiveness [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- A schedule of re-lamping based on lamp life is recommended; lamps should be monitored daily, replaced as quickly as possible (and ideally before they burn out), with replacement lamps stored on the premises because lighting is a definable measure of security [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Emergency lights and generators should be checked daily; battery-pack emergency lights typically include visible status indicators and test buttons [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Security systems, emergency lighting, and emergency generators must be checked frequently to ensure proper functioning, and time clocks may need to be corrected for daylight savings time [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Light to moderate oil stains are generally not detrimental to the structure but should be removed to improve facility appearance; heavy stains may be slippery and hazardous [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Ventilation and carbon monoxide monitoring systems in enclosed parking structures must be checked daily [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
