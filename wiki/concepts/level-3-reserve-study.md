# Level 3 Reserve Study (no-on-site-inspection update)

## Summary

A "Level 3" reserve study is a no-on-site-inspection update of an existing reserve study, generated from system data and prior-study records rather than a fresh physical site visit [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]]. CommunityPay's product positioning treats Level 3 as the default output of an in-platform, continuously-updated reserve-study module — produced from the platform's own component registry, ledger, and useful-life data — with the explicit caveat that for states requiring physical inspections or structural assessments, the Level 3 update should be paired with a qualified reserve specialist for an initial on-site evaluation, after which Level 3 studies typically fulfill requirements for ongoing maintenance and annual updates [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].

## Key claims

- A Level 3 study is produced without an on-site inspection [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Level 3 studies are typically used for ongoing maintenance and annual updates, after an initial on-site evaluation has been performed by a qualified reserve specialist [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- States that require physical inspections or structural assessments cannot rely on Level 3 alone for the initial study; the platform-generated Level 3 must be paired with an on-site evaluation in those jurisdictions [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Level 3 is the default output mode of CommunityPay's reserve-studies module, generated from the platform's own component registry and ledger data [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
