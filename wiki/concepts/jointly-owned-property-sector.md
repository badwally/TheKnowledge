# Jointly Owned Property (JOP) Sector (UAE)

## Summary

Jointly Owned Property (JOP) is the UAE regulatory designation for multi-owner real estate — the structural equivalent of the North American condominium/strata/HOA category and the deployment surface SmartProperty entered through its April 2025 partnership with Humberts Dubai [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].

## Key claims

- The Jointly Owned Property (JOP) sector is the UAE's named real estate category for shared-ownership buildings, and is the deployment surface that SmartProperty's Living Reserve Study entered via the Humberts Dubai partnership [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- Humberts Dubai is described as setting a "new benchmark for accuracy, transparency, and strategic financial planning in the Jointly Owned Property (JOP) sector" through its adoption of SmartProperty's platform [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- The JOP sector is the functional UAE analog to the North American condo/HOA and reserve-study customer base SmartProperty otherwise serves [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
