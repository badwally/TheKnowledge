# Market Maker Buy Model (Bullish PO3)

## Summary

The bullish form of ICT's Power of 3 / MMXM setup: price first reprices lower toward a higher-timeframe point of interest or sellside liquidity, then sets up a market maker model that favors the buy-side, after which distribution proceeds higher to take out buyside liquidity [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]]; the canonical MMXM PDF describes the same template as price expansion UPWARDS toward buyside liquidity, anchored on an HTF discount of an array, with an accumulation phase, displacement up, market structure shift, and re-accumulation in FVGs/OBs targeting offset-distribution above old highs [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- In a bullish PO3, "price will look to reprice lower (HTF POI) before setting up a mmxm that favours the buy-side" [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- During an uptrend, price seeks internal liquidity at a discount (manipulation) before continuing higher [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- An alternative bullish form: price drops lower before seeking a premium of the downtrend, which could indicate the start of a reversal from that down trend [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- A worked example: price has been in an uptrend, begins to accumulate, then makes a sudden drop lower to take out recent sellside liquidity; that manipulation down reverses back up and sets up the buy model to take out buyside liquidity [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- In strong uptrends or strong bull markets, "this is where BTFD comes into play" [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- The MMXM PDF describes a MMBM as price expansion UPWARDS towards buyside liquidity, with the initial base of the MMBM coming from an impulse move lower into a HTF discount of an array, followed by displacement and a market structure shift UP [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMBM accumulation phase is where sellers are absorbed by longs in liquidity — a quick drop followed by an energetic displacement up that creates the initial market structure shift [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, after the MSS the trader uses market structure to add into the trend at order blocks or FVGs, re-accumulating long positions "with smart money" to target the HTF draw on liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMBM workflow is to identify a higher time frame Point of Interest (Quarterly, Monthly, Weekly, Daily) using IPDA look back periods of 20, 40, 60 days, then identify the specific draw on liquidity (OLD highs/lows, liquidity pools, equal highs/lows, OLD PD Arrays, and inefficiencies such as SIBI/BISI, FVGs, voids, gaps), and watch for the model setup [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the terminus of the MMBM's current move up is at offset-distribution — above old highs where shorts have their stops — and from there price can base and continue the trend or reverse back down for an MMSM [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, retracements into FVGs are where longs re-accumulate, and OTE plus the "silver bullet" 2nd entry are the canonical entries identified inside the model [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
