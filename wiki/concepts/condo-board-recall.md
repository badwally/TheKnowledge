# Condo Board Recall

## Summary

Condo board recall is the statutory and administrative mechanism by which unit owners can remove and replace a sitting director. Florida codifies this in three distinct Florida Administrative Code rules within Chapter 61B-23, each governing a different recall pathway [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].

## Key claims

- FAC 61B-23.0026 establishes the general right to recall and replace a board member, with explicit treatment of developer-appointed directors, unit-owner-elected directors, and class voting (last amended 12/20/1995) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- FAC 61B-23.0027 governs recall of one or more board members at a unit-owner meeting, including filling resulting vacancies (last amended 1/18/2018) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- FAC 61B-23.0028 governs recall by written agreement of the voting interests, including filling resulting vacancies (last amended 1/18/2018) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- The 2018 amendments to both the meeting-based and written-agreement recall pathways indicate Florida actively maintains and tightens recall procedure, providing a useful compliance signal for any platform serving FL associations [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
