# Break of Structure (BOS)

## Summary

Break of Structure (BOS) is a smart-money price-action event in which price takes out a prior swing high (bullish continuation) or swing low (bearish continuation), confirming directional intent; in TTrades's entry models, BOS operates at two scales — an HTF BOS that establishes the directional premise and an LTF BOS combined with displacement and an FVG as the entry trigger — present unchanged across the 2022 [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] and 2023 [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]] versions of the reference card.

## Key claims

- BOS is the first ingredient listed in all four trigger-stack models in TTrades's reference card (Models 1–4), labeled simply "BOS" at both the HTF context level and the LTF entry level [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- The canonical LTF entry sequence pairs BOS with two other ingredients: "BOS + Displacement + FVG," indicating that BOS alone is insufficient and must coincide with energetic price movement and an unfilled imbalance [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- In Models 1 and 3 the structural ingredients are HTF BOS → HTF POI → LTF liquidity grab → BOS + Displacement + FVG; the LTF BOS is the final structural confirmation before the entry zone is qualified as in discount or premium [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- In Models 2 and 4, an Internal Liquidity (IDM) sweep is inserted between the HTF POI and the LTF liquidity grab, but the LTF BOS + displacement + FVG entry trigger is unchanged from Models 1 and 3 [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- The "Failed Swing" variation of Models 1–4 keeps BOS as the trigger but replaces the upstream liquidity-grab leg with a failed-swing structure [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
