# FX Vanilla Option

## Summary

A standard call or put option on a currency pair giving the buyer the right but not the obligation to exchange one currency for another at a specified strike rate on or before a specified expiration date; long calls profit from upside in the underlying, long puts profit from downside, and the corresponding short positions face mirror-image payoffs [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].

## Key claims

- Vanilla options are the standard call/put structures on FX, distinguished from "exotic" options that include path-dependent or non-standard features [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- The payoff of a long call option rises linearly with the underlying above the strike at expiration; the payoff of a short call is the mirror image [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- The payoff of a long put option rises linearly with the underlying below the strike at expiration; the payoff of a short put is the mirror image [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Vanilla option value is governed by a small set of factors (underlying spot, strike, time to expiration, volatility, and interest-rate differentials) whose effects on option value are summarized in a "factors and their effects on option value" table in the manual [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
