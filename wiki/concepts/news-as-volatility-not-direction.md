# News as Volatility, Not Direction

## Summary

Framing rule taught in ICT's 2022 mentorship and recapped by Trader Theory: the news cannot be used as a directional input to a trade — all that can be inferred from a scheduled high-impact release is that volatility will be introduced into the markets — which is why the methodology pairs the news calendar with no-trade blackout windows rather than directional setups, and recommends ForexFactory as the calendar of record [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].

## Key claims

- Trader Theory states the rule directly: "The news cannot be used as a directional tool, all we know is that volatility will be introduced into the markets" [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Recommends ForexFactory as the news calendar source [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Pairs with explicit blackout rules: "Do not trade Wednesday - Friday during NFP weeks" and "Do not trade big event days (FOMC-related or High-Impact Speeches)" — i.e., the response to a directional unknown plus elevated volatility is non-participation, not directional speculation [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Consistent with the methodology's broader posture that "It's okay to not have a bias or not know about where the market is going to move" and that, in such cases, the trader should leave the charts and come back later [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
