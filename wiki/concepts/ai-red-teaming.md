# AI Red-Teaming (Adversarial Testing)

## Summary

AI red-teaming is the practice of adversarial testing applied to AI systems to surface vulnerabilities prior to deployment; the ISED Voluntary Code of Conduct on Advanced Generative AI Systems treats it as a required Validity-and-Robustness measure for developers of advanced generative AI systems [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].

## Key claims

- The ISED Voluntary Code of Conduct on Advanced Generative AI Systems requires developers of advanced generative systems — both those with general-purpose capabilities and those made widely available for public use — to employ adversarial testing ("red-teaming") to identify vulnerabilities [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
- The code lists red-teaming under the Validity and Robustness outcome alongside wide-spectrum task-and-context testing, cyber-security assessment including data poisoning, and benchmarking against recognized standards [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
- Red-teaming under the code is an obligation of developers, not managers — managers' Validity-and-Robustness obligations are limited to cyber-security assessment for publicly available systems [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
