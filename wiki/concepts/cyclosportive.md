# Cyclosportive

## Summary

A cyclosportive is the competitive, time-measured counterpart to [[concepts/cyclotourism|cyclotouring]] — typically a stage race run on a fixed itinerary — illustrated by the [[entities/corsica-cyclo-gt-20|Corsica Cyclo "GT 20"]], a four-day stage cyclosportive held each May on the [[entities/gt-20|GT 20]] route [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].

## Key claims

- A cyclosportive can be structured as a multi-day stage race: the Corsica Cyclo "GT 20" runs as a four-day stage event [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- Cyclosportives are positioned in the brochure as "emblematic" — flagship events that anchor a region's cycling calendar [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- The format can share a route with cyclotouring: the same GT 20 itinerary serves both the cyclo-tourist crossing and the May cyclosportive, distinguishing them by pace and timing rather than terrain [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
