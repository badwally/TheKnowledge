# U.S. HOA Tax Return Deadlines and Fiscal Year End

## Summary

U.S. homeowners association federal tax returns are due two months fifteen days after the end of the fiscal year, in contrast with individual federal returns which are due three months fifteen days after year-end (April 15th) [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]]. For December 31st fiscal-year-end associations, the federal tax return due date is March 15th [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]]. Like individuals, an association can obtain a six-month filing extension, but the extension is to file the return — not to pay the taxes [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]]. Returns must be filed every year regardless of whether there is taxable income, because the corporation is a separate tax entity and must file annually from the date of incorporation [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].

## Key claims

- Association federal tax returns are due two months fifteen days after the end of the fiscal year [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- This differs from individual federal tax returns, which are due three months fifteen days after the year (April 15th) [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- For December 31st year-end associations, the due date is March 15th [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Like individuals, an association can get an extension of up to six months to file its federal taxes [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- The six-month extension is an extension to file the tax return — NOT an extension to pay the taxes [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Tax returns must be filed every year — whether or not there is taxable income [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- The corporation is a separate tax entity and must file a tax return annually from the date of incorporation [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
