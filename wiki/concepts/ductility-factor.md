# Ductility Factor

## Summary

The ductility factor is a structural-engineering measure of how far a member or section deforms inelastically relative to its yield deformation, used to characterize inelastic capacity and post-yield damage of components such as reinforced concrete piers.

## Key claims

- Ductility factors (DFs) along the height of a reinforced concrete bridge pier serve as identifiable nonlinear damage parameters in the PINN-based identification scheme of Yamaguchi (2025) [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
- Spatial distributions of estimated ductility factors revealed local decreases and increases that corresponded to the concrete and rebar damages observed in full-scale shaking table experiments [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
