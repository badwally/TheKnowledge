# Long COVID Vagal Dysfunction

## Summary

A clinical framing for Long COVID in which COVID-driven inflammation impairs the vagus nerve (CN-X), removing its normal anti-inflammatory braking on organs and producing perpetual inflammation plus high stress responses; per Dr. Carla Kuon, many of the most common Long COVID symptoms — tachycardia and lung, gastro, and adrenal symptoms — are mediated by CN-X dysfunction [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- The Vagus Nerve (CN-X) is exquisitely sensitive to high inflammation as caused by COVID, and there is documented evidence of Vagus dysfunction after COVID infection [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- When the Vagus is impacted, the body shows high stress responses and perpetual inflammation [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Many Long COVID symptoms — including tachycardia and lung, gastro, and adrenal symptoms — are mediated by CN-X [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- The framing implies a treatment lever: vagal-toning interventions can "break the cycle" of perpetual inflammation by reactivating the vagus's anti-inflammatory effect on organs [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
