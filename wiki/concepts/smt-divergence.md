# SMT Divergence

## Summary

SMT (Smart Money Technique) Divergence is an ICT tool that compares correlated instruments to spot non-confirmation as a hint of smart-money activity, but ICT explicitly limits its role: it must only confirm an established directional idea, never serve as a primary signal [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]]; TTrades operationalizes this constraint by including SMT as a named variation of his Models 1–4 — substituted for the upstream liquidity-grab leg only after the HTF POI premise is set, never as the entry trigger itself [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]]; Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023) provides the mechanical definition of SMT for both correlated and inversely correlated pairs, framing the crack in correlation as a "footprint of smart money and institutional accumulation" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- ICT teaches that SMT divergence is used to confirm an idea that was already established; by itself, SMT divergence doesn't mean anything [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- ICT reports having blown accounts by looking only for divergences without an underlying narrative — a cautionary anecdote attached to the rule [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- The trader must have a narrative explaining why a divergence would form before treating it as actionable [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- SMT sits inside the broader ICT confirmation stack alongside daily directional bias, presence of an order block, equal lows/highs as liquidity, and fib-level confluence [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- "SMT" is one of two named variations of every TTrades Models 1–4 setup (the other being Failed swing), diagrammed for both bullish and bearish directions [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- In the SMT variation of TTrades's models, the HTF POI premise, the BOS + displacement + FVG entry trigger, and the FVG-in-discount/premium qualification are unchanged — SMT divergence between correlated instruments substitutes for the LTF liquidity-grab confirmation [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- TTrades's placement of SMT as a substitute for liquidity-grab confirmation — rather than as a substitute for the HTF POI or BOS — is consistent with ICT's rule that SMT must confirm, not initiate [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Per Ali Khan, the algorithms that dictate the price of asset classes all work synergistically with one another — if one asset class is correlated with another, price fluctuations will have an impact on its correlated asset [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, for correlated pairs: if pair 1 makes a lower low (running sellside liquidity) but correlated pair 2 makes a higher low (failing to run its sellside liquidity), pair 2 shows strength and unwillingness to take its sellside — this divergence is a "footprint of smart money and institutional accumulation" indicating pair 1 is only going lower to take sellside liquidity before repricing higher with institutional sponsorship [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, for inversely correlated pairs: if pair 1 makes a higher low (failing to run sellside) while inversely correlated pair 2 makes a higher high (running buyside liquidity), pair 1 shows strength — the crack in correlation indicates pair 2 is only going higher to take buyside liquidity before repricing lower [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, the reverse logic applies for sellside delivery (bullish pair 1 with bearish pair 2 divergence) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
