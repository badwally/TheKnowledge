# Market Maker Sell Model (Bearish PO3)

## Summary

The bearish form of ICT's Power of 3 / MMXM setup: price first reprices higher to a higher-timeframe point of interest or external buyside liquidity, then sets up a market maker model that favors the sell-side, after which distribution proceeds to sellside liquidity below [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]]; the canonical MMXM PDF describes the same template as price expansion DOWNWARDS toward sellside liquidity, anchored on an HTF premium of an array, with an impulse move higher, displacement down, market structure shift, and re-distribution in FVGs/OBs targeting offset-distribution at old lows [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- In a bearish PO3, "price will look to reprice higher (HTF POI) before setting up a mmxm that favours the sell-side," which could indicate the start of a reversal [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- An alternative bearish form: in a downtrend, price seeks a premium of an array before continuing the trend lower [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- In both bearish cases, price manipulates higher seeking a premium of an array OR external liquidity, then sets up the market maker sell model to the downside [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- A worked example referenced January 17, 2023 price action: a clear accumulation phase, followed by manipulation up to take out buyside liquidity, with price then preparing for the distribution phase down (overnight) to seek sellside liquidity [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- Entry is taken once liquidity has been taken and there is a market structure shift in the direction of price distribution [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- The MMXM PDF describes a MMSM as price expansion DOWNWARDS towards sellside liquidity, starting with an impulse move higher into a HTF premium of an array where sellers distribute their positions [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, in the MMSM price displaces and creates a market structure shift DOWN — shorts position for a bigger move down while allowing longs to get out, and this model "likes to setup in a premium of a PD Array" [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, as price retraces back up after the MSS it enters supportive structure of the trend down toward the draw on liquidity, with FVGs and order blocks acting as the re-distribution zones where smart money shorts [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the move down terminates at offset-distribution — where sellers are absorbed by buyers in liquidity, at old lows where old longs are positioned with their stops — and from there price may base and continue the trend or reverse [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, retracements into FVGs are where shorts re-distribute, and OTE plus the "silver bullet" 2nd entry are the canonical entries inside the MMSM [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
