# Equities Tale of Two Halves (2023)

## Summary

Credit Suisse's framing of equity-market expectations for 2023 as a tale of two halves: markets first focused on the 'higher rates for longer' theme leading to muted equity performance and continued volatility in H1 as slower growth hits earnings, with bond/equity correlation expected to break down again as inflation peaks and monetary policy normalizes [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].

## Key claims

- 2023 is framed as a tale of two halves for equity markets [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Markets are likely to first focus on the 'higher rates for longer' theme, which should lead to a muted equity performance [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Equity markets could still be volatile in the first half of 2023 as slower economic growth hits company earnings [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Sectors and regions with stable earnings and low leverage are favored in the volatile period [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- The performance of bonds and equities should again diverge in 2023, after the correlated 2022 in which both fell together [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
