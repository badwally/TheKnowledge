# Flexible-Route Cycling Tour

## Summary

A flexible-route cycling tour is a multi-day, vehicle-supported guided holiday format in which each day offers multiple distance and elevation options — typically Easier, Classic, and Challenge — so mixed-ability groups can ride the same itinerary at different volumes without splitting up; [[entities/marmot-tours|Marmot Tours]]'s Classic Cols range is built around this format [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].

## Key claims

- Each riding day publishes 2–3 alternative routes with different distance and ascent — e.g., on the [[entities/corsican-highlights|Corsican Highlights]] holiday, Day 2 offers Easier 90 km / +900 m, Classic 105 km / +1,300 m, and Challenge 138 km / +2,300 m versions of the same St-Florent → Calvi stage [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Riders self-select the daily route based on how they feel — Marmot frames this as a feature for couples and mixed-ability groups, since each rider can 'get out of it what you want by choosing the daily route for you' [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- The format is non-competitive by design: Marmot states there is 'no pressure to keep up with anyone else' and that the quickest way to injure yourself or burn out is to ride faster than feels comfortable [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Two on-bike guides plus support vans cover the route, positioning at 'easy to miss' junctions and offering a lift up the hill on request — making the route format work without forcing riders into a single group pace [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Navigation is delivered via a tour app (maps, altitude profiles, safety notes, RWGPS turn-by-turn) plus .fit and .tcx GPS files for personal devices, so each rider can follow their chosen variant independently [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- The format is the operator's recommended on-ramp for road cyclists new to the mountains: introductory destinations such as the Pyrenees, Tenerife, Cévennes & Ardèche, and Sardinia use the same flexible-route structure with a less demanding Classic route [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
