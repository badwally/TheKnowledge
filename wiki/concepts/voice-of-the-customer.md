# Voice of the Customer

## Summary

Voice of the Customer (VoC) at Orita is the structured extraction of customer signal — sentiment, objections, persona-segmented language, product feedback — from the company's centrally-recorded Avoma transcript corpus, surfaced via a Claude Code agent on a regular cadence [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].

## Key claims

- VoC is one of Adrian's two top-priority agent buildouts (alongside competitor positioning) because it is "immediately useful" — directly queryable to answer questions like "what are customers saying about our SMS product" or "what are customers saying about Orita versus a specific competitor" [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
- The primary substrate is the Avoma transcript and video corpus, which captures every customer and sales call at Orita [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
- Persona segmentation matters: agency-side respondents and brand-side respondents can produce divergent signal that should be tracked separately [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
- VoC is conceptually adjacent to but distinct from competitive positioning intelligence; both draw on the same internal-call substrate but answer different strategic questions [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
- Aaron's podcast transcripts are a secondary, lower-priority VoC substrate compared to internal Avoma meetings [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
