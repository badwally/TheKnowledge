# Tournament-Win Flow Response

## Summary

The empirical finding from Lu, Mortal, and Ray (2020) that hedge fund investors deploy significantly more capital to managers after they cash in poker tournaments, with average monthly net inflows 1.2–1.8% higher than matched peers in the year following a tournament win — an effect concentrated in cases with media coverage, larger wins, and higher-profile tournament series [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].

## Key claims

- Fund managers experience an economically and statistically significant increase in net inflows after cashing in a poker tournament compared to matched peers [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Average monthly inflows in the year following a tournament win are 1.2–1.8% higher than average net inflows to matched peers who do not experience poker tournament success [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Increases in net inflows are concentrated in subsamples of managers who have media coverage of their poker playing (1.9–2.0% increase in monthly net inflows) [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Increases are also concentrated for managers who win cash prizes higher than the median tournament win — i.e., who place better in tournaments, win in larger tournaments, or cash in tournaments with higher buy-ins (1.5–1.8% increase in monthly net inflows) [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Managers who win in the World Series of Poker (WSOP) or World Poker Tour (WPT) — higher-profile, more likely televised tournaments — experience particularly large flow increases (1.9–3.5% increase in monthly net inflows) [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- The pattern is consistent with more visible and more difficult tournament wins being rewarded with higher flows from investors [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Whether the flow response is driven by investors proactively considering fund managers' poker skills or by managers incorporating poker success into marketing and networking opportunities is ambiguous, but the link between tournament success and subsequent fund flows is clear [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
