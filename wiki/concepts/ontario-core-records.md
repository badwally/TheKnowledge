# Ontario "Core Record" Statutory Definition (O. Reg. 48/01)

## Summary

A "core record" of an Ontario condominium corporation is a statutorily enumerated record type that the corporation must hold and produce on request under O. Reg. 48/01 made under the Condominium Act, 1998. The regulation enumerates 10 categories of core records spanning governance instruments (declaration, by-laws, rules), financial documents (current budget and amendments, most recent board-approved financial statements, most recent auditor's report), the board's current reserve fund funding plan, statutory disclosure certificates (periodic information certificates), meeting minutes within a 12-month lookback window, and any additional record types a corporation's by-law designates as a core record [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key claims

- "Core record" is defined in O. Reg. 48/01 as any of 10 enumerated record types of a condominium corporation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 1: a copy of the declaration, by-laws, and rules of the corporation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 2: an agreement mentioned in section 113 or subsection 154(5) of the Condominium Act entered into by or on behalf of the corporation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 3: the budget for the corporation's current fiscal year and all amendments, if any, made to that budget [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 4: the most recent financial statements that the board has approved under subsection 66(3) of the Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 5: the most recent auditor's report presented to the audit committee or to the board under subsection 67(6) of the Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 6: the current plan proposed by the board under subsection 94(8) of the Act for future funding of the reserve fund [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 7: the records that the corporation is required to maintain under subsection 46.1(3) or 83(3) of the Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 8: all periodic information certificates that the corporation, within the 12-month period before receiving a request for records or a requester's response, sent to the owners under section 26.3 of the Act or was required by that section to send to the owners [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 9: minutes of meetings of owners or meetings of the board held after section 51 of Schedule 1 to the Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 comes into force and within the 12-month period before the corporation receives a request for records or a requester's response [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Category 10: any other record that a by-law of the corporation specifies as a core record [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Core records are subject to the "request for records" regime — a request to examine or obtain copies of records under subsection 55(3) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The "board's response" to a request for records is defined under subsection 13.3(6) of the regulation, and the "requester's response" is defined under subsection 13.3(11) [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The "fee payable for the request" in connection with a request for records is defined as the fee described in paragraph 4 of subsection 13.3(7) [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The 12-month lookback window for periodic information certificates (Category 8) and meeting minutes (Category 9) means a corporation's core-record obligations are time-bounded and update continuously with the calendar [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
