# Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)

## Summary

The Optimal Trade Entry, abbreviated OTE in ICT terminology, is a Fibonacci-retracement-based execution zone defined by the 0.62–0.79 retracement levels of a chosen price swing; per Trader Theory's recap of ICT's 2022 mentorship, this band represents the Discount zone in a bullish bias and the Premium zone in a bearish bias and is where the trader looks to engage continuation after manipulation [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]]; TTrades adopts the same .62–.79 zone as the price refinement layered on top of his Model 1 and Model 2 trigger stacks, producing Models 3 and 4 [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]]; the canonical MMXM PDF places OTE alongside the "silver bullet" 2nd entry as the two canonical entries inside the re-accumulation (MMBM) or re-distribution (MMSM) leg of the model [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- Defined by Trader Theory as "a optimal entry for a trade. Put a fib over a price swing and the OTE is between 0.62 - 0.79 fib levels (showing the Discount or Premium)" [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- The OTE band is interpreted via Premium / Discount Arrays — one of the two ingredients (alongside Time) in the Time + Price = Optimal Setups equation Trader Theory uses to summarize ICT [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Functions as the price-side counterpart to the time-side filters (NY 8:30 / 9:30 opens, day-of-week, NFP / FOMC blackouts) that gate when an entry can be taken [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- TTrades's Model 3 (HTF + BOS + FVG + OTE) is explicitly framed as "Model 1 + OTE," adding the .62–.79 fib refinement on top of the standard HTF/BOS/FVG trigger stack to qualify the FVG In Discount (long) or FVG In Premium (short) entry [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- TTrades's Model 4 (HTF + BOS + IDM + FVG + OTE) is explicitly framed as "Model 2 + OTE," combining the Internal Liquidity (IDM) sweep with the .62–.79 fib refinement [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- In TTrades's framework, OTE is layered after the LTF BOS + displacement + FVG entry trigger has fired, and serves to refine the FVG into a discount-zone or premium-zone subset rather than to replace any other ingredient [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, OTE is one of two canonical entries inside both MMBM and MMSM expansions, paired with a "silver bullet" 2nd entry on retracements into FVGs [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the OTE is read from inside the model's range of opportunity — defined as "from the point of start of the model to the end at the target liquidity" — and is taken in re-accumulation (MMBM) or re-distribution (MMSM) FVGs/OBs [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
