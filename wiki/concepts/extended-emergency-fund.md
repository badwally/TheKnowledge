# Extended Emergency Fund for Traders (6–12 Months)

## Summary

Personal-finance rule of thumb articulated by TraderEdge's Alex: traders should hold 6–12 months of living expenses in an emergency savings account — double the standard 3–6 months — because trading is "one of the riskiest jobs in the world (financially speaking)" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key claims

- Most finance gurus recommend keeping 3–6 months of living expenses in an emergency savings account [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- TraderEdge's Alex argues traders should instead set aside 6–12 months of expenses, citing that trading is "one of the riskiest jobs in the world (financially speaking)" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The reserve is presented as one of three pillars of trader personal finance, alongside paying yourself 50% of monthly profits and the 80/20 expense rule [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
