# Data Union

## Summary

A data union is a proposed collective data-governance mechanism that adapts the labour-union model of collective bargaining to data subjects, allowing members to negotiate jointly over the terms on which their data is supplied to data buyers [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].

## Key claims

- Vincent, Prewitt and Li (2025) extend the data-union concept from data subjects to information producers — journalists, researchers, and creative professionals — arguing they need an analogous collective bargaining capacity vis-à-vis AI product builders [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- The paper frames coalition-style organizing (guilds or syndicates of information producers represented by trusted intermediaries) as the producer-side response to AI-driven capital concentration [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- Federated data management tools and explainable data value estimations are proposed as the technical infrastructure that makes data-union-style bargaining over information goods tractable [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
