# Community Association Banking division

## Summary

A Community Association Banking division is a dedicated business unit within a US chartered depository institution focused on serving homeowner association (HOA) and condominium clients with segment-specific deposit, lockbox, fraud-prevention, software-integration, and customer-service offerings [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. Pacific Premier Bank's Community Association Banking division — preserved intact through the August 31, 2025 Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger and adopted by the post-merger Columbia Bank entity — is the source-of-record instance of this organizational pattern, characterized by no-fee bank accounts, easy signature card updates, check lockbox processing, fraud prevention mechanisms, Smartwebs software integrations, competitive rates, and customer service [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key claims

- A Community Association Banking division is a bank business unit dedicated to serving HOA and condominium clients nationwide [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Pacific Premier Bank's Community Association Banking division supported HOA clients with no-fee bank accounts, easy signature card updates, check lockbox processing, fraud prevention mechanisms, visibility through software integrations, competitive rates, and customer service [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The Columbia Bank post-merger entity will adopt Pacific Premier's Community Association Banking division, retaining its leadership and staff to ensure continuity of service [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The combined Columbia Bank team is planning to invest in further enhancing the API technology that connects the Community Association Banking division to Smartwebs software and other resources [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Continuity through M&A of a Community Association Banking division — preserving accounts, routing numbers, signature cards, lockbox addresses, and software integrations — is the explicit promise to HOA customers in the Pacific Premier–Umpqua–Columbia transition [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
