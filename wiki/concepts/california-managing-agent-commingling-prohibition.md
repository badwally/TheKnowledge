# California Managing-Agent Commingling Prohibition (Civil Code § 5380(b)(3), (d))

## Summary

California Civil Code § 5380 imposes a two-pronged anti-commingling regime on managing agents of common interest development associations. § 5380(b)(3) requires that, where funds are deposited in a board-directed account under § 5380(b), those funds be kept separate, distinct, and apart from the funds belonging to the managing agent or to any other person for whom the managing agent holds funds in trust [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]]. § 5380(d) imposes a broader, freestanding prohibition: the managing agent shall not commingle the funds of the association with the managing agent's own money or with the money of others that the managing agent receives or accepts [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

## Key claims

- § 5380(b)(3) requires funds deposited in a board-directed § 5380(b) account to be kept separate, distinct, and apart from the funds belonging to the managing agent or to any other person for whom the managing agent holds funds in trust [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(d) prohibits the managing agent from commingling the funds of the association with the managing agent's own money or with the money of others that the managing agent receives or accepts [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The § 5380(d) anti-commingling prohibition operates independently of the § 5380(b) board-directed-deposit framework; it applies regardless of whether the funds are held in a trust fund account under § 5380(a) or a board-directed account under § 5380(b) [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The prevailing party in an action to enforce § 5380 — including its anti-commingling provisions — is entitled to recover reasonable legal fees and court costs under § 5380(e) [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The managing agent must maintain a separate record of the receipt and disposition of all association funds, including any interest earned, per § 5380(c) — operationalizing the anti-commingling regime via an explicit recordkeeping duty [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
