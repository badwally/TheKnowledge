# Anti-Inflammatory Diet for Long COVID

## Summary

Dietary protocol used by Dr. Carla Kuon in the UCSF Osher Center's Long COVID Group Medical Visits as the first-line intervention for the inflammation "bucket" of post-viral fatigue; presented as the umbrella framework into which two further patient-specific modifications — lectin elimination and a low-histamine diet — are nested when indicated [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].

## Key claims

- Adoption of an anti-inflammatory diet is described as the first step in resolving Long COVID symptoms [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- The protocol is supported with practical produce-selection guidance via the Environmental Working Group's "Dirty Dozen and Clean 15" lists (EWG.org) and the UCSF nutrition reference page osher.ucsf.edu/putting-nutrition-practice-tips-making-dietary-changes [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Two patient-specific modifications layer on top of the base diet: Modification #1 is lectin elimination (for autoimmunity and gut disorders), and Modification #2 is a low-histamine diet (for frequent rashes, elevated plasma histamine/tryptase, or mast cell activation) [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Session 2 extends the anti-inflammatory dietary frame with botanical therapies and correction of nutrient deficiencies (Vitamin B2, Ferritin, Vitamin D, Vitamin A, Zinc, Copper, etc., per the required intake panel) [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
