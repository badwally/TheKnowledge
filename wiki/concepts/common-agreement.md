# TEFCA Common Agreement

## Summary

The Common Agreement is the legal contract signed by every Qualified Health Information Network (QHIN) participating in TEFCA; it codifies the shared obligations — including privacy, security, and interoperability requirements — that hold the network-of-networks together. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]

## Key claims

- The Common Agreement is the legal contract signed by all QHINs as part of TEFCA. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- The Common Agreement is one of three foundational TEFCA documents, alongside the Trusted Exchange Framework (TEF) and the QHIN Technical Framework (QTF). [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- The Common Agreement was first published in January 2022 and updated in November 2023. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- The Recognized Coordinating Entity (RCE) — currently The Sequoia Project — develops, updates, implements, and maintains the Common Agreement. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- QTF requirements are updated periodically and incorporated by reference into the Common Agreement, so changes to the technical framework propagate to QHIN obligations via the legal contract. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- The Common Agreement defines a uniform set of data privacy and security requirements applicable to participating networks and IT developers regardless of HIPAA-covered-entity status. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- Signing the Common Agreement is one of the prerequisites for becoming a QHIN, alongside being a US entity and completing the QHIN application, onboarding, and designation process. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
