# Association Contract Bidding Requirements

## Summary

Florida regulates competitive bidding for condominium association contracts and the carve-out for employees through Florida Administrative Code Rule 61B-23.0022, in its current codified form since 4/1/1992 [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]]. This is the procurement-discipline layer of FL condo governance and is directly relevant to any vendor-management or work-order workflow that originates from association funds.

## Key claims

- FAC 61B-23.0022 establishes the bidding requirements for association contracts and codifies the employee carve-out, distinguishing routine staffing from contracted services [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- The rule has been in its current codified form since 4/1/1992, making it one of the longest-standing operational rules in Chapter 61B-23 [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
