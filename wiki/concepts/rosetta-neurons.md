# Rosetta Neurons

## Summary

Rosetta Neurons are individual neurons identified by Dravid et al. (2023) that are activated by the same patterns across a range of vision models, forming a common dictionary independently discovered by all models [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].

## Key claims

- The finding extends earlier kernel-level results on cross-model similarity (e.g., Moschella et al. 2022) down to the granularity of single neurons [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
- Huh et al. cite Rosetta Neurons as evidence of representational convergence at fine granularity — different vision models independently arrive at the same set of feature detectors [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
