# Coupled heat and moisture transport

## Summary

Coupled heat and moisture transport refers to the simultaneous, mutually dependent transfer of thermal energy and moisture through a material. It is the physical phenomenon whose reliable estimation underpins durability analysis of building structures.

## Key claims

- To assess the durability of structures, heat and moisture transport need to be analyzed [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- Heat and moisture transfer is modelled in a coupled setting, rather than as two independent phenomena [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- Reliable estimation of heat and moisture distribution in a structure depends on including all available information about loading conditions and material parameters, with credibility evaluations attached [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
