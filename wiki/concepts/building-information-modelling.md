# Building Information Modelling (BIM)

## Summary

Building Information Modelling (BIM) is a data-rich digital representation of a built asset; the 2025 ITcon paper by Porto Oliveira and Dominguez Sotelino uses an 'enriched BIM model' as the integration substrate for SHM data and ANN-derived damage assessments to support data-driven viaduct management.

## Key claims

- BIM and SHM have independently advanced viaduct management, but their integration is still underexplored [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The 2025 ITcon framework integrates SHM, BIM, and ANNs into an enriched BIM model that centralizes data for efficient visualization and decision-making [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The enriched BIM model is positioned as the user-facing surface through which asset managers can make data-driven decisions, overcoming the limitations of traditional visual inspections [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The authors propose to provide an intuitive BIM-based user interface for asset managers as part of the framework [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
