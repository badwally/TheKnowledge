# Florida Condominium Board Composition (Default Rules)

## Summary

Florida Statute §718.112(2)(a) sets the default composition of the board of administration of a Florida condominium association when the bylaws are silent. The default is a five-member board for most condominiums, reduced to a not-fewer-than-three-member board only when the condominium has five or fewer units and the association is a not-for-profit corporation. Officer roles default to president, secretary, and treasurer with corporate-customary duties, and absent contrary bylaws officers and board members serve without compensation and at the pleasure of the board [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].

## Key claims

- Absent a bylaw provision describing the form of administration, the board of administration is composed of five members, unless the condominium has five or fewer units [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- For condominiums with five or fewer units that are not-for-profit corporations, the board shall consist of not fewer than three members [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Absent contrary bylaw provisions, the board of administration shall have a president, a secretary, and a treasurer, who perform the duties customarily performed by officers of corporations [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Unless prohibited in the bylaws, the board may appoint other officers and grant them the duties it deems appropriate [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Unless otherwise provided in the bylaws, the officers shall serve without compensation and at the pleasure of the board of administration [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Unless otherwise provided in the bylaws, the members of the board shall serve without compensation [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The bylaws shall describe the form of administration, indicating the title of the officers and board of administration and specifying the powers, duties, manner of selection and removal, and compensation, if any [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
