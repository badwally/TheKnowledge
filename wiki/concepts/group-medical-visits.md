# Group Medical Visits

## Summary

A care-delivery format in which a clinician convenes patients in a series of group sessions to deliver a structured curriculum; used by Dr. Carla Kuon to provide Long COVID patients with a holistic plan to promote recovery, organized as a 3-visit series spanning anti-inflammatory diet, nature/nutrients, and mind/body interventions [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- Dr. Kuon's program — "Long COVID Care with Dr. Carla Kuon" — is delivered as group medical visits for post-viral recovery [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- The 3-visit series provides patients with a holistic plan to promote recovery from Long COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Part 1: The Anti-Inflammatory Diet, Lectins, and Histamine — recommends foods, herbs, and supplements that can boost the effectiveness of conventional therapies and counter inflammatory effects of COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Part 2: Using Nature and Nutrients to Heal — reviews what is known and not known about botanical therapies and how correcting nutrient deficiencies can help the body heal from Long COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Part 3: Beyond Traditional Medicine — covers when to consider complementary and alternative therapies and mind/body interventions, focusing on stress reduction, vagal toning exercises, and the concept of pacing in graduated exercise [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
