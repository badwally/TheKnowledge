# Evaluation Skill

## Summary

A new research skill—assessing AI-generated candidate predictions—that becomes increasingly valuable as machine prediction improves. Identified as the primary mechanism behind heterogeneous returns from AI in materials R&D [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].

## Key claims

- AI automates 57% of "idea-generation" tasks in materials discovery, reallocating scientists to evaluating model-produced candidate materials [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Time spent assessing candidate materials increases by 74% after AI introduction [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Differential evaluation skill explains the tool's heterogeneous treatment effects: top scientists prioritize promising candidates while others waste resources on false positives [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Assessing model predictions complements AI technologies and is the new bottleneck skill in AI-augmented research [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- 83% of scientists fired during the lab's workforce adjustment in the final month of the sample were in the bottom quartile of judgment ability [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- The lab more than offset these departures through increased hiring of strong evaluators, expanding its workforce on net [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
