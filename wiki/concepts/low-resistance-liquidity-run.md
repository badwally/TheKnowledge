# Low Resistance Liquidity Run

## Summary

In ICT (Inner Circle Trader) price-action analysis, a "Low Resistance Liquidity Run" is a price move toward a liquidity area with little resistance in its path — classically just under an old high or above an old low — and is most often associated with sharp reactions to economic news releases [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]]; the canonical MMXM PDF gives the complementary structural definition: structure should support the direction of price and not oppose it (it should not provide resistance to your narrative), and treats this condition as a precondition for any valid MMXM model setup [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- A Low Resistance Liquidity Run is when price has little resistance on its way running to an area of Liquidity [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- This pattern is classically seen just under an old high or above an old low [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- Price will move quickly normally on the release of an economic news release [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, a low-resistance liquidity run requires that structure support the direction of price and not oppose it — "it should not provide resistance to your narrative" [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMXM model is valid only when the draw on liquidity is paired with a low-resistance run; the different draws on liquidity provide a possible narrative, while market structure is what guides the trader's reading of HTF price action [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
