# Higher Time Frame Point of Interest

## Summary

A higher-time-frame point of interest (HTF POI) is, in the ICT-derived order-block playbook, a level identified from a higher time frame (e.g., the daily chart) that price is expected to interact with before a higher-probability setup can form; it is the first of three conditions the lesson requires for a high-probability OB trade [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].

## Key claims

- An HTF POI is identified from a higher time frame such as the daily chart — "we have our HTF PoI from the daily chart (will review this in future lessons)" [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- Higher-probability order blocks occur under three conditions, the first of which is that price enters an HTF POI; the other two are a liquidity grab and displacement with mss/imbalance [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- HTF order blocks are described as more important than lower-time-frame OBs and should always be favoured when looking at the price trend [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- The lesson's summary checklist begins with "HTF POI" as the first input the trader looks for — ahead of liquidity, displacement, MSS, and imbalance/OB/FVG [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- The lesson defers the full mechanics of identifying HTF POIs to a future lesson, treating it here as a precondition rather than the topic of the lesson itself [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
