# Liquidity raid

## Summary

A liquidity raid is a price excursion that takes out external or internal liquidity (resting stops and breakout orders at prior swing points); in the YouTube 2022 model it is the precondition for a market structure shift and a breaker block [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]]. ICT methodology characterizes the raided liquidity itself as the "open interest" of buyers and sellers clustered at or near specific price levels — typically trailed stops from existing positions plus stops from breakout traders [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].

## Key claims

- A run on external or internal liquidity is the first phase of a breaker block setup; the original order block fails because of this raid into key areas followed by a displacement back through them [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- In a bullish breaker block scenario, the liquidity raid takes out a swing low into a pool of liquidity, triggering stop losses of longs while shorts are induced to trade the breakout [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- In a bearish breaker block scenario, the liquidity raid takes out a swing high into a pool of liquidity, triggering stop losses of shorts while longs are induced to trade the breakout [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- After the liquidity raid, price reverses to set up the trade in the opposite direction off the breaker block [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- "Identify KEY liquidity that is being taken" is the first item on the YouTube 2022 model review checklist [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- Liquidity is defined as the "open interest" of buyers and sellers in the market and is further specified by the entities at or near specific price levels [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- A low under the current market will typically have trailed sell stops under it from long trades, plus sell stops from traders who wish to trade a breakout lower for a short position — these are the resting orders a downward run on liquidity targets [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Runs on liquidity are described as seeking to pair orders with the pending order liquidity sitting outside the trading range [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
