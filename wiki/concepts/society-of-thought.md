# Society of Thought

## Summary

A term coined by Evans, Bratton, and Agüera y Arcas (2026) for the internal multi-agent-like deliberation that frontier reasoning models simulate within their own chain of thought — distinct cognitive perspectives that argue, question, verify, and reconcile — and which the authors argue causally accounts for these models' accuracy advantage on hard reasoning tasks [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key claims

- Frontier reasoning models such as DeepSeek-R1 and QwQ-32B do not improve simply by "thinking longer"; instead they simulate complex, multi-agent-like interactions within their own chain of thought, which the authors term a "society of thought" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- These models spontaneously generate internal debates among distinct cognitive perspectives that argue, question, verify, and reconcile [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- This conversational structure causally accounts for the models' accuracy advantage on hard reasoning tasks, which the authors demonstrate by explicitly priming and amplifying multi-party conversation [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The behavior is emergent: none of these models were trained to produce societies of thought, yet when reinforcement learning is used to reward base models solely for reasoning accuracy, they spontaneously increase conversational, multi-perspective behaviors [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The authors interpret this as models rediscovering, through optimization pressure alone, what centuries of epistemology and decades of cognitive science have suggested: that robust reasoning is a social process even when it occurs within a single mind [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Today's reasoning models produce a single conversation — described as "an AI town hall transcript" — but effective groups exhibit hierarchy, specialization, division of labor, and structured disagreement, suggesting next-generation systems should support multiple parallel, converging, and diverging streams of deliberation [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- In such next-generation systems, brainstorming, devil's advocacy, and constructive conflict should be designed features rather than accidental emergent properties [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The toolkits of team science, small-group sociology, and social psychology are positioned as blueprints for next-generation reasoning-model development [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
