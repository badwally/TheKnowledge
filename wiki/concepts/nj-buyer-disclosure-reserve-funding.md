# NJ Buyer Disclosure Requirements for Reserve Funding

## Summary

S3992 (August 2025) added a new pre-sale buyer-disclosure obligation to NJ law: before a buyer completes the sale of a unit within a planned real estate development, the seller must provide the buyer with the most recent unit-owner notice regarding the association's reduced capital reserve funding plan [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]]. The obligation falls on the seller (not the association), and the disclosed document is the 20-point-bold-font notice generated when an association elects the 85% funding option.

## Key claims

- The disclosure obligation falls on the seller, not the association [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Disclosure must occur before the buyer completes the sale of a unit within the planned real estate development [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The disclosed document is the most recent notice to unit owners regarding the reduced capital reserve funding plan [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Before S3992, the law did not mandate notice requirements; sellers did not have to provide disclosures related to capital reserve funding [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The disclosure links the 85% funding option's owner-notice obligation to the resale-transaction stream, ensuring incoming owners are aware of anticipated future special assessments or loans [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
