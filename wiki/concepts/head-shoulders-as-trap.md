# Head & Shoulders Pattern as Market-Maker Trap (ICT)

## Summary

The ICT framing of the classic Head & Shoulders chart pattern as a "false top and bottom" — a pattern that retail traders seek on lower time frames due to a low understanding of market structure, and which therefore frequently fails and goes against the retail position [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].

## Key claims

- The Head & Shoulders pattern represents false tops and bottoms in price [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].
- Due to the low understanding of most retail traders, they seek this pattern on the lower timeframes [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].
- Many times these patterns will fail and go against the retail position [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].
