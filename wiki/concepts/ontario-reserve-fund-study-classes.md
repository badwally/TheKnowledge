# Ontario Reserve Fund Study Classes (Class 1 / 2 / 3)

## Summary

The Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 and its associated regulations classify Reserve Fund Studies into three statutory types — comprehensive (Class 1), updated with site inspection (Class 2), and updated without site inspection (Class 3). The classes are sequenced on a defined cadence after a corporation's first year of operation, and they differ in scope of physical inspection, records review, and stakeholder interviews [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].

## Key claims

- There are three statutory types of reserve fund studies in Ontario: a comprehensive study (Class 1), an updated study with a site inspection (Class 2), and an updated study without a site inspection (Class 3) [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The first reserve fund study a condominium corporation is required to have completed is a comprehensive (Class 1) study, which must be completed within the year following the registration of the corporation's declaration and description [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- A Class 1 study requires the RFS provider to physically visit the property to examine the common elements and assets, conduct a review of records, and hold interviews with the directors, employees, and agents of the condominium corporation [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- A Class 1 study establishes at least a 30-year timeline for projections; the 30-year minimum is legislated, and the CAO Best Practices Guide recommends that boards consider requesting a 45-year-or-longer timeline as best practice [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- A Class 2 study (updated with site inspection) includes a site inspection plus many of the requirements of the comprehensive Class 1 study [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- A Class 3 study (updated without site inspection) does not include a site inspection but does involve a review of records and interviews with the directors, employees, and agents of the condominium corporation [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- After the first Class 1 study is completed, Class 3 and Class 2 studies are done on an alternating basis at least every three years [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The CAO Best Practices Guide presents the required sequence as: Comprehensive Study (within 1 year) → Update of Study (without site inspection, within 3 years or less) → Update of Study (with site inspection, within 3 years or less) [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- Boards should work with their RFS provider to determine what timeline (within the 30-year minimum or beyond) will work best for the corporation, treating the 30-year horizon as a floor rather than a ceiling [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
