# HOAi Agentic AI Workforce (Vantaca)

## Summary

The HOAi agentic AI workforce is the operating model that Vantaca markets around its HOAi platform: AI agents that work inside Vantaca around the clock, handling complex multi-step processes so human CAM team members can focus on proactive, personal service [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]]. The stated growth thesis is that HOAi lets management companies expand portfolio size without proportionally expanding headcount, by routing routine work to AI behind the scenes while keeping each human-customer interaction timely, personal, and attentive [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key claims

- HOAi agents are positioned as a workforce — not a feature — operating inside the Vantaca platform around the clock [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The stated agent scope spans three named functional domains: Resident Support, Accounting, and Community Management [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Resident Support agents handle real-time responses across four named channels: email, chat, forms, and phone [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Accounting agents automate receivables, payables, and financial reporting [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Community Management agents handle board packets, budgets, ARC (architectural review committee), and violations [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The stated business benefit is portfolio growth without headcount growth, by handling routine work behind the scenes [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The product narrative explicitly contrasts "true agentic AI capabilities that transform daily operations" against the framing of "just digitizing old processes" [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The features page does not enumerate the underlying LLM provider, agent framework, tool-use surface, escalation thresholds, or human-in-the-loop checkpoints for the HOAi agents [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
