# Magnificent Seven

## Summary

Market nickname for a small group of seven mega-capitalization U.S. stocks whose 2023 appreciation was responsible for more than half of the S&P 500's 26.3% gain that year — used by Mauboussin and Callahan (2024) as the proximate driver of the recent surge in U.S. stock-market concentration to 27% top-10 weighting [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].

## Key claims

- The appreciation of seven stocks dubbed the "Magnificent Seven" was responsible for more than one-half of the S&P 500's 2023 gain of 26.3% [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Their dominance is the proximate driver of the U.S. top-10 concentration rising to 27% by year-end 2023, up from 14% a decade earlier [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- This is the empirical anchor for several investor concerns Mauboussin and Callahan address: difficulty beating the benchmark when so few stocks drive results, loss of diversification, bubble speculation, and fears that index-fund inflows have mindlessly bid up a handful of stocks [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Nvidia — a computing-infrastructure company benefiting from rising AI spending — reached the top 3 by market capitalization in early 2024, joining the historical short list of top-3 U.S. companies [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
