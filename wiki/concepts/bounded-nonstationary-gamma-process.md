# Bounded Nonstationary Gamma Process (BNGP)

## Summary

A bounded nonstationary gamma process (BNGP) is a deterioration model that imposes an upper bound on a nonstationary gamma process, providing an alternative to bounded transformed gamma process (BTGP) variants for representing infrastructure deterioration subject to physical or managerial limits. BNGP serves as the principal comparison benchmark in the Chen–Yuan (2025) study of unified deterioration modelling.

## Key claims

- BNGP is a bounded variant of the gamma process developed to model infrastructure deterioration processes constrained by physical or managerial limits, sitting alongside the BTGP family as a candidate for unified deterioration modelling [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- BNGP is used as the principal comparator for a newly proposed BTGP in the Chen–Yuan (2025) paper, with qualitative and quantitative comparisons made from both deterioration-modelling and asset-management decision-making perspectives [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- In an empirical study on real-world historical bridge condition data, the BNGP is benchmarked together with six other BTGP alternatives against the newly proposed BTGP; the authors report that results confirm the flexibility and significance of the proposed BTGP relative to the BNGP and the other alternatives [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
