# External range liquidity

## Summary

External range liquidity is the resting buy-side and sell-side liquidity that sits outside the boundaries of the current trading range; ICT methodology identifies it as the target of liquidity-raid moves that pair institutional orders with the pending order liquidity at those levels [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]]; the canonical MMXM PDF reinforces external liquidity as the structural destination of any MMXM expansion — above old highs (where shorts have stops) for an MMBM and below old lows (where longs have stops) for an MMSM — and as the zone where smart money exits positions [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- The current trading range will have buy-side liquidity above the range and sell-side liquidity below the range [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Runs on liquidity seek to pair orders with the pending order liquidity sitting outside the range [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, external liquidity above old highs is where longs exit and shorts have their stops — labeled "offset-distribution" — and it is the structural terminus of the MMBM's move up [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, external liquidity below old lows — where buyers absorb sellers and old longs have their stops — is the structural terminus of the MMSM's move down [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMXM trader's range of opportunity is from the start of the model to the end at the target external liquidity, and external liquidity is the profit-taking destination once smart money has been positioned via re-accumulation or re-distribution [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
