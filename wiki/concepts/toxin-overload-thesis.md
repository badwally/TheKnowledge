# Toxin overload thesis (Kuon)

## Summary

Claim in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Osher Long COVID protocol that environmental toxin burden — "forever chemicals," agricultural petrochemicals, infection-driven loads — combines with inflammation in a self-reinforcing loop, and that recovery requires both reducing exposure and increasing antioxidant intake [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- Environmental toxins are characterized as on the rise, with the EPA and CDC reporting universal presence of "forever chemicals" in human studies [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Since 1999, perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) has been detected in human urine universally, present in surfactants, resins, and plastics [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Forever chemicals are described as having a half-life of 30 or more years, implying about 60 years to clear from the body [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- The CDC has found phthalates and glyphosates (Roundup) in urine [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Agricultural toxins (Roundup, Monsanto) and petrochemicals are described as accumulating in human urine [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Infection and its resolution also create increased toxin loads on the body [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Toxins create inflammation and inflammation creates more toxins, forming a vicious cycle [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Counter-strategy: decrease exposure to toxins and increase antioxidant intake [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Dietary tactics: prioritize organic foods (especially the EWG "dirty dozen"), increase plant polyphenols (berries, cruciferous vegetables), and use spices including turmeric, cumin, clove, ginger, and green tea [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Environmental tactics: regular and infrared sauna for skin elimination, glass over plastic bottles (even BPA-free contains bisphenols), HEPA filtration in the bedroom, ceramic-coated non-stick cookware (Gotham Steel, Wear Ever) over Teflon, and an under-counter reverse osmosis filter (Pure Blue, Zero Water, Brondel, Water Drop) [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Pharmacologic tactic: high doses of glutathione or its precursor NAC [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
