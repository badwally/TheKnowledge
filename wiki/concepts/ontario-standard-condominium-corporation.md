# Ontario Standard Condominium Corporation

## Summary

Under Ontario Regulation 48/01 (General) made under the Condominium Act, 1998, "standard condominium corporation" is a defined term identifying the residual freehold-condominium category that is neither a common elements condominium corporation nor a vacant land condominium corporation. The standard-corporation classification is the default form for most Ontario condominium projects and is the form created by registration of a declaration and description absent contrary characterization [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].

## Key claims

- "Standard condominium corporation" means a freehold condominium corporation that is not a common elements condominium corporation or a vacant land condominium corporation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
- Section 3 of Ontario Regulation 48/01 classifies corporations created before Part II of the Act came into force, and continued as corporations under §178(1) of the Act, as standard condominium corporations — providing the legacy-corporation bridge to the post-Part-II classification taxonomy [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
- The first page of a declaration under §5(1)(c) of the Regulation must contain a statement that the registration of the declaration and description will create a standard condominium corporation, making the classification an explicit registration-time declaration rather than an implicit default [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
- The standard-corporation classification governs whether a corporation falls under the standard reserve-fund and records-disclosure rules versus the common elements or vacant land variants regulated elsewhere in the Act and Regulation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-0bc]].
