# Idea Generation vs Evaluation

## Summary

A task-level decomposition of the research process showing that generative AI in materials science substitutes for the idea-generation step while augmenting the candidate-evaluation step, dramatically reallocating researchers' time [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].

## Key claims

- Pre-AI, scientists spent nearly half their time conceptualizing potential materials; post-AI, this share falls to less than 16% [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- AI automates 57% of "idea-generation" tasks [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Time spent assessing candidate materials rises by 74% [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- The tool has countervailing labor effects: replacing labor in compound design while augmenting labor in the broader discovery process via complementarity with evaluation [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Task categorization was performed by combining rich text data on scientist activities with a large language model [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
