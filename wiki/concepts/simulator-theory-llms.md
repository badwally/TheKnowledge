# Simulator Theory of LLMs

## Summary

The simulator theory of LLMs is the view that large language models work as well as they do because, in the limit, a next-token predictor trained on human-generated text moves beyond memorizing surface-level patterns and starts grokking the underlying generator function of that data — namely, the language networks (and ultimately broader semantic systems) of the human brain [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].

## Key claims

- Universality plus the brain's exquisite efficiency suggests that a next-token predictor trained on human text will, in the limit, leap from surface-pattern memorization to modeling the underlying generator function — i.e., the brain mechanisms that produced the text [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
- The empirical case rests on a three-study arc: Goldstein et al. (2022) ECoG (brain and autoregressive LMs share specific computational mechanisms), a 2024 LLM/Functional-Brain-Network alignment study (sub-groups of artificial neurons mirror FBN organization), and TopoLM (2025) (spatial-smoothness loss elicits cortex-like spatial organization) [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
- Because language is embedded in a broader "semantic system" spread across the cerebral cortex, simulation spills into non-language regions: text-only LLM embeddings of scene captions were found to accurately characterize visual-cortex activity evoked by viewing natural scenes [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
- Basic instruction tuning further improves model-brain alignment with the Default Mode Network and other regions associated with cognitive control, suggesting brain-like sub-networks are latent in pretraining and surfaced (rather than installed) by post-training [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
- Post-training a base model with reinforcement learning can elicit reasoning capabilities that are latent in language data but which give rise to distinct reasoning circuits — additional evidence that simulator-style modeling extends into cognition-related brain functions [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
