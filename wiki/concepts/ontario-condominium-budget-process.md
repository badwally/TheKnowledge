# Ontario Condominium Budget Process

## Summary

The Ontario condominium budget process is structured in the CMRAO Financial Management Practice Guide as a defined eight-step process culminating in board presentation and approval. The budget is the operating-fund analogue to the reserve-fund study cycle — sized to the corporation's contractual and statutory obligations, including the contribution required by the Reserve Fund Study and disclosed in the Notice of Future Funding [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].

## Key claims

- The Practice Guide structures budget preparation and implementation as an eight-step process [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The reserve-fund contribution line in the operating budget is sized using the contribution table from the most recent Notice of Future Funding of the Reserve Fund (NOFF), which summarizes the RFS's required contributions for budgetary purposes [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- If extraordinary expenses are known at budget time, they should be included in the budget [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- If extraordinary expenses arise outside the normal budgeting cycle, the corporation may impose special assessments or levies to cover them [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The Practice Guide provides Appendix A, a list of common income and expense categories, as a reference baseline for budget line items [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The Practice Guide provides Appendix B, a checklist defining what must be included in a budget presentation to the Board of Directors [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
