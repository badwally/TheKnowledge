# HOA §528 Election (Annual Form 1120-H Election)

## Summary

The §528 election is the affirmative, year-by-year act by which a qualifying US homeowners association chooses to be taxed under the special §528 regime — excluding exempt function income from gross income at the cost of a 30% (or 32% for timeshares) flat rate on non-exempt taxable income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. The election is made by filing a properly completed Form 1120-H by the return's due date including extensions; once made, the election cannot be revoked for that year without IRS consent obtained through a ruling request and user fee [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. Because the election is annual, a board may compare its total tax under Form 1120-H against its total tax under Form 1120 each year and file whichever results in the lower tax [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. If an association fails to make the regulatory election timely, Treas. Reg. §301.9100-2 provides an automatic 12-month extension to make the §528 election provided corrective action is taken within 12 months of the due date (including extension) of the return [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].

## Key claims

- The §528 election is made separately for each tax year and must generally be made by the due date, including extensions, of the income tax return [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- The election is made by filing a properly completed Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Once filed, the association cannot revoke the election for that year unless the IRS consents [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- IRS consent to revoke an election is requested by filing a ruling request, accompanied by a user fee, under Rev. Proc. 2025-1 (or any successor) [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- If the association does not elect to use Form 1120-H, it must file the applicable income tax return such as Form 1120 [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- A homeowners association should compare its total tax computed on Form 1120-H with its total tax computed on Form 1120 and may file the form that results in the lowest tax [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- If the homeowners association fails to make the regulatory election to be treated as a homeowners association, it can get an automatic 12-month extension to make the §528 election, provided corrective action is taken within 12 months of the due date (including extension) of the return, per Treas. Reg. §301.9100-2 [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- If the association is tax-exempt under §501(a) it does not file Form 1120-H; if a §501(a) association loses its exempt status, Treas. Reg. §1.528-8(e) governs the consequences [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- A homeowners association files Form 1120-H to take advantage of certain tax benefits, in effect allowing the association to exclude exempt function income from gross income [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
