# Order Block

## Summary

In the market-structure framework taught in Lesson 2: Market Structure, an order block (OB) is a clue left behind by displacement that indicates where and how smart money is positioned — read off the candles that immediately precede a displacement move and marked off with a box on the chart [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]]; the MMXM PDF reframes order blocks as the re-accumulation (in an MMBM) or re-distribution (in an MMSM) zones inside the model where smart money joins the trend toward the higher-timeframe draw on liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]]; Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023) adds a detailed bullish OB definition: a down-close candle that runs sellside liquidity into an HTF support level, validated once price closes above its high, with a higher-quality signal when a FVG sits directly above (displacement), and the most sensitive retracement area being the high to the mean threshold (50%) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- Lesson 2 defines an order block as a clue that is left behind by displacement to indicate where and how smart money is positioned [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- Recognizing an order block requires understanding how candles are positioned at the moment of displacement, then marking the relevant pre-displacement candles with a box [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- A simplifying rule offered in Lesson 2: "the direction of displacement tells you whether it's bullish or bearish" [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- Order blocks showcase where sellers are going short and where buyers are going long — green candles before displacement down mark sellers; red candles before displacement up mark buyers [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- Lesson 2 frames the trader's stop-loss placement around the order block: stops go at the most recent swing high (or low) which often coincides with the order-block boundary [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The lesson explicitly notes the equivalence to supply-and-demand-trader vocabulary: bearish order blocks are supply zones (resistance), bullish order blocks are demand zones (support) [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- Lesson 2 ties an order-block invalidation rule to direction: if the trade direction is wrong, the order blocks left behind will be taken out quickly with displacement in the opposite direction [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- Lesson 2 flags that a more detailed treatment of order blocks is reserved for a later lesson; the lesson-2 takeaway is only that a large displacement which forms an imbalance leaves behind order blocks [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The MMXM PDF describes order blocks as one of the two add-into-trend zones (alongside FVGs) used during the re-accumulation (MMBM) or re-distribution (MMSM) leg of the model [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, in an MMBM the trader is "adding into internal liquidity FVG's and orderblocks" and selling for profit at external liquidity; the same logic applies in reverse for an MMSM [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, order blocks form during the displacement that follows the manipulation phase and are the structural bookmarks that smart money returns to in order to join the trend toward the HTF draw on liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per Ali Khan, a bullish order block is a down-close candle (or consecutive down-close candles) that run sellside liquidity below an old low [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, the down-close candle(s) must come into a higher-timeframe support level [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, the down-close candle(s) become a valid order block once price closes above the order block's high [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, a higher-quality order block will have a FVG directly above it — indicating displacement [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, when price returns back down to the order block, the most sensitive area will be the high to the mean threshold (50%) — the trader does not want to see price violate the mean threshold [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
