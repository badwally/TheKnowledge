# Bearish SMT divergence

## Summary

A bearish SMT (Smart Money Technique) divergence is the configuration in which one sibling instrument makes a higher high at a higher-timeframe point of interest while its correlated sibling fails to do so, favouring the lagging sibling to the downside once displacement and a market-structure shift confirm [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].

## Key claims

- Bearish SMT divergence template: $ES makes a higher high at the HTF PoI while $NQ fails to do so, favouring $NQ to the downside [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- Bearish daily-chart example given by the lesson: $ES manages to take out liquidity and put in a significantly higher high in comparison to $NQ — a bearish divergence favouring $NQ to the downside (although $ES can be taken too) [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- The pattern is one of two SMT divergence variants; confirmation comes from displacement and market structure, not from the higher-high asymmetry alone [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
