# Compounding small accounts

## Summary

Compounding small accounts is the ICT Month 2 framing for growing modest balances without high risk: avoid rushing large gains, accept that small trades compound, and target modest repeatable weekly outcomes that produce meaningful monthly compounding [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].

## Key claims

- Small accounts grow by avoiding four common errors: rushing to make massive gains, opening up to large risk, assuming small trades won't grow the account, and poor planning [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- The corresponding aims are favorable RR, explicit respect for the risk side over the reward, and trades with 3:1 RR or higher [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- The recommended initial target for a small account is 20 pips per week with 1.5% risk per trade at 1:1 RR, producing roughly 6% compounded per month [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- The "half-size recovery" rule mitigates initial losses: after a 1% loss, the next trade is taken at half size; once 2R is reached the prior loss is recovered, and at 3R the account is in profit — illustrating the importance of 1:3 RR trades [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- The Month 2 setup-localization recipe is to focus on the daily chart and locate an orderblock, framing the daily-chart orderblock as the home of the low-risk setups appropriate to small-account compounding [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
