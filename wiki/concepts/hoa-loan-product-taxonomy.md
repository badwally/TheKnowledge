# HOA Loan Product Taxonomy

## Summary

The HOA loan product taxonomy describes the canonical three-archetype structure observed in US community-association banking lending product lines: a Quick-Term Loan (rapid-underwriting fixed-rate financing for small-to-mid capital projects), a Traditional Term Loan (longer-tenor fixed-rate financing for major capital projects with no upper bound), and a Revolving Line of Credit (demand-note variable-rate working capital for smaller or recurring expenses). First Citizens Bank's HOA Lending Solutions product line documents the archetype with explicit per-product parameters across rate, term, loan size, fees, prepayment treatment, and a uniform 25-unit minimum association size [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].

## Key claims

- US community-association lenders organize HOA financing into three product archetypes — standard term loans, revolving lines of credit, and quick-term loans — sold side-by-side on the same product page rather than as substitutes [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Quick-Term Loans target small-to-mid capital projects with fixed rates, terms of 5, 7, or 10 years, no minimum loan amount, and (at First Citizens) a $3M maximum cap [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Quick-Term Loans differentiate from traditional term loans on underwriting speed — First Citizens advertises a 3-business-day average approval time versus 10 business days for standard term — at the cost of a hard maximum loan cap [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Traditional Term Loans target major capital projects with fixed rates, 5 to 15 year terms, a $100K minimum, and no maximum loan amount; documentation and legal fees scale up to $5,000 reflecting larger underwriting complexity [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Revolving Lines of Credit target working capital and smaller recurring expenses with a variable rate (First Citizens prices at WSJ Prime + 1% with a 5% floor), a demand-note structure (no fixed term), a $25K minimum, and a $250K maximum [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- A 25-unit minimum eligibility threshold applies uniformly across all three product archetypes at First Citizens Bank, signaling a shared underwriting floor on association size rather than product-specific minimums [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Prepayment penalty structure varies by archetype: 1% on both Quick-Term and Traditional Term Loans at First Citizens; 0% on Revolving Lines of Credit, consistent with the LOC's demand-note structure [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Origination fee structure varies by archetype: no origination on Quick-Term Loans, negotiable origination on Traditional Term Loans, and a 1% origination fee plus $250 annual renewal fee on Revolving Lines of Credit at First Citizens [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Documentation and legal fees scale with product complexity at First Citizens: a flat $500 on Quick-Term, $500 to $5,000 on Traditional Term, and $0 on Revolving Lines of Credit [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
- Lenders in this segment differentiate on the absence of brokerage fees as a positioning lever, treating HOA lending as a direct-origination practice rather than a broker-mediated channel [[sources/web-2026-01-01-33e]].
