# California HOA Financial Review Threshold (§5305 $75,000)

## Summary

Under California Civil Code §5305 (Davis-Stirling Act), a California HOA must obtain a GAAP-conforming Review of its financial statement by a California Board of Accountancy licensee whenever the association's gross income exceeds $75,000 in a fiscal year, and must distribute that Review to members within 120 days after fiscal-year close via Civil Code §4040 individual delivery. The threshold is a statutory baseline that governing documents may tighten but not relax [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].

## Key claims

- The §5305 trigger is gross income exceeding $75,000 in a single fiscal year — not annual assessments, not aggregated multi-year income, and not unit count [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- The §5305 trigger is a Review obligation, not an audit obligation; California's audit obligations arise from governing documents or voluntary engagement, not from §5305 itself [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- The Review must conform to generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- The Review must be prepared by a licensee of the California Board of Accountancy — out-of-state CPAs without a CA license do not satisfy the §5305 preparer requirement [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- The 120-day distribution deadline runs from the close of the fiscal year, not from the date the Review is completed [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Distribution must be by individual delivery per Civil Code §4040; posting alone does not satisfy the §5305 distribution requirement [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Associations at or below the $75,000 gross-income threshold have a statutory exception to the Review requirement, though governing documents may still mandate Review or Audit [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- HOACPA, as a CPA practitioner-of-record, treats the $75,000 baseline as a floor and identifies ten enumerated circumstances under which a Review or Audit should be performed even at sub-threshold income (see `cpa-recommended-audit-triggers`) [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
