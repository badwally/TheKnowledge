# Alberta Qualified Person (Reserve Fund Study)

## Summary

Under §21(1)(c) of Alta Reg 168/2000, "qualified person" is the definitional category of individual whom a condominium corporation's board must retain under §23(1) to carry out the statutory reserve fund study. Alberta's qualified-person test is criteria-based rather than license-based: knowledge — measured against reasonable and objective criteria — of the depreciating property, its operation and maintenance, and the costs of its replacement or repair is the regulatory standard, with no licensure prerequisite enumerated in the regulation [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].

## Key claims

- A qualified person is an individual who, based on reasonable and objective criteria, is knowledgeable with respect to (i) the depreciating property or that type of depreciating property, (ii) the operation and maintenance of the depreciating property or that type of depreciating property, and (iii) the costs of replacement of or repairs to, as the case may be, the depreciating property or that type of depreciating property [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §21(2) provides that, for the purposes of §23, a reference to a qualified person includes a corporate entity if that entity, in carrying out the functions of a qualified person, employs or otherwise retains the services of an individual who is a qualified person [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The Alberta qualified-person definition does NOT require professional licensure (e.g., P.Eng., RS™, AScT, or other credential); the regulatory test is knowledge against reasonable and objective criteria, not licensure status [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The §23(3)(b) reserve fund report must disclose whether the qualified person is an employee or agent of, or otherwise associated with, the corporation or any person who performs management or maintenance services for the corporation — an independence-disclosure obligation rather than an arm's-length prohibition [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The arm's-length-from-developer requirement under §1(2)(c) applies to cost consultants performing §14 functions for the developer, not to qualified persons performing the §23 reserve fund study [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
