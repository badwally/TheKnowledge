# Anti-inflammatory diet (Kuon Long COVID)

## Summary

Dietary co-prescription in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Osher Long COVID protocol, organized around low salt (2.3 g/day) and high potassium (except in renal/kidney impairment), abundant plant polyphenols (berries, cruciferous vegetables), and culinary use of turmeric, cumin, clove, ginger, and green tea — and framed as the topic of GMV Part 1 ("The Anti-Inflammatory Diet, Lectins, and Histamine") [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- Listed as a fixed component of both the basic supplement protocol and the mast-cell stabilization protocol: low salt (2.3 g) and high potassium (except for renal/kidney impairment) [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Boosts antioxidant intake via plant polyphenols in berries and cruciferous vegetables [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Uses spices for their anti-inflammatory effect: turmeric, cumin, clove, ginger, and green tea [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Pairs with the EWG Dirty Dozen guidance to prioritize organic versions of high-residue produce [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Frames Part 1 of the GMV series as covering the anti-inflammatory diet alongside lectins and histamine, with Part 2 (this deck) covering nature and nutrients and Part 3 covering complementary therapies including stress reduction, vagal toning exercises, and pacing in graduated exercise [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
