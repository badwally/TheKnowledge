# Virginia CIC Manager Trust Accounting and Internal Controls

## Summary

Virginia imposes manager-side trust-accounting and internal-controls obligations on common interest community (CIC) managers through Part V (Standards of Conduct and Practice) of the Common Interest Community Manager Regulations (18VAC48-50). Two sections carry the operative load: Section 160 ("Maintenance and management of accounts") and Section 210 ("Establishment of internal accounting controls") [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]]. The regime is structurally distinct from association-side reserve-funding statutes such as the Virginia Property Owners' Association Act §55.1-1826: it regulates the third-party manager (and certified principal/supervisory employees), not the association itself, and provides the compliance scaffolding within which any VA-deployed CAM accounting software must operate.

## Key claims

- 18VAC48-50 contains a dedicated section on maintenance and management of accounts at Section 160, situated in Part V (Standards of Conduct and Practice) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- The same regulation contains a separate section on establishment of internal accounting controls at Section 210, also in Part V [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Section 200 (Part V) requires establishment of a code of conduct, providing the conduct-level framing for the accounting obligations [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Section 220 (Part V) requires response to inquiry and provision of records, which complements the records-related obligations under Sections 160 and 210 [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Section 190 (Part V) enumerates prohibited acts, providing the disciplinary backstop to the trust-accounting requirements [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Section 140 (Part V) enumerates grounds for disciplinary action, which apply to violations of the conduct and accounting sections within the same Part [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- The trust-accounting and internal-controls regime applies to two regulated categories defined in Part II: licensed common interest community managers (Section 30) and certified principal or supervisory employees (Section 35, effective July 1, 2012) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- A reciprocity pathway (Section 37) means out-of-state managers entering Virginia must comply with the Part V trust-accounting and internal-controls obligations once licensed/certified in Virginia [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
