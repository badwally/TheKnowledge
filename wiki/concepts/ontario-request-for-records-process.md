# Ontario Request-for-Records Process (O Reg 48/01 §13.3)

## Summary

The Ontario request-for-records process is the procedural mechanism through which a condominium owner can examine or obtain copies of records held by the corporation, governed by §55(3) of the Condominium Act, 1998 as operationalized through §13.3 of O Reg 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]]. The regulation defines the three principal artifacts of the process — the request, the board's response, and the requester's response — and prescribes a fee structure ("fee payable for the request") under §13.3(7) paragraph 4 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]]. The process anchors the records-disclosure regime that requires every Ontario condo-software product to produce, on demand, the core records enumerated in O Reg 48/01.

## Key claims

- "Request for records" means a request to examine or obtain copies of records under §55(3) of the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- "Board's response" means the response that a board gives under §13.3(6) of O Reg 48/01 to a request for records [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- "Requester's response" means the response described in §13.3(11) of O Reg 48/01 that a requester returns to a corporation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- "Fee payable for the request," in connection with a request for records, means the fee described in §13.3(7) paragraph 4 of O Reg 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The rolling 12-month look-back windows for PIC production (item 8 of the core-record definition) and for board/owner-meeting minutes production (item 9 of the core-record definition) are pegged to the date the corporation receives the request for records or the requester's response, making the window-computation a query-time rather than ingest-time operation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
