# Ontario Credit Union Deposit Insurance Coverage

## Summary

The Ontario credit union deposit insurance regime is administered by the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA) and backed by the Deposit Insurance Reserve Fund (DIRF), pre-funded by Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. The regime provides $250,000 of basic coverage for insurable deposits held in non-registered accounts (principal and interest combined per depositor in each Ontario credit union or caisse populaire) and unlimited coverage for insurable deposits held in registered accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. Deposits held in different branches of the same credit union or caisse populaire are not separately insured [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. The regime provides separate coverage for joint accounts, trust accounts, registered accounts, and eligible business accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. The coverage limits and DIRF mechanism are the operative figures cited in Ontario reserve-fund investment analysis when condominium corporations evaluate credit union deposits and GICs against the Condominium Act, 1998 s.115(5) authorized-institution regime.

## Key claims

### Basic coverage

- $250,000 maximum for insurable deposits held in non-registered accounts, combining principal and interest, per depositor in each Ontario credit union and caisse populaire [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Unlimited coverage for insurable deposits held in registered accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Deposits in different branches of the same credit union or caisse populaire are not separately insured [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Coverage applies only to insurable deposits held in Canadian currency [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Coverage requires the depositor to be a member of the credit union or caisse populaire [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

### Insurable deposit categories

- Chequing and savings accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Guaranteed Investment Certificates (GICs) and other term deposits, regardless of term of investment [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Money orders [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Funds in transit [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Index-linked term deposits (principal portion only) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

### Registered account categories with unlimited coverage

- First home savings account (FHSA) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Locked-in retirement account (LIRA) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Life income fund (LIF) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Registered retirement savings plan (RRSP) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Registered retirement income fund (RRIF) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Registered disability savings plan (RDSP) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Registered education savings plan (RESP) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Tax-free savings account (TFSA) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

### Exclusions

- Mutual funds [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Membership shares [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Patronage, investment, or preferred shares issued by a credit union [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Foreign currency deposits [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Contents of safety deposit boxes [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Securities held for safekeeping [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

### Joint account coverage

- Deposits held jointly by depositors are insured separately from insurable deposits in each individual depositor's name, provided the credit union or caisse populaire records identify the name and address of each joint owner [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Maximum coverage for deposits having the same combination of joint owners at each credit union or caisse populaire is $250,000 (held jointly, not per individual owner) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

### Trust account coverage

- Insurable deposits held in trust accounts are insured separately from deposits owned by the trustee or the beneficiary [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Each beneficiary's portion is insured up to $250,000 [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Insurable deposits having both the same trustee and the same beneficiary combination are added together and the total is insured to a maximum of $250,000 for each beneficiary [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

### Payout mechanics

- Payments for insurable deposits are made as soon as possible when a credit union or caisse populaire goes out of business [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Payment includes principal and interest up to $250,000 for non-registered accounts (aggregated) and unlimited for registered accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Borrowers from an insolvent credit union or caisse populaire remain responsible for repayment of outstanding debt until paid in full; FSRA may exercise a right of set-off against deposits or deposit insurance payments [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
