# Florida Condominium Replacement-Cost Property Insurance Requirement

## Summary

Florida Statute §718.111(11)(a) requires every residential condominium in Florida to maintain adequate property insurance based on the replacement cost of the property to be insured, as determined by an independent insurance appraisal or update of a prior appraisal. The replacement cost must be re-determined at least once every 36 months. The requirement overrides any contrary provision in the declaration of condominium for coverage based on full insurable value or similar coverage, and applies regardless of the date of the condominium's declaration. The statute permits two alternative coverage mechanisms beyond a standard policy: a self-insurance fund complying with §§624.460–624.488, or a multi-community group insurance program covering at least three communities under a probable-maximum-loss model accepted by the Florida Commission on Hurricane Loss Projection Methodology [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].

## Key claims

- §718.111(11) applies to every residential condominium in the state of Florida, regardless of the date of its declaration of condominium [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- The stated legislative intent is to encourage lower or stable insurance premiums for associations subject to §718.111(11), per §718.111(11) [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- Adequate property insurance must be based on the replacement cost of the property to be insured, regardless of any requirement in the declaration of condominium for coverage at full insurable value, replacement cost, or similar coverage, per §718.111(11)(a) [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- Replacement cost must be determined by an independent insurance appraisal or update of a prior appraisal, per §718.111(11)(a) [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- Replacement cost must be re-determined at least once every 36 months, per §718.111(11)(a) [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- An association or group of associations may provide adequate property insurance through a self-insurance fund that complies with §§624.460–624.488, per §718.111(11)(a)1 [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- An association may also provide adequate property insurance for a group of at least three communities created and operating under Chapter 718, 719, 720, or 721 by maintaining coverage equal to the probable maximum loss for the communities for a 250-year windstorm event, determined through a model accepted by the Florida Commission on Hurricane Loss Projection Methodology, per §718.111(11)(a)2 [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
