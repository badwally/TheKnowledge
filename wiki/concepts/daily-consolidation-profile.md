# Daily Consolidation Profile

## Summary

A classification of the daily-timeframe range that pairs three speed/expansion attributes with three structural states, used by AMtrades as a top-of-funnel filter for whether the daily chart is offering a directional opportunity or should be sat out as internal-range chop [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].

## Key claims

- The profile is described along two axes — speed/range and displacement — and reduced to three structural states: directional, equilibrium, and lacking displacement [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- A daily range is classified by its external boundaries: an external range high acting as buyside liquidity and an external range low acting as sellside liquidity [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Counter-narrative read: when external liquidity has already been engaged, the next move targets the equilibrium of the range [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Directional-narrative read: when external liquidity is purged, the trade targets the opposite end of the range and continues into expansion [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Operational rule: avoid internal-consolidated-range participation on the daily timeframe — the engagement / purge of external liquidity is what makes the trade high-probability [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- The weekly profile and the economic calendar are used in tandem to time consolidation breakouts after manipulation, rather than acting on the daily-range classification in isolation [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
