# 30-Year Cash Flow Projection (Reserve Study)

## Summary

The 30-year cash flow projection is the standard long-horizon output of a reserve study, showing when major expenses hit, what contributions are required to meet a funding target, and whether the projected balance stays positive over the projection horizon [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]]. CommunityPay's product page enumerates a five-element decomposition of the projection — annual expenditures, reserve contributions, ending fund balance, percent funded trend, and deficit years — and pairs the projection with a what-if scenario modeling capability that lets associations adjust assumptions (contribution changes, special assessments, deferred replacements) and see the impact instantly [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]]. CommunityPay's self-service Reserve Health Check widget produces a shorter 20-year cash flow projection from three inputs (reserve balance, annual contribution, building details) [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].

## Key claims

- A 30-year cash flow projection shows when major expenses hit and whether current funding keeps pace [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- The five named elements of the projection are: annual expenditures, reserve contributions, ending fund balance, percent funded trend, and deficit years [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Annual expenditures are projected replacement costs by year based on remaining useful life and inflation [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Reserve contributions in the projection are the required annual funding to meet the target by method (full, baseline, threshold) [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Ending fund balance is the year-end reserve balance after contributions and expenditures [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Percent funded trend shows how the funding level changes over the 30-year horizon [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Deficit years are years in which the projected balance goes negative without action [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- What-if scenario modeling is paired with the projection so associations can model contribution changes, special assessments, or deferred replacements before committing [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- The CommunityPay self-service Reserve Health Check produces a shorter 20-year cash flow projection rather than the 30-year horizon, computed from three inputs (reserve balance, annual contribution, building details) [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
