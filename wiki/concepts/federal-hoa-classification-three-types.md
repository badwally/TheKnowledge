# Federal HOA Classification — Three §528 Association Types

## Summary

§528 recognizes three mutually-exclusive types of homeowners association, each of which qualifies (separately) for the favorable §528 tax regime via Form 1120-H: a condominium management association, a residential real estate management association, and a timeshare association [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. The association's type is reported on Item A of Form 1120-H and drives the applicable flat rate (30% for the first two, 32% for timeshare associations) [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. A timeshare association cannot also be a condominium management association — the classifications are mutually exclusive at the federal-tax-classification level [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]]. The 'substantially all' test of Treas. Reg. §1.528-4 governs whether the units (condominium management association) or lots/buildings (residential real estate management association) are 'homes for individuals' for purposes of the type definition [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].

## Key claims

- §528 recognizes three types of homeowners association: condominium management association, residential real estate management association, and timeshare association [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- A condominium management association is an organization organized and operated to acquire, build, manage, maintain, and care for the property in a condominium project substantially all of whose units are homes for individuals [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- A residential real estate management association is an organization organized and operated to acquire, build, manage, maintain, and care for a subdivision, development, or similar area substantially all of whose lots or buildings are homes for individuals [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- A timeshare association is an organization (other than a condominium management association) organized and operated to acquire, build, manage, maintain, and care for the property that has members who hold a timeshare right to use, or a timeshare ownership interest in, real property of the timeshare association [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- A timeshare association cannot be a condominium management association — the classifications are mutually exclusive [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- Treas. Reg. §1.528-4 governs the 'substantially all' test for condominium management associations and residential real estate management associations [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- The association's type is reported as Item A on Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
- The 30% flat tax rate applies to condominium management associations and residential real estate management associations; the 32% flat tax rate applies to timeshare associations [[sources/web-2025-12-31-dae]].
