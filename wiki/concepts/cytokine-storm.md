# Cytokine Storm

## Summary

A "tsunami of inflammation" — described in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Long COVID Group Medical Visit handout as the severe inflammatory state that COVID can trigger when the body's inflammatory pathways escalate to a level that overwhelms the organs and forces critical care [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].

## Key claims

- COVID can kick off inflammatory pathways in the body to such high levels that the body is unable to resolve them without added help [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Cytokine storm is a tsunami of inflammation that overwhelms the organs, leading to the need for critical care [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Risk of severe inflammation is the rationale Dr. Kuon gives for placing inflammation as bucket #1 in the four-buckets-of-fatigue framework and for prioritizing an anti-inflammatory diet as the first step in Long COVID recovery [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
