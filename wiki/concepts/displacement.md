# Displacement

## Summary

Displacement is, in the ICT-derived order-block playbook, the energetic, fast price move away from a zone — the leg that immediately follows an unbroken series of order-block candles and that, when paired with a market structure shift, supplies one of the conditions ICT traders require for a high-probability OB setup [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]]; in TTrades's reference card it is the middle term of the canonical entry trigger "BOS + Displacement + FVG" applied across all four trigger-stack models [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]]; in the canonical MMXM PDF it is the energetic move out of accumulation (MMBM) or distribution (MMSM) that creates the model's market structure shift, distinguished sharply from the initial "failure to displace" liquidity grab that precedes it [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]]; and Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023) explains displacement using ICT's elephant-in-a-children's-pool analogy, framing a FVG as the algorithmic footprint of institutional entry [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- Displacement is the energetic up- or down-move that immediately follows an unbroken series of order-block candles, and the OB is defined relative to it as the last opposite-direction candles before the displacement [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- Displacement combined with market structure shift (mss) is one of the conditions enumerated for both bullish and bearish high-probability setups [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- A first displacement can produce an OB and imbalance/FVG without yet producing an mss; the next displacement is what confirms the mss and validates the setup [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- The displacement leg itself becomes a future order block once price retraces back into it — "the displacement down from our area to enter short becomes a what? An OB!" — and price entering that green candle and failing to break higher is presented as the same concept playing out a layer deeper [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- Displacement is the third item in the lesson's summary checklist, sitting between Liquidity and Market Structure Shift in the OB playbook [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- In TTrades's framework, "BOS + Displacement + FVG" is the LTF entry-trigger phrase used for every one of Models 1–4, with displacement positioned between the LTF break of structure and the resulting FVG [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- TTrades treats displacement as a required co-occurrence — "BOS alone" is never sufficient — implying that the displacement must be present in the same leg that produces the BOS for the trigger to fire [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMBM's start is signaled by a quick drop into accumulation followed by an energetic displacement up that creates the initial market structure shift [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, the MMSM starts with an impulse move higher into a HTF premium of an array, followed by displacement down that creates a market structure shift down [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF distinguishes the model's displacement leg from the initial impulse leg, which it labels a "failure to displace" — a move whose only purpose is to grab liquidity and which precedes the true displacement that supplies the MSS [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per Ali Khan, a FVG can indicate that IPDA is in a hurry to move price in one direction over another — "an algorithmic footprint that we can see in price action" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, ICT uses the analogy of an elephant stepping into a children's pool — since the size and mass of the elephant's foot is so large, we will see a displacement of water — drawing a comparison to larger institutions with "deep pockets" entering the marketplace [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, there are caveats to when and where displacement can be expected and when to trust it, but keeping the elephant analogy in mind at key support/resistance levels "will serve you well" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
