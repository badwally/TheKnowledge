# EWG Dirty Dozen and Clean 15

## Summary

Produce-prioritization lists published by the Environmental Working Group at EWG.org, used in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Osher Long COVID protocol to direct patients toward organic purchasing of the most pesticide-laden produce ("Dirty Dozen") while accepting conventional versions of the lowest-residue produce ("Clean 15") [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- Authored and published by the Environmental Working Group (EWG.org) [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Cited in Kuon's deck alongside UCSF Osher's own nutrition-tips page (osher.ucsf.edu/putting-nutrition-practice-tips-making-dietary-changes) as a patient resource [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Operationalized in the protocol as: prioritize organic foods, especially the Dirty Dozen, when implementing the toxin-reduction strategy [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
