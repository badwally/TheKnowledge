# Judas Swing

## Summary

The Judas Swing is the ICT name for the manipulation leg of the Power of 3 / AMD cycle: a deliberate false move against the eventual directional bias, engineered early in the session to trap traders on the wrong side and to stop out positions placed at obvious swing highs or lows before the real expansion unfolds in the opposite direction [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].

## Key claims

- Defined by Trader Theory's recap of ICT's 2022 mentorship as "a false move against the bias (the manipulation part of PO3) to trap traders and to stop out already existing positions" [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Sits inside the Power of 3 (PO3) pattern, also known as AMD — Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution — where the Judas Swing is specifically the M (manipulation) phase before the D (distribution / real expansion) leg [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Operationally implies that the trader's bias should be set independently from the early-session move; the early-session move against bias is, by hypothesis, the bait rather than the signal [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Pairs with the pre-session preparation of marking the previous swing high and swing low, which Trader Theory frames as the "potential price sweeps" the Judas Swing is engineered to take out before reversing [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
