# AI-Native Property Management

## Summary

"AI-native property management" is the positioning frame used by emerging CAM vendors that claim to have built their platforms with AI as a core architectural primitive from inception, rather than adding AI features atop legacy property-management software. Daisy uses the phrase in self-description as "the fastest-growing AI-native property management company," explicitly contrasting itself with vendors that add "technology to old processes" by claiming it has "rethought property management from the ground up" [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. Vantaca uses related but distinct framing — "AI-first community association management platform" and "AI-native automation and operational excellence" — in its October 2025 capital-raise announcement, asserting "true agentic AI capabilities that transform daily operations rather than just digitizing old processes" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. The two vendors share the conceptual frame (AI as architectural primitive, not feature add-on) but differ in tier, geography, and operating model.

## Key claims

- The phrase "AI-native" is used as a vendor self-description in the CAM software market to distinguish architecturally-AI platforms from legacy platforms with retrofitted AI features [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]] [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Daisy frames its AI-native posture as having "rethought property management from the ground up so that buildings run better, people feel more connected, and every detail is handled without the stress" [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Vantaca uses related framing — "AI-first," "AI-native automation," and "true agentic AI capabilities that transform daily operations rather than just digitizing old processes" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Both vendors operationalize the AI-native frame partly through named AI agents owning workflows: Daisy's "Steven" (takeover onboarding) and "Lily" (resident support, 22% of incoming requests) [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]; Vantaca acquired HOAi in November 2024 to integrate advanced AI capabilities for the same purpose [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- A common implicit claim across AI-native CAM positioning is that legacy CAM platforms underinvest in technology: Daisy's Cohen states that less than 1% of CAM-company revenue is invested in technology, while 70% goes to labor performing manual tasks that technology could streamline [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- The "AI-native" frame is in active competitive use as of late 2025 across multiple vendors at different valuation tiers and geographic scopes — Daisy (NYC/NJ, private growth-stage) and Vantaca ($1.25B valuation, national CAM SaaS) [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]] [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
