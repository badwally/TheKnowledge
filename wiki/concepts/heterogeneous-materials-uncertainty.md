# Uncertainty in heterogeneous materials

## Summary

In the probabilistic description of a heterogeneous material, uncertainty has two distinct components: uncertainty about the particular value of an individual material characteristic, and uncertainty about its spatial fluctuations across the material. Separating these components is what makes Bayesian updating of material models non-trivial.

## Key claims

- In the probabilistic description of a heterogeneous material, the uncertainties consist of a particular value of individual material characteristics and their spatial fluctuations [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- Bayesian inference can be used to update this two-component uncertainty representation against observations of coupled heat and moisture fields [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
