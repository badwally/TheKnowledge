# Quebec Mandatory Electronic Corporate Filing (Fiscal Years Starting 2024)

## Summary

For fiscal years starting in 2024, electronic filing has become mandatory for most corporations at both Québec provincial and Canadian federal levels [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. In Québec, all corporate returns (CO-17 and CO-17.SP) must be filed online, with an exception only for registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. At the federal level, the T2 must also be submitted electronically, with an exception for certain tax-exempt entities under subsection 149 of the Income Tax Act, which includes many NPOs (such as residential condominium corporations under §149(1)(l)) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. Failure to comply with the electronic filing requirements may result in processing delays and, in some cases, administrative penalties [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key claims

- Electronic filing of CO-17 and CO-17.SP is mandatory in Québec for fiscal years starting in 2024, with an exception only for registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- The federal T2 must also be submitted electronically for fiscal years starting in 2024 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Certain tax-exempt entities under subsection 149 of the Income Tax Act, including many NPOs, are excepted from the federal mandatory electronic filing regime [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Failure to comply with electronic filing requirements may lead to processing delays [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Failure to comply with electronic filing requirements may, in some cases, lead to administrative penalties [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Practical compliance steps include preparing financial statements and key information (NEQ, directors, contact details); reviewing all required sections including box 39 if REQ updates apply; submitting electronically and keeping proof of transmission [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
