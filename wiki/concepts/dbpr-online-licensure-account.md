# DBPR Online Licensure Account (Florida CAM)

## Summary

The DBPR online licensure account is a digital licensure-tracking mechanism mandated by Florida CS/CS/HB 913 (2025), Chapter 2025-175, for community association managers (CAMs) and CAM firms operating in Florida [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]]. Every CAM licensee must create and maintain such an account, populate specified information, and update it within a specified timeframe; CAM firms must additionally use their account to identify the CAMs they employ to provide community association management services [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].

## Key claims

- HB 913 (2025) requires every CAM licensee to create and maintain an online licensure account with DBPR [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- The licensee must identify on the account information specified by the statute and provide additional specified information [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- That information must be updated within a specified timeframe set by the statute [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- A CAM firm must identify on its DBPR online licensure account the CAMs it employs to provide community association management services, creating a firm-to-licensee linkage in the DBPR record system [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
