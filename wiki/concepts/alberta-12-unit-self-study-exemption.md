# Alberta 12-Unit Self-Study Exemption (§22)

## Summary

Section 22 of Alta Reg 168/2000 carves out a self-study allowance for small Alberta condominium corporations: notwithstanding §21(2) (which permits a corporate entity to serve as a qualified person only if it employs or retains a qualified individual), a corporation whose condominium plan consists of not more than 12 units may itself carry out the §23 reserve fund study functions of a qualified person, provided it is authorized to do so by a special resolution [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].

## Key claims

- The §22 self-study allowance applies to condominium plans consisting of not more than 12 units [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The exemption permits the corporation itself to carry out the functions of a qualified person, in respect of the corporation's own plan [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The exemption is conditional on authorization by a special resolution of the corporation [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The §22 carve-out applies notwithstanding the §21(2) corporate-entity test that otherwise requires a qualified-person-employing entity [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The §22 exemption does NOT remove the substantive §23 study, report, and plan requirements — only the requirement to retain an external qualified person to perform them [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
