# Automated Lockbox (HOA)

## Summary

Automated lockbox in the HOA / community-association banking context is a bank- or check-processor-operated service that receives owner payments mailed to a dedicated address, processes the checks, captures payment metadata and AR check images, and posts the receipts back to the CAM platform's general ledger for auto reconciliation. AppFolio HOA Financials positions automated lockbox as one of the key features delivered through its HOA-banking integration partners rather than as a platform-native capability [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].

## Key claims

- AppFolio HOA Financials surfaces automated lockbox as a feature delivered by its bank integration partners — Alliance Association Bank, Pacific Premier Bank, First Citizens Bank, SmartStreet powered by Banc of California, and CheckAlt — rather than as a native platform feature [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- The automated lockbox feature is bundled with bank statements, Accounts Receivable (AR) check images, auto reconciliation, and Positive Pay in AppFolio's articulation of the bank-integration value proposition [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- CheckAlt — distinct from the four chartered banks in AppFolio's HOA partner roster — appears to specialize in lockbox/check-processing infrastructure rather than chartered deposit banking [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
