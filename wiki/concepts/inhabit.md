## Summary

Inhabit is a global PropTech software company serving the residential and vacation property management industries, founded in 2016 and headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee [[sources/web-2026-06-09-inh]]. Inhabit serves over 5 million units across the residential and short-term rental property management industries with a team of 1,500+ members [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]]. The company describes itself as "the residential industry's only enterprise-grade software provider offering both full-stack & platform-agnostic point solutions" [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]]. Inhabit operates as a portfolio of brands across software, payments, and insurance, including [[entities/shiftsuite]] (Canadian condo CAM), [[entities/resman]] (US multifamily PMS), [[entities/anyone-home]] (AI leasing), [[entities/sitecompli]] (regulatory compliance), [[entities/tenanttech]] (online leasing), [[entities/western-reporting]] (screening/fraud), [[entities/razz-interactive]] (websites/CMS), and [[entities/valencedocs]] (lease file audit AI) [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key claims

- Inhabit is a global PropTech software company serving over 5 million units across residential and short-term rental property management [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Inhabit has 1,500+ team members and operates across software, payments, and insurance [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Inhabit markets itself as the only enterprise-grade software provider offering both full-stack and platform-agnostic point solutions in the residential industry [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- At OpTech 2025 (Nov 17, 2025), Inhabit announced a slate of product features organized around three themes: next-generation AI, fraud prevention, and expanded compliance tools [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- The OpTech 2025 AI roadmap includes Anyone Home AI Leasing Assistant (multichannel voice/SMS/chat), SiteCompli Metro AI (NYC regulatory compliance assistant), Razz AI SEO and Copy Assistant (multifamily website CMS), and ValenceDocs Lease File Audit (coming early 2026) [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- The OpTech 2025 fraud-prevention roadmap includes Western Reporting Digital Income Verification (documentless income verification via third-party data) and Western Reporting Identity Verification (arriving early 2026) [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- The OpTech 2025 compliance roadmap includes TenantTech Blue Moon Software integration (attorney-reviewed rental applications and lease forms) and ResMan Affordable enhancements for HUD, LIHTC, and Rural Development properties [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- The entire OpTech 2025 product slate targets multifamily rental, vacation rental, and affordable housing operators — no condo/HOA, reserve-study, capital-planning, or Canadian-condo-specific product moves were announced [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Dan Roehl, EVP/Managing Director of Inhabit's Residential Division, framed the OpTech 2025 launch as "Hybrid Intelligence" combining AI with human oversight to mitigate the side effects of "first-generation AI tools" [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Inhabit's positioning emphasizes hybrid AI (AI + human-in-the-loop) over fully autonomous AI agents, with [[entities/anyone-home]] explicitly powering ResMan's Leasing Assistant [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
