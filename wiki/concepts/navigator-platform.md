# Navigator (Strategic Club Solutions Platform)

## Summary

Navigator is the Strategic Club Solutions club-management platform that, as of April 2025, embeds SmartProperty's Living Reserve Study® as its financial-planning component under a strategic partnership between the two firms [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]]. Navigator targets Large Scale Clubs and member communities and is marketed as a data-driven, integrated decision platform spanning financial planning, document management, scenario planning, project management, and decision support [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].

## Key claims

- Navigator's Living Reserve Study® component is a dynamic financial planning tool that evolves with the club's needs by combining SmartProperty's reserve study insights with SCS's strategic expertise [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- Navigator includes a Document Management & Storage module described as a secure, organized, and accessible repository for essential club documents [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- Navigator's Real-Time Scenario Planning module enables clubs to forecast and adjust financial and operational strategies in real time [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- Navigator includes a Project Management module covering oversight from inception to completion, framed as a schedule and budget control surface [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- Navigator includes a Decision Support System described as enhancing decision-making with comprehensive data analytics [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- Navigator's Thought Capital Dashboard visualizes strategic financial data and capital planning in a single dashboard surface [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- Navigator's Operational Integration module aligns asset management with strategic planning for execution [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- The Navigator platform is the delivery vehicle for SmartProperty's first publicly disclosed club-segment channel expansion outside the condominium/HOA market [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
