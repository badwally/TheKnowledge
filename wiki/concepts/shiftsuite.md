## Summary

Shiftsuite is a Canadian condo association management software platform serving the condo industry throughout Canada since 2002, now operating under [[entities/inhabit]] ownership [[sources/web-2026-06-09-shs]]. As of [[entities/inhabit]]'s OpTech 2025 product launch (Nov 17, 2025), Shiftsuite was not mentioned in any of the announced AI, fraud-prevention, or compliance product workstreams, and no reserve-study, capital-planning, or Canadian-condo-specific features were added to the Inhabit portfolio roadmap [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key claims

- Shiftsuite is a Canadian condo association management software platform serving the Canadian condo industry since 2002 [[sources/web-2026-06-09-shs]].
- Shiftsuite operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio [[sources/web-2026-06-09-shs]].
- Shiftsuite partners with [[entities/sparcpay]] for Digital Vendor Payables within the Shiftsuite condo management platform [[sources/web-2026-06-10-sps]].
- At Inhabit's OpTech 2025 product launch (Nov 17, 2025), Shiftsuite was not referenced in any announced product workstream — all product moves targeted multifamily rental, vacation rental, or US affordable housing operators [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- As of Nov 2025, no reserve-study, capital-planning, Decree 991-2025 investment-strategy, or Canadian-statutory-compliance product capabilities have been added to Shiftsuite via Inhabit's product roadmap [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Inhabit's OpTech 2025 narrative emphasized US-multifamily and vacation-rental priorities (Anyone Home AI Leasing, SiteCompli Metro AI for NYC, ResMan Affordable for HUD/LIHTC), indicating that Canadian-condo investment is not in the company's near-term product priority set [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
