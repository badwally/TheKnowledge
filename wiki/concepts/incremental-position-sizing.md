# Emotion-Anchored Incremental Position Sizing

## Summary

Position-sizing discipline in which the trader scales up size only as fast as the emotional reaction to the dollar P&L on the screen permits — sizing down when a new loss threshold feels too large, holding at a lower size until the new number stops being emotionally disruptive, then attempting another step up [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].

## Key claims

- BobaTrader scaled up position sizes slowly during years 3–5, "giving myself time to get adjusted to the new numbers on the screen" [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Worked example of the calibration loop: $150 losses became $300 losses, but $300 was a bit too much, so he sized down to keep losses at $200, and continued in this pattern — sizing up to a specific risk level then adjusting based on how emotionally impacted he was by the number [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Identified edge that allowed scaling up without blowing up: patience — not trying to double position size every week, taking it slow and steady on the belief that "if i trusted in the process eventually Id be doing bigger numbers" [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
