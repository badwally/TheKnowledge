# CMRAO Limited Licence

## Summary

The CMRAO Limited Licence is a subordinate Ontario condo-manager credential issued by the Condominium Management Regulatory Authority of Ontario for individuals who handle some daily administration tasks under the supervision of a general licensed manager [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key claims

- Limited licence holders handle some daily administration tasks but require no management experience or training and must be supervised by a general licensed manager [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- As of May 2023, Ontario had 1,638 limited license holders [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- Not all limited licence holders will aspire to become general licensees, limiting the pipeline's ability to relieve the general-licence shortfall [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- Dean McCabe (president, The Meritus Group Management Inc.) notes that, given the limited number of existing general licensees and education delays from recent regulatory changes, many management firms are continuously searching for qualified managers or developing new talent from the pool of limited licensees; boards must be willing to hire limited licensees gaining the experience required to advance [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
