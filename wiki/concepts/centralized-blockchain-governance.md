# Centralized Blockchain Governance

## Summary

A mitigation pattern in which a centralized administrator on an otherwise distributed network enforces membership rules, regulator access, and encoded behavioral constraints to offset the antitrust risks of competitor exclusion and weakened enforcement [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].

## Key claims

- Clear membership rules: regulators can offer guidance on access rules, and oversight ensures administrators of centralized blockchains incorporate and enforce those rules [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Regulator entry points: centralized administrators can design dedicated access points that give regulators special network access for enforcement [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Iterative encoded governance: administrators can encode behavioral measures directly into the network's governance to define appropriate actions and combat anticompetitive behavior before and after it occurs [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Centralized governance is one of three principal mitigation families alongside sensitive-data management and regulator transparency [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
