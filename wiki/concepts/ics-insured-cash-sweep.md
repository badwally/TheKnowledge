# ICS (Insured Cash Sweep)

## Summary

ICS — Insured Cash Sweep — is an IntraFi-administered deposit-network service offered alongside CDARS to HOA depositors as a named deposit-service component of the Pacific Premier Bank (post-merger: Columbia Bank) HOA banking offering [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. ICS is one of two IntraFi services explicitly committed to continue as usual through the August 31, 2025 Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger and subsequent Columbia Bank rebrand [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key claims

- ICS stands for Insured Cash Sweep [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- ICS is administered by IntraFi as one of two named IntraFi deposit services (the other being CDARS) [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- ICS is offered by Pacific Premier Bank to HOA customers and continues unchanged through the Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger and Columbia Bank rebrand [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
