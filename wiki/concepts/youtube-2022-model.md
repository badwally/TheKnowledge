# YouTube 2022 model

## Summary

The "YouTube 2022 model" is a discretionary trading setup template comprising a liquidity raid, a displacement, a market structure shift, and a breaker block / FVG entry; bullish and bearish variants share the same structure with direction reversed [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].

## Key claims

- All breaker blocks in the lesson form within the YouTube 2022 model setup, which the lesson presents as a general template for both bullish and bearish scenarios [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- The model's review checklist instructs the trader to: identify key liquidity being taken, watch for displacement and the market structure shift away from liquidity, identify the breaker block (the failed order block) at the swing high or low, label any FVGs or order blocks for the displacement, label the PD Array (premium vs discount), and define entry and stop loss (R for the trade) [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- The trader is advised to wait for this setup and avoid the random swings of price until structure gives an entry [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- In the bullish $ES example, displacement up creates an m5 FVG and breaker block ("giant launch pad"), and price retraces back into the breaker block / FVG before continuing toward buy-side liquidity [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
- In the bearish $ES example, the model sets up a bearish breaker block into the previous day's close; price respects the breaker block during the overnight session and is driven down to take out sell-side liquidity into the open [[sources/pdf-042302b20a5d]].
