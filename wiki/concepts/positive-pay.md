# Positive Pay

## Summary

Positive Pay is a bank-side fraud-prevention service surfaced by HOA-banking partners of community-association management (CAM) platforms such as AppFolio, where it is bundled alongside automated lockbox, bank statements, Accounts Receivable (AR) check images, and auto reconciliation as part of the platform's bank-integration value proposition [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]]. In the AppFolio HOA Financials product, Positive Pay is offered through integrations with named HOA-banking partners rather than as a platform-native feature.

## Key claims

- Positive Pay is offered by AppFolio HOA Financials as one of the value-add features delivered through bank integrations rather than as a platform-native feature [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- AppFolio surfaces Positive Pay through integrations with Alliance Association Bank, Pacific Premier Bank, First Citizens Bank, SmartStreet powered by Banc of California, and CheckAlt [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- AppFolio bundles Positive Pay alongside automated lockbox, bank statements, AR check images, and auto reconciliation as a single set of features delivered by the bank-integration layer [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
