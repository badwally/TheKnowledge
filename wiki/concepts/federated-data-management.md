# Federated Data Management Tools

## Summary

Federated data management tools, together with explainable data value estimations, are the technical-mechanism class Vincent, Prewitt and Li (2025) identify as the infrastructure enabling collective bargaining between information producers and AI builders [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].

## Key claims

- Vincent, Prewitt and Li (2025) name federated data management tools and explainable data value estimations as two concrete classes of technical mechanism that researchers and developers can build to inform and facilitate collective bargaining in the information economy [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- The technical layer is positioned as a precondition for the regulatory layer: trusted data intermediaries cannot represent producers credibly without infrastructure that tracks contributions and quantifies their value [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
