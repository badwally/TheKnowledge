# Counter-emotional positioning (Command 3)

## Summary

"I add when you subtract and subtract when you add" is the third of Tom Hougaard's three commands: when the 95% are reducing exposure on a losing trade (or adding to a losing trade out of hope), the 5% do the opposite, and the position-management contrast at the moment of pain is the lever Hougaard credits with producing the asymmetric P&L distribution between the two groups [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].

## Key claims

- The command is stated explicitly as "I add when you subtract and subtract when you add" — Hougaard's positioning rule against the crowd at moments of emotional charge [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The framing is structured as a fork: how the 95% react to a losing trade versus how the 5% react to a losing trade [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- It pairs with Hougaard's broader behavioural diagnosis: traders hope when they should fear and fear when they should hope — they are fearful when making a profit (afraid the market will take it away) and hopeful when making a loss (hoping the market will give it back) [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- "Hope is not a strategy" is the corollary chapter heading: positioning by hope reliably puts the trader on the wrong side of the 95/5 split [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The command operationalizes the Best-Loser-Wins thesis at the position-sizing layer: it is how losing well is implemented in basis points, not just in mindset [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
