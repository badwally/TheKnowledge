# AI-Augmented R&D

## Summary

The application of generative AI tools to the innovation process itself, distinct from automation of production tasks. Empirical evidence from a randomized rollout in the R&D lab of a large U.S. firm shows large gains in materials discovery, patent filings, and downstream product innovation [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].

## Key claims

- AI-assisted scientists discover 44% more materials than untreated controls in a randomized rollout to 1,018 researchers across three waves starting in May 2022 [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- The technology produces a 39% increase in patent filings and, several months later, a 17% rise in product prototypes incorporating the new compounds [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Accounting for input costs, the tool boosts overall R&D efficiency by 13–15% [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- AI-discovered compounds possess superior properties, indicating the model improves quality alongside quantity [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Effects propagate downstream into product innovation, demonstrating that later R&D stages do not fully bottleneck the gains [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Standard economic models imply automating research tasks has qualitatively different effects than automating production tasks because of compounding social returns to innovation (Aghion et al. 2019; Jones and Summers 2020) [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- The paper provides the first causal evidence of AI's impact on real-world R&D [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
