# NJ Removal of S2760 Special Assessment / Loan Authority Language

## Summary

S3992 (August 2025) removed language from S2760 that had provided NJ associations with explicit statutory authority to impose a special assessment or obtain a loan if a capital asset reached the end of its useful life sooner than projected in the reserve study [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]]. The Greenbaum alert characterizes the removal as part of S3992's broader clarification effort but does not address whether or how alternative special-assessment / loan authority remains available under separate NJ statutory provisions or association governing documents.

## Key claims

- S2760 originally provided that an association had authority to impose a special assessment or obtain a loan if a capital asset reached the end of its useful life sooner than projected in the reserve study [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Under S3992, that special-assessment-and-loan-authority language has been removed [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The removal is one of seven enumerated amendments in S3992, characterized by the Greenbaum alert as providing clarity to the existing law while providing relief for historically underfunded associations [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The source does not address whether alternative special-assessment / loan authority remains available under separate NJ statutory provisions or association governing documents [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
