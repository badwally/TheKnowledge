# GSE Investor Concentration Limit (Eliminated)

## Summary

The GSE Investor Concentration Limit was a long-standing 50% cap that disqualified condominium projects from GSE-backed conventional financing when investor-owned units exceeded 50% of total units. The March 18, 2026 joint update (Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 and Freddie Mac Bulletin 2026-C) eliminates this limit, removing a structural barrier to GSE-eligibility for condominium projects with high investor-ownership concentrations [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].

## Key claims

- The long-standing 50% investor concentration limit has been eliminated under the March 18, 2026 GSE update [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Elimination is part of the same package that retires limited review and expands waiver eligibility to projects with up to 10 units [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Combined with the limited-review retirement, the elimination shifts greater responsibility onto lenders and associations to demonstrate financial soundness under the remaining full-review or waiver paths [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
