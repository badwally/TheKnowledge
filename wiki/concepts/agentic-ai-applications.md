# Agentic AI Application Domains

## Summary

The set of real-world domains where Agentic AI's autonomy and adaptability provide value — surveyed by Acharya et al. as healthcare, finance, adaptive software systems, autonomous devices, collaborative robots, disaster relief, and cybersecurity [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].

## Key claims

- The Acharya et al. survey examines current and potential applications across healthcare, finance, and adaptive software systems, emphasizing the advantages of deploying agentic systems in real-world scenarios [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Disaster relief, healthcare, and cybersecurity are highlighted as domains where chaos is considerable and the ability to control a situation independently is critical — a key motivation for Agentic AI's design [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Autonomous devices, collaborative robots, and interactive decision-support systems in finance and healthcare are flagged as natural anchors for Agentic AI given their high-interaction requirements [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Agentic AI is positioned to take on tasks that reallocate human effort, increase productivity, and engage in situations where human presence may be undesirable or dangerous [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Adoption is expected to transform job structures so that AIs perform operational tasks while people deal with more complex and strategic roles [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Practical examples in the survey include Agentic-AI-powered trading systems that dynamically adjust strategies using real-time data, historical trends, and unexpected market shifts [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
