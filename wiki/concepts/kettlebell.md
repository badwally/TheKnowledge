# Kettlebell

## Summary

A kettlebell is a free-weight implement used as the central tool in programs such as [[entities/strongfirst|StrongFirst]]'s [[entities/dry-fighting-weight|Dry Fighting Weight]] and the [[entities/dfw-kettleballs-remix|DFW Kettleballs Remix]], which programme paired kettlebells at the lifter's 5RM press weight to drive [[concepts/clean-and-press|clean & press]], [[concepts/front-squat|front squat]], and [[concepts/kettlebell-swing|swing]] sessions [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key claims

- Programs such as the DFW Kettleballs Remix prescribe a *pair* of kettlebells (one in each hand) as the working implement, rather than a single bell [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Load is calibrated by a press-based [[concepts/rep-max|rep max]] — the Remix specifies "your 5RM press weight" as the working pair [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- The same paired bells are used across different exercises within a session — clean & press alternates with front squats on C&P/FS days, while swings plus rows fill volume days [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
