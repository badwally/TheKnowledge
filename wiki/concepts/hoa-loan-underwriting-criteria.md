# HOA Loan Underwriting Criteria

## Summary

HOA loan underwriting criteria are the documentary, structural, and governance preconditions a community-association bank applies when assessing an association's readiness for a loan. First Citizens Bank's published standard requires a minimum of 25 units, an effective collection policy, and up-to-date financials; loan application review additionally covers the HOA's income statement, balance sheet, and aging report, and may be conditional on a board vote or amended HOA declaration depending on the association's governing documents and applicable state law [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].

## Key claims

- First Citizens requires a minimum of 25 units for HOA loan applications [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- First Citizens requires an effective collection policy at the applicant association [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- First Citizens requires up-to-date financials at the applicant association [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- When processing an application, banks typically review the HOA's income statement, balance sheet, and aging report [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Depending on the association's governing documents and state laws, a board vote or an amended HOA declaration may also be required as a condition of the loan [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Once a community association decides to pursue financing, the application process can be straightforward when the underwriting preconditions are met [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Discussing the HOA's needs with an association banking professional is positioned by First Citizens as helping a board quickly determine an effective option for the association's situation [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
