# Sellside liquidity

## Summary

Sellside liquidity is the pool of resting sell-stop orders sitting below prior swing lows, which the open of a bullish Power of 3 candle seeks before expanding up — and which a bearish OHLC expansion targets after the open seeks a premium [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].

## Key claims

- In the bullish OLHC template, price opens and seeks a discount or sellside liquidity (LOW) before expanding up [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- In the bearish OHLC template, after seeking a premium, price expands down to attack sellside liquidity (LOW) and closes near the low [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- In the daily bullish PO3 example, sellside liquidity has been marked off and the key observation is that price sweeps the lows of December — a HTF liquidity point where reversals can occur [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The bullish January 2023 monthly OLHC example begins with the monthly open dropping into a discount of December's range, taking out December's lows (sellside liquidity), before returning to the open and expanding up [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
