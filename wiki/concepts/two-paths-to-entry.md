# Two Paths to Entry

## Summary

In ICT-style price-action analysis, the binary entry rule states that there are only two ways to participate in either direction: sell at a high or sell on a retracement back into a market-structure break, and buy at a low or buy on a return to a market-structure break — a discipline rule that maps onto the breaker / failure-swing dichotomy and constrains the trader to wait for one of these two conditions [[sources/pdf-734e612fdf31]].

## Key claims

- There are only two ways to sell: sell at a high or sell on a retracement back to a break in market structure [[sources/pdf-734e612fdf31]].
- There are only two ways to buy: buy at a low or buy on a return or retracement back to a market structure break [[sources/pdf-734e612fdf31]].
- Breaker and failure swing are framed as the only two conditions in the marketplace [[sources/pdf-734e612fdf31]].
- The aim is to get breaker swing points, but if they do not occur, traders can still trade off the failure swing pattern [[sources/pdf-734e612fdf31]].
- Because traders cannot know with great conviction whether a breaker setup will form, while waiting for it to occur they can anticipate the failure swing [[sources/pdf-734e612fdf31]].
