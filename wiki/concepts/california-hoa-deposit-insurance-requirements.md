# California HOA Deposit-Insurance Requirements (Civil Code § 5380)

## Summary

California Civil Code § 5380 conditions the eligibility of an account used to hold common interest development association funds on the depository's federal deposit-insurance status or its status as a guaranty corporation subject to California Financial Code § 14858. The § 5380(a) trust-fund-account pathway requires deposit at a California bank, savings association, or credit union insured by the federal government or operating as a § 14858 guaranty corporation; the § 5380(b) board-directed pathway requires deposit at a California bank, savings association, or credit union insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, the National Credit Union Administration Insurance Fund, or a § 14858 guaranty corporation, with all funds in the account covered by such insurance [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]]. The statute does not impose per-depositor coverage caps on association funds, but § 5380(b)(2) limits permissible investments to principal-protected accounts and explicitly prohibits stocks or high-risk investment options [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

## Key claims

- Under § 5380(a), the managing-agent trust fund account must be at a California bank, savings association, or credit union; the depository must qualify as a financial institution under California Financial Code § 31041 and be insured by the federal government or operate as a guaranty corporation subject to § 14858 of the Financial Code [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Under § 5380(b), the board-directed account must be at a California bank, savings association, or credit union insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, the National Credit Union Administration Insurance Fund, or a guaranty corporation subject to § 14858 of the Financial Code [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(2) requires that all of the funds in the board-directed account be covered by insurance provided by an agency of the federal government or a guaranty corporation subject to § 14858 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(2) restricts permissible investment vehicles for board-directed funds to accounts that protect the principal; in no event may those funds be invested in stocks or high-risk investment options [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The statute does not itself enumerate per-depositor insurance caps; the FDIC and NCUA standard coverage limits apply by reference to the federal deposit-insurance frameworks [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The managing agent must disclose to the board the nature of the account, how interest is calculated and paid, whether service charges will be paid to the depository and by whom, and any notice requirements or penalties for withdrawal — per § 5380(b)(4) — providing the board the information needed to verify that the deposit-insurance and principal-protection conditions are met [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
