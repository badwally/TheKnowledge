# Canadian Condominium Corporation Federal Tax Filing Obligations

## Summary

Every Canadian condominium corporation has two distinct federal income-tax filing obligations: an unconditional obligation to file Form T2 (Corporation Income Tax Return) each year under paragraph 150(1)(*a*) of the *Income Tax Act*, and — if it qualifies as a non-profit organization under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) — a parallel obligation to file Form T1044 (Non-Profit Organization Information Return) alongside the T2 [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. The T2 obligation applies regardless of whether the corporation has any taxable income or is exempt from Part I tax. The T1044 obligation is triggered by — not relieved by — claiming the NPO exemption.

## Key claims

- Paragraph 150(1)(*a*) of the *Income Tax Act* requires all corporations, including condominium corporations, to file an income tax return each year [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- The T2 filing obligation applies even if the corporation is exempt from paying tax under Part I [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- A residential condominium corporation that qualifies as a non-profit organization under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) must file Form T1044, *Non-Profit Organization (NPO) Information Return*, with its T2 tax return [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- The NPO exemption from Part I tax does not eliminate the T2 obligation; it adds the T1044 obligation [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- For Quebec condominiums, the syndicate of co-owners established under the Civil Code of Québec is considered to be a corporation for federal income tax purposes and is subject to the same T2 / T1044 regime [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
