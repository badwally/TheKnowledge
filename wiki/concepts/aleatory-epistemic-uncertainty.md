# Aleatory and epistemic uncertainty

## Summary

Aleatory and epistemic uncertainty is the standard two-type decomposition of uncertainty used in probabilistic life-cycle engineering: aleatory uncertainty captures intrinsic, irreducible variability while epistemic uncertainty captures reducible lack of knowledge. The 2026 asphalt pavement review uses this decomposition to organize sources of uncertainty across the pavement life-cycle.

## Key claims

- Asphalt pavement life-cycle analysis recognizes two types of uncertainty — aleatory and epistemic — whose sources can be identified throughout the pavement life-cycle [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Recognizing and distinguishing aleatory and epistemic uncertainty is treated as a prerequisite for rational probabilistic life-cycle multi-objective optimization of pavement systems [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
