# Price Delivery Cycle

## Summary

The price-delivery cycle is the algorithmic loop in which price moves through expansion → target liquidity → consolidation/expansion OR reversal; each expansion phase is expected to create a market structure shift (mss), and the cycle repeats indefinitely as consolidate → expansion → consolidate → expansion [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].

## Key claims

- Price goes from expansion → target liquidity → consolidation/expansion OR reversal [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- The cycle repeats: consolidate → expansion → consolidate → expansion → and so on [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- Each expansion should create a market structure shift (mss) [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- A break in market structure does not necessarily mean immediate reversal — it depends on the context of the market and the narrative for price (consolidate then continue, or reverse) [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- After an mss, traders use the market structure created by subsequent price action to determine what price will do next [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
- As a new trend hits targets, market structure should keep supporting the trend and forming even more market structure shifts [[sources/pdf-4e80c02052f4]].
