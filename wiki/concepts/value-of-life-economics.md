# Value of Life Economics

## Summary

A line of macroeconomic work, originating with Hall and Jones (2007) and extended by Jones (2016), showing that as consumption grows and the marginal utility of consumption declines, society places relatively more value on additional days of life than on increased per-day consumption; Aschenbrenner (2020) builds on this lineage to model preferences over existential-risk reduction [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].

## Key claims

- Hall and Jones (2007), cited in Aschenbrenner (2020), show that for a large class of conventional preferences, as consumption grows and the marginal utility of consumption declines, it becomes relatively more valuable to purchase additional days of life rather than increasing consumption on any given day of life [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Jones (2016), cited in the paper, shows how this can lead society to value safety over consumption growth, resulting in optimal consumption growth lower than what is feasible; in his richer endogenous growth model, lifesaving goods can be purchased to increase people's lifespan [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Aschenbrenner (2020) extends this framework from individual mortality to existential risk, with two critical differences: human activity itself can increase risk, and risk depends on aggregate rather than per-capita variables [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- The flow utility used in the paper is u(c_t) = ū + c_t^(1-γ)/(1-γ), where the constant ū specifies the upper bound of the utility of life relative to death; the utility of death is implicitly normalized to 0 [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- The value of ū parameterizes a level of consumption below which life is not worth living: ū is positive for γ > 1 and negative for γ < 1, following Hall and Jones (2007) [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Expected lifetime utility is U = ∫₀^∞ e^(−ρt) u(c_t) M_t dt, where M_t is the survival probability and ρ is the rate of pure time preference, modeling people who generally do not weight the long-run future heavily [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
