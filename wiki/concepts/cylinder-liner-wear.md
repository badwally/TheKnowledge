# Cylinder liner wear (marine diesel engines)

## Summary

Cylinder liner wear in diesel engines for marine propulsion is a canonical bounded-degradation application: the cylinder liner has finite material to wear away, so the degradation process has an intrinsic upper bound. Liner wear measurements from a marine propulsion diesel engine motivated and serve as the validation dataset for the transformed gamma (TG) process proposed by Fouladirad, Giorgio, and Pulcini.

## Key claims

- Wear measures of cylinder liners that equip a diesel engine for marine propulsion served as the motivating real-data application for the development of the transformed gamma (TG) process [[sources/web-2022-07-07-ac2]].
- The same cylinder liner wear data had previously been analyzed using an unbounded gamma process, providing a benchmark for the bounded TG process's fitting ability [[sources/web-2022-07-07-ac2]].
