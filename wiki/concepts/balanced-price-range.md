# Balanced Price Range

## Summary

A price-action concept attributed to The Inner Circle Trader (ICT) and presented as the subject of the 2022 ICT Mentorship Market Review of August 13, 2022; the underlying theory and worked examples are distributed in PDF form by Braveheart Trading, which explicitly notes that all of the material can be found on The Inner Circle Trader's YouTube channel as the original source [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].

## Key claims

- Presented as the focus of the August 13, 2022 ICT Mentorship Market Review titled "Balanced Price Ranges" [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- Organized in the distributed PDF as two sections: "Balanced Price Range Theory" and "Balanced Price Range Examples" — i.e., a theoretical exposition followed by chart-based illustrations [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- Attributed in the PDF to The Inner Circle Trader, with readers directed to the Inner Circle Trader YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/c/InnerCircleTrader) for the same material [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- The PDF carries an explicit disclaimer that none of its contents should be considered professional advice — "merely for entertainment purposes only" [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
