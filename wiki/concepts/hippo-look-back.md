# HIPPO 20-40-60 Look Back Period

## Summary

HIPPO is Hopiplaka's mnemonic for a time-based ICT trade plan structured around a 20-40-60 day look back window — keyed to the number 9 of the Tesla 3-6-9 framework — in which monthly target numbers (18, 27, 36, 45, 54, 63, 72, 81, 90–99, 108, 117, 126) line up with a recurring "look for number" calendar across the trading year [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].

## Key claims

- The 20-40-60 day look back system corresponds to the time component of Hopiplaka's Tesla 3-6-9 framework, with 9 as its keystone number [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- HIPPO is the named trade plan that operationalizes the look back system: a monthly play targeting hundreds of pips by looking for specific numbers on specific calendar dates [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Each calendar month maps to a specific number to look for: Jan 8 → 18, Feb 7 → 27, Mar 6 → 36, Apr 5 → 45, May 4 → 54, Jun 3 → 63, Jul 2 → 72, Aug 1 → 81, Sep 9 → 90–99, Oct 8 → 108, Nov 7 → 117, Dec 6 → 126 [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- The 20-day Average Daily Range setting (used for day-trading or scalping) and 12-week / 60-trading-day Average Weekly Range setting (used for OSOK or weekly-range trading) tie directly to the 20-40-60 look back partitions [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- The look back periods supply the time levels in Hopiplaka's three-step workflow (price levels from PO3 + Goldbach, time levels from look back + AMD cycle) [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- The HIPPO trade plan is one of several plans documented in Version 1.0.9 — alongside OSOK (catch 50–75 pips once a week), the personal 24-pips-per-week plan, the hidden-OTE plan, and the Goldbach trade-plan family (GB-OB, GB-Breaker, GB-stop-run, GB-equilibrium, GB-FVG) [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]]; Version 1.1.1 carries forward the same trade-plan family [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]]; Version 1.1.3 extends the family with GB-Einstein [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
