# Asphalt pavement life-cycle management

## Summary

Asphalt pavement life-cycle management is the integrated set of modeling, analysis, prediction, design, maintenance, monitoring, optimization, and management activities concerned with the performance of asphalt pavement over its service life, framed under uncertainty and oriented toward rational, probabilistic, multi-objective decision-making across reliability, cost, sustainability, and resilience objectives.

## Key claims

- The body of knowledge on modeling, analysis, prediction, design, maintenance, monitoring, optimization, and management associated with asphalt pavement life-cycle performance is actively evolving [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Despite advances in life-cycle research, the typical lifespan of asphalt pavements remains relatively short, ranging from 15 to 25 years, compared with other infrastructure types such as bridges, which can last up to 100 years [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Rational decisions regarding asphalt pavement systems should be made using an integrated probabilistic life-cycle multi-objective optimization methodology that considers factors such as safety level, total expected cost, and environmental effects [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Key technical sub-areas of asphalt pavement life-cycle management include distress/condition indicators, reliability, risk, life-cycle cost, sustainability, and resilience [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Probabilistic optimization methodologies for asphalt pavements are organized to account for different pavement life-cycle phases [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Uncertainty in asphalt pavement life-cycle management is decomposed into aleatory and epistemic sources identified throughout the pavement life-cycle [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The publicly accessible Long-Term Pavement Performance (LTPP) database, operated under the leadership of the Federal Highway Administration, is a foundational empirical resource for asphalt pavement life-cycle research [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
