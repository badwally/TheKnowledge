# 80/20 Personal-Spending Rule

## Summary

Personal-finance heuristic invoked by TraderEdge's Alex: 20% of the things a household spends money on make up 80% of monthly expenses — so the trader's discipline should focus on the big-ticket categories rather than discretionary trimming [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key claims

- "20% of the things you spend money on make up 80% of your monthly expenses" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The discipline is therefore to "focus on the big stuff and ensure you're not overspending on those," with the named examples being house, car, food, and insurance [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The rule is presented as one of three pillars of trader personal finance alongside the pay-yourself rule and the extended (6–12 month) emergency fund [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
