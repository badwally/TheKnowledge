# Trusted Data Intermediary

## Summary

A "trusted data intermediary" is a regulated organization that holds and negotiates over information producers' data on their behalf, functioning as a guild- or syndicate-style representative in collective bargaining with AI builders [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].

## Key claims

- Vincent, Prewitt and Li (2025) propose regulatory and policy interventions to recognize and support trusted data intermediary organizations representing guilds or syndicates of information producers — journalists, researchers, and creative professionals [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- Trusted data intermediaries are positioned as the producer-side counterweight to AI-driven power concentration, providing collective bargaining capacity that individual producers cannot generate on their own [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
- The intermediary model complements rather than replaces technical mechanisms (federated data tools, explainable data value estimations) that inform what the intermediary negotiates over [[sources/arxiv-2506.10272]].
