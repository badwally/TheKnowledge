# Network-Scale Deterioration Analysis

## Summary

Network-scale deterioration analysis extends element-level deterioration estimation to produce condition estimates for entire bridges and full networks of bridges by aggregating element-level posteriors and handling the large, partially-missing inspection datasets that characterise real asset-management contexts.

## Key claims

- Network-scale analysis aims to extend element-level deterioration estimates beyond individual structural elements to obtain category-, bridge-, and network-level deterioration states [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- At network scale the analyst is dealing with very large amounts of data — hundreds of thousands of structural elements and millions of historical inspections [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Two prerequisites are required to lift element-level estimates to the network: an approach for handling missing inspection and intervention data, and an aggregation method for combining element estimates upward through category, bridge, and network levels [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Missing-data challenges at network scale include erratic inspections, missing inspections, and unreported interventions [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- A demonstration of network-scale deterioration analysis was performed on the Quebec bridge network of approximately 10,000 bridges [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
