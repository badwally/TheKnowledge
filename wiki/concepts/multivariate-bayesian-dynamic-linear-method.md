# Multivariate Bayesian Dynamic Linear Method

## Summary

The multivariate Bayesian dynamic linear method is a state-space modelling approach used to jointly track and forecast multiple correlated time-varying quantities under uncertainty. Yang et al. (2024) apply it as the basis of a bridge performance degradation model that accounts for the combined influence of various factors on bridge structural performance.

## Key claims

- The multi-variate Bayesian dynamic linear method is used as the basis of a bridge performance degradation model in Yang et al. (2024), which frames bridge degradation as arising from the combined influence of various factors that must be jointly modelled for performance assessment and reliable prediction [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- The method underpins downstream work that extends the Bayesian dynamic linear modelling family, including an "enhanced Bayesian dynamic linear model leveraging the kernel regression basis function for severe environmental adaptation" that cites Yang et al. (2024) [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
