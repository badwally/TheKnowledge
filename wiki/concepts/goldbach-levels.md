# Goldbach Levels (Huddleston Levels)

## Summary

Goldbach levels — referred to in Hopiplaka's book as "Huddleston levels" and equated with the IPDA wireframe (IPDA = GOLDBACH) — are the internal price-delivery markers (consequent encroachment, mean threshold, equilibrium, external range demarkers) constructed inside a PO3 dealing range; the number 6 plays the structural role in their division and they govern OTE, order-block, breaker, FVG, and stop-run trade plans [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].

## Key claims

- Hopiplaka explicitly equates IPDA with GOLDBACH and labels the resulting price levels "Huddleston levels" after Michael J. Huddleston (ICT) [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Inside a PO3 dealing range, Goldbach divides the range into key markers including consequent encroachment, mean threshold, equilibrium, and external range demarkers [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- The number 6 is structurally central to the Goldbach division, paralleling the role of 3 (PO3 dealing ranges) and 9 (look back period) in Hopiplaka's Tesla 3-6-9 framework [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Goldbach levels are extended in the book into broader concepts of SMT and Goldbach, market symmetry, support/resistance, and unfulfilled-range analysis [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- The Goldbach framework underpins applied trade plans: hidden-OTE between two Goldbach levels; GB-OB (order block to breaker); GB-Breaker (breaker to high/low); GB-stop-run (dealing-range stop run to breaker); GB-equilibrium (mitigation block and equilibrium); GB-FVG (fair value gap and propulsion block) [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]]; Version 1.1.3 adds GB-Einstein (OB + LV + FVG combined) to this trade-plan family [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Hopiplaka prescribes profile-specific use of Goldbach levels for long-term/position traders, OSOK / short-term traders, day traders, and scalpers [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-94cecf7a170a]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Goldbach Time is the time-domain analog introduced in the book's "Putting it all together" chapter, integrating Goldbach-style structure with AMD cycles [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
