# Multi-Manager Correlation Sensitivity Risk

## Summary

The mathematical sensitivity of a multi-strategy fund's Net Sharpe Ratio and volatility to small increases in average pairwise strategy correlation — and the principal failure mode of the multi-manager structure during market-wide deleveraging events such as the Global Financial Crisis or March 2020, when normally uncorrelated strategies' realized correlations rise [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].

## Key claims

- As correlations between strategies increase, the fund's expected volatility rises while the fund's expected consistency of performance — its Net Sharpe Ratio — declines [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- For a 20-strategy fund: when average strategy correlations rise from 0.0 to just 0.1, modeled Net Sharpe Ratio drops by approximately 40% (from 2.4 to 1.4) and expected Fund Volatility increases by approximately 70% (from 5.4% to 9.1%) [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The sensitivity to changes in correlations increases with the number of strategies in a fund — there is a balance between gaining additional diversification from adding more strategies and having too many strategies [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- For a 100-strategy portfolio, an analogous correlation move from 0.0 to 0.1 implies an approximate 70% drop in Net Sharpe and a 230% rise in expected volatility [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- Common holdings across hedge funds running well-known strategies (such as index rebalance arbitrage or bond basis trading) drive up realized correlations in stressed market environments such as the GFC or March 2020 [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The mechanism: during market-wide deleveraging, funds must reduce positions across all of their fund's strategies as a means of managing risk and sustaining operations, which temporarily increases realized correlations across normally uncorrelated strategies [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- ClearAlpha's prescription: when evaluating multi-manager funds, focus on the fund's ability to find or develop truly uncorrelated and uncommon strategies, allocate meaningful amounts of risk to those strategies, and ensure the fund has the risk expertise and systems to monitor, adjust, and hedge various strategies to keep them independent [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
