# Power of 3 (PO3)

## Summary

The Power of 3 (PO3) is a price-action framework that describes how a candle on any timeframe forms via three phases — Open, then a stop-running raid in one direction, then an expansion to the opposing liquidity — captured by the OLHC (bullish) and OHLC (bearish) templates; the lesson presents PO3 as the basis for framing daily, weekly, and monthly bias [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]]; Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023) operationalizes PO3 as the "Classic Buy Day" and "Classic Sell Day" templates anchored to the midnight NY EST opening price, with the objective of buying below the open on a buy day and selling above the open on a sell day, and identifies the manipulation above/below the opening price as the Judas swing [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- PO3 is built on the OHLC / OLHC sequence — Open, High, Low, Close (bearish) or Open, Low, High, Close (bullish) — which gives an objective way to visualize how a candle on any time frame is forming and the direction in which price seeks liquidity [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The bullish template (OLHC) describes price opening, seeking a discount or sellside liquidity (LOW), then expanding up to attack buyside liquidity (HIGH) and closing near the high — this is how the Market Maker Buy Model (MMBM) forms [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The bearish template (OHLC) describes price opening, seeking a premium or buyside liquidity (HIGH), then expanding down to attack sellside liquidity (LOW) and closing near the low — this is how the Market Maker Sell Model (MMSM) forms [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- PO3 applies to any timeframe: monthly, weekly, and daily candles all use the same OHLC / OLHC logic, anchored to the corresponding open price [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- For monthly bias, the trader works from the monthly candle down to the daily candle, looking back at least 180 days for HTF objectives [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- For weekly and daily bias, the trader looks at recent swing lows/highs over the past 20, 40, and 60 days and identifies in which direction the market structure shift has occurred [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The PO3 framework is used across timeframes; how a trader applies it depends on their psychology and method of trading [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- Per Ali Khan, the PO3 concept enables traders to buy below the opening price on a buy day and sell above the opening price on a sell day [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, midnight NY EST time is the filter for the PO3 concept — the opening price at this time is the "true day opening price" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, on a Classic Buy Day the aim is to buy at or below the midnight open as the low is being formed when a bullish day is anticipated; the trader is not concerned about the close and the objective is to trade the bulk of the expansion move higher [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, on a Classic Sell Day the aim is to sell at or above the midnight open as the high is being formed when a bearish day is anticipated; the objective is to trade the bulk of the expansion move lower [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, ICT refers to the manipulation above/below the opening price as the Judas swing [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, together with time (killzones) and key levels in price (PD Arrays), traders can predict how high or low the Judas swing may reach [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
