# Purge and Revert

## Summary

Purge and Revert is an ICT setup in which price action sweeps (purges) sell stops below a recent low and then reverts back into the high of the last three daily candles — a daily-timeframe stop-hunt-and-reclaim pattern that ICT covers in a dedicated video [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].

## Key claims

- Purge and Revert is defined as price action that purges sell stops and then reverts back into the high of the last three daily candles [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- ICT has a separate, dedicated video on the Purge and Revert setup [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- The setup operationalizes the more general ICT principle that, anytime a significant price move higher is expected, traders should anticipate a stop hunt on sell stops or a short-term low being taken out before the pronounced upward move [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
