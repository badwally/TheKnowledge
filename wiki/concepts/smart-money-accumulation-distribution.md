# Smart money accumulation and distribution

## Summary

Smart money accumulation and distribution refers, in ICT methodology, to institutional positioning that becomes visible in price via the interest rate triad and at focus points such as orderblocks, liquidity pools, and fair value gaps; it is the underlying force a trader is asked to confirm before committing to a setup [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].

## Key claims

- The interest rate triad is presented as the tool that identifies smart-money accumulation and distribution in the interest market [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Overlaying the 30-Year Bond, 10-Year Note, and 5-Year Note shows where accumulation and distribution is taking place [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- A failure swing across the three rates is described as evidence of smart-money participation that validates trade setups [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- The ICT Action Plan operationalizes the concept: when price reaches a focus point, the trader consults the interest rate triad and the DXY to confirm that smart money is behind the trade idea [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- If there is no obvious indication that smart money is moving large funds, the methodology instructs the trader to pass on the trade idea [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
