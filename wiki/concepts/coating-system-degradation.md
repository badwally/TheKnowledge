# Coating system degradation

## Summary

Coating systems on engineered structures undergo measurable, progressive degradation that is well-suited to gamma-process modeling and is used by Wang et al. (2025) as the verification case for their semi-Markov-decision-process-based predictive maintenance policy under nonstationary gamma degradation.

## Key claims

- Wang et al. (2025) verify the effectiveness of their predictive maintenance policy for nonstationary gamma processes using a coating system case study [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The coating system in Wang et al. (2025) is treated as a system whose degradation level is fully revealed by periodic inspection [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
