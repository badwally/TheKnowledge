# National AI Investment Programs

## Summary

The wave of major government commitments — announced in early 2023 by the U.S., U.K., and India — to fund AI compute, data, and research infrastructure as a response to the foundation-model arms race [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key claims

- In January 2023, the U.S. announced a policy of investing US$2.6 billion (approximately 340 billion yen) in the development of a platform that will provide AI computing resources and data [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- In March 2023, the U.K. announced a £900 million (approximately 145 billion yen) investment in next-generation supercomputer development and maintenance, with a view to building a British large language model [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- The Indian government announced the establishment of three AI research centers and the construction of "the world's largest data set" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- The IMD's 2022 global digital competitiveness ranking placed Japan 29th out of 63 countries surveyed, with Japan ranked last in "data utilization" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Japan's prior strategy framework — "AI Strategy 2019," updated annually through "AI Strategy 2022" — covered human resource development, R&D, social implementation, and venture support, but no "AI Strategy 2023" was planned at the time of the whitepaper [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- The whitepaper recommends a new national AI strategy planned at a content and scale that gives Japan an international competitive advantage, including an expanded leadership function for AI policy within government [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Japan's recommended strategy reset includes a comprehensive and urgent review across research and development, economic structure, social infrastructure, human-resource development, and national security [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Japan plans to continuously implement and strengthen talent-development policies in line with AI Strategy 2022 to foster digital talent both for the development phase and for the utilization phase of the foundation-model era [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
