# Predictive maintenance

## Summary

Predictive maintenance uses industrial and business data to develop prognostic and diagnostic models that optimize the life-cycle of machine components.

## Key claims

- Predictive maintenance utilizes industrial and business data for developing prognostic and diagnostic models that allow the optimization of the life cycle of machine components [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The recent data surge of industrial and business data poses both opportunities and challenges for predictive maintenance because the wealth of information is usually buried in complex and frequently disconnected data sets [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- Survival analysis can be used to estimate survival curves and hazard rates for machine components as a basis for predictive-maintenance prognostics [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
