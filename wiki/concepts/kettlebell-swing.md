# Kettlebell Swing

## Summary

The kettlebell swing is a hip-hinge ballistic lift that serves as the volume cornerstone of [[entities/strongfirst|StrongFirst]]'s [[entities/dry-fighting-weight|Dry Fighting Weight]]-style programming — the [[entities/dfw-kettleballs-remix|DFW Kettleballs Remix]] prescribes 200 swings on each of its volume days, paired with rowing or pulling work [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key claims

- The DFW Kettleballs Remix prescribes 200 swings per volume day across weeks 1–4 [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Volume days pair the 200 swings with 10 sets of rows or pullups, indicating the swing is treated as the lower-body / posterior-chain volume component while the row-or-pullup pillar covers the upper-body pull [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Week 5 reduces swing prescription to "Swings and rows as desired," consistent with a deload-and-test wave at the end of the block [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
