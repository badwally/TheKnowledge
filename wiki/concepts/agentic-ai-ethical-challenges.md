# Agentic AI Ethical and Governance Challenges

## Summary

The ethical, societal, and regulatory issues raised by Agentic AI's autonomy — covering goal alignment, resource constraints, environmental adaptability, responsibility, equity, and transparency — and the safety/governance frameworks the Acharya et al. survey proposes for responsible integration [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].

## Key claims

- The survey addresses ethical challenges posed by Agentic AI and proposes solutions for goal alignment, resource constraints, and environmental adaptability [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- It outlines a framework for safely and effectively integrating Agentic AI into society and highlights the need for further research on ethical considerations to ensure beneficial societal impacts [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Section VIII raises social, ethical, and governance issues including responsibility, equity, and compliance with the law [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- The paper calls out the importance of approaches that deal with safety, transparency, and accountability when deploying systems based on this type of AI [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Section IX surveys existing systems that facilitate the constructive and responsible implementation of Agentic AI, including oversight and regulation components [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Recommended future-research directions include integrating issues of scale, context, and ethics into the implementation of Agentic AI, with cross-disciplinary connections required to move the field forward responsibly [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Index terms identified by the authors include human-AI collaboration, governance frameworks, and ethical AI [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
