# Florida Condominium Board Meeting Notice Requirements

## Summary

Florida Statute §718.112(2)(c) governs notice, agenda, and openness requirements for board of administration meetings of Florida condominium associations. The baseline rule is 48-hour conspicuous posting of notice with all agenda items, escalated to 14-day mailed/delivered/electronic notice (with affidavit evidence of compliance) for meetings considering a nonemergency special assessment or amendments to rules regarding unit use. Notice of meetings considering assessments must state the estimated cost and purposes; notice of meetings approving contracts must make the contract available for inspection [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].

## Key claims

- Adequate notice of all board meetings, which must specifically identify all agenda items, must be posted conspicuously on the condominium property at least 48 continuous hours before the meeting except in an emergency [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- If 20 percent of the voting interests petition the board to address an item of business, the board, within 60 days after receipt of the petition, shall place the item on the agenda at its next regular board meeting or at a special meeting called for that purpose [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- An item not included on the notice may be taken up on an emergency basis by a vote of at least a majority plus one of the board members; such emergency action must be noticed and ratified at the next regular board meeting [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Written notice of a meeting at which a nonemergency special assessment or an amendment to rules regarding unit use will be considered must be mailed, delivered, or electronically transmitted to the unit owners and posted conspicuously on the condominium property at least 14 days before the meeting [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Evidence of compliance with the 14-day notice requirement must be made by an affidavit executed by the person providing the notice and filed with the official records of the association [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The board shall, by duly adopted rule and upon notice to the unit owners, designate a specific location on the condominium property at which all notices of board meetings must be posted [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- If there is no condominium property at which notices can be posted, notices shall be mailed, delivered, or electronically transmitted to each unit owner at least 14 days before the meeting [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The association may, by reasonable rule, post and broadcast meeting notice on a closed-circuit cable television system serving the condominium, provided broadcast notice is repeated at least four times every broadcast hour of each day a posted notice is otherwise required [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The association may, by rule, adopt a procedure for conspicuously posting the meeting notice and agenda on a website serving the condominium association for at least the minimum period the notice must be physically posted, and must send an electronic notice with a hyperlink to that website to unit owners whose e-mail addresses are in the association's official records [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Notice of any meeting in which regular or special assessments against unit owners are to be considered must specifically state that assessments will be considered and provide the estimated cost and description of the purposes for such assessments [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- If an agenda item relates to the approval of a contract for goods or services, a copy of the contract must be provided with the notice and be made available for inspection and copying upon a written request from a unit owner, or made available on the association's website or through an application that can be downloaded on a mobile device [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Meetings of a committee to take final action on behalf of the board or to make recommendations to the board regarding the association budget are subject to the same notice and openness rules; other committee meetings are subject to §718.112 generally unless exempted by the bylaws [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The open-meeting requirement does not apply to meetings between the board (or a committee) and the association's attorney with respect to proposed or pending litigation when held to seek or render legal advice, or to board meetings held for the purpose of discussing personnel matters [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
