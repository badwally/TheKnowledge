# Ontario Condominium Corporation Classes

## Summary

O Reg 48/01 distinguishes among four classes of condominium corporation — standard, common elements, vacant land, and phased — each with structural consequences for governance, declaration registration, and the regulation's application [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]]. The classification governs how Schedules A through G of the declaration are constructed, which provisions of Part XI (phased corporations) apply, and which interpretive provisions of §1.1 apply to references to "a portion of the units" in the Act and regulation.

## Key claims

- "Standard condominium corporation" means a freehold condominium corporation that is not a common elements condominium corporation or a vacant land condominium corporation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Corporations created before Part II of the Act came into force and continued as corporations under §178(1) of the Act are classified as standard condominium corporations [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- A common elements condominium corporation has owners whose interest attaches to a parcel of tied land — defined as a parcel of land described in §139(1)(a) of the Act in the case of a common elements condominium corporation and to which a common interest of an owner in the corporation attaches under §139(2)(a) of the Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- A "phase" means the additional units and common elements in a phased condominium corporation that are created in accordance with Part XI of the Act upon the registration of an amendment to both the declaration and description [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- For common elements condominium corporations, the cross-reference rules of §138(4)(a)–(c) of the Act apply to O Reg 48/01 and to the deemed provisions set out in §6.1(1) and §24.1(1) of the regulation, except if otherwise specified [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The first page of the declaration for a standard condominium corporation must contain a statement that the registration of the declaration and description will create a standard condominium corporation, distinguishing it from other classes at the registration stage [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
