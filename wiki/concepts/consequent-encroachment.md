# Consequent Encroachment (C.E.)

## Summary

Consequent Encroachment (C.E.) is the midpoint or 50% equilibrium level of a Fair Value Gap; per Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023) it is one of three "true support & resistance" levels inside an FVG (alongside the premium/high and discount/low boundaries) and will mainly act as a support/resistance level inside the FVG [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- The Consequent Encroachment (C.E.) is the midpoint or 50% equilibrium level of a FVG [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, there are "many caveats" to this level that are beyond the scope of the foundational e-book, but it will mainly act as a support/resistance level inside a FVG [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- C.E. is one of three levels within an FVG that provide true support and resistance — the PREMIUM level (high), the DISCOUNT level (low), and the C.E. at the midpoint [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
