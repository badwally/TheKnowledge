# U.S. CAM Market TAM Sizing

## Summary

The U.S. community association management (CAM) market TAM is sized by Daisy CEO Yotam Cohen at $130 billion, predicated on the following structural facts: more than 40 million U.S. households are part of an HOA across 365,000 communities; approximately 73% of those communities are professionally managed by about 18,000 management companies; and the majority of those management companies are small mom-and-pop operations [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. Cohen frames the market's inefficiency thesis quantitatively: 70% of CAM-company revenue goes to labor performing manual tasks that technology could streamline, while less than 1% of revenue is invested in technology itself [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. NYC alone, where Daisy started, contains approximately 14,400 condo and co-op buildings [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].

## Key claims

- More than 40 million U.S. households are part of an HOA [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- HOA communities total 365,000 in the U.S. [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Approximately 73% of HOA communities are professionally managed [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- The professional-management market is served by approximately 18,000 management companies [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- The majority of those 18,000 management companies are small mom-and-pop operations [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- 70% of CAM-company revenue goes to labor performing manual tasks that technology could streamline [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Less than 1% of CAM-company revenue is invested in technology [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- The implied total addressable market is $130 billion [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- NYC alone contains approximately 14,400 condo and co-op buildings [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
