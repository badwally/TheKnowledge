# Stealth-a-majig (SRAM)

## Summary

"Stealth-a-majig" is SRAM's name for the hose-connection system used on [[entities/sram-hrd|HRD]] brakes — a hose barb plus compression-nut fitting installed at the lever and caliper when the brake hose is shortened or replaced [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].

## Key claims

- The HRD service manual treats Stealth-a-majig hose-barb-and-fitting installation as a discrete procedure, separate from the lever-side and caliper-side compression-nut installations [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Hose work proceeds in a specific order: disconnect the hose at the lever, disconnect at the caliper, route the hose, cut the hose, install the Stealth-a-majig barb and fitting, and reinstall compression nuts at the lever and caliper [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
