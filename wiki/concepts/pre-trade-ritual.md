# Pre-trade ritual

## Summary

Pre-trade ritual is Tom Hougaard's practice of running a personalized PowerPoint deck of past trades, mistakes, triumphs, inspirations, and warnings before each trading session — described as his cue to "become someone else" before engaging the market, by analogy with Maximus rubbing dirt in his hands before combat in Gladiator [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].

## Key claims

- Hougaard's office contains four screens, a keyboard, a mouse, and a PowerPoint presentation on a screen — and he describes the PowerPoint file as "the key to my success" [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The deck contains old trades, mistakes, triumphs, inspirations, and warnings, "visually arranged to prepare me for the day ahead" [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- Its function is to "become something else" before trading — to leave the default self behind, because trading successfully goes counter to virtually every piece of DNA stored in the body [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The named analogy is Maximus Decimus Meridius rubbing dirt on his hands before combat in Gladiator: a ritual of immunising himself before battle, becoming an instrument of death so he can survive another day [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- Hougaard describes the daily window of self-battle as 5am to 9pm and sometimes late into the night — the ritual is a daily practice scaled to the duration of his trading day [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The ritual is presented as the operational complement to the three commands: the commands describe what the trader does, while the ritual is how the trader is loaded into the state required to execute them [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
