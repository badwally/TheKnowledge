# Fair Valuation

## Summary

In ICT (Inner Circle Trader) price-action analysis, "Fair Valuation" describes the condition where price trades back inside its current range and returns to the levels it moved from; these levels are taught as good locations to take profits without needing to move outside the current range [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].

## Key claims

- When the price trades back inside its current range and returns to the levels it moved from, this is Fair Valuation [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- Fair Valuation levels are good locations to take profits on positions and do not require moving outside the current range [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- The related concept of a Fair Value Gap occurs when price leaves a level and only has a small section of price action in one direction; Fair Value Gaps can be objectives for profits or new setups depending on the market environment [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
