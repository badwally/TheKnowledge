# CDIC $100,000 Deposit Insurance Limit

## Summary

The Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC) limits deposit insurance to the first $100,000 of the total amount on deposit at a member financial institution. The limit is the operational constraint that drove a multi-year interpretive dispute over BC Strata Property Regulation §6.11 — strata corporations whose contingency reserve fund or special-levy balances exceeded $100,000 read the regulation to require dividing investments across multiple accounts to keep each balance under the insurance ceiling [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]]. Order in Council 101/2019 resolved the dispute in favour of the broader reading: an account is compliant under §6.11 even when only a portion of the deposit qualifies for CDIC coverage [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]]. CUDIC, the parallel BC credit-union deposit insurer, by contrast offers unlimited deposit insurance protection — meaning the same balance held in a CUDIC-member credit union does not face the partial-coverage problem at all [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].

## Key claims

- CDIC limits deposit insurance to the first $100,000 of the total amount on deposit [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
- Accounts at financial institutions that are members of CUDIC have unlimited deposit insurance protection, in contrast to CDIC's $100,000 ceiling [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
- Many BC strata corporations have CRF or special-levy account balances well in excess of $100,000, making the CDIC ceiling the operative constraint for a substantial portion of the strata-property investment universe [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
- Prior to OIC 101/2019, some strata corporations interpreted §6.11 of the Strata Property Regulation to mean that the entire amount on deposit had to be insured, and accordingly believed they were required to divide their investments across many accounts to ensure no account exceeded the $100,000 limit [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
- The Ministry of Housing characterized this multi-account practice as an 'administrative burden' never intended by the regulation, and stated that it was never intended that strata corporations administer a multitude of accounts to keep balances under the deposit insurance limit [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
