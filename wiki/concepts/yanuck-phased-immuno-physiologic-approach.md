# Yanuck phased immuno-physiologic approach

## Summary

Referenced framework for nutritional and supplement support in COVID-19, published as Yanuck et al., "Evidence to Support a Phased Immuno-physiologic Approach to COVID-19," 2020, Journal of Integrative Medicine, vol 19; cited in Kuon's UCSF Osher Long COVID protocol as the literature anchor for the basic and mast-cell-stabilization supplement stacks [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- Cited verbatim in Kuon's deck as: "Yanuck et al: Evidence to Support a Phased Immuno-physiologic Approach to COVID-19, 2020. Journal of Integrative Medicine, vol 19" [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Operationalized into Kuon's basic protocol of quercetin, vitamin C, zinc picolinate, melatonin, vitamin D3, NAC, turmeric (or alternative resveratrol), omega-3, low-salt/high-potassium anti-inflammatory diet, and optional famotidine [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Extended in the Kuon protocol to a mast-cell-stabilization variant that layers bromelain and perilla on top of the basic stack [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
