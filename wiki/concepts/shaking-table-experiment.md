# Shaking Table Experiment

## Summary

A shaking table experiment is a laboratory test in which a physical structural specimen is subjected to controlled base motions on a programmable table to study its seismic response, generate validation data, and observe damage progression.

## Key claims

- Full-scale shaking table experiments on a reinforced concrete bridge pier provided the real seismic responses used to validate the PINN-based damage identification method of Yamaguchi (2025) [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
- The shaking table tests enabled direct observation of concrete and rebar damage, which was then compared against the spatial distributions of elastic stiffness and ductility factor inferred by the PINN [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
