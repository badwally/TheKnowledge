# TRICKs Alliance

## Summary

"TRICKs" is Zoltan Pozsar's August 24, 2022 acronym for Turkey, Russia, Iran, China, and North Korea — a Eurasian "alliance of the sanctioned" that Pozsar argues is playing a fierce geo-strategic chess game on the Eurasian landmass and putting the U.S. in a checkmate-like position around chokepoints (Bosporus, Hormuz, Taiwan straits) [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].

## Key claims

- Defined as Turkey, Russia, Iran, China, and North Korea — economies sanctioned by the U.S. that are getting ever closer economically and militarily [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar instructs the reader to "forget the BRICS" and focus on TRICKs as the actual geo-strategic actor [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Concrete examples of cooperation cited in the dispatch: Russia and China hold naval exercises with Iran around the Strait of Hormuz; Iran has hosted talks between Russia and Turkey regarding grain shipments through the Bosporus Strait; the first Black Sea grain shipment sailed to the Syrian port of Tartus, which hosts a technical support point of the Russian Navy; Turkey and Russia agreed to clear bilateral trade flows, including gas, in rubles [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar argues the alliance is forcing friends of the U.S. to play pragmatically — for example, despite U.S. pressure India decided to increase imports from Russia and participate in Russia's Vostok-2022 military exercises [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- The TRICKs framing logically extends Chussia: where Chussia is the bilateral commodities-and-industry core, the TRICKs is the wider Eurasian network that controls strategic chokepoints [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar argues the geographic fact that NATO's access to the Black Sea goes through Turkey's Bosporus Strait makes U.S. threats of secondary sanctions on Turkey for financial cooperation with Russia "only that — a threat, or a 'check'" — an unenforceable check on the Eurasian chessboard [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- Pozsar argues forecasts of a rapid deceleration of inflation are "naively optimistic" because the TRICKs are poking holes in the Pax that historically underwrote globalization and lowflation [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
