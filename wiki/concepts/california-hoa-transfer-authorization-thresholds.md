# California HOA Transfer-Authorization Thresholds (Civil Code § 5380(b)(6))

## Summary

California Civil Code § 5380(b)(6) establishes a two-tier dollar-and-percentage threshold regime governing when transfers of funds out of a common interest development association's reserve or operating accounts require prior written board approval. The thresholds scale by association size — the lesser of $5,000 or 5% of estimated annual operating-budget income for associations of 50 or fewer separate interests, and the lesser of $10,000 or 5% of estimated annual operating-budget income for associations of 51 or more separate interests [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]]. Transfers below the applicable threshold may be executed by the managing agent without prior written board approval; transfers at or above the threshold require it [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

## Key claims

- Transfers of funds out of the association's reserve or operating accounts may not be authorized without prior written approval from the board of the association unless the amount of the transfer is less than the applicable threshold [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- For associations with 50 or fewer separate interests, the threshold is the lesser of $5,000 or 5% of the estimated income in the annual operating budget [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- For associations with 51 or more separate interests, the threshold is the lesser of $10,000 or 5% of the estimated income in the annual operating budget [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The thresholds use a "lesser of" rule — the binding cap is whichever value is smaller, making the percentage rule dispositive for associations with annual operating-budget income below $100,000 (small tier) or $200,000 (large tier) [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The threshold framework was set by Stats. 2021, Ch. 270, Sec. 1 (AB 1101), effective January 1, 2022 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The threshold applies to both reserve and operating account transfers — § 5380(b)(6) does not distinguish between the two account types for purposes of the prior-written-approval rule [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
