# Canadian Condo Corporation — Incidental Income Test under Paragraph 149(1)(l)

## Summary

The incidental income test is CRA's factual two-prong analysis for determining whether profit earned by a Canadian condominium corporation will preserve or jeopardize the corporation's paragraph 149(1)(l) tax-exempt status. Per CRA External Technical Interpretation 2014-0528171E5, income qualifies as "incidental" only if it is both (a) minor in scale and (b) directly related to activities undertaken to meet the corporation's not-for-profit objectives of managing and maintaining the condominium property and required reserves [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]]. Whether income meets the test is explicitly a question of fact, not a categorical rule [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].

## Key claims

- Incidental income is defined by a conjunctive two-prong test: minor AND directly related to NPO objectives [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].
- Whether income is incidental is a question of fact, determined case-by-case [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].
- Courts have recognized that an organization claiming the 149(1)(l) exemption can earn a profit so long as the profit is incidental and arises from activities directly connected to its not-for-profit objectives [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].
- Maintaining reasonable operating reserves or bank accounts required for ordinary operations is generally an activity undertaken to meet NPO objectives [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].
- Incidental profit arising from those reserves or accounts will not affect the tax-exempt status of an organization [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].
- Profit that is incidental and connected to NPO objectives, and that is used within the organization to support those objectives, generally is not taken into account in determining whether income is available for the personal benefit of a member [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].
- Incidental income from the rental of common areas may be treated as income of the Corporation and generally will not affect the corporation's tax-exempt status [[sources/web-2014-08-18-d79]].
