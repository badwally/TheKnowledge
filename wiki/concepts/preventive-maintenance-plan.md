# Preventive Maintenance Plan (BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E6.0)

## Summary

A Preventive Maintenance Plan, as scoped by BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB question E6.0, is a baseline (mandatory) plan for buildings seeking BOMA BEST certification that captures how day-to-day operations and maintenance affect energy consumption, carbon emissions, equipment longevity, and operating efficiency [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]]. It is the operational-practice counterpart to a reserve fund study: where the reserve study models capital replacement, the PM plan governs the inspections and maintenance actions that determine when those replacements actually become necessary [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key claims

- E6.0 is a baseline practice and minimum requirement for BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB certification, applicable to Office, Healthcare, Enclosed Shopping Centre, Universal, and Multi-Unit Residential buildings [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan must include an inventory of the building's systems and equipment components to be reviewed, including gas-fired boilers and other onsite combustion equipment [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan must specify the type of preventative action required, with the frequency of inspection vs. maintenance actions distinguished [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan must include sample logs showing that these actions have been taken and that follow-ups were done when needed [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan must record updates when new equipment is added and when old equipment is removed [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan must be signed by the building manager and dated within the last 12 months [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The required documentation is the Preventative Maintenance Plan itself, with maintenance logs [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The assessment methodology should at least align with ASHRAE Standard 180-2018 — Standard Practice for Inspection and Maintenance of Commercial Building HVAC Systems [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- BOMA Canada provides a Preventive Maintenance Best Practices template (BP01_PM-Program_Template-1.docx) as a reference resource [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The suggested lead is in-house, not third-party [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan should be revisited periodically to ensure building operations are optimized [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The E6.0 question is adapted from BOMA BEST 3.0 Best Practice 1, which asked whether a Preventative Maintenance Program was in place at the building [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
