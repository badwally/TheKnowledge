# Nova Scotia Trustee Diversification Requirement (§3B)

## Summary

§3B of the Nova Scotia Trustee Act, introduced by the 2002 c. 10 s. 45 amendment, requires a trustee to diversify the investment of trust property to an extent that is appropriate having regard to the requirements of the trust and general economic and investment market conditions [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]]. Diversification is one of the four core operative obligations introduced by the 2002 amendment alongside the prudent-investor care standard (§3A), the liability safe harbor (§3C), and the delegation regime (§3F) [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].

## Key claims

- §3B requires a trustee to diversify the investment of trust property [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- The required extent of diversification is judged by reference to (a) the requirements of the trust and (b) general economic and investment market conditions [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- §3B was introduced by 2002 c. 10 s. 45 as part of the broader prudent-investor amendment package [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- The diversification obligation operates in conjunction with the §3A care-skill-diligence-judgment standard and the §3(3) planning criteria, which together describe the trustee's planning posture for invested trust property [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
