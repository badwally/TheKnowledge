# NJ 85% Funding Option for Existing Associations

## Summary

S3992 (August 2025) reversed S2760's outright prohibition on funding NJ reserves at 85% of the study plan, and created a five-year transitional relief allowing associations existing as of January 8, 2024 to fund their capital reserve fund at 85% of the recommended plan — subject to a 20-point bold-font owner-notice obligation disclosing the anticipated year and amount of any resulting special assessment or loan [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]]. After five fiscal years, the association must transition to a plan satisfying the zero-dollar adequacy requirement.

## Key claims

- Available only to associations existing as of January 8, 2024 [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Qualifying associations may fund either according to their most recent capital reserve study or at 85% of the capital reserve recommended plan [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- An association funding at 85% must provide notice to all unit owners of the residential development [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The notice must be in 20-point bold font [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The notice must describe that the association's executive board is funding the capital reserve fund at 85% of what the association's 30-year plan recommended [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The notice must include the year in which the special assessment or loan is anticipated due to the reduced funding [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The notice must include the amount the board anticipates needing because of the reduction [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Associations may use the 85% option for only five fiscal years [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- After five fiscal years, the association must transition to a plan satisfying the zero-dollar requirement [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Before S3992, S2760 prohibited funding reserves at 85% of the study plan; the 85% option is a new transitional path created by S3992 [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The 85% notice is the upstream document that, under the NJ buyer-disclosure requirement, sellers must deliver to buyers before completion of a unit sale [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
