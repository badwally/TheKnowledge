# Pre-gap anticipatory drift

## Summary

Pre-gap anticipatory drift is the empirical pattern documented by Zarattini and Stamatoudis (2024) that, in the fifteen trading days preceding a ≥6% upside overnight gap, U.S. stocks exhibit a gradual price rise from roughly -26% up to 0%, consistent with anticipatory buying or positive sentiment ahead of the catalyst [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].

## Key claims

- In the fifteen days preceding a gap event, stock prices exhibit a gradual increase, starting from approximately -26% and moving towards 0% [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- This trend suggests a period of anticipatory buying or positive market sentiment, with traders likely positioning themselves ahead of expected positive news, contributing to a steady price rise [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- The progression from -26% to 0% indicates a systematic build-up in stock prices as market participants respond to signals and information that precede the gap event [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- The pattern is documented across the paper's full sample of 9,794 gap events on U.S. NYSE/Nasdaq stocks from 2016 to 2023, plotted as the -15-to-0 segment of the around-gap behavior figure [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
