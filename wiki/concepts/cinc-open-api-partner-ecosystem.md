# CINC Open API Partner Ecosystem

## Summary

The CINC Open API Partner Ecosystem is the integration architecture and approved-partner network that CINC Systems operates to extend its community association management platform across ten functional categories of association management — collections, resale documents, print and mail services, accounts payable, reserve planning, property inspections, vendor screening, staffing, e-filing, and payments [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]]. The ecosystem combines three architectural elements: an open API enabling approved partners to securely connect their solutions; a partnerships-team-administered application and approval process; and a value-exchange where partners gain access to CINC's network of community association management companies in return for delivering integrated solutions to CINC's client base [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].

## Key claims

- CINC operates an open API that allows approved partners to securely connect their solutions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- The API is positioned to ensure seamless data flow across key property management functions including collections, payments, and vendor management [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Partner solutions must support property management operations such as collections, document management, payments, or vendor services to qualify for the partner application and approval process [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- CINC's partnerships team operates as the gateway for the application and approval process [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- The ecosystem spans ten functional categories: collections, resale documents, print and mail services, accounts payable, reserve planning, property inspections, vendor screening, staffing, e-filing, and payments [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Partners receive three named benefits: access to a large network of community association management companies, increased exposure through the partner ecosystem, and the opportunity to deliver integrated solutions that improve client operations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- CINC publicly states it welcomes client feedback and regularly evaluates opportunities to expand the network of integrated software solutions based on customer needs [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- The 2026 published partner list includes named integrations across six of the ten categories: collections (Axela, TechCollect), resale documents (HomeWise, CondoCerts), print and mail services (HOA Mailers, Letter Stream), vendor management (VIVE, VendorSmart), staffing (Gordian, RocketStation), and payments (ClickPay) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Four categories appear in the ecosystem's category framing without named partners on the 2026 page: accounts payable, reserve planning, property inspections, and e-filing [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
