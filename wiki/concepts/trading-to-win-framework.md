# Trading to Win Framework

## Summary

A goal-oriented psychological-behavioral approach to trading, defined by Ari Kiev in his 1998 book "Trading to Win," intended to help traders maximize performance by tapping personal resources they might not know they had, developing a rational trading strategy, learning new psychological skills, and letting go of unproductive — even maladaptive — behavior patterns [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].

## Key claims

- Defined as a goal-oriented approach designed to help traders maximize performance in a unique way: by tapping personal resources, developing rational strategy, learning new psychological skills, and letting go of unproductive behavior patterns [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Puts special emphasis on learning to get rid of past memories and erroneous notions around which people have organized their lives [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Asks the trader to commit to a future goal by surrendering to it, while simultaneously relinquishing all thoughts of gain, achievement, or attachment — an explicitly paradoxical posture [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Encourages trusting a higher power that assists the trader in realizing the power within themselves; periodically helps the trader refocus on the goal, realigning oneself with objectives in the present moment [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Holds that giving up the goal of financial gain paradoxically yields the freedom to genuinely listen to the sounds of the marketplace and read stock-price movement in a way that increases the probability of success [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Operationalized through commitment, concentration, recovery, and preparation for the next day as the proactive trading strategies the framework requires [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per Kiev, the approach is "not for the fainthearted": it asks for counterintuitive behaviors — admitting uncertainty, fear, and lack of knowledge; asking for help; sharing information; and facing vulnerability — all of which require letting go of ego and arrogance [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per Steve Cohen's foreword, applying the framework helped SAC Capital Management grow from a $20 million fund to one handling over $500 million annually within five years, with some individual traders' profits up 100% [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
