# Canadian Condominium Corporation NPO Status (ITA §149(1)(l))

## Summary

Paragraph 149(1)(*l*) of the Canadian *Income Tax Act* exempts qualifying non-profit organizations from Part I income tax. CRA Interpretation Bulletin IT-304R2 (June 2, 2000) establishes the administrative position that 'most residential condominium corporations qualify as non-profit organizations within the meaning of' paragraph 149(1)(*l*), although qualification in any individual case remains a question of fact [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. NPO status is the principal mechanism by which Canadian residential condominium corporations achieve federal income-tax-exempt treatment on their operating activities, including the interest earned on operating and reserve funds.

## Key claims

- Paragraph 149(1)(*l*) of the *Income Tax Act* provides the non-profit organization exemption from Part I tax [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- A residential condominium corporation that qualifies as an NPO under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) is exempt from Part I tax on its taxable income [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Whether a particular condominium corporation qualifies for the exemption is a question of fact, per CRA's administrative position in IT-304R2 ¶ 4 [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- CRA's administrative position, stated in IT-304R2 ¶ 4, is that 'most residential condominium corporations qualify as non-profit organizations within the meaning of' paragraph 149(1)(*l*) [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- If a condominium corporation carries on a business, any profits from that business must be included in its income and the corporation will not be considered a non-profit corporation [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- The conditions necessary to qualify as a tax-exempt non-profit organization are described in the current version of CRA Interpretation Bulletin IT-496, *Non-Profit Organizations* [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- NPO status does NOT relieve the corporation of its obligation under paragraph 150(1)(*a*) to file a T2 corporate income tax return each year [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- NPO status TRIGGERS an additional obligation to file Form T1044, *Non-Profit Organization (NPO) Information Return*, alongside the T2 [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- CRA's revised position underlying IT-304R2 was first announced in the last article of Income Tax Technical News No. 4, dated February 20, 1995 [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
