# Reserve Fund Investment Objective Triad (Principal Protection / Liquidity / Returns)

## Summary

The reserve-fund investment objective triad is the practitioner framework that Will MacKay (CIBC Wood Gundy) advises Ontario condominium boards to apply when developing an investment plan under Condominium Act §115(5). The three objectives — in stated priority order — are (i) principal protection of investments and adherence to the Condominium Act, (ii) liquidity of funds and effective expenditure planning, and (iii) maximizing investment returns. The order is non-arbitrary: principal protection and statutory compliance precede liquidity, and liquidity precedes return-maximization, reflecting the fiduciary character of the reserve fund [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].

## Key claims

- The three most important areas for an Ontario condominium board to consider in developing a reserve-fund investment strategy are (i) principal protection of investments and adherence to the Condominium Act, (ii) liquidity of funds and how to meet expenditure requirements with effective planning, and (iii) maximizing investment returns [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The objectives are stated in priority order: principal protection and statutory adherence come first, liquidity second, and return-maximization third [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Adherence to the Condominium Act is bundled with principal protection rather than treated as a separate constraint, reflecting the fact that §115(5)'s eligible-securities definition is itself a principal-protection mechanism [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The triad maps directly onto the GIC laddering pattern: §115(5)-eligible CDIC/DICO-insured GICs satisfy principal protection; the laddered maturity schedule delivers liquidity; the duration premium on the longer ladder rungs delivers return [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- An effective Ontario reserve-fund investment plan applies the triad through a portfolio that combines project-specific duration-matched GICs (or HISAs), and a laddered general-reserve GIC tranche — not through a single instrument or strategy [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
