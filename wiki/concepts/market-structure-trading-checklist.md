# Market-Structure Trading Checklist

## Summary

The operational core of Lesson 2: Market Structure is a five-step checklist — Liquidity → Displacement → Find Order Blocks and Imbalances → Watch for price to enter the Point of Interest → Know your targets — used to filter setups, time entries, place stops, and avoid the consolidation chop [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].

## Key claims

- The Lesson 2 checklist is enumerated as: Liquidity (needs to be taken first); Displacement (or lack of displacement); Find your Order Blocks and Imbalances; Watch for price to enter your Point of Interest; Know your targets [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The first step requires liquidity to be taken before the rest of the checklist applies — failed displacement that runs stops counts as taking liquidity [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The displacement step asks whether price expanded out of consolidation with energy and whether that move hit its target — for example, in Lesson 2's worked bearish setup, the move down failed to hit the target swing low, which is the cue to look for a retrace and re-entry [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The OB-and-imbalance step requires marking the displacement-residue zone — green candles before displacement down (bearish OB) or red candles before displacement up (bullish OB) — alongside the candle-gap imbalance [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The POI step requires watching for price to enter the OB-and-imbalance zone and then move away from it with energy in the displacement direction [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The targets step asks which liquidity pool price is now seeking — Lesson 2 reasons that if buyside liquidity has been taken and price is displacing down, the targets are the swing lows on the left side of the chart [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- Lesson 2 motivates the checklist with a process claim: market structure lets the trader "see where smart money is positioned long or short," join the trend with a defined R, visualize the move, and avoid trading against the trend or trading in chop (consolidation phase of price) [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- Stop-placement is tied to the checklist itself — stops go at the most recent swing high or low, which often coincides with the order block boundary; if the trade direction were wrong, the OB would be taken out quickly with displacement in the opposite direction [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
- The checklist is presented in Lesson 2 as a setup for the next lesson, which is described as defining when price is becoming bullish or bearish and when to avoid trading the chop [[sources/pdf-81305d181382]].
