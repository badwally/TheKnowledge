# Cultural and Regional Bias in AI Training Data

## Summary

The phenomenon by which foundation models trained on data dominated by certain languages, regions, and cultures perform worse on inputs and outputs related to under-represented contexts — a quality gap with direct economic-competitiveness consequences for the under-represented region [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key claims

- Cultural and regional disparities (biases) in foundation model AI arise from biases in the data used for training [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Users of an overseas image-generation AI service have reportedly been unable to generate accurate or useful pictures of Japan because of insufficiently trained data on Japanese visual materials and cultural backgrounds — illustrated by an example using the prompt "Shrines of Japan" on a non-Japanese generative AI platform [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- If AI performance is internationally inferior in processing related to Japan, this could have a direct impact on Japan's economic competitiveness [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Japan's response is to promote use of public and private sector data through AI in the next review of the "Comprehensive Data Strategy," including development of a standard data model and clarification of data attributes and structure [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Even in areas of intrinsic Japanese strength — such as high-quality medical data accumulated under universal health insurance — data cannot be fully utilized because unified formats are lacking across systems [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Japan plans to archive public data held by national and local governments in formats usable by foundation models, and to clarify rules for third-party provision [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Japan plans to actively provide appropriate Japanese-language training data to domestic and international foundation models and to lead efforts to create and utilize Japanese-language corpora, in order to address data bias [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Japan also plans to enrich training data for source-code-generation AI to address software-developer shortage and improve productivity [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
