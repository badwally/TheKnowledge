# Plurality Model of Intelligence

## Summary

The framework advanced by Evans, Bratton, and Agüera y Arcas (2026) as an explicit alternative to the "monolithic singularity" view of AI: intelligence is high-dimensional and relational rather than a single quantity, and the next intelligence explosion will be plural, social, and deeply entangled with humans — "intelligence growing like a city, not a single meta-mind" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key claims

- The traditional AI singularity vision — a single titanic mind bootstrapping itself to godlike intelligence and consolidating all cognition into a cold silicon point — is, in the authors' assessment, "almost certainly wrong in its most fundamental assumption" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Intelligence is high-dimensional and relational, not a single quantity that must be unambiguously less or greater than human scale [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The notion of "human scale" is itself unclear given that human intelligence is already a collective property, not an individual one [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Each prior "intelligence explosion" was not an upgrade to individual cognitive hardware, but the emergence of a new, socially aggregated unit of cognition [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Primate intelligence scaled with social group size (Dunbar's social brain hypothesis), not habitat difficulty — cited as the canonical case of socially-aggregated cognition [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The vision is described as neither utopian nor dystopian but evolutionary: any emergent intelligence explosion will be seeded by eight billion humans interacting with hundreds of billions, eventually trillions, of AI agents [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The scaffold is "not a single mind ascending but a combinatorial society complexifying: intelligence growing like a city, not a single meta-mind" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- A monolithic-singularity framework leads to policies aimed at preventing a technology that may never exist; the authors instead direct attention to cooperative, competitive and creative interaction between multitudes of socially intelligent minds, most of which will be non-biological [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The plurality model focuses attention on the design of mixed human-AI social systems, the norms that govern them, and the institutions and protocols through which they conflict and coordinate [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The authors claim that "in a very real sense, the intelligence explosion is already here" — visible in the society of thought inside reasoning models, in centaur workflows reshaping knowledge professions, and in recursive agent ecologies beginning to fork and collaborate at scale [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
