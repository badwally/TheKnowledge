# Alberta Credit Union Deposit Coverage (100% Unlimited)

## Summary

Alberta credit union depositors enjoy 100% deposit coverage on all deposit types — chequing, savings, foreign currency, and term deposits (both registered and non-registered) of any length — with no dollar-amount ceiling, backed by the Government of Alberta through the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation (CUDGC) under the Alberta *Credit Union Act* [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]]. This unlimited-coverage regime is structurally analogous to CUDIC's unlimited coverage for British Columbia credit unions and stands in contrast to CDIC's $100,000-per-deposit ceiling (federal banks) and FSRA's $250,000 non-registered ceiling (Ontario credit unions, with unlimited coverage only for registered accounts). For Alberta condominium reserve funds held at credit unions, the unlimited-coverage feature eliminates the per-institution-aggregation problem that drives multi-institution GIC laddering strategies in CDIC-governed contexts.

## Key claims

- 100% of all deposits held in Alberta credit union chequing, savings, and foreign currency accounts are guaranteed [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- 100% of registered term deposits of any length are guaranteed [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- 100% of non-registered term deposits of any length are guaranteed [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- The Alberta guarantee is described as comprehensive and applies "up to any dollar amount" [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- The guarantee is backed by the Government of Alberta as the third statutory level of protection [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Non-deposit investments — common shares, investment shares, and self-administered savings plans — are explicitly excluded from coverage [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- The coverage regime applies only to Alberta credit unions and only to depositors who are members of those credit unions [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
