# Expect to be uncomfortable (Command 2)

## Summary

"Expect to be uncomfortable" is the second of Tom Hougaard's three commands: it instructs the trader to enter every position with the expectation of pain, contrasting how the 95% negotiate with pain (avoid, suppress, exit early) against how the 5% negotiate with pain (run toward it, embrace it, hold the position) [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].

## Key claims

- The command is structured as a behavioural fork: how the 95% negotiate with pain versus how the 5% negotiate with pain [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- Hougaard's contrast is explicit: while the majority look to run away and rid themselves of pain, his discipline is to do the opposite — run towards it and embrace it [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- Pain is treated as inevitable in life — Hougaard cites the everyday remedy of talking to a friend ("a problem shared is a problem halved") as ineffective inside the trade, where there is no time for verbalisation to dissipate the pain [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- "Run towards the danger" is the chapter heading for this idea: the speculator is made, not born, and skill is built by repeated exposure to discomfort rather than its avoidance [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
- The command is the practical operationalization of the loss-aversion-asymmetry finding: by pre-committing to discomfort, the trader interrupts the impulsive pain-relief response that produces 95%-behaviour outcomes [[sources/pdf-1590d4650297]].
