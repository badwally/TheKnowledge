# Platform Cooperativism

## Summary

Platform cooperativism is an organisational movement that proposes cooperatively owned and governed digital platforms as an alternative to investor-owned platform-capitalist firms, with data ownership and revenue distributed to users or workers rather than concentrated in a few corporate platforms [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key claims

- Platform cooperatives occupy positions analogous to those of platform-capitalist firms but change ownership structures and foreground ethical data use [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- The concept was developed by Trebor Scholz and Nathan Schneider (2016) and elaborated in subsequent work by Scholz (2023) [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Data cooperatives are closely related to, and frequently a subset of, platform cooperatives, since they similarly displace centralized data ownership with user- or worker-ownership [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
