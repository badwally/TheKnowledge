# Agentic AI

## Summary

An emerging AI paradigm denoting autonomous systems that pursue complex goals with minimal human intervention, combining adaptability, advanced decision-making, and self-sufficiency to operate in dynamic, uncontrolled environments [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].

## Key claims

- Agentic AI is defined as a class of autonomous AI systems that undertake complex tasks spanning long periods without human supervision, learning context and making decisions in changing settings [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Unlike traditional AI, which depends on structured instructions and close oversight, Agentic AI demonstrates adaptability, advanced decision-making capabilities, and self-sufficiency, enabling dynamic operation in evolving environments [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Agentic AI represents a qualitative leap in AI development, characterized by the ability to set complex goals in changing, uncontrolled situations and pursue them by autonomously managing resources [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- The paradigm sits between purely reactive, narrowly rule-based AI on one side and broader notions of AGI on the other, performing autonomous decision-making within defined boundaries [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Agentic AI surpasses classical and generative AI by being goal-oriented, input-formed, and adaptable, accomplishing intricate multi-layered tasks without a fresh set of instructions each time [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- The motivation for Agentic AI is the need for tools that operate in complex real-world conditions — disaster relief, healthcare, cybersecurity — where chaos is considerable and independent situational control is critical [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Agentic systems are not merely assistive: they take on tasks demanding high involvement and multi-tasking without constant human intervention, expanding AI from passive/reactive to strategic planning and problem-solving [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Core engineering challenges in building Agentic AI include goal design and convergence, context adaptation, and operating under limited resources [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
