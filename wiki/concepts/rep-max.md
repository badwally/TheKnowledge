# Rep Max (1RM, 5RM)

## Summary

A rep max — written as nRM, e.g., 1RM (one-rep max) or 5RM (five-rep max) — is the maximum load a lifter can move for that exact number of reps in a single set, used by programs such as [[entities/bethskw|/u/Bethskw]]'s [[entities/dfw-kettleballs-remix|DFW Kettleballs Remix]] both as a load-prescription input and as the end-of-cycle test [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key claims

- The DFW Kettleballs Remix prescribes working load in terms of a press-specific 5RM: "This is intended to be done with a pair of kettlebells at your 5RM press weight" [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- The same program's Week 5 closes with a retest session whose explicit aim is to "Find a new 1RM or rep max," using the rep max as the progress signal at the end of the block [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Different rep maxes serve different purposes within one program — the 5RM serves as a working-weight benchmark, while the 1RM serves as a peak-output benchmark used at retest [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
