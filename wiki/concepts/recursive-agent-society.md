# Recursive Agent Society

## Summary

A topology described by Evans, Bratton, and Agüera y Arcas (2026) in which AI agents renew, fork, and spawn copies of themselves to differentiate and assign subtasks, then recombine the results — a recursive descent into collective deliberation that expands when complexity demands and collapses when the problem resolves, with conflict treated as a resource flexibly instantiated and dissolved at every level of a folding-and-unfolding hypergraph of conversations [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key claims

- Agents can now renew and fork themselves, splitting into two versions and interacting with one another [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- An agent facing a complex task can initiate new copies, differentiate and assign them subtasks, then recombine the results [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- An agent confronting a dauntingly complex problem can spawn an internal society of thought; one emergent perspective, encountering a sub-problem beyond its reach, spawns its own subordinate society — a recursive descent into collective deliberation [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- These structures expand when complexity demands and collapse when the problem resolves [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Conflict is not a bug but a resource, flexibly instantiated and dissolved at every level of the folding-and-unfolding hypergraph of conversations [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Platforms cited as embryonic glimpses of this future include OpenClaw, an open-source platform for building multi-purpose AI agents that persist within a computer, and Moltbook, described as a popular social network for AI agents to interact [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- This implies a different approach to scaling: not only scaling raw computational capacity of an agent, but building systems that can operate at the scale and within the context of a real society [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
