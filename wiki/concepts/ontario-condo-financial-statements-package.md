# Ontario Condominium Financial Statements Package

## Summary

The CAO Guide on Condo Finances enumerates five financial statements that typically comprise an Ontario condominium corporation's financial statements package: a Statement of Financial Position, a Statement of Operations, a Statement of Operating Fund, a Statement of Reserve Fund, and a Statement of Cash Flows [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]]. The package exists in two parallel forms — internal unaudited financial statements (typically prepared monthly by the property manager, or by directors / a bookkeeper for self-managed corporations) and audited financial statements (prepared annually and presented to owners at the annual general meeting) — and has four principal user groups: directors, owners, auditors, and lenders [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].

## Key claims

- A condominium corporation financial statement package typically includes five statements: Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Operations, Statement of Operating Fund, Statement of Reserve Fund, and Statement of Cash Flows [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Financial statements summarize the financial activities of a condominium corporation and provide a picture of the financial position at a fixed point in time or over a longer period (a month, a year, or year-to-date) [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Two types of financial statements exist in parallel: internal unaudited financial statements (prepared by management if not self-managed) and audited financial statements [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Four main user groups of the financial statements: directors, owners, auditors, and lenders [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Directors review and use monthly unaudited financial statements to monitor the corporation's financial position throughout the year, and review and approve annual audited financial statements for presentation to owners at the annual general meeting [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Owners receive a copy of the annual audited financial statements once approved by the directors, in the annual general meeting package, or upon request during the year [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Potential purchasers of a condominium unit receive a copy of the annual audited financial statements as part of the status certificate package [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Auditors audit the annual internal financial statements and attach their auditor's report for distribution to owners [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Lenders require audited statements if the condominium corporation requires a loan [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Monthly unaudited financial statements are usually prepared by the corporation's manager and should typically be submitted for review to the board by the 20th of every month, before the monthly board of directors meeting; all directors should receive a copy [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- For self-managed condominium corporations, the financial statements are prepared by the directors or by a bookkeeper monthly [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Separate schedules for the Reserve Fund, Contingency Fund (if used), and Capital Asset Fund (if applicable) should be included in the financial statement package, each detailing contributions, withdrawals, and other fund-specific activity [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- A bank reconciliation should be included for each bank account in the monthly financial package [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
