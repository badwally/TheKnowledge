# BC Strata Trust Fund Obligations

## Summary

BC strata trust fund obligations are the disclosure, segregation, and fraud-prevention duties that flow to strata councils and strata managers when investing or holding contingency reserve fund and special levy money. CHOA Bulletin 600-003 operationalizes these duties: investments must be held in trust accounts named for the strata corporation, the strata manager must disclose account locations and provide monthly statements, and the strata council must independently scrutinize balances [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].

## Key claims

- Investments must always be placed in trust and accounted for separately; they are not in pooled investment accounts [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Trust accounts must be designated "in trust for the owners, strata plan _ _ _ _ _ _" [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The strata corporation must consult with a qualified official with a financial institution governed under the Bank of Canada Act or the Financial Institutions Commission of British Columbia [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- How investments are made, where the funds are held, and the term of investments are the decision of the strata council [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- All council decisions are made by a majority vote and are to be recorded in the council minutes; the minutes provide notice to owners as to how their money will be invested and provide the authority for the strata manager or an authorized council member to make the investment [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Strata managers in British Columbia are not licensed to provide investment advice; if a strata corporation is considering investments other than secure term deposits or GICs, it should seek independent legal and investment advice [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- For the strata manager to have authority to invest and transfer strata corporation funds, the strata council must give instructions in writing — by strata council minutes, contractual obligations, or an email or memo from the strata council — to avoid misunderstandings [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The decisions of council to invest the fund are subject to any resolutions passed by the owners at an annual or special general meeting or as stipulated in the bylaws of the strata corporation [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- If a strata manager holds funds for a strata corporation, the money must be held in a separate trust account in the name of the strata corporation [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The strata manager must disclose the location of the funds and provide the strata corporation with the actual monthly bank and investment statements of those accounts [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Trust funds held by a strata manager have limited protection by the Real Estate Special Compensation Fund [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Disclosure requirements are imposed on strata managers by the Real Estate Services Act and the Regulations and Rules of the Real Estate Council of British Columbia; non-compliance may indicate a serious problem requiring immediate strata council action [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The CHOA bulletin suggests that monthly financial reports produced by the treasurer or strata manager include statements of all investment funds, copies of the investment certificates, and yields, with the reports provided to strata council members for approval [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The CHOA bulletin suggests that copies of the investment certificates or instruments be provided as an attachment to the council meeting minutes following the investment taking place [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- It is prudent for a strata corporation to implement processes for fraud prevention, including ensuring signing authority is properly delegated, separate accounts for all funds, and reimbursements and replenishing petty cash only with approved receipts [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
