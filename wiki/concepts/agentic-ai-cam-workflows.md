# Agentic AI in CAM Workflows

## Summary

Agentic AI in community association management (CAM) refers to AI systems framed not as chatbots or copilots but as named "teammates" with full ownership of specific repeatable workflows, escalating to humans only at defined decision points. The pattern is in active deployment at two named CAM vendors as of 2024–2025: Daisy operates "Steven" (takeover-onboarding workflow with the prior management company) and "Lily" (resident-support workflow, 22% of all incoming requests as of September 2025) [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]; Vantaca acquired HOAi in November 2024 and characterized the deal as integrating "advanced AI capabilities" to "build the future of community management" [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]], later positioning itself in October 2025 around "true agentic AI capabilities that transform daily operations rather than just digitizing old processes" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. The shared operating thesis is that giving agents full workflow ownership lets the firm scale faster without proportional headcount growth or service degradation.

## Key claims

- Agentic-AI CAM workflows are framed as full-workflow-ownership AI agents that escalate to humans at decision points, not as chatbots or copilots assisting a human owner [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Daisy's "Steven" agent owns the takeover-onboarding workflow with prior management companies — chasing documents, following up on missing items, and checking in with humans at decision points [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Daisy's "Lily" support agent handles 22% of all incoming resident requests as of September 2025 [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Cohen attributes the agentic-AI posture to a measurable org-structure shift at Daisy: "Instead of throwing more people at problems, we're designing roles around what humans do best — and giving agents full ownership of repeatable workflows. That's allowed us to scale faster without sacrificing service" [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Vantaca characterizes its agentic-AI capabilities as transforming daily operations rather than just digitizing old processes [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Vantaca acquired HOAi in November 2024 and characterized the deal as integrating advanced AI capabilities to "build the future of community management" [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Daisy claims platform upgrades over the year preceding September 2025 — during which AI deployment scaled materially — reduced resolution times by over 30% [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
