# Long COVID — Four Buckets of Fatigue Framework

## Summary

Dr. Carla Kuon's organizing framework for her UCSF Long COVID Group Medical Visit series, which addresses post-viral fatigue across four causal "buckets"; Part 1 of the series focuses entirely on bucket #1 (inflammation) and positions adoption of an anti-inflammatory diet as the first step in resolving Long COVID symptoms [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].

## Key claims

- The 3-part series is explicitly framed as addressing "the 4 buckets of fatigue," with inflammation introduced as bucket #1 in Session 1 [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Identifying and decreasing all pathways of inflammation in the body is described as key to COVID recovery [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- COVID can kick off inflammatory pathways in the body to such high levels that the body is unable to resolve them without added help [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Adoption of an anti-inflammatory diet is the first step in resolving Long COVID symptoms [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Sessions 2 and 3 extend beyond inflammation into botanical therapies / nutrient repletion and into stress reduction, vagal toning, and pacing in graduated exercise — implicitly mapping to the remaining buckets [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
