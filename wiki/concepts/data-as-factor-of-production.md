# Data as a factor of production (China policy designation)

## Summary

A formal Chinese state designation that classifies data as a factor of production (alongside land, labor, capital) and permits enterprises to carry data assets on their balance sheets — institutional infrastructure that, per the March 2026 USCC "Two Loops" paper, is built to exploit the deployment-driven data accumulation generated by China's open AI ecosystem [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].

## Key claims

- Beijing has designated data as a formal factor of production and has permitted enterprises to carry data assets on their balance sheets — a policy move that institutionalizes data accumulation as an economic input rather than treating it as an incidental byproduct [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- This designation is presented as institutional infrastructure built to exploit the advantage created by open-model proliferation across China's manufacturing and robotics base, where specialized real-world data may compound into advantages that proprietary U.S. models cannot easily replicate [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- The framing supports the paper's "physical loop" thesis: deployment-driven data creation, formally treated as a factor of production, becomes increasingly independent of access to cutting-edge training hardware [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
