# Engineering Liquidity

## Summary

The smart-money behaviour of producing the consolidation, lack of follow-through, and manipulation needed to build resting orders into one side of a range before a directional release; AMtrades treats engineering-liquidity (low-probability for the retail trader) as the mirror image of engaging-liquidity (high-probability), with the daily consolidation profile and the economic calendar as timing inputs [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].

## Key claims

- Defined operationally as a consolidation profile characterised by lack of follow-through, with three observable phases on the daily/hourly: consolidation, release, and manipulation [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Frames internal-range moves as the engineering phase and external-range moves as the engaging phase: "engineering liquidity = low-probability, engaging liquidity = high-probability" [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Operationally instructs the trader to wait until external liquidity is engaged before participating, rather than trading the internal moves that build it [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- The weekly profile and the economic calendar are used together to time the breakout from consolidation after manipulation, treating high-impact prints as the catalyst that resolves the engineered range [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
