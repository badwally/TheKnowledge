# Pinning at strike on expiration

## Summary

An alternative end-of-day outcome to a gamma squeeze, in which large expiration hedging flows hold the underlying near a key option strike rather than push it through — leaving price magnetically attached to the strike into settlement [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].

## Key claims

- If large expiration hedging flows remain in place at the close, pinning at the call strike may occur instead of a breakout [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].
- Pinning is presented as the alternative outcome to the dealer-hedging-driven gamma squeeze toward the upper strike — the same dealer flows that can fuel a rally can also "pin" the underlying at the strike when hedging needs balance [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].
- Pinning is one of the candidate outcomes alongside the rapid upward move and the blow-off top in the source's end-of-day scenario summary [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].
