# Full Pass-Through Expense Model

## Summary

A hedge-fund fee arrangement under which fund expenses are passed through to investors rather than absorbed within a fixed management fee; per a July 2022 Barclays survey of 290 hedge funds cited by ClearAlpha, multi-manager funds using this model outperformed traditional 2/20 hedge fund structures on net returns, alpha, and net Sharpe Ratio despite their higher expenses [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].

## Key claims

- Multi-manager funds with full-pass-through expense models delivered 11.8% net returns versus 6.3% for traditional 2/20 hedge funds — nearly double the net returns despite higher fund expenses (Barclays, July 2022) [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- Same survey: full-pass-through multi-manager funds delivered 10.8% alpha versus 4.1% for 2/20 — more than twice the alpha [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- Same survey: full-pass-through multi-manager funds delivered a net Sharpe Ratio of 2.1 versus 0.6 for 2/20 — over three times the net Sharpe Ratio [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- Higher fund expenses under the model are economically justified by the structure's ability to pay for portfolio managers, afford data, and afford best-in-class risk management — keeping multi-manager funds competitive for talent and technology [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
