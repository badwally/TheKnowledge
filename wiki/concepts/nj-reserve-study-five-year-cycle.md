# NJ Reserve Study 5-Year Update Cycle

## Summary

New Jersey requires associations of planned real estate developments to update reserve studies on a five-year cycle, conducted by a licensed architect, engineer, or credentialed reserve specialist. S3992 (August 2025) amended the prior S2760 language by changing the responsible party from "covered building owners" to "an association of a planned real estate development," with the stated intent of giving associations accurate and updated projections of financial status [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]]. The 5-year cycle matches Ontario's RFS cycle and the BC depreciation-report cycle, while the qualification standard (architect, engineer, or credentialed reserve specialist) is broader than Nova Scotia's P.Eng.-sealed requirement.

## Key claims

- Reserve studies must be obtained at a minimum of once every five years [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Studies must be conducted by a licensed architect, engineer, or credentialed reserve specialist [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- S3992 amended the prior S2760 language by changing the responsible party from "covered building owners" to "an association of a planned real estate development" [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The stated intent of the amendment is to provide associations with accurate and updated projections of their financial status [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The 5-year minimum cycle was carried forward from S2760 unchanged; only the responsible-party language was amended [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
