# True Day Daily Range

## Summary

In ICT (Inner Circle Trader) price-action analysis, the "True Day Daily Range" is the window that IPDA defines as the meaningful daily range — 12:00 am to 3:00 pm New York Time — and the period outside this window is dismissed as "dead time" [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].

## Key claims

- IPDA defines the daily range between 12:00 am New York Time and 3:00 pm New York Time [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
- The period outside this specific range is referred to as "dead time" [[sources/pdf-67b6fe78b28c]].
