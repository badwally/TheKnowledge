# California Annual Reserve Funding Disclosure

## Summary

The California annual reserve funding disclosure is the Davis-Stirling Act regime under which a common interest development association must deliver, every fiscal year, a standardized Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary alongside the budget report required by Civil Code §5300, summarizing assessments, reserve adequacy across a 30-year horizon, percent-funded status, and the long-term interest and inflation assumptions used to prepare the summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].

## Key claims

- Statutory anchor: Civil Code §5570(a) (Davis-Stirling Act) prescribes the form that summarizes the article's required disclosures [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Delivery channel: the form must accompany each annual budget report or summary delivered pursuant to Civil Code §5300 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Disclosure scope on the form spans current-year regular assessments per ownership interest, scheduled and approved special assessments, the 30-year reserve sufficiency yes/no, unapproved additional contributions that would close any 30-year gap, an all-major-components-included declaration, current-year required reserves and percent-funded, and a five-year forward projection [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Modification latitude: associations may supplement or modify the form to clarify the information delivered, so long as the minimum information set out in §5570(a) is provided [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Caveat regime: the financial representations on the form are explicitly based on the best estimates of the preparer at that time and are subject to change [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Recency anchor: when reporting the required reserve amount at the end of the current fiscal year, the form must identify the preparer of the most recent reserve study or update and the month and year of preparation [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Disclosure-not-funding distinction: §5570(b)(4) explicitly states the prescribed calculation "shall not be construed to require the board to fund reserves in accordance with this calculation" — disclosure of the computed amount is mandatory; funding to that amount is not [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
