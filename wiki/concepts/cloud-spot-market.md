# Cloud Spot Market

## Summary

Marketplace for compute resources that "deploys and liquidates (increasingly commoditized) data center compute in a secure, fast and transparently spot priced manner," as proposed by the 2018 Akash Network position paper — settled in the network-native AKASH token to remove the cross-border FX frictions that normally arise from fiat settlement of compute payments [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].

## Key claims

- The Akash position paper bills its marketplace as "the worlds first global spot market for cloud computing" [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Pricing is determined competitively — "Our matching engine competitively prices each container compute against a prevailing market amount of Akash tokens" [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Deployment orders specify the maximum amount the client is willing to pay for a fixed bundle of computing units (measured by memory, cpu, storage, and bandwidth) for a specific amount of time [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Deployment orders are open for a client-defined length of time, "as measured to the second" [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Cross-border payment frictions are mitigated by transacting in AKASH tokens, which take "the place of fiat for these transactions" and remove the foreign-exchange risk that usually results from cross-border payments [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- The total addressable market the position paper points at is the cloud-infrastructure market that Gartner (2017) sized at $737 billion with well over 21% annual growth [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
