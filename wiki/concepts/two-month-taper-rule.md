# 2-month taper rule (Kuon Long COVID)

## Summary

Clinical heuristic in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Osher Long COVID protocol: stay on the full supplement stack until pre-COVID baseline returns, continue the full stack for two more months, then halve doses every two months as long as no flare occurs; flares mean revert to the prior dose and retry after another two months [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- Step 1: stay on the protocol until health returns to baseline pre-COVID state, then continue the full protocol for 2 months more [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Step 2: if Long COVID has lasted over a year, the recovery on protocol could take 6 months because the immune system has long memory [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Step 3: once ready, cut all doses by ½ — e.g., 500 mg vitamin C instead of 1000 mg — and proceed only if Long COVID does not return [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Tapering rhythm: try halving doses every 2 months [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Flare-up handling: if symptoms recur after a dose cut, revert to the previous dose, stay on it for another 2 months, then retry the taper [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
