# GSE Limited Review Retirement

## Summary

GSE Limited Review Retirement is the policy change under Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 and Freddie Mac Bulletin 2026-C (issued March 18, 2026) that fully eliminates the limited review process for condominium loan project review, applying to loans with application dates on or after August 3, 2026. Limited review historically represented roughly 40% of all project reviews; its retirement shifts a significant portion of condominium transactions toward the more rigorous full review process, with materially higher documentation and lender-questionnaire burden on associations, managers, and volunteer leaders [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].

## Key claims

- The retirement of the limited review process is one of the most notable changes in the March 18, 2026 GSE update [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Limited review will be fully eliminated for loans with application dates on or after August 3, 2026 [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Limited review historically represented roughly 40% of all project reviews [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The retirement shifts a significant portion of transactions toward a more rigorous full review process [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The transition requires substantially more documentation and formal lender questionnaires for nearly all condominium sales [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The transition increases administrative burden on community associations, managers, and volunteer leaders [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Without thoughtful implementation and support, CAI warns the change has potential to slow transactions and place additional strain on already-constrained communities [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- After retirement, lenders rely on either full review or waiver of project review (with waiver eligibility expanded to projects with up to 10 units) [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- The change shifts greater responsibility to lenders and associations to demonstrate that condominium projects are financially sound based on Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
