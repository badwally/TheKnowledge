# International Roughness Index (IRI)

## Summary

The International Roughness Index (IRI) is a quantitative measure of pavement roughness used as the operational deterioration metric in pavement-condition databases and pavement-performance research, including studies of flood-driven pavement deterioration.

## Key claims

- IRI is used to measure pavement roughness in pavement condition assessment and pavement deterioration research [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- IRI values can be compared before and after flooding events to quantify the effect of flood exposure on pavement condition and to calculate deterioration rates [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- Flood-affected pavements exhibit a more rapid increase in IRI (i.e., faster roughness growth) than non-flooded sections, according to a 20-year TxDOT PMIS analysis [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
