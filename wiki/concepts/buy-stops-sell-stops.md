# Buy Stops and Sell Stops

## Summary

Buy stops and sell stops are the two atomic forms of resting liquidity that ICT-derived frameworks use as targets: buy stops sit above old highs, sell stops sit below old lows, and price is theorized to gravitate toward these pools so smart money can pair offsetting orders against them [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].

## Key claims

- Liquidity "refers to a market or asset to a degree where it can be quickly bought or sold without affecting the asset's price," and the relevant reference points are areas where a high percentage of orders is resting in the form of buy and sell stops [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Buy stops sit above old highs; sell stops sit below old lows [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Smart money sells the buy stops above old highs (offloading sells to willing sellers in a discount) and buys the sell stops below old lows [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- The trader's job is not to read patterns but to identify "areas where orders may reside to target them" — making buy/sell stop pools the targeting layer rather than the price action that produces them [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
