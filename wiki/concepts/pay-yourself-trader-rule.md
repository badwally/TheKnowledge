# Pay-Yourself Rule for Traders

## Summary

Personal-finance heuristic articulated by TraderEdge's Alex: at the end of each month, a trader should pay themselves 50% of that month's profits — treating the trading account as a business that distributes a fixed share of earnings rather than letting all profit compound back into trading capital [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key claims

- "Pay Yourself Every Month: For example, at the end of the month, whatever your profits were, pay yourself 50% of them" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The rule is presented alongside an extended emergency fund (6–12 months of expenses) and the 80/20 expense rule as the three pillars of a "strong fundamental mindset for personal finance" for a profitable trader [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The pay-yourself rule sits on top of Morgan Housel's "The Psychology of Money" as a layer of practice on top of underlying mindset reading TraderEdge recommends [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
