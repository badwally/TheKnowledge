# Narrative-based asset allocation

## Summary

Narrative-based asset allocation is Bhargava, Lou, Ozik, Sadka, and Whitmore's (2022) dynamic-allocation strategy that uses media-derived narrative indicators — particularly the Market Crash narrative — to time exposure between equities and bonds, and which they report significantly outperforms equity-only, bond-only, and 50/50 balanced benchmarks [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key claims

- Inspired by the finding that media coverage of the Market Crash narrative has a negative and statistically significant predictive coefficient for future SPY returns (controlling for past returns and VIX), the authors apply narrative-indicator insights to asset allocation [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- The narrative-based dynamic asset-allocation strategy is reported to significantly outperform the equity-only, the bond-only, and the 50/50 equity/bond balance strategy [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- The strategy is one of three practical investment applications the paper develops, alongside narrative-driver identification and narrative-beta portfolio construction [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
