# Fleet-scale reliability assessment

## Summary

Fleet-scale reliability assessment estimates failure-time behaviour across a large, heterogeneous population of assets (such as wind turbine gearboxes) rather than for a single unit, providing the inferential basis for maintenance planning and long-term asset management. Hierarchical Bayesian models are positioned in the literature as a rigorous approach because they pool information across groups while preserving group-specific baselines.

## Key claims

- A Bayesian hierarchical Accelerated Failure Time framework provides a rigorous basis for fleet-scale reliability assessment, maintenance planning, and long-term asset management in wind energy systems [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- Fleet-scale datasets aggregate units exposed to heterogeneous environmental conditions, creating confounding between intrinsic design variability and environment-driven effects that any fleet-scale reliability assessment must address [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- Jacquet, Haus, Hermoso, and Pulikollu demonstrate the approach on a fleet of over 8,000 wind turbine gearboxes spanning 64,000 cumulative operating years [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
