# Markov Chain Monte Carlo

## Summary

A family of sampling algorithms for approximating posterior distributions, used in the rail surface defect HBM application of Khalaj et al. (2026) to estimate the posteriors of Homogeneous Poisson Process arrival-rate parameters across a network.

## Key claims

- In the rail surface defect HBM application of Khalaj et al., the posteriors of the model parameters are estimated using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) techniques [[sources/web-2026-03-04-157]].
- The MCMC posteriors are summarised through metrics developed to compare the posteriors from different areas of the rail network [[sources/web-2026-03-04-157]].
