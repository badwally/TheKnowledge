# Ontario Three-Tier Reserve Study Classification (O. Reg. 48/01)

## Summary

Ontario Regulation 48/01 classifies reserve fund studies into three tiers — comprehensive study, updated study based on a site inspection, and updated study not based on a site inspection — each with distinct currency and evidentiary requirements. The three-tier classification is the operative regulatory framework defining what counts as a reserve fund study under the Condominium Act, 1998, and the distinguishing variable between the two "updated" tiers is whether the revision is grounded in a site inspection of the property [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key claims

- A "comprehensive study" is a comprehensive reserve fund study that meets the requirements of O. Reg. 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- An "updated study based on a site inspection" is a comprehensive study that has been revised so that it is current as of the date of the revision, where the revision is based on a site inspection of the property and has been conducted in accordance with the requirements of the regulation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- An "updated study not based on a site inspection" is a comprehensive study that has been revised so that it is current as of the date of the revision, where the revision is not based on a site inspection and has been conducted in accordance with the requirements of the regulation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Both updated-study tiers are formally defined as revisions of a pre-existing comprehensive study; an updated study cannot exist absent the underlying comprehensive study [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The site-inspection criterion is the load-bearing variable distinguishing the two updated tiers — both must be "current as of the date of the revision" and both must comply with the regulation's procedural requirements; only the inspection grounding differs [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- All three tiers must conduct the (initial or revised) study in accordance with the requirements of O. Reg. 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
