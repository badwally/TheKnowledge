# Censoring (survival analysis)

## Summary

Censoring in survival analysis refers to incomplete observation of failure times. Different types of censoring can mask the presence of distinct subpopulations and cause severe problems for statistical estimators and the interpretation of results.

## Key claims

- Different types of censoring can mask the presence of different populations within a single survival dataset, which can cause severe problems for statistical estimators and the interpretations of results [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- In the prognostic modeling of mobile work equipment, the authors find the presence of different types of censoring masking different populations of machine components [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
