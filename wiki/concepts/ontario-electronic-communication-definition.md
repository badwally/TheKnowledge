# Ontario Electronic Communication Statutory Definition (O. Reg. 48/01)

## Summary

O. Reg. 48/01 defines "electronic communication" and "electronic mail" for purposes of the Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 by imposing three conjunctive functional requirements: transmission in digital or intangible form, recipient ability to view/store/retrieve/print content (including linked documents), and clear identification of each included document and separate electronic file. The definition is the load-bearing reference point for what counts as legally-sufficient electronic notice or service under the Act, and constrains how condo-software platforms must structure owner-facing digital communications [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key claims

- "Electronic communication" and "electronic mail" mean a communication that satisfies three conjunctive requirements per the regulation's subsection 1(2) [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Requirement (a): transmitted in digital form or in another intangible form by electronic, magnetic, or optical means or by any other means that has capabilities for transmission similar to those means [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Requirement (b): enables the recipient to view, store, retrieve, and print the contents of the communication, the contents of any documents included in the communication, and the contents of any links in the communication, including any links to external documents [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Requirement (c): clearly identifies each document mentioned in clause (b) and each separate electronic file, if any, of which the document consists [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The definition was introduced into the regulation by O. Reg. 180/17 and subsequently amended by O. Reg. 379/17 and O. Reg. 191/23 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The "view, store, retrieve, and print" requirement is conjunctive — a communication channel that allows viewing but not durable storage or printing does not satisfy the definition [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The linked-document requirement extends compliance to external resources referenced via hyperlink, not only to inline attachments [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
