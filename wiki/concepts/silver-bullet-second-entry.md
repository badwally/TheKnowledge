# Silver Bullet (2nd Entry)

## Summary

In the canonical MMXM framework, the "silver bullet" is the second of two named entries inside an MMXM expansion — paired with the Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) as the first entry — taken on a retracement into a re-accumulation FVG/OB (in an MMBM) or a re-distribution FVG/OB (in an MMSM) after the model's market structure shift has fired [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].

## Key claims

- The MMXM PDF instructs the trader, after spotting an MMSM or MMBM template, to ask: "Do you spot the Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)?" and then "Do you spot the silver bullet (the 2nd entry)?" — explicitly naming the silver bullet as the 2nd entry of the model [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, in an MMSM "retracements into FVG's are where shorts will re-distribute" — the silver bullet is taken on one of these retracements after the OTE has already fired [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, in an MMBM "pullbacks into FVG's are where longs will re-accumulate" — the silver bullet sits in this re-accumulation leg as the second-chance entry [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per the MMXM PDF, both entries fire within the model's range of opportunity, defined as "from the point of start of the model to the end at the target liquidity" — i.e., the leg between the MSS and the higher-timeframe draw on liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF's worked MMSM and MMBM templates each annotate two entry points — OTE and silver bullet — establishing them as a paired entry doctrine inside the model rather than two independent setups [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
