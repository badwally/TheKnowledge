# Stacked Bilinear Rotational Spring Model

## Summary

A stacked bilinear rotational spring model is a discretized structural representation in which a tall member such as a bridge pier is modelled as a vertical stack of bilinear (elastic-plastic) rotational springs, allowing nonlinear hysteretic behaviour and damage to be localized along the member's height.

## Key claims

- Yamaguchi (2025) adopts a stacked bilinear rotational spring model to represent a reinforced concrete bridge pier for the purpose of damage identification from seismic response data [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
- The stacked formulation enables parameters such as elastic stiffness and ductility factor to be estimated as spatial distributions along the height of the pier, supporting damage localization rather than only global damage indices [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
