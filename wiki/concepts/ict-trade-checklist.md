# ICT trade checklist

## Summary

The ICT trade checklist, as expanded in the Lesson 11 Divergence PDF, is a five-step sequence for vetting a trade idea: higher-timeframe point of interest (HTF PoI), liquidity, divergence, displacement, and structure — with divergence treated as the newly-added step that links the prior macro context to the entry-confirmation steps that follow [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].

## Key claims

- The checklist as expanded by the Divergence lesson reads: HTF PoI → Liquidity → DIVERGENCE* → Displacement → Structure [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- Divergence is marked with an asterisk in the lesson, indicating it is the new step the lesson adds to a prior version of the checklist [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- The lesson motivates the checklist by arguing that divergence helps the trader identify reversals, decide when to add to or sell into a Market Maker Buy/Sell Model (MMXM), play key levels with confidence, and identify strong structural components of the trend [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- After divergence is observed, displacement guides whether a specific MMXM is underway, and confirmation always comes from displacement and market structure rather than from the divergence alone [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- A divergence being present does not mean a good trade is present; the trader is instructed to always wait for displacement and a market structure shift to guide ideas [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
