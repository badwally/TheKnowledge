# System-level prognostics

## Summary

System-level prognostics treats the prediction of remaining useful life and degradation as a property of an entire engineering system (rather than a single component), using performance-level signals and ground-truth system performance metrics from high-fidelity simulation environments to validate predictive accuracy.

## Key claims

- Diaz-Gonzalez et al. (2025) frame their work as system-level prognostics, predicting the Remaining Useful Life (RUL) of industrial systems as a whole using performance metrics that govern a stochastic degradation process [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- The authors evaluate their proposed method using an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) simulation dataset that was developed specifically for system-level prognostics research [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- The UAV dataset is described as providing high-fidelity degradation signals together with ground-truth system performance metrics that are used to validate predictive accuracy [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- "System-level prognostics" is one of the listed keywords of the Diaz-Gonzalez et al. (2025) paper, alongside remaining useful life, particle filter methods, data-driven methods, and performance metrics [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
