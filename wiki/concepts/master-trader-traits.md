# Master Trader Traits

## Summary

The shared characteristics of "super-traders" as identified by Steve Cohen in his foreword to Ari Kiev's "Trading to Win" (1998) — discipline and total commitment to a chosen style, complete conviction in execution, willingness to take large financial risks, ability to admit mistakes and cut losses early, and intellectual independence — contrasted with the indecisiveness, lack of conviction, and unawareness of personality flaws that Cohen says characterize most traders [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].

## Key claims

- Per Cohen, super-traders share certain common traits no matter what discipline they might pursue [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- They are totally committed to their own particular style and demonstrate complete conviction when trading [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Their conviction enables them to take large financial risks, with the confidence and belief that probability is on their side [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- They have the ability to admit their mistakes and minimize their consequences — that is, losses — when they are wrong [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- They are independent and think for themselves, and are not easily influenced by others [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per Cohen, most traders, surprisingly, are not these things: they are indecisive, lack conviction, are afraid of taking risks and making mistakes, and are unaware of the personality flaws holding them back [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per Kiev, master traders trust their information, mastering the inevitable feeling of vulnerability and the desire to vacate the field; this requires an enormous ability to abandon pride and to maintain equanimity in the face of loss or excessive profit [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- For master traders, the monetary result is secondary to the gratification that comes from being able to make the right market call — they get their primary satisfaction from having an idea about a stock and implementing this idea in a profitable trade [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
