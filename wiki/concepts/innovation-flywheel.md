# Innovation flywheel (interlocking innovation flywheels)

## Summary

A frame for industrial competitiveness in which adjacent sectors (manufacturing, robotics, AI deployment, data production) generate self-reinforcing feedback loops; documented by the USCC's 2025 Annual Report for China's industrial base and extended to AI in Luong's March 2026 "Two Loops" paper [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].

## Key claims

- The USCC's 2025 Annual Report documented that China's industrial base generates "interlocking innovation flywheels" across adjacent sectors [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Open AI models accelerate this dynamic by enabling low-cost AI deployment across factories, logistics networks, and robotics, which in turn generates real-world data that feeds back into model improvement [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- A specific AI flywheel within China's open-model ecosystem: permissive licensing and aggressive pricing → widespread global adoption (e.g., Qwen's 100,000+ derivatives on Hugging Face by March 2026) → faster iteration → further adoption [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
