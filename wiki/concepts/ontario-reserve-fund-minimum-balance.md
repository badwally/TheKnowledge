# Ontario Reserve Fund Minimum Balance

## Summary

The minimum balance of an Ontario condominium reserve fund is the floor amount required at any point in the corporation's operating life, determined as part of the RFS's financial analysis under Section 29(3) of Ontario Regulation 48/01. The minimum balance is a buffer against emergency premature-failure events and is recommended by the RFS provider as a function of corporation size and other factors [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].

## Key claims

- The minimum balance is the minimum amount required for the reserve fund at any point, determined as part of the reserve fund study's financial analysis under Section 29(3) of Ontario Regulation 48/01 [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The minimum balance exists because, although the RFS prepares for expected major repairs and replacements, there is always a chance that the corporation may have an emergency — such as premature failure of a major piece of equipment — that requires going below the planned reserve trajectory [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The minimum balance amount is recommended by the RFS provider and depends on the size of the condominium corporation and other factors [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- Larger condominium corporations may need a larger minimum balance; smaller condominium corporations will typically not require as much [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- A large absolute reserve fund balance does not by itself indicate that the condominium corporation is in good financial standing; the amount in the reserve fund will fluctuate depending on the major repairs and replacements being conducted [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The health of a condominium corporation's reserve fund should not be judged from one point in time, but rather in its consistent ability to fund both expected and unexpected major repairs and replacements over the projection horizon [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The recommended funding plan in the RFS must show the minimum balance during the 30-year projection period as part of the financial analysis content required by O. Reg. 48/01 §29(3) [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
