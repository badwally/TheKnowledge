# Killzones (ICT Timing Windows)

## Summary

ICT's term for intraday windows when manipulation and distribution moves are most likely to occur, mapping to the early-morning and pre-NY-open periods that bracket the overnight accumulation range used in Power of 3 setups [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]]; Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023) publishes specific killzone times for FX (Asia, London, New York, London Close) and for Futures/Crypto (AM Session, PM Session), all in NY EST [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- Periods of accumulation should be looked for between 6:00 PM EST and 5:00 AM EST [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- Manipulation and distribution periods occur between 2:00 AM EST and 10:30 AM EST [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- Per the Power of 3 lesson, traders familiar with killzones already know when to look for these manipulations [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- The Power of 3 lesson explicitly positions its time windows as a beginner-simplified statement of the killzone concept [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- Per Ali Khan, time is the first component IPDA will refer to — once the time window is in operation, IPDA will then look to seek key levels (PD Arrays) in price [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, the manipulation of price will occur in these windows of time, which ICT refers to as killzones [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, FX killzones (all NY EST): Asia 8pm–12am, London 2am–5am, New York 7am–10am, London Close 10am–12pm [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, Futures/Crypto killzones (all NY EST): AM Session 8:30am–12pm, PM Session 1:30pm–4pm [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, news drivers within killzones are often the catalyst for IPDA to reprice to key levels by initiating buy/sell programs [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, during high-impact news events such as CPI, FOMC, or NFP, manual intervention can take place where IPDA's normal pricing parameters can be manually overridden, usually at the central bank level [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
