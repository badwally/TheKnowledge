# Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs)

## Summary

Privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs) are the technical mechanisms — including federated learning, differential privacy, synthetic data, and regulated sandboxes — that allow data-driven analytics and ML training while reducing the risk of identifying individuals in source datasets; Sen (2026) positions PETs as the technical layer of his proposed Canadian data-supply-chain framework [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].

## Key claims

- PETs identified in the source include differential privacy (DP), federated learning, synthetic data, and regulated sandboxes (secure digital environments) [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Federated learning stores data locally on a device and sends the ML model to that device to be trained locally, rather than uploading data to a less private and less secure cloud — described via Nair and Inverarity (2022) as cited by Sen (2026) [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Differential privacy adds statistical "noise" to individual data points to prevent inference of confidential individual-level data; one application is to release data at certain aggregation levels rather than at the individual level [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Synthetic data are artificially generated datasets that reflect the statistical properties of real data while reducing privacy risks; Sen recommends the federal government prioritize accelerated national production of synthetic datasets through appropriate regulations [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Recent studies cited by Sen (2026) show that individuals can be re-identified in supposedly de-identified datasets using relatively straightforward statistical and ML methods, raising the stakes for PET adoption [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Sen (2026) argues regulated sandboxes — modeled on the UK approach — should be established by sector and region and should encourage university–firm research collaboration [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Sen (2026) recommends that any private-sector access to confidential Statistics Canada data be governed by PETs and restricted to firms subject to enforceable Canadian privacy safeguards [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
