# Blow-off top into close

## Summary

A late-session exhaustion pattern that can follow a gamma-squeeze rally — once dealers finish covering their hedges, the buying pressure that drove the move evaporates, letting momentum chasers overshoot before price fades into settlement [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].

## Key claims

- If dealers finish covering, buying pressure may evaporate in the final minutes of the session [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].
- If momentum traders chase the move, the underlying may overshoot before fading into settlement [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].
- If price overshoots and stalls near the upper strike, traders should watch for signs of exhaustion or reversal in the final 1–2 minutes [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].
- The pattern is named alongside pinning as one of two candidate outcomes that can follow the rapid upward move of a gamma squeeze into the close [[sources/pdf-ea646c451aae]].
