# Ontario Periodic Information Certificate (PIC)

## Summary

The Periodic Information Certificate (PIC) is a defined form under O Reg 48/01 that an Ontario condominium corporation must send to owners under §26.3 of the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]]. The PIC, together with the Information Certificate Update (ICU) and the New Owner Information Certificate (NOIC), constitutes the Act's tiered owner-disclosure regime; all three are O Reg 48/01-defined certificate types that condo-software must template and timestamp [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]]. PICs sent in the rolling 12-month window before a records request must be produced as core records under paragraph 8 of the core-record definition [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key claims

- "Periodic information certificate" means the certificate described in §26.3(a) or (b) of the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- "Information certificate update" means the certificate described in §11.2(2) of O Reg 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- "New owner information certificate" means the certificate described in §11.3(3) of O Reg 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- All periodic information certificates that the corporation, within the 12-month period before receiving a request for records or a requester's response, sent to the owners under §26.3 of the Act or was required to send under that section, qualify as core records under paragraph 8 of the core-record definition [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The 12-month rolling window for PIC production is pegged to the date the corporation receives the request for records or the requester's response, requiring the record-management system to compute the look-back window at the point of disclosure [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
