# High-Impact AI Systems (AIDA)

## Summary

"High-impact AI systems" was the regulatory category at the centre of Canada's proposed Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA); developers, providers, and operators of such systems would have faced statutory requirements for risk assessment and mitigation, recordkeeping, and disclosure of key system information, backed by monetary penalties and criminal offences [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key claims

- AIDA's compliance obligations applied to developers, providers, and operators of "high-impact" AI systems in Canada [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- The obligations covered risk assessment and mitigation, recordkeeping, and disclosure of key system information [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Non-compliance was to be enforced through monetary penalties and criminal offences [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Critics deemed the statutory text describing these requirements vague and insufficient for protecting Canada against harmful AI impacts; the November 2023 amendments added specificity but did not resolve the underlying flaws [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
