# Data DAO (Decentralised Autonomous Organisation for Data Governance)

## Summary

A data DAO is the application of the decentralised autonomous organisation form — a blockchain-coordinated, smart-contract-governed collective — to the pooling, governance, and commercialisation of personal data, positioned as one of four commonly proposed collective data intermediaries [[sources/web-2023-09-05-c31]].

## Key claims

- Decentralised autonomous organisations are one of four collective data intermediaries explored in the source, alongside data trusts, data cooperatives, and data unions [[sources/web-2023-09-05-c31]].
- Like the other intermediary forms studied, DAO-based mechanisms build on individualist data rights by embracing data as a form of collective value and redistributing benefits to members [[sources/web-2023-09-05-c31]].
- Some DAO proposals act as brokers contractually shaping how members' data may be used; others imagine building (or even seizing) the infrastructures of data collection and storage to assert collective control over data production [[sources/web-2023-09-05-c31]].
- Demand for DAO-style collective data governance reflects part of a broader cross-ideological consensus on the need for collective interventions in datafication [[sources/web-2023-09-05-c31]].
