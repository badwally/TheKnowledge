# Non-stationary resistance degradation

## Summary

Non-stationary resistance degradation refers to deterioration processes whose statistical characteristics — rate, variance, or distributional shape — change over time, in contrast to stationary degradation models. It is a key complication in reliability analysis of long-lived infrastructure such as bridges.

## Key claims

- Bridge resistance deteriorates over time due to non-stationary degradation as well as increasing load frequency and intensity [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- A Gamma process-based approach is proposed to evaluate the time-dependent reliability of reinforced concrete structures specifically considering non-stationary resistance degradation [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
