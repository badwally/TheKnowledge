# Percent Funded

## Summary

Percent funded is described by CommunityPay's product page as "the industry-standard measure of reserve health" — the percentage of fully-funded reserve obligations actually held in the reserve fund at a point in time [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]]. CommunityPay's platform calculates percent funded in real time as fund balances and component data change, contrasting that posture against the traditional consultant cadence of recomputing once every three years [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]]. Within the platform's reserve-studies module, percent funded is one of five analysis outputs (alongside fully funded balance target, annual contribution requirements, special-assessment scenarios, and funding-gap analysis) and is also one of five elements projected over the 30-year horizon (alongside annual expenditures, reserve contributions, ending fund balance, and deficit years) [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].

## Key claims

- Percent funded is positioned as the industry-standard measure of reserve health [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- The metric represents the relationship between the current reserve fund balance and the fully funded balance target [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- The CommunityPay platform recalculates percent funded in real time as fund balances and component data change, not on a periodic schedule [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Percent funded appears both as a current-state analysis output and as a 30-year trend projection — the trend element is explicitly framed as showing "how funding level changes over the 30-year horizon" [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
- Percent funded is one of the values automatically recalculated on the platform's defined trigger events (reserve fund balance changes, component replacements, registry additions, contribution-rate changes, useful-life adjustments) [[sources/web-2024-11-01-fc2]].
