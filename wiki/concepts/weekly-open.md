# Weekly open

## Summary

The weekly open is the reference level used in the Power of 3 framework to determine weekly bias: in a bullish week the trader expects price to seek a discount under the weekly open before expanding up; in a bearish week, a premium over the open before expanding down [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].

## Key claims

- For weekly candles, the same PO3 logic as monthly candles applies, but the weekly opening price is the reference for determining bias [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- In a bullish week, price should seek a discount under the weekly open before moving up; in a bearish week, price should seek a premium over the weekly open before moving down [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- A strong bullish weekly candle barely drops below the weekly open before expanding up aggressively to target buyside liquidity (premium) and the HTF target — the small dip below the open is sometimes all that is needed before the expansion [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- A bullish consolidation/pullback week still uses the OLHC format but with a limited upside range; price drops into a discount of the previous week's range where bulls re-accumulate (visible via FVG or OB inside the discount), then closes near the high above the week's open [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- With a bullish monthly candle, each week provides an opportunity to capture some of the expansion up; weekly targets can be anchored to the monthly target, but price will not hit the HTF target in just one week — each week carves its own path [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- Weeks 1–4 of January 2023 are presented as bullish OLHC weekly examples: a hammer week, a strong expansion week, a consolidation/pullback week, and a second expansion week [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
