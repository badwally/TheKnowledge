# Long COVID

## Summary

Post-viral recovery syndrome following SARS-CoV-2 infection that Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Osher Center protocol treats as a chronic immune dysregulation requiring a phased anti-inflammatory, nutrient-repletion, and mast-cell-stabilization regimen sustained well past return to pre-COVID baseline because "your immune system has long memory" [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].

## Key claims

- The clinical workup uses ICD-10 code U09.9 to clear patients for UCSF Long COVID group medical visits [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Recovery is treated as a process, not an event: patients stay on the full protocol until they return to a pre-COVID baseline and then continue the full protocol for two months more [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- For patients who have had Long COVID for over a year, full recovery on the protocol can take 6 months because the immune system has long memory [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- Low vitamin D levels have been associated with higher mortality from COVID and a higher degree of Long COVID; the protocol targets vitamin D >40, ideally 50–60, especially in autoimmune illness [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
- The framework treats Long COVID as a multi-driver problem with three named buckets — inflammation, toxins, and low nutrients — addressed in separate sessions of the GMV series [[sources/pdf-50a1f07b475f]].
