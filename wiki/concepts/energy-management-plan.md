# Energy Management Plan (BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E2.0)

## Summary

An Energy Management Plan, as defined by BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB question E2.0, is a mandatory baseline practice that establishes a building's vision, targets, metrics, approaches, and milestones for energy and carbon reduction [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]]. It is the framework layer that turns the Energy and Carbon Assessment (E1.0b) into a multi-year, budgeted, accountable program [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key claims

- E2.0 is a baseline practice and minimum requirement for BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB certification, applicable to Office, Healthcare, Enclosed Shopping Centre, Universal, and Multi-Unit Residential buildings [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan should follow ISO 50001 guidance, or equivalent [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan must outline the vision for energy management going forward, including goals or targets in relation to the baseline or for managing carbon emissions [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Where portfolio-wide Energy Management Plans are referenced, the plan must include a narrative describing how the portfolio plan is applied at the building level [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- For each Energy Conservation Measure (ECM) listed in the assessment, the plan must determine: whether it will be implemented (and if not, why); the associated budget (implementation cost, savings, incentive); an overview of metrics to be used to measure progress; a timeline for completion (one year, five years, and 10 years); and the person responsible for implementation [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The required documentation is a building-specific Energy Management Goal and Plan [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The plan may include carbon emissions from on-site combustion (such as gas-fired boilers), refrigerant leaks (fugitive emissions), and purchased energy (such as electricity or steam) — typically referred to as Scope 1 and 2 emissions — and should also work toward developing an approach for assessing Scope 3 emissions from fleet vehicles and service providers (e.g., snow removal) [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The suggested lead is in-house [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The E2.0 question is adapted from BOMA BEST 3.0 Best Practice 4 ("Is an Energy Management Plan in place at the building?") and Best Practice 5 ("Is an energy reduction target in place at the building?") [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The baseline practice is waivable as "Not applicable" only when a Net Zero Transition Plan is available under question E2.1 [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
