# Vagus Nerve (CN-X)

## Summary

The longest cranial nerve in the human body — also called "The Wanderer" — that regulates all organs as a pacemaker, has anti-inflammatory effects across the body, and is exquisitely sensitive to high inflammation as caused by COVID, with documented evidence of vagal dysfunction after COVID infection [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key claims

- Longest cranial nerve in the body; regulates all organs and acts as a pacemaker for the organs [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Has stretch receptors in the ear, lungs, and diaphragm [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Has anti-inflammatory effects on all organs [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Is a regulator of fight/flight responses and decreases inflammation in organs and the body [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Is exquisitely sensitive to high inflammation as caused by COVID, and there is evidence of Vagus dysfunction after COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- When the Vagus is impacted, the body shows high stress responses and perpetual inflammation [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Many Long COVID symptoms — including tachycardia and lung, gastro, and adrenal symptoms — are mediated by CN-X [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Has outflow branches in the inner ear, voice box, lungs, diaphragm, and many organs; those outflow branches have stretch receptors that allow vagal-toning interventions to engage CN-X [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
