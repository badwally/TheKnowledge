# Goal-Directed AI

## Summary

The property of an AI system to set and pursue complex objectives — including toggling between multiple goals — even amid drastic environmental change, treated by the Acharya et al. survey as the central trait separating Agentic AI from both classical rule-based AI and pattern-generating AI [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].

## Key claims

- Agentic AIs are goal-directed even in situations involving drastic changes and multiple goals to toggle between [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Classical AI was never focused on broad capabilities like image recognition or language translation but instead aimed at very narrow goals [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Generative AI works in reverse-operations mode: it combines pieces of information learned through numbers and creates content such as words or images, but is not goal-pursuing [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Agentic AI surpasses both prior approaches by being simultaneously goal-oriented, input-formed, and adaptable — accomplishing intricate, multi-layered tasks without a new instruction set per task [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Goal design and convergence is identified as a primary engineering problem when constructing Agentic AI systems [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
- Goal-directed agents enable autonomous decision-making within defined boundaries, distinguishing them from purely reactive or narrowly rule-based AI technologies [[sources/pdf-83c3ccfff1f1]].
