# Institutional Sponsorship Criteria (ICT)

## Summary

The ICT framework's four-confluence checklist for identifying institutional sponsorship behind a setup: higher-time-frame price displacement, intermediate-term imbalance moves to discount or premium, liquidity above or below the market, and time of day measured by London or New York session highs and lows [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].

## Key claims

- Identifying institutional sponsorship for setups requires four confluences: (1) HTF price displacement — reversals, expansions, or return to fair value; (2) intermediate-term imbalance with moves to discount/premium; (3) liquidity above/below the market; and (4) time of day, specifically the London or New York Low/High of day [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].
- Price will seek to balance old imbalances and seek the largest levels of liquidity, and once it has absorbed a large area of liquidity it will seek the next area [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].
- When the daily chart shows price dropping below an old low — where sell stops are — an aggressive trader can at that time look for lower-timeframe charts to show similar price action of a low being violated, and a long can be taken [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].
- Once the analyst knows where price will go, these levels can be refined down to the lower time frames for trade setups [[sources/pdf-57b1d100694e]].
