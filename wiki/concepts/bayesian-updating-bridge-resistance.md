# Bayesian updating of bridge resistance with historical service information

## Summary

Bayesian updating of bridge resistance uses historical service-load information to refine the probabilistic estimate of an aging bridge's remaining resistance, reducing epistemic uncertainty and informing condition evaluation and maintenance decisions. The fidelity of the updated estimate depends sensitively on the assumed deterioration model.

## Key claims

- Making full use of historical service load information may improve the accuracy of bridge performance assessment with reduced epistemic uncertainty for existing aging bridges [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- Wang, Feng, Zhang, and Zou (2018) propose a novel method for updating the resistance of service-proven bridges with a realistic — specifically Gamma stochastic process — deterioration model, rather than deterministic or fully correlated deterioration assumptions [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- Parametric examples in the 2018 paper demonstrate that the choice of resistance deterioration model materially affects the updated estimate of bridge resistance derived from historical service information [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
