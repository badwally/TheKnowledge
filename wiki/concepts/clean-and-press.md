# Clean & Press

## Summary

The clean & press is a two-phase compound lift — clean the weight to the rack position, then press it overhead — and is the central pressing movement in [[entities/strongfirst|StrongFirst]]'s [[entities/dry-fighting-weight|Dry Fighting Weight]] program and its community remixes such as the [[entities/dfw-kettleballs-remix|DFW Kettleballs Remix]] [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key claims

- In the DFW Kettleballs Remix, the clean & press is performed with paired [[concepts/kettlebell|kettlebells]] at the lifter's 5RM press weight [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Each rep is cleaned individually rather than re-pressed from the rack — the Remix's notes specify "Do a set of clean & press, cleaning each rep" [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- The lift is alternated with the [[concepts/front-squat|front squat]] inside a 30-minute density block on the program's C&P/FS days [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Set structure follows a [[concepts/ladder-set-protocol|ladder]]: 1,2,3 reps in week 1, escalating to 1,2,3,4,(5) by week 4, before retesting against a [[concepts/rep-max|rep max]] in week 5 [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
