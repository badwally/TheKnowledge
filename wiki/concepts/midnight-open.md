# Midnight open

## Summary

The midnight open (EST) is the daily reference level used in the Power of 3 framework to determine the day's bias: if bullish, price should seek a discount under this open before moving up; if bearish, price should seek a premium over this open before moving down [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].

## Key claims

- The daily candle formation in Power of 3 uses the midnight open as the level at which the trader determines bias [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- If bullish, price should seek a discount under the midnight open before moving up; if bearish, price should seek a premium over the midnight open before moving down [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- Marking off the midnight open (EST) is the first step when projecting a daily PO3 setup on the hourly chart [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- A bullish OLHC daily example anchored to the midnight open begins with a drop below the open into a deep discount, an aggressive move up creating a displacement and market structure shift, a return to the order block, and another expansion up to the previous day's high target [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The time of week interacts with the midnight-open framing: bullish days typically print the low of week Monday–Tuesday and sometimes Wednesday, while bearish days typically print the high of week Monday–Tuesday and sometimes Wednesday [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
