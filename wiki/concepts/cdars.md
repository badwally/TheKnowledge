# CDARS (Certificate of Deposit Account Registry Service)

## Summary

CDARS — the Certificate of Deposit Account Registry Service — is an IntraFi-administered deposit-network service offered to HOA depositors as one of two named IntraFi deposit services in the Pacific Premier Bank (post-merger: Columbia Bank) HOA banking offering [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. The service is one of the named continuity commitments of the August 31, 2025 Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger, with the announcement confirming that IntraFi CDARS and ICS deposit services continue as usual through the transition [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key claims

- CDARS stands for Certificate of Deposit Account Registry Service [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- CDARS is administered by IntraFi as one of two named IntraFi deposit services (the other being ICS / Insured Cash Sweep) [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- CDARS is offered by Pacific Premier Bank to HOA customers and continues unchanged through the Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger and Columbia Bank rebrand [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
