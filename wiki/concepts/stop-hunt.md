# Stop Hunt

## Summary

A stop hunt, in daytradingrauf's DOL framework, is the price excursion beyond a swing high or swing low that runs through resting buy stops or sell stops, sweeping that external liquidity before price reverses to target internal-range liquidity; the stop hunt is the trigger event in the external-to-internal range routine [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].

## Key claims

- External liquidity is identified at swing highs and lows in price action, and the trader "wait[s] for a stop hunt into them" before targeting internal range liquidity — making the stop hunt the bridge between the two ranges [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- The stop-hunt mechanic is the operational expression of the underlying liquidity-pairing claim: smart money runs price through buy stops above old highs to pair sells with retail buy orders, and through sell stops below old lows to pair buys with retail sell orders [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- After the stop hunt occurs, the targets switch from external (swing highs/lows) to internal (fair value gaps, volume imbalances, gaps) [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
