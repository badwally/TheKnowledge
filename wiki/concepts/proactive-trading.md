# Proactive Trading

## Summary

The execution mode the Trading to Win framework asks of traders: deliberate modification of trading behavior and incorporation of successful changes into one's strategy, rather than passive reaction to market events; per Ari Kiev's 1998 framing, it is a set of philosophical and behavioral principles built around commitment, concentration, recovery, and preparation for the next day, designed to produce exponential rather than linear results [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].

## Key claims

- Per Kiev's "Trading to Win" (1998), proactive trading involves implementing a set of philosophical and behavioral principles, with proactive strategies that involve commitment, concentration, recovery, and preparation for the next day [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- It is described as enabling the trader to trust their true self [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- It is "not for the fainthearted" and is designed to produce exponential results [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- It encourages counterintuitive behaviors — admitting uncertainty, fear, and lack of knowledge; asking for help; sharing information; and facing vulnerability [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- All of this requires letting go of ego and arrogance, which blur the trader's focus on the marketplace [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- It compels the trader to learn to communicate directly and clearly with others, whether staff, associates, or floor brokers [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per Steve Cohen, SAC Capital Management implemented this proactively-modified-behavior model through weekly meetings with Kiev, in which traders learn to modify trading behavior and proactively incorporate successful changes into their strategies [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Cohen states the firm monitors trader performance closely and reports really impressive results, with some traders' profits up 100% since implementing the program [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
