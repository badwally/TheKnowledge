# Cash-account unlimited day-trade loophole

## Summary

A structural feature of US retail brokerage accounts: traders who open a "cash account" rather than a margin account can make unlimited day trades on balances under $25,000, sidestepping the Pattern Day Trader rule that otherwise limits day-trading frequency. Nikonomics flags this as essential for small-account beginners and lists it among Webull's defining advantages [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].

## Key claims

- If a Webull user opens a "cash account" at signup, they can make unlimited day trades while their account balance is under $25,000 [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Nikonomics calls this capability "essential if you're trading with a smaller amount of money," framing the cash-account choice as a precondition for day trading on a small account at all [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- The feature is enumerated alongside no-commission trading, in-app advanced charting, and options paper trading as the core reasons the curriculum is built on Webull [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
