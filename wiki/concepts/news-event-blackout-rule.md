# News-Event Blackout Rule

## Summary

A discretionary intraday-trading filter that bars participation in the windows immediately before, during, and through a high-impact economic release, while permitting participation around solitary 10:00 ET releases as long as the trader does not enter during or hold through the release; AMtrades codifies this rule and applies it day-by-day across NFP week [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].

## Key claims

- The rule directs traders to monitor the economic calendar (forexfactory.com/calendar) and apply the blackout to CPI, NFP, FOMC, PCE, GDP, and PPI releases [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Pre-release rationale: before a high-impact print, the market is likely to be stagnant and lag to price objectives, so intraday participation is avoided [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- During-release rationale: high-impact news events are drivers of volatility, which the rule treats as too uncontrolled for intraday execution [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Post-release rationale: once the print is out, the market provides opportunity to catch expansion within risk to defined targets, so participation resumes [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Conditional carve-out: intraday trading before a solitary 10:00 ET high-impact release is allowed, provided the trader does not enter during the release or hold through it [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- NFP-week schedule: avoid intraday participation on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday London; on Wednesday New York and Thursday; and on Friday before 8:30 ET — and resume intraday participation on Friday after the 8:30 release [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Strategic framing: bias and profiles can be framed around planned high-impact economic events, so the calendar is treated as a structural input to the daily plan rather than only a hazard list [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
