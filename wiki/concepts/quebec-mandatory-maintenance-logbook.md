# Quebec Mandatory Standardized Maintenance Logbook

## Summary

The Quebec mandatory standardized maintenance logbook (*carnet d'entretien*) is the central operational document the Bill 16 implementing regulation (Decree 991-2025) makes mandatory for every divided co-ownership in Québec as of August 14, 2025, with a three-year compliance window through August 14, 2028 [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].

## Key claims

- The logbook must be prepared by an accredited / licensed professional — engineer, architect, or other qualified expert [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Article 4 of Decree 991-2025 requires the board of directors to update the maintenance log at least once a year [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The logbook must be subject to a full review every 5 or 10 years, with the cadence determined by the building's characteristics (e.g., size) [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The logbook must include a 25-year forecast of major repairs [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Compliance deadline for the logbook obligation: August 14, 2028 [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- After the deadline, a non-compliant or outdated maintenance log may complicate resale or expose the syndicate board to legal risk [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- The logbook is the data substrate for the contingency fund study — the fund study "must be based on data from the logbook" [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
