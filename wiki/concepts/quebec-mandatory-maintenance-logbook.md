# Quebec Mandatory Standardized Maintenance Logbook

## Summary

The Quebec mandatory standardized maintenance logbook is the central operational document the Bill 16 implementing regulation makes mandatory for the management of all divided co-ownership (copropriété divise) buildings in Quebec, with effect from August 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]]. The logbook must be drafted or reviewed by an independent qualified professional, updated annually by the board of directors, and reviewed every five years (or every ten years for buildings with a maximum of 8 private units or 3 above-ground storeys, and for qualifying horizontal co-ownerships) [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]]. The logbook serves as the upstream data source for the periodic contingency fund study, making it the foundational document of the Bill 16 financial-planning architecture [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].

## Key claims

- The maintenance logbook is mandatory for all divided co-ownership buildings in Quebec under the Bill 16 implementing regulation [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The logbook must be drafted or reviewed by an independent qualified professional with no ties to the co-ownership [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Eligible qualified professionals for the maintenance logbook are limited to engineers, architects, chartered appraisers, and professional technologists [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The board of directors must update the logbook annually [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The logbook must be reviewed every five years by a qualified professional [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The review cycle extends to every ten years for buildings with a maximum of 8 private units or 3 above-ground storeys [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The 10-year review cycle also applies to horizontal co-ownerships, but legal qualification as a co-ownership should be confirmed by a notary or lawyer in such cases because it is a complex issue [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The 10-year extension was introduced by Bill 31's adjustments to the framework [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: a detailed inventory and description of the materials, equipment, and systems located in the common portions, as well as those located in private portions but under the syndicate's responsibility [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: a plan for major repairs and replacements expected over a 25-year period [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Required content: a record of past interventions performed on the inventoried materials or equipment, including maintenance, repairs, warranties, contracts, and inspections [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- The contingency fund study is based on data from the maintenance logbook, making the logbook the upstream input to the periodic financial planning cycle [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Transitional rule: maintenance logbooks produced within the two years prior to August 14, 2025 may be recognized as valid for five years if they meet the regulatory requirements (including preparation by an independent qualified professional) [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
