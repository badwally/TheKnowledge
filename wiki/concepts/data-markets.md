# Data Markets

## Summary

Data markets are proposed marketplaces in which individuals could sell information they generate through online interactions with digital platforms, structurally similar to how Big Tech firms currently license private data for advertising and AI; Sen (2026) evaluates them as an alternative to public-sector data-sharing and finds them limited in practice [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].

## Key claims

- Some experts have proposed data markets in which individuals sell information generated through online interactions with digital platforms [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Licensing of privately collected platform data is already common; Reddit is cited as earning significant revenues from data licensing as an example of platform-side data monetization [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Three concerns have prevented general data markets from evolving: privacy risks; difficulty of data portability; and "thin markets" with too few buyers and sellers or insufficient product diversity [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Sen (2026) concludes that, given these limitations, an attractive alternative is regulated private-sector access to confidential Statistics Canada datasets rather than market-based individual data sales [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
