# AI VC Valuation Premium

## Summary

The systematic overvaluation of AI-focused venture-backed companies relative to non-AI peers, measurable in both late-stage valuation multiples and share of total VC dollars deployed; per SVB's H2 2025 "State of the Markets," AI companies command an 85% median valuation premium at Series D, AI accounts for 58% of all US VC investment, and AI startups see revenue growth at 2-5X the rate of prior-generation SaaS startups — offset by higher burn multiples and lower profit margins [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].

## Key claims

- AI companies command a median valuation premium of 85% above non-AI companies at the Series D stage [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- AI accounts for 58 cents of every dollar deployed in US VC in 2025 [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Per Jeff Richards (Managing Partner): "The AI category is seeing revenue growth at an unprecedented rate — literally 2-5X for early-stage companies vs. what we saw for prior generation SaaS startups" [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Richards notes key metrics like NDR (net dollar retention), TCV (total contract value), gross margins, and barriers to switching "are in many cases also very early in their development," meaning "some of the trusted metrics investors have come to rely on are still early in their formation" [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- AI companies are characterized as less efficient than their non-AI peers, with high burn multiples and low profit margins [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- The distribution of late-stage valuations has grown far wider than historical norms, making median valuation metrics less useful to individual companies — SVB notes they will "likely sunset this metric in future reports, given changing dynamics" [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- AI labor displacement is characterized as "limited for now" but teams are leaner [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
