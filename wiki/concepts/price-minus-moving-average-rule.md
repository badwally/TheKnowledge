# Price-minus-moving-average rule

## Summary

A market-timing rule that generates Buy/Sell signals from the difference between the current price and a moving average over k lagged prices; in Zakamulin's anatomy it is shown to be one-to-one equivalent to a corresponding moving-average-change-of-direction rule, and to admit a representation as a weighted moving average of price changes [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].

## Key claims

- The price-minus-moving-average rule is one of the four main moving-average market-timing rules considered in Zakamulin (2016), alongside the momentum rule, the change-of-direction rule, and the double-crossover method [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
- Zakamulin establishes a one-to-one equivalence between a price-minus-moving-average rule and a corresponding moving-average-change-of-direction rule [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
- Like every other moving-average rule in the anatomy framework, its trading indicator can equivalently be interpreted as a weighted moving average of price changes [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
- It can also be presented as a weighted average of momentum rules computed over different averaging periods, since every trading rule in the anatomy admits this representation [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
