# Tech-Enabled Reserve Study Firm

## Summary

The condo-capital-infra synthesis recommends an "AI-native, agentic-first, tech-enabled reserve study firm with a proprietary probabilistic engine" — a software-led, services-delivered business shape with no hardware in a 24-month window [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]]. The mix is roughly 70% software / 30% services / 0% hardware, layering a subscription tail on top of transactional reserve studies to build a recurring-revenue and data-flywheel motion absent from pure service firms [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].

## Key claims

- Recommended business shape: AI-native, agentic-first, tech-enabled reserve study firm with proprietary probabilistic engine; software-led delivery (~70%), services (~30%), no hardware in 24 months [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Year 1 staffing: 2-3 licensed reserve analysts plus a small engineering team; build the core probabilistic engine and ship 50-100 studies in Florida at $8-15K each [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Year 2: expand to CA and NJ as their reserve-study bills land; 200-400 studies; productize the engine as a $99-299/mo subscription tail; begin insurer / reinsurer conversations [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Pure service firms cannot escape the 3-5 year repurchase cycle; the subscription tail (re-forecasting, scenario tools, board-facing dashboard) converts a transactional study into recurring revenue and creates the data flywheel that improves the engine over time [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Hardware is excluded for the 24-month window because the data needed to beat incumbents (work-order history) already exists and is unused; sensors are framed as a year-3 upsell, not an R&D line [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- The licensed reserve analyst stays human due to E&O exposure and sign-off requirements; software cannot replace the RA [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Funded plan: ~$471K Year 1 cost, $82K Year 1 revenue, ~$390K Year 1 burn; crossover at 3x YoY growth lands late Year 5 / early Year 6; total cumulative funding to crossover ~$8-10M across seed, Series A, Series B [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Bootstrap variant on $100K is feasible only with a technical founder, deposits-on-signing pricing, warm Florida pipeline, and a committed 1099 reserve analyst at launch; cash-flow positive monthly by month 4-5; cumulative break-even month 11-12 [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
