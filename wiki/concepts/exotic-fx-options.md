# Exotic FX Options

## Summary

FX options with non-standard features beyond plain calls and puts — including path-dependent structures, barrier conditions, and combinations — that require trading conventions and pricing approaches distinct from vanilla options; covered as a dedicated section in the Lehman Brothers FX training manual after the vanilla framework and the Greeks have been established [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].

## Key claims

- Exotic options are introduced in the Lehman manual after vanilla options, the Greeks, and second-order Greeks have been established as prerequisite background [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Exotic options carry their own "trading conventions" — distinct quoting and hedging norms among market-makers — that differ from vanilla-option conventions [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- The manual then transitions from exotics into "trading strategies" — combinations of vanillas and/or exotics that express specific views on price, volatility, or term structure [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- The FX Options section closes with a glossary, signaling that exotic-option vocabulary is treated as part of the desk's foundational lexicon [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
