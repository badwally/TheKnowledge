# Data Cooperative

## Summary

A data cooperative is a member-owned organisation that holds and administers personal data on behalf of its members, who govern the entity democratically and pool their data to repurpose it in their collective interest [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].

## Key claims

- The Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council report positions data cooperatives as the appropriate governance mechanism when individuals want to voluntarily pool data resources and repurpose the data in the interests of those it represents [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- Data cooperatives are the go-to mechanism when relationships are formed between peers or like-minded people who join forces to collectively steward their data and create one voice in relation to a company or institution [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- Data cooperatives are one of three legal mechanisms (with data trusts and corporate/contractual mechanisms) in the Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council data-governance toolbox; the choice among them depends primarily on the purpose, the benefits, and the nature of the relationship between actors [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
