# Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations (LIME)

## Summary

Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations (LIME) is an explainable-AI technique that approximates a complex model's local behaviour around a specific prediction with a simpler, interpretable surrogate model. It is one of the two XAI methods used in the Peng et al. (2025) study of flood-driven pavement deterioration.

## Key claims

- LIME is an Explainable AI (XAI) technique applied to assess the impact of input features on model predictions [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- LIME was used together with SHAP in the Peng et al. (2025) study to assess the impact of flooding on pavement performance [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
