# Brake Bed-in Procedure

## Summary

The bed-in procedure is the post-installation step for new brake pads or rotors that conditions the friction surfaces before the brakes deliver full stopping performance — both SRAM's HRD service workflow and the eTap AXS Systems install manual position it as the final step after [[concepts/brake-bleed|bleeding]] and pad reinstallation [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]] [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].

## Key claims

- SRAM's HRD service manual closes with a dedicated "Bed-in Procedure" section, positioning it as the required final step before the brakes are returned to normal use [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- The eTap AXS Systems User Manual likewise schedules the Bed-in Procedure after Hose Shortening and Bleed and before the rider heads out, treating it as part of the standard new-system install flow rather than an optional polish step [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].
