# Training Stress Score (TSS)

## Summary

Training Stress Score (TSS) is a [[entities/trainingpeaks|TrainingPeaks]]-trademarked load metric used throughout the 2021 cycling guide as part of a family of load-management indicators that includes TSB, ATL, CTL, IF, and Normalized Power [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].

## Key claims

- TSS is one of several metrics the guide explicitly attributes to TrainingPeaks LLC as a trademark or item of intellectual property, alongside Training Stress Balance (TSB), Acute Training Load (ATL), Critical Training Load (CTL), Intensity Factor (IF), and Normalized Power [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
- These metrics form the load-monitoring vocabulary the guide uses across its planning, periodisation, and monitoring sections [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
