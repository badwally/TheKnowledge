# Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)

## Summary

Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) is the discipline of acquiring and analyzing measurements of a structure's behaviour in order to assess its condition and detect damage, and is one of the two technology streams (alongside BIM) that the 2025 ITcon framework by Porto Oliveira and Dominguez Sotelino seeks to integrate for viaduct management.

## Key claims

- SHM has independently advanced viaduct management as a technology stream parallel to Building Information Modelling, but its integration with BIM is still underexplored [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- In the Rio Claro Viaduct case study, SHM was operationalized through field tests including ambient vibration analysis to capture the viaduct's dynamic behaviour [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- SHM-derived dynamic data was used as the input for calibrating a finite element model, which in turn generated simulated damage scenarios for training ANN-based damage assessment models [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The 2025 ITcon paper frames SHM-based assessment as a means of overcoming the limitations of traditional visual inspections for infrastructure managers [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
