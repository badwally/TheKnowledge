# Ontario Board's Future-Funding Plan for the Reserve Fund (s. 94(8))

## Summary

The board's "current plan ... for future funding of the reserve fund" is the artifact, proposed under subsection 94(8) of the Ontario Condominium Act, 1998, that operationalizes the reserve fund study by setting the corporation's actual contribution trajectory. It is treated by O. Reg. 48/01 as a distinct artifact from the reserve fund study itself, and is explicitly enumerated as Category 6 of the "core record" definition — making it producible under the regulation's request-for-records regime [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key claims

- The "current plan proposed by the board under subsection 94(8) of the Act for future funding of the reserve fund" is paragraph 6 of the "core record" definition in O. Reg. 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- As a core record, the plan must be producible under the "request for records" regime — a request to examine or obtain copies of records under subsection 55(3) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The plan is a board-proposed artifact, distinct from the reserve fund study itself; the regulation treats the s. 94(8) plan as the implementing artifact that the reserve fund study informs but does not constitute [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The regulation defines this plan using "current" as a temporal qualifier, implying any prior plan is superseded once the board proposes a new one; only the "current" plan is a core record [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
