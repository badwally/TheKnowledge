# Time-dependent reliability

## Summary

Time-dependent reliability characterises how the probability that a system fulfils its function evolves over its service life as resistance deteriorates and load conditions change — a central object of analysis in structural and infrastructure safety assessment.

## Key claims

- Bridge resistance deteriorates over time, impacting safety and time-dependent reliability due to non-stationary degradation and increasing load frequency and intensity [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- The time-dependent reliability of reinforced concrete structures can be evaluated using a Gamma process-based approach that accounts for non-stationary resistance degradation [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
