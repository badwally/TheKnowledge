# Bridge performance degradation

## Summary

Bridge performance degradation refers to the deterioration of a bridge's structural performance over time. It is framed in the literature as arising from the combined influence of various factors, motivating multivariate statistical and Bayesian modelling approaches for performance assessment and reliable prediction.

## Key claims

- The degradation of bridge structural performance arises from the combined influence of various factors, requiring performance assessment and reliable prediction methods that can integrate these factors rather than treating them in isolation [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- Yang et al. (2024) address bridge performance degradation by constructing a model based on the multi-variate Bayesian dynamic linear method, treating degradation as a multivariate phenomenon [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- Downstream extensions of this line of work include strain prediction in large-span arch bridges using the TimeXer model with temperature and traffic loads, and uncertainty-aware multi-step prediction of structural health monitoring sensor streams under extreme typhoon events with an enhanced Bayesian dynamic linear model [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
