# Parking Structure Maintenance Categories (PCI MNL-136-04)

## Summary

PCI MNL-136-04 establishes three broadly accepted categories of maintenance specifically required for precast, prestressed concrete parking structures — Housekeeping, Preventive Maintenance, and Structural Repairs — plus a Periodic Structural Audit treated as a separately cadenced activity, all reflecting the unique exposure burden of parking structures relative to other building types [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].

## Key claims

- The three broad maintenance categories accepted by PCI for precast parking structures are Housekeeping, Preventive Maintenance, and Structural Repairs [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- A Periodic Structural Audit (Section 2.5 of the manual) is treated as a distinct fourth maintenance activity, separate from routine housekeeping, preventive maintenance, and reactive structural repairs [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- PCI positions parking structures as requiring more maintenance attention than other building types because structural components are exposed directly to weather and environmental conditions including extreme temperature changes, rain, snow, deicing salts, road grime, and dampness [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Severity of maintenance demand depends on the geographic location of the structure and local environmental conditions, but in all locations the added burden of direct exposure dictates greater maintenance attention than other building types [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Without proper maintenance any structure's life will be compromised, including precast structures whose factory-controlled fabrication otherwise gives the owner/operator a decisive durability edge from the start [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- A comprehensive maintenance program both increases service life / reduces long-term structural repairs and produces a cleaner, safer, more user-friendly atmosphere that promotes repeat business and discourages littering and loitering [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- A routine maintenance program should be set up immediately upon turnover of the parking structure [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Although the PCI manual targets precast/prestressed concrete, parking structures commonly incorporate cast-in-place concrete, steel, masonry, aluminum, or wood, plus non-structural systems (elevators, escalators, people movers, mechanical units, electrical, security, landscaping) that have specific manufacturer-specified maintenance requirements outside the PCI manual's scope [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
