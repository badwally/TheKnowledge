# Liquidity Pairing (Smart Money Order Pairing)

## Summary

The ICT-style explanation of why price drives toward buy-stop and sell-stop pools: smart money cannot fill large positions without an offsetting counterparty, so price is engineered into these pools so smart-money sells can pair with retail buy stops above old highs and smart-money buys can pair with retail sell stops below old lows [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].

## Key claims

- The market "tends to refer to areas with liquidity to pair orders" — pairing offsetting flow is the driving mechanism, not pattern formation [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Above old highs: smart money sells into the buy stops, offloading their sells to willing buyers — "smart money sell ... offload their sells for buy stops to willing sellers in a discount" [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Below old lows: smart money buys against the sell stops [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- The implication daytradingrauf draws is that the trader should target the pools of resting orders rather than read the patterns that produce them: "We do not look at patterns we look at areas where orders may reside to target them" [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
