# Buyside liquidity

## Summary

Buyside liquidity is the pool of resting buy-stop orders sitting above prior swing highs, which a bullish Power of 3 expansion targets after the open seeks a discount [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].

## Key claims

- In the bullish OLHC template, after seeking a discount, price expands up to attack buyside liquidity (HIGH) and closes near the high [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- In a bearish OHLC scenario, price first seeks a premium by taking out buyside liquidity (HIGH) above the open before expanding down [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- A strong bullish weekly candle expands up above the weekly open to target buyside liquidity (premium) and the HTF target [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- The previous day's high serves as a buyside liquidity target inside a daily PO3 setup [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
- December highs are presented as a buyside liquidity objective for the January 2023 bullish monthly setup [[sources/pdf-075e9932265b]].
