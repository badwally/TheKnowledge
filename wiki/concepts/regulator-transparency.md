# Regulator Transparency in Blockchain Networks

## Summary

A network-design family that exposes blockchain transaction data and audit trails to antitrust regulators, improving the speed and accuracy of investigations and converting standardized chain data into an enforcement asset [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].

## Key claims

- Audit-trail visibility: blockchain networks can be designed to give antitrust investigators a clear life-cycle audit trail of an asset as it moves through a firm's supply chain [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Comprehensive transaction data: networks can supply investigators with more accurate, reliable, and comprehensive transaction data than the piecemeal records they typically receive [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Industry-wide regulator-only ledger: an envisioned blockchain accessible only by select parties or regulators could contain industry-wide transaction data, providing an unmatched investigative tool [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Standardization speedup: the standardized data format on a blockchain may lead to faster resolution of antitrust investigations [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
