# Inspection Planning Decision Support

## Summary

A class of decision-support frameworks that translate fitted defect-arrival models into inspection intervals by trading off a risk proxy — such as the age of the oldest unobserved defect — against inspection frequency, used when explicit cost data is unavailable.

## Key claims

- Khalaj et al. develop an inspection decision support model based on fitted Homogeneous Poisson Process models to assess the probability distribution of the oldest defect, treated as a proxy for defect severity because a detailed propagation model is not available [[sources/web-2026-03-04-157]].
- In the absence of cost data, the HPP models are used to examine the trade-off between the oldest unobserved defect — the risk proxy — and the inspection frequency [[sources/web-2026-03-04-157]].
- The framework is intended to provide new insight into inspection planning for rail asset management and decision-making, including setting inspection and grinding intervals appropriately for different tracks [[sources/web-2026-03-04-157]].
