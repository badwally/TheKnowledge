# Ontario Reserve Fund Monitoring

## Summary

Ontario reserve fund monitoring is the ongoing operational practice — distinct from the periodic Reserve Fund Study itself — of tracking reserve fund spending and balance against the most recent RFS and the contribution and cash-flow tables published in the Notice of Future Funding. This is one of the CMRAO-defined licensee obligations for condominium managers in Ontario, and is statutorily braided to the Board's Section 115(8) investment-plan duty: monitoring informs the liquidity profile the investment plan must satisfy [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].

## Key claims

- Monitoring reserve fund spending and balance relative to the Reserve Fund Study is one of the CMRAO-defined licensee obligations for condominium managers under the CMSA [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The contribution table from the NOFF is used to determine the required contributions to the Reserve Fund for budgetary purposes [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The cash flow table from the NOFF is used to monitor the reserve fund balance by comparing expected cash flows to the financial statements [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- Reserve fund spending monitoring is treated by the Practice Guide as a distinct stream from operating-budget monitoring [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The Practice Guide explicitly addresses cash flow, operating surpluses and deficits, and shortfalls as monitoring sub-topics for the corporation's overall financial position [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The Practice Guide treats reserve fund investments as a sub-topic of monitoring, reflecting that reserve funds held in trust under Section 115 must be actively invested, with the manager supporting the Board in the investment process [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The statutory grounding for the investment sub-topic is Section 115(8) of the Condominium Act, 1998, which requires the Board to develop an investment plan based on the anticipated cash requirements of the reserve fund as set out in the most recent RFS before investing any part of the reserve fund [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- The investment plan should be reviewed annually and refreshed at minimum every time the reserve fund study is updated, making the monitoring-and-investment-plan cycle a paired operational artifact [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- The monitoring obligation closes with an explicit duty to keep the Board informed of the financial status of the corporation [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
