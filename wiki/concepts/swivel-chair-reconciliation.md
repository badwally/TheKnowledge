# Swivel Chair Reconciliation Problem

## Summary

"Swivel chair" reconciliation is the industry term for the manual, multi-system workflow that real estate managers — including community-association managers — must perform when their accounting software and their banking systems are not connected by middleware. CINC Systems uses the term as the explicit competitive narrative anchor for the June 2023 launch of TresRE, framing manual cross-system reconciliation as a debilitating problem that requires teams of people across multiple departments and that TresRE collapses into "just a few clicks" [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key claims

- The "swivel chair" problem describes real estate managers having to log into bank portals that are not designed for real estate, separately from logging into their accounting software, in order to make transfers, pull statements, examine check images, and reconcile books [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- The root cause framed in CINC's launch narrative is that legacy accounting and banking solutions are not connected by middleware [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- CINC's CRO Shea Dittrich characterized the swivel chair approach to reconciling numerous accounts at the end of the month as debilitating and as requiring teams of people across multiple departments [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- CINC positions integration middleware — first inside its own accounting platform, then via Paygami (2018), and most recently exposed to third-party accounting platforms via TresRE (June 2023) — as the architectural answer to the swivel chair problem [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- CINC quantifies its solution to the swivel chair problem for its existing customers as saving the average CINC customer "hundreds of hours per week" of in-software banking versus separate bank-portal workflows [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Valley Bank SVP Marc Nuzzolo echoed the framing on the bank-partner side, citing TresRE as letting him provide real estate clients with efficient processes that eliminate manual reconciliations [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
