# Frontier-model depreciation

## Summary

The empirical pattern in which frontier foundation models lose their leadership position within weeks-to-months of release, driven by competitive launches and rapid open-source convergence. Innovation Endeavors' June 2025 "State of Foundation Models" report quantifies the depreciation: "the typical model becomes obsolete within 3 weeks of launch" and frontier models depreciate on a 6–12 month timescale [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].

## Key claims

- The typical frontier model becomes obsolete within approximately 3 weeks of launch, attributed to competitive pressure and open-source convergence [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Frontier models depreciate on a 6–12 month timescale even when not surpassed within weeks [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- A concrete illustration: GPT-4 cost more than $100M to train and was released in March 2023 (closed-source); within roughly a year, DeepSeek-VL was released in March 2024 (open-source) at less than $10M to train [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Open source continues to converge with closed source over time, as documented by Artificial Analysis Q4 2024 reporting [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Innovation Endeavors frames the broader implication that the economics of foundation models are "confusing": OpenAI and Anthropic show truly unprecedented growth at $B+ annual revenue while end-to-end training costs near $500M and models become obsolete quickly [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
