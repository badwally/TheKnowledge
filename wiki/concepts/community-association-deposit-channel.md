# Community Association Deposit Channel

## Summary

The community association deposit channel is the segment of US bank funding sourced from operating accounts, reserve accounts, and assessment-receivable accounts held by homeowner and condominium associations (HOAs) and their managing-agent firms. Acquirers of HOA-banking franchises (e.g., CIT's January 2020 acquisition of Mutual of Omaha Bank) characterize the channel as a stable, lower-cost source of deposits relative to retail and commercial banking franchises, and use that funding-cost differential as the principal strategic rationale for franchise acquisition [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].

## Key claims

- Acquirers of HOA-banking franchises characterize the community association deposit channel as a stable source of deposits [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Acquirers characterize the channel as a lower-cost source of deposits relative to the acquirer's existing funding mix [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- CIT explicitly identified "the addition of the homeowner association deposit channel" as a driver of reduced overall cost of funds in its January 2020 acquisition rationale for Mutual of Omaha Bank [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- CIT framed the channel as having "significant growth potential" in its post-close strategic narrative [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- The scale of a single market-leading franchise channel is on the order of $4.5 billion in community association deposits, the figure CIT reported for the Mutual of Omaha Bank book as of September 30, 2019 [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- The community-association-deposit channel sits inside the broader "community association banking" line of business, which CIT enumerated alongside commercial financing, middle market banking, equipment and vendor financing, factoring, railcar financing, treasury and payments solutions, and capital markets and asset management as components of its commercial banking segment [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- The market-leading position in the channel was held by Mutual of Omaha Bank prior to its January 1, 2020 acquisition by CIT Bank, N.A. [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
