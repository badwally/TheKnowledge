# Corporate and Contractual Data-Sharing Mechanisms

## Summary

Corporate and contractual data-sharing mechanisms are the third legal mechanism in the Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council 2021 toolbox for data stewardship; they are used to design an ecosystem of trust where a group of organisations see benefits in sharing data under mutually agreed terms in a controlled way [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].

## Key claims

- Corporate and contractual mechanisms are better suited than trusts or cooperatives for creating data-sharing relationships between organisations (B2B), rather than between individuals or between individuals and an institution [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- The involvement of an independent data steward is envisaged as the means of creating a trusted environment in which stakeholders feel comfortable sharing data with other parties they may not know or have a prior trust relationship with [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- The mechanism sits alongside data trusts and data cooperatives in the three-part Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council toolbox; the choice depends on the purpose, the benefits sought, and the nature of the relationships among actors [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
