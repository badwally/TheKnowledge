# Smart Contracts

## Summary

Smart contracts are computerized transaction protocols that automatically execute the terms of a contract once specified conditions are met, eliminating the need for human intervention in enforcement [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].

## Key claims

- Smart contracts are pieces of software that automatically execute contract terms when conditions are satisfied [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- In contexts where trust between parties is low — such as between two competitors sharing data — smart contracts ensure no party can gain advantage by failing to honor an agreement [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Combined with blockchain's distributed ledger, smart contracts can act as an incontestable enforcer among trading parties, facilitating financial transactions among unknown counterparties without dispute [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Smart-contract automation can ensure safe cargo shipping in cross-border trades, minimize paperwork, save labor costs, and protect data integrity [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
