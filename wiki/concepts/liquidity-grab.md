# Liquidity Grab

## Summary

A liquidity grab is, in the ICT-derived order-block playbook, the move in which price runs recent highs (buyside liquidity) or recent lows (sellside liquidity) before reversing; it is one of three conditions the lesson requires before a high-probability order-block setup can form, and it sits underneath the broader "why OBs work" argument — that breaking old structure takes out old players and lets big/smart money reposition into easy liquidity [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]]. In TTrades's framework, liquidity grabs appear at two scales: an HTF liquidity grab into the directional premise and an LTF liquidity grab as the standalone first-stage trigger before BOS + displacement + FVG [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].

## Key claims

- Higher-probability order blocks require, among other conditions, a liquidity grab — "price enters a HTF POI / liquidity grab / Displacement, mss, imbalance" [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- In a bearish setup, price runs recent highs (annotated "$$$" as liquidity being taken from buyside) into the HTF POI before displacing to the downside [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- In a bullish setup, price runs recent lows (annotated "$$$" as liquidity being taken from sellside) before aggressively displacing up [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- The lesson's framing of "why OBs work" rests on liquidity: old structure breaks and gives the trader the long or short setup because it takes out old "players" in the game and allows big/smart money to reposition and take advantage of "easy" liquidity [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- Liquidity is the second item in the summary checklist, immediately after HTF POI and before Displacement [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- Liquidity also defines the trade target: the playbook ends at "Target Liquidity," with the target above (long) or below (short) representing the opposing pool of resting orders the setup is designed to engineer [[sources/pdf-69a1f97797ce]].
- TTrades places "HTF LIQ GRAB / FVG / POI" at the top of the Models 1–4 scaffold, indicating that the HTF liquidity grab and HTF POI/FVG can be substitutes for one another in establishing the directional premise [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- TTrades names the "LTF Liquidity Grab" as the standalone first-stage trigger that fires before "BOS + Displacement + FVG" — a sweep at the lower timeframe must precede the structural confirmation for an entry to qualify [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- The "Failed swing" variation of TTrades's Models 1–4 substitutes a failed-swing structure for the LTF liquidity grab while leaving the rest of the entry sequence intact, treating an inability to take liquidity as an equally valid premise [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
