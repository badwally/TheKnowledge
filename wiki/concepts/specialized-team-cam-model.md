# Specialized-Team CAM Operating Model

## Summary

The specialized-team CAM operating model is an alternative to the conventional community-association-management staffing pattern in which a single property manager handles all aspects of a building — resident requests, building emergencies, vendor coordination, finances, compliance — for a portfolio of associations. Daisy positions itself as the named exemplar of the specialized-team model, asserting that the one-property-manager-handles-everything pattern leads to internal burnout and a reactive customer experience, and substitutes a dedicated resident-request team plus an operations team that supports supers and on-site staff, with routing systems that send work to the right specialist rather than piling it on one person [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. Daisy frames this as the structural complement to its AI-routing layer: AI handles intelligent task routing and compliance automation, and the specialized human teams handle what humans do best.

## Key claims

- The conventional CAM model assigns a single property manager to handle all aspects of a building (resident requests, building emergencies, vendor coordination), leading to internal burnout and a reactive customer experience [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Daisy substitutes specialized teams: a dedicated resident-request team and an operations team that supports supers and on-site staff [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Daisy's specialization is complemented by routing systems that ensure work goes to the right specialist rather than piling on one person [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- The model is designed to optimize for two outcomes: getting things done quickly, and giving everyone full transparency into the process [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Daisy frames the model as the combination of "human care with smart automation," with the explicit claim that technology alone is not the answer [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
