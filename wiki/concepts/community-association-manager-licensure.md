# Community Association Manager Licensure (Florida)

## Summary

Florida regulates the licensure of community association managers (CAMs) and CAM firms through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation. CS/CS/HB 913 (2025), Chapter 2025-175, layers post-revocation prohibitions and DBPR online-account obligations on top of the existing CAM licensure framework [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].

## Key claims

- A person whose CAM license is revoked is prohibited under HB 913 (2025) from holding any direct or indirect ownership interest in, or being an employee, partner, officer, director, or trustee of, a community association management firm for a specified timeframe [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- HB 913 (2025) requires every CAM licensee to create and maintain an online licensure account with DBPR and to identify specified information on that account [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- HB 913 (2025) requires CAM firms to identify, on their DBPR online licensure account, the CAMs they employ to provide community association management services [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- HB 913 (2025) was approved by the Governor on 6/23/2025 and codified as Chapter 2025-175 on 6/24/2025, anchoring the effective date of the new CAM licensure controls [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
