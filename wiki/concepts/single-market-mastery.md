# Single-Market Mastery

## Summary

Discretionary-trading discipline rule taught in ICT's 2022 mentorship and recapped by Trader Theory: focus on one market only and master it, rather than diversifying attention across multiple instruments — on the implicit theory that the time-of-day, day-of-week, and seasonality patterns the methodology exploits are instrument-specific and require dedicated screen time to internalize [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].

## Key claims

- Trader Theory states the rule directly: "Focus on one market only and master it" [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Pairs with the methodology's instrument-specific timing rules — e.g., "Indices price action is worse in the Summer months" — implying that the regularities being learned do not transfer cleanly across instruments [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Sits in the same "Extras" cluster as the rule that it is acceptable to not have a bias and to leave the charts when uncertain — framing mastery as quality of attention rather than breadth of coverage [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
