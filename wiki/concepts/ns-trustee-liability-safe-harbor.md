# Nova Scotia Trustee Liability Safe Harbor (§3C, §3D)

## Summary

§§3C and 3D of the Nova Scotia Trustee Act, introduced by the 2002 c. 10 s. 45 amendment, define the trustee's liability regime under the prudent-investor standard: §3C provides a plan-conditional safe harbor for investment losses arising from conduct that conformed to a prudent-investor plan or strategy, and §3D permits courts assessing damages payable by a trustee to take into account the overall performance of the investments rather than the outcome of any individual security [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]]. §11(2) provides a separate, narrower safe harbor for continuing to hold an investment that has ceased to be authorized [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].

## Key claims

- §3C provides that a trustee is not liable for a loss to the trust arising from the investment of trust property if the conduct that led to the loss conformed to a plan or strategy for the investment of trust property — comprising reasonable assessments of risk and return — that a prudent investor could adopt under comparable circumstances [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- §3D allows a court assessing damages payable by a trustee for a loss arising from the investment of trust property to take into account the overall performance of the investments [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- The §3C safe harbor is plan-conditional: a trustee invoking the safe harbor must demonstrate the existence of a plan or strategy comprising reasonable assessments of risk and return [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- §11(2) provides a separate, narrower safe harbor: no trustee shall be liable for any breach of trust by reason only of continuing to hold an investment that has ceased to be one authorized by the instrument of trust or by the general law [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
- §13(1) provides a limited-liability rule for improper mortgage advances: where a trustee improperly advances trust money on a mortgage security that would at the time have been a proper investment for a smaller sum, the security shall be deemed an authorized investment for the smaller sum and the trustee shall only be liable to make good the excess plus interest [[sources/web-2010-04-07-c59]].
