# Bullish SMT divergence

## Summary

A bullish SMT (Smart Money Technique) divergence is the configuration in which one sibling instrument makes a lower low at a higher-timeframe point of interest while its correlated sibling fails to do so, with the lagging sibling favoured to the upside once displacement and a market-structure shift confirm [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].

## Key claims

- Bullish SMT divergence template: $ES makes a lower low at the HTF PoI while $NQ fails to do so, favouring $NQ to the upside [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- Bullish daily-chart example given by the lesson: $ES takes out liquidity and immediately begins an upwards trend, putting in higher lows in comparison to $NQ; $NQ struggles to get back above the original HTF PoI, and by the time it does, $ES has already made two higher lows — labelled by the lesson as a bullish divergence favouring $ES to the upside [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
- Confirmation always comes from displacement and market structure rather than from the divergence asymmetry alone [[sources/pdf-19ce7da9ecfc]].
