# Ontario Condominium Authority Delegation Framework

## Summary

The Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 establishes a delegation-based regulatory architecture in which the Lieutenant Governor in Council designates a corporation as the "condominium authority," specifies which provisions of the Act and the regulations are delegated to that authority, and enters into an administrative agreement governing the relationship [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]]. The delegated provisions are administered by the condominium authority rather than directly by the Ministry, creating a co-regulator model that pairs ministerial policy oversight with arms-length day-to-day administration, including a corresponding Tribunal regime [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].

## Key claims

- "Condominium authority" means the corporation that the Lieutenant Governor in Council has designated as such under clause 1.1(1)(a) [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- "Delegated provisions" means the provisions of the Act and the regulations that the Lieutenant Governor in Council specifies under clause 1.1(1)(b) and of which the administration is delegated to the condominium authority under subsection 1.1(3) [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- "Administrative agreement" means the agreement described in subsection 1.2(1), establishing the governance relationship between the Crown and the condominium authority [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- The Table of Contents enumerates structural mechanisms supporting the delegation regime, including Designation of condominium authority, Administrative agreement, Policy directions, Consultation, Compliance by condominium authority, Review, Conflict, Minister's authority to appoint administrator, Status of board during administrator's tenure, and Revocation of designation [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- The Table of Contents further provides for governance-of-the-authority provisions including Condition precedent for exercise of certain powers, Criteria and directives re board members, Board appointments, Change in number of directors, Appointment of chair, Public access to corporate by-laws, Public access to information, Disclosure of compensation and other payments, Employees, Not Crown agency, No personal liability employee of the Crown, No Crown liability, Indemnification of the Crown, No personal liability board members and others, Not public money, Audit, and Annual report — operationalizing transparency, accountability, and Crown-immunity boundaries [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- The Act establishes a Condominium Authority Tribunal as a separate Part with its own definitions, Establishment, Chair / vice-chairs, Termination of appointments, Applications, Combined applications, Parties to a proceeding, Right of affected persons, Alternative dispute resolution, Power to dismiss applications, Jurisdiction, Order during proceeding, Orders at end of proceeding, Payment under order for compensation, costs or a penalty, Appeals, Settlement, and Publication of orders provisions [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
- "Condominium management provider," "condominium management services," and "condominium manager" all have the same meaning as in the Condominium Management Services Act, 2015 — establishing a cross-statute integration with the parallel CMRAO licensing regime [[sources/web-2014-07-24-031]].
