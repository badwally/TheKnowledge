# Volume Imbalance (V.I.)

## Summary

A Volume Imbalance (V.I.) is an area of price that has been left without a candle body — there is up-and-down movement in the overlapping of the wicks, but since the bulk of the volume is in the body of the candle, this area is termed a volume imbalance; per Ali Khan's "The ICT Bible" (2023), V.I. is distinguished from a standard FVG (which has no wick overlap in the gap zone) and from a Gap Imbalance (which has no price delivery at all, including wicks) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key claims

- A Volume Imbalance (V.I.) occurs when there is an area of price that has been left without a candle body [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- There is up-and-down movement in the overlapping of the wicks, however since the bulk of the volume is in the body of the candle, this area is dubbed a Volume Imbalance [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- V.I. is distinct from a standard FVG (where no wicks overlap in the gap) and from a Gap Imbalance (where there is no price delivery at all, including wick movement) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
