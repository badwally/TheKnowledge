# Liquidity pool

## Summary

A liquidity pool, in ICT methodology, is a cluster of resting buy or sell orders at or near a specific price level — characterized as the "open interest" of buyers and sellers — that price is drawn toward when smart money seeks to pair institutional orders against retail stops and breakout entries [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].

## Key claims

- Liquidity is described as the "open interest" of buyers and sellers in the market and can be further defined by those entities at or near specific price levels [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- A low that is under the current market will typically have trailed sell stops under it from long trades, plus sell stops from traders who wish to trade a breakout lower for a short position — these are the resting orders a downward run on liquidity targets [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- The ICT Action Plan treats a liquidity pool as one of the three focus points (alongside orderblock and fair value gap) where the trader consults the interest rate triad and DXY to confirm smart-money participation before committing to a trade [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
