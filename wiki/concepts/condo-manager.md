## Summary

Condo Manager is a Canadian-market integrated accounting, management, and communication software platform for condo and HOA management, now operating as a sister product within the [[entities/property-control]] portfolio under [[entities/vertica-capital]] ownership [[sources/web-2025-07-02-a17]] [[sources/web-2025-10-31-2a4]]. The Condo Manager to Property Control relationship is derived from live product pages; no standalone acquisition press release has been identified [[sources/web-2025-07-02-a17]].

## Key claims

- Positions as a leader in integrated accounting, management, and communication software for condo/HOA management [[sources/web-2025-10-31-2a4]]
- Listed as a named product under Property Control's portfolio, with a direct link from propertycontrol.com to condomanager.com [[sources/web-2025-07-02-a17]]
- Bilingual platform (English + French), targeting Canadian market across Ontario, Quebec, and other provinces [[sources/web-2025-10-31-2a4]]
- Core modules: accounting, communication, management, condo inspection, web portal and mobile app [[sources/web-2025-10-31-2a4]]
- Integrations include a Condo Manager API, direct payment via SparcPay, owners' meeting management, LeapAP digital bill approval and payment, smart lockers, and barcode reader [[sources/web-2025-10-31-2a4]]
- Supports unlimited units and corporations per manager login [[sources/web-2025-10-31-2a4]]
- No independently published acquisition date or deal terms found as of June 2026 [[sources/web-2025-07-02-a17]]
