# Buyside and Sellside Liquidity

## Summary

In ICT trading, buyside and sellside liquidity refer to pools of resting stop orders above old highs (buyside) and below old lows (sellside) that price gets manipulated toward during the Power of 3 manipulation phase before reversing into the distribution phase [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].

## Key claims

- Power of 3 helps visualize price by pairing overnight trading ranges with "points of liquidity" [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- In a bearish PO3 example, manipulation up takes out buyside liquidity, then price prepares for the distribution phase down (overnight) to seek sellside liquidity [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- In a bullish PO3 example, manipulation down takes out recent sellside liquidity, then price reverses back up and sets up the buy model to take out buyside liquidity [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
- The execution rule is to enter after liquidity has been taken and there is a market structure shift in the direction of price distribution [[sources/pdf-a3789262265a]].
