# Fed Pivot Trade

## Summary

Investor positioning for an anticipated softening or reversal of Federal Reserve policy tightening; in Eric Stein's December 12, 2022 Morgan Stanley outlook framing, the pivot trade is the dominant factor for fixed-income markets in 2023 because the market is forward-looking and prices the pivot before it actually arrives, with three concrete area implications: high-quality spread products, U.S. duration, and emerging-markets debt [[sources/pdf-bf1afc73ac8f]].

## Key claims

- Per Eric Stein, while the Fed pivot clearly hasn't happened yet by December 2022, the market is a forward-looking mechanism, and investors now expect rate increases to come at a slower pace than most of the second half of 2022 [[sources/pdf-bf1afc73ac8f]].
- Stein argues the Fed pivot trade is starting to get priced in but will be the major factor in considering fixed-income opportunities in 2023 [[sources/pdf-bf1afc73ac8f]].
- First implication — high-quality spread products: particularly agency MBS and securitized products, where spreads have stayed wide and have not come in as much as high yield and investment grade issues; investors still wanting extra yield over Treasurys will draw flows there [[sources/pdf-bf1afc73ac8f]].
- Second implication — U.S. duration: Stein sees value in U.S. Treasurys when the 10-year is in the 3.60%–3.80% range (down from a recent peak of 4.20%); execution options include direct U.S. Treasury issues or duration-heavy strategies like core plus or municipal bonds [[sources/pdf-bf1afc73ac8f]].
- Third implication — emerging-markets debt: attractive for investors comfortable with a riskier asset class, particularly local-currency issues, given a weakening but still-strong dollar; EM corporate debt typically does well in a weak-dollar environment [[sources/pdf-bf1afc73ac8f]].
- Stein's anchor variable for the pivot trade is medium-term inflation: he expects inflation will continue to come down in combination with a significant slowdown of the U.S. economy [[sources/pdf-bf1afc73ac8f]].
