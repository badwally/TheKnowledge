# Florida Condominium Limited Proxies

## Summary

Florida Statute §718.112(2)(b) restricts how unit owners in residential condominiums may vote by proxy. General proxies are prohibited for residential unit-owner votes; only limited proxies substantially conforming to a form adopted by the division may be used. Limited proxies are required for the most consequential association votes — reserve waivers/reductions, financial-reporting waivers, declaration amendments, and bylaw/articles amendments — and proxies of any kind are prohibited in board-member elections except as provided in paragraph (d). Proxies are time-limited (90 days from the first meeting) and revocable at any time [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].

## Key claims

- Except as specifically otherwise provided, unit owners in a residential condominium may not vote by general proxy but may vote by limited proxies substantially conforming to a limited proxy form adopted by the division [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Limited proxies must be used for votes to waive or reduce reserves in accordance with §718.112(2)(f)2 [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Limited proxies must be used for votes to waive the financial reporting requirements of §718.111(13) [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Limited proxies must be used for votes to amend the declaration pursuant to §718.110 [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Limited proxies must be used for votes to amend the articles of incorporation or bylaws pursuant to §718.112 [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Limited and general proxies may both be used to establish a quorum [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Except as provided in paragraph (d), a proxy (limited or general) may not be used in the election of board members in a residential condominium [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- General proxies may be used for other matters for which limited proxies are not required, and for nonsubstantive changes to items for which a limited proxy is required and given [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- A voting interest or consent right allocated to a unit owned by the association may not be exercised or considered for any purpose, including a quorum, an election, or otherwise [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- A proxy is effective only for the specific meeting for which originally given and any lawfully adjourned meetings thereof [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- A proxy is not valid longer than 90 days after the date of the first meeting for which it was given [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- Each proxy is revocable at any time at the pleasure of the unit owner executing it [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
- The limited-proxy restrictions do not apply to timeshare condominium associations or nonresidential condominium associations [[sources/web-2018-07-01-694]].
