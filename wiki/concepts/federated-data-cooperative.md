# Federated Data Cooperative

## Summary

A federated data cooperative is a proposed cooperative-of-cooperatives structure in which national or regional data cooperatives aggregate members under their respective regulatory regimes and then share data between cooperatives across jurisdictional boundaries [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key claims

- Federation is proposed as a response to the global patchwork of national privacy regulations that makes truly international data cooperatives impractical and limits most cooperatives to national operations [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- The structure is discussed in the literature (e.g. Tanwar et al. 2021) as a way to preserve regulatory compliance at the national layer while still enabling cross-border data utility [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
