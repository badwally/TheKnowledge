# Trading as a Business

## Summary

Framing of trading as strictly a business operation rather than a sequence of individual P&L events — measured by quarterly and annual results rather than per-trade or per-day outcomes — articulated by TraderEdge as a prerequisite to applying the infinite-game mindset and personal-finance discipline to consistent profitability [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key claims

- Trading is strictly a business: it has days where the trader makes a profit, days of losses, and "you might even have losing months" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- What matters in trading "is what's left over at the end of the quarter and the end of the year" — not the outcome of any single day or trade [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The longer the time horizon a trader looks at, the more likely they are to succeed [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Adopting the trading-as-business mindset is positioned as the precondition for adopting the broader "infinite game" framing — a trader who sees trading as a business and an infinite game is "armed with the correct mindsets to move forward successfully" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
