# Plyometrics for Cyclists

## Summary

Plyometric exercises — squat jumps and box jumps — train explosive lower-body power for cyclists by combining a [[concepts/squat|squat-pattern descent]] with a maximal vertical jump and a controlled landing [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].

## Key claims

- **Squat jump** works glutes, hamstrings, and quads with the goal of boosting explosive power; sit back into a squat with knees behind toes, then immediately jump vertically by extending through the hips and swinging the arms for propulsion, landing softly back into the squat [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
- **Box jump** works quads, calves, and hamstrings to build explosive lower-body power for strength and speed — jump from a crouched position onto the centre of a box, landing softly with the legs absorbing impact [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
- Form is the gating constraint: stop the set when form or speed are compromised, and start with a low box [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
- Progressions: increase reps and reduce rest (squat jump); use a higher box or platform (box jump) [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
- Substitutes: low-impact weighted squat variations or leg press for the squat jump; squat jumps or step-ups for the box jump [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
