# Data Stewardship

## Summary

Data stewardship is the practice of deciding who should have access to data, for what purposes, and to whose benefit — a framing the Open Data Institute uses to position multiple access-and-governance models (including data trusts) as alternative approaches to a shared problem [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].

## Key claims

- Trustworthy data stewardship is presented as a precondition for societies and economies to realise the potential benefits of data [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].
- The ODI characterises data trusts as one approach to data stewardship rather than the only one [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].
- The ODI's Data Access Map catalogues the range of approaches to data stewardship and the inconsistent terminology used to describe them [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].
