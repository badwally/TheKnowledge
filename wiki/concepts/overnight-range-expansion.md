# Overnight Range Expansion

## Summary

A New-York-session intraday playbook that uses the Asia + London overnight range and the daily open as the reference structure for the day, then maps two volatility-injection windows — 8:30 ET (AM session) and 13:00 ET (PM session) — to two canonical reaction patterns: reversal-expansion and retracement-expansion [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].

## Key claims

- The structure is anchored on the daily open and the overnight range built by the Asia and London sessions before NY opens [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- AMtrades names two volatility-injection times for the NY day: 8:30 ET (AM session) and 13:00 ET (PM session) [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Two canonical post-injection patterns are recognised: reversal-expansion and retracement-expansion, both of which assume "no reaction upon news (if any)" — i.e., the rule applies on quiet-calendar days [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Operational rule for inside-day variants of the pattern: when the daily printed an inside day and the overnight failed to expand, avoid AM-session participation and seek PM-session opportunity [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
