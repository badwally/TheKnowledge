# Quebec Syndicate Annual Information Return Obligation

## Summary

Under Québec tax law, a condominium syndicate is recognized as a legal entity and a non-profit organization (NPO); while usually exempt from income tax, it must still file an annual information return with Revenu Québec via Form CO-17.SP [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. The CO-17.SP filing confirms the syndicate's non-profit status, keeps its Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ) registration current, and ensures ongoing compliance [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. This Québec-side obligation runs in parallel with the federal T2 and (where thresholds are exceeded) T1044 obligations administered by the CRA [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key claims

- A Québec condominium syndicate is recognized as a legal entity and a non-profit organization under Québec tax law [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- A syndicate is usually exempt from income tax but must still file an annual information return with Revenu Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- The CO-17.SP filing confirms the syndicate's non-profit status, keeps its REQ registration current, and ensures ongoing compliance [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Filing deadline is within six months following the end of the fiscal year [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- A registered Québec syndicate (NEQ starting with "11") must additionally file a federal T2 with the CRA, attach schedules 100, 125, and 141, and file Form T1044 if CRA thresholds apply [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- The CRA threshold trigger for T1044 (also reflected in CO-17.SP Section 3) requires either: assets > $200,000 during the fiscal year; investment/property income > $10,000; or a previous T1044 obligation [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Board members — typically the president or treasurer — are responsible for ensuring the syndicate's information return is filed within the prescribed deadline [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Failure to file the CO-17.SP can result in: delays in updating the syndicate's REQ record; obstacles during property transactions or resale of condo units; loss of credibility and non-compliance status with tax authorities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- The CO-17.SP filing integrates with the REQ Annual Updating Declaration through box 39 when filed within six months of fiscal year-end [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Late filing carries a penalty of $25 per day (minimum $100, maximum $2,500) along with reminder notices and possible delays in REQ updates or future assessments [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
