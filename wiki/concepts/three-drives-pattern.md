# Three Drives Pattern

## Summary

The Three Drives Pattern, as taught in ICT's 2022 mentorship and summarized by Trader Theory, is a sequence of three continuous higher highs (in a bullish drive) or lower lows (in a bearish drive) used to take out resting liquidity at successive swing extremes, after which a large reversal expansion is expected in the opposite direction — making it the structural twin of the Judas Swing at a multi-leg scale [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].

## Key claims

- Defined by Trader Theory as "3 continuous higher highs or lower lows to take out liquidity and then a large reversal expansion in the opposite direction" [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Treats the sequence of three extremes as engineered liquidity-taking, not trend continuation — each drive is presumed to be hunting stops resting above/below the prior swing extreme [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Resolves with a reversal expansion, aligning the pattern with ICT's broader thesis that price moves to take liquidity and to rebalance inefficiently delivered price [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
