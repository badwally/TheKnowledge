Instead, the specific transcript for this video focuses entirely on the supplement **berberine** as a potential cheap alternative for patients who cannot afford expensive prescription medications [1] [[sources/yt-P_ZctuzCXP4]]. Dr. Nadolsky explains that while early studies showed 500 milligrams of berberine HCL could lower blood sugar as effectively as metformin, he **strongly advises against using berberine in place of FDA-approved drugs** [1] [[sources/yt-P_ZctuzCXP4]]. He emphasizes that **a lack of long-term clinical studies** makes it difficult to know the long-term consequences of taking the supplement, leading him to stop promoting it [1] [[sources/yt-P_ZctuzCXP4]].

If you are looking for Dr. Nadolsky's specific insights on how GLP-1s impact sexual desire and the reward system, those were covered in his related short video, "Do Glp1s like Ozempic or zepbound affect libido? In that video, he confirms that **high doses of GLP-1s can induce anhedonia**—a flat affect caused by a lack of dopamine and motivation—which actively suppresses a patient's libido [2] [[sources/yt-3yY4Kfonw7k]]. His direct clinical strategy for dosing optimization to resolve this reward-dampening side effect is to **actively lower the patient's dose**, allowing their natural drive to return [2] [[sources/yt-3yY4Kfonw7k]].
