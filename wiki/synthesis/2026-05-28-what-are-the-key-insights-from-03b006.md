# What are the key insights from "Smart money thesis scanner." in the context of AI-native business design for solo founders and tiny teams running on AI substrate? The source describes: Use claude to save 4 hours of research

## Synthesis

**Codify repeatable workflows into automated "Skills."** To operate efficiently, solo founders must turn recurring manual tasks into standardized, plug-and-play assets. The source highlights the use of a "Claude Skill," which is simply a file uploaded once that contains repeatable instructions and workflows [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. By packaging complex, multi-step processes into a single file, a tiny team can execute the exact same high-level research consistently on any new target without wasting time writing new prompts from scratch [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]].

**Borrow the research, not the conviction.** The scanner is explicitly designed not to pick stocks for the user, but rather to show who owns them and why [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. The AI performs the tedious labor of filtering out noise, pulling direct quotes from elite investor letters, and mapping out where experts agree or disagree [1, 2]. For an AI-native business, this perfectly mirrors the principle that the AI's role is to compress hours of preparation into minutes, leaving the solo founder to apply their own judgment and conviction to the final decision [1, 3].

**Synthesize elite expertise on demand.** Instead of starting from zero, the scanner allows a solo operator to instantly begin with years of elite investor research as a starting point [3] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. A tiny team running on an AI substrate can essentially deploy an automated analyst to gather and synthesize high-value intelligence, saving hours of manual research labor with just one click [3] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]].
