## Synthesis

**1. The Catastrophic Volatility of Linear Deterministic Funding**
The final approved "Immediately Adequately Funded" spreadsheet starkly illustrates the jagged contribution curves imposed by legacy deterministic math. To keep the fund balance positive without levying a special assessment, the model forces a massive **80.8% fee spike in a single year**, jumping the annual contribution from $320,000 in 2025 to $578,619 in 2026 [1] [[nlm:754250f7-4187-4551-b74b-0e18c4178d13]]. **AI-native probabilistic platforms solve this exact problem** by utilizing Monte Carlo simulations and stochastic financial optimization algorithms to mathematically smooth contribution schedules, balancing statutory compliance with long-term fee stability to prevent such severe financial shocks [2, 3].

**2. The Brittleness of Flat Macroeconomic Assumptions**
The June 2025 spreadsheet calculates its 10- and 25-year cash flow projections using rigidly flat, hardcoded financial inputs: a **2.5% inflation rate and a 2.0% interest rate** [1] [[nlm:754250f7-4187-4551-b74b-0e18c4178d13]]. This static approach represents a major vulnerability, as it completely fails to capture real-world economic volatility such as sudden construction-cost spikes or labor-supply shocks [4] [[sources/pdf-condominium-authority-2024-report-on-resrve]]. Modern AI-native reserve engines abandon these brittle inputs and instead deploy **regime-switching cost-escalation models** that dynamically stress-test total funding requirements against shifting economic climates [5] [[nlm:7ab3c01e-1e20-4a3d-92f4-7b80b4f9a7ef]].

**3. The Danger of Distant Single-Point Estimates**
The spreadsheet forecasts exact, single-point dollar amounts and rigid failure years for massive capital events. Most notably, it maps the $1,290,000 North Podium waterproofing replacement to precisely 2036 and 2037 [6] [[nlm:754250f7-4187-4551-b74b-0e18c4178d13]]. Because traditional studies rely on guessing a single fixed year for failure, boards are mathematically forced to blindly hoard cash for that specific date. An AI-native probabilistic engine replaces these fixed point estimates with **calibrated uncertainty bands (P10/P50/P90)** using Weibull-based reliability modeling [7, 8], allowing boards to continuously quantify the statistical risk of early failure rather than over-capitalizing against a single guessed timeline [3, 9].

**4. The Flaw of Age-Based, Static Replacement Cycles**
The projected expenditures table locks infrastructure maintenance into rigid, predefined chronological cycles, such as a 10-year cycle for balcony condition evaluations or a 5-year cycle for concrete repair allowances [10, 11]. This reflects the inherent flaw of deterministic studies: relying on generic age-based assumptions rather than actual component health. **An AI-native platform transitions from age-based guessing to condition-based forecasting** by automatically ingesting live CMMS work-order history and operational telemetry, continuously adjusting the remaining useful life of components based on ground-truth performance data [5, 12].
