# What are the established best practices for proactive / preventive maintenance of 100+ unit condominium facilities in the United States and Canada, outside the reserve-study mandate? Cover: PM cadences by building system (HVAC, elevators, plumbing risers, fire-life-safety, envelope, balconies, parking deck), regulatory PM drivers by jurisdiction (NYC LL11/FISP, NYC LL87, CA SB 721/326, FL HB 913 operational layer, Ontario Condo Act section 94, BC SPA, Quebec Bill 16 carnet entretien, NJ inspection, Chicago facade, Alberta), industry frameworks (BOMA, IFMA, CAI, CHOA, ACMO), CMMS/EAM stacks for residential (BuildingLink, CondoControl, Yardi, Building Engines), predictive/condition-based PM in residential, insurance loss-control PM, and a minimum-defensible PM program for a 200-unit Florida condo today.

## Synthesis

**Proactive and Preventive Maintenance (PM) Cadences by Building System** [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
Based on comprehensive engineering asset inventories and lifecycle forecasting, large condominium facilities must execute the following PM cadences: [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
*   **HVAC & Mechanical:** Motors, fan blades, and bearings on supply/exhaust fans should be inspected and components cyclically replaced every 2 to 3 years [1, 2]. Make-up air units require belt, pulley, and motor replacements approximately every 8 years, with complete rebuilds every 20 years [3] [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]. [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **Elevators:** Geared traction elevators require overload device testing and full-load performance tests every 2 years [4] [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]. Hoist ropes generally require replacement every 15 years, while controls and door operators face modernization cycles every 20 years [5, 6]. [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **Plumbing Risers & Drainage:** Underground sanitary and storm drainage piping should undergo CCTV video inspections and power-flushing every 5 to 10 years to prevent backups and identify sumping (slope reversal) [7-9]. Major domestic water distribution piping requires comprehensive third-party testing at the 30-year mark to predict imminent failure [10, 11]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **Fire & Life Safety:** Sprinkler piping (both wet and dry systems) mandates flow testing every 5 years, along with high-temperature testing for sprinkler heads [12-14]. Fire alarm battery packs require replacement every 5 years, and exterior fire hydrant caps and threads must be lubricated and repainted every 8 years [15, 16]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2022-01-01-e0b]]
*   **Building Envelope:** Exterior wood and fiber-cement trim requires cleaning and repainting every 6 to 10 years to maintain weather seals [17-20]. Insulated glazing units (IGUs) in windows should be reviewed every 2 years for condensation or misting failures [21] [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]. [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **Balconies:** Exposed vinyl balcony waterproofing membranes typically last 15 years before requiring complete replacement [22, 23]. Aluminum guardrails should be inspected and re-certified concurrently with membrane renewals [24, 25]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **Parking Decks:** Concrete slabs-on-grade require heavy-duty cleaning and the re-application of concrete sealers every 5 years to mitigate oil damage and salt/water intrusion [26] [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]. Gas detection sensors in enclosed garages must be replaced every 5 years to ensure life safety [27] [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]. [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]

**Regulatory PM Drivers by Jurisdiction** [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
Condominium maintenance is increasingly dictated by stringent, localized statutory requirements targeting life safety and structural integrity: [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
*   **Florida HB 913 (SIRS):** Associations must complete a Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) by December 31, 2025. This mandates fully funded reserves for 8 critical elements: roofs, load-bearing structures, fire protection, plumbing, electrical, waterproofing/painting, and windows/doors [28-30].  [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-361]]
*   **NYC LL11/FISP & LL87:** The Façade Inspection & Safety Program (FISP) requires buildings over 6 stories to undergo hands-on exterior examinations by a Qualified Exterior Wall Inspector (QEWI) every 5 years, categorizing conditions as Safe, Unsafe, or SWARMP (Safe with a Repair and Maintenance Program) [31-35]. Local Law 87 requires energy audits and retro-commissioning every 10 years for buildings over 50,000 square feet to optimize energy efficiency [36, 37]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2025-02-27-05e]]
*   **California SB 721 & SB 326:** Targeted at elevated exterior elements (balconies/decks) relying on wood support. SB 721 (multifamily rentals) requires a 15% sample inspection every 6 years [38, 39]. SB 326 (condominiums) requires a 95% statistical confidence inspection every 9 years by a licensed structural/civil engineer or architect [40, 41]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2025-12-08-df6]]
*   **Ontario Condo Act (Section 94):** Mandates Reserve Fund Studies on a 3-year rotating cycle (alternating between comprehensive Class 1, updated Class 2 with site inspection, and Class 3 financial updates) [42, 43]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2024-05-23-ea9]]
*   **British Columbia SPA:** As of July 1, 2024, strata corporations with 5+ units must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; the loophole allowing owners to vote to defer this has been eliminated. Stratas must also contribute a minimum of 10% of their operating budget to the reserve fund [44-46]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2013-11-28-cda]]
*   **Quebec Bill 16:** Requires syndicates to maintain an electronic *carnet d'entretien* (maintenance logbook) that is updated annually and fully reviewed by a professional every 5 years. It also mandates a 25-year contingency fund study to map out major repairs [47-58]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2026-01-13-360]]
*   **New Jersey (S2760/A4384):** Imposes routine structural inspections for buildings with concrete, masonry, or steel load-bearing systems, alongside periodic reserve studies offering a 10-year deficit "catch-up" period [59-61]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2024-01-11-e67]]
*   **Chicago Façade Ordinance:** High-rises over 80 feet must undergo critical hands-on examinations every 4, 8, or 12 years (depending on building category), with ongoing inspection reports filed at the halfway mark [62-66]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-58c]]
*   **Alberta:** Requires a reserve fund study every 5 years outlining a 30-year capital replacement budget, conducted by designated professionals [67-73]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]

**Industry Frameworks**
Standardization of PM protocols is heavily guided by established industry associations: [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
*   **CAI (Community Associations Institute) & FCAR:** Publishes Best Practices Reports focused on reserve studies, aging infrastructure, and transition management [74, 75]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2025-10-13-a40]]
*   **BOMA & IFMA:** BOMA’s BESt standard drives commercial and residential sustainability, requiring proof of ongoing commissioning and preventive maintenance programs [76, 77]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **ACMO (Association of Condominium Managers of Ontario):** Provides the ACMO 2000 certification for management firms and advocates for strict adherence to electrical PM standards like CSA-Z462 and OESC Rule 12-300 [78-80]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-93d]]
*   **CHOA & VISOA:** Offer extensive maintenance guidelines in British Columbia, emphasizing proactive building envelope and HVAC care [81-85]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2026-03-29-4b9]]

**CMMS / EAM Stacks for Residential** [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
Automating these schedules requires robust Computerized Maintenance Management Systems (CMMS) or Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) platforms: [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
*   **BuildingLink:** Features a comprehensive maintenance module that tracks service requests, schedules recurring PM tasks, and logs building equipment/warranties in an asset directory accessible via staff mobile apps [86-89]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **CondoControl:** Streamlines PM workflows by allowing managers to schedule recurring maintenance activities, customize system categories, assign tasks directly to staff, and track everything in a searchable database [90-93]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2024-10-15-6c9]]
*   **Yardi (Facility Manager/Elevate):** An enterprise-grade solution that automates preventive maintenance schedules, handles code compliance checks, manages purchase orders, and empowers technicians with a field app that syncs without a data connection [94-102]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2026-02-18-dff]]
*   **Building Engines (Prism):** A modern operations platform featuring AI-powered insights, advanced HVAC inventory visibility, tenant compliance tracking, and comprehensive work order and inspection management [103-107]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2026-02-20-0f3]]

**Predictive / Condition-Based PM & Insurance Loss-Control** [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
The industry is shifting from reactive/calendar-based PM to data-driven predictive maintenance (PdM) using IoT and digital twins. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **Predictive Maintenance:** By tracking high-resolution BMS data, managers can catch faults before catastrophic failure. Examples include monitoring boiler flue gas temperatures to predict scale formation, tracking elevator door cycle/leveling accuracy to predict brake wear, and using vibration sensors on HVAC motors to catch failing bearings early [108-115]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
*   **Insurance Loss-Control:** Water damage is the most expensive risk for high-rise condos, with average urban claims exceeding $55,000 [116] [[sources/web-2025-08-01-8c2]]. Installing smart leak detection systems (like ProSentry) in vulnerable areas mitigates multi-floor damage, effectively converting a building from "uninsurable" to protected, and slashing skyrocketing insurance premiums [116-123].

**A Minimum-Defensible PM Program for a 200-Unit Florida Condo Today** [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
To protect board members from liability and preserve property values in today's strict Florida regulatory and lending environment, a 200-unit association must implement the following baseline program: [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]]
1.  **SIRS & Fannie Mae Compliance:** Immediately commission a Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) ahead of the Dec 31, 2025 deadline [30] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-361]]. Align the operating budget to fully fund the 8 mandated structural elements. Failure to do so will result in the building being designated "unwarrantable" under new Fannie Mae guidelines, freezing unit sales and collapsing equity [28, 29, 124, 125].
2.  **Digital CMMS Deployment:** Implement a platform like Yardi or BuildingLink to digitize the asset inventory. Automate recurring work orders for mandatory life-safety tasks, such as 5-year fire sprinkler flow tests and 2-year elevator load tests [4, 12, 87-89]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
3.  **IoT Risk Mitigation:** Install connected water leak sensors in common area plumbing risers, mechanical rooms, and HVAC condensate pans. Pair this with combustible gas detectors to actively suppress insurance claims and secure favorable policy renewals [119, 120]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-2025-08-01-8c2]]
4.  **Routine Envelope Monitoring:** Conduct visual inspections of the roof, balcony waterproofing, and exterior paint/stucco annually, documenting all findings in the CMMS to satisfy insurers and prove that the building is not deferring critical maintenance [22, 126, 127]. [[nlm:97fc8200-55c5-433c-853e-b5861dd8ace0]] [[sources/web-1998-01-01-7e4]]
