# What are the key insights from "Smart money thesis scanner." in the context of AI-native business design for solo founders and tiny teams running on AI substrate? The source describes: Use claude to save 4 hours of research

## Synthesis

**Codify repeatable workflows into automated "Skills."** To operate with maximum leverage, solo founders must turn recurring, manual analytical tasks into standardized, plug-and-play assets [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. The source illustrates this through the use of a "Claude Skill"—a single file uploaded once that contains repeatable instructions and complex workflows [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. By packaging these multi-step processes into a single artifact, a tiny team can execute the exact same high-level research consistently on any new target without wasting time writing new prompts from scratch [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]].

**Borrow the research, not the conviction.** The scanner is explicitly designed not to pick stocks for the user, but rather to show who owns them and why [1, 2]. The AI performs the tedious labor of filtering out noise (like index funds or sub-2% positions), pulling direct quotes from elite investor letters, and mapping out where experts agree or disagree [1, 3]. For an AI-native business, this perfectly mirrors the principle that **the AI's role is to compress hours of preparation and synthesis into minutes, leaving the solo founder to apply their own judgment and conviction to the final decision** [1, 2]. 

**Synthesize elite expertise on demand.** Instead of starting from zero, the scanner allows a solo operator to instantly begin with years of elite investor research as a starting point [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. A tiny team running on an AI substrate can essentially **deploy an automated analyst to gather and synthesize high-value intelligence**, saving hours of manual research labor with just one click [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. This fundamentally changes the economics of information gathering, granting a solo founder the analytical firepower of a much larger organization [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]].
