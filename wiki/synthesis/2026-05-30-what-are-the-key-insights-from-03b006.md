# What are the key insights from "Smart money thesis scanner." in the context of AI-native business design for solo founders and tiny teams running on AI substrate? The source describes: Use claude to save 4 hours of research

## Synthesis

**Codify repeatable workflows into automated assets.** To operate with maximum leverage, solo founders must turn recurring, manual analytical tasks into standardized, plug-and-play tools. The source illustrates this through the use of a "Claude Skill," which is simply a file uploaded once that contains repeatable instructions and workflows [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. By packaging these multi-step processes into a single artifact, a tiny team can execute the exact same high-level research consistently on any new target with just one click, without wasting time writing new prompts from scratch [1, 2].

**Borrow the research, not the conviction.** A core design principle for AI-native businesses is understanding exactly what labor to delegate. The scanner is explicitly designed not to pick stocks for the user, but rather to show who owns them and why [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. The AI performs the tedious labor of filtering out noise (like index funds or sub-2% positions), pulling direct quotes from elite investor letters and interviews, and mapping out where experts agree or split [1, 3]. **The AI's role is to compress hours of preparation into minutes, leaving the solo founder to apply their own judgment and conviction to the final decision** [1, 2]. 

**Synthesize elite expertise on demand.** Instead of starting from zero, the scanner allows a solo operator to instantly begin with "years of investor research" as a starting point [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. A tiny team running on an AI substrate can essentially **deploy an automated analyst to gather and synthesize high-value intelligence**, saving 4 hours of manual research labor per task [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. This fundamentally changes the economics of information gathering, granting a solo founder the analytical firepower and preparation level of a much larger organization.
