# What are the key insights from "Smart money thesis scanner." in the context of AI-native business design for solo founders and tiny teams running on AI substrate? The source describes: Use claude to save 4 hours of research

## Synthesis

**Codify repeatable workflows into automated assets.** To operate with maximum leverage, solo founders can turn recurring analytical tasks into standardized tools. The source illustrates this through the use of a "Claude Skill," which is simply a file uploaded once that contains "repeatable workflows and instructions" [1] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. By packaging these multi-step processes into a single artifact, a tiny team can execute the exact same high-level research consistently on any new target with "One click" [1, 2].

**Borrow the research, not the conviction.** A core design principle for AI-native businesses is understanding exactly what labor to delegate to the machine. The scanner is explicitly designed "Not to pick stocks for you," but rather to show "Who owns it" and "Why they bought it" [1, 2]. The AI performs the tedious labor of filtering out noise (like index funds and sub-2% positions), pulling direct quotes from investor letters and decks, and mapping out where experts agree or split [1, 3]. **The AI's role is to act as a "faster starting point," leaving the solo founder to apply their own judgment and conviction to the final decision** [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]].

**Synthesize elite expertise on demand to save massive time.** Instead of starting from zero, the scanner allows a solo operator to instantly begin with "years of investor research" as a baseline [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. A tiny team running on an AI substrate can essentially deploy this system to gather and synthesize high-value intelligence, saving "4 hours of research" in just "Minutes" [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]]. **This fundamentally changes the economics of information gathering, granting a solo founder massive analytical firepower to instantly see what drives stock returns and what questions must be answered before investing** [2] [[nlm:7471d30a-d281-4781-a98b-f2961bd4fe38]].
