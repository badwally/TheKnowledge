## Synthesis

Based on the sources provided, the paper "Does Information About Climate Risk Affect Property Values?" by Miyuki Hino and Marshall Burke (2020) does not contain any information or insights related to cycling or endurance fitness. It is entirely focused on real estate economics, floodplains, and climate risk. 

If you are interested in the actual economic and climate insights from the Hino & Burke paper, they include:
*   **Market Inefficiency Regarding Flood Risk:** The authors investigated whether US housing markets efficiently price in information about flood risk. They found little evidence that the housing market fully prices this information in aggregate [1] [[sources/pdf-a96a6a5b955a]].
*   **The "Flood Zone Discount":** The price penalty for a property being located in a flood hazard area is larger when the buyers are better informed. Specifically, the discount is more pronounced for commercial buyers and in states with strict laws requiring sellers to disclose flood risk information to potential buyers [1-3]. 
*   **Property Overvaluation:** Because the market does not fully account for these climate and flood risks, the authors estimate that floodplain homes in the United States are currently overvalued by a total of $34 billion [1, 4].
