# What are the key insights from "The Marc Andreessen Agent with Claude" in the context of AI-native business design for solo founders and tiny teams running on AI substrate? The source describes: How to scrape his entire public catalog, build the system prompt and ask the questions that actually matter

## Synthesis

**Synthesize an Elite Board of Advisors on Demand:** Most founders lack direct access to top-tier investors to ask high-stakes questions, such as whether to raise capital, rebuild for AI, or if a new big tech competitor is a threat or a validation [1] [[nlm:57b7bb8d-6ed0-4ec2-84fe-de1b56495388]]. By scraping an expert's extensive public catalog—like Marc Andreessen's 20 years of published thinking on startups, markets, and technology—solo founders can effectively **put world-class expertise on "speed dial"** to help guide their tiny teams [1] [[nlm:57b7bb8d-6ed0-4ec2-84fe-de1b56495388]].

**Ground the Agent in Actual Data, Not Generic Personas:** To make this workflow truly valuable, founders must avoid relying on a standard chatbot that is simply guessing what an expert sounds like [2] [[nlm:57b7bb8d-6ed0-4ec2-84fe-de1b56495388]]. Instead, they need to execute proper context engineering by **scraping the expert's writing into a single folder, loading it into a tool like Claude Code, and building a strict system prompt** that grounds every response firmly in the actual texts [2] [[nlm:57b7bb8d-6ed0-4ec2-84fe-de1b56495388]].

**Force Citations to Ensure Authentic Judgment:** To prevent AI hallucinations and ensure the advice is genuinely rooted in the expert's historical thinking, **the agent must be explicitly prompted to cite the specific source file before it provides an answer** [2] [[nlm:57b7bb8d-6ed0-4ec2-84fe-de1b56495388]]. By doing so, founders can interrogate the agent like a real advisor and receive highly informed, verifiable guidance [2] [[nlm:57b7bb8d-6ed0-4ec2-84fe-de1b56495388]].
