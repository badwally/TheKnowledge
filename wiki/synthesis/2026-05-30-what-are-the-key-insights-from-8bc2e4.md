# What are the key insights from "Choosing a depreciation report provider for a strata" in the context of AI-native probabilistic reserve studies for condominium associations under regulated reserve-study mandates? The source describes: Some suggestions for choosing a depreciation report provider.

## Synthesis

**1. The Regulatory "Human-in-the-Loop" Mandate**
British Columbia’s regulations strictly require that depreciation reports be obtained from specific designated professional groups, such as professional engineers, architects, applied science technologists, accredited appraisers, certified reserve planners, or professional quantity surveyors [1-3]. Because of this regulatory boundary, **AI-native platforms must operate as tech-enabled services rather than fully autonomous software**. While the AI engine handles complex probabilistic forecasting, a licensed human analyst from an approved profession must remain in the loop to review the output and provide the required statutory sign-off.

**2. The Provincial Push for Electronic Access and "Living" Documents**
When advising strata boards on evaluating a provider, the provincial guidelines explicitly suggest asking: **"Are materials electronically available for easy access and updating?"** [4] [[nlm:6b6d27e2-1f39-4fc6-99d9-d280802b3d98]]. This question highlights a recognized weakness of the traditional consulting model, which delivers static PDF documents that inherently begin to age upon delivery. Modern AI-native platforms natively fulfill this provincial mandate by replacing static reports with interactive digital software, which dramatically reduces the friction and cost of the mandatory five-year update cycle [4, 5].

**3. The Demand for Dynamic Scenario Adjustments and Customized Cash Flows**
The province encourages strata boards to evaluate providers based on their ability to customize the report, specifically looking for tailored **"phasing of major expenditures"** and **"cash flow models which reflect the strata corporation's circumstances"** [4] [[nlm:6b6d27e2-1f39-4fc6-99d9-d280802b3d98]]. Furthermore, boards are advised to ask: **"What are the post-presentation services? Can requests for changes in replacement scheduling be made following the initial presentation? If so, is there a fee involved?"** [4] [[nlm:6b6d27e2-1f39-4fc6-99d9-d280802b3d98]]. While legacy deterministic consultants often charge extra fees and require slow, manual recalculations for "what-if" scenarios, **AI-native platforms utilize stochastic financial optimization to allow boards to dynamically adjust schedule sliders and instantly visualize customized funding outcomes** without waiting or paying for manual engineering revisions.

**4. Algorithmic Scaling for Varying Building Complexities**
The source points out that the knowledge and expertise required to prepare a report for a simple six-plex is considerably different from what is needed for a complex high-rise condo tower with its own power-generating plant, sections, and an underground parkade [6] [[nlm:6b6d27e2-1f39-4fc6-99d9-d280802b3d98]]. It also differs significantly from bare land stratas responsible for roads and sewage utilities [7] [[nlm:6b6d27e2-1f39-4fc6-99d9-d280802b3d98]]. While traditional engineering firms often need to manually assemble a specialized team of people to bridge this gap [7] [[nlm:6b6d27e2-1f39-4fc6-99d9-d280802b3d98]], **an AI-native probabilistic engine systematically scales to match the precise structural complexity of the building** by utilizing hierarchical data models keyed to specific structure-type cohorts, seamlessly handling everything from simple townhomes to massive residential towers.
