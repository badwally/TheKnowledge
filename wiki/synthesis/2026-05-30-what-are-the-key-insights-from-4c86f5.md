# What are the key insights from "Weekly Dose of Optimism #194" in the context of AI-native business design for solo founders and tiny teams running on AI substrate? The source describes: Reta Phase 3, Colossal Eggs, SpaceX, OpenAI x Erdős, SendCutSend + Extra Doses

## Synthesis

**Attack "Soft Costs" to Maximize Leverage**
SendCutSend reached roughly $200 million in revenue by aggressively attacking the "soft costs" of manufacturing—such as quoting, programming, and billing—to achieve an impressive ~$275K in revenue per employee [1] [[nlm:13823490-7e36-4e22-992c-b39ae78c2109]]. **For a solo founder running on an AI substrate, this serves as the ultimate operating manual: you can build a massive, highly leveraged business simply by using AI and software to entirely eliminate the administrative, coordination, and back-office overhead that typically requires large human teams** [1] [[nlm:13823490-7e36-4e22-992c-b39ae78c2109]].

**AI is Now a Frontier R&D Partner, Not Just a Junior Assistant**
An internal OpenAI reasoning model recently solved an 80-year-old math problem posed by Paul Erdős, generating a novel, *Annals of Mathematics*-worthy proof [2, 3]. Remarkably, the model achieved this breakthrough by thinking for an extended period and burning an estimated maximum of only $1,000 worth of compute tokens [2] [[nlm:13823490-7e36-4e22-992c-b39ae78c2109]]. **For tiny teams, this proves that models have crossed the threshold into being capable of original, ingenious ideas, allowing a solo founder to deploy a relatively tiny compute budget to tackle deep, complex domain problems that previously required entire human research departments** [2, 3].

**Let the Giants Absorb the Capex While You Rent the Intelligence**
SpaceX's S-1 filing revealed staggering infrastructure investments, including $7.7 billion spent on AI capex in a single quarter and a deal where Anthropic will pay $1.25 billion *per month* for Colossus 2 compute [4, 5]. **For a solo operator, this underscores the core economic advantage of the AI-native model: you do not need to compete at the capital-intensive infrastructure layer, but instead get to seamlessly rent the output of billions of dollars of compute to access world-class reasoning while maintaining high margins and remaining nimble** [4, 5].
