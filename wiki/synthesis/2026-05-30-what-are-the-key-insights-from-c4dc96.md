# Ozempic, GLP-1 Drugs &amp; The Truth About Weight Loss | Former FDA Chief Explains

**Channel:** Live Well Be Well with Sarah Ann Macklin  
**Duration:** PT56M22S  
**Views:** 5358  
**Published:** 2025-

## Synthesis

**Dopamine as a "Learning" Molecule**
Former FDA Commissioner Dr. David Kessler emphasizes that obesity and the struggle to maintain weight loss are driven by the brain's neural reward circuits, which become hijacked by highly palatable, ultra-processed foods [1] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [2] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. A fundamental neurobiological insight he shares is that **dopamine acts as a "learning" and "gating" molecule rather than just a "pleasure" chemical** [3] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [4] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. Dopamine locks in our attention to specific cues—such as a time of day or a feeling of pressing unease—and triggers a powerful, cue-induced "wanting" or craving to self-medicate with food [3] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [4] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]].

**Counterbalancing Reward Through Aversive Signaling**
To combat these deeply ingrained addictive circuits, GLP-1 medications target the hindbrain, specifically the nucleus tractus solitarius (NTS) and the area postrema, which is the brain's vomiting center [5] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. Dr. Kessler explains that the drugs work by slowing gastric emptying and utilizing these aversive circuits [5] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. The mechanism of action relies heavily on titrating the dose to **push a patient right to the "edge of nausea"** [6] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. This physiological aversive signal acts as a counterbalance, effectively tamping down the hyperactive reward circuits that drive overeating [7] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]].

**The Dangers of "Microdosing" and Vanity Use**
When discussing dosing trends, Dr. Kessler expresses significant concern regarding individuals **"microdosing" or tampering with dosages simply to lose a few cosmetic pounds** [8] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [9] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. He warns that these are very powerful medications with real risks, including severe gastrointestinal distress, pancreatitis, and potential familial cancers [10] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. Because the medication is so effective at suppressing appetite, patients often consume fewer than 1,000 calories a day [11] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. Dr. Kessler refers to this severe restriction as **"forced anorexia,"** warning that it can lead to severe malnourishment and eating disorders if not clinically supervised [11] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [12] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. He stresses that GLP-1 dosing should be reserved for individuals needing to treat metabolic dysfunction and visceral adiposity, not for cosmetic weight loss [13] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]].

**Managing Relapse and Long-Term Dietary Optimization**
Real-world data shows that patients typically remain on GLP-1 medications for an average of 8 to 9 months in the US [14] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. Crucially, Dr. Kessler points out that the drug merely suppresses the urge to eat while active; **the underlying addictive reward circuits remain completely intact, leading to relapse and weight regain once the medication is stopped** [2] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [15] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. To optimize long-term success, patients must use the temporary window provided by the drug's aversive signaling to actively retrain their relationship with food and establish new behavioral habits [15] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [16] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. Furthermore, because we are facing an epidemic of hyperinsulinemia, Dr. Kessler recommends actively optimizing the diet by **restricting rapidly absorbable carbohydrates** [17] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [18] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]]. Consuming these carbohydrates in an insulin-resistant state adds "fuel to the fire" and increases visceral fat, making foundational nutritional changes mandatory for long-term optimization [17] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]], [18] [[nlm:1f3fde93-f23a-42fb-993d-9df14b706863]].
