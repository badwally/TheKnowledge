# What are the key insights from "AI vs SaaS: Peak Pessimism" in the context of AI-native business design for solo founders and tiny teams running on AI substrate? The source describes: 10 Potential Rebounds

## Synthesis

**Code is Now Table Stakes; True Defensibility is Intangible**
Because AI drastically lowers software development costs for everyone, the ability to rapidly write and ship code is no longer a competitive moat [1] [[nlm:3bf6eb6d-d9d0-4316-b45f-f7d9fd399c63]]. A solo founder running on an AI substrate cannot rely on software alone to protect their business. Instead, survival depends on building the intangible assets that AI cannot conjure out of thin air: **brand, customer trust, regulatory standing, embedded workflows, network density, and proprietary data** [1, 2]. 

**Agents Will Not Replace Workflow Software; They Require It**
The pessimistic narrative that autonomous agents will completely replace traditional SaaS and workflow software is playing out as the exact opposite in the field [2] [[nlm:3bf6eb6d-d9d0-4316-b45f-f7d9fd399c63]]. Agents desperately need structured environments to be useful—**they require workflow orchestration, audit trails, governance, and tool-calling rails** [2] [[nlm:3bf6eb6d-d9d0-4316-b45f-f7d9fd399c63]]. For a tiny team, this means your product should not just be a raw "agent," but rather the secure, auditable software scaffolding that allows those agents to operate safely and effectively within a customer's business [2] [[nlm:3bf6eb6d-d9d0-4316-b45f-f7d9fd399c63]]. 

**Complementary Assets Capture the Profits, Not the Innovators**
Drawing on David Teece's classic 1986 observation, the source highlights that innovators rarely capture the profits of a major technological shift; **the owners of the complementary assets do** [1] [[nlm:3bf6eb6d-d9d0-4316-b45f-f7d9fd399c63]]. For an AI-native micro-business, this is a crucial strategic framing. You do not need to invent the core AI technology or have the smartest model. Your goal is to leverage commoditized AI to build robust complementary assets—like deep customer relationships, specialized regulatory compliance, and sticky, embedded workflows—because that is where the actual wealth accumulation will happen [1] [[nlm:3bf6eb6d-d9d0-4316-b45f-f7d9fd399c63]].
