One thing that helps a lot is asking exactly how the company builds its cost projections. Some reserve studies rely heavily on generic databases or national averages, which can drift pretty far from actual local contractor pricing after a few years.

A good firm will usually look at your specific components in detail and ask for maintenance history, recent repair invoices, and any upcoming projects the board is already aware of. That information can make a big difference in how realistic the projections are.

It is also worth asking how often they recommend updating the study and whether they offer update reviews between full studies. Many communities do a full study every few years but review and adjust numbers annually as real project bids come in.
