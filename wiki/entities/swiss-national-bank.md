# Swiss National Bank

## Summary

Swiss central bank that on September 22, 2022 raised its policy rate by 0.75 percentage points from -0.25% to 0.50%, ending the negative interest rate policy it had implemented in January 2015 — the largest SNB policy rate increase since March 2000 [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].

## Key facts

- On September 22, 2022, the SNB raised its policy rate to 0.50% [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- The hike was 0.75 percentage points, from -0.25% to 0.50% [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- The hike represented the SNB's largest policy rate increase since March 2000 [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- The decision ended the negative interest rate policy the SNB had implemented in January 2015 [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- The SNB stated alongside the decision that it remains willing to intervene in the foreign exchange market [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
