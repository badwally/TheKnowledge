# Solume

## Summary

Solume is a U.S.-only Atlanta, Georgia-headquartered AI-powered HOA and condo association management platform whose November 8, 2025 third-party editorial launch coverage (Endowment Lock / King Newswire syndication) frames it as one of the only HOA management platforms to incorporate "dynamic reserve study management" alongside ARC workflows, dues collection, budgeting, vendor payments, financial reconciliation, and communications tools, with an LLM-based AI assistant scoped to interpreting governing documents, policy language, and procedural standards rather than predictive condition modeling [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]]. Solume's January 2025 vendor-curated multi-state reserve-study state-requirements guide covers 13 US states (CA, CO, DE, FL, HI, MD, NV, NJ, OR, TN, UT, VA, WA) and is one of two named multi-state vendor-curated regulatory-interpretation cross-references in the condo-capital-infra wiki (alongside PropFusion's April 2026 FL SIRS guide), framing CAI-baseline deterministic methodology as the operative interpretive default in vendor education content across the US Year-2 expansion footprint [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]]. Solume's January 23, 2025 Florida-specific marketing landing page (getsolume.com/florida-reserve-study) operationalizes active FL SIRS / HB 913 GTM with a deterministic-to-dashboard methodology framing (no probabilistic / Monte Carlo / Bayesian layer) and a marketing-tier statutory-compliance claim [[sources/web-2025-01-23-a1d]]. Solume is identified in the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis vendor-watch frame as a named CI competitor in the U.S. multi-state HOA management software market, with zero Canadian-jurisdiction coverage — establishing a U.S.-only competitive perimeter relative to the ADR-0004 Canada-first GTM sequence [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]] [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].

- Public contact phone: +1-888-868-9886 [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Product website: https://www.community.solume.com/ [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Marketing-tier company website (prior): getsolume.com, with jurisdiction-specific landing pages including getsolume.com/florida-reserve-study [[sources/web-2025-01-23-a1d]].
- November 8, 2025 launch coverage distributed via King Newswire press-release distribution; the press release explicitly disclaims editorial responsibility on the part of the publishing site (Endowment Lock), framing the content as vendor-supplied promotional material with no third-party verification of claims [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].

### Product positioning (per Nov 8 2025 launch coverage)

- Self-positioned as an AI-powered HOA and condo association management platform designed to simplify board operations, reduce administrative strain, and strengthen financial transparency across communities [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Self-positioned as "one of the only HOA management platforms to incorporate dynamic reserve study management" — a marketing-tier competitive-differentiation claim distinguishing Solume from the broader HOA management software field [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Self-positioned as built specifically for homeowner and condo association management rather than adapted from business accounting software, with the framing argument that HOA governance has unique requirements involving resident relations, compliance obligations, and shared property maintenance [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Self-positioned to enable board members to take a more active and confident role in guiding their communities, reducing reliance on third-party management companies for oversight — an explicit self-management posture [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Available for community associations of all sizes; the company allows boards to test the system before committing [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].

### Feature inventory (per Nov 8 2025 launch coverage)

- Reserve study management: "dynamic" / "updates in real time based on financial data, vendor expenses, and projected maintenance timelines" — marketing language; no methodology specification (no disclosure of probabilistic modeling, calibration, validation, or component-level treatment) [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Architectural review (ARC) request processing: homeowners submit requests through the platform; board members review materials and reference governing documents in one view; structured workflow positioned to promote fair decisions and reduce inconsistent approvals [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Financial management tools: dues collection, expense tracking, vendor payments, reporting; real-time monitoring of operating funds and reserve accounts; financial statements designed to be shared in clear language without requiring specialized accounting expertise [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Communication tools: integrated directly into the system, enabling boards to send updates, reminders, notices, and schedules without relying on third-party platforms [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Budget tools: support for accurate budgeting integrated with reserve study planning [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- Compliance workflows: structured workflows for architectural and compliance requests [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].

### AI scope (per Nov 8 2025 launch coverage)

- Built-in AI assistant designed to help board members interpret governing documents, policy language, and procedural standards [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- AI scope framing positions the assistant as reducing uncertainty in decision-making for volunteer board members who step into their roles without prior experience in governance or community regulations [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- AI scope is explicitly LLM-based document-Q&A / policy interpretation — there is no claim of predictive condition modeling, deterioration forecasting, probabilistic deterioration modeling, or component-level engineering inference [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- The AI-scope confirmation — governing-document / policy-language interpretation only, no predictive condition modeling — is the load-bearing competitive-differentiation data point against the condo-capital-infra probabilistic reserve engine: Solume's AI envelope does not extend into the six-probabilistic-components scope (roof, building envelope, plumbing risers, HVAC central plant, elevators, parking deck / podium structure) and does not engage probabilistic deterioration modeling or Monte Carlo aggregation [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].

### Multi-state vendor-curated regulatory-interpretation guide (January 2025)

- January 2025 publication of a vendor-curated multi-state HOA/condo reserve-study state-requirements guide covering 13 US states: CA, CO, DE, FL, HI, MD, NV, NJ, OR, TN, UT, VA, WA [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]].
- The guide describes reserve-study practice in deterministic point-estimate terms throughout, with no engagement with probabilistic deterioration modeling, Monte Carlo aggregation, or distribution-based forecasting — confirming that CAI's deterministic methodology baseline is operating as the interpretive default across vendor-curated multi-state regulatory-interpretation content well beyond the California DRE and Florida SIRS state-government adoption surfaces [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]].
- The guide independently summarizes California Civil Code § 5550 as requiring a triennial reserve study with annual review, framing California as having "one of the most stringent reserve study requirements for HOAs" — a vendor-curated regulatory-interpretation cross-reference parallel to PropFusion's vendor-curated FL SIRS guide [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]].
- The guide documents the SB 4-D (May 2022) and SB 154 (2023) legislative ancestry of the current Florida SIRS regime, establishing Solume as a second multi-state vendor-curated regulatory-interpretation cross-reference alongside PropFusion's April 2026 guide [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]].
- The guide frames the SB 4-D / SB 154 regime as imposing budgetary adjustment requirements on associations — incorporating SIRS findings and funding recommendations into annual budgets, with sufficient reserves allocated to maintain and replace critical structural components, which may necessitate increased assessments or dues to meet mandatory reserve funding levels [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]].
- The guide documents the 14-year minimum financial-records retention requirement applicable to SIRS records under the SB 4-D / SB 154 regime [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]].

### Florida-specific marketing landing page (January 23, 2025)

- January 23, 2025 Florida-specific marketing landing page at getsolume.com/florida-reserve-study confirms active FL SIRS / HB 913 GTM [[sources/web-2025-01-23-a1d]].
- Product positioning frames Solume as converting "outdated PDF reserve studies into interactive financial dashboards that adapt to Florida's unique maintenance needs" — a deterministic-to-dashboard methodology framing with no probabilistic / Monte Carlo / Bayesian layer [[sources/web-2025-01-23-a1d]].
- Marketing-tier statutory-compliance claim: "Meet all Florida Statute requirements for HOA and condo reserve funding" — without specific reference to HB 913, the 8-category SIRS list, the $25K threshold, or DBPR reporting [[sources/web-2025-01-23-a1d]].

### Competitive-perimeter posture

- U.S.-only multi-state vendor whose state-by-state coverage spans 13 jurisdictions with zero Canadian content (no BC SPA, no ON Condo Act, no NS Act, no depreciation-report terminology) — confirming a U.S.-only competitive perimeter parallel to PropFusion's U.S.-only footprint, relative to the condo-capital-infra Canada-first GTM sequence under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-01-07-b6e]].
- Operationalizes multi-state coverage through jurisdiction-specific marketing landing pages — establishing a per-jurisdiction product-marketing decomposition rather than a single national SKU [[sources/web-2025-01-23-a1d]].
- The Nov 8 2025 launch coverage's positioning of Solume as a self-management platform ("boards can take a more active and confident role in guiding their communities" / "instead of relying on manual processes or relying heavily on management companies for oversight") frames the addressable buyer as the self-managed HOA / condo board rather than the property management company — a distinct GTM channel from FirstService Residential, Associa, and the broader CAM-firm-mediated channel that CINC Systems serves [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].

### Implications for condo-capital-infra positioning

- The Nov 8 2025 launch coverage's explicit AI-scope confirmation — LLM document-Q&A on governing documents and policy language, NOT predictive condition modeling — is load-bearing competitive-intelligence: Solume's AI envelope does not threaten the probabilistic deterioration / six-component scope of the condo-capital-infra engine, but does occupy the governance-and-operations envelope and could foreclose the operational-PM ancillary surface if not deliberately ceded [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
- The "dynamic reserve study management that updates in real time" marketing framing, absent any methodology specification, is consistent with the deterministic-to-dashboard methodology framing documented from the January 2025 FL landing page — real-time data refresh of point-estimate inputs rather than probabilistic recomputation [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]] [[sources/web-2025-01-23-a1d]].
- Solume's self-managed-HOA buyer framing surfaces a distinct addressable-market segment from the property-management-firm-mediated channel that CINC Systems serves, framing a different competitive vector than the primary CINC-as-acquirer thesis [[sources/web-2025-11-08-926]].
