# Aspire Community Management

## Summary

Aspire Community Management is a community association management (CAM) operator identified as a Vantaca customer on the Vantaca features page, with Kathy Bollo named in the testimonial copy and attributed as the source of the transparency-and-board-trust quotation about Vantaca [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key facts

- Identified as a CAM operator and Vantaca customer in published Vantaca marketing copy [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Kathy Bollo is named in the Vantaca testimonial as the spokesperson representing Aspire Community Management [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The operator's published testimonial attributes "much greater transparency than we ever did before" to the Vantaca platform, with board members able to check in anytime and see that the management company is actively doing its job [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The operator's testimonial also attributes Vantaca with automatic service-request follow-up that visibly signals to board members that requests are not being ignored [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The Vantaca features page does not specify Aspire Community Management's jurisdiction, association portfolio size, headcount, or service-line scope [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
