# SwingAlgo+

## Summary

Trading-indicator vendor that sells the WEEKLY BIAS indicator for TradingView, gated on a verified PayHip purchase receipt and provisioned by emailing weeklybias@swingalgo.info with the buyer's full name, PayHip receipt number, and TradingView username [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].

## Key facts

- Sells the WEEKLY BIAS indicator on TradingView, fulfilling orders through the PayHip e-commerce platform [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- Access-request email address is weeklybias@swingalgo.info, with the required subject line "WEEKLY BIAS Access Request" [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- The required email body contains three fields: the buyer's full name, PayHip receipt number, and TradingView username [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- Quotes a 24–48 hour processing window for access requests, followed by a confirmation email when WEEKLY BIAS access is granted on TradingView [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- Provides a copy/paste email template addressed to "[SWINGALGO+ WEEKLY BIAS]" with placeholder fields for the buyer's name, PayHip receipt number, and TradingView username [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- Troubleshooting guidance directs missing-receipt buyers to check their email spam/junk folder and to double-check that all submitted details are correct before sending [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- Publishes a standalone Trading Disclaimer that frames all information — including market analyses, educational materials, and trading signals — as informational and educational only, not financial advice [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- The disclaimer asserts no guarantee of accuracy, completeness, suitability, or profit; declines liability for any direct, indirect, incidental, consequential, or special damages; and reserves the right to change the disclaimer without notice [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- The disclaimer is binding by use: "BY APPLYING SWINGALGO+ WEEKLY BIAS TO YOUR CHART - YOU AGREE TO THIS TRADING DISCLAIMER" [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
