# Peter Yang

## Summary

Peter Yang is the former Chief Technology Officer of CINC Systems, transitioning out of the CTO role in October 2025 to take a sabbatical to travel the world while remaining as a technology advisor to incoming CTO Duri Chitayat and the company [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. CEO Ryan Davis publicly characterized Yang as having "played a pivotal role in helping us firmly establish our company as the industry leader," framing the transition as a clean CTO-succession-with-advisor-retention pattern under Hg sponsorship rather than a destabilizing departure [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Yang's transition is the load-bearing CTO-departure signal in the Hg-CINC governance narrative within the 22-month-post-investment window, with the sabbatical-with-advisor-retention framing operating as the public posture-statement that the executive turnover is positioned as orderly rather than disruptive [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

## Key facts

### Role and transition

- Former Chief Technology Officer of CINC Systems [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Departed the CTO role in October 2025 as part of the second-wave executive build-out announced October 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Succeeded by Duri Chitayat (formerly CTO of Safeguard Global) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Remains as a technology advisor to Chitayat and the company post-transition [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Reason for transition: elected to take a sabbatical and travel the world [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Public framing of the transition

- CEO Ryan Davis's framing: "Peter's played a pivotal role in helping us firmly establish our company as the industry leader. I'll be forever grateful to Peter for his leadership, and I'm thankful he's playing the key role of transitioning Duri into this role" [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Davis closed the press release with explicit leadership-stability framing characterizing CINC's executive turnover (including the Yang → Chitayat transition) as part of the "stability we've had amongst our leadership team" that is "unique in this industry and speaks to our leadership position" — positioning the transition as orderly and advisor-retained rather than destabilizing [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- The Yang → Chitayat CTO succession with sabbatical-and-advisor-retention is the load-bearing CTO-departure signal in the Hg-CINC governance narrative within the 22-month-post-investment window, surfacing CINC's preference for orderly, retention-anchored executive transitions consistent with PE-portfolio-company governance norms [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- The advisor-retention structure of Yang's transition surfaces a potential template for founder/executive retention in any AI-native reserve-study tuck-under acquisition by CINC, where retained-advisor relationships preserve domain knowledge during integration without committing the acquired executive to full-time post-acquisition operating roles [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
