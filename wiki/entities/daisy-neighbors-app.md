# Daisy Neighbors App

## Summary

The Daisy Neighbors app is the resident-facing component of the Daisy property-management platform, used by condo and co-op residents in Daisy-managed buildings to pay charges, track packages, and connect with their community [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. The Neighbors app is one of multiple role-specific connected apps in the Daisy product family — others serve boards, supers, vendors, accountants, and brokers — all running on the same underlying Daisy system [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].

## Key facts

- Resident-facing component of the Daisy platform [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Allows residents to pay charges [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Allows residents to track packages [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Allows residents to connect with their building community [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- One of multiple role-specific connected apps in the Daisy product family; boards, supers, vendors, accountants, and brokers each operate in their own connected apps on the same underlying Daisy system [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Marketed by Daisy as part of an Uber-analogue user experience: residents see what's happening in real time while operational complexity is handled behind the scenes [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
