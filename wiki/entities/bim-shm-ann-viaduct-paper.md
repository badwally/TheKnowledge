# Infrastructure management via BIM model: integration of structural health monitoring and ANN-based damage assessment

## Summary

A 2025 *Journal of Information Technology in Construction* (ITcon) paper by José Guilherme Porto Oliveira and Elisa Dominguez Sotelino proposing a novel framework that integrates Structural Health Monitoring (SHM), Building Information Modelling (BIM), and Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) for comprehensive viaduct management, demonstrated on the Rio Claro Viaduct.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by José Guilherme Porto Oliveira and Elisa Dominguez Sotelino, both of the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at PUC-Rio [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The paper was submitted in May 2025, revised in September 2025, and published in October 2025 in *Journal of Information Technology in Construction* (ITcon), Vol. 30, pp. 1637-1658 (DOI 10.36680/j.itcon.2025.067) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The motivating premise is that bridges and viaducts are critical infrastructure assets whose maintenance remains a challenge due to aging, increased traffic loads, and insufficient documentation [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The paper observes that while SHM and BIM have independently advanced viaduct management, their integration is still underexplored [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The proposed framework integrates SHM, BIM, and ANNs for comprehensive viaduct management [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Field tests including ambient vibration analysis were conducted to capture the Rio Claro Viaduct's dynamic behaviour [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The captured dynamic behaviour data was used to calibrate a finite element model of the viaduct [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Simulated damage scenarios were created from the calibrated FE model to train ANNs that use modal curvature damage indices for damage detection and severity assessment [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The integration of these components into an enriched BIM model centralizes data for efficient visualization and decision-making [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The framework demonstrated high accuracy, with ANNs achieving an average precision of 85% in damage classification and an R² of 0.96 in severity prediction [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Validation using a decade-separated dataset confirmed the framework's robustness, showing negligible structural deterioration over time on the Rio Claro Viaduct [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The paper states an intent to provide an intuitive user interface so that asset managers can make data-driven decisions, overcoming the limitations of traditional visual inspections [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The paper is editor by Kumar B and the full text is distributed as a 2.052 MB PDF [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Keywords listed by the authors are: structural health monitoring, building information modelling, artificial neural networks, damage assessment, infrastructure maintenance [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
