# BOMA Canada (Building Owners and Managers Association of Canada)

## Summary

BOMA Canada is the publisher of the BOMA BEST sustainable-buildings certification program and authored the BOMA BEST 4.0 Questionnaire for Sustainable Buildings — Multi-Unit Residential Technical Field Guide released in June 2023 [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]]. It also publishes the BOMA BEST Preventive Maintenance Best Practices template that serves as a reference resource for the BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E6.0 baseline practice [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key facts

- Released the BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB Technical Field Guide in June 2023 with the tagline "Creating better spaces to live, work and play" [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Hosts and distributes the BOMA BEST Preventive Maintenance Best Practices template at bomacanada.ca, referenced by the BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E6.0 question as a resource for compliance [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Operates the BOMA BEST certification program across multiple asset classes including Office, Healthcare, Enclosed Shopping Centre, Universal, Light Industrial, Open-Air Retail, and Multi-Unit Residential buildings [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
