# ClickPay

## Summary

ClickPay is a payments vendor named by CINC Systems as the sole payments-category software partner in CINC's published software partner ecosystem as of January 2026 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]]. CINC's open API enables ClickPay to securely connect to the CINC platform for data flow across payments workflows in community association management operations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].

## Key facts

- Named by CINC Systems as the sole named payments-category integration partner in CINC's published software partner list as of January 2026 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Connects to CINC via CINC's open API, which allows approved partners to securely connect their solutions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Integration positioned by CINC as ensuring seamless data flow across key property management functions including payments [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
