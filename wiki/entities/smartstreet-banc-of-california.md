# SmartStreet (powered by Banc of California)

## Summary

SmartStreet, branded by AppFolio as "SmartStreet powered by Banc of California," is named as one of five HOA-segment banking and check-processing partners that AppFolio integrates with to provide automated lockbox, bank statements, AR check images, auto reconciliation, and Positive Pay to community-association customers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]]. The SmartStreet brand sits atop Banc of California's underlying banking charter and operations.

## Key facts

- Branded by AppFolio as "SmartStreet powered by Banc of California," indicating that SmartStreet is the customer-facing HOA-banking product and Banc of California is the underlying chartered bank [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- Listed by AppFolio as an HOA-banking integration partner in the HOA Financials product narrative [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- The AppFolio integration is positioned as providing automated lockbox, bank statements, Accounts Receivable (AR) check images, auto reconciliation, and Positive Pay [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- Appears both in AppFolio's narrative partner enumeration and in the separate partner-icon list (under the short brand "SmartStreet"), suggesting the integration is currently active in AppFolio's UI [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
