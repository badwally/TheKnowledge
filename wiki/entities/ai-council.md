# AI Council (UK)

## Summary

The AI Council is a UK government advisory body on artificial intelligence; it co-published the 2021 "Exploring legal mechanisms for data stewardship" report with the Ada Lovelace Institute, which set out the three-mechanism legal toolbox (data trusts, data cooperatives, corporate/contractual) for responsible data stewardship [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].

## Key facts

- The AI Council is named as joint publisher of the 2021 legal-mechanisms report alongside the Ada Lovelace Institute [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- Its participation in the report situates the three-mechanism framework as advice carrying UK-government-adjacent institutional weight, rather than purely academic commentary [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
