# Northwestern University

## Summary

Private research university whose Department of Political Science is the institutional home of Daniel J. Galvin and Chloe N. Thurston, co-authors of the June 2018 IPR working paper "The Limits of Policy Feedback as a Party-Building Tool" [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]]; also the institutional home of Craig Garthwaite (Kellogg School of Management), Matthew J. Notowidigdo (Department of Economics), and Nicole F. Ozminkowski, three of the four co-authors of the NBER Working Paper No. 27734 "Who Profits From Amateurism? Rent-Sharing in Modern College Sports" (August 2020), which characterizes the economic rents from NCAA amateurism rules and estimates rent-sharing elasticities using comprehensive FBS athletic department data [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the institutional affiliation for Daniel J. Galvin (Associate Professor of Political Science) and Chloe N. Thurston (Assistant Professor of Political Science) on the paper [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]].
- Houses the Institute for Policy Research (IPR), which Galvin is identified as a Fellow of and Thurston as an Associate of, and which publishes the Working Paper Series in which WP-18-16 appears [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]].
- Craig Garthwaite is affiliated with the Kellogg School of Management, 2211 Campus Drive, Evanston, IL 60208; listed as an NBER affiliate [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Matthew J. Notowidigdo is affiliated with the Department of Economics, 2001 Sheridan Road, Evanston, IL 60208-2600; listed as an NBER affiliate [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Nicole F. Ozminkowski is listed with a Northwestern University affiliation on NBER Working Paper No. 27734 [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- The college-sports rent-sharing paper combines comprehensive revenue and expenses data for FBS schools (2006–2019) with a newly assembled dataset of complete rosters for every sport matched to neighborhood socioeconomic characteristics [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
