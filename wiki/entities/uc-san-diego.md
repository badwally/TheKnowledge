# University of California, San Diego

## Summary

Public research university in San Diego, California, whose Department of Computer Science and Engineering is the institutional home of all nine authors of the 2026 paper "Pancake: Hierarchical Memory System for Multi-Agent LLM Serving" [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].

## Key facts

- All nine authors of the Pancake paper — Zhengding Hu, Zaifeng Pan, Prabhleen Kaur, Vibha Murthy, Zhongkai Yu, Yue Guan, Zhen Wang, Steven Swanson, and Yufei Ding — are affiliated with Computer Science and Engineering at UC San Diego [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].
