# Vancouver Island Strata Owners Association (VISOA)

## Summary

The Vancouver Island Strata Owners Association (VISOA) is a British Columbia strata-owner advocacy and education organization that publishes practitioner-facing resources for strata corporations, councils, and individual strata owners across Vancouver Island and the broader BC strata sector [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]]. VISOA operates a public resources library (visoa.bc.ca/resources) plus a member-restricted resource set (including sample financial statements, budget worksheets, and AGM preparation materials), and publishes the VISOA *Bulletin* magazine that carries longer-form practitioner articles such as the November 2015 Jonathan Wright piece on preserving strata tax-exempt status [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].

## Key facts

- BC-based strata-owner advocacy and education organization; operates the visoa.bc.ca domain [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Operates a tiered resource library: publicly accessible practitioner guides (e.g., the October 2025 "Filing income tax returns" guide) plus member-only and corporate-member-only resources [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Publishes the VISOA *Bulletin* magazine; the November 2015 issue carried the load-bearing Jonathan Wright article "Income of Strata Corporations: How Not To Lose Tax-Exempt Status," cited by VISOA's own tax-filing guide as the authoritative practitioner treatment for stratas with non-incidental commercial revenue exposure [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Publishes guidance restating federal CRA doctrine — including IT-304R2's §149(1)(l) NPO classification framework and §150 T2 filing obligation — operationalized for BC strata corporations with jurisdiction-specific application detail (T1044 thresholds, T2 short-form eligibility, NPO Code 10, RC-1 E (23) Business Number registration channel) [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Publishes guidance restating BC SPA §35 records-retention and §36 inspection-right doctrine, including the Strata Property Regulation §4.1(3) six-year minimum retention period for tax returns [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Member-only resources include sample financial statements, an Excel worksheet for creating a budget and calculating strata fees by unit entitlement, an Excel worksheet for calculating a special levy by unit entitlement, AGM preparation slides, and an AGM preparation webinar Q&A; sample financial statements and the worksheets are flagged as corporate-member-only [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Cross-references CRA primary sources (T2 Corporation Income Tax Return guide; Income Tax Guide to the T1044) and the BC Strata Housing program's "Preparing a financial statement" and "Financial best practices for stratas" materials as the authoritative upstream references for the guidance it restates [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
