# Uncertainty Updating in the Description of Coupled Heat and Moisture Transport in Heterogeneous Materials

## Summary

A 2011 arXiv preprint (subsequently published in *Applied Mathematics and Computation*, 2013) by Anna Kucerova and Jan Sykora that applies Bayesian inference to combine different sources of information — loading conditions, material parameters, and their credibility — for a more accurate estimation of coupled heat and moisture fields in heterogeneous materials, with the goal of supporting structural durability assessment.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Anna Kucerova and Jan Sykora and was posted as arXiv 1102.5239 on 25 February 2011 [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The paper appeared in *Applied Mathematics and Computation*, volume 219, issue 13, pages 7252-7261, 2013 (DOI 10.1016/j.amc.2011.02.078) [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The arXiv preprint is classified under math.NA (primary) with cross-listing in physics.data-an [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The motivating problem is the assessment of structural durability, which requires analysis of heat and moisture transport [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The paper argues that reliable estimation of heat and moisture distribution in a structure requires including all available information about loading conditions and material parameters, accompanied by a corresponding evaluation of its credibility [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The authors apply Bayesian inference to combine different sources of information so as to provide a more accurate estimation of heat and moisture fields [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The procedure is demonstrated on a probabilistic description of heterogeneous material in which the uncertainties consist of particular values of individual material characteristics and their spatial fluctuations [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- Heat and moisture transfer is modelled in a coupled setting [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
