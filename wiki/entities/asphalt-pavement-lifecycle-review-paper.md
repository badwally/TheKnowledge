# Life-cycle performance, design, maintenance, optimization, and decision-making of asphalt pavement under uncertainty: a review

## Summary

A 2026 review paper by Jiyu Xin, Dan M Frangopol, Mitsuyoshi Akiyama, Lianzhen Zhang, and Jianzhong Pei surveying the state-of-the-art in asphalt pavement life-cycle performance, identifying sources of aleatory and epistemic uncertainty across the pavement life-cycle, and presenting probabilistic optimization methodologies that span pavement life-cycle phases.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Jiyu Xin, Dan M Frangopol, Mitsuyoshi Akiyama, Lianzhen Zhang, and Jianzhong Pei [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper was published on 1 February 2026 with DOI 10.1080/15732479.2025.2552239 [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper presents a comprehensive review of state-of-the-art asphalt pavement research covering modeling, analysis, prediction, design, maintenance, monitoring, optimization, and management associated with asphalt pavement life-cycle performance [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The motivating observation is that the typical lifespan of asphalt pavements remains relatively short — ranging from 15 to 25 years — compared to other types of infrastructure such as bridges, which can last up to 100 years [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper argues that rational decisions regarding asphalt pavement systems should be made using an integrated probabilistic life-cycle multi-objective optimization methodology considering factors such as safety level, total expected cost, and environmental effects [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The review identifies the sources of two types of uncertainties — aleatory and epistemic — throughout the pavement life-cycle [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper highlights key accomplishments in the areas of distress/condition indicators, reliability, risk, life-cycle cost, sustainability, and resilience of asphalt pavement [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper presents probabilistic optimization methodologies that account for different pavement life-cycle phases [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper addresses current and emerging trends and innovations in technology for advancing the life-cycle management of asphalt pavement [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The authors acknowledge the publicly accessible database provided by the Long-Term Pavement Performance (LTPP) program under the leadership of the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
