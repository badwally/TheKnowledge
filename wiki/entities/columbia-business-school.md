# Columbia University Business School

## Summary

Graduate business program at Columbia University; institutional home of Joel Greenblatt's Special Situation Investing class, which ran in the form captured by the source from 2002 through 2006 and is the basis of the class-notes PDF under review [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].

## Key facts

- Hosts the Special Situation Investing classes that Joel Greenblatt taught from 2002 through 2006 [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
- The first class of the 2005 series was held September 7, 2005 [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
- The class notes were taken by an investor who audited Greenblatt's class across all four years; per the auditor's introduction, "different years may have an overlap of material and concepts covered by the prior year's notes but the repetition and supplemental material may improve retention" [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
