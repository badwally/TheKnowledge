# Qing-Hong Cao

## Summary

Physics researcher at Peking University, jointly affiliated with the School of Physics and the Center for High Energy Physics; co-author of the 2026 Letter "Detailed balance in large language model-driven agents" [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the School of Physics, Peking University, Beijing 100871, China [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
- Also affiliated with the Center for High Energy Physics, Peking University, Beijing 100871, China [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
- Co-author of "Detailed balance in large language model-driven agents" (2026), which proposes a method based on the least action principle to estimate a potential function V_T describing LLM-driven agent dynamics [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
