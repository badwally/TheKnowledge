# The Meritus Group Management Inc.

## Summary

The Meritus Group Management Inc. is an Ontario condominium management firm whose president Dean McCabe commented on the limited-licensee pipeline as a partial mitigation for the manager shortfall [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key facts

- Dean McCabe is president of The Meritus Group Management Inc. [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- McCabe stated that, with a limited number of existing general licensees and recent delays in providing education to management candidates due to changes in educational requirements, many management firms are continuously searching for qualified managers or trying to develop new talent from the pool of limited licensees, and boards must be willing to hire limited licensees who are gaining the required experience while they work [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
