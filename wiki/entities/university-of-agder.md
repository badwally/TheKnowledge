# University of Agder

## Summary

Norwegian university whose School of Business and Law in Kristiansand is the institutional home of finance researcher Valeriy Zakamulin (a.k.a. Valeri Zakamouline), author of the 2016 paper "Market Timing with Moving Averages: Anatomy and Performance of Trading Rules" [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].

## Key facts

- Listed institutional address on the paper: School of Business and Law, University of Agder, Service Box 422, 4604 Kristiansand, Norway [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
- Author contact at the School of Business and Law uses the uia.no domain (Valeri.Zakamouline@uia.no) and phone (+47) 38 14 10 39 [[sources/pdf-ec9fdef2193c]].
