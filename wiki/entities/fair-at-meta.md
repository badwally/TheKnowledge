# FAIR at Meta

## Summary

Meta's Fundamental AI Research division; institutional home of co-authors of the 2026 HyperAgents paper, including its corresponding author Tatiana Shavrina, and the host of the released codebase [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the institutional affiliation of Jakob Foerster and Tatiana Shavrina on the HyperAgents paper [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Several authors marked their HyperAgents contributions as "work done during internship at Meta" or "work done at Meta" [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- The HyperAgents code is released at github.com/facebookresearch/Hyperagents under the FAIR organization [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
