# MDS

## Summary

MDS is a property-management accounting software vendor named as one of 17 accounting integration partners in BuildingLink's resident-engagement platform [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. The MDS–BuildingLink integration runs on a 24-hour batch sync that propagates resident information from MDS into BuildingLink, and is one of two BuildingLink accounting integrations — alongside Jenark — that carries a distinct integration fee [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].

## Key facts

- Named as an accounting integration partner in BuildingLink's accounting-integration catalog [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Syncs with BuildingLink every 24 hours [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Updates to resident information in MDS appear in BuildingLink the next day [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- The integration eliminates the need for staff to add or remove residents on both platforms manually [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- MDS charges an integration fee (one of two named BuildingLink accounting partners doing so, the other being Jenark) [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
