# Chris Trower

## Summary

Chris Trower is the founder of Electric Law and a board member of CINC Systems, joining the CINC board in conjunction with Spectrum Equity's June 10, 2020 first institutional investment in CINC [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]]. Trower is framed in the June 2020 CINC announcement as having more than 40 years of experience as counsel to companies and as an operator and board member [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]]. Trower completes the three-person new-board cohort (alongside Mike Farrell of Spectrum Equity and Robert Petrie of Origami Risk) appointed at the June 2020 deal close [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].

## Key facts

- Founder of Electric Law as of the June 10, 2020 CINC Systems announcement [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Joined CINC Systems' board of directors in conjunction with Spectrum Equity's June 10, 2020 growth investment in CINC [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Framed in the June 2020 CINC announcement as having "more than 40 years of experience as counsel to companies and as an operator and board member" [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Joined the CINC board alongside Mike Farrell (Spectrum MD) and Robert Petrie (CEO of Origami Risk — a fellow Spectrum portfolio company) at the June 2020 deal close as part of a three-person new-board cohort — surfacing Trower as the non-Spectrum-affiliated independent board addition in the cohort [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
