# Movable Ink

## Summary

Movable Ink is an AI-powered decisioning platform specializing in email and mobile personalization, identified by Hightouch as a peer AI Decisioning vendor alongside Aampe and OfferFit [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].

## Key facts

- Uses AI to craft personalized messages that drive business outcomes for email and mobile channels [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Includes generative AI capabilities that assist with subject-line creation and accelerate content production and experimentation [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Hightouch characterizes Movable Ink as oriented toward managed-service delivery rather than self-serve or highly customizable use, framing this as a limitation for buyers seeking flexibility [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
