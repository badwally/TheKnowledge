# Alan Cohen

## Summary

Alan Cohen is the General Manager, Financial Services of CINC Systems — a new role created for him at CINC — leading CINC's outbound payments and AP innovation including partnerships [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Cohen started in his role earlier in 2025 and has overseen the October 2025 launch of Payables+, a significant upgrade to CINC's previous AP solution leveraging CINC's native Cephai+ AI technology to speed and simplify invoice ingestion, ledger coding, and digital payments [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Cohen brings more than 30 years of B2B payments expertise across fintech and banking to the role, surfacing a CINC strategic priority on payments / AP innovation that informs the acquirer-side build-vs-buy framing in the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis-acquirer-mapping [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

## Key facts

### Role and tenure

- Title: General Manager, Financial Services, CINC Systems [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- The General Manager, Financial Services role is a new role for CINC, created specifically for Cohen [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Started in the role earlier in 2025; the exact start month was not disclosed in the October 14, 2025 announcement [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Part of CINC's three-executive second-wave build-out announced October 14, 2025 alongside Duri Chitayat (CTO) and Philip Dolan (CMO) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Explicit remit

- Leads CINC's outbound payments and AP innovation, including partnerships [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Oversaw the October 2025 launch of Payables+, a significant upgrade to CINC's previous AP solution leveraging CINC's native Cephai+ AI technology to speed and simplify invoice ingestion, ledger coding, and digital payments [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- CEO Ryan Davis's framing: "Alan is making payment outflows much more efficient and valuable for both clients and their vendors leveraging AI" — confirming Cohen's payments-and-AI focus as the operational anchor of the new GM role [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Prior experience

- More than 30 years of B2B payments expertise across fintech and banking [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- The creation of a dedicated General Manager, Financial Services role with 30+ years of B2B payments expertise overseeing the Payables+ launch surfaces a CINC strategic priority on payments / AP innovation in the months leading into Q4 2025, with Cephai+ AI explicitly framed as the technology stack underpinning the upgrade [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- The payments-strategic-priority signal is a divergence from a pure reserve-study / capital-planning adjacency and indicates that CINC's near-term build-vs-buy roadmap may prioritize payments expansion over capital-planning expansion absent a specific opportunity-side trigger; this informs the condo-capital-infra acquirer-engagement timing model by suggesting that direct reserve-study-acquisition outreach should be timed against signals of strategic-priority rotation rather than the current Q4-2025 payments-focused posture [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
