# Ontario Condominium Management Services Act, 2015 (CMSA)

## Summary

The Ontario Condominium Management Services Act, 2015 (CMSA) is the provincial statute that licenses condominium managers and management companies operating in Ontario. The CMRAO administers licensing under this Act, and licensees must carry out tasks ensuring condominium corporations meet their legal financial-management obligations under the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].

## Key facts

- Establishes the licensing regime for condominium managers and condominium management companies in Ontario [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- Licensees are obligated to ensure that condominium corporations meet the legal requirements for managing the condominium's financial obligations imposed by the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The CMRAO's Competency Profile (2019) defines the substantive scope of licensee obligations under the CMSA [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- Specific financial-management licensee obligations under the CMSA include preparing/presenting/implementing the annual budget, collecting common expenses, reviewing the general ledger, interpreting financial statements and explaining variances to the Board, monitoring the corporation's financial status, supporting the annual audit, monitoring reserve fund spending and balance relative to the RFS, supporting the reserve fund investment process, and implementing financial controls to protect the corporation's financial assets [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
