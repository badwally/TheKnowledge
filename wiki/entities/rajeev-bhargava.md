# Rajeev Bhargava

## Summary

Managing Director and head of investor behavior research at State Street Associates; lead author of the 2022 paper "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets," which applies NLP to digital media to construct narrative indicators and demonstrates their predictive power for market returns and use in asset allocation [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with State Street Associates, 140 Mt. Auburn St, Cambridge, MA, on the paper [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- First-listed author of "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" (July 15, 2022; SSRN id 4166640) with Xiaoxia Lou, Gideon Ozik, Ronnie Sadka, and Travis Whitmore [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Title on the paper is Managing Director and head of investor behavior research [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
