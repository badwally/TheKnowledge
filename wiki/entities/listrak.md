# Listrak

## Summary

Listrak is a multi-channel marketing automation platform whose vendor pitch positions it as going "far beyond batch-and-blast" by unifying customer data and behavior to deliver personalized, real-time email experiences that drive engagement and revenue [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]]. Capabilities include predictive segmentation, automated lifecycle campaigns, and AI-powered recommendations spanning welcome series through win-back flows [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]]. The product is targeted at "leading retailers and brands" — established organizations managing customer engagement and marketing at scale, rather than very small businesses [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]]. On Capterra it holds a 4.0 overall rating across 22 verified reviews as of June 2026, making it a mid-rated incumbent in the ecommerce marketing-automation category Orita competes in [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].

## Key facts

- Vendor categorizes Listrak under Email Marketing, Customer Engagement, and Marketing Automation on Capterra [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Channel coverage spans email, SMS, push notifications, and chat — i.e. multi-channel rather than email-only [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Feature surface includes marketing automation, email marketing, SMS marketing, customer segmentation, personalization, campaign scheduling, A/B testing, real-time analytics, workflow automation, and eCommerce integration for engagement and conversion tracking [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Capterra lists 120 distinct features for Listrak, with Campaign Management (5.0/5 from 3 raters), Contact Management (4.3/5 from 3 raters), Campaign Scheduling (4.5/5 from 2 raters), CAN-SPAM Compliance (5.0/5 from 2 raters), Lead Qualification (3.5/5 from 2 raters), and List Management (4.5/5 from 2 raters) among the most-reviewed feature dimensions [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Component Capterra subscores: Ease of Use 3.7, Features 4.0, Customer Service 4.1, Likelihood to Recommend 5.7/10, Value for Money 3.7/5 from 19 raters [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Reviews sentiment breakdown on Capterra: 73% positive, 14% neutral, 14% negative [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Pricing is gated — starting price is not published; a free trial is available, no free version exists, and prospects must contact the vendor to get a quote [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Target users per Capterra's FAQ synthesis: marketing leaders, email and digital marketing specialists, and ecommerce teams; Directors and VPs use it for campaign strategy and reporting, while specialists and ecommerce managers execute segmentation, automation, personalized promotions, and lifecycle marketing across channels [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Onboarding modalities offered: in-person training, live online sessions, webinars, and documentation [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Support channels: Email/Help Desk, FAQ/Forum, Knowledge Base, Phone Support, 24/7 Live Rep, and Chat; reviewers describe support as "personal and reachable" with helpful live chat and knowledgeable account guidance, though setup is noted as "a bit difficult" and some situations benefit from contact with a specific rep [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Capterra's featured "top alternative" to Listrak is Xtremepush (4.5 across 55 reviews, $250 per-feature starting price), and Capterra's broader alternatives carousel surfaces CleverTap (4.4/59), EngageBay CRM (4.7/908, $14.99/user/month), Marketo Engage (4.3/711), and Phonexa (5.0/75) as comparable products [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Head-to-head Capterra subscores against Xtremepush show Listrak trailing on Ease of Use (3.7 vs 4.5), Value for Money (3.7 vs 4.4), and Customer Service (4.1 vs 4.5) — i.e. Listrak rates worse than its featured alternative on every component Capterra publishes [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
- Capterra page was last updated June 3, 2026 and the listing is currently unclaimed by the vendor ("Do you work for Listrak? Manage this product listing") [[sources/web-2026-06-15-78b]].
