# Parlee Z-Zero XD

## Summary

The Parlee Z-Zero XD is a custom-geometry road frame built by [[entities/parlee|Parlee Cycles]], offered with SRAM [[entities/sram-etap-axs|eTap AXS]] electronic groupsets and sold and assembled by dealers such as [[entities/studio-velo|Studio Velo]] [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].

## Key facts

- The frame is sold with a "Custom Geo" specification — i.e., bespoke geometry per rider [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- A specific 2019 build (Studio Velo work order #23617) was specced with SRAM eTap AXS at a frame-and-build price of $15,708.00 before tax and discounts [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- That build initially shipped with [[entities/parlee|Parlee]]-branded G23 wheels, which Studio Velo returned to Parlee in favour of separately-supplied "3.4" wheels at the customer's request [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- Studio Velo's Pro Bike Build process for the frame included two staff test rides (initials "sTp" and "BW") after assembly [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- The customer of record on that work order requested seeing the frame in its raw state before final build [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- On 2019-09-09, Studio Velo packed customer Andrew Grant's bike — described on the work order simply as "Parlee XD" — into a [[entities/trico-case|Trico case]] for shipping under work order #23982; the work-order notes recorded that the customer would need a BB wrench to re-install rotors at the destination and that the spare tire was packed inside the case alongside the bike [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
