# Value Investors Club

## Summary

Invitation-only online community for sharing investment write-ups, founded by Joel Greenblatt about six years before his 2005 Columbia class after he discovered that another investor on Yahoo.com had independently identified one of his best investments — a complicated capital-structure stock trading at half cash value with a good business attached [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].

## Key facts

- Founded by Joel Greenblatt about six years before the 2005 class — i.e., approximately 1999 — after discovering an independent investor on Yahoo.com who had analyzed the same complicated-capital-structure investment correctly [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
- Membership is gated by an application procedure that requires applicants to know certain valuation metrics that Yahoo members would not know [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
- Greenblatt told his students: "If you get A+ in this class you could get in" [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
- Write-ups are scored by reviewers; Greenblatt recommends reading reviews above 5.7 with many reviewers, and that 5.5 and above is generally good [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
- Functions as both a learning tool and a research archive: "You can search by rating or person," "You see smart investors asking questions," and the historical write-ups can be checked against what happened years later [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
- Greenblatt told students never to share their VIC ID with anyone [[sources/pdf-d0b9b7e97b5c]].
