# PostPilot

## Summary

PostPilot is a direct mail platform named in Orita's Marketing Automation Plan as the kind of vendor for which Orita's engagement scoring selects high-intent audiences, reducing per-piece waste in postal campaigns [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].

## Key facts

- Direct mail platform cited as an integration target for Orita's audience-selection ML [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Used as an exemplar in Orita's direct-mail channel expansion (currently underway) [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
