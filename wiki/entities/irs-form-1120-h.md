# IRS Form 1120-H (U.S. Income Tax Return for Homeowners Associations)

## Summary

IRS Form 1120-H is the U.S. federal income tax return form specifically designed for homeowners associations (HOAs), and is the easiest to prepare and most common tax form used by U.S. associations [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]]. Form 1120-H offers a unique election regime found in no other area of U.S. tax law: an association may file as a homeowners' association using Form 1120-H or as a regular corporation using Form 1120, and this decision can change annually [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]]. Under Form 1120-H, non-exempt function income — which includes interest, nonmember fees, and per-use rental income, net of expenses — is taxed at a flat rate of 30% [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].

## Key facts

- Specifically designed for homeowners associations [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- The easiest of the available HOA-eligible tax returns to prepare [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- The most common tax form used by U.S. associations [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Taxes non-exempt function income at a flat rate of 30% [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Non-exempt function income under Form 1120-H includes interest, nonmember fees, and per-use rental income, net of expenses [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Reserve interest income is taxable on Form 1120-H regardless of whether the underlying reserve project falls under the IRS definition of a "capital" project [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Under Form 1120-H, the type of reserve projects in the Reserve Study (capital vs. non-capital by IRS definition) does not matter for tax purposes [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Form 1120-H allows associations to responsibly fund painting, asphalt sealing, tree trimming, and other major "non-capital" (by IRS definition) projects through Reserves without needing to involve a CPA during tax season [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- An association may elect annually whether to file Form 1120-H or Form 1120 [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- The decision between Form 1120-H and Form 1120 can change from one tax year to the next [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Reserve interest income is taxable whether the association files Form 1120 or Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Membership income, including assessments, is generally non-taxable under Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Tax returns must be filed every year, whether or not there is taxable income, because the corporation is a separate tax entity [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
