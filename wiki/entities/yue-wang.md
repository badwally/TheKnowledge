# Yue Wang

## Summary

Yue Wang is the first-listed author of the 2025 paper proposing an optimal predictive maintenance policy for systems whose degradation follows a nonstationary gamma process.

## Key facts

- Wang is the first listed author of 'Optimal predictive maintenance for a nonstationary gamma process,' published on 7 April 2025 with DOI 10.17531/ein/203543 [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The paper is co-authored with Ganlong Wang, Yanxia Wu, Guoyin Zhang, and Rui Zheng [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
