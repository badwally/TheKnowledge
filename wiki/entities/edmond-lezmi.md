# Edmond Lezmi

## Summary

Quantitative researcher at Amundi Asset Management in Paris; second-listed author of the September 2017 Amundi working paper "Understanding the Momentum Risk Premium: An In-Depth Journey Through Trend-Following Strategies" [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].

## Key facts

- Listed affiliation on the paper: Quantitative Research, Amundi Asset Management, Paris [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Listed contact email: edmond.lezmi@amundi.com [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Co-author of "Understanding the Momentum Risk Premium" (September 2017) with Paul Jusselin, Hassan Malongo, Côme Masselin, Thierry Roncalli, and Tung-Lam Dao [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
