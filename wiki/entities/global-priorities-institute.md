# Global Priorities Institute

## Summary

Research institute at the University of Oxford that publishes economic and philosophical research on global priorities, including foundational work on existential risk; publisher of Aschenbrenner's "Existential Risk and Growth" working paper [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].

## Key facts

- Hosted at the University of Oxford [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Published Leopold Aschenbrenner's "Existential Risk and Growth" as GPI Working Paper No. 6-2020 in September 2020 [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Aschenbrenner lists GPI as an institutional affiliation alongside Columbia University on the paper [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
