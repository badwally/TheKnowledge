# Anne Richards

## Summary

CEO of Fidelity International; author of the foreword to the firm's Investment Outlook 2023 — "Navigating the polycrisis" — in which she frames the year ahead as shaped by the lasting impact of the Ukraine war, a regime shift in monetary policy, and increasingly tight liquidity conditions [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].

## Key facts

- Listed as CEO of Fidelity International on the Investment Outlook 2023 foreword [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
- Argues the chief financial-market consequence of the past year is "the shift in monetary regime, from one supportive of global markets to one with a focus on taming inflation" [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
- Argues central banks' tightening of financial conditions in response to embedded inflationary pressures increases the risk of a hard landing — playing out as economic contraction and labour-market weakness, with lower corporate earnings for equities and rising default rates and downgrades in debt markets [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
- Identifies geopolitical uncertainty and a gradual decoupling of large economies from globalised supply and cooperation networks as factors that may exacerbate the challenges facing asset markets in 2023 [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
- Warns that consumer spending is at risk of a sharp decline as households battle higher energy and debt-servicing costs [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
- Notes signs of easing pressures into 2023: falling air, sea, and land freight prices and easing Covid backlogs that may soften the blow on consumption [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
- Reads the gradual removal of quarantine restrictions globally as a confidence boost, with China the only major economy where significant restrictions remain in place [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
- Maintains a cautious outlook for the next 12 months while acknowledging that markets are forward-looking and "sentiment will rise before the data shows the economy to be on an improving track" [[sources/pdf-90e7f9350657]].
