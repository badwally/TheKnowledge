# Aming Zou

## Summary

Aming Zou is the fourth (final) listed co-author of the 2018 *Journal of Traffic and Transportation Engineering (English Edition)* paper proposing a Gamma stochastic process-based method for estimating the resistance of aging service-proven bridges.

## Key facts

- Zou is the fourth (final) listed author of "Estimating the resistance of aging service-proven bridges with a Gamma process-based deterioration model," published on 12 December 2018 (DOI 10.1016/j.jtte.2018.11.001) [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- The paper is co-authored with Cao Wang, Kairui Feng, and Long Zhang [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
