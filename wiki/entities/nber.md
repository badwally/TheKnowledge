# National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER)

## Summary

U.S. economic-research nonprofit at 1050 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge, MA; publisher of working-paper series in which Acemoglu, He, and le Maire's 2022 paper "Eclipse of Rent-Sharing" (Working Paper No. 29874) [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]] and Gabaix and Koijen's 2021 paper "In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis" (Working Paper No. 28967) [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]] both appear; Acemoglu lists NBER as an institutional affiliation alongside MIT on both papers as well as on his 2024 paper "The Simple Macroeconomics of AI" [[sources/pdf-fb3e28ab676a]].

## Key facts

- Address: 1050 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge, MA 02138 [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]] [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Publisher of "Eclipse of Rent-Sharing: The Effects of Managers' Business Education on Wages and the Labor Share in the US and Denmark" by Daron Acemoglu, Alex He, and Daniel le Maire (NBER Working Paper No. 29874, March 2022) [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- Publisher of "In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis" by Xavier Gabaix and Ralph S. J. Koijen (NBER Working Paper No. 28967, June 2021) [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- NBER working papers are circulated for discussion and comment purposes; they have not been peer-reviewed or been subject to the review by the NBER Board of Directors that accompanies official NBER publications [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]] [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Acemoglu lists NBER as an institutional affiliation on "Eclipse of Rent-Sharing" alongside MIT [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- Both Gabaix (Harvard) and Koijen (Chicago Booth) list NBER as an institutional affiliation on the inelastic-markets paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- The Acemoglu-He-le-Maire paper notes that the views expressed are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the views of the NBER [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]]; the Gabaix-Koijen paper carries an equivalent disclaimer [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Acemoglu's 2024 "Simple Macroeconomics of AI" paper was prepared for Economic Policy and presented at the MIT Solow Memorial conference; Acemoglu's NBER affiliation is listed alongside MIT [[sources/pdf-fb3e28ab676a]].
