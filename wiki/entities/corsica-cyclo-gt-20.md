# Corsica Cyclo "GT 20"

## Summary

The Corsica Cyclo "GT 20" is an emblematic four-day stage [[concepts/cyclosportive|cyclosportive]] held each May on the [[entities/gt-20|GT 20]] route across [[entities/corsica|Corsica]], giving competitive cyclists a structured way to ride the island's signature crossing [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].

## Key facts

- The event is run as a four-day stage race each May on the GT 20 route [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- It is described as an "emblematic cyclosportive" — i.e., framed as a flagship event for Corsica's cycling calendar [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- It is positioned as an opportunity for cyclists to discover the GT 20 in a competitive format [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
