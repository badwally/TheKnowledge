# National Parking Association

## Summary

The National Parking Association is a U.S. industry body for parking facility operators, identified by PCI MNL-136-04 as the publisher of the Parking Garage Maintenance Manual, which PCI recommends as a complementary reference for parking-structure maintenance [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].

## Key facts

- Published the Parking Garage Maintenance Manual referenced by PCI MNL-136-04 as an additional source of maintenance guidelines for parking structures, particularly for non-precast and operational systems beyond the PCI manual's scope [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
