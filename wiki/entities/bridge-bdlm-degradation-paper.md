# Bridge performance degradation model based on the multi-variate bayesian dynamic linear method

## Summary

A 2024 *Advances in Structural Engineering* paper by Guojun Yang, Li Tian, Jianbo Mao, Guangwu Tang, and Yongfeng Du proposing a bridge performance degradation model based on the multi-variate Bayesian dynamic linear method, motivated by the observation that bridge structural performance degradation arises from the combined influence of various factors.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Guojun Yang, Li Tian, Jianbo Mao, Guangwu Tang, and Yongfeng Du [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- The paper was first published online on 23 July 2024 and appeared in the October 2024 issue of *Advances in Structural Engineering*, with DOI 10.1177/13694332241266541 [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- The paper's motivating premise is that the degradation of bridge structural performance arises from the combined influence of various factors, and that performance assessment and reliable prediction of bridge behaviour are needed to manage this complexity [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- The paper proposes a bridge performance degradation model based on the multi-variate Bayesian dynamic linear method [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- The article reported 126 total views and downloads as of indexing and had two citing publications recorded in both Web of Science and Crossref, including "Strain prediction in a large-span arch bridge using the TimeXer model considering temperature and traffic loads" and "Uncertainty-aware, high-precision multi-step prediction of structural health monitoring sensor streams under extreme typhoon events: an enhanced Bayesian dynamic linear model leveraging the kernel regression basis function for severe environmental adaptation" [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
