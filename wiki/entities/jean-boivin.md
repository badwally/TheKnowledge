# Jean Boivin

## Summary

Head of the BlackRock Investment Institute; named contributor on the BII "2023 Global Outlook: A new investment playbook," which argues the Great Moderation is over and a new regime of greater macro and market volatility calls for more frequent portfolio changes and granular sector/region views [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].

## Key facts

- Listed title on the 2023 Global Outlook: Head, BlackRock Investment Institute [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
- Named as one of six contributors to the outlook alongside Philipp Hildebrand, Wei Li, Alex Brazier, Vivek Paul, and Scott Thiel [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
