# Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act

## Summary

The Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act is the California statute governing common interest developments (condominiums, planned developments, stock cooperatives, and community apartment projects) and the homeowners associations that administer them. Within its reserve-funding regime, the Act prescribes a standardized Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary form at Civil Code §5570 that associations must deliver annually with the budget report under §5300, summarizing regular and special assessments, reserve adequacy over a 30-year horizon, percent-funded status, and the long-term interest and inflation assumptions used to prepare the summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]]. The §5570 disclosure regime was amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 349, Sec. 1, effective January 1, 2016 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].

## Key facts

- Establishes the standardized Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary form at Civil Code §5570 that associations must use each fiscal year [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- The §5570 disclosure form must accompany each annual budget report or summary delivered pursuant to Civil Code §5300 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- The form may be supplemented or modified to clarify the information delivered, so long as the minimum information set out in §5570(a) is provided [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Defines "Estimated remaining useful life" at §5570(b)(1) as the time reasonably calculated to remain before a major component will require replacement [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Defines "Major component" at §5570(b)(2) by reference to Civil Code §5550 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Permits components with an estimated remaining useful life of more than 30 years to be included in a reserve study as a capital asset or disregarded from the reserve calculation, provided the decision is revealed in the reserve study report and reported in the Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Specifies the component method for calculating reserve needs at §5570(b)(4): current cost of replacement or repair multiplied by the number of years the component has been in service divided by the useful life of the component; this calculation is not construed to require the board to fund reserves in accordance with it [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- §5570 was most recently amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 349, Sec. 1, effective January 1, 2016 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
