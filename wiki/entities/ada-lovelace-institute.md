# Ada Lovelace Institute

## Summary

The Ada Lovelace Institute is a UK-based independent research institute focused on data and AI governance; it co-published the 2021 "Exploring legal mechanisms for data stewardship" report with the AI Council, which set out the three-mechanism (data trust, data cooperative, corporate/contractual) toolbox for responsible data stewardship [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].

## Key facts

- The Ada Lovelace Institute produced the 2021 report through a working group of legal, technical and policy experts, with endorsements from the Open Data Institute, the City of London Law Society and the Data Trusts Initiative [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- In 2020, in partnership with Understanding Patient Data at the Wellcome Trust, the Institute convened patient roundtables and citizen juries across the UK and commissioned a nationally representative survey of 2,095 people on NHS data-sharing practices [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- That survey found that 82% of respondents expect the NHS to publish information about data-access partnerships, 63% are unaware that the NHS gives third parties access to data, and 75% believe the public should be involved in decisions about how NHS data is used [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- The Institute also flagged a forthcoming (March 2021) companion report on participatory mechanisms for data stewardship, indicating an ongoing programme of work on data governance beyond the legal-mechanism report [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
