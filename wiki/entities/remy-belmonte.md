# Remy Belmonte

## Summary

Instructor at Université Paris-Dauphine; co-instructor of the 2021 masterclass "Algorithmic and advanced Programming in Python" with Eric Benhamou, responsible for the lab sessions [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].

## Key facts

- Contact email remy.belmonte@dauphine.eu listed on the course material [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
- Led the lab sessions for the algorithms masterclass, described as where students "play with the concepts and start getting more and more familiar with how this works in real life" [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
