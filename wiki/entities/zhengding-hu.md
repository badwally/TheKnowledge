# Zhengding Hu

## Summary

Computer science researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of California, San Diego, and lead author of the 2026 paper "Pancake: Hierarchical Memory System for Multi-Agent LLM Serving" [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Computer Science and Engineering, University of California, San Diego, on the Pancake paper [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].
- Listed as the first author of "Pancake: Hierarchical Memory System for Multi-Agent LLM Serving" (arXiv:2602.21477v1, February 25, 2026) [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].
- Co-authors include Zaifeng Pan, Prabhleen Kaur, Vibha Murthy, Zhongkai Yu, Yue Guan, Zhen Wang, Steven Swanson, and Yufei Ding, all at UC San Diego CSE [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].
