# Ambrus Capital

## Summary

Investment firm and author of the 2022 paper "Volatility and the Changing Market Structure Driving U.S. Equities," which argues that U.S. equity markets are increasingly fragile because reflexive feedback loops — particularly dealer gamma hedging — mask accumulating risk during calm periods [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].

## Key facts

- Authored the 2022 paper "Volatility and the Changing Market Structure Driving U.S. Equities" [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
- Central thesis: equity markets have become increasingly fragile due to structural changes; days with wild swings are now common, but reliable dip-buying gives investors a false impression of stability [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
- Identifies six causes of increased reflexivity: speculative frenzy, changes to market microstructure, unprecedented stimulus, growth of option trading, dealer concentration, and Dodd-Frank-induced bank risk limits [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
- Frames the modern market with a forest-fire metaphor — naturally occurring fires have been doused quickly each time, leaving "immense amounts of dry kindling just waiting to explode" [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
- Draws an analogy between modern dealer gamma hedging and the 1980s LOR Associates portfolio-insurance product, citing both as price-insensitive selling regimes that pull liquidity when prices fall [[sources/pdf-04854302f962]].
