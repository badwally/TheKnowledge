# Samer Madanat

## Summary

Samer Madanat is an infrastructure-systems researcher and the first-listed author of the 1998 *Journal of Infrastructure Systems* paper that introduces a structured econometric approach to correct for selectivity bias in modelling the effectiveness of highway pavement maintenance and rehabilitation (M&R) activities.

## Key facts

- Madanat is the first listed author of "Selectivity Bias in Modeling Highway Pavement Maintenance Effectiveness," published in the *Journal of Infrastructure Systems*, vol. 4, no. 3, 20 September 1998 (DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)1076-0342(1998)4:3(134)) [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
- The paper is co-authored with Rabi Mishalani [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
- The paper appears under the copyright of the American Society of Civil Engineers [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
- Madanat is also co-author of the earlier related paper "Estimation of infrastructure transition probabilities from condition rating data," with Mishalani and Wan Ibrahim, published in the *Journal of Infrastructure Systems*, ASCE, 1(2), 120-125, 1995 [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
