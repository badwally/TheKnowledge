# Massimiliano Giorgio

## Summary

Massimiliano Giorgio is a co-author of the 2022 paper introducing a transformed gamma (TG) process for bounded degradation phenomena, applied to cylinder liner wear in marine diesel engines.

## Key facts

- Giorgio is the second listed author of 'A transformed gamma process for bounded degradation phenomena,' deposited at HAL (hal-04063942) on 7 July 2022 [[sources/web-2022-07-07-ac2]].
- The paper is co-authored with Mitra Fouladirad and Gianpaolo Pulcini [[sources/web-2022-07-07-ac2]].
