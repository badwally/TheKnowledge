# Phil Mosley

## Summary

Phil Mosley is the author of the *Cycling Strength & Conditioning Guide*, a structured exercise reference designed to slot into a cyclist's training plan as one or two strength sessions per week, with prescription that evolves across the Prep, Base, Build, and Peak training phases [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].

## Key facts

- Author of the *Cycling Strength & Conditioning Guide*, an illustrated reference of strength and flexibility exercises chosen specifically to support cycling training rather than general gym goals [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
- The guide is structured around four cycling-training phases — Prep, Base, Build, and Peak — and prescribes how strength sessions should evolve across each phase [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
- Recommends that cyclists new to strength work book a session or two with a personal trainer to verify technique before training independently [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
- Frames the seven trained qualities as power, range of movement, coordination, flexibility, agility, strength, and stability — explicitly positioning S&C as a 'solid internal scaffold' for the rest of the training plan [[sources/pdf-d2f73a919c86]].
