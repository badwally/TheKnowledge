# Blair Attard-Frost

## Summary

Blair Attard-Frost is a PhD candidate at the University of Toronto whose research and teaching focuses on the governance of AI systems in Canada and globally [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]]. She authored a January 2025 Montreal AI Ethics Institute op-ed analyzing the death of Canada's Artificial Intelligence and Data Act and the post-AIDA Canadian AI-regulation landscape [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- PhD candidate at the University of Toronto researching AI-systems governance in Canada and globally [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Author of the January 2025 Montreal AI Ethics Institute op-ed "The Death of Canada's Artificial Intelligence and Data Act" [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Has written separately on "AI countergovernance" as a framework for bottom-up AI regulation, referenced in the same op-ed [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
