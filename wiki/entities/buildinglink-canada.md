# BuildingLink Canada

## Summary

BuildingLink Canada is the Toronto-based Canadian subsidiary of BuildingLink LLC, established in 2012 by Toronto entrepreneur Nicholas Gill together with BuildingLink LLC founders Jerry and Zach Kestenbaum [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]]. Gill — who had previously pioneered one of the first cloud-based residential property management solutions in Canada — combined local Canadian market expertise with the BuildingLink platform's established U.S.-market track record to launch the Canadian operation, which self-characterizes as a prop-tech leader in Canada heading into its second decade [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]]. BuildingLink Canada is the condo-and-strata property management software vendor named in the source's page title and the primary-source anchor for the project's Canadian CMMS / work-order data-relationship and acquirer-adjacent competitive-intelligence track [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].

## Key facts

- Canadian subsidiary of U.S. parent BuildingLink LLC, established in 2012 [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].
- Co-founded by Toronto-based entrepreneur Nicholas Gill and BuildingLink LLC founders Jerry and Zach Kestenbaum [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].
- Prior to founding the Canadian operation with the Kestenbaums, Nicholas Gill had pioneered one of the first cloud-based residential property management solutions in Canada — framed in the source as the basis for Gill being "an ideal partner for BuildingLink as they looked to build on their success in the U.S. market" [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].
- The Canadian operation's stated value-creation thesis is the combination of Gill's local market expertise with BuildingLink's proven U.S.-market platform [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].
- Nicholas Gill's current title at BuildingLink Canada is Director of Marketing and Sales [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].
- Categorized by its own corporate site as condo and strata property management software [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].
- Gill self-characterizes the company at its first-decade anniversary as positioned for its "next decade of BuildingLink as a prop-tech leader in Canada" — establishing a 2022-era reference point for the operation having reached its tenth year of Canadian operations [[sources/web-1998-01-01-da9]].
