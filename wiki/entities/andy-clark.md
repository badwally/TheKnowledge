# Andy Clark

## Summary

Andy Clark is Professor of Logic and Metaphysics in the School of Philosophy, Psychology, and Language Sciences at the University of Edinburgh in Scotland [[sources/pdf-5f41a1d2e45f]]. He authored the 2013 *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* target article "Whatever next? Predictive brains, situated agents, and the future of cognitive science," which concludes that the hierarchical prediction-machine approach "offers the best clue yet to the shape of a unified science of mind and action" [[sources/pdf-5f41a1d2e45f]].

## Key facts

- Author of six monographs including *Being There: Putting Brain, Body and World Together Again* (MIT Press, 1997), *Mindware* (Oxford University Press, 2001), *Natural-Born Cyborgs: Minds, Technologies and the Future of Human Intelligence* (Oxford University Press, 2003), and *Supersizing the Mind: Embodiment, Action, and Cognitive Extension* (Oxford University Press, 2008) [[sources/pdf-5f41a1d2e45f]].
- Elected Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh in 2006 [[sources/pdf-5f41a1d2e45f]].
- "Whatever next?" generated an exceptionally large commentary response and inspired a Frontiers in Theoretical and Philosophical Psychology special topic edited by Axel Cleeremans and Shimon Edelman, comprising a preface, 25 commentaries, and a separate rejoinder from Clark [[sources/pdf-5f41a1d2e45f]].
