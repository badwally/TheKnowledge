# Allan M. Malz

## Summary

Economist at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York and author of the June 2014 FRBNY Staff Report No. 677, "A Simple and Reliable Way to Compute Option-Based Risk-Neutral Distributions," which describes a cookbook-style technique for extracting risk-neutral probability density functions from the option-implied volatility smile and applies it to equities, currencies, and long-term interest rates [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Federal Reserve Bank of New York; contact email on the paper is amalz@nyc.rr.com [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Sole author of "A Simple and Reliable Way to Compute Option-Based Risk-Neutral Distributions," Federal Reserve Bank of New York Staff Reports, no. 677, June 2014, JEL classification G01, G13, G17, G18 [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Frames the paper as cookbook-style guidance whose aim is to reduce complexity and provide a robust technique that avoids no-arbitrage violations leading to negative probabilities and other implausible results [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Gives worked examples of the technique for three asset classes — exchange-traded equity products (Black-Scholes vol vs ratio-to-spot), currencies and gold (Black-Scholes vol vs spot delta), and swaptions (Black vol vs bps from forward) [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Cites his own earlier work — Malz (2013) — as the application of the technique to the measurement of systemic risk, and notes that the technique is also used in the Federal Reserve Bank of New York's market monitoring [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- The paper acknowledges Sirio Aramonte, Bhupinder Bahra, Benson Durham, Stephen Figlewski, Will Melick, Carlo Rosa, Joshua Rosenberg, Ernst Schaumburg, and seminar participants at the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System for comments [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- The paper credits Juan Navarro-Staicos, Kale Smimmo, and Steven Burnett as collaborators on the implementation of the techniques described [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Notes that the views expressed are those of the author and do not necessarily reflect the position of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York or the Federal Reserve System [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
