# Merritt Hummer

## Summary

Merritt Hummer is a Partner at Bain Capital Ventures (BCV) and the named relationship lead on BCV's co-investment in MaintainX's $150 million Series D round announced July 9, 2025 at a $2.5 billion valuation [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

## Key facts

- Title: Partner at Bain Capital Ventures (BCV) [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Named as BCV's relationship lead on the MaintainX Series D investment [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Articulated her investment thesis on MaintainX: "Companies are increasingly turning to operational technology not just to improve performance, but to protect margins and preserve jobs" [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Framed MaintainX's effect as driving a shift from manual, reactive maintenance to a data-rich, AI-powered advantage [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Described the platform as introducing a new operating model where AI surfaces insights, predicts failures, and unlocks entirely new revenue streams and efficiencies [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Characterized the investment as backing a step-change in how businesses manage assets and plan for the future [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
