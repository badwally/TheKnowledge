# Philip Dolan

## Summary

Philip Dolan is the Chief Marketing Officer of CINC Systems, having started in April 2025; the role is part of CINC's three-executive second-wave build-out formally announced October 14, 2025 alongside Duri Chitayat (CTO) and Alan Cohen (General Manager, Financial Services) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Dolan brings more than 30 years of combined consumer brand management and private equity-backed SaaS marketing and sales leadership experience to the role; his explicit remit at CINC is sharpening CINC's value proposition, accelerating demand, and driving adoption of CINC's new technologies — including several new Cephai+ intelligent agents and CINC Connect (the modern resident and community manager digital experience integrated with CINC Accounting) — and expanding use of CINC Circle, the client-centric digital hub connecting community leaders with resources, training, and tailored co-branded marketing tools [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Dolan's PE-backed-SaaS marketing-leadership lineage is the load-bearing signal that CINC's marketing function is being explicitly aligned with Hg's PE-portfolio-company operational playbook, strengthening the alignment between CINC's go-to-market posture and Hg's value-creation thesis through the Year-3 hold-period window [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
