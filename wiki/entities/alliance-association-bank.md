# Alliance Association Bank (AAB)

## Summary

Alliance Association Bank (AAB) is a division of Western Alliance Bank that operates a dedicated community-association banking franchise across all 50 states [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]]. AAB was founded in 2008 and as of May 2022 employed 80 direct AAB staff [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]]. The bank's product surface spans lockbox, electronic payment, deposit, and lending solutions for community associations, anchored by the ConnectLive platform that interfaces with the community-association industry's leading management-software providers [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]]. AAB is named by AppFolio's HOA Financials product as one of five HOA-segment banking and check-processing partners (alongside CheckAlt, Pacific Premier Bank, First Citizens Bank, and SmartStreet/Banc of California) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].

## Key facts

### Corporate structure

- Division of Western Alliance Bank; not a separately chartered depository [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Founded in 2008 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- 80 direct AAB employees as of May 2022 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Operates in all 50 states [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Steve Curley serves as President of Alliance Association Bank [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Parent Western Alliance Bank led by Kenneth Vecchione as President and CEO [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Member FDIC through the Western Alliance Bank charter [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].

### Products and services

- ConnectLive platform interfaces with the community-association industry's leading software providers, surfacing key banking information in a single platform [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Homeowner payment and management portal provides management companies a single, secure tool to access, analyze, and retrieve online payment information for homeowners, while providing homeowners self-service access to manage payment methods and view electronic payment history [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Lockbox solutions marketed as robust, with enhanced administrative tools; deposited-funds availability follows Western Alliance Bank's funds-availability policy as outlined in the Deposit Account Agreement Disclosure [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Electronic payment avenues with enhanced administrative tools [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Focused deposit products and lending solutions explicitly targeted at the community association industry [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- All offers of credit are subject to credit approval [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].

### Positioning and personnel

- All operations, products, and bankers are characterized by AAB as exclusively dedicated to the community association industry [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- AAB states that many relationship managers and executive team members previously owned or worked at community management companies, or served directly on local association boards [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Markets itself as combining national bank resources with industry-specific expertise [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].

### Integration ecosystem

- Named by AppFolio HOA Financials as one of five HOA-segment banking and check-processing partners providing automated lockbox, bank statements, AR check images, auto reconciliation, and Positive Pay [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
