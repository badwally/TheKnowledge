# King Abdullah University of Science and Technology

## Summary

Saudi Arabian research university (KAUST) whose researchers led the 2026 paper "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents," the first systematic study of LLM agents under multi-principal interaction settings [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].

## Key facts

- Two of the eight authors of "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents" — corresponding lead author Shu Yang and co-author Di Wang — list KAUST as their primary affiliation [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
- Anchors the KAUST side of a KAUST–Stanford–Toronto–MIT collaboration on multi-user LLM agents [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
