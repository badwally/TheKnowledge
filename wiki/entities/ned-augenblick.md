# Ned Augenblick

## Summary

Finance researcher at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley; lead author of "Overinference from Weak Signals and Underinference from Strong Signals" (September 2024, co-authored with Eben Lazarus and Michael Thaler), which establishes that people systematically overinfer from weak information and underinfer from strong information across controlled experiments, naturalistic experiments, sports betting markets, and financial markets [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].

## Key facts

- Listed institutional affiliation: Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
- Listed contact email: ned@haas.berkeley.edu [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
- First-listed author of "Overinference from Weak Signals and Underinference from Strong Signals" (September 2024), co-authored with Eben Lazarus (UC Berkeley Haas) and Michael Thaler (UCL) [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
- The paper was circulated in an early version as Thaler (2021) [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
- The paper's experiments were approved by IRBs at Princeton University, UCL, and UC Berkeley, with pre-analysis plans registered at AsPredicted [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
- The paper thanks the editor Andrei Shleifer and four anonymous referees, as well as Nick Barberis, Francesca Bastianello, Roland Benabou, Ben Enke, Xavier Gabaix, Nicola Gennaioli, Thomas Graeber, and numerous other researchers and seminar participants [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
