# Mike Bollinger

## Summary

Mike Bollinger is the founder and CEO of PayHOA, the Kentucky-based SaaS startup serving self-managed homeowner associations that on May 15, 2024 announced a $27.5 million Series A round led by Elephant Ventures [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]]. Bollinger founded PayHOA in 2018 after selling two prior companies — LegFi.com (fraternity and sorority financial management) and File990.org (nonprofit tax compliance) — to Togetherwork in 2018, establishing a serial-entrepreneur pattern in volunteer-and-nonprofit-organization financial software [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]]. He holds a finance degree, framed by TechCrunch as the educational foundation he "has been putting to good use" in operating PayHOA [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].

## Key facts

- Founder and CEO of PayHOA as of May 15, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Finance degree holder [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Founded PayHOA in 2018 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Prior to founding PayHOA, founded LegFi.com (a startup focused on fraternity and sorority financial management) and File990.org (a startup focused on nonprofit tax compliance) [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Sold both LegFi.com and File990.org to Togetherwork in 2018 — the same year he founded PayHOA [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Stated motivation for founding PayHOA: experience working with volunteer-based organizations fueled his desire to create the company [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Public framing of the self-managed-HOA market gap: "While larger companies catered to professional property managers, self-managed HOAs struggled. They were forced to cobble together solutions with unconnected tools or generic software not designed for their specific needs — some even came to us with shoe boxes of paper receipts" [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Public framing of the Series A inflection-point rationale: "We'd found product market fit and were growing at a rapid rate. The additional capital and investor guidance will guide the business to the next level" [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Bollinger has noticed an increased number of property management companies signing up for the PayHOA platform, framing this as opening up the company's total addressable market beyond the self-managed segment [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Bollinger is the named CEO-counterparty whose public framing of PayHOA's segment positioning operationalizes the self-managed-HOA-segment ICP-exclusion anchor for Longspan's condo-capital-infra reserve-study GTM under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
