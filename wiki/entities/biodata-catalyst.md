# BioData Catalyst (BDC)

## Summary

BioData Catalyst (BDC) is a cloud-based biomedical data platform funded by the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI) that hosts heart, lung, blood, and sleep research data for cloud-based analysis [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]]. BDC describes itself as a custodian of data — it cannot control the quality of data ingested but harmonizes through its Data Management Core in collaboration with data providers [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].

## Key facts

- Primary funder is the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI) [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Studies submitted to BDC must be registered in dbGaP [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Data are either 'streamed in real time' or ingested in batches; datasets are added by a user, ingested from a controlled source, or transferred from collaborative programs [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- BDC characterizes itself as a custodian of data and acknowledges it cannot control the quality of data ingested [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- The Data Management Core works with data providers to assess data with the intent of harmonizing it [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Two access tiers: open and controlled [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Authentication for the open tier accepts eRA Commons, Google, or ORCID; the controlled tier requires eRA Commons [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Access is granted by a Data Access Committee (DAC) using the Data Access Request (DAR), dbGaP consent codes, and Data Use Limitations (DULs); a Cloud Use Statement may be required [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Security measures include Public Trust Clearance for all staff and contractors and encryption of data within the cloud; participant-level controlled-access data cannot be downloaded [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- User obligations include not re-identifying participants and not sharing logins [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- All activities are logged and regularly reviewed and monitored [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- User institutions are accountable and may be subject to sanctions [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
