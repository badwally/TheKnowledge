# Apollo

## Summary

Apollo is the prospecting and contact-enrichment platform in Orita's internal stack, used for the contact database and outbound sequences [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]]. Apollo is also the source of Orita's planned "always-on" prospecting cadence — weekly list pulls filtered through Orita's ICP and routed through the same identify-filter-enrich-validate-to-HubSpot pattern used for event lists [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].

## Key facts

- Prospecting and enrichment platform used by Orita's GTM motion [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
- Operates inside the Pipeline Agent's tool surface alongside HubSpot MCP and other enrichment APIs (PDL, ZeroBounce) [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Weekly Apollo list pulls are the always-on backbone of Orita's planned continuous pipeline generation [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
