# Quebec Bill 16 (Divided Co-Ownership Framework)

## Summary

Quebec Bill 16 is the provincial legislation that establishes a new regulatory framework governing the management of divided co-ownerships (copropriétés divises) in Québec, amending the Civil Code of Québec and related statutes to impose mandatory operational, financial, disclosure, and buyer-protection standards [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]]. The substantive operational requirements take effect through the Bill 16 implementing regulation (Decree 991-2025) published in the *Gazette officielle du Québec* on July 30, 2025, with the regulation coming into force August 14, 2025 — the fifteenth day following publication [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].

## Key facts

- Establishes the statutory framework that the Bill 16 implementing regulation (Decree 991-2025) operationalizes for all divided co-ownerships in Quebec [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Implementing regulation is in force as of August 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Imposes four principal duties on syndicates of co-owners: (1) maintenance logbook prepared by an accredited professional, (2) periodic contingency (reserve) fund study, (3) syndicate certificate for unit sales, and (4) trust-protected buyer deposits on new construction [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Compliance deadline for the maintenance logbook and reserve fund study requirements: August 14, 2028 (three years after the in-force date) [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Syndicate certificate and buyer-deposit trust rules apply immediately as of August 14, 2025 (no grace period) [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- A pre-existing maintenance log dated before 2020 is presumptively out of compliance with the new standards [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
