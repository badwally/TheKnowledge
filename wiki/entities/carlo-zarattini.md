# Carlo Zarattini

## Summary

Founder/researcher at Concretum Research in Lugano, Switzerland; first-listed and corresponding author of the April 10, 2023 paper "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable? Evidence of Sustainable Long-term Profits from Opening Range Breakout (ORB) Day Trading Strategy vs. Benchmark in the US Stock Market," which empirically demonstrates that a simple 5-minute ORB strategy on QQQ produced an annualized alpha of 33% (net of commissions) from January 1, 2016 to February 17, 2023 [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Concretum Research, Piazza Molino Nuovo 8, 6900 Lugano, Switzerland on the paper [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- Listed corresponding author email: c.zarattini@concretumresearch.com [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- Co-author with Andrew Aziz of the April 10, 2023 paper on the 5-minute ORB strategy applied to QQQ and TQQQ over the period January 1, 2016 to February 17, 2023 [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- The paper backtested the ORB strategy in MATLAB R2022 using aggregated data provided by Interactive Brokers [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- The paper deliberately did not optimize parameters, framing its goal as comparing a simple ORB strategy with a simple buy-and-hold benchmark rather than introducing a high-performance trading algorithm [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
