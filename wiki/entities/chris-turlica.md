# Chris Turlica

## Summary

Chris Turlica is the CEO and Co-Founder of MaintainX, the AI-powered maintenance and asset management platform headquartered in San Francisco [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. He was identified as CEO and Co-Founder in the company's July 9, 2025 announcement of its $150 million Series D round led by Bessemer Venture Partners at a $2.5 billion valuation [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

## Key facts

- Title: CEO and Co-Founder of MaintainX [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Identified as a co-founder of the company, which was founded in 2018 [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Articulated MaintainX's founding rationale: "Equipment failures cost companies $1.4 trillion annually, and many still rely on outdated tools. We built MaintainX to change that" [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Articulated the company's positioning in 2025 against supply chain disruptions and external cost pressures: customers offset external pressures by reducing unplanned asset downtime, parts, and labor costs while turning frontline professionals into knowledge workers with AI [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
