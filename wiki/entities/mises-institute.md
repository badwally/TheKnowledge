# Mises Institute

## Summary

Education and research center for the study of Austrian economics, founded in 1982 and based in Auburn, Alabama; publisher of Per Bylund's "How to Think about the Economy: A Primer" (2022) and advocate for "a radical shift in the intellectual climate, away from statism and toward a private property order" [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].

## Key facts

- Founded in 1982 [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- Located at 518 West Magnolia Avenue, Auburn, Alabama 36832 [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- Described as "an education and research center for the study of Austrian economics" supporting "the school of thought represented by Ludwig von Mises, Murray N. Rothbard, Henry Hazlitt, and F.A. Hayek" [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- Activities include publishing books and journals, sponsoring student and professional conferences, and providing online education [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- Advocates "a radical shift in the intellectual climate, away from statism and toward a private property order" [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- Published "How to Think about the Economy: A Primer" by Per Bylund in 2022 under the Creative Commons Attribution NonCommercial 4.0 International License, ISBN 978-1-61016-755-0 [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- Website: mises.org; contact: info@mises.org; phone: 1.800.OF.MISES [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
