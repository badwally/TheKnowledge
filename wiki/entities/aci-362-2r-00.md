# ACI 362.2R-00 — Guide for Structural Maintenance of Parking Structures

## Summary

ACI 362.2R-00, "Guide for Structural Maintenance of Parking Structures," is an American Concrete Institute report identified by PCI MNL-136-04 as a recommended reference for maintenance guidelines applicable to non-precast concrete systems incorporated into parking structures [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].

## Key facts

- Published by the American Concrete Institute as Report CI 362.2R-00, focused on structural maintenance of parking structures [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Cited by PCI MNL-136-04 as a recommended additional reference for maintenance guidelines on non-precast concrete systems such as cast-in-place concrete, steel, masonry, aluminum, or wood incorporated into parking structures [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
