# Studio Velo

## Summary

Studio Velo is a Mill Valley, California cycling shop that sells high-end bikes and components, performs professional bike fits, and builds custom-geometry frames such as the [[entities/parlee-z-zero-xd|Parlee Z-Zero XD]] [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]]. It also offers paid labor for packing bikes into travel cases for shipping and stocks branded merchandise alongside third-party tires and tools [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].

## Key facts

- Located at 31 Miller Ave, Mill Valley, CA 94941, with phone 415.380.1882 and web www.studiovelocycling.com [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]] [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Sales contact email is sales@studiovelocycling.com [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]] [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Acts as a [[entities/parlee|Parlee]] dealer — selling custom-geometry frames and handling returns of Parlee-branded components such as G23 wheels back to the manufacturer [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- Performs cycling bike fits during which compatible cleats (e.g., [[entities/speedplay|Speedplay]] Zero Aero Walkable) are installed [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- Offers a "Pro Bike Build" labor service that includes assembly and post-build test rides by shop staff [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- Offers a "Labor Pack Bike for Shipping" service billed at $100 per bike, with packing notes recorded on the work order (e.g., what tools the customer will need to re-install components at the destination, what spares to include in the case) [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Uses [[entities/trico-case|Trico cases]] as the destination container for bikes packed for shipping [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Sells branded merchandise such as the Studio Velo Fog Bottle 26oz at $12.99 each [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Stocks third-party tires and tools — e.g., the [[entities/schwalbe-g-one-speed|Schwalbe G-One Speed]] tubeless tire ($80.00) and the [[entities/park-tool-bbt-9|Park Tool BBT-9]] Shimano Hollowtech II bottom-bracket cup tool ($21.49) [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Operates under a return policy of "no returns on sale items," with non-sale returns accepted up to 30 days for store credit only and a possible restocking fee [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]] [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Charges 8.25% sales tax on the merchandise portion of a sale; the labor line on the September 2019 transaction was excluded from the taxed subtotal of $127.47 (out of a $227.47 subtotal) [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Identifies sales staff by first name on receipts (e.g., "Employee: Colin" on work order #23982) [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
