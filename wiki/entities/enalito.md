# Enalito

## Summary

Enalito is a Shopify-distributed omnichannel marketing automation app that bundles AI audience segmentation, on-site personalization widgets, AI-generated "individual personal stores," and an AI chatbot with campaign delivery across email, SMS, WhatsApp, and in-bot offers [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]]. Launched January 15, 2020 by Mumbai-based Enalito Automation Pvt. Ltd., it sits in the Orita-adjacent competitive set as a low-price-point full-stack agentic personalization play with broader native channel reach than email-only competitors but a small Shopify App Store footprint — only 3 reviews accumulated over six years on the listing [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].

## Key facts

- Distributed via the Shopify App Store under the listing title "Enalito: Email, SMS, ChatBot" [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Launched January 15, 2020 [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Developed by Enalito Automation Pvt. Ltd., headquartered at Flat-3 Floor-1 Wing-B 6-1, Doodhsagar CHSL, CIBA Road, Near Westin Hotel, Goregaon East, Mumbai, MH 400063, India [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Positions its AI segmentation engine as the mechanism that "spots additional revenue, stops attrition, and automates campaigns" — the same audience-intelligence-as-revenue-lever pitch that Orita uses [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Native channel coverage spans email campaigns, SMS campaigns, WhatsApp campaigns, AI chatbot conversations, and on-site personalization widgets — a wider channel surface than email-only Shopify ecommerce competitors [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Campaign-type catalog advertised on the listing: newsletters, pop-ups, discounts, promotions, upsell emails, cross-sell emails, cart emails, checkout emails, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, welcome emails, follow-up emails, price-drop emails, back-in-stock emails, win-back emails, product recommendations, drip campaigns, product reviews, and custom campaigns [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Differentiating product features called out by the vendor include "on-site personalization widgets and individual personal stores using AI chatbot" and real-time event triggers that fan out automated action series across email, SMS, and the WhatsApp bot [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Pricing tiers on the Shopify listing (USD/month): Free plan (250 contacts, 1,000 emails/month); Basic $29 (1,000 contacts, 10,000 emails); Standard $49 (3,000 contacts, 25,000 emails); Regular $99 (9,000 contacts, 60,000 emails) [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Overage pricing across paid tiers is $100 per additional 100,000 emails and 10,000 contacts [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- All paid tiers carry a 14-day free trial [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Listing supports English only [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- Shopify App Store rating is 5.0 stars from only 3 reviews — a low-volume signal indicating limited adoption or limited review-solicitation discipline despite a six-year run on the store [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
- One reviewer testimonial claims a 3x conversion lift attributed to the AI-driven personalized customer and product campaigns plus vendor-side campaign-setup support; sample size (n=3 total reviews) precludes treating this as benchmark-grade evidence [[sources/web-2020-01-15-0c4]].
