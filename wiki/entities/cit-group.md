# CIT Group Inc.

## Summary

CIT Group Inc. (NYSE: CIT) was a US financial holding company with over a century of experience, operating CIT Bank, N.A. (Member FDIC, Equal Housing Lender) as its principal bank subsidiary [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]]. On January 1, 2020, CIT Bank, N.A. completed the acquisition of Mutual of Omaha Bank for approximately $1 billion, adding $4.5 billion of community association deposits to its franchise and positioning CIT as a leading national bank in the homeowner-association banking segment [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]]. The January 2, 2020 press release announcing transaction close is presently hosted on newsroom.firstcitizens.com, the newsroom domain of First Citizens BancShares.

## Key facts

### Corporate structure

- Financial holding company with over a century of experience [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- NYSE-listed under the ticker CIT [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Principal bank subsidiary: CIT Bank, N.A. (Member FDIC, Equal Housing Lender) [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Operates a commercial banking segment and a consumer banking segment [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Commercial banking segment includes commercial financing, community association banking, middle market banking, equipment and vendor financing, factoring, railcar financing, treasury and payments solutions, and capital markets and asset management [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Consumer banking segment includes a national direct bank and regional branch network [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Stated mission: empower businesses and personal savers with the financial agility to navigate their goals [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Corporate website: cit.com/about [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].

### Mutual of Omaha Bank acquisition (Jan. 1, 2020)

- CIT Bank, N.A. completed the acquisition of Mutual of Omaha Bank on January 1, 2020 [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Approximate purchase price: $1 billion, comprised of $850 million in cash plus approximately 3.1 million shares of CIT stock issued to Mutual of Omaha Insurance Co. [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Transaction included $6.8 billion in deposits, of which $4.5 billion were community association deposits [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Transaction included $8.3 billion of total assets, including $3.9 billion of middle-market commercial loans [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Deposit and asset figures are as of September 30, 2019; pro forma financial data based on unaudited CIT and Mutual of Omaha Bank data as of the same date [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Post-close pro forma financial scale: approximately $42 billion total deposits and $60 billion total assets [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- CIT's strategic rationale: add a stable, lower-cost homeowner association deposit channel from the market-leading community association banking business [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Secondary strategic rationale: build on CIT's commercial banking strengths through the addition of relationship banking teams and expanded product and technology solutions [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Acquisition framed by CIT as accelerating its strategic plan to enhance its capability as a leading national bank and reduce overall cost of funds [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Brand integration: Mutual of Omaha Bank scheduled to begin transitioning to the CIT brand following close; retail branch locations to adopt the CIT Bank brand over the coming months [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Customer continuity: customer accounts remained unchanged at close, with continued access through Mutual of Omaha Bank branches, website, mobile apps, and relationship managers [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
