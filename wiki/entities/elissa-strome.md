# Elissa Strome

## Summary

Elissa Strome was Executive Director of the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy at CIFAR at the time of the Phase 2 launch in June 2022 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].

## Key facts

- Held the role of Executive Director, Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy at CIFAR as of June 22, 2022 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Characterized CIFAR's PCAIS Phase 1 leadership as having "forged Canada's reputation as a global leader in advancing research excellence and responsible AI development," in partnership with Amii, Mila and the Vector Institute [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Framed CIFAR's continuation into Phase 2 as ensuring "Canadian AI delivers positive social benefits and better economic opportunities for Canadians and the world" [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
