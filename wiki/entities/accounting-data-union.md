# Accounting Data Union (Datavault AI)

## Summary

The Accounting Data Union is a tokenized data-monetization venture announced by Datavault AI Inc. on 27 October 2025, structured as a collaboration with "the top two private accounting firms from each U.S. state" to build a tokenized private-equity-style index spanning thousands of companies across market sectors [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].

## Key facts

- Announced on 27 October 2025 by Datavault AI Inc. (Nasdaq: DVLT) [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Founding-member pool described as the top two private accounting firms from each U.S. state [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Stated objective is construction of a private-equity-style index dataset spanning thousands of companies across market sectors [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The press release estimates the global accounting-services market at $650 billion as the addressable opportunity [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- U.S. Patent No. 12,198,201 ("Platform and Method for Preparing a Tax Return") is cited as the foundational technology for tokenizing tax-related data within the union [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Member firms are described as converting data into U.S. currency via wallets and smart-contract-enabled tokens issued through Datavault AI's Data Vault Bank infrastructure [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- No member counts, governance bylaws, antitrust review, or independently audited revenue figures are disclosed in the announcement [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
