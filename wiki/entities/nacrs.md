# National Ambulatory Care Reporting System (NACRS)

## Summary

The National Ambulatory Care Reporting System (NACRS) is a Canadian administrative health dataset distributed via Statistics Canada that contains millions of records on individual admissions for day surgery, outpatient and community-based clinics, and emergency departments across the country [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].

## Key facts

- Contains millions of observations on individual admissions for day surgery, outpatient and community-based clinics, and emergency departments across the entire country and over several years [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Includes complexity of health conditions at the time of admission, time to treatment by physicians, time to admission, and demographic characteristics [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Sen (2026) cites NACRS as a candidate dataset for ML training on health-utilization and cost prediction and notes its near-universal coverage minimizes selection bias relative to private datasets [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
