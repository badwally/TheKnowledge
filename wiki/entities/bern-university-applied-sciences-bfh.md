# Bern University of Applied Sciences (BFH)

## Summary

Bern University of Applied Sciences (BFH) is a Swiss university of applied sciences that co-developed the open-source software underlying the MIDATA cooperative's data platform [[sources/web-2021-05-05-125]].

## Key facts

- Co-developed the MIDATA platform software jointly with ETH Zurich [[sources/web-2021-05-05-125]].
- The resulting software is published as "Open MIDATA Server" under GNU General Public License v3.0 [[sources/web-2021-05-05-125]].
