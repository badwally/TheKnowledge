# ONR Applications, Inc.

## Summary

ONR Applications, Inc. ("ONR") is a cutting-edge mobile application designed to enhance the resident experience for board members, owners, and residents of community associations, providing a single platform to book amenities, find local services, and connect with their community [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]]. Founded in 2018, ONR's singular focus has been providing a best-in-class experience for board members, owners and residents of community associations, with a user-friendly interface and robust features positioning the product as a resident-experience-inward complement to back-end-forward community-management platforms [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]]. On January 6, 2025, CINC Systems announced its acquisition of ONR, with ONR's co-founders and executive team — including co-founder and CEO Carlos Guzman — all joining the CINC team and becoming shareholders, and CINC and ONR expanding the integration between the two platforms to form one seamless product [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]]. ONR is the load-bearing primary-source acquired-company entity for CINC Systems' first publicly-confirmed acquisition under Hg Capital sponsorship, evidencing CINC's capability-gap-buy posture (resident-engagement-platform acquisition by a back-end-management-software incumbent) and a founders-retained-as-shareholders integration pattern relevant to the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis acquirer playbook [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].

## Key facts

### Corporate identity

- Cutting-edge resident engagement platform that streamlines communications for management companies with best-in-class apps and websites [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Founded in 2018 [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Singular focus since founding: providing a best-in-class experience for the board members, owners and residents of community associations [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Self-described as "a cutting-edge mobile application designed to enhance the resident experience" with a user-friendly interface and robust features providing residents with a single platform to book amenities, find local services, and connect with their community [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Website: www.onrapp.com [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].

### Leadership

- Carlos Guzman: co-founder and CEO of ONR at the time of the January 6, 2025 CINC acquisition; named spokesperson on the acquisition announcement [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- ONR's co-founders and executive team all join the CINC team via the acquisition and become shareholders — a founders-retained-as-shareholders integration pattern [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].

### Product feature set

- Advanced communication tools include resident feeds, amenity reservations, seamless payment features, concierge services, and package delivery tracking [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Product positioning at acquisition: "built from the resident experience inward," complementing CINC Systems' "built from the back end forward" platform — framing ONR's product as the resident-experience layer of what CINC characterizes post-acquisition as the industry's first truly end-to-end community-management solution [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Carlos Guzman's stated product vision at acquisition: "Imagine a platform where residents can book a pickleball court, hire a babysitter, receive a package, or seamlessly pay their dues — all with the simplicity and aesthetic expected in the digital age" [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].

### Acquisition by CINC Systems (January 6, 2025)

- On January 6, 2025, CINC Systems ("CINC") announced via PRNewswire from Duluth, Georgia that it had acquired ONR Applications, Inc. [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Headline acquisition framing: "By acquiring ONR, CINC boasts unrivaled resident engagement capabilities and will offer the only end-to-end platform in the industry" [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Transaction terms (price, deal structure, earnouts) were not disclosed in the announcement [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Post-acquisition integration architecture: ONR's advanced communication tools — resident feeds, amenity reservations, seamless payment features, concierge services, and package delivery tracking — are integrated into CINC's robust back-end systems to create the industry's first truly end-to-end solution; CINC and ONR have expanded the integration between the two platforms to form one seamless product, with continued effort including working closely with clients who have seen the benefits of both systems [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Customer-validation testimony at acquisition: Steve Weclew, CEO of Ardoor — "This is a game changer for our communities. CINC and ONR together are a powerful force. This combined solution will revolutionize how our communities operate and the possibilities for increased resident engagement and improved services are endless" [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
