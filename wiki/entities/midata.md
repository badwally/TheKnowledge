# MIDATA

## Summary

MIDATA is a Swiss nonprofit cooperative founded in 2015 that operates a citizen-owned data platform and acts as a trustee for the collection, consent management, and secondary use of members' health data [[sources/web-2021-05-05-125]]. It is one of the most-cited working examples of a health data cooperative [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key facts

- Founded in 2015 as a Swiss nonprofit cooperative providing a citizen-owned health-data platform [[sources/web-2021-05-05-125]].
- The underlying software was co-developed with ETH Zurich and Bern University of Applied Sciences (BFH) [[sources/web-2021-05-05-125]].
- Cited in the Internet Policy Review glossary as a representative example of a successful health data cooperative in which individuals upload health data to inform their doctors and to support scientific research and intervention development [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Allows users to change access permissions at any time and to see which groups are using their data and for what purpose, in contrast to the conventional model in which consent is given (if at all) at point of collection and data then disappears into inaccessible databases [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Listed alongside SAOS and GISC as cooperatives whose monetisation models rely on membership fees or dues [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
