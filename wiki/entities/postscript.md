# Postscript

## Summary

Postscript is an SMS marketing and sales platform designed specifically for Shopify brands, offering tools for list growth, campaign creation and management, compliance monitoring, deliverability optimization, and deep Shopify integration [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]]. The product's stated ambition is to make SMS the number one revenue channel for ecommerce brands by providing a full suite of conversational commerce solutions tailored to each stage of the customer journey [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]]. As of June 2026 Capterra lists Postscript with a 4.7/5 overall rating based on nine reviews and positions it in the SMS Marketing software category [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].

## Key facts

- Postscript targets Shopify merchants, ecommerce brands, and SMS marketers; Capterra notes the catalog does not specify a company-size segment, so the audience is best understood by industry and use case rather than SMB/mid-market/enterprise tier [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Pricing tiers are Starter ($0; campaigns and automations, unlimited segments, dedicated toll-free number), Growth ($100/month; adds unlimited opt-in keywords and live chat / email support), and Professional ($500/month; adds API access, enhanced analytics, and priority live chat / email support) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Both a free version and a free trial are available [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Capterra catalogs 21 product features: 2-Way Messaging, SMS Messaging, Mass Texting, Scheduled Messaging, Marketing Automation, Automated Responses, Campaign Planning, Campaign Scheduling, Multi-Campaign, Multi-Language, Contact Management, Customer Segmentation, Compliance Management, Reporting/Analytics, Activity Tracking, Engagement Tracking, Interaction Tracking, Open Rate Tracking, Message Personalization, Alerts/Notifications, and Communication Management [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Reviewer-rated feature scores include Campaign Planning 4.8/5 (n=4), Compliance Management 4.5/5 (n=4), Message Personalization 5.0/5 (n=4), Communication Management 4.7/5 (n=3), Interaction Tracking 4.3/5 (n=3), and Automated Responses 4.5/5 (n=2) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Composite Capterra sub-ratings: Ease of Use 4.6, Features 4.3, Customer Service 4.0, Value for Money 3.8, Likelihood to Recommend 6.7/10 [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Postscript integrates with more than 50 third-party tools and platforms, including Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Cordial, Listrak, Gorgias, Help Scout, Kustomer, Front, Gladly, AfterShip, Builder.io, Black Crow AI, Elevar, Justuno, Growave, Littledata, Loop, LoyaltyLion, Malomo, Shopify, Zapier, and Omnisend [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Customer support is offered via email/help desk and chat; Capterra reports no reviewer feedback on support experience is available in the catalog data, so user sentiment on response time or helpfulness cannot be stated [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- Capterra lists Twilio (4.4/5 over 504 reviews; usage-based pricing starting at $0.0079) as the featured top alternative, with EZ Texting, Goldie, Omnisend, and ClickSend as additional alternatives in the SMS Marketing category [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
- The Capterra listing was last updated on 2026-06-03, with provider data verified by Capterra's Software Research team and reviews moderated by its Reviews Verification team [[sources/web-2026-06-15-d6f]].
