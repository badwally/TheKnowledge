# Jingran Sun

## Summary

Jingran Sun is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2025 *Buildings* paper applying explainable AI to evaluate pavement deterioration rates due to flooding events using TxDOT pavement condition data.

## Key facts

- Sun is the fourth (final) listed author of "Evaluating Pavement Deterioration Rates Due to Flooding Events Using Explainable AI," published in *Buildings* 15.9 (2025): 1452, DOI 10.3390/buildings15091452 [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- The paper is co-authored with Lidan Peng, Lu Gao, and Feng Hong [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
