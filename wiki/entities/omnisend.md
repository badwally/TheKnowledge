# Omnisend

## Summary

Omnisend is an all-in-one ecommerce marketing platform that bundles email campaigns, SMS messaging, pop-up and landing-page forms, and workflow automation for shop owners on platforms like Shopify and WooCommerce [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]]. It is positioned for time-constrained SMB ecommerce merchants who want to grow revenue through personalized customer connections without heavy technical setup [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].

## Key facts

- Omnisend reports being trusted by 150,000+ ecommerce businesses as of June 2026 [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Capterra aggregates a 4.7/5 overall rating from 836 user reviews, with sub-scores of 4.7 ease of use, 4.6 features, 4.5 customer service, and 4.6 likelihood to recommend [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Reviewer sentiment on Capterra is 98% positive, 2% neutral, 0% negative [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Pricing starts at $16/month for the Standard plan (up to 500 contacts) and $59/month for the Pro plan (2,500 contacts); a free version is offered but no free trial [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- The reviewer base skews small-business: 96% of reviewers identify as small businesses, with top industries Retail (33%), Marketing and Advertising (26%), Consumer Goods (19%), and Apparel & Fashion (14%) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Top reviewer job functions are Marketing (32%), Administrative (19%), and Entrepreneurs/Owners (15%) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- The core feature set includes a drag-and-drop email builder, customizable templates, audience segmentation, real-time reporting, workflow automation for welcome flows, abandoned cart recovery, and post-purchase messages, SMS marketing, and pop-up and landing-page forms [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Omnisend advertises 100+ integrations; reviewer-confirmed connectors include Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, Google Ads, Meta for Business, ShipStation, Zapier, Adobe Commerce, AfterShip, Attentive, and 99minds [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- The platform appears on the 2026 Capterra Shortlist for SMS Marketing and earned 2026 Capterra "Best of" badges for All-in-One Marketing Platform, Campaign Management, Content Marketing, Email Tracking, Lead Nurturing, and Marketing Analytics [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Reviewer pros emphasize an intuitive interface (96% positive across 190 mentions), streamlined email campaign management (97% positive across 60 mentions), and measurable sales growth from automations (98% positive across 40 mentions) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- The most-cited reviewer complaint is design and formatting limitations, with 39% negative sentiment across 66 mentions, especially around content-block manipulation and branded customization [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Support channels include email/help desk, FAQ/forum, knowledge base, 24/7 live representatives, and chat, with reviewer complaints noting slower response on some issues and limited live-chat access on lower-tier plans [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Capterra-listed alternatives to Omnisend include HubSpot Marketing Hub, Bitrix24, Marketo Engage, and Constant Contact [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
- Capterra last verified the Omnisend listing on June 3, 2026 [[sources/web-2026-06-15-20d]].
