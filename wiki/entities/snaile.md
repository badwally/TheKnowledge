# Snaile

## Summary

Snaile is a package locker vendor identified by BuildingLink as one of four named "leading package locker solutions" integrated into the BuildingLink CAM platform — alongside Parcel Pending, Package Concierge, and Luxer One [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]]. Its inclusion in BuildingLink's Canadian-facing (en-ca) integration portfolio is notable in a smart-locker category otherwise dominated by US-headquartered providers [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].

## Key facts

- Identified as one of four named "leading package locker solutions" integrated with BuildingLink [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Listed in BuildingLink's Canadian-facing (en-ca) integration portfolio, indicating availability for Canadian Strata and condo properties [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Integration positioned to enable secure, automated delivery storage so residents can retrieve packages at their convenience and reduce staff workload [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
