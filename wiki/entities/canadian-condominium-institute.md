# Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI)

## Summary

The Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI) is a Canada-wide industry institute identified by ACMO as a partner for public-awareness work on the Ontario condo manager shortfall [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key facts

- ACMO recommends generating awareness of the Ontario condo manager shortfall via ACMO, the Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI), and the Community Associations Institute (CAI), including providing extra support, resources and continuing education to promote the profession as a viable career path [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
