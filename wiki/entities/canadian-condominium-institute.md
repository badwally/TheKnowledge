# Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI)

## Summary

The Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI) is a national Canadian industry association that serves the condominium community across all facets — managers, boards, owners, vendors, and professional advisors. CCI provides education, information, awareness, and access to expertise by and for its members, and operates through regional chapters including the Toronto and Area Chapter, which publishes the Condovoice magazine [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]], and the Golden Horseshoe Chapter, which publishes the CCI GHC Condo News Magazine [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].

## Key facts

- CCI National leads the national condominium industry by providing education, information, awareness and access to expertise by and for its members [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- CCI does not represent any one profession or interest group; it represents all facets of the condominium community, encouraging all interest groups to work together [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- Operates regional chapters including the Toronto and Area Chapter, which publishes the Condovoice magazine [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- Operates the Golden Horseshoe Chapter (CCI GHC), which publishes the CCI GHC Condo News Magazine; Volume 12 was the Summer 2022 issue [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Published "Canadian Condominium Statistics" (June 2016) cited by industry authors as the reference for jurisdictional condominium counts [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- Per the June 2016 CCI Statistics, Ontario contained more than 9,000 condominium corporations representing some 600,000 units [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- Per the same statistics, British Columbia contained over 30,000 strata corporations representing more than 1,000,000 units [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- Per the same statistics, Alberta contained approximately 8,000 condominium corporations representing approximately 440,000 units [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]].
- Operates a resource centre at cci.ca that disclaims contents as a curation of materials, not all of which are created/provided/vetted by CCI; jurisdictional applicability varies and information may not reflect changes in legislation or case law [[sources/web-2026-01-01-3bf]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The CCI resource centre carries CCI GHC chapter articles in its central catalog (e.g., the MacKay 2022 reserve-fund investing piece is accessible at cci.ca/resource-centre/view/1268), suggesting cross-chapter editorial syndication [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
