# S&P 500

## Summary

Market-capitalization-weighted index of approximately 500 large-capitalization U.S. stocks that together account for about 80% of the total U.S. stock market; constructed by an index committee using soft eligibility criteria rather than hard rules, and the most popular benchmark for U.S. equity funds [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].

## Key facts

- Approximately 500 U.S. stocks with large capitalizations comprising about 80% of total U.S. stock market capitalization [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Each constituent's weight reflects its relative market capitalization; a $1,000 investment in the index allocates more to the largest companies and less to the smallest [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Constructed by an index committee that follows criteria but no hard and fast rules [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Current eligibility criteria for inclusion include a market capitalization of at least $18 billion (with a sufficient amount that trades freely), profitability in the most recent quarter, cumulative profitability over the past four quarters, and sector representation consistent with the total market [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- The committee performs no macroeconomic forecasts, sector or industry limits, position weightings, factor analyses, or performance expectations — "more notable than what the index committee does is what it does not do" [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- On average there are about 25 changes in the S&P 500 annually, or about 5% of the total number of stocks, with one removal and one addition counting as a single change [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Has low turnover and invests for the long term [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- The index returned 26.3% in 2023, with more than half of that gain attributable to the appreciation of the seven "Magnificent Seven" stocks [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Is by far the most popular benchmark for U.S. equity funds and is the de facto reference for the SEC's requirement that U.S. mutual funds report results against an "appropriate broad-based securities market index" [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
