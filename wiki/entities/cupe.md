# Canadian Union of Public Employees (CUPE)

## Summary

The Canadian Union of Public Employees (CUPE) is a Canadian public-sector union that submitted a brief to the INDU committee following the November 2023 amendments to AIDA, characterizing the bill as "a deeply flawed legislative framework on a pivotal matter for all Canadians" and asking the committee to allow additional stakeholder time to analyze the amendments [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- Submitted a brief to INDU after the November 2023 amendments to AIDA were proposed [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Called the post-amendment AIDA "a deeply flawed legislative framework on a pivotal matter for all Canadians" [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Requested additional analysis time for stakeholders to comment on the new amendments [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
