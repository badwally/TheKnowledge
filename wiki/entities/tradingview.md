# TradingView

## Summary

Charting and indicator platform on which the SwingAlgo+ WEEKLY BIAS indicator runs; access to the indicator is granted on a per-TradingView-username basis after SwingAlgo+ verifies a PayHip purchase receipt [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].

## Key facts

- Hosts the SwingAlgo+ WEEKLY BIAS indicator, which buyers apply to their own charts on the platform [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- The TradingView username is one of three required fields a buyer must email to SwingAlgo+ to be granted access to WEEKLY BIAS [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- SwingAlgo+ provisions access on TradingView within a quoted 24–48 hour window after receiving a valid access request [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- Per the SwingAlgo+ disclaimer, applying WEEKLY BIAS to a TradingView chart constitutes the user's agreement to the SwingAlgo+ Trading Disclaimer [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
