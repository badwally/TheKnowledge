# Ali Modarressi

## Summary

NLP researcher at LMU Munich's Center for Information and Language Processing (CIS) and the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML), and lead author of the 2025 NoLiMa long-context evaluation benchmark [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Center for Information and Language Processing (CIS), LMU Munich, Germany, and with the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML) [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
- Listed as corresponding author with email amodaresi@cis.lmu.de [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
- Lead author of "NoLiMa: Long-Context Evaluation Beyond Literal Matching," Proceedings of the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2025; arXiv:2502.05167v1 [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
- The NoLiMa work was performed during an internship at Adobe Research [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
- Co-authors on NoLiMa include Hanieh Deilamsalehy, Franck Dernoncourt, Trung Bui, Ryan Rossi, and Seunghyun Yoon (all Adobe Research) and Hinrich Schütze (LMU/MCML) [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
