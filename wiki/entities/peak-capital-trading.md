# Peak Capital Trading

## Summary

Vancouver, BC-based proprietary trading firm; institutional affiliation of Andrew Aziz on the April 10, 2023 paper "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?" co-authored with Carlo Zarattini [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].

## Key facts

- Listed address on the paper: 744 West Hastings Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada V6C 1A5 [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- Andrew Aziz, affiliated with Peak Capital Trading, is a corresponding author of the paper at andrew@peakcapitaltrading.com [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- Shares the same Vancouver address with Bear Bull Traders, Aziz's other listed affiliation on the paper [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
