# Juho Kanniainen

## Summary

Juho Kanniainen is a co-author of the 2022 *Scientific Reports* paper on prognostic modeling of predictive maintenance with survival analysis for mobile work equipment.

## Key facts

- Kanniainen is the second listed author of 'Prognostic modeling of predictive maintenance with survival analysis for mobile work equipment,' published 20 May 2022 in *Scientific Reports* (PMID 35595821, DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-12572-z) [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The paper is co-authored with Zhen Yang, Tomi Krogerus, and Frank Emmert-Streib [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
