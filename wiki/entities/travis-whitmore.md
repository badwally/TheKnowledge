# Travis Whitmore

## Summary

Vice President and Senior Quantitative Researcher at State Street Associates; co-author of the 2022 paper "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Vice President, Senior Quantitative Researcher at State Street Associates, with office address 18 Stephanstrasse, Frankfurt am Main, Germany, on the paper [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Co-author of "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" (July 15, 2022) with Rajeev Bhargava, Xiaoxia Lou, Gideon Ozik, and Ronnie Sadka [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
