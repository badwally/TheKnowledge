# Marmot Tours

## Summary

Marmot Tours is a UK-based road-cycling holiday operator whose flagship 'Classic Cols' range introduces riders to mountain cycling through guided, vehicle-supported holidays in Corsica, Sardinia, Provence, Spain, Slovenia, the Dolomites, the Pyrenees, the Cévennes & Ardèche, the Alps, and Tenerife — each holiday built around a flexible-route, flexible-pace format with Classic, Challenge, and often Easier routes for every day [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].

## Key facts

- Operates from the UK, contactable at cycle@marmot-tours.co.uk [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Markets a 'Classic Cols' range of holidays across Corsica, Sardinia, Provence, Spain, Slovenia, the Dolomites, the Pyrenees, the Cévennes & Ardèche, the Alps, and Tenerife [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Recommends Pyrenean itineraries, the Tour of Tenerife, Cévennes & Ardèche, or Sardinian Highlights as introductory destinations for cyclists new to mountains [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Each day on a Classic Cols holiday offers Classic and Challenge route options, often plus an Easier route — letting mixed-ability groups ride the same itinerary at different volumes and ascents [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Each holiday is supported by two guides and support vans; guides are positioned at 'easy to miss' junctions and the van and guides circulate regularly [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Provides the Marmot Tours App with maps, altitude profiles, safety notes, and RWGPS-driven turn-by-turn directions, available from 7 days before to 7 days after each holiday [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Distributes hotel-to-hotel GPS tracks in .fit and .tcx formats compatible with most Garmin and Wahoo devices [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Offers airport transfers timed to fit common flight schedules; private transfers may be arranged for groups of 6 or more [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Frames its holidays as explicitly non-competitive: 'no pressure to keep up with anyone else' — riders self-pace, can request a van lift up a hill, and choose their daily route based on how they feel [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Operates the 6-day [[entities/corsican-highlights|Corsican Highlights]] holiday on [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Other Challenge-tier itineraries — Picos & Angliru and Andalusia — are rated by Marmot as comparable in difficulty to the Corsican Highlights Challenge route [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
