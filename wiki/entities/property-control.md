# Property Control

## Summary

Property Control is the parent brand and enterprise-grade operating system for property management firms overseeing mixed portfolios, unifying four sister products — Condo Control, HOA Central, HOA Sites, and Patrol Points — under a single command center spanning condo towers, HOAs, commercial sites, and mobile patrol teams [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]].

## Key facts

- Property Control launched as a parent brand on August 29, 2025, bringing together Condo Control, HOA Central, HOA Sites, and Patrol Points into a single command center for mixed portfolios [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Property Control homepage also lists Condo Manager as a named sister product, linked directly to condomanager.com under Products [[sources/web-2025-07-02-a17]]
- Brian Bosscher is CEO of Property Control [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The portfolio targets four operational categories: condominium and HOA management (Condo Control), self-managed HOAs and communications (HOA Central), public-facing HOA and management-company websites (HOA Sites), and security/compliance operations including geo-verified tours and incident reporting (Patrol Points) [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The platform offers multi-brand single sign-on and a unified subscription across the four sister brands [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Property Control ships native integrations to Yardi, Stripe, Zego, and VMS, plus an open API [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Product feature set emphasizes AI automation, including natural language bylaw search, smart routing, automated workflows, and a 24/7 AI help desk [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Executive-dashboard positioning highlights arrears and at-risk communities across regions for portfolio-wide oversight [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Customer success model is positioned as "white glove," with a named CSM, structured onboarding, training, and certification [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Existing customers continue on their current products with no disruption at launch; cross-brand features can be enabled through single sign-on [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Press release is dated New York, NY, August 29, 2025, authored by Phillip Livingston on the condocontrol.com blog [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Vertica Capital Partners is the private equity owner of Property Control; the June 2024 strategic investment is on record as the firm's third property management software acquisition [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]]
