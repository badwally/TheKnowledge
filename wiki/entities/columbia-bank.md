# Columbia Bank

## Summary

Columbia Bank is the largest bank headquartered in the Western U.S., with over $70 billion in assets, operating a dedicated Community Association Banking team that delivers HOA and Property Management products including API Banking, Lockbox Services, Online Banking, Remote Deposit Capture, and HOA lending [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. Columbia is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].

## Key facts

- Largest bank headquartered in the Western U.S. [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Over $70 billion in assets [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Operates an experienced Community Association Banking team dedicated to HOA banking [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Product surface includes API Banking, Lockbox Services, Online Banking, Remote Deposit Capture, and HOA lending [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Positioned as combining the resources, sophistication, and expertise of a national bank with a commitment to deliver superior, personalized service [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Named banking partner in the Vantaca partner directory [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
