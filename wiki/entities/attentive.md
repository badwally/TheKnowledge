# Attentive

## Summary

Attentive is an AI-powered SMS and email marketing platform positioned for retail enterprises and ecommerce entrepreneurs, marketed around customer engagement, audience targeting, and metric optimization across a connected marketing stack [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]]. On Capterra, Attentive carries a 4.8 / 5 rating across 108 verified user reviews as of the March 2026 listing update, placing it among the top-rated entries in the SMS marketing category [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].

## Key facts

- Attentive is self-described as an AI-powered SMS & email platform helping retail enterprises and e-commerce entrepreneurs engage customers and drive billions in revenue [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Attentive markets over 100 flexible integrations intended to connect SMS/email programs to the rest of a marketing stack [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Attentive's Capterra integration catalog lists more than 50 integrations including Klaviyo, Gorgias, WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento, Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, AfterShip, ActiveCampaign, and AWeber, covering ecommerce, customer support, and email marketing categories [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Pricing is contact-vendor (not publicly listed) and Attentive offers a free 30-day trial; no free version is available [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- On Capterra (108 reviews), Attentive scores 4.8 overall, 4.7 ease of use, 4.7 features, 4.5 customer service, 4.4 value for money, and 4.8 likelihood to recommend, with 98% positive reviewer sentiment [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Capterra reviewers skew strongly toward small business (82% of reviewers) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Top reviewer industries are Consumer Goods (28%), Retail (28%), Apparel & Fashion (26%), and Marketing and Advertising (13%); the FAQ block separately reports Apparel & Fashion leading at 22%, Retail 21%, and Consumer Goods 20% based on the same 108-review base [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Top reviewer job functions are Marketing (38%), Entrepreneurs/Owners (38%), Program & Project Management (13%), and Sales & Business Development (13%) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Capterra-rated feature set includes mass texting (4.8), scheduled messaging (4.8), message personalization (4.5), reporting/analytics (4.0), 2-way messaging (4.0), and automated responses (4.0), with 46 features total across the catalog [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Additional features called out in reviews include sign-up forms for SMS and email capture, segmentation, A/B testing, automated journeys, and campaign analytics dashboards [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Per reviewer-derived FAQ summaries, Attentive is used to solve low customer engagement, weak list growth, and manual SMS execution by launching compliant campaigns, automated journeys, and sign-up forms quickly [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Customer support options include email/help desk, FAQ/forum, knowledge base, and phone support; reviewers describe support as fast, accessible, and hands-on with helpful account managers and proactive onboarding guidance, though some reviewers cite technical issues or gaps in customer-service tooling [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Onboarding and training options include live online sessions, webinars, documentation, and videos [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- A recurring reviewer criticism is reporting depth: brands and agencies want more granular filters, combined cost-and-revenue reporting, in-platform exports, and less manual spreadsheet work [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Reviewers report Attentive is easy to use for beginners, small teams, and agency users, while non-Shopify integrations can require extra steps or outside help [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
- Capterra surfaces EZ Texting as the featured side-by-side alternative; the broader alternative set includes Omnisend (4.7 / 836 reviews, from $16/user/mo), TextP2P (5.0 / 125, from $21/user/mo), Clearstream (4.8 / 679, from $29/user/mo), and Project Broadcast (4.9 / 647, from $21/user/mo) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-95e]].
