# NYC Local Law 84 (Energy & Water Benchmarking)

## Summary

NYC Local Law 84 is the New York City benchmarking statute requiring covered buildings to file annual reports of their energy and water usage with the city [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. The data reported under LL84 is consumed by Local Law 97's carbon emissions calculation, making LL84 accuracy a structural input to a building's projected LL97 penalty exposure — inaccurate LL84 estimates submitted by a prior property management company can inflate the city's LL97 penalty projection for a building, and corrected resubmissions can reduce that projection materially [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].

## Key facts

- New York City statute requiring annual reporting of a building's energy and water usage [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- LL84 filings feed directly into the LL97 carbon emissions calculation [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Inaccurate LL84 estimates have downstream financial consequence: they can inflate a building's projected LL97 penalty exposure [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Daisy's documented Upper West Side takeover case (2025) found that the prior management company had submitted inaccurate LL84 usage estimates; correcting and resubmitting accurate LL84 reports reduced the building's projected LL97 penalties from ~$58,000 to zero [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- This pattern positions LL84 audit as a load-bearing component of CAM takeover-onboarding workflow for NYC buildings [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
