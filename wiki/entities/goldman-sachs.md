# Goldman Sachs

## Summary

US-based investment bank whose Global Markets Division published "The Multiplier Effect" (December 5, 2022), a report on multi-manager hedge funds; cited by ClearAlpha as the empirical source for the claim that multi-manager fund assets grew at 18.8% per year from 2017 to 2022 versus 2.6% per year for the rest of the hedge fund industry [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].

## Key facts

- Published "The Multiplier Effect" (Goldman Sachs Global Markets Division, December 5, 2022), a report on multi-manager hedge funds [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The report documented multi-manager fund assets growing at 18.8% per year compared to 2.6% per year for the rest of the hedge fund industry over the 2017–2022 period [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- Despite the growth differential, the report noted multi-manager funds still account for only 8% of total hedge fund industry assets [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
