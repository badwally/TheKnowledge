# Douglas Elliman

## Summary

Douglas Elliman is a publicly-listed residential real estate company whose property management arm was sold to Associa, through Associa's wholly-owned subsidiary PMG Holdings, for $85 million in a transaction announced via SEC filing on October 24, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]]. The proceeds, combined with cash on hand, were used to pay off $50 million in convertible debt from Kennedy Lewis Investment Management — debt that was redeemed for $95 million — leaving Elliman with $122 million in cash on hand following the deal [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]]. The board voted unanimously to approve the sale with the exception of board member and Kennedy Lewis partner David Chene, who resigned following the deal; the SEC filing notes the resignation "did not result from any dispute or disagreement" [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]]. Douglas Elliman is the seller-side counterparty in the load-bearing October 2025 Associa M&A transaction surfaced as a named-backup-acquirer-target confirmation in the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis-acquirer-mapping under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

## Key facts

### Corporate identity

- Publicly-listed real estate company filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Nearly $1 billion in revenue in 2024 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Property management arm (pre-sale)

- In 2024, the property management arm employed over 216 people [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- In 2024, the property management arm accounted for 3.7 percent — or $36 million — of Douglas Elliman's nearly $1 billion in revenue [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Sale of property management arm to Associa (October 24, 2025)

- Announced October 24, 2025 via SEC filing: sale of the property management arm to PMG Holdings, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Associa [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Sale price: $85 million for all of the equity interest in Douglas Elliman Property Management [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Board approval: unanimous, with the exception of board member and Kennedy Lewis partner David Chene, who resigned following the deal; the SEC filing notes the resignation "did not result from any dispute or disagreement" [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- David Chene was appointed to a board seat as part of Kennedy Lewis' investment in Elliman [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Five-year licensing agreement: Elliman and PMG entered an agreement permitting PMG to continue using the Douglas Elliman brand as part of its property management services [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Pre-existing referral relationship between the Elliman brokerage and the property management firm is maintained following the deal [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Use of proceeds (October 2025 sale)

- Proceeds from the $85 million sale, along with cash on hand, were used to pay off $50 million in convertible debt taken from Kennedy Lewis Investment Management in 2024 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- The Kennedy Lewis convertible debt was redeemed for $95 million [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Elliman will have $122 million in cash on hand as a result of the deal [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Elliman expects to recognize more than a $75 million after-tax gain in the fourth quarter of 2025 as part of the deal [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Strategic context (2025)

- Earlier in 2025, Elliman received a takeover bid from Anywhere Real Estate that eventually fizzled out [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- The Anywhere takeover offer followed a successful first quarter for Elliman, which narrowed its losses to $6 million in Q1 2025 from $42 million in Q1 2024 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Elliman backslid in the second quarter of 2025, reporting a net loss of $23 million versus the $2 million loss in the previous-year second quarter [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- Douglas Elliman is the seller-side counterparty in the operative October 24, 2025 Associa M&A transaction that confirms Associa's active-acquirer posture in U.S. property management consolidation — surfaced in the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis-acquirer-mapping as a named-backup-acquirer-target validation [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- The seller-side framing — divestiture driven by Elliman's need to retire Kennedy Lewis convertible debt rather than by strategic interest in property management itself — surfaces a structural dynamic in which non-CAM-native parent companies hold property management arms as candidates for divestiture to CAM-native consolidators, a pattern that strengthens the Associa-as-acquirer thesis [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
