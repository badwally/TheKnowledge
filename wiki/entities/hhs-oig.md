# HHS Office of Inspector General (OIG)

## Summary

The Office of Inspector General (OIG) of the US Department of Health and Human Services is the enforcement body for information-blocking investigations under the 21st Century Cures Act, with investigative jurisdiction across all three Cures Act actor classes — healthcare providers, health information networks and exchanges (HIEs/HINs), and health IT developers of certified health IT [[sources/web-2026-04-08-4dc]].

## Key facts

- Has authority to investigate claims of possible information blocking across all three Cures Act actor classes: healthcare providers, HIEs/HINs, and health IT developers of certified health IT [[sources/web-2026-04-08-4dc]].
- OIG findings of information blocking against healthcare providers trigger the HHS disincentives regime established by the final rule under section 4004 of the Cures Act [[sources/web-2026-04-08-4dc]].
