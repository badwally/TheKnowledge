# Anindya Sen

## Summary

Anindya Sen is the author of the 2026 C.D. Howe Institute Commentary 706, "The Missing Pillar of Canada's AI Strategy: Data Supply Chains," which argues that Canada's AI strategy must explicitly prioritize data supply chains alongside its existing pillars [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].

## Key facts

- Author of C.D. Howe Institute Commentary 706 (2026), "The Missing Pillar of Canada's AI Strategy: Data Supply Chains" [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Proposes a three-principle policy framework: expanded-but-guarded data access; integration of privacy-enhancing technologies; and transparent governance grounded in cost-benefit analysis [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Co-authored Sen et al. (2025), cited as demonstrating that health-data sharing guidelines can be constructed on cost-benefit principles letting data-hosting organizations weigh social benefits from innovation against privacy and liability costs [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Cites Lehrer and Xie (2026) in characterizing Statistics Canada datasets as a "gold-standard" for ML training data despite not being collected in real time [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
