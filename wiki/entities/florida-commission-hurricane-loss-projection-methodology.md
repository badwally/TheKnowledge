# Florida Commission on Hurricane Loss Projection Methodology (FCHLPM)

## Summary

The Florida Commission on Hurricane Loss Projection Methodology (FCHLPM) is the Florida regulatory body designated as the methodological gatekeeper for hurricane-loss models used in Florida insurance regulation. Under Florida Statute §718.111(11)(a)2, an association or group of three or more associations seeking to provide property insurance covering the probable maximum loss for a 250-year windstorm event must use a hurricane-loss model that has been accepted by the FCHLPM [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].

## Key facts

- Designated by §718.111(11)(a)2 of the Florida Condominium Act as the body responsible for accepting hurricane-loss models used to compute the probable maximum loss for a 250-year windstorm event [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- Acceptance by the FCHLPM is a precondition for using a windstorm-loss model to underwrite a group property-insurance policy or program covering at least three communities under Chapters 718, 719, 720, or 721 [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
- A group property-insurance policy relying on a 250-year-PML calculation may not be issued or renewed after July 1, 2008 unless it has been reviewed and approved pursuant to procedures referencing the FCHLPM-accepted model [[sources/web-2024-01-01-4d7]].
