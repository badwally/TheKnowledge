# André T. Beck

## Summary

André T. Beck is a structural reliability researcher who contributes to three articles in the March 2025 JCSS-themed special issue of *Structural Safety*, spanning terrorism risk assessment, methods of structural reliability, and the philosophical interpretation of probability.

## Key facts

- Beck is a co-author of "Assessment of risk reduction strategies for terrorist attacks on structures" (article 102381) in the March 2025 issue of *Structural Safety*, with Stewart and Thöns [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Beck is a co-author of "Development of methods of structural reliability" (article 102474), led by Ellingwood [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Beck is the second listed author of "Interpretation of probability in structural safety – A philosophical conundrum" (article 102473), led by Vrouwenvelder [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
