# ERSAR (Entidade Reguladora dos Serviços de Águas e Resíduos)

## Summary

ERSAR is the Portuguese national regulator for water and waste services, and a co-publisher of the foundational technical guides on infrastructure asset management for water and wastewater systems that underpin the AWARE-P methodology.

## Key facts

- ERSAR is co-publisher (with LNEC and IST) of Technical Guide n.16, 'Infrastructure asset management of water services' (Alegre & Covas, 2010, Lisbon, 472 pp., ISBN 978-989-8360-04-5) [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- ERSAR is co-publisher (with LNEC and IST) of Technical Guide n.17, 'Infrastructure asset management of wastewater and stormwater services' (Almeida & Cardoso, 2010, Lisbon, ISBN 978-989-8360-05-2) [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
