# François-Philippe Champagne

## Summary

François-Philippe Champagne was Canada's Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry at the launch of Phase 2 of the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy in June 2022 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].

## Key facts

- Held the portfolio of Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry on June 22, 2022, when he announced Phase 2 of the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy in Toronto [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Framed Phase 2 as a vehicle to "accelerate trustworthy technology development, while fostering diversity and cooperation across the AI domain" and to "solidify Canada as a global leader in artificial intelligence and machine learning" [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Spoke for the federal government in committing more than C$443M from Budget 2021 to Phase 2 implementation across CIFAR, the national AI institutes, the Standards Council of Canada, and Canada's Global Innovation Clusters [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
