# CINC Up 2025

## Summary

CINC Up 2025 is CINC Systems' annual client conference, held in Austin, Texas in September 2025, and the launch venue at which CINC announced CINC Connect — its new resident portal and mobile app platform — on September 16, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]]. The conference drew more than 650 attendees for networking, learning, and product innovation, establishing it as CINC's primary product-launch venue and a recurring acquirer-side scale-and-roadmap signal for the condo-capital-infra exit thesis under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

## Key facts

- Held by CINC Systems at the company's annual CINC Up client conference in Austin, Texas in September 2025 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- More than 650 attendees gathered for networking, learning, and product innovation [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Used as the public launch venue for CINC Connect on September 16, 2025 — confirming CINC Up as the company's primary product-launch venue rather than an externally-facing trade show [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
