# LDP Project Team on the Evolution and Implementation of AI

## Summary

A project team established on February 3, 2023 under the Liberal Democratic Party of Japan's Digital Society Promotion Headquarters to design national AI policy in response to the rapid social implementation of large language models, chaired by Masaaki Taira [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key facts

- Established on February 3, 2023 under the Liberal Democratic Party's Digital Society Promotion Headquarters [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Chaired by Masaaki Taira [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Authored "The AI White Paper: Japan's National Strategy in the New Era of AI" (April 2023), which recommends formulating a new national AI strategy and establishing a leadership function for AI policy within the Japanese government [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Also produced a summary version of the whitepaper (April 2023) covering the same five-part structure: necessity of a new strategy, AI development capacity, government utilization, private-sector utilization, and a new approach to AI regulations [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Conducted seven hearings with leading AI experts to inform the whitepaper, including Yutaka Matsuo (University of Tokyo), Kazuto Ataka (Keio University SFC), and Hiroaki Kitano (Sony Computer Science Laboratories) [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Supported by a working group of outside experts including lawyers with high expertise in the AI field, who assisted in organizing and writing the recommendations [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Recommends establishing a command tower for AI policies with expanded organizational capacity and active incorporation of expert and private-sector knowledge from both domestic and foreign sources [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
