# Form T1044 — Non-Profit Organization (NPO) Information Return

## Summary

Form T1044, the *Non-Profit Organization (NPO) Information Return*, is the CRA-prescribed annual information return that a residential condominium corporation qualifying as a non-profit organization under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) of the *Income Tax Act* must file alongside its T2 corporate income tax return, even though the corporation is exempt from Part I tax [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. T1044 is a disclosure instrument — it does not produce a tax liability — but the filing obligation runs in parallel with, not in lieu of, the T2 obligation under paragraph 150(1)(*a*) [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. The T1044 filing trigger is conditional on quantitative thresholds (assets, taxable revenue) and is sticky once triggered: a strata or condominium corporation that has filed T1044 in any prior year must continue to file it with every subsequent T2 return regardless of later asset or revenue levels [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]]. In Québec, the T1044 federal-threshold disclosure regime is mirrored in Section 3 of the provincial CO-17.SP, which is triggered when the same CRA thresholds are exceeded [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Required to be filed by a residential condominium corporation that qualifies as a non-profit organization under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) of the *Income Tax Act* [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Filed *with* the corporation's T2 income tax return, not as a substitute for it [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Filing requirement runs in parallel with the paragraph 150(1)(*a*) requirement that all corporations file an income tax return each year, even when exempt from Part I tax [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Established by CRA as the prescribed information return for NPOs claiming the paragraph 149(1)(*l*) exemption [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].

### Filing trigger thresholds

- A strata or condominium corporation must file Form T1044 with its T2 if the corporation's assets were more than $200,000 at the end of the fiscal year [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- A strata or condominium corporation must file Form T1044 with its T2 if it received or was entitled to receive taxable dividends, interest, rentals, or royalties totalling more than $10,000 in the fiscal period [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- A strata or condominium corporation must file Form T1044 with its T2 if it had to file an NPO Information Return for a previous fiscal year [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- T2inc.ca's November 2025 practitioner guide frames the asset trigger as "assets > $200,000 during the fiscal year" (versus the CRA's "at the end of the fiscal year" formulation); the practitioner phrasing is looser than the CRA-canonical formulation [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Sticky-filer rule

- Once a strata corporation has filed a T1044 for any fiscal year, it must continue to file the T1044 with every subsequent T2 tax return regardless of the amount of its revenues or assets in those later years [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- The sticky-filer rule operates as a one-way ratchet: the obligation only attaches in one direction, with no statutory mechanism for opting out once any threshold has been crossed [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].

### Quebec parallel disclosure via CO-17.SP Section 3

- Québec condominium syndicates must mirror the federal T1044 disclosure obligations in Section 3 of the provincial CO-17.SP when the same CRA threshold conditions are met: investment or property income (interest, rents, royalties, dividends) over $10,000; total assets exceeding $200,000 at the end of the previous fiscal year; or a previous obligation to send the T1044 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Some Québec tax-exempt entities may also need to file Form TP-997.1 (Information Return for Tax-Exempt Entities) depending on activities and assessment category — a parallel Québec provincial disclosure instrument that supplements (but does not replace) CO-17.SP Section 3 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- The federal T1044 is filed separately with the CRA and is NOT attached to the provincial CO-17.SP filed with Revenu Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
