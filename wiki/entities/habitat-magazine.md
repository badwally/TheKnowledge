# Habitat Magazine

## Summary

Habitat Magazine is a NYC-focused industry-journalism publication serving the New York condominium, cooperative, and HOA market. Its recurring Bricks & Bucks column covers financial, regulatory, and policy developments affecting boards, managers, and owners. Habitat's April 2026 Bricks & Bucks piece by Emily Myers — "Mortgage eligibility for condos: New rules and relief" — is a primary NYC-market secondary-reporting source on the Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac March 2026 GSE policy update, with expert commentary from Strategic Inspections CEO Orest Tomaselli and Moritt Hock & Hamroff partner David Fitzhenry [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].

## Key facts

- NYC-market industry-journalism publication serving the condominium, cooperative, and HOA segment [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].

- Coverage frames the March 2026 GSE update as a combined burden-and-relief moment for NYC boards: 15% reserve floor as cost pressure, Limited Review retirement as compliance escalation, ineligibility-cascade to jumbo and commercial as market-wide impact, and the roof-insurance rollback as relief [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
