# BankUnited, N.A.

## Summary

BankUnited, N.A. is a National Commercial Bank with a focus on specialty businesses including HOA and Property Management [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. BankUnited's HOA & Property Management Group provides specialized treasury solutions to help association-management clients streamline operations and is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].

## Key facts

- National Commercial Bank [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Specialty-business focus that includes HOA and Property Management [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Operates an HOA & Property Management Group providing what it markets as "white glove" service along with specialized treasury solutions [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- HOA & PM team positioned as combining banking-professional and technology-consultant capabilities [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Named banking partner in the Vantaca partner directory [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
