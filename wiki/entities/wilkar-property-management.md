# Wilkar Property Management

## Summary

Wilkar Property Management is a Quebec-based condo management firm and a published UpperBee customer; firm President Michael Wilk attributes Wilkar's growth into "one of the largest property managers in Quebec" to its use of UpperBee and references planned expansion into the Ontario market as the next growth phase [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Condo management firm operating in the Quebec market [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Published UpperBee customer with testimonial copy on the UpperBee integrations page [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- President: Michael Wilk [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Self-positioning in the Wilk testimonial: "one of the largest property managers in Quebec" [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Forward-looking statement: planned expansion into the Ontario market ("as we move into the Ontario market") [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Wilk's articulated value proposition for UpperBee: efficient resident communication plus transparent financial visibility, which Wilk credits with creating trust and long-lasting client relationships [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
