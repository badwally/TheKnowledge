# Hoover Institution

## Summary

Public-policy research center at Stanford University; one of the three Stanford bodies that jointly produce the Stanford Emerging Technology Review (SETR) and the publisher of the 2025 study showing that more than half of the leading Chinese AI researchers behind DeepSeek's breakthrough large language model were educated and trained entirely in China [[sources/pdf-stanford-2026-the-stanford-emerging]].

## Key facts

- Co-produces the SETR annually together with Stanford University's School of Engineering and Stanford's Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) [[sources/pdf-stanford-2026-the-stanford-emerging]].
- Published a 2025 study cited in SETR 2026 finding that more than half of China's leading AI researchers behind DeepSeek's breakthrough large language model were educated and trained entirely in China [[sources/pdf-stanford-2026-the-stanford-emerging]].
