# John Deere

## Summary

John Deere is an agricultural equipment and agtech company that participates in Ag Data Transparent (ADT) as an ADT-certified company, signaling commitment to ADT's principle that farmers should control their own data [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].

## Key facts

- Publicly identified as an ADT-certified company [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- Cited by ADT as committed to farmers controlling their own data via ADT certification [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
