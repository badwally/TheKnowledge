# Karen Ward

## Summary

Chief Market Strategist for EMEA at J.P. Morgan Asset Management and lead-listed author of the firm's November 2022 "Investment Outlook 2023," which lays out a mild-recession base case and a bullish reset for fixed income [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Chief Market Strategist for EMEA on the 2023 Investment Outlook [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
- Frames the central 2023 question as "will inflation start to behave as economic activity slows?" — arguing there are already convincing signs that inflationary pressures are moderating [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
- Argues a deep 2008-style housing-led recession is unlikely because housing construction was relatively subdued for much of the prior decade and recent buyers were constrained by stricter loan-to-value and loan-to-income ratios [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
- Identifies two bearish counter-camps the outlook explicitly rejects: a 1970s-style inflation persistence camp and a "slowdowns spiral" camp; counters that busts follow booms and "booms were notably absent in the last decade" [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
