# Duri Chitayat

## Summary

Duri Chitayat is the Chief Technology Officer of CINC Systems, having joined CINC in October 2025 as part of a three-executive second-wave build-out announced on October 14, 2025; Chitayat succeeds Peter Yang as CTO, with Yang remaining as a technology advisor while taking a sabbatical to travel the world [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Chitayat brings decades of engineering, product innovation, consulting, and transformation leadership experience around the world to the role and was most recently CTO at Safeguard Global, a leading workforce enablement platform company; his explicit remit at CINC is to oversee the ongoing development of CINC's platform, ensuring its reliability, speed, and security as CINC continues to grow, and to bring together CINC's AI-development operations, Colombia-based technology operations acquired as part of CINC's ONR acquisition earlier in 2025, and the company's ongoing technical operation [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Chitayat's appointment is the load-bearing CTO-succession signal in the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis-acquirer-mapping, surfacing CINC's near-term technical operating-model integration as consolidating recently-acquired engineering capacity rather than expanding into new adjacencies via further acquisition through approximately the first 6-12 months of his tenure [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

## Key facts

### Role and tenure

- Title: Chief Technology Officer, CINC Systems [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Start month: October 2025; began the new role the same month CINC announced the appointment [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Succeeds Peter Yang as CTO; Yang remains as a technology advisor to Chitayat and the company while taking a sabbatical [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Part of a three-executive second-wave build-out announced October 14, 2025 alongside Alan Cohen (General Manager, Financial Services) and Philip Dolan (Chief Marketing Officer) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Prior experience

- Most recently served as CTO at Safeguard Global, a leading workforce enablement platform company [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Brings decades of engineering, product innovation, consulting, and transformation leadership experience around the world to the role [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Explicit remit at CINC

- Oversee the ongoing development of CINC's platform, ensuring its reliability, speed, and security as CINC continues to grow [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- Bring together CINC's AI-development operations, the Colombia-based technology operations acquired as part of CINC's ONR acquisition earlier in 2025, and the company's ongoing technical operation [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- CEO Ryan Davis's framing: "Duri's strong leadership will ensure CINC's platform is ready to add millions more homes to CINC's client base" — surfacing growth-target framing tied directly to the CTO role [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- Chitayat's appointment surfaces CINC's near-term technical operating-model integration as consolidating recently-acquired engineering capacity (AI-development operations + ONR-acquired Colombia tech operations + ongoing technical operation) rather than expanding into new adjacencies via further acquisition; this is a near-term acquisition-pipeline-dampening signal for the first 6-12 months of his tenure [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
- The CTO consolidation arc suggests acquirer engagement timing for an AI-native reserve-study tuck-under may favor the late-Q1-2026 / Q2-2026 window after Chitayat's initial integration mandate completes, rather than the immediate post-October-2025 window [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
