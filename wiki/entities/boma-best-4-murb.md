# BOMA BEST 4.0 Multi-Unit Residential Buildings (Technical Field Guide)

## Summary

BOMA BEST 4.0 Multi-Unit Residential Buildings (MURB) is a sustainable-buildings certification questionnaire and technical field guide published by BOMA Canada in June 2023, tailored to the multi-unit residential asset class [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]]. It is the residential-specific instance of the BOMA BEST 4.0 framework and is the most directly applicable BOMA standard for condominium common-element operating practice [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key facts

- Published June 2023 by BOMA Canada as the BOMA BEST 4.0 Questionnaire for Sustainable Buildings — Multi-Unit Residential Technical Field Guide [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Organized into six focus areas: Energy and Carbon (E), Water (W), Indoor Air Quality & Hazards (I), Accessibility & Wellness (A), Custodial & Waste (P), and Resilience & Site (R) [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Each focus area contains baseline practices (mandatory questions whose requirements must be met to achieve BOMA BEST certification) plus additional point-scoring topics [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The Energy and Carbon focus area contains three baseline practices: E1.0b — Energy and Carbon Assessment, E2.0 — Energy Management Plan, and E6.0 — Preventative Maintenance [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The Water focus area contains two baseline practices: W1.0b — Water Assessment and W5.0 — Mould and Water Damage Management [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The Indoor Air Quality & Hazards focus area contains two baseline practices: I1.0a — Owner or landlord manages whole building IAQ and I4.0 — IAQ Management in Construction [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The Accessibility & Wellness focus area contains two baseline practices: A1.0 — Accessibility Awareness and A4.0 — Occupant Engagement [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The Custodial & Waste focus area contains two baseline practices: P2.0 — Green Cleaning Program and P4.0a — Waste Audit [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The Resilience & Site focus area contains one baseline practice: R2.0 — Past Climate Hazards [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Each question card in the Field Guide includes Focus Area, Topic, Question number, Question, Answer options, Points/Max Points Available, Description, Requirements, Documentation, Adapted BB3 Question, Applicability, Suggested Lead, References, Crosswalk, and Other Notes [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Adapts and supersedes BOMA BEST 3.0 questions; applicants must fulfill the new BOMA BEST 4.0 requirements rather than the BB3 ancestor questions [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Lead-execution model: questions are scoped "In-house unless stated otherwise," with "Third-party" lead reserved for tasks where the expertise is not typically found in-house [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- For all applicable asset classes, requirements apply to the systems managed by the owner or landlord, owner or building manager [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
