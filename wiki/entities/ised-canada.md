# Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED)

## Summary

Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) is the Canadian federal department that published the Voluntary Code of Conduct on the Responsible Development and Management of Advanced Generative AI Systems in September 2023 [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].

## Key facts

- Published the Voluntary Code of Conduct on the Responsible Development and Management of Advanced Generative AI Systems in September 2023 [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
- The code is the vehicle through which signatory organizations voluntarily commit to measures across six outcomes — Accountability, Safety, Fairness and Equity, Transparency, Human Oversight and Monitoring, and Validity and Robustness — for advanced generative AI systems [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
- ISED published companion materials including an FAQ and an implementation guide for managers of AI systems [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
