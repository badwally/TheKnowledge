# Community Financials

## Summary

Community Financials is a US homeowner association (HOA) financial services provider that has used Pacific Premier Bank (post-merger: Columbia Bank) as a key banking partner of its operations since 2018 [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. The company supports its HOA clients nationwide via Pacific Premier's no-fee bank accounts, easy signature card updates, check lockbox processing, fraud prevention mechanisms, software integrations, competitive rates, and customer service, and published the August 28, 2025 client communication framing the Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger and Columbia Bank rebrand impact for HOAs and condos [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key facts

- US-based HOA financial services provider supporting homeowner association clients nationwide [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Has used Pacific Premier Bank as a key banking partner of its operations since 2018 [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The Pacific Premier relationship supports Community Financials's HOA-segment offering with no-fee bank accounts, easy signature card updates, check lockbox processing, fraud prevention mechanisms, visibility through software integrations, competitive rates, and customer service [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Published an August 28, 2025 explainer at communityfinancials.com communicating the August 31, 2025 Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger and subsequent Columbia Bank rebrand to its HOA clients [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Operates as a vendor-side intermediary in the HOA banking value chain — managing the bank relationship on behalf of HOA clients rather than the HOA contracting directly with the bank [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Committed to continue working closely with its Pacific Premier (post-merger: Columbia Bank) contacts to ensure a smooth transition for HOA clients [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
