# Jiyu Xin

## Summary

Jiyu Xin is the first-listed author of the 2026 review paper on life-cycle performance, design, maintenance, optimization, and decision-making of asphalt pavement under uncertainty.

## Key facts

- Xin is the first listed author of 'Life-cycle performance, design, maintenance, optimization, and decision-making of asphalt pavement under uncertainty: a review,' published on 1 February 2026 with DOI 10.1080/15732479.2025.2552239 [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper is co-authored with Dan M Frangopol, Mitsuyoshi Akiyama, Lianzhen Zhang, and Jianzhong Pei [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
