# Ngor Luong

## Summary

Senior Policy Analyst and author of the March 2026 U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission (USCC) Research Working Group paper "Two Loops: How China's Open AI Strategy Reinforces Its Industrial Dominance" [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Senior Policy Analyst on the USCC Research Working Group paper, published March 23, 2026 [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Argues in the paper that China's open AI strategy and its manufacturing dominance are mutually reinforcing, generating "interlocking innovation flywheels" across adjacent sectors [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Argues that U.S. export controls primarily target the digital loop of frontier model training and are not well suited to addressing the physical loop of deployment-driven data creation across China's manufacturing base [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
