# IRS Revenue Ruling 83-74

## Summary

IRS Revenue Ruling 83-74 is the federal tax ruling that permits a homeowners association whose Revenue Ruling 70-604 rollover election has been disallowed by IRS to amend its filed returns to Form 1120-H (the IRC §528 election form), thereby retroactively converting the association's filing posture and limiting the tax impact of the disallowed §277 / 70-604 position [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]]. Rev. Rul. 83-74 is the "only escape route" available to the tax preparer when an association is being audited on a disallowed 70-604 election; the practitioner must plead ignorance and invoke 83-74 to switch the original return to Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].

## Key facts

- A federal IRS revenue ruling that permits a homeowners association to amend filed returns to Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- The ruling functions as the practitioner's escape route when an association's Revenue Ruling 70-604 rollover election has been disallowed by IRS in audit [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- The invocation pattern: the tax preparer pleads ignorance, then utilizes the provisions of Rev. Rul. 83-74 to amend the returns to Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Rev. Rul. 83-74 amendment is the only out described by Gary Porter, CPA when an association is discovered (typically in IRS audit) to have failed the technical requirements of Rev. Rul. 70-604 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
