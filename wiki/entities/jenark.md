# Jenark

## Summary

Jenark is a CAM accounting software vendor named as one of 17 accounting integration partners in BuildingLink's resident-engagement platform [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. The Jenark–BuildingLink integration runs on a typically-overnight 24-hour sync and surfaces Jenark account balance and transaction history to unit owners on the BuildingLink resident site [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. Jenark is one of two BuildingLink accounting partners — alongside MDS — that charges an integration fee distinct from the BuildingLink platform fee [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].

## Key facts

- Named as an accounting integration partner in BuildingLink's accounting-integration catalog [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Syncs with BuildingLink every 24 hours, typically overnight [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- After the sync, unit owners can view their Jenark account balance and transaction history on the BuildingLink resident site [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Jenark charges an integration fee (one of two named BuildingLink accounting partners doing so, the other being MDS) [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
