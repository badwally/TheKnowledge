# Frédéric Roy-Gobeil, CPA

## Summary

Frédéric Roy-Gobeil is the named author of T2inc.ca's November 6, 2025 practitioner guide on the Revenu Québec CO-17.SP filing obligation for condominium syndicates [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. The guide articulates T2inc.ca's positioning of CO-17.SP as a mandatory annual information return for most Québec condominium syndicates, covers the form's interaction with federal T2/T1044 obligations, and outlines the mandatory-electronic-filing regime that took effect for fiscal years starting in 2024 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Authored "CO-17.SP Form: Essential Tax Filing Requirement for syndicates of co-owners in Quebec" published on T2inc.ca on November 6, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Identified as the article's named author on the T2inc.ca blog; T2inc.ca positions itself as a Québec corporate tax return specialist serving small businesses and condo syndicates [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
