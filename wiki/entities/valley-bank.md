# Valley Bank

## Summary

Valley Bank is the named launch bank partner for CINC Systems' TresRE treasury middleware product, announced June 6, 2023. Valley clients access TresRE through Valley's online banking platform, with Valley positioning the integration as eliminating manual reconciliation across real estate clients' accounting and banking accounts and matching incoming payments to the correct residents [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

- Selected TresRE to power real estate payments across multiple real estate verticals as of the TresRE June 2023 launch [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Valley Bank clients access TresRE via a user-friendly online banking platform [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Marc Nuzzolo, Senior Vice President, is the named Valley Bank executive responsible for the TresRE relationship and the broader real-estate-vertical client base [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Nuzzolo characterized TresRE's value as letting him add new real estate clients, provide better and more efficient processes that eliminate manual reconciliations, and ensure payments are matched to the right residents [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
