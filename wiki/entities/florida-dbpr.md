# Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation

## Summary

The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) is the state agency that licenses community association managers (CAMs) and operates the online licensure account system that Florida CS/CS/HB 913 (2025), Chapter 2025-175, requires every CAM and CAM firm to maintain [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].

## Key facts

- DBPR is the agency identified in CS/CS/HB 913 (2025) as the host of the online licensure account that every CAM licensee must create and maintain [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- Under HB 913 (2025), CAM firms must identify, on their DBPR online licensure account, the individual CAMs they employ to provide community association management services [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
- DBPR contains the Division of Condominiums (also referenced via FAC Chapter 61B-23 promulgation), giving the same agency jurisdiction over both the CAM licensure surface and the operational-governance rule chapter for Florida condominium associations [[sources/web-2025-04-30-984]].
