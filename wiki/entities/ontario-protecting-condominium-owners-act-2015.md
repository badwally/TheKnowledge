# Ontario Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 (PCOA)

## Summary

The Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 is the Ontario omnibus condominium-reform statute whose Schedule 1 amends the Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 with successive in-force provisions that, in turn, revoke and re-enact numerous interpretive paragraphs of O Reg 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]]. O Reg 48/01's interpretation provisions — particularly §1.1 — track PCOA Schedule 1 in-force events with paragraph-by-paragraph revocation notes, making the PCOA the principal modernization vehicle for Ontario's condominium records, governance, and disclosure regime.

## Key facts

- Schedule 1 of the Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 amends the Condominium Act, 1998 across multiple substantive sections; sections enumerated by cross-reference in O Reg 48/01 §1.1(2) include §11(8)(b), §22, §26.1, §26.2, §40, §42, §45–46, §51, §52, §74, §82.1, §88, §97, §106, §123, and §174 of the Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- O Reg 48/01 carries multiple "Note" paragraphs flagging that certain interpretive paragraphs of §1.1(2) are revoked on the day the corresponding PCOA Schedule 1 section comes into force, with cross-references to O. Reg. 180/17 amendments [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The PCOA Schedule 1 in-force schedule does not move uniformly — different sections of the Schedule have come into force at different times, with O Reg 48/01 carrying multiple "as it read immediately before" / "as enacted by" / "as re-enacted by" cross-references reflecting partial implementation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- §51 of PCOA Schedule 1 introduced the meeting-minutes core-record requirement now in paragraph 9 of the O Reg 48/01 core-record definition, gating the inclusion of board- and owner-meeting minutes in core records to those held on or after that section's in-force date [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
