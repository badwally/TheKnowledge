# Silicon Valley Bank

## Summary

Commercial bank and pillar of the US venture ecosystem; publisher of the semiannual "State of the Markets" report, with the H2 2025 edition (30th edition) presenting proprietary data and analysis on VC fundraising, deal activity, AI investment concentration, IPO dynamics, and macro headwinds — co-authored by President Marc Cadieux and Head of Investor Coverage Mark Gallagher with the SVB Market Insights analytics team [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].

## Key facts

- Publishes the "State of the Markets" report series; the H2 2025 edition is described as the 30th edition [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- SVB Commercial Bank is the operating division; led by Marc Cadieux (President) and Mark Gallagher (Head of Investor Coverage) [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- The SVB Market Insights team leverages proprietary data, subject-matter experts, and relationships with investors and founders; team includes Eli Oftedal (Senior Analytics Researcher), Josh Pherigo (Senior Analytics Researcher), Jake Ledbetter (Senior Analytics Researcher, CFA), and Anjalika Komatireddy (Analytics Researcher) [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Reports that venture has evolved from "a mere cottage industry to a pillar of private markets and technological innovation," with funds now operating coast to coast, drawing on global capital, utilizing new structures, and blurring lines between venture, private equity, and private debt [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Reports US VC fundraising in H1 2025 at $28B against an $80B full-year outlook (2024 actual: $71B), as volatile tariff policies and inflationary headwinds led investors to wait on Fed and economic signals [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Reports AI accounts for 58 cents of every VC dollar deployed in 2025, with unprecedented fundings like the $40B OpenAI deal pushing investment totals higher while deals under $100M remained stubbornly low [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Reports companies graduating from one series to the next at the lowest rates in history, and when they do graduate it takes far longer than it used to [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Identifies a "new equilibrium" for VC-backed tech companies: revenue growth rates low and slow, profitability higher than in the past, period of cost cutting and austerity measures over, founders and CFOs tasked with staying the course and letting companies grow [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Reports 10 VC-backed tech IPOs on major US exchanges in H1 2025 (2024 actual: 7), with equity markets near all-time highs and the IPO window appearing to open [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Reports at least 59 of the top 100 highest-valued US unicorns have a foreign-born founder, collectively accounting for more than $1.5T of aggregate value, including the three highest-valued unicorns: OpenAI, SpaceX, and Stripe [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Reports AI companies are less efficient than prior-generation startups, with high burn multiples and low profit margins, even as the AI category sees revenue growth at 2-5X the rate of prior SaaS cohorts [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
