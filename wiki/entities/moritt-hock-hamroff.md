# Moritt Hock & Hamroff

## Summary

Moritt Hock & Hamroff is a law firm cited in Habitat Magazine's April 2026 Bricks & Bucks coverage of the Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac March 2026 GSE policy update. The firm's partner David Fitzhenry serves as the legal-side expert source in NYC-market industry coverage of the 15% reserve-funding floor and the retirement of Limited Review [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].

## Key facts

- Identified in Habitat Magazine's April 2026 coverage as the law firm whose partner David Fitzhenry serves as the legal-side expert source on the March 2026 GSE policy update [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
- Partner David Fitzhenry framed the 15% reserve-funding floor as "another sticker shock" for boards and characterized the 36-month reserve-study workaround as a time-limited mechanism [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
