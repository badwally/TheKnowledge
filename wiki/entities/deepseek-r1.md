# DeepSeek-R1

## Summary

Open-weight large language model released by DeepSeek in January 2025; reported as a near-frontier model whose efficient training and competitive benchmarks signaled that Chinese labs could reach the AI frontier despite U.S. semiconductor export controls [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].

## Key facts

- Released January 2025 by DeepSeek and described as efficient and near-frontier; Time Magazine named it one of the "Best Inventions of 2025" [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Its release signaled that Chinese labs could approach frontier performance despite U.S. export controls on advanced semiconductors [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Did not introduce the mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture, but popularized the use of large-scale MoE architectures in open frontier models [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Helped boost the popularity of chain-of-thought reasoning, in which models reason through intermediate steps before producing a final answer [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
