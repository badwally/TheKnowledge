# Catena-X

## Summary

Catena-X is positioned by its own association as the world's first end-to-end, collaborative and open data ecosystem for the automotive industry, connecting stakeholders along the entire value chain [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]]. Founded in 2021 as a non-commercial association (Catena-X Automotive Network e.V.), it provides neutral governance for standardised, interoperable and data-sovereign collaboration, and serves as a model for Manufacturing-X initiatives in other industries [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].

## Key facts

- Founded in 2021 [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Operates as a non-commercial association under the umbrella of the Catena-X Automotive Network e.V. [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Members include OEMs, suppliers, technology providers, and industry organisations such as ADAC, AIAG, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Renault, Volkswagen Group companies, Volvo, VDA, TÜV SÜD, Fraunhofer, Amazon Web Services, Huawei, Microsoft, and Magna [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Has hubs in the United States, China, Spain, Sweden and France, with more than 300 experts working across over 40 working groups [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Eight of the world's ten largest automotive suppliers are already exchanging data via the Catena-X ecosystem, and leading OEMs are increasingly mandating participation in new supplier contracts [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Co-launched the €23 million Data Space Accelerator with the International Data Spaces Association (IDSA) and Cofinity-X on 8 June 2026 to onboard SMEs into the data space [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Hanno Focken serves as Managing Director Activation, Governance and Operations [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Reports concrete operational impact: shared standards reduced due-diligence reporting effort by a factor of 100; a mid-sized supplier reports Product Carbon Footprint calculations performed 3–5× more efficiently, saving more than €10,000 per calculation; quality issues are detected an average of four months earlier through standardised data exchange [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- In one quality case study, an investigation into a critical electrical component that previously required inspection of 1.4 million vehicles was narrowed to just 14 via end-to-end data flows [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Josef Mitterhuber, CEO of Silver Atena, expects a cost reduction of 50–75% in Sustainability Rating for Customers through Catena-X [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
