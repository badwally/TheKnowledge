# PMG Holdings

## Summary

PMG Holdings is the wholly-owned subsidiary of Associa through which Associa executed its $85 million October 24, 2025 acquisition of Douglas Elliman's property management arm; per Douglas Elliman's SEC filing announcing the deal, PMG also entered a five-year licensing agreement with Elliman to continue using the Douglas Elliman brand as part of its property management services and maintains the pre-existing referral relationship between the Elliman brokerage and the acquired property management business [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]]. PMG Holdings is the operational vehicle through which Associa absorbed the Douglas Elliman Property Management business, framing the structural pattern by which Associa retains acquired property-management brands within Associa-controlled holding entities rather than rolling them directly under the Associa name [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

## Key facts

- Wholly-owned subsidiary of Associa [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Acquired all of the equity interest in Douglas Elliman Property Management for $85 million on October 24, 2025, announced via Douglas Elliman's SEC filing [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Entered a five-year licensing agreement with Douglas Elliman permitting PMG to continue using the Douglas Elliman brand as part of its property management services [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Maintains the pre-existing referral relationship between the Douglas Elliman brokerage and the acquired property management firm following the close [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- The PMG Holdings vehicle structure — wholly-owned Associa subsidiary acquiring an external property-management brand under a five-year brand-retention license — is the operative October 2025 primary-source evidence of Associa's M&A integration playbook: absorb property-management operations while preserving the seller's brand identity through licensed continuation, a structural pattern relevant to evaluating Associa as an acquirer target for an AI-native reserve-study firm under the condo-capital-infra ADR-0004 exit thesis [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
