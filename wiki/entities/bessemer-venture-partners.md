# Bessemer Venture Partners

## Summary

Bessemer Venture Partners is a global venture capital firm that backs entrepreneurs from their early days through every stage of growth, with more than 145 IPOs and 300 portfolio companies in the enterprise, consumer, and healthcare spaces [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. The firm has more than $19 billion of assets under management with investment teams located in Tel Aviv, Silicon Valley, San Francisco, New York, London, Hong Kong, Boston, and Bangalore [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. On July 9, 2025, Bessemer led MaintainX's $150 million Series D funding round at a $2.5 billion valuation; Partner Byron Deeter served as the firm's named relationship lead on the investment [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

## Key facts

### Firm profile

- More than 145 IPOs across its portfolio history [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- More than 300 portfolio companies in the enterprise, consumer, and healthcare spaces [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- More than $19 billion of assets under management [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Investment teams in Tel Aviv, Silicon Valley, San Francisco, New York, London, Hong Kong, Boston, and Bangalore [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Named portfolio companies include Pinterest, Shopify, Twilio, Yelp, LinkedIn, PagerDuty, DocuSign, Wix, Fiverr, Toast, and ServiceTitan [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Self-described origin: born from innovations in steel more than a century ago [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Publishes investment-decision retrospectives (Memos) and learns from missed investments (Anti-Portfolio) as part of its public-facing posture [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

### MaintainX Series D investment

- Led MaintainX's $150 million Series D announced July 9, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Investment priced MaintainX at a $2.5 billion post-money valuation [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Named relationship lead: Partner Byron Deeter [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Deeter's articulated thesis on the investment: MaintainX achieved remarkable product-market fit by addressing a critical challenge that affects virtually every physical asset-driven industry [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Deeter framed AI-powered insights as the differentiating intelligent layer transforming raw operational data into predictive recommendations [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
