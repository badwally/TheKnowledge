# Wei Li

## Summary

Global Chief Investment Strategist at the BlackRock Investment Institute; named contributor on the BII "2023 Global Outlook: A new investment playbook," which lays out the production-constraints thesis and a tactical framework for navigating a new regime of macro volatility [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].

## Key facts

- Listed title on the 2023 Global Outlook: Global Chief Investment Strategist, BlackRock Investment Institute [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
- Named as one of six contributors alongside Philipp Hildebrand, Jean Boivin, Alex Brazier, Vivek Paul, and Scott Thiel [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
