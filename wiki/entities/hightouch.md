# Hightouch

## Summary

Hightouch is an AI Decisioning vendor whose product sits on top of the customer's data warehouse and uses AI agents plus reinforcement learning to optimize content, timing, channels, and offers for 1:1 customer engagement at scale [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]]. It is a competitive-set entry for Orita and the publisher of the analysis comparing Aampe, Movable Ink, and OfferFit [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].

## Key facts

- Hightouch AI Decisioning is warehouse-native: customer data stays inside the customer's existing warehouse infrastructure rather than being moved into a third-party platform [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Architecture combines AI agents with reinforcement learning to make thousands of decisions per interaction that improve with every interaction [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Surfaces actionable analytics such as subject-line performance by region for human marketer reuse across strategies [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Positions warehouse-native architecture as a differentiator against Aampe, which requires ingest from a CDP because it is not warehouse-native [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Publishes vendor-comparison content on its blog covering Aampe, Movable Ink, and OfferFit as alternatives within the AI Decisioning category [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- The published comparison content is vendor-promotional (filter rationale flagged it as such); buyers should treat the framing of competitors as marketing positioning, not independent analysis [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
