# Marc Kennedy

## Summary

Marc Kennedy is a researcher at the Food and Environment Research Agency (Fera) who co-led the 'Bayesian analysis in microbial risk assessment' project that served as a principal spur for the development of the Sheffield Elicitation Framework (SHELF).

## Key facts

- Kennedy co-led the project 'Bayesian analysis in microbial risk assessment' at the Food and Environment Research Agency (Fera), jointly with Helen Clough at the University of Liverpool [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
- Discussions within this project provided the principal spur for the development of SHELF by Tony O'Hagan and Jeremy Oakley [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
