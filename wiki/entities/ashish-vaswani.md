# Ashish Vaswani

## Summary

AI researcher at Google Brain and lead author of "Attention Is All You Need" (NIPS 2017), the paper that introduced the Transformer architecture and established self-attention as the foundation for modern sequence transduction models [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Google Brain, with email avaswani@google.com on the paper [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
- Listed as an equal-contribution co-first author of "Attention Is All You Need" alongside Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, and Illia Polosukhin [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
- The paper's contribution footnote credits Vaswani, with Illia Polosukhin, as having "designed and implemented the first Transformer models" and as having been "crucially involved in every aspect of this work" [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
- The paper was presented at the 31st Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NIPS 2017), Long Beach, CA [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
