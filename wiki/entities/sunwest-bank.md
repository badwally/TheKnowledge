# Sunwest Bank

## Summary

Sunwest Bank is a privately held commercial bank founded in 1969 with over $3 billion in assets, headquartered in Sandy, UT, with operations in California, Arizona, Idaho, and Florida [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. Sunwest has been specializing in Community Associations for over 40 years and is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
