# JMI Equity

## Summary

JMI Equity is a growth equity software investor founded in 1992, based in the greater Washington, D.C. area and San Diego, with a focus exclusively on software companies [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. The firm has invested in more than 190 companies since inception, completed more than 120 exits, and facilitated 19 IPOs; as of June 30, 2025 its portfolio of cloud software companies represented $10 billion in combined revenue, $84 billion in aggregate enterprise value, and over 38,000 jobs [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. JMI is an existing minority investor in Vantaca and continued in the cap table alongside Cove Hill Partners after the October 15, 2025 $300+ million growth round that priced Vantaca at $1.25 billion [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].

- David Greenberg serves as JMI's named Partner-relationship lead on the Vantaca investment [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Greenberg characterized JMI's tenure with Vantaca as a period of tremendous growth and execution and indicated continued partnership as the company accelerates innovation and extends market share [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
