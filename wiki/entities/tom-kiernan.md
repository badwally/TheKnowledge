# Tom Kiernan

## Summary

Tom Kiernan is a CINC Systems board member as of July 22, 2024, identified publicly in CINC's leadership-additions press release as co-founder and former CEO of payments provider ClickPay [[sources/web-2024-07-22-f1f]]. Kiernan's board appointment coincides with the creation of a new Chief Payments Officer role at CINC (filled by former CPO Chris Millner), establishing a deliberate payments-industry reinforcement in CINC's governance and executive structure roughly six months after Hg's January 2024 closing of its strategic growth investment [[sources/web-2024-07-22-f1f]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]]. The combined Kiernan/Millner payments-domain signal is consistent with Hg's broader integrated-payments thesis surfaced in Hg's 19-year, $10B tax-and-accounting-software-segment franchise framing — making Kiernan's board appointment a load-bearing payments-industry data point in CINC's post-Hg governance configuration.

## Key facts

- Role at CINC Systems: board member, joined the board as part of CINC's July 22, 2024 leadership-build-out announcement [[sources/web-2024-07-22-f1f]].
- Prior role: co-founder and former CEO of ClickPay, a payments provider [[sources/web-2024-07-22-f1f]].
- Working relationship at CINC: identified in the July 2024 announcement as working closely with Chris Millner — CINC's newly-titled Chief Payments Officer (formerly Chief Product Officer) — on the integrated payments strategy [[sources/web-2024-07-22-f1f]].
- Strategic context: Kiernan's appointment coincides with CINC's framing of an industry-leading payment experience for consumers and management companies as a load-bearing product-strategy priority post-Hg-investment [[sources/web-2024-07-22-f1f]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- Kiernan's board addition reinforces the payments-industry signal in CINC's governance composition under Hg, consistent with Hg's broader tax-and-accounting-software-segment franchise framing and the integrated payments-and-banking heritage of CINC's original 2005 founding posture — strengthening the read that CINC's post-Hg roadmap will continue to lean into integrated payments as a primary growth vector, with implications for how an AI-native reserve-study tuck-under would integrate at the payments-and-billing layer rather than at the accounting platform alone [[sources/web-2024-07-22-f1f]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]].
