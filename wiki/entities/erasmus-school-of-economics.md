# Erasmus School of Economics

## Summary

Economics faculty at Erasmus University Rotterdam; institutional home of two of the four authors of the 2020 paper "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" — Guido Baltussen and Sten Lammers — and the listed corresponding-author institution for the work [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].

## Key facts

- Listed address: Burgemeester Oudlaan 50, Rotterdam 3000 DR, Netherlands [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- Affiliated with Guido Baltussen (corresponding author, baltussen@ese.eur.nl) and Sten Lammers on "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" (26 August 2020) [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
