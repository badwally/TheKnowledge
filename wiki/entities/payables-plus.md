# Payables+

## Summary

Payables+ is a CINC Systems accounts-payable product launched in October 2025 — a significant upgrade to CINC's previous AP solution — that leverages CINC's native Cephai+ AI technology to speed and simplify invoice ingestion, ledger coding, and digital payments for community association management (CAM) clients and their vendors [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. The launch was overseen by Alan Cohen, who joined CINC earlier in 2025 in the newly-created General Manager, Financial Services role and brings 30+ years of B2B payments expertise across fintech and banking [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]]. Payables+ is the load-bearing Q4-2025 product-launch evidence that CINC's near-term build-vs-buy roadmap is prioritizing payments / AP innovation under Cephai+ AI rather than capital-planning expansion, informing the condo-capital-infra acquirer-engagement timing model by surfacing payments as the current strategic priority rotation at the acquirer target [[sources/web-2025-10-14-ce0]].
