# Jason Delgado

## Summary

Jason Delgado is the named RISE Association Management Group executive across two consecutive 2025 CINC Systems product-launch announcements, framed as Managing Partner at RISE in the August 5, 2025 Cephai+ adoption announcement [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]] and as CEO of RISE Association Management Group in the September 16, 2025 CINC Connect launch announcement [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]] — establishing Delgado as the load-bearing named CAM-customer-executive whose public endorsements anchor CINC's product-validation framing across the Cephai+ and CINC Connect product lines. Delgado is the operational counterparty an acquirer-side outreach would reach for CINC-platform customer-validation diligence in the condo-capital-infra Year-3 exit window under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]] [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

## Key facts

- Managing Partner at RISE Management Group as of August 5, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- CEO of RISE Association Management Group as of September 16, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- The title transition from Managing Partner (August 2025) to CEO (September 2025) over a five-week window surfaces either (a) a title elevation at RISE during the August → September 2025 interval or (b) co-existing roles framed differently for each press release; both framings are consistent with Delgado being the same named RISE executive across the two announcements [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]] [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- RISE Association Management Group is based in Houston, Texas [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Surfaced as the named customer-reference voice in CINC Systems' August 5, 2025 PRNewswire announcement of the Cephai+ adoption milestone (100+ management companies signed up; 31,000+ invoices processed by early adopters) — establishing Delgado and RISE as part of the pre-broad-launch Cephai+ adopter cohort [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Surfaced as the named customer-reference voice in CINC Systems' September 16, 2025 PRNewswire announcement of the CINC Connect launch — establishing Delgado and RISE as part of the pre-launch CINC Connect adopter cohort with public-endorsement positioning [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Public framing of Cephai+ board-packet agent value (August 2025): "Before Cephai+, our managers spent hours compiling board packets — downloading reports, formatting them, and adding commentary. Now, our board packets are generated automatically, complete with AI-written executive summaries and agendas. We've seen our managers reclaim nearly a full week of work every month. This is more than automation — it's empowerment. We now have time to focus on what truly matters: our boards, our residents, and the health of our business" — surfacing the one-week-per-manager-per-month reclaimed-time metric as the load-bearing customer-productivity outcome [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Public framing of CINC Connect value proposition (September 2025): "CINC Connect is exactly what our residents have been asking for — one place to communicate, organize, and manage daily needs. The simplicity is remarkable. Residents update the app they already use and instantly get news feeds, group chat, and effortless amenity bookings. We expect stronger adoption, fewer calls, and a more connected community from day one" — surfacing both the resident-driven-demand narrative and the expected adoption-velocity outcome (stronger adoption, fewer calls, day-one engagement) [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Delgado's two consecutive CINC product-launch endorsements (Cephai+ board-packet automation in August 2025; CINC Connect resident engagement in September 2025) establish him as a CINC strategic-reference-account executive across CINC's product portfolio — strengthening the named-counterparty positioning for any acquirer-side CINC customer-validation diligence [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]] [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
