# Université Paris-Dauphine

## Summary

Paris-based university; institutional home of Eric Benhamou and Remy Belmonte, who co-taught the 2021 masterclass "Algorithmic and advanced Programming in Python" covering algorithm analysis, data structures, and algorithm design patterns, published on SSRN [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].

## Key facts

- Institutional affiliation for Eric Benhamou (eric.benhamou@dauphine.eu) and Remy Belmonte (remy.belmonte@dauphine.eu) on the algorithms masterclass [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
- The masterclass was evaluated with 30% project (due before December 22, 2021) and 70% written test (week of January 10, 2022), with a bonus of up to 20% for exceptional projects [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
