# Wim Hof Method (WHM)

## Summary

Cold-exposure-and-breath-work training framework originated by Wim Hof; combines deliberate cold exposure with a structured breathing protocol that, per Brass Monkey, advances the body's response by adding the respiratory system to the nervous, immune, endocrine, and cardiovascular systems already engaged by cold alone — and can temporarily alter biochemistry with or without cold exposure [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].

## Key facts

- Combines cold exposure with breath-work to engage the nervous, immune, endocrine, cardiovascular, and respiratory systems simultaneously [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Brass Monkey's founder describes the first WHM experience in late 2018 as "bloody awful" but recognizable as "right for me," suggesting an aversive-but-tolerable initiation [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Daniel Kluken is named as a key Wim Hof team member contributing to the body of WHM source material relied on by Brass Monkey [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- The breath-work component can temporarily alter biochemistry on its own — cold exposure is not strictly required to access some of the WHM effects [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Hosts in-person trainings in Poland that Brass Monkey's founder attended with Wim Hof in person [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
