# An Evaluation on the Time-Dependent Reliability of Reinforced Concrete Structures Considering Non-Stationary Resistance Degradation: A Comprehensive Gamma Process-Based Approach

## Summary

A 2025 paper by Conghe Jin, Tao Yang, Wangxi Xu, Yongjiu Qian, and Junhao Huang investigating the time-dependent reliability of reinforced concrete structures by modelling resistance deterioration with a Gamma process under non-stationary degradation and increasing load frequency and intensity.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Conghe Jin, Tao Yang, Wangxi Xu, Yongjiu Qian, and Junhao Huang and was published on 31 January 2025 with DOI 10.17531/ein/200284 [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- The motivating premise is that bridge resistance deteriorates over time, impacting safety and time-dependent reliability due to non-stationary degradation and increasing load frequency and intensity [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- The paper investigates the reliability of reinforced concrete structures, focusing on non-stationary degradation and increasing load frequency and intensity [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- A Gamma process is used to model resistance degradation in the proposed approach [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
