# Innovation Endeavors

## Summary

Venture capital firm that published "State of Foundation Models, 2025," a June 2025 industry survey authored by Davis Treybig covering frontier-model scaling, training economics, reasoning models, AI-native applications, and market structure [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].

## Key facts

- Published "State of Foundation Models, 2025" in June 2025, authored by Davis Treybig [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- The report organizes its analysis into seven sections: Setting the Stage; Models; Use Cases & Applications; Building Foundation Model Products; Market Structure & Dynamics; What's Next; and What We're Excited to See Built [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Headline framing claims include that "1 in 8 workers worldwide now uses AI every month, with 90% of that growth happening in just the last 6 months" and that AI-native applications are now in the billions of dollars of annual run-rate [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Argues that all technical metrics for foundation models continue to improve more than 10× year-over-year, including cost, intelligence, and context windows, and that the average duration of human task a model can reliably do is doubling every 7 months [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
