# Matthew J. Notowidigdo

## Summary

Economist in the Department of Economics at Northwestern University and NBER affiliate; co-author of NBER Working Paper No. 27734 "Who Profits From Amateurism? Rent-Sharing in Modern College Sports" (August 2020, revised October 2020), which estimates rent-sharing elasticities in NCAA college athletics using comprehensive revenue and expenses data for FBS schools between 2006 and 2019 [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].

## Key facts

- Institutional affiliation: Northwestern University, Department of Economics, 2001 Sheridan Road, Evanston, IL 60208-2600 [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- NBER affiliate [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Contact email: noto@northwestern.edu [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Co-author of "Who Profits From Amateurism?" with Craig Garthwaite, Jordan Keener, and Nicole F. Ozminkowski [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Hosts a data page for the research at http://users.nber.org/~notom/research/ncaa.html, with a separate data appendix available at http://www.nber.org/data-appendix/w27734 [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
