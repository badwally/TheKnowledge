# Black Crow AI

## Summary

Black Crow AI is an ecommerce AI platform that positions as a full-funnel predictive AI and purchase-intent tool for ecommerce growth, making it a direct competitor to Orita in the AI audience-intelligence segment serving Shopify direct-to-consumer brands [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]]. The Capterra catalog as of June 2026 describes Black Crow AI as a Shopify tool that uses machine learning to predict shopping behavior patterns in order to acquire and retain customers across digital marketing channels [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].

## Key facts

- Black Crow AI is built specifically for Shopify-powered, non-headless direct-to-consumer brands; the Capterra catalog explicitly excludes headless commerce setups and non-ecommerce verticals from its target ICP [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- The platform's stated value propositions are managing customer data, personalizing customer relationships across marketing channels, and growing email and SMS lists through onsite opt-ins [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Marketed benefits include growing email/SMS lists through more onsite opt-ins, increased abandonment email/SMS flows, and better personalization and customer-journey identification [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Three core feature claims are highlighted in the catalog summary: privacy-friendly tracking, customer tracking, and user recognition [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Capterra lists 99 catalog features for Black Crow AI spanning AI/ML, predictive analytics, real-time analytics, audience targeting, campaign management, attribution modeling, conversion tracking, personalization, customer journey mapping, cohort analysis, cross-channel attribution, behavior/behavioral analytics, customer segmentation, and dashboard/reporting/workflow-automation primitives [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Black Crow AI integrates with three named third-party platforms in the Capterra catalog: Attentive, Klaviyo, and Shopify Plus — i.e. its product shape is a predictive intelligence layer that augments existing messaging (Attentive, Klaviyo) and storefront (Shopify Plus) infrastructure rather than replacing it [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Training and onboarding options listed are live online training, webinars, and documentation [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Customer support channels listed are Email/Help Desk, a Knowledge Base, and Phone Support; Capterra notes no reviewer feedback about support is available in the catalog data, so support quality and response times cannot be assessed from user comments [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Pricing is gated behind vendor contact — no public price is disclosed on Capterra; a free trial is offered but there is no free version [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- As of the June 3, 2026 Capterra refresh, Black Crow AI carries 8 verified reviews with an aggregate 4.9 overall rating; sub-scores are Ease of Use 4.8, Features 4.9, Customer Service 4.5, Value for Money 4.4, and Likelihood to Recommend 4.5; the reviews-sentiment composite is reported as 6.3/10; the small review count (n=8) materially limits the inferential weight of these aggregates [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
- Capterra categorizes Black Crow AI under Machine Learning Software, AI Marketing Tools, and Artificial Intelligence Software [[sources/web-2026-06-15-ec8]].
