# Logic Tensor Networks

## Summary

A neuro-symbolic system (Badreddine et al. 2022) that allows neural training to be influenced by logical structure interpreted via fuzzy (or real-valued) truth of the labels; cited by Belle and Marcus (AAAI-26) alongside DeepProbLog as a representative example of neuro-symbolic programs [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].

## Key facts

- Logic Tensor Networks (Badreddine et al. 2022) allow neural training to be influenced by logical structure under a fuzzy (real-valued) interpretation of the truth of the labels [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- Logic Tensor Networks is named as a representative example in the "neuro-symbolic programs" line of research, alongside DeepProbLog [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- Logic Tensor Networks differs from DeepProbLog primarily in semantics: fuzzy / real-valued truth versus a probabilistic interpretation [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- The choice between fuzzy and probabilistic semantics for neuro-symbolic logical variables can impact the learned hypothesis and gradient computation (van Krieken, Acar, and van Harmelen 2022) [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
