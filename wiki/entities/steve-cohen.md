# Steve Cohen

## Summary

Founder of SAC Capital Management; attributes poker as "the biggest determinant in [his] learning to take risks," a quote cited by Lu, Mortal, and Ray (2020) as evidence that prominent hedge fund managers view poker skill as transferable to investing [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]]; per the 1998 foreword to Ari Kiev's "Trading to Win," grew SAC from a $20 million hedge fund to one handling over $500 million annually within five years, crediting a proactive trading-psychology program co-developed with psychiatrist Ari Kiev [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].

## Key facts

- Attributes poker as "the biggest determinant in [his] learning to take risks" [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Cited alongside David Einhorn by Lu, Mortal, and Ray (2020) as a fund manager who views poker skill as correlated with investment skill [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Founded and led SAC Capital Management [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per his foreword to "Trading to Win" (1998), grew SAC from a $20 million hedge fund to one handling over $500 million annually after only five years [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Implemented a trading-psychology program at SAC with weekly meetings run by psychiatrist Ari Kiev; per Cohen, some SAC traders' profits had gone up 100% since implementing the program [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Under the program, SAC's horizons expanded from equity trading alone into foreign exchange, convertibles, options, junk bonds, risk arbitrage, and systems trading [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
