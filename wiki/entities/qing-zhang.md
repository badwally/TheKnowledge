# Qing Zhang

## Summary

Mathematician at the Department of Mathematics, University of Georgia; co-author of the 2015 paper "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules," which proves the optimality of a trend-following all-in / all-out strategy in a bull-bear regime-switching market — and, in earlier work, of the contra-trend optimality result in mean-reverting markets cited in the same paper [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Department of Mathematics, University of Georgia (Athens, GA), on the paper [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- Co-author of "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules" with Min Dai, Zhou Yang, and Qiji Jim Zhu [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- Co-author (Zhang and Zhang) of an earlier result cited in the paper showing that the optimal trading strategy in a mean-reverting market is contra-trend — the complementary regime to the trend-following result of the present paper [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
