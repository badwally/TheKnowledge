# Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)

## Summary

The Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) is the Canadian federal private-sector privacy statute named in the ISED Voluntary Code of Conduct on Advanced Generative AI Systems as an existing legal obligation that the voluntary code does not modify [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].

## Key facts

- ISED's Voluntary Code of Conduct on Advanced Generative AI Systems explicitly states the code does not change organizations' existing legal obligations, citing PIPEDA as a worked example of preserved obligations [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a90]].
