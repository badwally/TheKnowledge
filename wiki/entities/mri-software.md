# MRI Software

## Summary

MRI Software is a real-estate technology vendor that markets a dedicated Condo and HOA management software product alongside its broader property-management platform [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]]. The condo/HOA solution is positioned as covering the full operational surface of community management — resident screening for sublets, collections and delinquency management, online and mobile e-payments for maintenance fees, automated resident communications, board communications, vote administration, and association documents — and is marketed under a balancing-act framing of managing properties, satisfying residents, and protecting profit margins [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]]. MRI's posture is that of a broad real-estate platform with a condo/HOA module, rather than a community-association-management (CAM) specialist [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].

## Key facts

### Product positioning

- Markets a dedicated Condo and HOA management software product at mrisoftware.com/solutions/condo-hoa-management-software/ [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Positions the product under the value proposition that managing properties, satisfying residents, and protecting profit margins is a difficult balancing act that the MRI solution addresses [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Frames the solution as spanning from initial resident screening for sublets through collections, delinquencies, and ongoing resident communications [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].

### Functional feature surface

- Resident screening for sublets [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Collections and delinquency management [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Online and mobile applications for e-payments to handle maintenance fees [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Automated online and mobile communications suite for managing and improving resident interactions across communities [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Integrated solutions for board communications [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Vote administration [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Association documents management [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Built-in reporting tools positioned as enabling decisions based on accurate, timely data [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Customizable dashboards and data visualization tools for a broader look at the business [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Stakeholder notification, revenue tracking, and operational-efficiency framing as the three named efficiency outcomes [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
- Condo Management sub-product surface includes automated allocations for revenue collection, simplified board management, and visibility into key unit information [[sources/web-2026-01-16-af3]].
