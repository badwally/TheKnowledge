# BayesWorks

## Summary

BayesWorks is a research-dissemination outlet that publishes presentations on Bayesian methods applied to civil-infrastructure deterioration modelling, including stochastic state-space models for bridge visual-inspection data and their extension from element level to network-scale analysis.

## Key facts

- BayesWorks publishes presentations on Bayesian deterioration modelling on its YouTube channel at https://www.youtube.com/@BayesWorks [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- BayesWorks published the presentation "Stochastic Model for Estimating Network-Scale Deterioration and Effect of Interventions on Bridges," delivered by a presenter introduced as Zachary [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- The presentation states that the underlying research has been recently published under the same title as the talk [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- BayesWorks presentations cite a connected line of prior published work by the same group covering element-level state-space deterioration modelling, kernel-regression extensions, and intervention-effect quantification [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
