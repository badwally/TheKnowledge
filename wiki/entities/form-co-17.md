# Form CO-17 — Quebec Corporation Income Tax Return

## Summary

Form CO-17 is the Revenu Québec corporate income tax return for taxable corporations, or for any corporation that claims a provincial tax credit or has taxable income to report in Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. Within the Québec corporate-return family, CO-17 is the taxable-corporation counterpart to CO-17.SP, which serves non-profit organizations (including most condominium syndicates) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. For fiscal years starting in 2024, electronic filing is mandatory in Québec for both CO-17 and CO-17.SP, with an exception only for registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Issued by Revenu Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Applies to taxable corporations or to any corporation that claims a provincial tax credit or has taxable income to report [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Distinguished from CO-17.SP: CO-17.SP applies to NPOs (including most condominium syndicates), while CO-17 applies to taxable corporations [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- For fiscal years starting in 2024, electronic filing of CO-17 is mandatory in Québec, with the only exception being registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
