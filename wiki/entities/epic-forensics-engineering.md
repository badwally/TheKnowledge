# Epic Forensics & Engineering, Inc.

## Summary

Epic Forensics & Engineering, Inc. is a named HomeRun IQ customer firm whose Professional Engineer Samie Hatim is featured in HomeRun IQ's published customer testimonials [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]]. The testimonial attributes a 20% reduction in man-hours per reserve study and an 80% net revenue increase in the firm's first year on the HomeRun IQ platform [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].

## Key facts

- Engineering firm performing reserve studies; named customer of HomeRun IQ [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Samie Hatim, P.E. is identified as a representative of Epic Forensics & Engineering, Inc. in the HomeRun IQ features-page testimonial copy [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Attributed quantified outcomes from HomeRun IQ adoption (per Hatim testimonial): 20% reduction in man-hours per study, 80% net revenue increase in the first year [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Hatim characterizes the man-hours reduction as the load-bearing driver of the revenue increase, rather than fee growth or volume growth in isolation [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
