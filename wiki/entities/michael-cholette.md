# Michael E. Cholette

## Summary

Michael E. Cholette is a co-author of the 2026 paper proposing a hierarchical Bayesian Homogeneous Poisson Process approach for rail surface defect prediction and inspection planning under limited maintenance data.

## Key facts

- Cholette is the third listed author of "Rail surface defect prediction and inspection planning using limited maintenance data – a hierarchical Bayesian approach," published 4 March 2026 with DOI 10.1080/23248378.2025.2508803 [[sources/web-2026-03-04-157]].
- The paper is co-authored with Saeed Khalaj, Huy Truong-Ba, Sinda Rebello, and Tommy H T Chan [[sources/web-2026-03-04-157]].
