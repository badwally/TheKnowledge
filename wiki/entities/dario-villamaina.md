# Dario Villamaina

## Summary

Quantitative researcher at Capital Fund Management and co-author of the 2024 paper "Ponzi Funds," which decomposes ETF returns into a self-inflated price-pressure component and a fundamental component using the ETF arbitrage mechanism as a laboratory [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Capital Fund Management on the paper [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
- Co-authored "Ponzi Funds" (May 22, 2024; arXiv:2405.12768) with Philippe van der Beck and Jean-Philippe Bouchaud [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
- The paper estimates that around 50% of the initial daily price impact from flow-induced trades reverts in the subsequent 5–10 days [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
