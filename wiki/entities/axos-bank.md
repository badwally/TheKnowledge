# Axos Bank

## Summary

Axos Bank is a fully digital nationwide FDIC-insured bank founded in 2000 and operating under parent company Axos Financial, Inc. (NYSE: AX), bolstered by approximately $19.8 billion in assets [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. Axos operates an HOA-banking practice that is integrated as a named banking partner in Vantaca's payment-processing and lockbox surface, marketed under the tagline "association management banking, evolved" [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].

## Key facts

- Founded in 2000 [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Fully digital nationwide FDIC-insured bank [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Parent company: Axos Financial, Inc. (NYSE: AX) [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Approximately $19.8 billion in assets [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Self-described as well-capitalized with a proven track record of steady growth [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Offers comprehensive financing for community associations and HOA management organizations [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Named banking partner in the Vantaca partner directory; integrates with Vantaca's payment processing surface [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Markets continuity with Vantaca's lockbox system: Vantaca clients adopting Axos do not need to switch away from Vantaca's lockbox [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Markets customizable and competitive money market rates for association deposits [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Markets a specialized HOA banking team with extensive experience serving community management professionals [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
