# Yan Lu

## Summary

Finance researcher at the University of Central Florida, College of Business; lead author of "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" (2020, SSRN id 3392130), which finds that hedge fund managers who win poker tournaments significantly outperform their non-poker-playing peers by approximately 10 basis points per month, but that the alpha signal is eroded by the investor inflows it generates [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Central Florida, College of Business, Box 161400, Orlando FL 32816 [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Contact email: yan.lu@ucf.edu; phone: +1-407-823-1237 [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Lead author of "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" (2020) with Sandra Mortal and Sugata Ray [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Co-author of Brown, Lu, Ray, and Teo (2018), cited in "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" for documenting the correlation between managers' hobbies, purchases, and indiscretions with excessive risk taking and fraud [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Co-author of Lu, Ray, and Teo (2016), cited in "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" for documenting the value-destroying distraction arising from fund managers' romantic relationships [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
