# Florida Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS)

## Summary

A Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) is a Florida-specific budget planning tool and type of reserve study required of condominium associations that examines the parts of a building a condominium association is required to maintain, the status of the condominium's existing reserve fund, and the amount of funding needed to offset anticipated expenses [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]]. A SIRS is purpose-built to ensure condo owners are reserving the funds needed for future major repairs and replacement of structural elements on the condominium property, and is enforced through a public reporting database operated by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) Division of Florida Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]]. The SIRS regime is anchored by three statutory deadlines: a 45-day post-completion electronic submission requirement effective July 1, 2024; an October 1, 2025 deadline for all condominium and cooperative associations to create an online account with the Division; and a December 31, 2025 deadline for completing the SIRS itself [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].

## Key facts

### Definition and purpose

- A SIRS is a budget planning tool that looks at the parts of a building a condominium association is required to maintain, the status of the condominium's existing reserve fund, and the amount of funding needed to offset anticipated expenses [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- A SIRS is a type of reserve study used to ensure condo owners are reserving the funds needed for future major repairs and replacement of structural elements on the condominium property [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].

### Eight required structural elements

A SIRS includes eight specific structural elements critical to the safety of the building [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]]:

1. Roof [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
2. Structural Systems [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
3. Fireproofing & Fire Protection Systems [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
4. Plumbing [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
5. Electrical Systems [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
6. Waterproofing & Exterior Painting [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
7. Windows & Exterior Doors [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
8. Other elements over $25,000 (or the adjusted reserve threshold posted on the Division's website) that have an impact on the structural integrity of the building as determined by the visual portion of the SIRS [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].

### Reserve threshold

- The $25,000 threshold for the "Other elements" category is an annually adjusted reserve threshold posted on the Division of Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes' website [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- The Division maintains a dedicated Reserve Threshold Page providing more information about the annually adjusted reserve threshold [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].

### Statutory submission deadlines

- As of July 1, 2024, within 45 days of the SIRS being completed, a condominium association must electronically submit a completed SIRS Reporting Form to the Division of Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- By October 1, 2025, all condominium and cooperative associations are required to create an online account with the Division of Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Associations are required to complete a Structural Integrity Reserve Study by December 31, 2025 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Submission of SIRS information is done through the association's online account in the Department's online account portal [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Associations confirm completion of the SIRS through the association's online account [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].

### Public SIRS Reporting Database

- Information in the SIRS Reporting Database is displayed exactly as submitted [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Only complete submissions of the SIRS Reporting Form are displayed in the databases [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- SIRS reported using the association's online account with DBPR are reflected in the database within one business day [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- The Division publishes two public submission lists: condominiums and cooperatives that submitted their SIRS prior to July 2025, and condominiums and cooperatives that submitted their SIRS in July 2025 or later [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].

### Administering authority

- Administered by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation, Division of Florida Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Division contact for SIRS or Milestone Inspections questions: (954) 202-6831 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Division email for SIRS Reporting Form questions: ctmheducation@myfloridalicense.com [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
