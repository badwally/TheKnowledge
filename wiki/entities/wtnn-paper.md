# WTNN: Weibull-Tailored Neural Networks for survival analysis

## Summary

WTNN is a 2025 arXiv preprint introducing a Weibull-Tailored Neural Network framework for survival analysis of systems under maintenance, designed to handle proxy indicators and right-censored observations through time-dependent covariates.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Gabrielle Rives, Olivier Lopez, and Nicolas Bousquet, posted on arXiv on 9 December 2025 with identifier 2512.09163 [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
- Its primary subject classification is stat.ML, with cross-listings in cs.LG, stat.AP, and stat.ME [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
- The work is motivated by the analysis of a fleet of military vehicles operating in highly variable and demanding environments, as well as by limitations observed in existing survival-modeling methodologies [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
- The paper introduces WTNN as a new neural-network-based modeling framework specifically designed for Weibull survival studies, claiming through numerical experiments that the approach can be reliably trained on proxy and right-censored data and produces robust, interpretable survival predictions that can improve on existing approaches [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
