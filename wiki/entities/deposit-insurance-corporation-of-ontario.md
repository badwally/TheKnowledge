# Deposit Insurance Corporation of Ontario (DICO)

## Summary

The Deposit Insurance Corporation of Ontario (DICO) is the Ontario provincial deposit insurer for credit union and caisse populaire deposits. DICO eligibility — alongside CDIC — is one of the two insurance criteria that qualify deposit-type instruments as "eligible securities" for an Ontario condominium corporation's reserve fund (and operating fund) under §115(5) of the Condominium Act, 1998. DICO provides $250,000 of coverage per member institution, contrasting with CDIC's $100,000 per-institution limit [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].

## Key facts

- Ontario provincial deposit insurer that insures most deposits in credit unions and caisses populaires in Ontario [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- DICO coverage is set at $250,000 per Deposit Insurance Corporation of Ontario member institution [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- §115(5) of the Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 (S.O. 1998, c. 19) qualifies as eligible securities those bonds, debentures, GICs, deposit receipts, certificates of deposit, term deposits, or similar instruments "issued by an institution located in Ontario insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation or the Deposit Insurance Corporation of Ontario" — DICO is one of the two insurance regimes whose coverage confers §115(5) eligibility [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The $250,000 DICO ceiling sits above the $100,000 CDIC per-member ceiling, materially expanding the per-counterparty placement capacity available to Ontario condominium reserve funds that use Ontario credit unions or caisses populaires [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
