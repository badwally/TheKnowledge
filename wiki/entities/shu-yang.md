# Shu Yang

## Summary

AI researcher at KAUST and corresponding lead author of the 2026 paper "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents," the first systematic study of LLM agents in multi-user, multi-principal settings [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with KAUST on the paper, with email shu.yang@kaust.edu.sa [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
- Listed as the first author and a corresponding author of "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents" (2026), alongside corresponding co-author Jiaxin Pei (Stanford) [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
- Co-authors include Shenzhe Zhu (University of Toronto), Hao Zhu (Stanford), José Ramón Enríquez (Stanford), Di Wang (KAUST), Alex Pentland (Stanford and MIT), and Michiel A. Bakker (MIT) [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
