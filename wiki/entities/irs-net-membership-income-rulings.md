# IRS Revenue Rulings 75-370 and 75-371 (Net Membership Income Determination)

## Summary

IRS Revenue Rulings 75-370 and 75-371 are the companion federal tax rulings that govern the determination of net membership income for non-exempt membership organizations subject to IRC §277, including homeowners associations and condominium associations filing Form 1120 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]]. Failure by tax preparers to consider the 75-370/75-371 framework is identified by Gary Porter, CPA as a major source of post-audit surprise tax liability — many associations discovered during IRS audit that they had net membership income they did not know existed [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].

## Key facts

- Two federal IRS revenue rulings (75-370 and 75-371) that must be applied in determining net membership income for an IRC §277 non-exempt membership organization [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Failure to consider these rulings alongside Rev. Rul. 70-604 is identified as a major failure pattern for tax preparers serving the HOA industry [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- The practical consequence of failing to apply 75-370 / 75-371: associations discover during IRS audit that they had net membership income they did not know existed [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Combined with the failures around Rev. Rul. 70-604, these omissions effectively mean no proper election under Rev. Rul. 70-604 was ever made [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
