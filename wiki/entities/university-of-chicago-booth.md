# University of Chicago Booth School of Business

## Summary

Business school at the University of Chicago; institutional home of all three authors of the 2024 paper "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models" — Alex G. Kim, Maximilian Muhn, and Valeri V. Nikolaev — and supporter of the underlying research through several internal centers [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]]; the institutional home of Dacheng Xiu, co-author with Bryan Kelly (Yale, AQR, NBER) of the 2023 SSRN survey "Financial Machine Learning" [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]]; and the institutional home of Tobias Moskowitz, lead author with Yao Hua Ooi and Lasse H. Pedersen of the 2011 working paper "Time Series Momentum," which documents significant return predictability from a security's own past returns across 58 liquid futures instruments [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the institutional affiliation for Alex G. Kim, Maximilian Muhn, and Valeri V. Nikolaev on "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models" [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- The University of Chicago Research Support Center, the Fama-Miller Center for Finance Research, and the Stevens Doctoral Program at Chicago Booth provided financial support for the research [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- Workshop participants from the University of Chicago provided comments on the paper [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- Listed as the institutional affiliation for Dacheng Xiu on "Financial Machine Learning" (Kelly and Xiu, 2023, SSRN id 4501707) [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
- Xiu's contact email on the paper resolves to the school via dacheng.xiu@chicagobooth.edu [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
- Listed as the institutional affiliation for Tobias Moskowitz on "Time Series Momentum" (Working Paper No. 79, Chicago Booth Paper No. 12-21, September 2011; SSRN abstract id 2089463) [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
- The Initiative on Global Markets at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business provided financial support for the time series momentum research, alongside CRSP [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
- The time series momentum paper was published under the Initiative on Global Markets banner, described as "Providing thought leadership on international business, financial markets and public policy" [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
