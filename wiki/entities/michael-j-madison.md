# Michael J. Madison

## Summary

Michael J. Madison is a legal scholar and co-author of "Constructing Commons in the Cultural Environment," a 2010 article in the Cornell Law Review that, together with co-authors Brett M. Frischmann and Katherine J. Strandburg, lays the foundation for the Governing Knowledge Commons (GKC) framework — an adaptation of Elinor Ostrom's commons-governance work to information, data, and other cultural-environment resources [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].

## Key facts

- Co-authored "Constructing Commons in the Cultural Environment," 95 Cornell L. Rev. 657 (2010), with Brett M. Frischmann and Katherine J. Strandburg [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].
- The article appeared in volume 95, issue 4 of the Cornell Law Review [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].
- Through this article he is one of three canonical scholars associated with the Governing Knowledge Commons (GKC) framework [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].
