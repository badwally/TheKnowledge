# IntraFi

## Summary

IntraFi operates a network of US member banks that powers two named coverage-maximization products marketed to homeowners associations: the Certificate of Deposit Account Registry Service (CDARS) and the IntraFi Cash Service (ICS) [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]]. Both products allow an HOA to deposit funds in excess of the FDIC $250,000 per-depositor / per-bank / per-ownership-category limit while preserving full FDIC insurance, by distributing the HOA's funds across multiple member banks under a single banking relationship [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]]. CDARS distributes funds as CDs; ICS distributes funds into Demand Deposit Accounts placed into interest-bearing savings accounts at network banks [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].

## Key facts

- Operates a network of US member banks supporting two named products: CDARS and ICS [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Both products allow HOAs to access FDIC insurance on deposits exceeding the $250,000 per-depositor/per-bank/per-ownership-category limit [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Both products allow an HOA to maintain a single banking relationship while funds are distributed across multiple member banks for FDIC-coverage purposes [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- The two products differ in the underlying deposit instrument: CDARS uses certificates of deposit (CDs), while ICS uses Demand Deposit Accounts (DDAs) placed into savings accounts [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Marketed as multi-million-dollar deposit-capable while ensuring no single member-bank balance exceeds the FDIC per-bank limit for the HOA [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
