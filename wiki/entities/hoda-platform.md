# HODA™ Platform

## Summary

HODA™ is a named platform that integrates with FirstService Residential's cyan™ all-in-one association management platform; the cyan↔HODA integration is positioned in FirstService's marketing copy as the architectural component that makes cyan a one-of-a-kind technology available only to FirstService Residential associates and customers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]]. The HODA™ platform's specific function, vendor, and architectural detail are not disclosed in the source-of-record [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].

## Key facts

- Named platform that integrates with FirstService Residential's cyan™ platform [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- The cyan↔HODA integration is the load-bearing architectural component that FirstService cites as making cyan a one-of-a-kind technology available only to FirstService Residential associates and customers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Trademark notation suggests HODA is a named/branded platform (TM symbol attached) but the source does not disclose whether HODA is a FirstService-owned product, a sister-brand product within the FirstService corporate family, or a third-party platform white-labeled or partnered into the cyan stack [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- The source-of-record provides no functional scope detail (resident engagement vs. accounting vs. workflow vs. analytics), no vendor identification, no architectural detail, and no pricing or customer-count detail for HODA [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
