# Dan M Frangopol

## Summary

Dan M Frangopol is a structural reliability and life-cycle engineering researcher, co-author of the 2026 review paper on life-cycle performance of asphalt pavement under uncertainty, and a co-author of two articles in the March 2025 JCSS-themed special issue of *Structural Safety*.

## Key facts

- Frangopol is the second listed author of 'Life-cycle performance, design, maintenance, optimization, and decision-making of asphalt pavement under uncertainty: a review,' published on 1 February 2026 with DOI 10.1080/15732479.2025.2552239 [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper is co-authored with Jiyu Xin, Mitsuyoshi Akiyama, Lianzhen Zhang, and Jianzhong Pei [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- Frangopol is a co-author of "Moving toward resilience and sustainability in the built environment" (article 102449) in the March 2025 JCSS special issue of *Structural Safety*, led by Sanchez-Silva [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Frangopol is a co-author of "On information value and decision analyses" (article 102481) in the same issue, led by Thöns [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
