# Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ)

## Summary

The Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ) is the Québec business registrar that maintains the official registry of legal entities — including condominium syndicates — operating in Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. Before a syndicate can file the CO-17.SP, it must be registered with the REQ; if the syndicate does not appear in the REQ database, registration must be completed online or via the notary who prepared the declaration of co-ownership [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. The REQ assigns each registered entity a Québec Enterprise Number (NEQ); a syndicate registered as a condominium entity has an NEQ starting with "11" [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. The REQ also collects an annual registration fee that is integrated with the CO-17.SP filing flow via box 39 of the form [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Québec business registrar maintaining the registry of legal entities, including condominium syndicates [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Assigns a Québec Enterprise Number (NEQ) to each registered entity; a registered condominium syndicate has an NEQ starting with "11" [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- A syndicate must be registered with the REQ before completing the CO-17.SP [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- If a syndicate does not appear in the REQ database, registration can be completed online or by contacting the notary who prepared the declaration of co-ownership [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Requires an Annual Updating Declaration from each registered organization [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- If a syndicate's CO-17.SP is filed within six months following the fiscal year-end and "YES" is checked in box 39, the Annual Updating Declaration is automatically considered completed — though the REQ annual fee still applies [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Proper REQ registration ensures the CO-17.SP return can be processed within the prescribed time frame and associated with the correct legal entity [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Failure to file the CO-17.SP can result in delayed REQ record updates and obstacles during property transactions or resale of condo units [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
