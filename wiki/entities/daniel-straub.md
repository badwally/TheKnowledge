# Daniel Straub

## Summary

Daniel Straub is a structural reliability researcher and the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2022/2023 paper comparing off-line and on-line Bayesian filters for uncertainty quantification of structural deterioration model parameters, and a frequent contributor to the March 2025 JCSS-themed special issue of *Structural Safety*.

## Key facts

- Straub is the fifth (final) listed author of 'On off-line and on-line Bayesian filtering for uncertainty quantification of structural deterioration' [[sources/arxiv-2205.03478]].
- The paper was posted as arXiv 2205.03478 on 6 May 2022 and published in *Data-Centric Engineering*, volume 4, 2023, article e17 (DOI 10.1017/dce.2023.13) [[sources/arxiv-2205.03478]].
- The paper is co-authored with Antonios Kamariotis, Luca Sardi, Iason Papaioannou, and Eleni Chatzi [[sources/arxiv-2205.03478]].
- Straub is a co-author of "Development of methods of structural reliability" (article 102474) in the March 2025 JCSS special issue of *Structural Safety*, led by Ellingwood [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Straub is a co-author of "Interpretation of probability in structural safety – A philosophical conundrum" (article 102473) in the same issue, led by Vrouwenvelder [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Straub is a co-author of "Models and methods for probabilistic safety assessment of steel structures subject to fatigue" (article 102446) in the same issue, led by Maljaars [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Straub is a co-author of "Moving toward resilience and sustainability in the built environment" (article 102449) in the same issue, led by Sanchez-Silva [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Straub is a co-author of "On information value and decision analyses" (article 102481) in the same issue, led by Thöns [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
