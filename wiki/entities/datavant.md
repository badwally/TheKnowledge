# Datavant

## Summary

Datavant is a private-sector partner that contributed privacy-preserving record linkage (PRL) technology to the NAIRR Secure NIH component of the NAIRR pilot and to elements of the future NAIRR software stack [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].

## Key facts

- Datavant contributed $2.6 million in the form of access to its privacy-preserving record linkage platform, privacy-preserving data discovery tools, and staff expertise to NAIRR [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- Datavant's contribution supports the NAIRR Secure NIH component as well as elements of the future NAIRR software stack [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
