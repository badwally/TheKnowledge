# Simon AI

## Summary

Simon AI (the Capterra listing title is "Simon Data (Simon AI)") is a composable customer data platform combined with agentic AI, positioned to let marketing teams deliver 1:1 personalization across every customer touchpoint by starting from a marketer-stated goal — such as reducing churn or driving repeat purchases — and letting intelligent agents handle execution [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]]. The vendor markets the platform as uncovering hidden signals, activating "100x more customer and contextual data," and automating execution across engagement channels so that small marketing teams can perform like much larger ones [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]]. Capterra cites ASOS and SeatGeek among Simon AI's reference customers [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].

## Key facts

- Simon AI is categorized on Capterra as Customer Data Platform and Marketing Automation software [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- Simon AI is positioned for businesses of all sizes — from small companies to enterprise teams — that need to use customer data for multi-channel marketing testing [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- Simon AI's Capterra profile lists 20 features, including campaign management, multi-channel marketing, drip campaigns, customer profiles, customer segmentation, behavioral analytics, personalization, dynamic content, A/B testing, multivariate testing, predictive analytics, predictive modeling, ROI tracking, search marketing, social marketing, and website visitor tracking [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- Simon AI lists 9 third-party integrations on its Capterra profile: Shopify, Snowflake, Oracle Commerce, Braze, Iterable, Attentive, Movable Ink, Lob, and Lobbipad [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- Simon AI does not publish a starting price on Capterra ("Get Price" gated), does not offer a free trial, and routes prospects through a demo [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- As of the April 13, 2026 Capterra update, Simon AI carried zero verified user reviews on Capterra, yielding a 0.0 aggregate rating across overall, ease of use, features, customer service, value for money, and likelihood to recommend [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- Simon AI offers in-person training, live online sessions, and written documentation for customer onboarding [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- Listed support channels are email/help desk, FAQ/forum, and a knowledge base; no phone support is listed [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
- Capterra's "compare with a popular alternative" widget pairs Simon AI against Brevo as the featured top alternative, and lists Omnisend, HubSpot Marketing Hub, Customer.io, and Xtremepush among the highest-rated alternatives in the category [[sources/web-2026-06-15-f6f]].
