# CINC Connect

## Summary

CINC Connect is the resident portal and mobile app platform launched by CINC Systems on September 16, 2025 at the company's annual CINC Up client conference in Austin, Texas, framed by the company as redefining the resident experience for HOAs and condominium associations and forming — alongside the Cephai+ AI suite — one of "the two essential engines to move community association management forward" [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]]. The product launched in three tiers (Essential, Lifestyle, Premium), with upgrades beginning in Q1 2026 and the Essential tier included at no additional cost for communities already using CINC's homeowner app — operationalizing CINC's commercialization of the resident-engagement-platform capability acquired with ONR Applications earlier in 2025 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]]. CINC Connect is the load-bearing primary-source evidence that CINC's post-ONR integration product surface is non-probabilistic — its capability stack covers communications, amenity bookings, surveys, package tracking, and access management rather than reserve-study methodology, component deterioration modeling, or capital-renewal forecasting [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

## Key facts

### Launch and venue

- Launched September 16, 2025 by CINC Systems at the company's annual CINC Up client conference in Austin, Texas; the conference drew more than 650 attendees for networking, learning, and product innovation [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Public announcement issued via PRNewswire dated September 16, 2025 from Duluth, GA (CINC Systems' headquarters location surfaced in the dateline) [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Product positioning

- Self-described as a "new resident portal and mobile app platform that modernizes community living and streamlines community association management" [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Framed at launch as redefining the resident experience for HOAs and condominium associations, offering tools that boost engagement while reducing management workload [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Native integration with CINC's community association management platform — i.e., CINC Connect is positioned as the resident-facing extension of CINC's existing CAM SaaS rather than as a standalone product [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Feature stack (across tiers)

- Customizable community news feeds and group chat that management companies can tailor to their residents' needs, creating the right balance of communication and choice [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Amenity reservations for clubhouses, tennis courts, pools, and more [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Surveys, polls, and voting tools to increase resident involvement [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Package delivery tracking for seamless notifications [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Communications Center

- At the core of the platform sits a new Communications Center that streamlines outreach from managers to communities [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Managers can send real-time or scheduled emails, text messages, and in-app notifications, ensuring critical updates and reminders reach residents through the channels they prefer [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Three-tier pricing architecture

- **Essential** — included at no extra cost with CINC's Enterprise Package; available to communities already using CINC's homeowner app; represents a significant upgrade from the current app and WebAxis; includes enhanced board reporting, a modern communications center, customizable news feeds, and group chat [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- **Lifestyle** — designed for "the rapidly growing number of lifestyle communities across the U.S."; delivers new resident-engagement features such as e-voting, parking management, resident surveys, pet registries, and e-signatures [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- **Premium** — built for highly amenitized, large-scale and vertical communities; adds advanced capabilities such as access management and package tracking [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Rollout timeline

- Upgrades will begin in Q1 2026 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- For communities already using the CINC resident app, the Essential tier is included at no additional cost; clients will need to sign up for the upgrade, with sign-up beginning in January 2026 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Customers interested in exploring Lifestyle and Premium tiers can book a meeting with their CINC rep [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- When a management company completes its upgrade, the apps its residents use will automatically update and upgrade to the new version [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Two-pillar strategic positioning

- CINC frames its platform strategy as "Two Core Pillars: AI + Resident Experience" — Cephai+ delivering AI Automation (intelligent agents for back-office tasks including invoice coding, EFT reconciliation, budget creation, board packet preparation, and violation tracking) and CINC Connect delivering Resident Engagement (digital tool adoption, reduced phone calls, stronger community bonds, modern communication tools) [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- Ryan Davis, CEO of CINC Systems, framed the two-engine architecture at launch: "You can't have just one engine — it takes both. AI alone won't drive adoption, and engagement without automation creates inefficiency. By bringing them together, Cephai+ and CINC Connect create unmatched efficiency and community connection — empowering managers to scale, boards to govern effectively, and residents to thrive" [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- The September 2025 launch operationalizes CINC's commercialization of the resident-engagement capability acquired via the January 2025 ONR Applications acquisition, which the company described in the September 2025 announcement as having "since been fully integrated into CINC's platform" [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Customer reference

- Jason Delgado, CEO of RISE Association Management Group (Houston), provided the named customer endorsement: "CINC Connect is exactly what our residents have been asking for — one place to communicate, organize, and manage daily needs. The simplicity is remarkable. Residents update the app they already use and instantly get news feeds, group chat, and effortless amenity bookings. We expect stronger adoption, fewer calls, and a more connected community from day one" [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### CINC corporate scale at launch (September 2025 snapshot)

- CINC Systems is described in the announcement as "the nation's leading provider of community association management software to more than 6 million doors" [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- CINC's single, integrated, AI-powered platform was framed as powering more than 50,000 communities and more than 6 million doors at the September 2025 launch — a useful mid-period scale snapshot between the June 2020 Spectrum-Equity-anchor framing (~16K HOAs / 2M homes / 26 states) and the April 2026 Cephai+ framing (55,000+ associations / $25B payments processed / 6M doors per project memory) [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- CINC remains backed by Hg Capital and Spectrum Equity per the September 2025 boilerplate, confirming the dual-sponsor governance structure continuing through the CINC Connect launch [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra GTM

- CINC Connect's feature scope — communications, amenity bookings, surveys, package tracking, access management — confirms that CINC's post-ONR product surface is non-probabilistic and resident-engagement-focused rather than reserve-study-methodology-focused; the load-bearing strategic implication for Longspan is that an AI-native reserve-study firm should not build a resident portal as a competitive feature, since the resident-engagement gap that ONR was acquired to fill is now closed under CINC Connect [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- The CINC Up annual client conference (650+ attendees in Austin, Texas in September 2025) is documented as CINC's primary product-launch venue, surfacing the conference as a recurring acquirer-side scale-and-roadmap signal worth monitoring for the Year-3 exit-window engagement model under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
- CINC's continued dual-sponsor (Hg + Spectrum) governance through the September 2025 CINC Connect launch reinforces the structural continuity of the acquirer-thesis governance assumption baked into the condo-capital-infra exit thesis [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
