# Stephen Figlewski

## Summary

Finance researcher cited by Allan Malz (2014) for the intuitive put-price formulation of the relationship between option prices and risk-neutral probability distributions, and acknowledged for comments on the FRBNY Staff Report No. 677 "A Simple and Reliable Way to Compute Option-Based Risk-Neutral Distributions" [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].

## Key facts

- Acknowledged by Malz alongside Sirio Aramonte, Bhupinder Bahra, Benson Durham, Will Melick, Carlo Rosa, Joshua Rosenberg, Ernst Schaumburg, and seminar participants at the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System for comments on the paper [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Author of Figlewski (2010), which Malz cites as providing intuition for the put-price-schedule statement of the relationship between option prices and risk-neutral probabilities — that as the exercise price varies from low to high, the put value's slope and value move from near zero to near intrinsic value with slope erτ [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Per Malz, the Figlewski (2010) intuition justifies the discrete approximation Π̃(X+Δ) ≈ (1/Δ) e^(rτ) [p(t,τ,X+Δ) − p(t,τ,X)], where the cumulative risk-neutral probability is recovered from differences in put prices across exercise prices [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
