# TrackandFix

## Summary

TrackandFix is a web and mobile inspection and after-sales follow-up tool that is integrated with the UpperBee property management platform as the named inspections-and-follow-ups partner [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]]. The tool is independently integrated with Autodesk BIM360 Field and Procore, positioning it as a construction- and delivery-side issue management product that delivery and after-sales teams can use to assign corrective tasks in real time during inspections, eliminating the need for manual report entry [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Web-based tool for PC and mobile platforms, designed to streamline issue identification, organization, assignment, and resolution across multiple projects in real time [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Integrated with the UpperBee property management platform as UpperBee's named inspections-and-follow-ups partner [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Independently integrated with Autodesk BIM360 Field [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Independently integrated with Procore [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Use case: delivery and after-sales teams assign corrective tasks in real time during inspections, eliminating manual report entry [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Marketed outcomes: improved communication, faster corrective work, increased customer satisfaction [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Public-facing site: trackandfix.com [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
