# Byron Deeter

## Summary

Byron Deeter is a Partner at Bessemer Venture Partners and the named relationship lead on Bessemer's lead investment in MaintainX's $150 million Series D round announced July 9, 2025 at a $2.5 billion valuation [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

## Key facts

- Title: Partner at Bessemer Venture Partners [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Named as Bessemer's relationship lead on the MaintainX Series D investment [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Articulated his investment thesis on MaintainX: "MaintainX has achieved remarkable product-market fit by addressing a critical challenge that affects virtually every physical asset-driven industry" [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Identified customer feedback as a load-bearing diligence signal: "What impressed us most was the overwhelmingly positive feedback from customers who have transformed their maintenance operations using the platform" [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Framed AI-powered insights as the differentiating intelligent layer transforming raw operational data into predictive recommendations that drive value creation [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Expressed confidence in Chris Turlica and MaintainX's ability to lead the global digital transformation of maintenance and asset management [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
