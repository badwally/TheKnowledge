# Eclipse Community Management

## Summary

Eclipse Community Management is a community-association management (CAM) firm operating in Ohio and Northern Kentucky that provides HOA and condo management services to condominium associations, including reserve planning, budget analysis, insurance coordination, lender questionnaire completion, and documentation practices [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]]. Eclipse publishes board-facing implementation guidance on federal GSE policy developments — translating Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 and Freddie Mac Bulletin 2026-C into board-level talking points — making it a representative of the regional-CAM tier of vendors operating in the GSE-policy implementation channel [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].

## Key facts

- Operates as a community-association management (CAM) firm [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Service territory covers Ohio and Northern Kentucky [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Services include reserve planning, budget analysis, insurance coordination, lender questionnaire completion, and documentation practices [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Positions itself as interfacing directly with lenders to provide GSE-required association documentation on the association's behalf during Full Review transactions [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Publishes board-facing GSE-policy explainers; the April 15, 2026 article "2026 Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac Condo Requirements: What Boards Need to Know" was authored by Chris Vecchi, MPA, CMCA, AMS, PCAM and published at eclipsecommunities.com/2026-fannie-freddie-condo-requirements/ [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Recommended that proactive boards (a) review what percentage of annual assessment income currently goes to reserves and budget for an adjustment if below 15% without a qualifying reserve study, (b) commission or update a reserve study if older than three years, and (c) have insurance reviewed by a professional familiar with the new GSE requirements ahead of the July 1, 2026 deductible-cap effective date [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
