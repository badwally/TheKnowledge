# José Guilherme Porto Oliveira

## Summary

José Guilherme Porto Oliveira is a D.Sc. researcher in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at PUC-Rio, and the first-listed (corresponding) author of the 2025 ITcon paper proposing an integrated BIM/SHM/ANN framework for viaduct management.

## Key facts

- Porto Oliveira is the first listed and corresponding author of 'Infrastructure management via BIM model: integration of structural health monitoring and ANN-based damage assessment,' published in *Journal of Information Technology in Construction* (ITcon), Vol. 30, pp. 1637-1658, October 2025 (DOI 10.36680/j.itcon.2025.067) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- He holds a D.Sc. and is identified as a Researcher in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at PUC-Rio [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- His ORCID identifier is 0000-0002-8571-7216 [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- His corresponding email address is joseguilhermeporto@gmail.com [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The paper is co-authored with Elisa Dominguez Sotelino [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
