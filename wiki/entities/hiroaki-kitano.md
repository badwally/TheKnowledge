# Hiroaki Kitano

## Summary

Leader of Sony Computer Science Laboratories and AI expert who advised Japan's LDP Project Team on the Evolution and Implementation of AI during preparation of the April 2023 AI Whitepaper [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key facts

- Serves as Director of Sony Computer Science Laboratories per the full whitepaper [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]]; the summary version refers to him as President and CEO of Sony Computer Science Lab [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Spoke at the 5th LDP project team hearing on March 14, 2023, describing AI as "an invention equivalent to the internal combustion engine, the semiconductor, and the Internet ... happening before our eyes at an explosive rate" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Quoted in the whitepaper summary describing the moment as "something that rivals the invention of the engine, semiconductor, and the Internet ... happening at an explosive pace" [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
