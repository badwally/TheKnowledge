# Maximilian Muhn

## Summary

Researcher at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business; co-author of the 2024 paper "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models," which demonstrates that GPT-4 Turbo with chain-of-thought prompting can predict the direction of next-period earnings more accurately than the median financial analyst, using only anonymized balance sheets and income statements [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Chicago Booth School of Business; contact email maximilian.muhn@chicagobooth.edu [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- Co-author of "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models" (May 20, 2024 draft) with Alex G. Kim and Valeri V. Nikolaev [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- The paper's headline finding is that GPT-4 with chain-of-thought prompting achieves 60% direction-of-earnings prediction accuracy on the Compustat universe, exceeding human analyst forecasts and on par with a specialized ANN trained on 59 financial-statement predictors [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- Establishes that LLM prediction does not stem from training memory; instead the model generates useful narrative insights about a company's future performance [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
