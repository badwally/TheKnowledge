# ISO 50001 — Energy Management Systems Standard

## Summary

ISO 50001 is the international Energy Management Systems standard cited by BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB question E2.0 as the guidance framework (or equivalent) that condominium and other multi-unit residential associations should follow when developing an Energy Management Plan [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key facts

- BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E2.0 — Energy Management Plan instructs applicants to "Follow ISO-50001 guidance, or equivalent" when building the foundation for an energy and carbon reduction program [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E2.0 References list points to Natural Resources Canada's ISO 50001 overview page and to the CSA Group store entry for CSA ISO 50001:19 as authoritative sources for the standard [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
