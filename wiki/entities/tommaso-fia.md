# Tommaso Fia

## Summary

Tommaso Fia is a legal scholar working on EU data law, data commons, and collective data governance, and a co-author of the 2025 Technology and Regulation article "Through thick and thin: data commons, community and the struggle for collective data governance" with Gijs van Maanen [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].

## Key facts

- Co-authored the 2025 Technology and Regulation paper that interrogates the under-analysed role of community as a social institution in data-commons scholarship and policy [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].
- Argues, jointly with van Maanen, that the moral-political affordances of the community grounding a data commons carry concrete implications for designing collective-data-governance policy [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].
- The 2025 article frames data commons as one of a class of collective data-governance mechanisms positioned against both individualistic data protection and data markets [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].
