# T2inc.ca

## Summary

T2inc.ca is a Québec-based corporate tax service firm that specializes in corporate tax return preparation for small businesses and condo syndicates, offering an online solution for managing CO-17, CO-17.SP, and federal T2 filings [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. T2inc.ca publishes practitioner-facing guides on Québec corporate tax obligations, including a November 6, 2025 guide on the CO-17.SP filing requirement for condominium syndicates authored by Frédéric Roy-Gobeil, CPA [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Québec-based corporate tax service firm [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Specializes in corporate tax return preparation including the federal T2 and the Québec CO-17 and CO-17.SP returns [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Markets an affordable online solution designed for small businesses and condo syndicates [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Handles e-filing procedures for clients and supports compliance at both federal and provincial levels [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Publishes practitioner-facing guides on Québec corporate tax topics; the November 6, 2025 CO-17.SP guide was authored by Frédéric Roy-Gobeil, CPA [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Also publishes a detailed T2 Form Guide for Canadian Corporations referenced from the CO-17.SP guide [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Markets itself as committed to helping business owners manage their company's tax affairs so they can grow their business [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
