# Wealthsimple Business chequing account

## Summary

The Wealthsimple Business chequing account is an interest-earning, no-monthly-fee CAD chequing account offered by Wealthsimple to incorporated Canadian businesses, with feature parity to Wealthsimple's personal chequing accounts plus business-specific functionality including CRA payment rails [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]]. The product is structurally inaccessible to strata or condominium corporations: Wealthsimple's published eligibility regime enumerates seven criteria for opening a Business chequing account, one of which categorically excludes "a strata or condominium corporation" — making Wealthsimple Business the only documented Canadian fintech business-banking product with an explicit, primary-source-confirmed condo/strata categorical exclusion [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]]. This eligibility exclusion is independently verifiable from Wealthsimple's own customer-facing support documentation and is architecture-determinative for any Canadian multi-institution sweep-deposit analog evaluation.

## Key facts

### Product positioning

- Interest-earning chequing account with no monthly fees, designed for incorporated businesses [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Offers many of the same features as Wealthsimple's personal chequing and savings accounts, with added business functionality including CRA payments [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].

### Eligibility (seven enumerated criteria)

- The business must be registered in Canada [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- The business must be a Canadian-controlled private corporation, or a charity/association registered with the CRA; the applicant must provide a CRA registration number to proceed [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- The business must have a single layer of ownership for incorporated entities [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- The business must be an active or passive non-financial entity (cannot be a financial institution) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- **The business must not be a strata or condominium corporation** — this is the load-bearing categorical eligibility exclusion that closes off Wealthsimple Business as a viable Canadian banking product for any strata or condo corporation [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- The business must have a 9-digit federal business number [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- The applicant must be a director or owner of the business, or officially authorized to act on behalf of the business to sign agreements and handle accounts with Wealthsimple [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Wealthsimple does not support corporate accounts for sole proprietorship or partnership businesses [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].

### Features

- No monthly fees; same interest-earning structure as Wealthsimple's personal chequing accounts [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Bill Pay, including payments to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for Corporate Income Tax, GST/HST, and Payroll Remittances [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Bulk schedule recurring CRA payments for corporate income tax and payroll source deductions; GST/HST payments cannot yet be bulk-scheduled [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- CDIC coverage up to $100,000 CAD [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Chequebook ordering available only in the Wealthsimple mobile app; cheque e-deposit and cheque-for-delivery ordering supported [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Interac e-Transfer for sending and receiving money to the business email address; autodeposit supported only for receiving transfers [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Wire transfers for sending CAD only; sending or receiving USD wires is not supported [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Monthly statements available in PDF and CSV formats [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Account integration to QuickBooks and other accounting software [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Prepaid Mastercard and credit card products are not currently available for Business chequing accounts; Wealthsimple states it is working toward future availability [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].

### Multi-director account mechanics

- All authorized directors on a multi-director Business account can view shared account information and perform activities like making payments [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Account nicknames and Interac e-Transfer / wire / bill payee contacts are tied to the individual director's profile and are not visible to other directors [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Only the Authorized Person who originally set up the corporation can open new accounts under that corporation and link external bank accounts [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].

### Account-opening flow

- Account-opening is initiated from the Wealthsimple app (Home → Add or move account → Open a new account → View all accounts → Business chequing) or from the Wealthsimple web profile (Home → Add an account → Business tab → Business chequing) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- The Wealthsimple app sorts opened Business accounts into a dedicated Business section in the user's accounts list [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
