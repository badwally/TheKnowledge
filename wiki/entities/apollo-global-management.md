# Apollo Global Management

## Summary

US alternative-asset manager; publisher of the December 2022 white paper "2023 Economic and Capital Markets Outlook" by Chief Economist Torsten Sløk, which argues 2023 will be defined by a Fed pivot trade, a soft-landing path for the US economy, and a rotation from a disastrous 2022 for 60/40 portfolios into private markets — where Apollo specifically sees a "historic entry point in private credit and attractive opportunities in private equity" [[sources/pdf-d196e853ebc0]].

## Key facts

- Listed copyright holder of the December 2022 white paper "2023 Economic and Capital Markets Outlook": "@ 2022 APOLLO GLOBAL MANAGEMENT, INC. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED" [[sources/pdf-d196e853ebc0]].
- Employs Torsten Sløk as Chief Economist; he is the listed author of the white paper [[sources/pdf-d196e853ebc0]].
- The white paper opens with the disclaimer that "the information herein is provided for educational purposes only and should not be construed as financial or investment advice, nor should any information in this document be relied on when making an investment decision" [[sources/pdf-d196e853ebc0]].
- The white paper sees a "historic entry point in private credit and attractive opportunities in private equity for investors able to be providers of capital in a time of stressed and distressed markets" — positioning Apollo's private-markets franchise as the destination for capital rotating away from a disastrous 2022 in 60/40 portfolios [[sources/pdf-d196e853ebc0]].
- The white paper argues high-yield primary credit markets will likely stay virtually shut down in 2023, an environment in which providers of private capital have outsized leverage [[sources/pdf-d196e853ebc0]].
- The Apollo Chief Economist team ("Apollo Analysts") is referenced as the data-and-views source on every exhibit in the white paper [[sources/pdf-d196e853ebc0]].
