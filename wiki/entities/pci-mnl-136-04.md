# PCI MNL-136-04 — Maintenance Manual for Precast Parking Structures

## Summary

PCI MNL-136-04 is the Maintenance Manual for Precast Parking Structures published by the Precast/Prestressed Concrete Institute (copyright 2004, ISBN 0-937040-70-3), authored by the PCI Committee on Parking Structures and providing maintenance guidance for owners, operators, architects, and engineers of precast parking structures [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].

## Key facts

- Published by the Precast/Prestressed Concrete Institute, copyrighted 2004, ISBN 0-937040-70-3, document number MNL-136-04 [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Authored by the PCI Committee on Parking Structures with Walter Korkosz as Chairman and a roster of approximately 40 contributing members [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Frames parking structures as requiring more maintenance attention than other building types because structural components are exposed directly to weather, extreme temperature changes, rain, snow, deicing salts, road grime, and dampness [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Organizes maintenance of precast parking structures into three broadly accepted categories — Housekeeping, Preventive Maintenance, and Structural Repairs — and adds a Periodic Structural Audit as a separately cadenced activity in Section 2.5 [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Includes Table A (Housekeeping Schedule) and Table B (Preventative Maintenance Schedule) in Section 3.0 (Planning and Documentation), and an Appendix A of Documentation Forms [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Recommends two complementary references for non-precast and equipment-specific maintenance: ACI Report CI 362.2R-00 (Guide for Structural Maintenance of Parking Structures) and the National Parking Association's Parking Garage Maintenance Manual [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- States that a routine maintenance program should be set up immediately upon turnover of the parking structure [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Disclaims that the manual is intended for use by professional personnel competent to evaluate the significance and limitations of its contents, and that special project conditions may require more specific evaluation and engineering judgment [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Notes that although the manual targets precast/prestressed concrete, parking structures commonly incorporate cast-in-place concrete, steel, masonry, aluminum, or wood, and non-structural systems (elevators, escalators, people movers, mechanical, electrical, security, landscaping) that have manufacturer-specified maintenance requirements outside the manual's scope [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
