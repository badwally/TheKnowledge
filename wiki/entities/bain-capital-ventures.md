# Bain Capital Ventures (BCV)

## Summary

Bain Capital Ventures (BCV) is a multi-stage venture capital firm with over $10 billion under management, investing from seed through IPO and acquisition for over 20 years across seven core domains: AI applications, AI infrastructure, commerce, fintech, healthcare, industrials, and security [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. BCV has backed more than 400 companies and offers distinctive access to Bain Capital's broader industry expertise and portfolio of companies [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. BCV participated as a co-investor in MaintainX's $150 million Series D round announced July 9, 2025; Partner Merritt Hummer served as the firm's named relationship lead on the investment [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

## Key facts

### Firm profile

- Multi-stage venture capital firm with over $10 billion under management [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Operates as an affiliate of Bain Capital, offering distinctive access to Bain Capital's broader industry expertise and portfolio of companies [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Invests across seven core domains: AI applications, AI infrastructure, commerce, fintech, healthcare, industrials, and security [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- For over 20 years, BCV has invested from seed through IPO and acquisition [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Backed more than 400 companies [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Named portfolio companies include Attentive, Bloomreach, Clari, Docusign, Flywire, LinkedIn, Moveworks, Redis, Rubrik, and ShipBob [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

### MaintainX Series D investment

- Co-invested in MaintainX's $150 million Series D announced July 9, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Named relationship lead: Partner Merritt Hummer [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Hummer's articulated thesis on the investment: companies are increasingly turning to operational technology not just to improve performance but to protect margins and preserve jobs [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Hummer framed MaintainX's value as transforming maintenance from a manual, reactive process into a data-rich, AI-powered advantage [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Hummer characterized the platform's effect as introducing a new operating model where AI surfaces insights, predicts failures, and unlocks new revenue streams and efficiencies [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
