# Carl Menger

## Summary

Founder of the Austrian school of economics; named first in Per Bylund's dedication of "How to Think about the Economy: A Primer" (2022) alongside Ludwig von Mises as one of "the giants upon whose shoulders I have the great honor of standing" [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].

## Key facts

- Named alongside Ludwig von Mises in the dedication of Bylund's 2022 primer as a foundational figure of Austrian economics [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- Described by Bylund as one of "the giants upon whose shoulders I have the great honor of standing" [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
