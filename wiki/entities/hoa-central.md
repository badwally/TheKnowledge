# HOA Central

## Summary

HOA Central is the Property Control portfolio's dedicated platform for homeowners associations and self-managed communities, focused on communications, compliance, and member satisfaction [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]].

## Key facts

- HOA Central is one of four sister products consolidated under the Property Control parent brand effective August 29, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The product is positioned as a dedicated platform for HOAs and self-managed communities with communications, compliance, and member satisfaction features [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- HOA Central is accessible via multi-brand single sign-on across the Property Control portfolio [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The product carries Property Control's portfolio-level native integrations to Yardi, Stripe, Zego, and VMS, plus an open API [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Existing HOA Central customers continue on the product with no disruption at the Property Control parent-brand launch [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
