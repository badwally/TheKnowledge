# Spectrum Equity

## Summary

Spectrum Equity is a growth-equity investor whose 2019 investment in CINC Systems is credited by CINC company materials as the trigger for the accelerated growth that funded CINC's continued innovation on the core CINC accounting platform and the subsequent build-out of TresRE as the banking-and-payments middleware product [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

- Joined CINC Systems as an investor in 2019 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- CINC company materials attribute the company's accelerated growth — and the continued innovation it provides through both the CINC core platform and the TresRE banking-and-payments solution — to Spectrum Equity's 2019 investment [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
