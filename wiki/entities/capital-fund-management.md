# Capital Fund Management

## Summary

Quantitative investment firm; institutional home of two of the three authors of the 2024 paper "Ponzi Funds" — Jean-Philippe Bouchaud and Dario Villamaina [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the institutional affiliation of Jean-Philippe Bouchaud (alongside Ecole polytechnique and the France Académie des Sciences) on "Ponzi Funds" [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
- Listed as the sole institutional affiliation of Dario Villamaina on the same paper [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
