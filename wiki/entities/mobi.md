# Mobility Open Blockchain Initiative (MOBI)

## Summary

MOBI is a blockchain-based consortium in the automotive sector that brings together direct competitors to test data sharing through distributed ledger technology [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].

## Key facts

- Members include BMW, Ford, GM, Honda, and Renault — automakers that normally compete with one another [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- The initiative tests blockchain approaches to sharing autonomous vehicle test data and digital vehicle identities across competing manufacturers [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Cited by Harvard JOLT commentators as a representative real-world blockchain data-sharing consortium that could implicate antitrust scrutiny [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
