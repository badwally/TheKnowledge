# Texas Department of Transportation Pavement Management Information System (PMIS)

## Summary

The Texas Department of Transportation's Pavement Management Information System (PMIS) is a state-maintained database of pavement condition observations used as a primary data source for pavement deterioration research, including studies of flood-driven roughness change.

## Key facts

- The Peng et al. (2025) explainable-AI study of flood-driven pavement deterioration used 20 years of pavement condition data from TxDOT's PMIS database [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- That study integrated PMIS data with flood event data including duration and spatial extent [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- The same study used PMIS-derived International Roughness Index (IRI) values to quantify pavement condition before and after flooding [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
