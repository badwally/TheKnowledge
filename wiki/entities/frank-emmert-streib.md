# Frank Emmert-Streib

## Summary

Frank Emmert-Streib is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2022 *Scientific Reports* paper on prognostic modeling of predictive maintenance with survival analysis for mobile work equipment.

## Key facts

- Emmert-Streib is the fourth (final) listed author of 'Prognostic modeling of predictive maintenance with survival analysis for mobile work equipment,' published 20 May 2022 in *Scientific Reports* (PMID 35595821, DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-12572-z) [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The paper is co-authored with Zhen Yang, Juho Kanniainen, and Tomi Krogerus [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
