# Tao Yang

## Summary

Tao Yang is a co-author of the 2025 paper proposing a comprehensive Gamma process-based approach for the time-dependent reliability of reinforced concrete structures considering non-stationary resistance degradation.

## Key facts

- Yang is the second listed author of 'An Evaluation on the Time-Dependent Reliability of Reinforced Concrete Structures Considering Non-Stationary Resistance Degradation: A Comprehensive Gamma Process-Based Approach' [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- The paper was published on 31 January 2025 with DOI 10.17531/ein/200284 [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- The paper is co-authored with Conghe Jin, Wangxi Xu, Yongjiu Qian, and Junhao Huang [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
