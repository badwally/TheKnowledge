# SAC Capital Management

## Summary

Hedge fund founded by Steve Cohen; per the 1998 foreword to Ari Kiev's "Trading to Win," the firm grew from a $20 million hedge fund into one handling over $500 million annually within five years, growth Cohen attributes to a proactive trading-psychology program co-developed with psychiatrist Ari Kiev [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]]; Cohen is also cited by Lu, Mortal, and Ray (2020) as attributing poker as "the biggest determinant in [his] learning to take risks" [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].

## Key facts

- Founded and led by Steve Cohen [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per Cohen's foreword (1998), grew from a $20 million hedge fund to one handling over $500 million annually after only five years [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Has traders working with Ari Kiev in weekly meetings to identify and modify limiting thoughts and bad habits and proactively incorporate successful changes into their trading strategies [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Monitors trader performance closely; per Cohen's foreword, some SAC traders' profits had gone up 100% since implementing the program [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- The firm's horizons expanded under the program from equity trading alone into foreign exchange, convertibles, options, junk bonds, risk arbitrage, and systems trading [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Acknowledged in the book's acknowledgments alongside Steve Cohen for their support to Ari Kiev over six years of weekly trader meetings [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Cohen attributes poker as "the biggest determinant in [his] learning to take risks," cited by Lu, Mortal, and Ray (2020) as evidence that prominent fund managers view poker skill as transferable to investing [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
