# Santa Fe Institute

## Summary

Independent research institute focused on complex adaptive systems; institutional affiliation of James Evans and Blaise Agüera y Arcas on the 2026 "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion" essay, which frames the next intelligence explosion as a complex-systems phenomenon — "intelligence growing like a city, not a single meta-mind" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the institutional home of two of three authors of the Evans, Bratton, Agüera y Arcas paper: James Evans and Blaise Agüera y Arcas [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The paper's plurality framing — that any emergent intelligence explosion will be seeded by eight billion humans interacting with hundreds of billions, eventually trillions, of AI agents — sits firmly within the complex-systems tradition associated with the Institute [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
