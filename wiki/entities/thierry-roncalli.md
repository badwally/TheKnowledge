# Thierry Roncalli

## Summary

Quantitative researcher whose institutional home moved from Lyxor Asset Management's Research & Development group in Paris (2011) to Amundi Asset Management's Quantitative Research group in Paris (2017); co-author of the December 2011 Lyxor working paper "Trend Filtering Methods for Momentum Strategies" [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]] and corresponding author of the September 2017 Amundi working paper "Understanding the Momentum Risk Premium: An In-Depth Journey Through Trend-Following Strategies," which extends the Bruder-Gaussel (2011) continuous-time trend-following model to the multivariate case [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].

## Key facts

- Listed affiliation on the 2011 Lyxor paper: Research & Development, Lyxor Asset Management, Paris, with contact email thierry.roncalli@lyxor.com [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- Listed affiliation on the 2017 Amundi paper: Quantitative Research, Amundi Asset Management, Paris [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Listed contact email on the 2017 paper: thierry.roncalli@amundi.com [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Co-author of "Trend Filtering Methods for Momentum Strategies" (December 2011) with Benjamin Bruder, Tung-Lam Dao, and Jean-Charles Richard, surveying linear and nonlinear econometric trend estimators and their application to the S&P 500 [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- Designated corresponding author of "Understanding the Momentum Risk Premium" (September 2017), co-authored with Paul Jusselin, Edmond Lezmi, Hassan Malongo, Côme Masselin, and Tung-Lam Dao [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Acknowledges Benjamin Bruder and Nicolas Gaussel as the originators of the univariate theoretical framework that the 2017 paper extends [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Acknowledges Tung-Lam Dao for working on the multivariate case during a 2011 internship and obtaining some of the formulas presented in the 2017 working paper, justifying Dao's authorship credit [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Cited in the 2017 paper as Roncalli (2017) for prior work arguing that carry, momentum, and value should be included as three risk premia in a strategic asset allocation in order to improve diversification of traditional risk premia portfolios [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Co-authored Cazalet and Roncalli (2014) on the multi-factor portfolio framework exposing institutional investors to size, value, momentum, low beta, and quality risk factors [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
