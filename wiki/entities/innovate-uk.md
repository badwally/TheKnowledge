# Innovate UK

## Summary

Innovate UK is the UK government's innovation agency, which in 2018 partnered with the Open Data Institute and the UK Office for Artificial Intelligence to assess data trusts as a potential approach to data stewardship [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].

## Key facts

- Co-sponsored the ODI's 2018 data-trust research programme alongside the UK Office for Artificial Intelligence [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].
