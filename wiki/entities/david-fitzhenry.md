# David Fitzhenry

## Summary

David Fitzhenry is a partner at Moritt Hock & Hamroff and the legal-side expert source in Habitat Magazine's April 2026 Bricks & Bucks coverage of the Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac March 2026 GSE policy update [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]]. He frames the 15% reserve-funding floor as "another sticker shock" for boards already absorbing inflationary cost pressure and identifies the time-limited nature of the 36-month reserve-study workaround as the principal long-run constraint on boards seeking to avoid holding the full 15% [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].

## Key facts

- Partner at Moritt Hock & Hamroff [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
- Quoted in Habitat Magazine's April 2026 coverage of the GSE update; characterized the policy as "well intentioned" but as imposing "another sticker shock" on boards [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
- Stated that buildings will "eventually run out of that reserve study benefit," framing the 36-month reserve-study workaround as a time-limited mechanism rather than a permanent alternative to the 15% reserve floor [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
- Stated that without the 15% capital reserve minimum, boards will increasingly find loans are not approved for their building [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
