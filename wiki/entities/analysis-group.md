# Analysis Group

## Summary

Analysis Group is an economic consultancy whose principals publish commentary on antitrust, technology, and litigation economics, including blockchain-based competitor data sharing [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].

## Key facts

- Principal Almudena Arcelus specializes in applying economics, finance, and information technology to business litigation in technology-intensive industries [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Vice president Mihran Yenikomshian focuses on data analysis and economic modeling and has consulted on blockchain and cryptocurrency, software, and cybersecurity matters [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- Associate Noemi Nocera applies microeconomics, econometrics, and statistical methods to litigation and government investigations in technology and manufacturing industries [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
