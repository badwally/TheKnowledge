# FTB Form 1028 — Guidelines for Homeowners' Associations

## Summary

FTB Form 1028 (*Guidelines for Homeowners' Associations*) is the California Franchise Tax Board's primary FAQ-style administrative publication on the California income-tax treatment of tax-exempt homeowners' associations, last revised December 2020 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]]. It articulates the qualification, application, and filing regime for HOAs seeking tax-exempt status under California Revenue and Taxation Code Section 23701t (patterned after Internal Revenue Code Section 528 and Federal Treasury Regulations Section 1.528) and details the Form 3500 application process plus the Form 199 / FTB 199N / Form 100 annual filing matrix [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].

## Key facts

- Publication number: Form 1028 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Issuing agency: California Franchise Tax Board [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Revision date: December 2020 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Format: FAQ-style guidance addressing the most frequently asked questions for tax-exempt HOAs [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Hosted at ftb.ca.gov/forms/misc/1028.html [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Establishes that HOAs apply for California tax-exempt status under R&TC Section 23701t, which is patterned after IRC Section 528 and Federal Treasury Regulations Section 1.528 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Defines the universe of eligible organization types: condominium management associations, residential real estate management associations, cooperative housing corporations, time-share associations, and mutual water companies [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Specifies the property must be at least 85 percent residential for HOA tax-exempt eligibility [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Notes the California-federal divergence: California requires affirmative application and an Exempt Determination Letter; federal law allows annual election by filing IRS Form 1120H [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Notes that California does not have an equivalent to IRC Section 501(c)(12); mutual water companies must qualify under R&TC §23701t to gain CA exemption [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Lists the application process: complete Form 3500 (Exemption Application), submit at least 90 calendar days before tax-exempt status is needed, mail to Franchise Tax Board PO Box 1286, Rancho Cordova CA 95741-1286 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Articulates the two-part qualification test: organizational test (corporations must meet CA Corporations Code mutual-benefit requirements plus §23701t requirements; unincorporated associations must adopt qualifying constitution/bylaws language) and operational test [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Enumerates the operational test components: 85% residential test, 60% source-of-income test, 90% expenditure test, no private inurement, trust requirement on unused dues/assessments, and two-units-owned-by-different-persons membership minimum [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Specifies that organizations not receiving CA tax-exempt status remain subject to the minimum franchise or income tax [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Defines the three CA filing forms applicable to tax-exempt HOAs: Form 199 (Exempt Organization Annual Information Return), FTB 199N (California e-Postcard), and Form 100 (Corporation Franchise or Income Tax Return) [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Establishes that the Form 199 / 199N obligation is independent of the Form 100 obligation; the amount and type of income received are the determining factors [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Sets the Form 199 / FTB 199N threshold (tax year 2012 and later): gross receipts normally ≤ $50,000 → file FTB 199N; gross receipts > $50,000 → file Form 199 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Sets the Form 100 threshold: file Form 100 if taxable income exceeds $100 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Eliminates the Form 199 filing fee for returns due on or after 01/01/2021 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Establishes a $5/month delinquent-filing penalty (max $40) for late Form 199 under R&TC Section 23772 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Defines taxable income for an exempt HOA filing Form 100: total gross income minus exempt function income (dues, fees, assessments from members) minus deductions directly connected with the production of nonexempt function income [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Enumerates taxable-income inclusions: interest income (including interest from members), dividends, nonmember receipts, gains from sale of property, health service fees from members, meals/food service fees from members, housekeeping service fees from members, laundry-use fees from members, and amounts received from members for use of a facility for a discrete period [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
