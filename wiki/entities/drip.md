# Drip

## Summary

Drip is an email marketing platform positioned for ecommerce SMBs that helps teams send hyper-segmented emails through customizable templates and prebuilt workflows for use cases such as new-subscriber discounts, repeat-buyer referrals, and loyalty-based offers [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]]. Capterra categorizes Drip under Email Marketing, Marketing Automation, and All-in-One Marketing software, and its reviewer-attested differentiators are a visual workflow builder, behavior-based automation, tagging and segmentation, and event-triggered campaigns — placing it as a legacy-email-platform peer to Klaviyo and a category competitor relevant to the Orita CMO landscape [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].

## Key facts

- Drip is an email marketing platform whose core value proposition is hyper-segmented email delivery through customizable templates and prebuilt workflows [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Capterra lists Drip's primary use cases as Email Marketing, Marketing Automation, and All-in-One Marketing Platform [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Drip starts at $39/month (flat rate, per month) for its Email Marketing plan, with a free trial available and no free version [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Pricing is tied to contact list size, and reviewers report that costs rise sharply above 5,000 subscribers, which makes Drip less appealing for small businesses or rapidly growing lists [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Drip carries a 4.4/5 overall rating on Capterra across 188 user reviews, with sub-scores of 4.4 Overall, 4.1 Ease of Use, 4.4 Features, and 4.2 Customer Service, and a 7.4/10 Likelihood to Recommend [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Capterra-derived review sentiment is 91% positive, 4% neutral, and 5% negative [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Reviewer-cited feature differentiators include a visual workflow builder, behavior-based automation, tagging and segmentation, and event-triggered campaigns [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Reviewer-highlighted integrations include Shopify and Facebook Custom Audiences, and Drip's official catalog includes BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce, Calendly, ClickFunnels, Acuity Scheduling, Close, MemberMouse, and Make, with more than 90 integrations overall [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Drip is used primarily by small businesses (95% of reviewers), with the top industries being Marketing and Advertising (19%), Information Technology and Services (10%), and Computer Software (7%) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Common buyer roles are business owners, founders, and executives needing scalable email marketing without a large team, plus marketing managers, email specialists, and web developers supporting ecommerce integrations and personalized journeys [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Drip addresses cart abandonment, lead nurturing, win-back campaigns, and transactional emails as recurring buyer workloads cited in reviews [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Drip offers email/help desk, FAQ/forum, a knowledge base, phone support, and chat support; reviewer experiences with support quality are mixed, with some reporting same-day chat replies and others reporting multi-day delays [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Onboarding resources include live online sessions, webinars, documentation, and videos; basic email tasks are quick to learn, while workflows, tags, segmentation, and funnels have a noticeable learning curve [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
- Capterra surfaces Bitrix24, Constant Contact, LeadSquared, HubSpot Marketing Hub, and EngageBay CRM as Drip's most prominent listed alternatives [[sources/web-2026-06-15-789]].
