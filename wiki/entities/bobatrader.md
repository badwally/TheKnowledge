# BobaTrader

## Summary

Discretionary options day trader and author of "BobaTrader: Guide to Consistent Daytrading" (2022), a 31-page personal guide that recounts blowing up multiple accounts before reaching consistency, then codifies a price-action-only methodology centered on a single signature setup — the Morning 15-Min Supply/Demand Zone Reversal — combined with day-of-week rules for options strike and expiration selection, scaled-out profit taking, and patient, emotion-anchored position sizing [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].

## Key facts

- Authored "BobaTrader: Guide to Consistent Daytrading" (2022, 31 pages) [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Year 1 of the journey: discovered daytrading from Reddit, deposited $20k of job savings, almost doubled the account by going all-in on penny stocks with the whole account, then lost almost 90% doing the same things, and concluded "this is just gambling" before vowing off trading [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Year 1 (later): deposited $10k into options trading after a friend showed gains, repeatedly went all-in trying to double the account on each trade — the most ever up in a day was $25k from going all-in on next-day-expiration TSLA calls; the same approach the next week wiped out about 80% of the account; ended Year 1 down $30k (most of his savings) [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Year 2: decided to LEARN — researched technical analysis, charting, supports/resistances, key levels, and candle patterns and noticed the same patterns repeating; learned risk management by implementing a hard 15% stop on every trade for about two months, but the stop was triggered too often and was abandoned in favor of technicals; also learned to scale out winners instead of closing the entire position; this is where he first became consistently profitable [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Year 3–5: scaled position sizes slowly, adjusting risk level based on the emotional impact of the dollar amount on screen — when $300 losses felt too large he sized down to keep them at $200, then continued the pattern [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Strictly an options trader: long via Calls, short via Puts [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Strike/expiration rules — Mon–Wed: same-week Friday expiration, at the money (closer to current price); Thu–Fri: next-week expiration, slightly out the money [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Methodology rejects indicators and candlestick analyzing in favor of price, volume, supply/demand, and supports/resistances [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Signature setup is the 15-Min Supply/Demand Zone Reversal — taken about 90% of the time, with zones plotted on the 15-minute timeframe over the previous 2–5 days and execution watched on the 5-minute chart at open [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Favorite trading window is 9:30–11 a.m. EST, when volume is highest and zones are most likely to react [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Profit-taking ladder on the signature setup: cut 1/4 at +15%, cut another 50% (of original position) at +20–25%, let the remaining 25% run to significant levels [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
- Rarely doubles down on losing positions [[sources/pdf-9613f301a3b5]].
