# Associa

## Summary

Associa is a Richardson, Texas-headquartered community association and property management firm founded in 1979, characterized in the October 24, 2025 SEC-filing-based reporting as one of the largest property management firms in the country [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]]. As of the year preceding the October 2025 announcement, Associa operated 300 offices across 42 states and in four countries outside the United States, following a 30 percent jump in revenue over the prior two years and a 2024 headquarters relocation to two office buildings in Richardson, Texas [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]]. On October 24, 2025, Associa announced — via Douglas Elliman's SEC filing — that its wholly-owned subsidiary PMG Holdings had agreed to acquire all of the equity interest in Douglas Elliman Property Management for $85 million, with PMG entering a five-year licensing agreement permitting continued use of the Douglas Elliman brand and maintaining the pre-existing referral relationship between the Elliman brokerage and the property management firm [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]]. The transaction is the operative October-2025 primary-source confirmation of Associa's posture as an acquirer-active CAM consolidator and underwrites Associa's position as a named backup acquirer target in the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis-acquirer-mapping under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

## Key facts

### Corporate identity

- Founded in 1979 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Headquarters located in Richardson, Texas; Associa moved its headquarters to two office buildings in Richardson in 2024 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Characterized in the October 24, 2025 reporting as "one of the largest property management firms in the country" [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Scale and growth (as of 2024)

- Operated 300 offices across 42 states and in four countries outside the U.S. as of the year before the October 2025 announcement [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Revenue jumped 30 percent over the prior two years preceding the 2024 headquarters relocation [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Douglas Elliman Property Management acquisition (October 2025)

- Announced October 24, 2025 via Douglas Elliman's filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission; Associa's wholly-owned subsidiary PMG Holdings agreed to acquire all of the equity interest in Douglas Elliman Property Management [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Purchase price: $85 million for all of the equity interest in Douglas Elliman Property Management [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Acquisition vehicle: PMG Holdings, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Associa [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- Five-year licensing agreement: Elliman and PMG entered an agreement permitting PMG to continue using the Douglas Elliman brand as part of its property management services [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- PMG and Elliman will maintain the referral relationship that had existed between the brokerage and the property management firm prior to the deal [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Acquired business profile (Douglas Elliman Property Management at the time of acquisition)

- In 2024, Douglas Elliman's property management arm employed over 216 people [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
- In 2024, Douglas Elliman's property management arm accounted for 3.7 percent — or $36 million — of Douglas Elliman's nearly $1 billion in revenue [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].

### Industry-consolidation context (October 2025)

- The October 2025 reporting frames the Associa / Douglas Elliman transaction as occurring "in the midst of the residential real estate industry undergoing rapid consolidation," alongside Compass's $1.6 billion deal for Anywhere Real Estate (parent of Corcoran, Sotheby's International Realty, and Coldwell Banker) announced the prior month and Rocket Companies' $14 billion deal for Mr. Cooper (the largest home loan servicer in the country) closed earlier in October 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-24-c1b]].
