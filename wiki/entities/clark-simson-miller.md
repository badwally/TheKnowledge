# Clark Simson Miller

## Summary

Clark Simson Miller is a US remote HOA management services provider that publishes practitioner-facing articles on HOA financial management, including a March 6, 2024 explainer on the FDIC reserve-funds limit and the IntraFi CDARS/ICS coverage-maximization products [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]]. The company markets remote HOA management services to condominium associations and homeowners associations, and uses its clarksimsonmiller.com publication as a lead-generation surface for prospective association clients [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].

## Key facts

- Operates the clarksimsonmiller.com domain [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Markets remote HOA management services to condominium associations and homeowners associations [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Authored the March 6, 2024 article "What Is The HOA Reserve Funds FDIC Limit?" attributed to "Admin; The Clark Simson Miller Team" [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Article served as a vendor-published encyclopedic explainer on FDIC reserve-funds limits, HOA ownership-category classification, and the IntraFi CDARS/ICS coverage-maximization mechanisms [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Public contact: 865.315.7505 [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Article concludes with a sales call-to-action directing readers to contact the company online or by phone to request a proposal [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
