# James Evans

## Summary

Sociologist whose work spans the University of Chicago, Santa Fe Institute, and Google's Paradigms of Intelligence Team; lead author of the 2026 essay "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion," which argues that the next intelligence explosion will be plural, social, and hybrid rather than a monolithic singularity [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Paradigms of Intelligence Team at Google, the University of Chicago, and the Santa Fe Institute on the paper [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Co-authored "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion" (2026) with Benjamin Bratton and Blaise Agüera y Arcas [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Listed as a co-author on the related paper "Reasoning Models Generate Societies of Thought" (Kim, Lai, Scherrer, Agüera y Arcas, Evans; arXiv:2601.10825, 2026), which is cited as the empirical basis for the society-of-thought claim [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Cited as co-author of "Flat Teams Drive Scientific Innovation" (Xu, Wu, Evans; PNAS 119(23), 2022), referenced as motivation for thinking about AI reasoning architecture in terms of team-science findings [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Cited as co-author of "Large AI Models Are Cultural and Social Technologies" (Farrell, Gopnik, Shalizi, Evans; Science 387(6739), 2025), used as the basis for the claim that LLMs encounter externalized social intelligence on a new substrate [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
