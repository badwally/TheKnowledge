# Simon Sinek

## Summary

Author of "The Infinite Game"; cited by TraderEdge's Alex as the source of the finite-vs-infinite-game distinction that the 2023 email "I'm profitable, now what?" applies to trading [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key facts

- Author of the book "The Infinite Game" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Develops a framework distinguishing finite games — "like football or chess," where the players are known, the rules are fixed, the endpoint is clear, and the objective is to win — from infinite games — "like business or politics," where the players come and go, the rules are changeable, there is no defined endpoint, and there are no winners or losers, only "ahead and behind" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The Infinite Game concept is invoked by TraderEdge to argue that trading should be approached as an infinite game focused on long-term sustainability and adaptability rather than short-term wins and losses [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
