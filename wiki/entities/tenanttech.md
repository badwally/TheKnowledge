# TenantTech

## Summary

TenantTech is [[entities/inhabit]]'s online leasing solution focused on rental application and lease document workflows for multifamily property management companies [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- TenantTech operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- At OpTech 2025 (Nov 17, 2025), TenantTech announced an integration with [[entities/blue-moon-software]] providing attorney-reviewed rental applications and lease forms in regions where TenantTech customers did not previously have access to such documents [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- TenantTech serves as a distribution channel for [[entities/western-reporting]] Digital Income Verification and (in early 2026) Identity Verification [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- TenantTech is positioned exclusively for multifamily rental leasing workflows; no condo/HOA fee-collection or governance-document use cases are announced [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
