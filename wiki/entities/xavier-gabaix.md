# Xavier Gabaix

## Summary

Economist at the Harvard University Department of Economics; co-author of the June 2021 NBER Working Paper No. 28967 "In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis," which argues that the aggregate stock market is highly inelastic and that flows of $1 into equities raise aggregate market value by approximately $5 [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Department of Economics, Harvard University, Littauer Center, 1805 Cambridge St., Cambridge, MA 02138, and NBER on the paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Contact email xgabaix@fas.harvard.edu on the paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Co-authored "In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis" (NBER Working Paper No. 28967, June 2021) with Ralph S. J. Koijen [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Acknowledges the Sloan Foundation for financial support on the paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Co-authored the stand-alone Gabaix and Koijen (2020) paper that worked out the "granular instrumental variables" methodology used as the empirical core of the inelastic-markets paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
