# ASHRAE Standard 180-2018 — Standard Practice for Inspection and Maintenance of Commercial Building HVAC Systems

## Summary

ASHRAE Standard 180-2018, titled "Standard Practice for Inspection and Maintenance of Commercial Building HVAC Systems," is the HVAC inspection-and-maintenance standard explicitly cited by BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB question E6.0 as the minimum methodology to which a building's Preventive Maintenance Plan must align [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]]. The standard distinguishes inspection (which monitors and documents the condition of equipment and components over time) from maintenance actions [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].

## Key facts

- BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E6.0 — Preventative Maintenance requires that the assessment methodology of a building's Preventive Maintenance Plan "at least align with ASHRAE Standard 180-2018" [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- The standard frames inspection as serving to monitor and document the condition of equipment and components over time, distinct from maintenance actions [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
- Distributed via Techstreet (techstreet.com/ashrae/standards/ashrae-180-2018) per the BOMA BEST 4.0 MURB E6.0 References list [[sources/pdf-6302e90be3b6]].
