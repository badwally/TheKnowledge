# Bill Blanton

## Summary

Bill Blanton is the founder of CINC Systems, which he established in 2005. He is identified as a third-generation banker whose founding insight was streamlining association management companies' banking, software, and payments under a single SaaS platform — the architecture that CINC and the subsequent Paygami and TresRE products are built on [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

- Founded CINC Systems in 2005 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Described in CINC company materials as a third-generation banker [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Founding thesis was streamlining association management companies' banking, software, and payments under one platform [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Credited in CINC company materials with modernizing the association management industry through that banking-software-payments integration [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
