# Florida DBPR SIRS Reporting Form

## Summary

The Florida DBPR SIRS Reporting Form is the official paper-submission instrument issued by the Division of Florida Condominiums, Timeshares and Mobile Homes by which condominium and cooperative associations report their Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) information to the state, authorized by ss. 718.112(2)(g)9 & 10, F.S. (condominiums) and 719.106(1)(k)9 & 10, F.S. (cooperatives) [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]]. The form (Revised 9/2024) defines the regulatory minimum data set the state collects per building — preparer identity and credential category, association and project licensing information, year of construction, unit and floor counts, SIRS cost, assessment outcome (with cost-per-unit breakdown), completion date, and unit-owner distribution method — and prescribes per-building filing with a postmark deadline of 12/31/24 for the initial reporting window [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]]. PropFusion's April 2026 vendor-curated FL SIRS guide confirms the form's four-credential-category checkbox set maps to the statutory qualified-preparer set under HB 913 (FL-licensed PE, FL-licensed architect, RS/PRA-credentialed professional, plus a fourth catch-all) [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]].

## Key facts

### Statutory authority

- Authorized under ss. 718.112(2)(g)9 & 10, F.S. (condominium associations) and ss. 719.106(1)(k)9 & 10, F.S. (cooperative associations), making the form the operational reporting artifact for both condominium and cooperative SIRS regimes [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- The completed SIRS must be available for inspection and copying by written request per ss. 718.112(2)(g)9 & 10 and 719.106(1)(k)9 & 10, F.S. [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].

### Submission mechanics

- Mail submission to: Division of Florida Condominiums, Timeshares and Mobile Homes, Attention: Structural Integrity Reserve Study Reporting, 2601 Blair Stone Road, Tallahassee, Florida 32399-1030 — a SIRS-reporting-specific mailbox ZIP (-1030) distinct from the general DBPR mailing ZIP (-0791) [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Associations with multiple projects or buildings must complete a separate form for each one; all individual forms may be submitted together [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Forms must be postmarked by 12/31/24 for the initial reporting window [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- For projects with buildings at multiple addresses, an "Additional Building Address Form" must be completed and provided for each additional address [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Form revision marker: "Revised 9/2024" — pre-dates the HB 913 (2025) requirement that DBPR initiate (by October 1, 2025) rulemaking to create a new standardized SIRS form in coordination with the Florida Building Commission [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].

### Data fields collected per form

- Contact identification: contact name, contact phone number, contact email address [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Project identification: name of project, project license number, name of condominium or cooperative association, condominium or cooperative association license number (managing entity) [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Project type: Condominium or Cooperative — the form unifies condominium and cooperative reporting through a single project-type checkbox [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Address of project: street address, address line 2, city, state, ZIP code, county [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Building physical attributes: year of building construction (based on certificate of occupancy); total number of units per building; total number of floors per building [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Preparer identification: name of individual that conducted the SIRS; name of company that conducted the SIRS [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- SIRS cost: total cost of the SIRS, explicitly excluding any resulting assessment cost [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Assessment outcome: whether an assessment resulted from the association's SIRS (Yes/No); if yes, total cost of the assessment and breakdown of cost per unit [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- SIRS completion date — the date the Structural Integrity Reserve Study was completed [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Unit-owner distribution method (checkbox set): Sent by Mail; Electronic Transmission (E-mail); Sent by Personal Delivery; Facsimile Transmission [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- Certification block: printed name and signature of an individual certifying that the information provided is true, accurate, and complete to the best of their knowledge [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].

### Preparer credential taxonomy (per form)

- The form codifies four preparer-credential categories via a single "Who completed your SIRS?" checkbox set: Architect, Engineer, Reserve Specialist, Qualified Person [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- This four-category taxonomy is the form-level operationalization of the SIRS performer eligibility set whose complaint-routing paths DBPR maps to the Florida Board of Architecture and Interior Design (architects), the Florida Board of Professional Engineers (engineers), and DBPR itself for licensed Community Association Managers acting as certified reserve specialist or professional reserve analyst [[sources/pdf-b922a12f47f0]].
- PropFusion's April 2026 vendor-curated FL SIRS guide enumerates the three statutory qualified-preparer categories under HB 913: a Florida-licensed Professional Engineer; a Florida-licensed Architect; and a professional holding the Reserve Specialist (RS) or Professional Reserve Analyst (PRA) designation — mapping directly to three of the form's four credential checkboxes (Architect, Engineer, Reserve Specialist), with the form's fourth category "Qualified Person" extending the preparer set beyond PropFusion's enumerated statutory three categories and surfacing the "additional professionals with nationally recognized credentials" framing introduced by the 2025 statute's expanded credential allowance [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]].
- PropFusion's 2026 guide further documents HB 913's tightened conflict-of-interest disclosure rule for SIRS preparers — professionals who bid on a SIRS must disclose if they intend to bid on repair work, with undisclosed conflicts rendering the contract voidable — establishing an independence-disclosure norm that operates upstream of the form's preparer-identification fields [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]].
