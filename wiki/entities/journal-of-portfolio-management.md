# The Journal of Portfolio Management

## Summary

Finance journal whose 50th Anniversary Issue is the publication venue for Cliff Asness's "The Less-Efficient Market Hypothesis," in which he argues that markets have become less informationally efficient over his 30+-year career and that disciplined, value-based stock picking is both riskier and likely more rewarding long term [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].

## Key facts

- Cliff Asness's "The Less-Efficient Market Hypothesis" is forthcoming in the 50th Anniversary Issue [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].
