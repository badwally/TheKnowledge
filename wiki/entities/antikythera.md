# Antikythera

## Summary

A research program of the Berggruen Institute focused on the philosophy of computation and planetary-scale technical systems; institutional affiliation of Benjamin Bratton on the 2026 "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion" essay, and the publication venue for two companion Substack pieces by the same authors [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Benjamin Bratton's institutional affiliation on the paper, identified specifically as Antikythera at the Berggruen Institute [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- The paper cites two Substack publications hosted at antikythera.substack.com by Agüera y Arcas, Bratton, and Evans: "The Silicon Interior: What Do Agents Believe?" and "Agentic AI's Emergent Phenomenological Lexicon" (both 2026) [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
