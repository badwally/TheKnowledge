# Google Brain

## Summary

Google's deep-learning research group; institutional home of most authors of "Attention Is All You Need" (NIPS 2017), the paper that introduced the Transformer architecture [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].

## Key facts

- Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, and Łukasz Kaiser are listed as Google Brain affiliates on the Transformer paper, alongside Google Research affiliates Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, and Llion Jones, and Aidan N. Gomez at the University of Toronto (work performed while at Google Brain) [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
- The Transformer architecture introduced in the paper achieved 28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-German translation and 41.0 BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-French translation, the latter trained for 3.5 days on eight GPUs — a small fraction of the training cost of the best prior models [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
