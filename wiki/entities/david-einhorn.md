# David Einhorn

## Summary

Hedge fund manager who explicitly likens poker and investing, stating that both "are games of incomplete information…. You have a certain set of facts and you are looking for situations where you have an edge"; cited by Lu, Mortal, and Ray (2020) as the archetypal practitioner who views poker skill as correlated with investment skill [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].

## Key facts

- Likens poker and investing: both "are games of incomplete information…. You have a certain set of facts and you are looking for situations where you have an edge, whether the edge is psychological or statistical" [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Cited by Lu, Mortal, and Ray (2020) as a practitioner example supporting the widely held belief that poker skills correlate with success in investment and trading [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Interviewed by Dealbreaker under the title "Heads Up Play With David Einhorn" about the intersection of poker and fund management [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
