# Brian Bosscher

## Summary

Brian Bosscher is the founder of Condo Control, a condominium and HOA community management software company; he built the product after first-hand experience serving on his own condominium's finance committee and Board of Directors [[sources/web-2024-12-17-df4]].

## Key facts

- Bosscher purchased his own condo in 2006 and joined the building's finance committee shortly afterward, where he encountered the operational complexities affecting boards, property managers, security, and owners [[sources/web-2024-12-17-df4]].
- He volunteered on the Board of Directors as Treasurer the year after his condo purchase (i.e., 2007) and later served as Board President of the same building [[sources/web-2024-12-17-df4]].
- His board experience — specifically the inefficiencies he witnessed impeding the community from reaching its goals — inspired him to build Condo Control as a long-term software solution [[sources/web-2024-12-17-df4]].
