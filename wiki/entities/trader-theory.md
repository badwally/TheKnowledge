# Trader Theory

## Summary

Trading-education X (Twitter) account `@Trader_Theory`; author of the September 22, 2022 thread "ICT's 2022 Mentorship Summarized," a 20-tweet recap of Inner Circle Trader's 2022 mentorship that condenses the methodology into a Time + Price = Optimal Setups equation, the PO3 / AMD cycle, the Judas Swing, the Optimal Trade Entry, and a tight set of bias, FVG-selection, news-blackout, anti-martingale, and stop-trailing rules for intraday index trading [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].

## Key facts

- Operates the Twitter handle `@Trader_Theory` and published the 20-tweet thread "ICT's 2022 Mentorship Summarized" on September 22, 2022, archived as a PDF via Rattibha [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Frames the ICT methodology around the equation: Time (Time of Day, Day of Week, Week of Month, Month of Year, Seasonality, News Events, 8:30 & 9:30 New York Open) + Price (Premium Discount Arrays) = Optimal Setups (Old High/Low Sweeps + MSS + Displacement + FVG) [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Asserts as a foundational claim that "the prices of markets are controlled by AI-driven algorithms based on the elements of time and price" and that "price always moves to take liquidity and to rebalance inefficiently delivered price" — explicitly rejecting the framing that price moves due to buying or selling pressure [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Defines the indices intraday session windows used in the thread: NY AM session 8:30–12:00, NY PM session 13:00–16:30, with no trades during the NY lunch hour [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Tells readers to mark the previous swing high and swing low before the session opens as the potential price sweeps [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Promotes a Telegram community called "The Trader's Club" at t.me/TraderTheory and a partner signals service "Formidable Indices Powerhouse" at t.me/FormidableIndicesPowerhouse, advertised in the thread with a free-trial enrollment window of Sept 23–30, 2022 [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
- Credits `@daytradingrauf` for the photo/diagram in the thread [[sources/pdf-a98b496e5936]].
