# FRONTSTEPS Caliber

## Summary

FRONTSTEPS Caliber is a community-association / property-management accounting software platform named by CINC Systems in the June 2023 TresRE launch as one of two non-CINC accounting systems explicitly targeted as integration counterparties for the new TresRE middleware [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]]. The naming establishes FRONTSTEPS Caliber as a competitor to CINC's core accounting product whose customers CINC nonetheless wants to monetize via the TresRE payments and treasury layer.

## Key facts

- Named by CINC Systems in the June 2023 TresRE launch announcement as one of the third-party accounting platforms whose customers CINC's TresRE middleware integrates with [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- The TresRE integration brings CINC's bank-and-payments middleware — previously available only to CINC accounting-software customers — to FRONTSTEPS Caliber customers [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
