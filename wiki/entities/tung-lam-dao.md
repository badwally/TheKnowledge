# Tung-Lam Dao

## Summary

Independent researcher in Paris; co-author of the September 2017 Amundi working paper "Understanding the Momentum Risk Premium" — credited for working on the multivariate extension of the Bruder-Gaussel (2011) trend-following model during a 2011 Amundi internship, and lead author of the earlier Dao et al. (2016) paper on the relationship between trend-following strategies and the term structure of realized volatility [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Independent Researcher, Paris on the paper [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Listed contact email: daotunglam2001@gmail.com [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Co-author of "Understanding the Momentum Risk Premium" (September 2017) with Paul Jusselin, Edmond Lezmi, Hassan Malongo, Côme Masselin, and Thierry Roncalli [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Acknowledged in the paper as having worked on the multivariate case during his 2011 internship at Amundi and obtained some of the formulas presented in the working paper, justifying his co-authorship [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Lead author of Dao et al. (2016), one of four research works the paper identifies as essential for understanding the dynamics of trend-following strategies beyond overall performance [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Dao et al. (2016) showed that "the performance of the trend is positive when the long-term volatility is larger than the short-term volatility," implying that trend followers must risk-manage short-term volatility to exhibit positive skewness and convexity [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Dao et al. (2016) replicated the cumulative performance of the SGA CTA Index, the benchmark used by professionals for analyzing CTA hedge funds [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Dao et al. (2016) demonstrated that the payoff of the trend-following strategy is similar to the payoff of an equally-weighted portfolio of ATM strangles, and concluded that "even if options provide a better hedge, trend-following is a much cheaper way to hedge long-only exposures" [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
