# Assembly of First Nations

## Summary

The Assembly of First Nations is the national representative organization of First Nations in Canada; in its INDU-committee submission on AIDA, it stated that a lawsuit against the federal government was likely because the government failed to uphold Indigenous rights by not consulting First Nations during the drafting of the AIDA [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- Submitted a brief to INDU stating a lawsuit against the federal government was likely over its failure to consult First Nations during AIDA's drafting [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Wrote that "AI has the potential to destroy First Nations' cultures, threaten First Nations' security, and increase demand for our resources" [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Sources
