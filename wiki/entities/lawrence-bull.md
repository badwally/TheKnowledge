# Lawrence A. Bull

## Summary

Lawrence A. Bull is the lead author of the 2022 paper introducing a hierarchical Bayesian multitask learning framework for knowledge transfer across engineering fleets, published in *Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering*.

## Key facts

- Bull is the first listed author of "Hierarchical Bayesian Modelling for Knowledge Transfer Across Engineering Fleets via Multitask Learning" (2022) [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
- The paper appears in *Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering* and corresponds to arXiv preprint 2204.12404 with DOI 10.1111/mice.12901 [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
- The work is co-authored with D. Di Francesco, M. Dhada, O. Steinert, T. Lindgren, A. K. Parlikad, A. B. Duncan, and M. Girolami [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
