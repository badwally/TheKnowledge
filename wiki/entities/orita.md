# Orita

## Summary

Orita is an AI-powered audience intelligence layer for ecommerce brands. Its core ML scoring engine rates every customer profile daily for purchase intent, then executes smart suppression — removing low-intent profiles from email and SMS sends to improve deliverability and conversion economics [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]]. Orita is a seed-stage startup; Aaron serves as CEO and Adrienne as CMO [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].

## Key facts

- **Core product**: Daily ML purchase-intent scoring across the full customer list, with smart suppression as the primary output action [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]]
- **Primary channel integration**: Klaviyo is the primary channel partner and GTM beachhead; Orita's ML scoring feeds directly into Klaviyo send decisions [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]]
- **CRM**: HubSpot serves as customer source-of-truth for both current customers and target-list prospects [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]]
- **Prospect data**: StoreLeads supplies a 418K Klaviyo customer domain list used for outbound prospecting [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]]
- **GTM motion**: Agency channel — ecommerce agencies-of-record are the high-leverage GTM node, each connected to multiple ecommerce brands [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]]
- **Stage**: Seed-stage AI customer segmentation startup [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]]
- **Named direct competitor**: Black Crow AI competes in full-funnel predictive AI and purchase-intent modeling for ecommerce; its AI Storefronts product targets the post-click conversion layer (storefront generation) rather than the email suppression and audience-intelligence layer where Orita operates [[sources/web-2026-05-19-2c0]]
