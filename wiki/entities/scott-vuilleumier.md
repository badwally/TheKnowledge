# Scott Vuilleumier

## Summary

Scott Vuilleumier is the named author of CommunityPay's technical and regulatory blog content, the architect of the company's 548-statute, 51-state Living Legal Corpus, and the named inventor on two CommunityPay patent filings: PAT-002 (Enforcement Dispatcher) and PAT-003 (Living Legal Corpus) [[sources/web-2026-02-11-4eb]]. He is the named author of the February 11, 2026 piece that establishes CommunityPay's Condo Questionnaire API as the company's load-bearing Fannie Mae 1076 / Freddie Mac 1077 auto-fill product and is identified as the most recent content reviewer on that piece (last reviewed May 10, 2026) [[sources/web-2026-02-11-4eb]].

## Key facts

- Author of the February 11, 2026 CommunityPay blog post documenting the Form 1076 auto-fill product, the 8-section questionnaire surface, and the CARI consent framework [[sources/web-2026-02-11-4eb]].
- Built the legal corpus tracking 548 statutes across 51 U.S. states that underlies CommunityPay's regulatory disclosure product line [[sources/web-2026-02-11-4eb]].
- Named inventor on CommunityPay patent filing PAT-002 (Enforcement Dispatcher) [[sources/web-2026-02-11-4eb]].
- Named inventor on CommunityPay patent filing PAT-003 (Living Legal Corpus) [[sources/web-2026-02-11-4eb]].
- Identified as the most recent content reviewer on the February 11, 2026 Form 1076 piece, last reviewed May 10, 2026 [[sources/web-2026-02-11-4eb]].
