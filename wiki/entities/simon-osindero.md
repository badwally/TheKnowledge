## Summary

Researcher at Google DeepMind and co-author of the 2026 paper "Intelligent AI Delegation," which proposes an adaptive framework for task allocation, authority transfer, and trust in human-AI agentic networks [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].

- Co-authored "Intelligent AI Delegation" with Nenad Tomašev (lead) and Matija Franklin [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
