# MSCI All Country World Index

## Summary

Global equity index covering mid- and large-capitalization stocks in 23 developed and 24 emerging economies — about 85% of the global investable equity universe; cited by Mauboussin and Callahan (2024) as evidence that the U.S. concentration trend is mirrored globally because U.S. companies dominate the top of the index [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].

## Key facts

- Includes mid and large capitalization stocks in 23 developed and 24 emerging economies, covering about 85% of the global investable equity universe [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- The top 10 stocks accounted for about 19% of the index's total capitalization at the end of 2023 — more than double the level ten years earlier [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- 9 of the 10 largest constituents on December 30, 2023 were U.S. companies [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Top-10 concentration rose to about 20% by the end of Q1 2024 [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
