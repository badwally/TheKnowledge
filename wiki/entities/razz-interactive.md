# Razz Interactive

## Summary

Razz Interactive is the websites and CMS product within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio, focused on multifamily-property websites with AI-enabled SEO and copy tooling [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- Razz Interactive operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- The Razz Interactive relaunch announced at OpTech 2025 (Nov 17, 2025) emphasizes "smart technology and bold designs that drive leases and elevate brands" for multifamily websites [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- The Razz CMS and [[entities/resman]] Websites include AI-enabled tools — the Razz AI SEO and Copy Assistant — that help marketers create SEO-optimized content, meta tags, and descriptions [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Razz Interactive is positioned exclusively for multifamily property marketing; no condo/HOA owner-portal or board-website use cases are announced [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
