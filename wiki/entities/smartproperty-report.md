# SmartProperty Report

## Summary

The SmartProperty Report is the board- and community-facing deliverable of the SmartProperty × CINC Systems integration, which went live in May 2023 [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]]. The report is marketed as a critical tool for HOA and COA boards and community members, enabling decisions based on data synchronized in real time between property information in CINC and SmartProperty [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].

## Key facts

- Deliverable of the SmartProperty × CINC Systems integration that went live as of May 5, 2023 [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- Available to SmartProperty customers who have CINC Systems as their underlying management-company accounting platform [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- Positioned as a critical tool for boards and community members [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- CINC Chief Product Officer Chris Millner framed the report as transforming the way HOA and COA boards receive communication from their management companies [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- Data is synchronized in real time between property information in CINC and SmartProperty, supporting board decisions made with confidence that the information is accurate and up-to-date [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- Provides managers and boards with insight into reserves versus accounting actuals — the load-bearing functional claim of the integrated product [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
