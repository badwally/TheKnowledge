# Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT)

## Summary

The Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT) is the state agency that maintains the Pavement Management Information System (PMIS), the large pavement condition dataset that underlies the 2024 *Transportation Research Record* study of graph neural network-based pavement deterioration modeling.

## Key facts

- TxDOT maintains the Pavement Management Information System (PMIS), from which more than half a million pavement condition observations were drawn for the Gao, Yu, and Lu study [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- The PMIS dataset maintained by TxDOT is described as a large pavement condition data set used to evaluate whether incorporating spatial structure of the road network improves the prediction performance of pavement deterioration models [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
