# Robert Petrie

## Summary

Robert Petrie is the Chief Executive Officer of Origami Risk and a board member of CINC Systems, joining the CINC board in conjunction with Spectrum Equity's June 10, 2020 first institutional investment in CINC [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]]. Petrie is described in the June 2020 CINC announcement as an industry veteran with more than 20 years of operating experience [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]]. Because Origami Risk is itself a Spectrum Equity portfolio company (referenced in both the June 2020 and December 2023 Spectrum representative-investments lists), Petrie's CINC board appointment surfaces a Spectrum-portfolio-cross-pollination governance pattern in which a Spectrum portfolio-company CEO was placed on a Spectrum new-investment's board at deal close [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]].

## Key facts

- Chief Executive Officer of Origami Risk as of the June 10, 2020 CINC Systems announcement [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Joined CINC Systems' board of directors in conjunction with Spectrum Equity's June 10, 2020 growth investment in CINC [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Framed in the June 2020 CINC announcement as "an industry veteran with more than 20 years of operating experience" [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Origami Risk is itself a Spectrum Equity portfolio company, surfaced in both Spectrum's June 2020 representative-investments list and Spectrum's December 2023 representative-investments list — surfacing Petrie's CINC board appointment as a Spectrum-portfolio-cross-pollination governance pattern in which the CEO of one Spectrum portfolio company was placed on the board of another Spectrum portfolio company at deal close [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]].
- Joined the CINC board alongside Mike Farrell (Spectrum MD) and Chris Trower (founder of Electric Law) at the June 2020 deal close as part of a three-person new-board cohort [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
