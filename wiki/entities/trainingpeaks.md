# TrainingPeaks

## Summary

TrainingPeaks LLC is the company whose trademarks define much of the load-management vocabulary used in modern endurance training, including TSS, TSB, ATL, CTL, IF, and Normalized Power [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].

## Key facts

- The 2021 *Cycling Physiology & Training Science Guide* explicitly attributes the following terms to TrainingPeaks LLC as trademarks and/or intellectual property: Training Stress Score (TSS), Training Stress Balance (TSB), Acute Training Load (ATL), Critical Training Load (CTL), Intensity Factor (IF), and Normalized Power [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
- These metrics form the backbone of the guide's load-monitoring and periodisation chapters [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
