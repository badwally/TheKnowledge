# Pacific Premier Bank

## Summary

Pacific Premier Bank is a US chartered depository institution that operated a Community Association Banking division supporting homeowner association (HOA) clients nationwide [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. On August 31, 2025, Pacific Premier officially merged with Umpqua Bank, and the combined institution will subsequently be rebranded as Columbia Bank with over $70 billion in combined assets [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. Columbia Bank will adopt Pacific Premier's Community Association Banking division, retaining leadership and staff to ensure continuity of service to HOA customers [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key facts

- Operated a Community Association Banking division supporting HOA clients nationwide [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Has been a key banking partner of Community Financials's operations since 2018, supporting HOA clients with no-fee bank accounts, easy signature card updates, check lockbox processing, fraud prevention mechanisms, visibility through software integrations, competitive rates, and customer service [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Officially merged with Umpqua Bank on August 31, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Following the merger, the combined institution will be rebranded as Columbia Bank later in 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The combined post-merger institution will hold over $70 billion in combined assets [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Pre-merger account and routing numbers remain unchanged for HOA customers through the merger [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Pre-merger signature cards, supporting documents, and lockbox addresses remain valid post-merger; the bank name simply updates over time [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Existing online banking tools and Smartwebs software integrations continue without interruption post-merger [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Columbia Bank will adopt Pacific Premier's Community Association Banking division, retaining its leadership and staff to ensure continuity of service [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The combined team is planning to invest in further enhancing API technology that connects to Smartwebs software and other resources [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Pacific Premier-issued debit and credit cards continue to work without changes post-merger; replacements, if necessary, will be issued by direct bank notification [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Loan payments, CDs, and FDIC coverage remain unaffected by the merger [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- IntraFi CDARS and ICS deposit services continue as usual to HOA customers post-merger [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
