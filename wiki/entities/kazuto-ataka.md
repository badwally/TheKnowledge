# Kazuto Ataka

## Summary

Professor at Keio University Shonan Fujisawa Campus (SFC) and AI expert who advised Japan's LDP Project Team on the Evolution and Implementation of AI during preparation of the April 2023 AI Whitepaper [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Keio University SFC as a professor [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Spoke at the 2nd LDP project team hearing on February 17, 2023, characterizing the LLM moment as "an era of unlimited content creation through the use of machines" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Quoted in the whitepaper summary as observing that "from now on 'machines' will be creating an infinite amount of content" [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
