# BC Housing

## Summary

BC Housing is the Province of British Columbia's housing agency and the publisher of the Maintenance Matters bulletin series — a set of practical residential building-maintenance guidance documents prepared by a consortium of building envelope experts and produced with industry collaborators that vary by bulletin [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]]. Its bulletins are an authoritative Canadian source for prior calibration of the roof, building envelope, and parking deck / podium components in the condo-capital-infra probabilistic reserve engine [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].

## Key facts

- Publishes the "Maintenance Matters" series of building maintenance bulletins and companion videos designed to provide practical information on maintaining residential buildings [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Industry collaborators on Maintenance Matters bulletins vary by bulletin: MM-06 (Decks and Balconies) lists Polygon and Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]]; MM-02 (Maintaining Your Roof) lists Polygon and the Condominium Home Owners' Association (CHOA) [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletin content is prepared by a consortium of building envelope experts [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletin No. 2 ("Maintaining Your Roof") covers roof types (low-slope vs. steep-slope), membrane and shingle/shake/tile failure modes, inspection cadence (twice yearly plus post-storm), and replacement planning, and references the BC Strata Property Act Contingency Reserve Fund as the funding mechanism for common-expense roof replacement [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletin No. 6 ("Decks and Balconies") covers membrane types, service-life ranges, inspection cadence, guardrails / guardwalls, drainage components (drains, gutters, scuppers), and venting [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]].
- Also publishes a companion "Builder Insight" bulletin series — e.g., Builder Insight No. 8 on Compatibility of Fasteners and Connectors with Pressure Treated Wood — cross-referenced from the Maintenance Matters bulletins [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]].
- BC Housing also publishes the "Building Enclosure Design Guide – Wood-Frame Multi-Unit Residential Buildings" as a companion technical reference for residential building envelope design [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
