# Alberto Pliego Marugán

## Summary

Alberto Pliego Marugán is the first-listed author of the 2024 *Reliability Engineering & System Safety* paper introducing a reinforcement-learning agent for maintenance of deteriorating systems under increasingly imperfect repairs.

## Key facts

- Marugán is the first listed author of "A reinforcement learning agent for maintenance of deteriorating systems with increasingly imperfect repairs" [[sources/arxiv-2505.20725]].
- The paper was published in *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*, volume 252, article 110466, December 2024 (DOI 10.1016/j.ress.2024.110466) [[sources/arxiv-2505.20725]].
- The arXiv preprint (2505.20725) is classified under cs.LG (primary) and math.OC [[sources/arxiv-2505.20725]].
- The paper is co-authored with Jesús M. Pinar-Pérez and Fausto Pedro García Márquez [[sources/arxiv-2505.20725]].
