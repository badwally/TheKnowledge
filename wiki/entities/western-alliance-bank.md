# Western Alliance Bank

## Summary

Western Alliance Bank is a US chartered depository institution that serves the homeowners association (HOA) segment through a dedicated HOA-banking practice anchored by the WA ConnectLive API platform. The bank operates Alliance Association Bank (AAB) as a division dedicated exclusively to the community association industry, founded in 2008 and operating in all 50 states [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]]. The HOA practice is marketed under the Western Alliance Bancorporation umbrella and is positioned as a community-association banking partner whose platform integrates with 11 named community association management (CAM) software products and exposes three treasury services — Lockbox, Positive Pay, and Integrated Payables — to associations and their managing agents [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]]. Western Alliance has been ranked in the Forbes "Best Banks in America" Top 15 consecutively each year from 2016 onward, and #1 on the S&P Global Market Intelligence 50 Largest Public U.S. Banks list for 2019 and 2020 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].

## Key facts

### Corporate

- President and CEO: Kenneth Vecchione, as of May 2022 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Member FDIC [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Operates Alliance Association Bank (AAB) as a division dedicated to the community association industry; AAB was founded in 2008, employed 80 direct staff as of May 2022, and operates in all 50 states under the leadership of President Steve Curley [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].

### Industry recognition

- Ranked in the Top 15 of Forbes's "Best Banks in America" list consecutively each year from 2016 onward [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Named #1 Best-Performing Bank on the S&P Global Market Intelligence 50 Largest Public U.S. Banks list for 2019 and 2020 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Ranked #2 on the same S&P Global Market Intelligence list for 2021 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].

### WA ConnectLive platform

- Operates WA ConnectLive, an API platform marketed to HOAs and CAM operators for managing association finances through a single digital tool [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- The platform was branded as "ConnectLive" (without the "WA" prefix) at the Alliance Association Bank division level in 2022, before the "WA ConnectLive" parent-brand alignment reflected on the 2026 product page [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- WA ConnectLive is positioned by vendor marketing as a "robust" API enabling association financial management through one intuitive digital tool [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Integrates with 11 named CAM software programs: AppFolio, Buildium, C3, Caliber by Frontsteps, CINC, Enumerate, eUnify, Smartwebs, SPI, Vantaca, and VMS [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Offers three named treasury services through the WA ConnectLive ecosystem: Lockbox, Positive Pay, and Integrated Payables [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Lockbox-deposited funds follow Western Alliance Bank's funds-availability policy as outlined in the Deposit Account Agreement Disclosure [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Positive Pay enrollment requires the customer to maintain a checking account and to execute an updated Treasury Management Services agreement [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Integrated Payables carries explicit per-use charges, distinguishing it from bundled treasury services [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Referenced by Dennis Abbott, CMCA, AMS, PCAM (President, Carolinas FirstService Residential) as the depository institution holding over 200 association accounts that complete monthly reconciliations accurately by the second business day of the month [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Vendor product page (westernalliancebancorporation.com/expertise/homeowners-associations/technology/connectlive) is the marketing surface that enumerates integration partners and treasury services [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
