# Optimal predictive maintenance for a nonstationary gamma process

## Summary

A 2025 paper by Yue Wang, Ganlong Wang, Yanxia Wu, Guoyin Zhang, and Rui Zheng proposing a predictive maintenance policy for systems whose degradation follows a nonstationary gamma process, formulated as a semi-Markov decision problem whose decision variables include the maximum number of repairs and the preventive maintenance thresholds, with a coating system used to verify the method.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Yue Wang, Ganlong Wang, Yanxia Wu, Guoyin Zhang, and Rui Zheng [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The paper was published on 7 April 2025 with DOI 10.17531/ein/203543 [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The motivating premise is that nonstationary gamma processes have extensive applications in depicting the degradation of many practical systems [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The paper proposes a predictive maintenance policy that involves various types of maintenance actions for a nonstationary gamma process [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- Periodic inspections fully reveal the degradation levels of the system in the proposed policy [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The model synthesizes information on age, degradation, and the number of conducted preventive maintenance actions for decision-making, which the authors say distinguishes their model from most existing models that consider only degradation states [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The stated objective is to find the maximum number of repairs and the best thresholds for preventive maintenance by minimizing the expected average cost in an infinite time horizon [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The maintenance problem is addressed as a semi-Markov decision problem [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- An optimization algorithm is developed to find the optimal values of the decision variables [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The effectiveness of the proposed method is verified by a coating system case study [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The paper carries 43 references covering condition-based maintenance, prognostics and health management, gamma and Wiener degradation processes, hidden Markov reliability models, opportunistic maintenance, and imperfect maintenance [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
