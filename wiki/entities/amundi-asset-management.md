# Amundi Asset Management

## Summary

Paris-based asset manager; through its Quantitative Research group, publisher of the September 2017 working paper "Understanding the Momentum Risk Premium: An In-Depth Journey Through Trend-Following Strategies," which extends the Bruder-Gaussel (2011) trend-following model to the multivariate case and analyzes the impact of asset universe, correlations, and moving-average choice on the risk/return profile of trend-following strategies [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].

## Key facts

- Quantitative Research group based in Paris; institutional home of five of the six authors of the paper — Paul Jusselin, Edmond Lezmi, Hassan Malongo, Côme Masselin, and Thierry Roncalli [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- The paper acknowledges Alexandre Burgues, Edouard Knockaert, Didier Maillard, Raphaël Sobotka, and Bruno Taillardat for helpful comments [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- The paper is grounded in earlier Amundi research, citing Bruder and Gaussel (2011) as the original univariate framework being extended, and Tung-Lam Dao's 2011 Amundi internship work as the source of some multivariate formulas reused in the paper [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
- Amundi research cited in the paper for the strategic-asset-allocation argument that carry, momentum, and value are three risk premia that should be included in SAA portfolios includes Hamdan et al. (2016), Cazalet and Roncalli (2014), and Roncalli (2017) [[sources/pdf-5a6062a63a4b]].
