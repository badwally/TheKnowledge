# Tom Bell

## Summary

Tom Bell is co-author, with [[entities/emma-wilkins|Dr Emma Wilkins]], of the 2021 *Cycling Physiology & Training Science Guide*, which targets self-coached cyclists and walks through physiology, planning, training zones, and monitoring [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].

## Key facts

- Co-authored the 2021 guide whose stated goal is to help self-coached athletes understand where they are physiologically and what training will move them [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
- The guide makes use of TrainingPeaks-trademarked metrics (TSS, TSB, ATL, CTL, IF, Normalized Power) under attribution [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
