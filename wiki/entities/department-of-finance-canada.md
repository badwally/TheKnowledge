# Department of Finance Canada

## Summary

The Department of Finance Canada is the federal department responsible for policy and legislative/regulatory development of Canada's consumer-driven banking framework [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]]. While day-to-day oversight is delegated to the Bank of Canada, the Department retains the policy-and-rules role and conducts consultations on the framework's scope expansion [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].

## Key facts

- The Department of Finance authored the Budget 2025 framework document setting out the design, governance, scope, accreditation, and common rules of Canada's consumer-driven banking framework [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].
- The Department is conducting 12–18 months of consultation and policy work to advance a second phase of consumer-driven banking — broader functionality (including "write access" / payment initiation) and broader participant scope [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].
- The Department will continue to consult with stakeholders to determine the appropriate timeline for bringing the screen-scraping prohibition into force [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].
- The Department frames the PIPEDA data-mobility amendment as a foundation for cross-sector "open finance" and "open data" frameworks beyond banking [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].
