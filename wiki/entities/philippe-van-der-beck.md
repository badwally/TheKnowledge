# Philippe van der Beck

## Summary

Financial economist at Harvard Business School and lead author of the May 2024 paper "Ponzi Funds," which decomposes active-fund returns into a self-inflated (price-pressure) component and a fundamental component and shows that investors are unable to distinguish between them [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Harvard Business School on the paper [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
- First-listed author of "Ponzi Funds" (May 22, 2024; arXiv:2405.12768) alongside Jean-Philippe Bouchaud and Dario Villamaina [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
- The paper estimates that flows chasing self-inflated returns reallocate roughly $500 million of investor wealth per day in ETFs alone [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
- The paper proposes fund illiquidity as a simple regulatory reporting measure that captures a fund's potential for self-inflated returns [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
