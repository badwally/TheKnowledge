# Emma Wilkins

## Summary

Dr Emma Wilkins is co-author, with [[entities/tom-bell|Tom Bell]], of the 2021 *Cycling Physiology & Training Science Guide*, a self-published reference covering energy-system physiology, periodisation, training zones, and load monitoring [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].

## Key facts

- Holds a doctoral title ("Dr") and writes on cycling physiology aimed at self-coached athletes [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
- Co-authored the 2021 guide that frames training science around three energy systems and TrainingPeaks-style load metrics [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
- The guide is explicitly framed as the authors' personal views and not medical advice [[sources/pdf-21a07e2e3e63]].
