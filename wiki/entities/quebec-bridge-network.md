# Quebec Bridge Network

## Summary

The Quebec Bridge Network refers to the approximately 10,000 bridges located in the Canadian province of Quebec that serve as the case-study population for BayesWorks' research on stochastic, network-scale deterioration modelling using visual inspection data.

## Key facts

- The Quebec bridge network comprises approximately 10,000 bridges located in the province of Quebec [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Some bridges in the network are similar in material or traffic load, while others are completely different from each other [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Each bridge contains two main groups of structural elements: those directly associated with structural safety (such as beams and slabs) and those associated with serviceability (such as safety barriers and pavement elements) [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Each structural category may comprise multiple individual elements per bridge, for example two slabs or twenty beams [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Visual inspections are performed at the element level by a human inspector visiting a bridge in a given year [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Inspection frequency varies by bridge importance, typically every two or three years [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- Some structural elements have intervention records in the database that include both intervention type and date [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
- The network forms the demonstration corpus for the stochastic state-space deterioration model presented by BayesWorks, covering hundreds of thousands of elements and millions of inspections [[sources/yt-vx6ATEoEuUE]].
