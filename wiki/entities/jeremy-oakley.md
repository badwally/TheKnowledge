# Jeremy Oakley

## Summary

Jeremy Oakley is a co-developer of the Sheffield Elicitation Framework (SHELF), originally working in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Sheffield.

## Key facts

- Oakley developed SHELF together with Tony O'Hagan, originally in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Sheffield [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
- Oakley's work on SHELF is described as arising out of a long-standing commitment to research and practice in elicitation [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
