# Gijs van Maanen

## Summary

Gijs van Maanen is a Tilburg-affiliated researcher working on open government data, data governance and data commons, and a co-author of the 2025 Technology and Regulation article "Through thick and thin: data commons, community and the struggle for collective data governance" with Tommaso Fia [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].

## Key facts

- Co-authored the 2025 Technology and Regulation paper that critically examines how the language of community functions in data-commons scholarship and policy [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].
- Jointly argues that analysing the moral-political affordances of community has direct implications for collective-data-governance policy design [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].
- The 2025 article positions data commons within a wider class of collective data-governance mechanisms (trusts, cooperatives, DAOs, unions) offered as alternatives to individualistic data protection and data markets [[sources/web-2025-06-02-56c]].
