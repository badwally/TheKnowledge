# Gabrielle Rives

## Summary

Gabrielle Rives is the first-listed author of the 2025 arXiv preprint introducing Weibull-Tailored Neural Networks (WTNN) for survival analysis of maintained systems.

## Key facts

- Rives is the first listed author of "WTNN: Weibull-Tailored Neural Networks for survival analysis," arXiv 2512.09163, posted 9 December 2025 [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
- The paper is co-authored with Olivier Lopez and Nicolas Bousquet [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
