# Gianpaolo Pulcini

## Summary

Gianpaolo Pulcini is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2022 paper introducing a transformed gamma (TG) process for bounded degradation phenomena, applied to cylinder liner wear in marine diesel engines.

## Key facts

- Pulcini is the third (final) listed author of 'A transformed gamma process for bounded degradation phenomena,' deposited at HAL (hal-04063942) on 7 July 2022 [[sources/web-2022-07-07-ac2]].
- The paper is co-authored with Mitra Fouladirad and Massimiliano Giorgio [[sources/web-2022-07-07-ac2]].
