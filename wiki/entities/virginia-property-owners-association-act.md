# Virginia Property Owners' Association Act (Title 55.1, Chapter 18)

## Summary

The Virginia Property Owners' Association Act is codified at Title 55.1 (Property and Conveyances), Chapter 18 of the Code of Virginia, and governs the formation, operation, and ongoing fiduciary obligations of property owners' associations (POAs) in the Commonwealth [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]]. Within Chapter 18, §55.1-1826 sets the association's annual-budget, reserve-study, and capital-component reserve-funding obligations, with cross-reference to the "capital components" definition at §55.1-1800 [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]]. The Act applies to POAs — Virginia's statutory category for HOAs governing lot-owner communities — distinct from the Virginia Condominium Act, which governs condominium associations under a separate statutory title. The Act is also distinct in regulated-party from the Virginia Common Interest Community Manager Regulations (18VAC48-50), which regulate the third-party manager rather than the association.

## Key facts

- Codified at Title 55.1 (Property and Conveyances), Chapter 18 of the Code of Virginia [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- §55.1-1826 is the section governing annual budget, reserve study, and reserves for capital components [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- §55.1-1826 cross-references §55.1-1800 as the controlling definition of "capital components" for reserve-study and reserve-funding purposes [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- §55.1-1826 was originally enacted as 2002, c. 459, §55-514.1 (predecessor title), amended in 2019 by chapters 33, 44, and 712, and most recently amended in 2024 by chapter 324 [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
