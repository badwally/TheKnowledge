# Canadian Labour Congress

## Summary

The Canadian Labour Congress (CLC) is a national Canadian labour federation that submitted a brief to the INDU committee deeming AIDA insufficient for protecting Canadian workers and recommending the legislation be "reconceived from a human, labour, and privacy rights-based perspective, placing transparency, accountability and consultation at the core of the approach to regulating AI" [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- Submitted a brief to INDU during the AIDA study calling the legislation insufficient for protecting Canadian workers against harmful AI systems [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Recommended AIDA be "reconceived from a human, labour, and privacy rights-based perspective" with transparency, accountability and consultation at the core [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
