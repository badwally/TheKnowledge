# Wim Hof

## Summary

Dutch extreme-cold-exposure practitioner and originator of the Wim Hof Method (WHM); cited by Brass Monkey as the principal influence on its DCE/CWI guide and quoted for the framing "We're the captains of our ship!" — the assertion that conscious choice to enter the cold drives the body's adaptive response [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].

## Key facts

- Originator of the Wim Hof Method (WHM), a combination of cold exposure and breath-work [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Hosts in-person training in Poland; Brass Monkey's founder cites Poland with Hof himself as part of his own WHM journey [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Coined the framing quoted in the guide: "We're the captains of our ship!" — used to assert conscious control over the body's adaptation to cold [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Brass Monkey identifies Wim and the Wim Hof team as primary sources for "mapping the terrain" of cold exposure, alongside Daniel Kluken, Dr Rhonda Patrick, Prof. Huberman, and Tim Ferriss [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
