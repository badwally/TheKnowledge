# Strategic Data Sharing between Competitors (Tsoy & Konstantinov, 2023)

## Summary

"Strategic Data Sharing between Competitors" is a NeurIPS 2023 paper by Nikita Tsoy and Nikola Konstantinov that models the economic incentives for competing firms to pool training data via collaborative learning [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]]. It introduces a three-component framework — firms' production decisions, the effect of additional data on model quality, and the data-sharing negotiation process — and instantiates it on a conventional market model from economic theory to identify which market conditions drive collaboration [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].

## Key facts

- Authored by Nikita Tsoy and Nikola Konstantinov [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
- Posted to arXiv on 25 May 2023 as arxiv:2305.16052 and accepted to NeurIPS 2023 [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
- Primary subject classification cs.LG with cross-listing in cs.GT (game theory) [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
- Frames data sharing between competitors as a dilemma in which collaboration improves a firm's ML model but also benefits competitors and may reduce profits [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
- Introduces a general framework with three components representing the firms' production decisions, the effect of additional data on model quality, and the data-sharing negotiation process [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
- Studies an instantiation of the framework based on a conventional market model from economic theory to identify key factors affecting collaboration incentives [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
- Finds that reduced competition — operationalized as greater similarity dissimilarity (differentiation) between the firms' products — fosters collaboration [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
- Finds that harder learning tasks also foster collaboration [[sources/arxiv-2305.16052]].
