# Dennis Abbott, CMCA, AMS, PCAM

## Summary

Dennis Abbott is the President of Carolinas FirstService Residential and a credentialed community association manager (CMCA, AMS, PCAM). He is named on Western Alliance Bank's WA ConnectLive product page as the customer-testimonial source attesting to the platform's operational utility — specifically, that his Carolinas business operates over 200 association accounts at Western Alliance Bank and completes monthly reconciliations accurately by the second business day of the month, enabling faster financial-report delivery to client associations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].

## Key facts

- Title: President, Carolinas FirstService Residential [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Holds three CAI-conferred credentials: CMCA (Certified Manager of Community Associations), AMS (Association Management Specialist), and PCAM (Professional Community Association Manager) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- His Carolinas business operates over 200 association accounts at Western Alliance Bank as of the source publication [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Credits the WA ConnectLive banking platform with enabling his organization to complete monthly bank reconciliations accurately by the second business day of the month [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Frames the reconciliation-cycle compression as enabling much earlier financial-report delivery to client associations, which clients value [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
