# Rajasekhar Pulikollu

## Summary

Rajasekhar Pulikollu is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2026 paper introducing a Bayesian hierarchical Accelerated Failure Time framework for fleet-scale wind turbine gearbox reliability assessment.

## Key facts

- Pulikollu is the fourth (final) listed author of 'Bayesian Survival Models Reveal Wind-Driven Reliability Patterns in Turbines,' published on 1 May 2026 with DOI 10.1088/1742-6596/3232/1/012018 [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The paper is co-authored with Clément Jacquet, Liliana Haus, and Nicolas Marx Hermoso [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
