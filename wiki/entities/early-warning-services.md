# Early Warning Services (EWS)

## Summary

Early Warning Services is a wholly bank-owned US data and payments utility that operates a cross-competitor data pool: member banks contribute proprietary deposit-risk and fraud data into shared infrastructure used by all members for account-opening risk assessment, fraud detection, and identity verification [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]]. Early Warning markets itself as serving more than 2,500 companies that rely on its intelligence and bank-contributed data to open new accounts, get in front of fraud, and move to faster payments [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].

## Key facts

- Banks began collaborating in the 1990s with the goal of reducing deposit risk and strengthening fraud detection; Early Warning was established to steward this effort on behalf of the banking industry [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- Between 2006 and 2012, Early Warning became wholly bank-owned, which it frames as reinforcing its role within the financial institution ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- Early Warning's core capabilities include fraud detection, deposit-risk management, identity-risk management, and safer account-opening practices [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- In the 2013–2018 period, Early Warning expanded layered, multi-factor authentication to reduce risk across increasingly real-time interactions, supporting financial institutions as banking shifted to mobile [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- Early Warning launched the Zelle® network in the 2013–2018 period to support direct payments between people through their financial institutions [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- In 2024, the Zelle® network operated by Early Warning helped American consumers and small businesses send over $1 trillion [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- In the 2024–present period, Early Warning introduced Paze® as an online checkout solution intended to reduce friction in digital commerce [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- Early Warning positions its purpose as "powering prosperity for all" by supporting how people and institutions participate in the financial system [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
