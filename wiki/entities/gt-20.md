# GT 20 (Grande Traversée de la Corse)

## Summary

The GT 20 ("Grande Traversée de la Corse") is a 600 km, 12-stage cyclo-tourist itinerary that crosses [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] from north to south, accumulating roughly 10,000 m of elevation gain — promoted by Agence du Tourisme de la Corse as the island's signature [[concepts/cyclotourism|cyclotouring]] route, rideable on either a standard or electric bicycle [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].

## Key facts

- The GT 20 covers ~600 km from Bastia to Bonifacio (or vice versa) and is broken into 12 stages with ~10,000 m of cumulative elevation difference [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- It is positioned as accessible to all types of cyclists, with intermediate-level riders typically completing it in 5–8 days and beginners taking longer at a slower pace [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- The route can be ridden on a normal bicycle or an electric bike [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- The 12 stages, with distance / climb / descent, are: Bastia → Centuri (54 km, +676 m / −676 m); Centuri → San Fiurenzu (58 km, +850 m / −849 m); San Fiurenzu → Belgudè (61 km, +1181 m / −885 m); Belgudè → Calinzana (43 km, +463 m / −494 m); Calinzana → Galeria (31 km, +412 m / −676 m); Galeria → Portu (50 km, +722 m / −697 m); Portu → Verghju (33 km, +1478 m / −45 m); Verghju → Corti (51 km, +345 m / −1388 m); Corti → Ghisoni (58 km, +1354 m / −1136 m); Ghisoni → Zicavu (39 km, +721 m / −646 m); Zicavu → Zonza (44 km, +788 m / −737 m); Zonza → Bunifaziu (71 km, +1014 m / −1783 m) [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- The single hardest climbing stage is Portu → Verghju at 33 km with +1478 m of gain, topping out at 1476 m elevation [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- The route is also the venue for the [[entities/corsica-cyclo-gt-20|Corsica Cyclo "GT 20"]] cyclosportive in May [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- An OpenRunner 3D rendering of the route is offered alongside the brochure [[sources/pdf-432fe168a2d1]].
- The GT 20 is additionally promoted on a 2019 "kakemono" (tall hanging-banner) map titled *carte GT20 kakemono*, depicting the full route with Corsican-language place names: Centuri, San Firenze, Bastia, Lisula, Belgudè, Calvi, Calinzana, Galeria, Bocca a Verghju, Portu, Corti, Venacu, Ghisoni, Aiacciu, Zicavu, Zonza, Conca, Porti Vechju, Figari, and Bunifaziu [[sources/pdf-c985b0372d8a]].
- The 2019 kakemono additionally surfaces intermediate towns not enumerated in the 12-stage breakdown — Lisula (Île-Rousse) on the north coast, Venacu in the centre, and Conca and Figari in the south [[sources/pdf-c985b0372d8a]].
- The same kakemono depicts the [[entities/gr20|GR20]] long-distance walking trail running alongside the GT 20 at multiple points across the island, indicating the geographic corridors where the cycling and hiking routes overlap [[sources/pdf-c985b0372d8a]].
