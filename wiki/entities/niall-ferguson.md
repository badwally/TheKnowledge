# Niall Ferguson

## Summary

Historian credited by Zoltan Pozsar in the August 24, 2022 Credit Suisse dispatch "War and Industrial Policy" as the originator of the term "Chimerica" — the U.S.-China commercial union that, alongside Pozsar's own coinage "Eurussia," frames the breakdown of the lowflation world order [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].

## Key facts

- Pozsar credits Ferguson with calling the U.S.-China commercial-and-financial entanglement "Chimerica": "Niall Ferguson called the first one 'Chimerica'. I will call the other one 'Eurussia'" [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
- The Chimerica framing is paired in the dispatch with two giant geo-strategic and geo-economic blocks (Chimerica and Eurussia) that Pozsar argues were both "heavenly matches" entangled commercially as well as financially, and that have both broken down [[sources/pdf-503a04772afb]].
