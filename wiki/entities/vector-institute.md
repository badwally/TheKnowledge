# Vector Institute

## Summary

The Vector Institute is the Toronto-based national AI institute and one of three CIFAR partner institutes delivering the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy alongside Amii and Mila [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].

## Key facts

- The Vector Institute is one of Canada's three national AI institutes (with Amii in Edmonton and Mila in Montréal) under the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- CIFAR led PCAIS Phase 1 in partnership with Amii, Mila and the Vector Institute beginning in 2017 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Under PCAIS Phase 2 (Budget 2021), the Vector Institute shares in a C$60M allocation across the three national AI institutes for AI research commercialization and business adoption [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- As of the June 2022 Phase 2 announcement, the national AI institutes (including Vector) had collectively trained over 1,500 graduate students and post-doctoral fellows since 2017 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
