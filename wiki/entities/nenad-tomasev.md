# Nenad Tomašev

## Summary

Research scientist at Google DeepMind; lead and corresponding author of the 2026 paper "Intelligent AI Delegation," which proposes an adaptive framework for task allocation, authority transfer, accountability, and trust between human and AI delegators and delegatees in complex agentic networks [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Google DeepMind on the paper, listed as corresponding author with email nenadt@google.com [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
- Co-authored "Intelligent AI Delegation" (arXiv:2602.11865v1, dated 2026-02-12) with Matija Franklin and Simon Osindero, all at Google DeepMind [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
- Cited in the paper as a co-author of "Tomasev et al., 2025" on large-scale AI agent coordination in virtual economies, invoked as motivation for studying agent-agent delegation at web scale [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
