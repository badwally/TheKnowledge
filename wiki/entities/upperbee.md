# UpperBee

## Summary

UpperBee is a property management software vendor whose platform spans condominium and rental management, with named sub-products UpperBee Condo, UpperBee Rental, and UpperBee Manager [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]]. The platform is positioned as an "all-in-one" online condo management solution and exposes a documented third-party integration surface across business accounting, payments, virtual meetings, inspections, pre-rental screening, and smart parcel lockers [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]]. Customer references in UpperBee's published testimonial set include Quebec-headquartered firms Wilkar Property Management (positioning as one of the largest property managers in Quebec, expanding into Ontario), Trivium Property Management, SolutionCondo (cited at 15,000 doors of growth over 10 years), Nova Property Management Experts, and The Condo Collective [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

### Product family

- UpperBee Manager — the business-side product that integrates with Xero and QuickBooks for accounting unification across property and business books [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental — the condominium and rental property management sub-products that bind to the smart-locker and screening integrations [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- UpperBee Payment Solution — UpperBee's own online payment platform marketed as "the only online payment platform fully integrated into the accounting system of all your properties managed with UpperBee," supporting rent and condominium fee collections and payments to vendors and stakeholders [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Meeting by UpperBee — virtual owners' meeting product available as a stand-alone solution or integrated with the UpperBee platform; supports video conferences for groups of over 1,000 participants with proxy, quorum, and vote management [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

### Integration partners (third-party)

- Xero — business accounting integration with UpperBee Manager, positioned as automating everyday business tasks and producing up-to-date financials [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- QuickBooks — business accounting integration with UpperBee Manager, positioned as unifying business and client property accounting systems [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- TrackandFix — web and mobile inspection and after-sales follow-up tool, separately integrated with BIM360 Field and Procore [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Trustii — pre-rental screening tool offering bank authentication for identity and financial-reliability validation; Trustii reports integrate directly with UpperBee to enable automatic e-lease generation using pre-entered information [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Snaile Lockers (snailelockers.com) — parcel locker integration with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Expedibox (expedibox.com) — parcel locker integration with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- BlueBox / BlueBits (bluebits.ca) — parcel locker integration with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

### Named customers

- Wilkar Property Management — Quebec-headquartered customer; Michael Wilk (President) credits UpperBee with helping Wilkar become "one of the largest property managers in Quebec" and references planned expansion into the Ontario market [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Trivium Property Management — customer with approximately one year of UpperBee usage as of the testimonial; Chris Andrews credits the platform's accounting tools and centralization of management operation [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Nova Property Management Experts — Joshua VanHooydonk cites onboarding thoroughness and feature alignment with the firm's condo-management philosophy [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- SolutionCondo — has used UpperBee since the company's beginning; Elise Beauchesne states the firm grew "from 0 to 15k doors in 10 years" using the software [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- The Condo Collective — Mark Blanchette positions UpperBee as "the only software that fully supports our mission" of modern condo management [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

### Positioning

- Markets itself under the "all-in-one" CAM software positioning, with the integration page framed as evidence of breadth across operational, financial, and resident-facing workflows [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Three of three smart-locker partners are positioned with integration depth into both UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental, indicating parcel/package management is treated as a horizontally productized integration category rather than a single-vendor partnership [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Customer concentration in published testimonials is heavily Quebec-weighted (Wilkar, SolutionCondo, The Condo Collective), consistent with a Quebec-rooted vendor footprint with Ontario expansion narrative through Wilkar [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Page is vendor marketing material; technical details (API surfaces, data models, money-flow design specifics) and regulatory positioning are not disclosed despite the platform operating in QC/ON [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
