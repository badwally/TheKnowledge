# Resource Property Management

## Summary

Resource Property Management is a community association management (CAM) operator identified as a Vantaca customer on the Vantaca features page, with Debbie Reinhardt named in the testimonial copy and attributed as the source of the financial-close-cycle quotation about Vantaca [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key facts

- Identified as a CAM operator and Vantaca customer in published Vantaca marketing copy [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Debbie Reinhardt is named in the Vantaca testimonial as the spokesperson representing Resource Property Management [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The operator's published testimonial attributes Vantaca with a financial-statement production cycle of approximately 10 days, contrasted against a prior baseline where statement production took "almost the whole month" — a roughly 3x close-cycle compression attributed to the platform [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The Vantaca features page does not specify Resource Property Management's jurisdiction, association portfolio size, headcount, or service-line scope [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
