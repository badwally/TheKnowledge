# Kids First Data Resource Center (Kids First DRC)

## Summary

The Kids First Data Resource Center (Kids First DRC) is a cloud-based biomedical data platform funded by the NIH Common Fund that hosts pediatric genomic data, focusing on data consented for disease-specific research [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]]. It is one of the five NIH cloud-based platforms surveyed by Dahlquist, Nelson, and Fullerton (2023) [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].

## Key facts

- Primary funder is the NIH Common Fund [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Hosts pediatric data consented only for disease-specific research [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Data Use Limitations (DULs) that impede the ability to access, use, or analyze data will not be prioritized for ingestion [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Among Dahlquist et al.'s 94 reviewed documents, seven pertained to Kids First DRC — the smallest documentary footprint of the five surveyed platforms [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
