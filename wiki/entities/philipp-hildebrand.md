# Philipp Hildebrand

## Summary

Vice Chairman of BlackRock; named contributor on the BlackRock Investment Institute's "2023 Global Outlook: A new investment playbook," which argues the Great Moderation is over and a new investment playbook is needed for a regime of greater macro and market volatility [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].

## Key facts

- Listed title on the 2023 Global Outlook: Vice Chairman, BlackRock [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
- Named as one of six contributors to the outlook alongside Jean Boivin, Wei Li, Alex Brazier, Vivek Paul, and Scott Thiel [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
