# Zhen Hu

## Summary

Zhen Hu is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2025 IMAC conference paper on degradation model correction of miter gates through the synthesis of hybrid modeling with recursive Bayesian state estimation.

## Key facts

- Hu is the fourth (final) listed author of 'Degradation Model Correction of Miter Gates Through Synthesis of Hybrid Modeling with Recursive Bayesian State Estimation' [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The paper appears as Article 5 in *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3* — the proceedings of the 43rd IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics, 2025 — with DOI 10.13052/97887-438-0148-1_5 [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The paper is co-authored with Zhao Zhao, Yichao Zeng, and Michael D. Todd [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
