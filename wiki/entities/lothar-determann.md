# Lothar Determann

## Summary

Lothar Determann is a legal scholar who authored "No One Owns Data," a 2019 Hastings Law Journal article arguing that data is not — and should not be — subject to property rights in either the United States or the European Union [[sources/web-2019-01-23-bbd]].

## Key facts

- Author of "No One Owns Data," published as 70 Hastings L.J. 1 (2019), in the UC Law Journal (Hastings) repository [[sources/web-2019-01-23-bbd]].
- Argues unambiguously, with respect to both existing law and future lawmaking in the US and EU — jurisdictions with notably divergent attitudes to privacy, property, and individual freedoms — that data should not be subject to property rights [[sources/web-2019-01-23-bbd]].
- Characterises the existing legal landscape as one in which the competing interests of individuals, businesses, governments, and the public are protected by "an intricate net of existing laws, which deliberately refrain from granting property rights in data" [[sources/web-2019-01-23-bbd]].
