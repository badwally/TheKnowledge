# Genispec

## Summary

Genispec is a Québec-based consulting engineering firm whose engineers are members of the Ordre des ingénieurs du Québec (OIQ), specializing in regulatory-compliance inspections and studies for divided co-ownerships and other building classes under Québec's Bill 16 (Law 16) and Bill 122 (Law 122) regimes [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].

## Key facts

- Headquartered in Québec; staffed by building engineers who are members of the OIQ [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Provides façade inspections and underground parking inspections under Québec Law 122 [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Provides reserve fund studies and maintenance logbooks (*carnets d'entretien*) under Québec Law 16 (Bill 16 / Decree 991-2025) [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Inspects commercial, multi-residential, industrial, and condominium buildings [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- Issues building condition certificates and conducts pre-delivery inspections [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
- All reports are validated by a qualified (OIQ-member) engineer; published articles are reviewed and approved by a qualified team member [[sources/web-2025-08-06-94b]].
