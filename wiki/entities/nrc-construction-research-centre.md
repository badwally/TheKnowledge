# National Research Council Canada Construction Research Centre

## Summary

The National Research Council Canada Construction Research Centre is the construction-research arm of Canada's federal National Research Council and the publisher of the 2025 Service Life Dataset for Non-Structural Building Envelope Materials [[sources/web-2026-05-10-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-4ef]]. As a national-laboratory publisher of Reference Service Life (RSL) data, it is the federal-tier Canadian counterpart to BC Housing's provincial Maintenance Matters series and a primary candidate source for envelope-component prior calibration in the condo-capital-infra engine [[sources/web-2026-05-10-f34]]. A 2026 methodology publication by Sara Sadeghi (DOI 10.4224/40003911) complements the dataset by documenting how it was compiled and analyzing data-quality limitations in the underlying EPD source materials [[sources/web-2026-05-11-852]].

## Key facts

- Federal construction-research centre within the National Research Council Canada; cited affiliation reads "National Research Council Canada. Construction" [[sources/web-2026-05-10-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-4ef]].
- Publishes building-materials Reference Service Life (RSL) datasets through the NRC Research Data collection in the NRC Digital Repository [[sources/web-2026-05-10-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-4ef]].
- Authored a 2025 Reference Service Life dataset for non-structural building envelope materials whose subject scope includes Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) [[sources/web-2026-05-10-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-4ef]].
- Identified researchers associated with the envelope-RSL work: Sara Sadeghi, Marzieh Riahinezhad, Elnaz Esmizadeh, and Michael A. Lacasse (all with ORCID identifiers attached to the dataset record) [[sources/web-2026-05-10-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-f34]] [[sources/web-2026-05-11-4ef]].
- Sara Sadeghi (NRC Canada Construction) is the cited author of a 2026 companion methodology publication, "Reference Service Life Dataset for non-structural building envelope materials: current state, knowledge gaps and inconsistencies" (DOI 10.4224/40003911), which documents the dataset compilation methodology and surfaces RSL data-quality limitations in EPDs [[sources/web-2026-05-11-852]].
- The Centre's published RSL work is positioned by its authors as support for Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of buildings in the context of Canada's Emissions Reduction Plan (40% greenhouse-gas reduction 2005 to 2030), reflecting a federal climate-policy frame around the construction-research mandate [[sources/web-2026-05-11-852]].
