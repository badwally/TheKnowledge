# G2

## Summary

G2 is a peer-to-peer software review platform that aggregates customer reviews and issues quarterly industry "Leader" awards across software categories, including property management [[sources/web-2025-01-13-d27]]. G2 describes itself as the largest and most trusted software marketplace and reports that more than 1 million professionals use the platform every year to inform software-purchase decisions [[sources/web-2025-01-13-d27]].

## Key facts

- Operates a quarterly Leader award program; Winter 2025 winners were selected based on reviews collected through November 19, 2024 [[sources/web-2025-01-13-d27]].
- Leader designations are assigned via proprietary algorithms that score market presence and customer satisfaction from review data [[sources/web-2025-01-13-d27]].
- Market-presence inputs include website traffic, new-visitor growth, company age, number of employees, website authority, and number of objective reviews from current customers [[sources/web-2025-01-13-d27]].
- The Winter 2025 property-management category contained 435 competing software companies, of which 15 received Leader designations [[sources/web-2025-01-13-d27]].
- Self-describes as the largest and most trusted software marketplace, with more than 1 million professionals using G2 every year [[sources/web-2025-01-13-d27]].
