# Joint Committee on Structural Safety (JCSS)

## Summary

The Joint Committee on Structural Safety (JCSS) is a long-standing committee whose work is the thematic subject of the March 2025 special issue of *Structural Safety*, with contributions assessing JCSS's contribution to structural safety over half a century, the future development of the JCSS Probabilistic Model Code, and a perspective on the committee's past, present, and future.

## Key facts

- The March 2025 issue of *Structural Safety* is a special issue themed on the Joint Committee on Structural Safety, with a preface titled "Preface of the Special Issue: The Joint Committee of Structural Safety: past, present and a perspective on the future" edited by Köhler, Vrouwenvelder, Faber, and Limongelli (article 102542) [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- The special issue includes "The JCSS – A major contributor to structural safety through half a century" by Vrouwenvelder, Baker, and Faber (article 102468), framing JCSS as a half-century-long contributor to structural safety [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- The JCSS maintains a Probabilistic Model Code, whose future developments are discussed in "The JCSS probabilistic model Code, future developments" by Steenbergen and Vrouwenvelder (article 102540) in the same issue [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
