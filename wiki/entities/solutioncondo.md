# SolutionCondo

## Summary

SolutionCondo is a Quebec condo management firm and a published UpperBee customer; SolutionCondo's representative Elise Beauchesne states the firm grew "from 0 to 15k doors in 10 years" using UpperBee, making SolutionCondo the largest-scale customer cited by name in UpperBee's integrations-page testimonial set [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Condo management firm and published UpperBee customer [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Has used UpperBee "since the beginning" of the firm's existence per Beauchesne's testimonial [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Reported scale: grew from 0 to 15,000 doors in 10 years using UpperBee [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Largest-scale customer cited by name in UpperBee's published integrations-page testimonial set [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Beauchesne's articulated value proposition for UpperBee: the software development and support/training teams listen and adjust the software and support tools to SolutionCondo's needs in the field [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
