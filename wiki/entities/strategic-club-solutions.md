# Strategic Club Solutions

## Summary

Strategic Club Solutions (SCS) is a club-management consulting firm specializing in operational and strategic planning for Large Scale Clubs and member communities [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]]. In April 2025 SCS announced a strategic partnership with SmartProperty to embed SmartProperty's Living Reserve Study product inside SCS's Navigator platform for club and community clients [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].

## Key facts

- Strategic Club Solutions is the counterparty named in the April 7, 2025 SmartProperty partnership announcement targeting Large Scale Clubs [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- The firm describes itself as bringing "expertise in operational and strategic planning" to the partnership, positioned as a complement to SmartProperty's financial and asset management software [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- SCS operates the Navigator product, the platform through which the joint SmartProperty + SCS offering is delivered to clubs [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- Ryan Doerr is President and CEO of Strategic Club Solutions [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- SCS positions Navigator as a data-driven decision platform for club and community management, integrating financial planning, document management, scenario planning, project management, and decision support [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
