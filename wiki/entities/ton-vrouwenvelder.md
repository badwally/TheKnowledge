# Ton Vrouwenvelder

## Summary

Ton Vrouwenvelder (also indexed as A. C. W. M. Vrouwenvelder) is a structural reliability researcher closely associated with the Joint Committee on Structural Safety (JCSS), and contributes four times to the March 2025 JCSS-themed special issue of *Structural Safety*, including the historical retrospective on JCSS and the future-developments article on the JCSS Probabilistic Model Code.

## Key facts

- Vrouwenvelder is the first listed author of "Interpretation of probability in structural safety – A philosophical conundrum" (article 102473) in the March 2025 issue of *Structural Safety*, co-authored with Beck, Proske, Faber, Köhler, Schubert, Straub, and Teichgräber [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Vrouwenvelder is the first listed author of "The JCSS – A major contributor to structural safety through half a century" (article 102468), co-authored with Baker and Faber [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Vrouwenvelder is the second listed author of "The JCSS probabilistic model Code, future developments" (article 102540), co-authored with Steenbergen [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Vrouwenvelder is the second listed editor of "Preface of the Special Issue: The Joint Committee of Structural Safety: past, present and a perspective on the future" (article 102542), co-edited with Köhler, Faber, and Limongelli [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
