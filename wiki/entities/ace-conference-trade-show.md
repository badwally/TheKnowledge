# ACE Conference & Trade Show

## Summary

The ACE Conference & Trade Show is an Alberta condominium-industry conference and trade show characterized by UpperBee's April 1, 2026 announcement as "a major industry event" that "brings together condominium board members, property managers, and service" providers across Alberta [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]]. The 2026 edition was held Saturday, May 9, 2026 at the Edmonton Convention Centre, with UpperBee sponsoring the VIP Lounge [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
