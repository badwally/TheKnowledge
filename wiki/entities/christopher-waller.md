# Christopher Waller

## Summary

Member of the Federal Reserve Board of Governors quoted in the July 13, 2022 Q3-2022 IACFM presentation from a Virtual NABE Event on July 7, 2022, arguing that the Fed should look seriously at CPI alongside its formal PCE target because CPI better represents the inflation experience of lower- and moderate-income households [[sources/pdf-853a7755a470]].

## Key facts

- Identified in the IACFM presentation as a Member of the Federal Reserve Board of Governors [[sources/pdf-853a7755a470]].
- Quoted from a Virtual NABE (National Association for Business Economics) Event on July 7, 2022 [[sources/pdf-853a7755a470]].
- Said: "I look a lot at CPI and I know PCE headline is our number but I like both of them. The CPI is more heavily weighted towards shelter and one reason I like to look at that is that's more consistent with what lower and moderate income groups face" [[sources/pdf-853a7755a470]].
- Argued that lower and moderate income groups "face a much bigger share of their disposable income going to shelter, food and energy than upper income groups do" [[sources/pdf-853a7755a470]].
- Concluded: "So I don't mind CPI as being kind of a good example of what lower and moderate income group in terms of inflation so I don't dismiss it. I look at it very seriously" [[sources/pdf-853a7755a470]].
