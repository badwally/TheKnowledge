# Robeco Quantitative Investing

## Summary

Quantitative-investment arm of Robeco, headquartered in Rotterdam; institutional home of two of the four authors of the 2020 paper "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" — Guido Baltussen and Martin Martens [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].

## Key facts

- Listed address: Weena 850, 3014 DA Rotterdam [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- Listed as the institutional affiliation of Guido Baltussen (jointly with Erasmus School of Economics) and of Martin Martens on "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" (26 August 2020; SSRN abstract 3760365) [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
