# U.S. Department of Energy (DOE)

## Summary

The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) is the federal agency that co-leads the NAIRR Secure focus area with the National Institutes of Health and contributes large-scale computing infrastructure to NAIRR [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].

## Key facts

- DOE provides allocations on the Summit pre-exascale supercomputer as well as access to AI testbeds, training resources, and associated expertise to NAIRR [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- DOE co-leads the NAIRR Secure pilot effort with NIH [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
