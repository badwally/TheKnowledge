# MKT MediaStats

## Summary

Quantitative research firm specializing in media-coverage analytics; institutional home of three of the five co-authors of the 2022 paper "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" — Gideon Ozik (founder and managing partner), Ronnie Sadka (founder and managing partner), and Xiaoxia Lou (head of research) [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key facts

- Office address listed on the paper: 140 Mt. Auburn St, Cambridge, MA, with an additional Newark, DE address (42 Amstel Ave) tied to Lou's University of Delaware affiliation [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Founded and managed by Gideon Ozik and Ronnie Sadka, with Xiaoxia Lou serving as head of research [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- The firm's underlying dataset, used by the 2022 paper, collects articles every day from over 150,000 global digital media sources, organized into reservoirs by general topic (e.g., domestic matters, international politics) and asset coverage (e.g., corporations, currencies, country equity indexes) [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Uses proprietary algorithms based on keyword searches and textual conditions to tag each article for relevance to a predefined set of 73 narratives [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Assigns each article a sentiment score, adjusted for the overall daily tone of the articles in the reservoir from which the article is extracted [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
