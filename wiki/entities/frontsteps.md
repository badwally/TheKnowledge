# FRONTSTEPS

## Summary

FRONTSTEPS is a US-based community association management (CAM) software vendor that markets an HOA community management platform consolidating community operations, financial management, payment processing, and safety/security into a single product surface [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]]. The vendor positions its platform as serving three customer segments: community management companies, community management professionals (CAMs), and homeowners and association board members [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]]. The homepage markets "AI-powered insights" as a component of the financial management module, but provides no independently verifiable claims about customer base, architecture, integration partners, or regulatory posture [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].

## Key facts

### Vendor identity and positioning

- Operates the frontsteps.com web domain and self-positions as a provider of HOA community management software [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
- Markets the platform under the tagline "Better tools. Better communities." framing security, payments, accounting, and resident engagement as a "single streamlined flow" [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
- Public-facing platform branding marketed as the "FRONTSTEPS Ecosystem" [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].

### Product surface (four marketed modules)

- Community Operations — marketed as providing homeowners with 24/7 mobile access, supporting CAMs in the field, and helping teams work smarter [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
- Financial Management — marketed as automating workflows, digitizing documents, and using "AI-powered insights" to simplify financial tasks [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
- Payment Processing — marketed as accepting and managing online payments securely covering both accounts payable (AP) and accounts receivable (AR) [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
- Safety & Security — marketed as controlling visitor access and monitoring community security within the same app [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].

### Target customer segments

- Community Management Companies — positioned around connected solutions, revenue and profitability lift via efficiency gains [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
- Community Management Professionals (CAMs) — positioned as the "heroes of the community" whose needs drive platform development [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
- Homeowners and Association Board Members — positioned around easy-to-use tools for improving community living [[sources/web-2024-09-04-5f4]].
