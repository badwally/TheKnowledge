# Ardoor

## Summary

Ardoor is a community-association-services organization and joint CINC Systems + ONR Applications customer surfaced as the load-bearing named customer-validation reference in CINC's January 6, 2025 acquisition announcement of ONR [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]]. CEO Steve Weclew provided the customer-side endorsement of the CINC-ONR combined platform — "This is a game changer for our communities. CINC and ONR together are a powerful force. This combined solution will revolutionize how our communities operate and the possibilities for increased resident engagement and improved services are endless" — establishing Ardoor as part of the pre-acquisition customer cohort that operated on both CINC and ONR systems and therefore experienced the integration narrative CINC is operationalizing under Hg Capital sponsorship [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].

## Key facts

- Joint CINC Systems and ONR Applications customer as of January 6, 2025 — operating both platforms prior to the formal CINC-ONR acquisition and explicitly identified by CINC as having "seen the benefits of both systems" [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- CEO: Steve Weclew [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Public CINC + ONR endorsement at acquisition (Steve Weclew): "This is a game changer for our communities. CINC and ONR together are a powerful force. This combined solution will revolutionize how our communities operate and the possibilities for increased resident engagement and improved services are endless" [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Operates communities served by both CINC's back-end accounting/management platform and ONR's resident-engagement platform — surfacing Ardoor as part of the cross-platform customer cohort referenced by CINC as the basis for continued integration effort post-acquisition ("That continued effort will include working closely with clients who have seen the benefits of both systems") [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
