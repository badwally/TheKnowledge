# Ben Currin

## Summary

Ben Currin is the Chief Executive Officer of Vantaca, the AI-first community association management platform. He was identified as CEO at the November 19, 2024 Vantaca Vision annual customer conference where Vantaca announced its acquisition of HOAi [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]], and remained CEO at the October 15, 2025 announcement of Vantaca's $300+ million minority growth investment at a $1.25 billion valuation led by Cove Hill Partners [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. Currin is part of the founding management team that continues to lead Vantaca's strategy and operations following the Cove Hill transaction [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].

## Key facts

- Title: Chief Executive Officer of Vantaca, confirmed November 20, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]] and October 15, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Identified as part of the founding management team continuing to lead the company's strategy and operations after the Cove Hill-led growth round [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Articulated Vantaca's strategic positioning as "defining the future of community management through AI-native automation and operational excellence" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Characterized Vantaca's product differentiation as delivering "true agentic AI capabilities that transform daily operations rather than just digitizing old processes" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Framed the Cove Hill / JMI investment as accelerating Vantaca's mission to help management companies scale profitably while delivering exceptional homeowner experiences [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Characterized the November 2024 HOAi acquisition as "a game-changer in our mission to build the future of community management" [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Articulated the HOAi acquisition rationale: "By integrating HOAi's advanced AI capabilities, while allowing it to operate independently, we ensure rapid development and deployment of next-generation solutions that will significantly enhance our customers' experience and operational efficiency" [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
