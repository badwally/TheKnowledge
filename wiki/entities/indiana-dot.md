# Indiana Department of Transportation (INDOT)

## Summary

The Indiana Department of Transportation (INDOT) is the U.S. state-level transportation agency that supplied the highway pavement section data used as the case study in Madanat and Mishalani's 1998 *Journal of Infrastructure Systems* paper correcting for selectivity bias in pavement maintenance and rehabilitation (M&R) effectiveness modelling.

## Key facts

- The Indiana Department of Transportation provided the data set used for model estimation in Madanat and Mishalani's 1998 paper on selectivity bias in modelling highway pavement maintenance effectiveness [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
- The INDOT data supported a case study demonstrating the applicability and value of a structured econometric approach combining a discrete M&R-choice model with continuous pavement-response models, one per activity [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
