# Association of Professional Reserve Preparers (APRA)

## Summary

The Association of Professional Reserve Preparers (APRA) is a US-based professional association of reserve study preparers, cited by the California Department of Real Estate in its August 2010 Reserve Study Guidelines alongside the Community Associations Institute (CAI) as one of the two industry-standard reference frameworks for reserve study practice in California [[sources/pdf-dre-2010-reserve-study-guidelines]]. APRA is the second pillar (alongside CAI) of the methodology-baseline framework that California state-government interpretive guidance defers to for Civil Code § 1365 / § 1365.5 (predecessor to Davis-Stirling § 5550) reserve-study compliance [[sources/pdf-dre-2010-reserve-study-guidelines]].

## Key facts

- The 2010 California DRE Reserve Study Guidelines explicitly state that the Department's August 2010 revision was updated to ensure alignment with current California Law and the guidelines of both the Association of Professional Reserve Preparers (APRA) and the Community Associations Institute (CAI) [[sources/pdf-dre-2010-reserve-study-guidelines]].
- APRA is cited as a parallel industry standards body to CAI in California state-government interpretive guidance on Civil Code § 1365 / § 1365.5 reserve-study practice — making APRA, along with CAI, one of the two industry bodies whose methodology baselines are explicitly endorsed by the state-government interpretive framework [[sources/pdf-dre-2010-reserve-study-guidelines]].
- Roy Helsing — the individual who performed the August 2010 DRE Guidelines revision — holds the "PRA, RS" credentials (Professional Reserve Analyst, Reserve Specialist), credentials associated with the APRA professional designation regime, demonstrating cross-credentialing of APRA practitioners into CAI-aligned state-government interpretive work [[sources/pdf-dre-2010-reserve-study-guidelines]].
