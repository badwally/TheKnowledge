# CARE-W (Computer Aided Rehabilitation for Water Networks)

## Summary

CARE-W (Computer Aided Rehabilitation for Water Networks) is an EU research project whose methodologies were integrated by the subsequent AWARE-P platform for urban water infrastructure asset management.

## Key facts

- CARE-W (Computer Aided Rehabilitation for Water Networks) was an EU research project under grant EVK1-CT-2000-00053, published by IWA Publishing in 2005 as a 208-page volume edited by S. Saegrov (ISBN 1843390914) [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- CARE-W is cited as one of the prior R&D projects whose methodologies AWARE-P integrates, providing analytical foundations for rehabilitation planning of urban water distribution networks [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
