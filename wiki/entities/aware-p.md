# AWARE-P

## Summary

AWARE-P is an open-source software system and methodology for urban water infrastructure asset management. It provides a set of tools and models supporting the analyses and decision-making involved in utility planning, implementing a system-centric approach that balances performance (level-of-service), risk, and financial effort across multiple planning horizons.

## Key facts

- AWARE-P is an open-source software system based on a set of tools and models that assist in the analyses and decision support involved in the planning process for urban water services [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- AWARE-P was developed in response to high levels of deferred maintenance and rehabilitation and overwhelming investment needs in urban water services infrastructure, which together demand wise spending and innovative, efficient planning [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- AWARE-P implements system-centric asset management methods rather than component-centric approaches such as like-for-like prioritization and replacement, aiming to ensure the best possible compromise between performance (level-of-service), risk, and financial effort [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- The AWARE-P software employs various models for assessing water systems over multiple planning horizons, improving diagnostic capabilities for utility decision-making [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- AWARE-P integrates methodologies from prior R&D projects, with explicit continuity from the EU-funded CARE-W and CARE-S frameworks, and is designed for usability and compatibility with utility data systems [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- AWARE-P's methodology emphasises metrics including hydraulic performance, supply interruption risk, and service cost, derived from comprehensive simulation models such as EPANET [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- The 2012 paper introducing AWARE-P and its design requirements was authored by Diogo Vitorino, with the Baseform organisation (baseform.org) referenced as the continuing host of the software [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
