# Clément Jacquet

## Summary

Clément Jacquet is the first-listed author of the 2026 paper introducing a Bayesian hierarchical Accelerated Failure Time model for wind turbine gearbox reliability.

## Key facts

- Jacquet is the first listed author of 'Bayesian Survival Models Reveal Wind-Driven Reliability Patterns in Turbines,' published on 1 May 2026 with DOI 10.1088/1742-6596/3232/1/012018 [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The paper is co-authored with Liliana Haus, Nicolas Marx Hermoso, and Rajasekhar Pulikollu [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
