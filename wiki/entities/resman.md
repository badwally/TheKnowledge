# ResMan

## Summary

ResMan is a multifamily property management software product within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio, serving US rental-housing operators including HUD, LIHTC, and Rural Development affordable-housing programs [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- ResMan operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] residential portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- ResMan's next-generation Leasing Assistant is powered by [[entities/anyone-home]] technology [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- ResMan Websites uses [[entities/razz-interactive]] AI-enabled tools (Razz CMS) for SEO-optimized content, meta tags, and descriptions [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- ResMan integrates [[entities/western-reporting]] Digital Income Verification and (in early 2026) Identity Verification within the screening workflow [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- ResMan Affordable is the product line dedicated to HUD, LIHTC, and Rural Development properties, with a Nov 2025 update featuring a new certification workflow wizard, affordable-housing waitlist enhancements, TIC form updates, and expanded import/export capabilities [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- ResMan is positioned exclusively as a multifamily / affordable-housing rental-operations product; no condo/HOA capabilities are announced or implied in [[entities/inhabit]]'s OpTech 2025 product slate [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
