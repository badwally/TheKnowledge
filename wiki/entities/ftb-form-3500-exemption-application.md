# FTB Form 3500 — Exemption Application

## Summary

FTB Form 3500 (*Exemption Application*) is the application form a California homeowners' association must file with the California Franchise Tax Board to obtain a state Exempt Determination Letter granting tax-exempt status under California Revenue and Taxation Code Section 23701t [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]]. Without an FTB-issued Exempt Determination Letter resulting from a Form 3500 filing, an HOA remains subject to the California minimum franchise or income tax [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].

## Key facts

- Form number: FTB Form 3500 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Title: *Exemption Application* [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Issued by the California Franchise Tax Board [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Required for California-state tax-exempt status; FTB issues an Exempt Determination Letter upon approval [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- FTB recommends submission at least 90 calendar days before tax-exempt status is needed to allow adequate processing time [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Mailing address: Franchise Tax Board, PO Box 1286, Rancho Cordova CA 95741-1286 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Form retrieval: ftb.ca.gov, search keyword "3500" [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Required content elements: all General Questions in Parts I through VI; Section T of the Specific Sections Questions; original signature of an authorized individual (elected officer, director, or authorized representative) [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Organizations not receiving California tax-exempt status remain subject to the minimum franchise or income tax [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- The Form 3500 application is the only path to California HOA tax-exempt status; California does not provide an annual-election mechanism analogous to IRS Form 1120H [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
