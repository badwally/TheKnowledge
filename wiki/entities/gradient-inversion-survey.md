# A Survey on Gradient Inversion: Attacks, Defenses and Future Directions (Zhang et al., 2022)

## Summary

"A Survey on Gradient Inversion: Attacks, Defenses and Future Directions" is a 2022 survey paper authored by Rui Zhang, Song Guo, Junxiao Wang, Xin Xie, and Dacheng Tao [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]]. The paper surveys gradient inversion (GradInv) attacks — a class of attacks that recover training samples from gradients exchanged during distributed training — together with emerging defense strategies and open research directions [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]]. It was accepted at IJCAI-ECAI 2022 [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].

## Key facts

- Authors: Rui Zhang, Song Guo, Junxiao Wang, Xin Xie, and Dacheng Tao [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].
- Venue: IJCAI-ECAI 2022 [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].
- arXiv ID: 2206.07284, primary category cs.LG, published 2022-06-15 [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].
- The paper proposes a taxonomy that partitions GradInv attacks into two paradigms: iteration-based attacks and recursion-based attacks [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].
- The paper identifies three critical ingredients of iteration-based attacks: data initialization, model training, and gradient matching [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].
- The paper organizes emerging defenses into three perspectives: data obscuration, model improvement, and gradient protection [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].
- The paper closes by discussing promising directions and open problems for further GradInv research [[sources/arxiv-2206.07284]].
