# First Nations Information Governance Centre (FNIGC)

## Summary

The First Nations Information Governance Centre (FNIGC) is a First Nations–led organization that, with its regional partners, developed the First Nations Data Governance Strategy (FNDGS) and delivered it to the Government of Canada in spring 2020 [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].

## Key facts

- FNIGC partnered with regional First Nations entities to develop the First Nations Data Governance Strategy (FNDGS) [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- FNIGC and its regional partners completed the FNDGS in two years rather than the originally-funded three, attributing the accelerated timeline to the strategic importance of the priority, the state of community readiness, and the advancement of the New Fiscal Relationship between Canada and First Nations [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- FNIGC is advancing a vision of a national network of interconnected, First Nations–led Regional Information Governance Centres (RIGCs) [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- FNIGC frames the RIGC network as the operational vehicle through which First Nations' ownership and control of their data informs decisions, improves outcomes, and enables collaboration on common priorities across communities [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
