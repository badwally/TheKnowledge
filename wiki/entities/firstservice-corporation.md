# FirstService Corporation

## Summary

FirstService Corporation (NASDAQ and TSX: FSV) is a publicly-listed North American leader in providing essential property services to a wide range of residential and commercial clients [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]]. Its FirstService Residential subsidiary is a major North American condominium and HOA property manager and the CAM operating division on which the condo-capital-infra Year-3 exit thesis's secondary-acquirer-target framing is anchored [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]] [[sources/web-2026-02-04-5bb]].

## Key facts

- Publicly-listed corporation with tickers NASDAQ: FSV and TSX: FSV [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]] [[sources/web-2026-02-04-5bb]].
- Self-characterized as a North American leader in providing essential property services to a wide range of residential and commercial clients [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- FirstService Residential is the community association management operating division and a subsidiary of FirstService Corporation [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]] [[sources/web-2026-02-04-5bb]].
- Operates ancillary financial- and energy-service brands referenced as market-leading programs within FirstService Residential's offering: FirstService Financial and FirstService Energy [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- The FirstService Residential subsidiary reported FY2025 revenue of $2.3 billion (up 7% over 2024) with 4% organic growth, FY2025 EBITDA up 13% year over year, full-year EBITDA margin of 9.8% (upper end of the 9-10% management-stated band), and management guidance for mid-single-digit organic growth in 2026 [[sources/web-2026-02-04-5bb]].
- In May 2025, FirstService Residential acquired Edmonton-based Core Real Estate Group, pushing its Canadian residential-units-under-management portfolio past 250,000 — a Canadian property-management consolidation milestone for the parent FirstService Corporation and a signal of active platform expansion in Western Canada ahead of the condo-capital-infra ADR-0004 Canada-first GTM sequence [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
