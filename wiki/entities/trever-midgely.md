# Trever Midgely

## Summary

Trever Midgely is identified as a representative of HOA Strategies in the customer testimonial copy published on the Vantaca features page, attributed as the source of HOA Strategies' published quotation about Vantaca's work-queue impact on day-to-day operations [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key facts

- Attributed quotation: "The biggest impact to our team's day-to-day operations has been Vantaca's work queue. Our team knows exactly what's showing up on their desktop, what's in their queue, and what needs to get done in a timely manner. Those workflows put the right job on the right desk for each employee, so things get done quickly and efficiently." [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]]
- The Vantaca features page does not specify Midgely's title, role, or tenure at HOA Strategies [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
