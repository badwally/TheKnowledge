# Julien Delavande

## Summary

AI researcher affiliated with Hugging Face and ENS Paris-Saclay; lead author of the 2025 paper "Video Killed the Energy Budget," which characterizes the latency and energy consumption of state-of-the-art open-source text-to-video models [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Hugging Face and ENS Paris-Saclay, with email julien.delavande@ens-paris-saclay.fr [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- Listed as first author of "Video Killed the Energy Budget: Characterizing the Latency and Power Regimes of Open Text-to-Video Models," presented at the NeurIPS 2025 Workshop "What Makes a Good Video: Next Practices in Video Generation and Evaluation" [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- Co-authored the paper with Regis Pierrard and Sasha Luccioni, both at Hugging Face [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
