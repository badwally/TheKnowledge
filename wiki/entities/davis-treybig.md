# Davis Treybig

## Summary

Author at Innovation Endeavors of "State of Foundation Models, 2025," a June 2025 industry survey of foundation-model scaling, training economics, reasoning models, and AI-native applications [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the author of "State of Foundation Models, 2025," dated June 2025, affiliated with Innovation Endeavors [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Argues in the report that post-training "may become more important than pre-training," driven by reinforcement learning and reward models for reasoning systems [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Argues that AI-native organizations "will look very different" — flatter teams of capable generalists will become the norm as generative AI lessens the value of specialized skills, and roles such as product, design, and engineering will blur [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Frames the moment as the fastest rate of adoption of any new technology of all time, citing ChatGPT reaching 100M users in 60 days [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
