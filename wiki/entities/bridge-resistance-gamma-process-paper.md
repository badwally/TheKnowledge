# Estimating the resistance of aging service-proven bridges with a Gamma process-based deterioration model

## Summary

A 2018 paper by Cao Wang, Kairui Feng, Long Zhang, and Aming Zou proposing a novel method for updating the resistance of service-proven bridges using a Gamma stochastic process to model time-dependent resistance deterioration, with the aim of incorporating historical service load information to reduce epistemic uncertainty in bridge condition evaluation.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Cao Wang, Kairui Feng, Long Zhang, and Aming Zou and was published on 12 December 2018 in the *Journal of Traffic and Transportation Engineering (English Edition)* (DOI 10.1016/j.jtte.2018.11.001) [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- The motivating premise is that environmental and anthropogenic factors to which in-service bridges are subjected reduce bridge performance, leading to service risk and an increased probability of substantial economic losses [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- The paper frames probability-based estimation of bridge resistance as an essential indicator for bridge condition evaluation and for optimisation of bridge maintenance/repair decisions [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- The authors argue that making full use of historical service load information may improve the accuracy of bridge performance assessment with reduced epistemic uncertainty for existing aging bridges [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- The authors observe that in prior analyses updating bridge resistance with past service information, the models of resistance deterioration have been assumed as either deterministic or fully correlated, which they argue may differ significantly from the realistic case [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- The paper proposes a novel method for updating the resistance of service-proven bridges with a realistic deterioration model [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- The Gamma stochastic process is adopted to describe the probabilistic behavior of structural time-dependent resistance, on the grounds that it has been suggested in the literature for this purpose [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- An illustrative bridge example is presented to demonstrate the applicability of the proposed method [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
- Parametric examples are conducted to investigate the role of the resistance deterioration model in the updated estimate of bridge resistance given historical service information [[sources/web-2018-12-12-ce5]].
