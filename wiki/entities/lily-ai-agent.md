# Lily (Daisy AI Support Agent)

## Summary

"Lily" is the support-facing AI agent in the Daisy property-management platform, handling 22% of all incoming requests across Daisy-managed buildings as of September 2025 [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. Lily is one component of Daisy's broader AI-agent posture, in which agents are framed as teammates with full ownership of repeatable workflows rather than as assistants supplementing a human owner [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].

## Key facts

- Support-facing AI agent in the Daisy platform [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Handles 22% of all incoming requests as of the September 2025 Cohen interview [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Identified by Cohen as one of the company's most significant milestones to date [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Component of the broader Daisy AI-agent posture: agents framed as teammates with full ownership of repeatable workflows rather than as assistants to a human owner [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
