# NVIDIA Dynamo

## Summary

NVIDIA Dynamo is an inference operating system from NVIDIA cited by Belcak et al. (2025) as explicitly providing support for high-throughput, low-latency SLM inference in both cloud and edge deployments — operational substrate for the SLM-first paradigm the paper advocates [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].

## Key facts

- An NVIDIA inference operating system positioned by Belcak et al. (2025) as a recent advance in inference systems [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Explicitly provides support for high-throughput, low-latency SLM inference [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Targets both cloud and edge deployment scenarios [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Cited alongside the observation that SLMs require less or no parallelization across GPUs and nodes, lowering the maintenance and operational expense of the serving infrastructure [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
