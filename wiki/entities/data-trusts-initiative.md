# Data Trusts Initiative

## Summary

The Data Trusts Initiative is a UK-based research and advocacy initiative focused on the design and piloting of data trusts; it is named as one of the institutional endorsers of the Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council 2021 report "Exploring legal mechanisms for data stewardship" [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].

## Key facts

- The Data Trusts Initiative endorsed the 2021 Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Council legal-mechanisms report alongside the Open Data Institute and the City of London Law Society [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
- Its endorsement positions the Initiative within the UK policy community shaping the operational definition and legal framing of data trusts [[sources/web-2021-03-04-e0f]].
