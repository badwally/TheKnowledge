# House of Commons Standing Committee on Industry and Technology (INDU)

## Summary

The House of Commons Standing Committee on Industry and Technology (INDU) is the Canadian parliamentary committee that conducted the in-depth study of Bill C-27 and the Artificial Intelligence and Data Act beginning in April 2023; the study was left incomplete when Parliament was prorogued in early 2025, and the AIDA died on the committee's table [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- Conducted the in-depth study of Bill C-27 / AIDA, beginning April 2023 [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Received submissions from 137 witnesses and 113 briefs during the AIDA study [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- AIDA and the rest of Bill C-27 died on the table of INDU following the prorogation of Parliament [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
