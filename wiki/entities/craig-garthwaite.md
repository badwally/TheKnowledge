# Craig Garthwaite

## Summary

Economist at the Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University, and NBER affiliate; first-listed author of NBER Working Paper No. 27734 "Who Profits From Amateurism? Rent-Sharing in Modern College Sports" (August 2020, revised October 2020), which characterizes the economic rents created by NCAA compensation limits on student-athletes, estimates rent-sharing elasticities showing how football and men's basketball revenue flows to other sports, facilities, and coaches' salaries, and documents that the redistribution transfers resources from students more likely to be Black and from poor neighborhoods toward students more likely to be white and from higher-income neighborhoods [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].

- Institutional affiliation: Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University, 2211 Campus Drive, Evanston, IL 60208 [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].

- Contact email: c-garthwaite@kellogg.northwestern.edu [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- First-listed author of "Who Profits From Amateurism? Rent-Sharing in Modern College Sports" (NBER Working Paper No. 27734, August 2020, revised October 2020), co-authored with Jordan Keener, Matthew J. Notowidigdo, and Nicole F. - The paper uses comprehensive revenue and expenses data for NCAA FBS athletic departments between 2006 and 2019 combined with a newly assembled dataset of complete rosters for every sport matched to neighborhood socioeconomic characteristics [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
