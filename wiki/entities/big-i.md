# Independent Insurance Agents & Brokers of America ("Big I")

## Summary

The Independent Insurance Agents & Brokers of America — colloquially "Big I" — is a U.S. trade association of independent insurance agents and brokers, named on 27 October 2025 as the partner trade body behind Datavault AI's Insurance Data Union venture [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].

## Key facts

- Short name used in the partnership announcement is "Big I" [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Named on 27 October 2025 as the partner trade association for the Insurance Data Union announced by Datavault AI Inc. (Nasdaq: DVLT) [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The partnership scope, as described in the announcement, covers recruitment of independent insurance agents and brokers nationwide into a tokenized data-pooling arrangement operated on Datavault AI's Information Data Exchange [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
