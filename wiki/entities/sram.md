# SRAM

## Summary

SRAM, LLC is a bicycle component manufacturer whose product range spans wireless electronic groupsets such as [[entities/sram-etap-axs|eTap AXS]] and hydraulic road disc brakes such as the [[entities/sram-hrd|Hydraulic Road Disc (HRD)]] system [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]] [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].

## Key facts

- Operates as SRAM, LLC and asserts trademark and IP rights on its product names and technologies in service manuals (© 2023 SRAM, LLC for the HRD manual; © 2021 SRAM, LLC for the eTap AXS Systems User Manual) [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]] [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].
- Publishes service documentation in nine languages — English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese — for international dealer support [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]] [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].
- Service manuals direct customers to its corporate domains for warranty (www.sram.com/warranty) and trademark policy (www.sram.com/website-terms-of-use) [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Recommends that SRAM components be installed by a qualified bicycle mechanic, citing the safety risk of improperly installed components — a warning carried verbatim in both the HRD and eTap AXS manuals [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]] [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].
- Develops proprietary brake technologies including the [[concepts/bleeding-edge|Bleeding Edge]] bleed port and the [[concepts/stealth-a-majig|Stealth-a-majig]] hose-connection system [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Publishes a dedicated companion app — the [[entities/sram-axs-app|SRAM AXS App]] — for configuring its wireless electronic systems, treated by the eTap AXS manual as a prerequisite step in the install flow [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].
- Maintains an "AXS Components Compatibility Map" as the canonical reference for which AXS components can be combined; AXS components are only compatible with other AXS components [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].
- Hosts component-level service documentation at www.sram.com/service, with system-level manuals (e.g., the eTap AXS Systems User Manual, 95-7918-013-000 Rev J) cross-referencing it whenever a separate manual is required [[sources/pdf-f13aa755f8e2]].
