# Helen Clough

## Summary

Helen Clough is a researcher at the University of Liverpool who led the 'Bayesian analysis in microbial risk assessment' project that served as a principal spur for the development of the Sheffield Elicitation Framework (SHELF).

## Key facts

- Clough led the project 'Bayesian analysis in microbial risk assessment' at the University of Liverpool, jointly with Marc Kennedy at the Food and Environment Research Agency (Fera) [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
- Discussions within this project provided the principal spur for the development of SHELF by Tony O'Hagan and Jeremy Oakley [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
