# Structural Safety (journal)

## Summary

*Structural Safety* is a scholarly journal in the structural reliability and safety field; its March 2025 issue is a special issue themed on the Joint Committee on Structural Safety (JCSS).

## Key facts

- The journal published a March 2025 issue indexed by Structurae as "Structural Safety - mars 2025" [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- The March 2025 issue contains 14 articles, including a preface on the JCSS, methodological reviews of structural reliability, and topical articles on resilience, deterioration of reinforced concrete, and probabilistic safety assessment of steel structures subject to fatigue [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- The articles in this issue carry page numbers in the 102381–102542 range [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
