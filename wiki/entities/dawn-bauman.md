# Dawn Bauman, CAE

## Summary

Dawn Bauman, CAE is the named author of CAI Advocacy's March 18, 2026 explainer on Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 and Freddie Mac Bulletin 2026-C — the primary-adjacent grounding source for the GSE policy regime that imposes the 15% reserve funding floor and retires Limited Review. The CAE credential identifies her as a Certified Association Executive, and her authorship of the CAI Advocacy explainer positions her as CAI's principal advocacy spokesperson on the GSE policy update [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].

## Key facts

- Authored the March 18, 2026 CAI Advocacy article "What Fannie Mae & Freddie Mac's Latest Policy Changes Mean for Condominium Associations, Lenders, and Homeowners" [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Credentialed as CAE (Certified Association Executive) [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Article published on CAI's advocacy subdomain (advocacy.caionline.org) [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Framed the GSE update as consequential for community associations, board members, managers, and business partners, and explicitly stated that without thoughtful implementation the changes could slow transactions and place additional strain on already-constrained communities [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
- Committed CAI to continue monitoring implementation and providing resources to support community associations navigating the evolving GSE requirements [[sources/web-2026-03-18-a4e]].
