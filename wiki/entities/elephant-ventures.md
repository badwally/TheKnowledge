# Elephant Ventures

## Summary

Elephant Ventures is the venture capital firm that led PayHOA's $27.5 million Series A round announced May 15, 2024 — PayHOA's first outside capital raise after operating as a bootstrapped, profitable Kentucky-based self-managed-HOA SaaS startup since 2018 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]]. Peter Fallon, a general partner at Elephant Ventures, is the named partner-counterparty on the round and provided the investor-side framing of the investment thesis, positioning the self-managed-HOA segment as a structurally underaddressed market gap [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].

## Key facts

- Venture capital firm; led PayHOA's $27.5 million Series A round announced May 15, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- The PayHOA round was raised in an environment where nearly $30 million Series A rounds were no longer common, with PayHOA's profitability and positive EBITDA explicitly cited as enabling the raise in a challenging fundraising environment, "especially for non-AI startups" [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Peter Fallon serves as a general partner at Elephant Ventures and is the named partner-counterparty on the PayHOA Series A [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Stated investment thesis on PayHOA (per Fallon): "Many HOAs manage their communities themselves, and for too long, their needs haven't been fully addressed. PayHOA recognizes this gap and provides a comprehensive platform designed specifically for self-managed HOAs. This empowers them to access powerful tools typically reserved for larger communities" — explicit institutional-capital framing of the self-managed-HOA segment as an underaddressed market gap [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Elephant Ventures' lead investment in PayHOA's May 2024 Series A is the load-bearing primary-source evidence that institutional growth capital has validated the self-managed-HOA segment as a fundable category structurally separate from the professionally-managed-HOA market targeted by the CAM SaaS exit-thesis acquirer set under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
