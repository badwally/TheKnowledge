# Michael Havbro Faber

## Summary

Michael Havbro Faber is a structural reliability researcher who contributes four times to the March 2025 JCSS-themed special issue of *Structural Safety*, including the special-issue preface and the historical retrospective on the Joint Committee on Structural Safety.

## Key facts

- Faber co-edited "Preface of the Special Issue: The Joint Committee of Structural Safety: past, present and a perspective on the future" (article 102542), as the third listed editor with Köhler, Vrouwenvelder, and Limongelli [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Faber is the third listed author of "The JCSS – A major contributor to structural safety through half a century" (article 102468), with Vrouwenvelder and Baker [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Faber is a co-author of "Interpretation of probability in structural safety – A philosophical conundrum" (article 102473), led by Vrouwenvelder [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Faber is a co-author of "On information value and decision analyses" (article 102481), led by Thöns [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
