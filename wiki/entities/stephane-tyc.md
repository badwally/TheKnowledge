# Stéphane Tyč

## Summary

Researcher at Quincy Data; co-author with Riadh Zaatour of the December 2013 paper "The Fed Robbery Revisited," which forensically reconstructs the information-propagation sequence of the September 18, 2013 FOMC announcement using exchange and pcap timestamps [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].

## Key facts

- Listed second of two authors on "The Fed Robbery Revisited" (Quincy Data, December 4, 2013) [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
- Affiliated with Quincy Data [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
