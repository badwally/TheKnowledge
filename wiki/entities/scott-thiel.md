# Scott Thiel

## Summary

Chief Fixed Income Strategist at the BlackRock Investment Institute; named contributor on the BII "2023 Global Outlook: A new investment playbook," which argues that fixed income finally offers income after yields surged but that long-term government bonds won't play their traditional portfolio-diversifier role due to persistent inflation and a broken stock-bond correlation [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].

## Key facts

- Listed title on the 2023 Global Outlook: Chief Fixed Income Strategist, BlackRock Investment Institute [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
- Named as one of six contributors alongside Philipp Hildebrand, Jean Boivin, Wei Li, Alex Brazier, and Vivek Paul [[sources/pdf-won-2022-bii-global-outlook]].
