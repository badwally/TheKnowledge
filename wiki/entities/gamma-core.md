# Gamma core

## Summary

Transcutaneous vagal nerve stimulation (TVNS) device approved by the FDA in 2020 for treatment of acute COVID for pulmonary exacerbations in hospitalized patients; cited in Dr. Carla Kuon's 2023 Long COVID Part 3 group medical visit as the device behind the FDA TVNS approval, with several trials completed and more underway evaluating TVNS for Long COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key facts

- Identified in the source as "Gamma core, a TVNS device" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- The FDA approved Gamma core in 2020 for treatment of acute COVID for pulmonary exacerbations in hospitalized patients [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Several trials have been completed and more are underway evaluating TVNS for Long COVID [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Per Dr. Kuon's clinical guidance, TVNS dosing using such a device should start with 5 minutes and not exceed 20 minutes daily [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
