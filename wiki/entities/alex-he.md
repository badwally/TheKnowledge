# Alex He

## Summary

Finance researcher at the Robert H. Smith School of Business, University of Maryland; co-author of the 2022 NBER working paper "Eclipse of Rent-Sharing: The Effects of Managers' Business Education on Wages and the Labor Share in the US and Denmark" [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with 4411 Van Munching Hall, Robert H. Smith School of Business, University of Maryland, College Park, MD 20742 on the paper [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- Contact email axhe@umd.edu on the paper [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- Co-author of "Eclipse of Rent-Sharing" (NBER Working Paper No. 29874, March 2022) with Daron Acemoglu and Daniel le Maire [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- The paper acknowledges Marco Tabellini and Guglielmo Zappala for help with the BoardEx data used to identify managers and their educational backgrounds in the US sample [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
