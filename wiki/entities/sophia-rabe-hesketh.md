# Sophia Rabe-Hesketh

## Summary

Sophia Rabe-Hesketh is a statistician who presented the Stan-channel talk 'Predictive Information Criteria in Hierarchical Bayesian Models for Clustered Data,' covering DIC, WAIC, and leave-one-out cross-validation in multilevel models, structural equation models, and item response theory.

## Key facts

- Rabe-Hesketh introduced herself as the speaker for the talk 'Predictive Information Criteria in Hierarchical Bayesian Models for Clustered Data' published on the Stan YouTube channel [[sources/yt-WJ1BvfOm-94]].
- She described the talk as joint work with co-authors, including a co-presenter (referred to in the talk as 'Dan') who delivered the item response theory portion, and a further co-author who was unable to attend [[sources/yt-WJ1BvfOm-94]].
- The talk covers predictive information criteria (DIC, WAIC) and their connection to leave-one-out cross-validation, the structure of hierarchical Bayesian models for clustered data, and the distinction between marginal and conditional versions of the information criteria [[sources/yt-WJ1BvfOm-94]].
- She cited Edgar Merkle as a co-author who implemented marginal-likelihood evaluation for structural equation models in the R package blavaan [[sources/yt-WJ1BvfOm-94]].
