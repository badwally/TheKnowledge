# Michael Wilk

## Summary

Michael Wilk is the President of Wilkar Property Management and is identified by name in UpperBee's published integrations-page testimonial set as the spokesperson for Wilkar's UpperBee adoption narrative [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Title: President of Wilkar Property Management [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Named spokesperson in UpperBee's published testimonial set [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Attributed quotation positions UpperBee as an "essential tool in helping grow our condo management business" enabling efficient staff-to-resident communication and transparent financial visibility [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- States UpperBee "played an important role in Wilkar becoming one of the largest property managers in Quebec" [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Articulates a forward-looking commitment to expanding the Wilkar–UpperBee relationship as Wilkar moves into the Ontario market [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
