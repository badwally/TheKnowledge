# Champlain Towers South

## Summary

Champlain Towers South was a condominium building in Surfside, Florida that partially collapsed in June 2021, killing 98 people. The collapse is the causal trigger for the post-Surfside wave of US condominium reform — including state-level reserve-study and reserve-funding mandates and the Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac post-Surfside underwriting standards that began rolling out in late 2021 and culminated in the March 2026 GSE policy update [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].

## Key facts

- Located in Surfside, Florida [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Partially collapsed in June 2021 [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- The collapse killed 98 people [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Investigations revealed long-standing structural deterioration [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Investigations revealed documented but unaddressed deferred maintenance [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Investigations revealed chronically underfunded reserves [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac began implementing new condominium underwriting standards starting in late 2021 in direct response to the collapse [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
- CAI's Summary of State Reserve Fund Laws (September 2024 edition) italicizes the names of states — Florida, Maryland, and Tennessee — whose reserve laws changed in response to the collapse [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
- Industry commentators describe the March 2026 Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac update as "the latest and among the most consequential chapter" in the ongoing GSE response to the Surfside collapse [[sources/web-2026-04-15-f75]].
