# Smartwebs

## Summary

Smartwebs is a homeowner association (HOA) management software product whose banking-data integration with Pacific Premier Bank (post-merger: Columbia Bank) is one of the named continuity commitments of the August 31, 2025 Pacific Premier–Umpqua merger [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]]. The combined Columbia Bank team is planning to invest in further enhancing the API technology connecting to Smartwebs software post-merger [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key facts

- HOA management software product with banking-data API integration to Pacific Premier Bank's Community Association Banking division [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The Smartwebs–Pacific Premier integration was named in the August 28, 2025 Community Financials client communication as a continuity commitment of the post-merger Columbia Bank entity [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- Online banking tools and Smartwebs software integrations remain unchanged post-merger and continue without interruption [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The combined Columbia Bank team is planning to invest in further enhancing the API technology connecting to Smartwebs software [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
