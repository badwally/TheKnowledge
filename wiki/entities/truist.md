# Truist Financial Corporation

## Summary

Truist is the bank holding company formed by the merger of BB&T and SunTrust, headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, with 275 years of combined BB&T and SunTrust history and approximately 12 million household customers [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. As of the source publication, Truist is identified as the sixth-largest commercial bank in the United States and is enrolled as a banking partner in Vantaca's CAM software integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].

## Key facts

- Formed by the merger of BB&T and SunTrust under a shared stated purpose of inspiring and building better lives and communities [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Combined BB&T and SunTrust history of 275 years [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Serves approximately 12 million households with leading market share in many high-growth markets [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Identified as the sixth-largest commercial bank in the U.S. as of the source publication [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Truist Bank is a Member FDIC [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Service lines include retail, small business and commercial banking; asset management; capital markets; commercial real estate; corporate and institutional banking; insurance; mortgage; payments; specialized lending; and wealth management [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Named banking partner in the Vantaca partner directory [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
