# ValenceDocs

## Summary

ValenceDocs is an AI lease-file audit product within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio, announced at OpTech 2025 for an early-2026 launch [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- ValenceDocs operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- At OpTech 2025 (Nov 17, 2025), ValenceDocs announced a Lease File Audit product for early-2026 release [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- ValenceDocs' Lease File Audit is positioned to instantly analyze any lease, deliver real-time results, and uncover actionable portfolio insights [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- ValenceDocs is positioned exclusively for multifamily-rental lease documents; no condo/HOA declarations, bylaws, or rules-and-regulations audit use cases are announced [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
