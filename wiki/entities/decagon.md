# Decagon

**Archetype:** AI-native services / vertical agent

AI-native customer-service platform; replaces tiered per-seat pricing with per-resolution outcome-based billing.

## Why it's in this domain

The canonical example of the pricing pivot away from Zendesk-style $115/month per-agent seat pricing toward outcome-based ("per-resolution") billing — central to the Pillar 4 (GTM/pricing) and economics-pillar argument that AI-as-substrate breaks legacy SaaS unit economics.
