# Col de Bavella

## Summary

The Col de Bavella is a 1,218 m mountain pass on [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] — described in Marmot Tours' Corsican Highlights brochure as 'one of the most beautiful mountain passes in Europe' and used as the grand-finale objective for the Day 6 ride out of Solenzara [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].

## Key facts

- 1,218 m summit elevation [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Backdrop of rugged granite peaks; the surrounding terrain is a Mecca for rock climbers and walkers [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Forms the Day-6 grand-finale objective on the [[entities/corsican-highlights|Corsican Highlights]] cycling holiday from [[entities/marmot-tours|Marmot Tours]] [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Can be climbed from the east (Bavella E) and from the west (Bavella W) — Marmot's recommended pattern is to descend to Zonza after the east climb and re-ascend the col from the west [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Marmot's Classic Day-6 ride is the east climb only at 61 km / +1,500 m; the Harder pairing of east + west is 79 km / +2,000 m; the Challenge variant adds a Zonza loop into the interior for 121 km / +2,800 m [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
