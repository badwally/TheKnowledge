# HOA Sites

## Summary

HOA Sites is the Property Control portfolio's turnkey public-website product for HOAs and management companies, providing mobile-responsive sites that inform and engage owners [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]].

## Key facts

- HOA Sites is one of four sister products consolidated under the Property Control parent brand effective August 29, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The product is positioned as turnkey, mobile-responsive public websites for HOAs and management companies that inform and engage owners [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- HOA Sites is accessible via multi-brand single sign-on across the Property Control portfolio [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The product carries Property Control's portfolio-level native integrations and open API [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Existing HOA Sites customers continue on the product with no disruption at the Property Control parent-brand launch [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
