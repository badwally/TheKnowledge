# Regroupement des gestionnaires et copropriétaires du Québec (RGCQ)

## Summary

The Regroupement des gestionnaires et copropriétaires du Québec (RGCQ) is a Quebec-based industry association serving divided co-ownership (copropriété divise) managers and co-owners. The organization operates a members-only resource portal at rgcq.org, publishes practitioner-facing analysis of regulatory developments affecting Quebec condominiums, and convenes practitioner symposia — including a September 12, 2025 event scheduled to discuss the practical implications of the Bill 16 regulatory framework that came into force on August 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].

## Key facts

- Quebec-based industry association serving divided co-ownership managers and co-owners [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Operates a public news section and a members-only resource portal at rgcq.org [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Membership is required to access the full resource library; the news section is publicly readable [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Published practitioner-facing analysis of the regulation implementing Bill 16, covering the standardized maintenance logbook, contingency fund study, buyer certificate, and deposit-trust rules [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Hosted a symposium scheduled for Friday, September 12, 2025 to discuss the practical implications of the Bill 16 regulatory framework [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Functions as the Quebec-side practitioner counterpart to organizations like the Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI) and the Condominium Home Owners' Association of British Columbia (CHOA) in the broader Canadian condominium-association ecosystem [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
