# ICS (IntraFi Cash Service)

## Summary

ICS is IntraFi's deposit-placement service that allocates customer funds into demand deposit accounts and money market deposit accounts at participating network banks, providing FDIC pass-through insurance above the $250,000 single-institution maximum [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]]. ICS is offered through bank partners to a range of customer segments, including community associations served by Alliance Association Bank, where it is used to insure HOA, CID, and PUD funds beyond the per-bank Standard Maximum Deposit Insurance Amount (SMDIA) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-21d]].

## Key facts

- Splits a customer's deposit into sub-$250,000 increments placed across multiple network banks so that each tranche stays within the standard FDIC insurance maximum [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]].
- Places funds into two account types: demand deposit accounts and money market deposit accounts [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]].
- Provides daily liquidity for funds placed via the service [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]].
- Returns are described as comparable to those earned from Treasuries and government money market mutual funds [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]].
- Eliminates the depositor's need to track changing collateral values or footnote uninsured deposits in financial statements [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]].
- Funds placed through ICS can stay local to support lending at the originating bank or be exchanged via the reciprocal-deposits feature [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]].
- Pass-through FDIC insurance requires that certain conditions be satisfied [[sources/web-2008-01-01-0ad]].
- A depositor's balances at the institution that places ICS deposits may exceed the SMDIA before settlement for deposits or after settlement for withdrawals, or be uninsured if the placing institution is not itself an FDIC-insured bank [[sources/web-2026-01-01-21d]].
- Offered to HOA, CID, and PUD accounts through Alliance Association Bank (a Western Alliance Bank division) as a named community-association banking application [[sources/web-2026-01-01-21d]].
- ICS is a registered service mark of IntraFi Network LLC [[sources/web-2026-01-01-21d]].
