# Klaviyo

## Summary

Klaviyo is the email and SMS marketing platform that serves as Orita's primary channel partner and go-to-market beachhead [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]]. Orita's ML technology operates on customer-engagement data and is platform-agnostic at the algorithmic level; Klaviyo prioritization is a GTM choice (driven by market concentration and complementarity that Klaviyo cannot replicate today), not a technical dependency [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]]. The CMO has informally referred to Orita as "built on" the Klaviyo ecosystem in describing customer overlap [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]] — a description capturing practical customer-experience rather than product architecture, which extends across SMS, direct mail, and ad-audience optimization beyond Klaviyo as the ESP layer [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
## Key facts

- Email and SMS marketing platform; Orita's primary channel partner [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
- Holds Orita at Premier Partner tier, Klaviyo's highest partner level [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- 167,000+ customers globally [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- 44.9% (~75,000–79,000) of Klaviyo customers are US-based [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Fastest customer growth is in the $5M–$50M GMV cohort [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- ~25,850 Shopify Plus stores are US-based within the Klaviyo footprint [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Orita's expansion path beyond Klaviyo (Iterable, Braze, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Ometria) is treated as a future growth vector that widens SAM without core technology changes [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
