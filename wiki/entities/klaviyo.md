# Klaviyo

## Summary

Klaviyo is the email and SMS marketing platform that serves as Orita's primary channel partner and go-to-market beachhead [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]]. As of June 2026 Klaviyo's own platform positioning on Capterra is broader: "the all-in-one platform purpose-built to help B2C businesses grow, combining marketing, analytics, and service in one intuitive solution," spanning email, SMS/RCS, WhatsApp, push, and ads, powered by unified customer data and built-in AI [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].

## Key facts

- Klaviyo holds a 4.6/5 overall Capterra rating across 528 verified reviews as of June 2026, with sub-scores of 4.5 ease of use, 4.6 features, 4.6 customer service, and 4.3 value for money [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Klaviyo advertises 400+ native integrations, 100+ prebuilt templates and flows, and "unlockable" analytics such as RFM and funnel reporting [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Pricing starts with a free plan for up to 250 active profiles; paid Email plans begin at $20/month and Email + SMS at $35/month for the 251–500 profile tier; reviewers consistently cite pricing scaling by active profiles as the platform's primary friction at larger list sizes [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Capterra's reviewer base for Klaviyo skews heavily small B2C: 92% small-business reviewers, with Retail (20% overall / 27% of recent reviewers), Marketing & Advertising (16% / 37% of recent), Apparel & Fashion (9% / 19% of recent), and Consumer Goods (10% of recent) the leading industries [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Among recent reviewers, top job functions are Marketing (37%), Administrative (16%), Entrepreneurs/Owners (13%), and Creative & Design (10%) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Klaviyo earned 2026 Capterra Shortlist recognition in three categories: CRM, Email Marketing, and SMS Marketing [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Reviewer-aggregated differentiators include detailed segmentation, behavior-based triggers (abandoned-cart and post-purchase flows), A/B testing, RFM/funnel analytics, and SMS marketing for cross-channel messaging; ecommerce integration depth (Shopify in particular) is the most-cited adoption driver [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Capterra-listed integration partners include Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, WordPress, and Adobe Commerce on the storefront side, plus Yotpo (reviews), Google Analytics 360 (analytics), and Gorgias (customer support) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Capterra's featured Klaviyo alternatives are Brevo (4.6 / 3,473 reviews / $20/mo start), Constant Contact (4.3 / 2,925 / $12/mo), Omnisend (4.7 / 836 / $16/mo), HubSpot Marketing Hub (4.5 / 6,234 / $15/mo), and EngageBay CRM (4.7 / 908 / $14.99/mo) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Top reviewer-cited pros: comprehensive email marketing suite (94% positive across 118 mentions) and an intuitive, accessible platform; top cons: high and complex pricing (54% negative across 100 mentions) and frequent technical glitches [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
- Capterra reviewers report onboarding is fast for marketers running basic Shopify campaigns via live sessions, webinars and documentation, while admins setting up flows, segments, analytics, or integrations need more time and guided training — consistent with Orita's thesis that the segmentation/flow layer is the high-leverage automation target [[sources/web-2026-06-15-07f]].
