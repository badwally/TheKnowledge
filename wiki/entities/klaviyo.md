# Klaviyo

## Summary

Klaviyo is the email and SMS marketing platform on which Orita's AI customer segmentation product is built; Orita serves brands "in the Klaviyo ecosystem" who typically also run on Shopify [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]]. In Orita's internal GTM planning, Klaviyo is treated as the beachhead market — chosen because it dominates US ecommerce email marketing and cannot replicate Orita's scoring at a technical level today — rather than as a permanent ceiling on Orita's addressable market [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].

## Key facts

- Email and SMS marketing platform; Orita's primary channel partner [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]].
- Holds Orita at Premier Partner tier, Klaviyo's highest partner level [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- 167,000+ customers globally [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- 44.9% (~75,000–79,000) of Klaviyo customers are US-based [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Fastest customer growth is in the $5M–$50M GMV cohort [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- ~25,850 Shopify Plus stores are US-based within the Klaviyo footprint [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
- Orita's expansion path beyond Klaviyo (Iterable, Braze, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Ometria) is treated as a future growth vector that widens SAM without core technology changes [[sources/docx-25c1bcf28fb8]].
