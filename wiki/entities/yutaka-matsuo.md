# Yutaka Matsuo

## Summary

Professor at the University of Tokyo and AI expert who advised Japan's LDP Project Team on the Evolution and Implementation of AI during preparation of the April 2023 AI Whitepaper [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Tokyo as a professor [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]] [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
- Spoke at the 2nd LDP project team hearing on February 17, 2023, where he assessed that LLM social implementation "is likely to affect nearly every white-collar job" [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Quoted in the whitepaper summary as warning that "there is a high possibility that almost all white-collar jobs could be affected" by foundation-model AI [[sources/pdf-ai-2023-japan-s-ai]].
