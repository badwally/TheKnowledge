# Jennifer Widom

## Summary

Co-chair of the Stanford Emerging Technology Review (SETR), the Stanford initiative that publishes an annual survey of ten emerging technology fields and their policy implications [[sources/pdf-stanford-2026-the-stanford-emerging]].

## Key facts

- Listed as one of three co-chairs of the SETR 2026, alongside Condoleezza Rice and Amy Zegart [[sources/pdf-stanford-2026-the-stanford-emerging]].
