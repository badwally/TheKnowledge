# Tom B. Brown

## Summary

AI researcher at OpenAI and co-author of "Measuring the Algorithmic Efficiency of Neural Networks," which shows a 44x reduction over 2012–2019 in the compute required to reach AlexNet-level ImageNet performance [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with OpenAI on the paper, with email tom@openai.com [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
- Per the contribution footnote, Brown paired with Danny Hernandez on initial experiments, scoping, and debugging, while Hernandez led the research [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
