# Virginia Code §55.1-1826 (Annual budget; reserve study; reserves for capital components)

## Summary

Virginia Code §55.1-1826, within the Property Owners' Association Act (Title 55.1, Chapter 18), is the operative provision that imposes annual-budget disclosure, a five-year reserve-study cycle, annual reserve-adequacy review, and itemized reserve-related budget content on Virginia property owners' associations [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]]. The section also preserves explicit board discretion over the funding mechanism — replacement reserves, additional assessments, or borrowed funds — to meet repair and replacement obligations [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].

## Key facts

- Subsection A: prior to the commencement of the fiscal year, the board of directors shall make available to lot owners either (i) the annual budget of the association or (ii) a summary of such annual budget [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection B(1): the board shall conduct at least once every five years a study to determine the necessity and amount of reserves required to repair, replace, and restore the capital components as defined in §55.1-1800 [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection B(2): the board shall review the results of that study at least annually to determine if reserves are sufficient [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection B(3): the board shall make any adjustments to the annual budget and annual assessment the board deems necessary to maintain reserves, as appropriate [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection C: to the extent the reserve study indicates a need to budget for reserves, the association budget shall include four enumerated items [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection C(1): current estimated replacement cost, estimated remaining life, and estimated useful life of the capital components as defined in §55.1-1800 [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection C(2): as of the beginning of the fiscal year, the current amount of accumulated cash reserves set aside to repair, replace, or restore capital components, and the amount of the expected contribution to the reserve fund for that year [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection C(3): a statement describing the procedures used for estimation and accumulation of cash reserves pursuant to this section [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection C(4): a statement of the amount of reserves recommended in the study and the amount of current cash for replacement reserves [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Subsection D: the board has discretion to meet repair and replacement requirements through replacement reserves, additional assessments, or borrowed funds [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- The five-year reserve-study floor is a minimum, not a fixed cadence: §55.1-1826(B)(1) uses "at least once every five years" [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- The statute does not mandate a minimum reserve funding level; it requires disclosure of the reserve study's recommended amount versus current cash, but explicitly leaves the funding mechanism to board discretion under subsection D [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
- Citation history: 2002, c. 459, §55-514.1; 2019, cc. 33, 44, 712; 2024, c. 324 [[sources/web-2026-05-23-01f]].
