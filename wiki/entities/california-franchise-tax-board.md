# California Franchise Tax Board (FTB)

## Summary

The California Franchise Tax Board (FTB) is the state tax agency that administers California's personal income tax and corporate franchise/income tax regimes, including the tax-exempt status determination and annual filing obligations of California homeowners' associations under California Revenue and Taxation Code Section 23701t [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]]. FTB issues Form 1028 (*Guidelines for Homeowners' Associations*) as its primary FAQ-style administrative publication for HOAs and operates the Form 3500 (Exemption Application), Form 199 (Annual Information Return), FTB 199N (e-Postcard), and Form 100 (Corporation Franchise or Income Tax Return) filings that govern California HOA tax compliance [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].

## Key facts

- State tax agency administering California's personal and corporate income/franchise tax laws [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Issues Exempt Determination Letters granting California tax-exempt status to qualifying HOAs under R&TC §23701t [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Publishes Form 1028, *Guidelines for Homeowners' Associations*, as a public FAQ-style guidance document; current revision December 2020 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Receives exemption applications by mail at Franchise Tax Board, PO Box 1286, Rancho Cordova CA 95741-1286 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Recommends applicants submit Form 3500 at least 90 calendar days before tax-exempt status is needed to allow adequate processing time [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Operates the FTB 199N California e-Postcard electronic filing system; does not offer a paper FTB 199N form [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Imposes the minimum franchise or income tax on HOAs that do not receive California tax-exempt status [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Public-facing forms website: ftb.ca.gov; Form 3500 search keyword: "3500" [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Grants an automatic six-month extension to file Form 199 if the organization is not suspended on the original due date and files on or before the extended due date [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Imposes a $5-per-month delinquent-filing penalty (maximum $40) when Form 199 is not filed by the extended due date, per R&TC §23772 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
