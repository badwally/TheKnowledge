# Virginia Common Interest Community Manager Regulations (18VAC48-50)

## Summary

The Virginia Common Interest Community Manager Regulations are codified at Title 18 of the Virginia Administrative Code, Agency 48, Chapter 50 (18VAC48-50), titled "Common Interest Community Manager Regulations" [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]]. The chapter establishes the entry, fee, renewal, conduct, accounting, training, and examination requirements for common interest community (CIC) managers and certified principal or supervisory employees operating in the Commonwealth of Virginia, organized into six Parts [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]]. Part V (Standards of Conduct and Practice) is the operative source of Virginia's manager-side trust-accounting and internal-controls regime: Section 160 governs maintenance and management of accounts, and Section 210 governs establishment of internal accounting controls [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]]. The regulation regulates the third-party manager (and certified principal/supervisory employees), distinct from the association-side reserve-funding obligations imposed on POAs under the Virginia Property Owners' Association Act.

## Key facts

- Codified at Title 18 of the Virginia Administrative Code, Agency 48, Chapter 50 (18VAC48-50) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Titled "Common Interest Community Manager Regulations" [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Organized into six Parts: I (General), II (Entry), III (Fees), IV (Renewal and Reinstatement), V (Standards of Conduct and Practice), and VI (Training Programs and Examination) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Part I (General) contains a single section: Section 10 (Definitions) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Part II (Entry) contains Section 20 (Application procedures), Section 30 (Qualifications for licensure as a common interest community manager), Section 35 (Qualifications for certification as a certified principal or supervisory employee effective July 1, 2012), Section 37 (Licensure and certification by reciprocity), and Section 40 (Application denial) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Part III (Fees) contains Section 50 (General fee requirements) and Section 60 (Fee schedule); Sections 70 and 80 are repealed [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Part IV (Renewal and Reinstatement) contains Section 90 (Renewal required), Section 100 (Expiration and renewal), Section 110 (Reinstatement of common interest community manager license and certified principal or supervisory employee certificate required), Section 120 (Status of license or certificate during the period prior to reinstatement), and Section 130 (Board discretion to deny renewal or reinstatement) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Part V (Standards of Conduct and Practice) contains Section 140 (Grounds for disciplinary action), Section 150 (Maintenance of license or certificate), Section 160 (Maintenance and management of accounts), Section 170 (Change of business entity requires a new license), Section 180 (Notice of adverse action), Section 190 (Prohibited acts), Section 200 (Establishment of code of conduct), Section 210 (Establishment of internal accounting controls), and Section 220 (Response to inquiry and provision of records) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Part VI (Training Programs and Examination) contains Section 230 (Training programs generally), Section 240 (Approval of common interest community manager training programs), Section 250 (Introductory and comprehensive training program requirements), Section 253 (Virginia common interest community law and regulation training program requirements), Section 255 (Fair housing training program requirements), Section 257 (Documentation of training program completion required), Section 260 (Maintenance of records), Section 270 (Reporting of changes), Section 280 (Withdrawal of approval), and Section 290 (Examinations) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- The certified principal or supervisory employee category was established effective July 1, 2012 (Section 35) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Includes a forms section identified as "FORMS (18VAC48-50)" [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Vests a Board with discretionary authority over renewal/reinstatement (Section 130), training-program approval (Section 240), and withdrawal of training-program approval (Section 280) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
- Recognizes reciprocity-based licensure and certification (Section 37) [[sources/web-2026-05-23-1d6]].
