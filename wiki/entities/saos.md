# SAOS

## Summary

SAOS is cited in the Internet Policy Review glossary entry on data cooperatives as a working agricultural data cooperative — one of the small set of producer-owned data cooperatives the source uses to anchor the definition outside of the health domain [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key facts

- Identified as a farming-sector data cooperative whose members are agricultural producers [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Operates on a membership-fee monetisation model, alongside other cooperatives that may rely entirely on dues or seek additional sources of funding [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Used by the source as one of the few non-health examples demonstrating that the cooperative form extends to producer data outside of personal/consumer contexts [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
