# British Columbia Law Institute (BCLI)

## Summary

The British Columbia Law Institute (BCLI) is a BC-based law-reform organization that publishes practitioner-facing analysis of BC statutory and regulatory developments, including amendments to the Strata Property Act and Strata Property Regulation [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]]. BCLI's strata-property commentary serves as the primary-adjacent secondary source documenting the timing, text, and stated rationale of regulatory amendments such as Order in Council 101/2019 [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].

## Key facts

- BC-based law-reform organization (bcli.org) [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
- Publishes practitioner-facing analysis of BC strata-property regulatory developments [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
- Published Kevin Zakreski's March 7, 2019 commentary on Order in Council 101/2019 the day after the amendment's announcement [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
