# Bill C-27 (Digital Charter Implementation Act)

## Summary

Bill C-27, collectively known as the Digital Charter Implementation Act, was a Canadian federal omnibus bill tabled in Parliament in June 2022 that bundled three new legislative acts: the Consumer Privacy Protection Act, the Personal Information and Data Protection Tribunal Act, and the Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA) [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]]. C-27 failed to become law and died on the table of the House of Commons Standing Committee on Industry and Technology after the early-2025 prorogation of Parliament [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- C-27 packaged three acts: the Consumer Privacy Protection Act and the Personal Information and Data Protection Tribunal Act (modernizing Canada's consumer data-protection and privacy laws) plus the Artificial Intelligence and Data Act [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- The bill was tabled in the House of Commons in June 2022 [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- C-27 was studied by the INDU committee starting April 2023; that study was left incomplete when Parliament was prorogued [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- C-27 failed to pass; its death is attributed in part to AIDA's unclear and incomplete scope, limited public participation, and a federal government described in the source as "now-imploding" [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
