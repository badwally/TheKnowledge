# Structural Safety, March 2025 – JCSS Special Issue

## Summary

The March 2025 special issue of *Structural Safety* devoted to the Joint Committee on Structural Safety (JCSS), comprising 14 articles spanning JCSS history, the JCSS Probabilistic Model Code, methods of structural reliability, philosophical interpretation of probability, deterioration of reinforced concrete, life safety in reliability-based design, fatigue safety of steel structures, system risk modelling, value of information, resilience and sustainability, and terrorism risk reduction.

## Key facts

- The issue contains a preface, "Preface of the Special Issue: The Joint Committee of Structural Safety: past, present and a perspective on the future," by Köhler, Vrouwenvelder, Faber, and Limongelli (article 102542) [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "The JCSS – A major contributor to structural safety through half a century" by Vrouwenvelder, Baker, and Faber (article 102468) frames the issue's retrospective on JCSS [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Codes and standards for structural design - developments and future potential" by Köhler, Sørensen, and Ellingwood (article 102495) discusses the evolution of structural-design codes and standards [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Development of methods of structural reliability" by Ellingwood, Maes, Bartlett, Beck, Caprani, Der Kiureghian, Dueñas-Osorio, Galvão, Gilbert, Li, Matos, Mori, Papaioannou, Parades, Straub, and Sudret (article 102474) is a multi-author review of the development of structural reliability methods [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Interpretation of probability in structural safety – A philosophical conundrum" by Vrouwenvelder, Beck, Proske, Faber, Köhler, Schubert, Straub, and Teichgräber (article 102473) addresses the interpretation of probability in structural safety [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "The JCSS probabilistic model Code, future developments" by Steenbergen and Vrouwenvelder (article 102540) covers future developments of the JCSS Probabilistic Model Code [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Life safety in the Reliability-Based design and assessment of structures" by Pandey, Viljoen, Way, Fischer, Sýkora, Diamantidis, Steenbergen, Lind, Frangopol, Yang, Retief, André, Nathwani, and Lenner (article 102453) addresses life safety in reliability-based design and assessment [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Models and methods for probabilistic safety assessment of steel structures subject to fatigue" by Maljaars, Leander, Nussbaumer, Sørensen, and Straub (article 102446) covers probabilistic fatigue safety of steel structures [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Moving toward resilience and sustainability in the built environment" by Sanchez-Silva, Gardoni, Val, Yang, Frangopol, Limongelli, Honfi, Acuña, and Straub (article 102449) addresses resilience and sustainability of the built environment [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "On information value and decision analyses" by Thöns, Caprani, Faber, Frangopol, Gardoni, Giordano, Honfi, Iannacone, Khan, Köhler, Kim, De Koker, Limongelli, Miraglia, Nielsen, Pandey, and Viljoen (article 102481) discusses the value of information and decision analyses in structural safety [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "On reliability assessment of existing structures" by Diamantidis, Tanner, Holický, Madsen, and Sýkora (article 102452) addresses reliability assessment of existing structures [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Probabilistic modelling of deterioration of reinforced concrete structures" by Val, Andrade, Sýkora, Stewart, Bastidas-Arteaga, Mlcoch, Truong, and El Soueidy (article 102454) covers probabilistic deterioration modelling of reinforced concrete structures [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Recent developments in mechanical and uncertainty modelling of concrete" by Chen, Ren, Feng, Köhler, Sørensen, Wu, Le, and Caspeele (article 102526) addresses mechanical and uncertainty modelling of concrete [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "System risk modelling and decision-making – Reflections and common pitfalls" by Høj, Kroon, Nielsen, and Schubert (article 102469) reflects on common pitfalls in system risk modelling and decision-making [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- "Assessment of risk reduction strategies for terrorist attacks on structures" by Stewart, Thöns, and Beck (article 102381) covers risk-reduction strategies for terrorist attacks on structures [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
