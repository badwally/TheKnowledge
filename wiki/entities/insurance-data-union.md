# Insurance Data Union (Datavault AI)

## Summary

The Insurance Data Union is a tokenized data-monetization venture announced by Datavault AI Inc. on 27 October 2025, structured as a "data union" of independent U.S. insurance agents and brokers who contribute anonymized data to a Datavault-operated platform in exchange for cash payments and smart-contract-settled tokens [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]]. The press release positions the venture as a partnership with the Independent Insurance Agents & Brokers of America ("Big I") [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].

## Key facts

- Announced on 27 October 2025 by Datavault AI Inc. (Nasdaq: DVLT) [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Named partners include the Independent Insurance Agents & Brokers of America ("Big I") and independent agents and brokers nationwide [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Member-onboarding is described as registration of independent insurance, brokerage, or agency firms; contributed data assets are scored for "quality, completeness, accuracy and governance" using Datavault AI's patented DataScore, DataValue, and Data Vault Bank components [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Members receive cash payments for qualified data assets and hold smart-contract-settled tokens redeemable when data copies are vended through the Information Data Exchange [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The press release projects the global insurance market at $8 trillion in 2025 as the addressable opportunity [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The release frames the union as a vehicle to aggregate holdings "rivaling those of giants like State Farm" so that independent agents gain access to large datasets "for AI-driven insights and competitive advantages" [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- An adjacent proposal in the same release extends the model to title insurance combined with real-estate tokenization, naming Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac as potential beneficiaries of fractional ownership and "efficient risk management" workflows [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The announcement is a forward-looking corporate communication; it discloses no member counts, transaction volumes, governance bylaws, antitrust analysis, or third-party validation of the projected ARR [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
