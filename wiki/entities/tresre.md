# TresRE

## Summary

TresRE is a treasury management middleware product launched by CINC Systems on June 5, 2023, positioned as a bank-facing payments-and-banking integration layer that brings banking, software, and payments together for real estate managers who would otherwise "swivel chair" between disconnected accounting and banking systems [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]]. TresRE extends CINC's earlier Paygami platform (launched 2018) by adding the integration middleware that previously powered only CINC's own accounting-software clients, and exposes that middleware to third-party accounting platforms including Jenark and FRONTSTEPS Caliber [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]]. Valley Bank is the named launch bank partner [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

- Launched by CINC Systems on June 5, 2023; the launch announcement was published via PRNewswire on June 6, 2023 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Positioned as a treasury management solution that combines banking, software, and payments for real estate managers [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Built on the same integration middleware that powered CINC's accounting-software clients, now made available to real estate managers using multiple other software systems [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Named integration targets at launch include Jenark and FRONTSTEPS Caliber [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Valley Bank is the named launch bank partner; Valley Bank's clients access TresRE via Valley's online banking platform [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Marc Nuzzolo, Senior Vice President at Valley Bank, is the named Valley Bank executive responsible for the TresRE relationship [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Processes over $6 billion in annual payments through its integrations with CINC and other software providers, per the June 2023 launch announcement [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Target buyer segments are banks, property management firms, and software providers serving real estate verticals — explicitly broader than CINC's association-management-only accounting platform [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Hosted at tresre.com [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- CINC's COO Robert McCullar stated at launch that TresRE benefited from over 30 integrated bank partners and software integrations, with additional deals in deep discussion across real estate verticals [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Marketed as resolving the "swivel chair" reconciliation problem in just a few clicks, eliminating manual reconciliations and matching payments to the right residents [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Described in CINC's company materials as the banking and payments solution that underpins the success of real estate management software providers, distinct from but complementary to the CINC core accounting platform [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
