# Jakob Foerster

## Summary

AI researcher at FAIR at Meta and co-author of the 2026 HyperAgents paper, which introduces self-referential agents capable of metacognitive self-modification [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with FAIR at Meta on the HyperAgents paper [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Co-author of "HyperAgents" alongside Jenny Zhang (lead), Bingchen Zhao, Wannan Yang, Jeff Clune, Minqi Jiang, Sam Devlin, and Tatiana Shavrina [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
