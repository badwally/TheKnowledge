# Nannette Hechler-Fayd'herbe

## Summary

Head of Global Economics & Research at Credit Suisse; co-author of the editorial in the Credit Suisse Investment Outlook 2023 framing 2023 as the dawn of a multipolar world in which geopolitics has returned as a key driver of the global economy and a new monetary policy regime prioritizes inflation stability over growth [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].

## Key facts

- Listed title at Credit Suisse: Head of Global Economics & Research [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Co-authored the editorial in the Investment Outlook 2023 with Michael Strobaek and Philipp Lisibach [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Argues geopolitics has made a comeback as a key driver of the global economy [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Argues the West-Russia confrontation over Ukraine has triggered an energy crisis as well as soaring food prices [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Argues that international commerce, far from normalizing, has reorganized according to political alliances, marking the dawn of a multipolar world [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Frames the resulting new economic reality as having more elevated inflation and a monetary policy regime prioritizing inflation stability over growth [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Argues interest rates are at their highest in years while economic growth is slowing as a result of the new regime [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
