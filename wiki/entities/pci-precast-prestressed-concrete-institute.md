# Precast/Prestressed Concrete Institute (PCI)

## Summary

The Precast/Prestressed Concrete Institute (PCI) is the U.S. industry body for precast/prestressed concrete, headquartered in Chicago, and the publisher of MNL-136-04, the Maintenance Manual for Precast Parking Structures, authored by its Committee on Parking Structures [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].

## Key facts

- Headquartered at 209 W. Jackson Boulevard, Chicago, IL 60606, with telephone (312) 786-0300 and website pci.org [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Maintains a PCI Committee on Parking Structures, chaired by Walter Korkosz, that authored MNL-136-04 with a roster of approximately 40 contributing members [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Operates a PCI plant certification program in which precast plants are monitored for quality control, fabrication, and operating procedures, supporting durable concrete mixes with specialty curing that conventional field-placed concrete systems typically do not achieve [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Published MNL-136-04 (copyright 2004, ISBN 0-937040-70-3) as a guide for owners, operators, architects, and engineers of precast parking structures [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
- Asserts that, based on national surveys, precast concrete parking structures offer superior resistance to deterioration relative to conventional field-placed concrete construction [[sources/pdf-6b0d49e91108]].
