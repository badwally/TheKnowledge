# First Resource Bank

## Summary

First Resource Bank is a Minnesota-based community bank whose lineage traces to Eagle Valley Bank (chartered St. Croix Falls, Wisconsin, 1919); the parent company acquired First Resource Bank in 2017 and merged the two entities in 2018, retaining the First Resource Bank name [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. In 2023, First Resource Bank launched OnPoint HOA Financial, dedicated to serving HOAs and property management companies within and beyond Minnesota, and it is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].

## Key facts

- Originated as Eagle Valley Bank, chartered in St. Croix Falls, Wisconsin, in 1919 [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Parent company acquired First Resource Bank in 2017 [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Eagle Valley Bank and First Resource Bank merged in 2018, resulting in the current First Resource Bank name [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Acquired two Lake Area Bank branches in Forest Lake and Lindstrom, Minnesota in 2022 [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Achieved SBA Preferred Lender status in 2022 [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Launched OnPoint HOA Financial in 2023, dedicated to HOAs and property management companies within and beyond Minnesota [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Self-positioned as one of the fastest growing banks in the region [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Named banking partner in the Vantaca partner directory [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
