# Togetherwork

## Summary

Togetherwork is the company that acquired Mike Bollinger's two prior startups — LegFi.com (fraternity and sorority financial management) and File990.org (nonprofit tax compliance) — in 2018, providing the exit liquidity that preceded Bollinger's founding of PayHOA in the same year [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]]. Togetherwork's acquisition of Bollinger's prior portfolio establishes a consolidator-of-volunteer-organization-financial-software posture relevant for understanding the adjacent-market consolidation landscape around self-managed HOA software [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].

## Key facts

- In 2018, acquired LegFi.com (Mike Bollinger's startup focused on fraternity and sorority financial management) [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- In 2018, acquired File990.org (Mike Bollinger's startup focused on nonprofit tax compliance) [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- The two 2018 acquisitions provided Bollinger the exit liquidity that preceded his founding of PayHOA in the same year (2018) [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Operating perimeter inferred from the acquired portfolio: volunteer-organization financial software (sorority/fraternity treasury management; nonprofit tax compliance) — adjacent to but distinct from the self-managed-HOA segment PayHOA subsequently targeted [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
