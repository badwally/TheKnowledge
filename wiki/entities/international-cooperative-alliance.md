# International Cooperative Alliance (ICA)

## Summary

The International Cooperative Alliance (ICA) is the global federation of cooperatives that maintains the canonical definition of a cooperative enterprise and the seven core cooperative principles cited by most cooperative legal regimes worldwide [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key facts

- ICA defines a cooperative as "an autonomous association of persons united voluntarily to meet their common economic, social and cultural needs and aspirations through a jointly-owned and democratically controlled enterprise" [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- ICA's seven cooperative principles are: (1) voluntary membership, (2) democratic member control, (3) economic participation of members, (4) autonomy and independence, (5) education, training and information, (6) cooperation among cooperatives, and (7) concern for community [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Data cooperatives that adopt the ICA framework inherit these principles as the basis for their governance design [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Some authors (Cheney et al. 2023; the distributed cooperative organisation, 2019) propose modifications or additions to the ICA principles, but the ICA set remains the reference baseline [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
