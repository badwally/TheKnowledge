# Prognostic modeling of predictive maintenance with survival analysis for mobile work equipment

## Summary

A 2022 *Scientific Reports* paper by Zhen Yang, Juho Kanniainen, Tomi Krogerus, and Frank Emmert-Streib that applies parametric and non-parametric survival analysis to estimate survival curves and hazard rates for machine components from mobile work equipment, identifying censoring-induced subpopulations and arguing that hazard functions across components are generally best modeled non-parametrically, with notable Weibull and Generalized-gamma exceptions.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Zhen Yang, Juho Kanniainen, Tomi Krogerus, and Frank Emmert-Streib and was published on 20 May 2022 in *Scientific Reports* (PMID 35595821, DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-12572-z) [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The paper addresses the modeling of prognostics of machine components from mobile work equipment [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The authors estimate survival curves and hazard rates using parametric and non-parametric models to characterize time-dependent failure probabilities of machine components [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The authors find the presence of different types of censoring masking the presence of different populations, which can cause severe problems for statistical estimators and the interpretations of results [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The authors report that the obtained hazard functions for different machine components are complex and versatile and are best modeled via non-parametric estimators [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- Notable exceptions are found for individual machine components that are amenable to Generalized-gamma and Weibull models [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The work is motivated by the recent data surge of industrial and business data and the opportunity to use predictive maintenance to optimize the life cycle of machine components [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
- The paper is indexed under MeSH terms 'Models, Statistical', 'Probability', 'Prognosis', and 'Survival Analysis' [[sources/pubmed-35595821]].
