# Helena Alegre

## Summary

Helena Alegre is a Portuguese researcher in infrastructure asset management for urban water services, co-author of foundational ERSAR/LNEC technical guides and of the integrated IAM approach that underpins AWARE-P.

## Key facts

- Alegre co-authored 'Integrated approach for infrastructure asset management of urban water systems' (Proc. IWA 4th LESAM, September 2011, Germany) with M.C. Almeida, D.I.C. Covas, M.A. Cardoso, and S.T. Coelho, providing one of the methodological precursors cited in the AWARE-P paper [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- Alegre co-authored 'Infrastructure asset management of water services' (Technical Guide n.16, 2010, ERSAR/LNEC/IST, Lisbon, 472 pp., ISBN 978-989-8360-04-5) with D. Covas — a foundational reference for the AWARE-P methodology [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
