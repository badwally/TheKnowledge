# Yao Hua Ooi

## Summary

Researcher at AQR Capital Management; co-author of the 2011 working paper "Time Series Momentum," which documents significant positive return predictability from a security's own past returns across 58 liquid futures and forward contracts [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with AQR Capital Management on the paper [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
- Second-listed author of "Time Series Momentum" (Working Paper No. 79, Chicago Booth Paper No. 12-21, September 2011), co-authored with Tobias Moskowitz and Lasse H. Pedersen [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
