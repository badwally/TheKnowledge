# Schaun Wheeler

## Summary

Schaun Wheeler is a co-founder of Aampe, the agentic AI personalization platform founded in 2020 [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].

## Key facts

- Co-founded Aampe in 2020 alongside Paul Meinshausen and Sami Abboud [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Worked with massive unstructured data for Army Intelligence prior to founding Aampe [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
