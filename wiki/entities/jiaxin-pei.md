# Jiaxin Pei

## Summary

AI researcher at Stanford University and corresponding co-author of the 2026 paper "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents," which formalizes multi-user LLM interaction as a multi-principal decision problem and stress-tests frontier LLMs on multi-user instruction following, privacy preservation, and coordination [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Stanford University on the paper, with email pedropei@stanford.edu [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
- Listed as a corresponding author of "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents," alongside corresponding co-author Shu Yang (KAUST) [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
