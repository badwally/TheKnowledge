# Patrol Points

## Summary

Patrol Points is the Property Control portfolio's security and compliance operations product, providing geo-verified tours, incident reporting, real-time dashboards, and mobile guard apps [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]].

## Key facts

- Patrol Points is one of four sister products consolidated under the Property Control parent brand effective August 29, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The product is positioned for security and compliance operations with geo-verified tours, incident reporting, and real-time dashboards [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Patrol Points contributes mobile guard apps to the broader Property Control resident-and-staff experience set [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- The product supports audit-ready archives including patrol logs and e-voting records as part of the portfolio's security and compliance assurance positioning [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Patrol Points is accessible via multi-brand single sign-on across the Property Control portfolio [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
- Existing Patrol Points customers continue on the product with no disruption at the Property Control parent-brand launch [[sources/web-2025-08-29-62d]]
