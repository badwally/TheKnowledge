# Simon Fraser University

## Summary

Canadian research university whose School of Computing Science hosts three of the five co-authors of MacoCache, the multi-agent deep reinforcement learning framework for mobile edge caching of video content [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].

## Key facts

- Hosts the School of Computing Science in Canada, the institutional home listed for MacoCache co-authors Fangxin Wang, Jiangchuan Liu, and Ryan Shea [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].
- Anchors the SFU side of an SFU–University of Mississippi–Tsinghua University collaboration that produced "Intelligent Video Caching at Network Edge: A Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach" [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].
