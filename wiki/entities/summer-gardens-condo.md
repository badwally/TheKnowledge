# Summer Gardens — Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130

## Summary

Summer Gardens is a 21-storey, 118-suite, 1987-vintage residential condominium at 1470 Summer Street in downtown Halifax, Nova Scotia, registered as Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130 [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]]. It is the load-bearing primary-source component-level calibration anchor for the Year-1 Halifax arm of the condo-capital-infra Canada-first GTM under ADR-0004 — the June 11, 2025 Sense Engineering Reserve Fund Study covers five of the six probabilistic components in the engine's scope (envelope, roofing, plumbing, HVAC central plant, parking garage structural concrete) with itemized year-by-year 25-year cost projections, an Immediately Adequately Funded Plan cash-flow trajectory, and a 2021 BRK Engineering predecessor study providing a 4-year-prior comparison anchor [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]]. Property management is by Real Estate 360 (Dartmouth, NS) [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].

### 2025 Reserve Fund Study cash-flow trajectory (Immediately Adequately Funded Plan)

- Approved funding plan: Immediately Adequately Funded — contribution level required to cover anticipated repairs and replacements without a deficit; subsequent annual contribution increases needed only to keep pace with inflation [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra engine calibration

- Summer Gardens is the first primary-source NS reserve fund study in the wiki and provides a 25-year itemized expenditure trajectory plus 4-year-prior 2021 BRK comparison anchor — exactly the calibration shape the engine v0.5 needs for the building-envelope, roofing, plumbing-riser, HVAC-central-plant, and parking-deck components on a 1987-vintage 21-storey concrete-highrise typology [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- The 80.8% one-time contribution increase in FY2026 (followed by 2.5% annual increases) is a real-world data point quantifying the funding-adequacy correction NS condominium boards face under the Immediately Adequately Funded plan structure post-Bill-106 — directly supporting the Halifax engagement (Brief-0004) demand-side narrative under ADR-0004 [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- The absent elevator capital expenditure in the 2025-2030 window is a data-quality signal that elevator-component coverage in the engine cannot rely on the 2025 Summer Gardens RFS alone — either elevators were recently modernized (pre-2025) and have remaining-useful-life beyond the 25-year window, or the building's elevator system is treated outside the RFS scope and would need to be sourced separately for elevator-component prior calibration [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
