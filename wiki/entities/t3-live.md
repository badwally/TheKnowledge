# T3 Live

## Summary

Financial publisher and trader-education brand co-founded by Scott Redler in 2007; affiliated by common ownership with T3 Trading Group, LLC (an SEC-registered Broker-Dealer and FINRA SIPC member), but managed separately as a non-broker-dealer informational/educational publisher whose products include the Virtual Trading Floor, articles, commentary, videos, blogs, and social media postings [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].

## Key facts

- Co-founded by Scott Redler as part of T3 Companies, LLC in 2007 [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- Disseminates information about economic, business, and capital markets issues through various media; explicitly not a Broker-Dealer, Investment Adviser, or any other type of business subject to regulation by the SEC, CFTC, state securities regulators, or any "self-regulatory organization" such as FINRA [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- Programs are framed as for informational and educational purposes only, and no one associated with T3 Live is authorized to make personalized investment advice [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- Affiliated with T3 Trading Group, LLC (an SEC-registered Broker-Dealer and member of FINRA SIPC) by virtue of common ownership; the two companies are managed separately and engage in different businesses [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- All trades made by Scott Redler are placed through T3 Trading Group, LLC, where he is an associated member [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- Publishes "The Ultimate Guide to Moving Averages" by Scott Redler (2023, 23 pages) [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- Operates the "Virtual Trading Floor" as a flagship product, used by Redler as a venue where his MA commentary is delivered alongside CNBC and Twitter [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
- Hosts "Redler All-Access," a service through which Redler shares his market view with traders, money managers, and individual investors [[sources/pdf-a0072c763cf8]].
