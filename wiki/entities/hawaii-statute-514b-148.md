# Hawaii Revised Statutes §514B-148 (Condominium Reserves)

## Summary

Hawaii Revised Statutes §514B-148 governs condominium reserve studies, budgeting, and the computation of replacement reserves, requiring an independent reserve-study preparer review on a three-year cycle and thirty-year cash-flow projections [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].

## Key facts

- Condominium budgets must include the amount of money in reserve, future reserve estimates based on a reserve study performed by the association, an explanation of how reserves are computed, and the amount to be collected for reserves in the year ahead [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
- A condominium's reserve study must be reviewed by an independent reserve study preparer and updated every three years [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
- The association computes estimated replacement reserves by a formula based on the estimated life and the estimated capital expenditure or major maintenance required for each part of the property [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
- Cash-flow plans must be based on thirty-year projections [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
- Estimated replacement reserves must include adjustments for revenues received and expenditures made before the beginning of the fiscal year to which the budget relates [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
- The statute requires separate, designated reserves for each part of the property whose capital expenditures or major maintenance will exceed $10,000; parts whose expenditures will not exceed $10,000 may be aggregated in a single designated reserve [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
- Italicized portions of the CAI text reflect amendments effective January 1, 2023 per HB 2272 [[sources/pdf-fc88aa6c2822]].
