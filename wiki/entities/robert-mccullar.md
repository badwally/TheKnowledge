# Robert McCullar

## Summary

Robert McCullar is the Chief Operating Officer of CINC Systems as of June 2023, named in the TresRE launch announcement as the executive responsible for characterizing the breadth of CINC's bank and software integration network and the company's view of the addressable market across real estate verticals [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

- Holds the title Chief Operating Officer at CINC Systems as of June 2023 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Stated at the June 2023 TresRE launch that CINC had over 30 integrated bank partners and software integrations at the time of launch [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Stated that CINC remained in deep discussion with many additional bank and software companies across real estate verticals, framing the TresRE opportunity as transforming the banking experience for real estate managers broadly [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
