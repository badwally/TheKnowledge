# Aware Buildings

## Summary

Aware Buildings is an IoT sensor platform vendor positioned as BuildingLink's named partner for proactive-maintenance use cases, providing real-time monitoring of environmental conditions — temperature, humidity, leaks, and air quality — so property managers can detect issues before they escalate [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]]. The product is characterized in BuildingLink's marketing copy as an "advanced IoT sensor platform" supporting cost reduction, energy efficiency, and resident-comfort outcomes [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].

## Key facts

- Integrated with the BuildingLink CAM platform as the sole named IoT-sensor partner in BuildingLink's 2025 integration portfolio post [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Provides real-time monitoring of temperature, humidity, leaks, and air quality [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Positioned to support proactive maintenance — detecting issues early so staff can address problems before they escalate [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Marketed outcomes per BuildingLink integration copy: reduced costs, improved energy efficiency, enhanced resident comfort [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
