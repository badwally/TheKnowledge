# Lasse H. Pedersen

## Summary

Finance professor at New York University and Copenhagen Business School with affiliations at NBER, CEPR, and AQR Capital Management; co-author of the 2011 working paper "Time Series Momentum," which documents significant positive return predictability from a security's own past returns across 58 liquid futures and forward contracts spanning four asset classes over more than 25 years [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with New York University, Copenhagen Business School, NBER, CEPR, and AQR Capital Management on the paper [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
- Third-listed author of "Time Series Momentum" (Working Paper No. 79, Chicago Booth Paper No. 12-21, September 2011; SSRN abstract id 2089463), co-authored with Tobias Moskowitz and Yao Hua Ooi [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
- Co-authored Asness, Moskowitz, and Pedersen (2010), which studied cross-sectional momentum and value strategies across several asset classes including individual stocks — a companion paper to the time series momentum work [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
- Co-authored Garleanu and Pedersen (2009), which showed how to trade optimally on momentum and reversal in light of transaction costs [[sources/pdf-lasse-pedersen-2011-title-how-to]].
