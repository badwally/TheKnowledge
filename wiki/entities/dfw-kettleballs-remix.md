# DFW Kettleballs Remix

## Summary

The DFW Kettleballs Remix is a 5-week, 3-day-per-week paired-[[concepts/kettlebell|kettlebell]] strength program assembled by Reddit user [[entities/bethskw|/u/Bethskw]] for the [[entities/kettleballs-subreddit|r/Kettleballs]] community as a calendarised remix of [[entities/strongfirst|StrongFirst]]'s [[entities/dry-fighting-weight|Dry Fighting Weight]] program [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key facts

- Runs for 5 weeks with 3 training sessions per week [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Designed to be done with a pair of kettlebells at the lifter's 5RM press weight [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Each training week alternates two day types: a [[concepts/clean-and-press|clean & press]] / [[concepts/front-squat|front squat]] day and a volume day consisting of 200 [[concepts/kettlebell-swing|kettlebell swings]] plus 10 sets of rows or pullups [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- C&P/FS days follow a 30-minute density structure: do a set of clean & press (cleaning each rep), rest, then a set of front squats, rest, repeating for 30 minutes — and the calendar's notes add that doing more is "strongly encouraged" [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- The [[concepts/ladder-set-protocol|set/rep ladder]] for C&P/FS days escalates across weeks: Week 1 sets of 1,2,3 / sets of 1 / sets of 2; Week 2 sets of 1,2,3 / sets of 1 / sets of 3; Week 3 sets of 1,2,3,4 / sets of 2 / sets of 3; Week 4 sets of 1,2,3,4,(5) / sets of 2 / sets of 3-4 [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Week 5 is a deload-and-test wave: only 2 C&P/FS sessions of 3 sets of 3 reps, swings and rows "as desired," and a closing session to find a new 1RM or [[concepts/rep-max|rep max]] [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- The source spreadsheet credits /u/Bethskw for assembling the remix and links out to /r/Kettleballs and the StrongFirst original [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
