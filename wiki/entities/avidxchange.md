# AvidXchange

## Summary

AvidXchange is a leading provider in accounts payable (AP) automation, offering intelligent AP software and payment solutions specifically designed for mid-market businesses and their suppliers [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]]. With 25 years of industry experience as of February 2026, AvidXchange empowers over 8,500 growth-driven businesses and has securely processed payments to more than 1.3 million suppliers through its proprietary payment network over the past five years [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]]. AvidXchange is the named third-party payments-platform partner underpinning CINC Systems' Payables+ AP automation solution, officially launched February 26, 2026, with the AvidXchange electronic-payment layer coupled to CINC's proprietary Cephai+ AI to unify invoice processing and vendor payments on a single platform [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]]. AvidXchange is the load-bearing third-party integration partner enabling the AP-to-revenue-sharing mechanic underpinning Payables+'s revenue-generating product positioning for CAM customers in the condo-capital-infra exit-thesis acquirer set under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].

### Regulatory posture (money transmitter licensing)

- Licensed money transmitter for B2B payments in the United States [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Licensed as a Money Transmitter by the New York State Department of Financial Services [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Licensed in all other states that require AvidXchange to have a license — establishing comprehensive state-by-state money-transmitter regulatory coverage as a structural compliance-infrastructure capability rather than a single-jurisdiction posture [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].

### CINC Systems Payables+ partnership (February 26, 2026)

- Named third-party payments-platform partner underpinning CINC Systems' Payables+ AP automation solution, officially launched February 26, 2026 [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- The AvidXchange electronic-payment layer is coupled to CINC's proprietary Cephai+ AI functionality, unifying invoice processing and vendor payments from start to finish on a single platform — surfacing AvidXchange as the payment-execution layer beneath CINC's AI-driven invoice-ingestion and ledger-coding capabilities [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- The CINC-AvidXchange integration enables community association management companies to generate incremental revenue with digital payment transactions — operationalizing the AP-to-revenue-sharing mechanic that converts CINC clients' AP from a pure cost center into a "high-margin revenue operation," per CINC's load-bearing revenue-model disclosure [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- CINC's Feb 26, 2026 official-launch announcement frames AvidXchange's payments platform as "industry leading" — a vendor-side endorsement that situates AvidXchange as the preferred payments-partner counterparty for the CAM segment under CINC's Hg-sponsored product strategy [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- AvidXchange is the load-bearing third-party integration partner enabling the AP-to-revenue-sharing mechanic in CINC's Payables+ product — making AvidXchange a structural dependency in any condo-capital-infra Year-3 acquirer engagement thesis targeting CINC, where the Payables+ revenue line directly underwrites CINC's stated platform revenue-model thesis [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- AvidXchange's licensed-money-transmitter regulatory posture (NY DFS + all licensing-required states) establishes the regulatory perimeter through which Payables+ B2B payment flows are processed — a compliance-infrastructure layer external to CINC's own product perimeter, surfacing the strategic dependency CINC has on AvidXchange's regulatory licensing footprint for the AP-revenue-sharing economics to operate; any acquirer-side diligence on CINC under the project's Year-3 exit window needs to model AvidXchange's regulatory continuity as a structural risk factor independent of CINC's internal capabilities [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- The 1.3M-supplier scale + 25-year operating history + comprehensive state-by-state money-transmitter licensing posture position AvidXchange as a difficult-to-replicate payments-infrastructure counterparty — increasing the strategic value of the CINC-AvidXchange integration as a competitive moat for CINC's payments adjacency vs. competitors who would need to either build equivalent payments infrastructure or contract with a comparable third-party partner [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
