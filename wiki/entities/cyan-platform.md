# cyan™ (FirstService Residential Integrated Tech Platform)

## Summary

cyan™ is the cloud-based digital platform designed exclusively for FirstService Residential, used by community managers and heart-of-house staff to deliver community association management services to FirstService's clients through a suite of integrated tools [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]]. Integration with the HODA™ platform is positioned in FirstService's marketing copy as making cyan™ a one-of-a-kind technology available only to FirstService Residential associates and customers — i.e., the platform is not licensed externally to other CAM operators [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]]. The product is marketed as an "all-in-one association management" platform that brings FirstService's property management tools together in a single, intuitive interface for board members, residents, and associates [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].

## Key facts

- Cloud-based digital platform; designed exclusively for FirstService Residential [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Marketed as "all-in-one association management" — brings FirstService's property management tools together in a single, intuitive interface [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Primary in-house user constituencies: community managers and heart-of-house staff [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- External-facing user constituencies: board members, residents, and associates of FirstService-managed communities [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Provides information, documents, and tools to support board members, residents, and other associates [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Integrates with the HODA™ platform; the cyan↔HODA integration is framed as making cyan a one-of-a-kind technology available only to FirstService Residential associates and customers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Stated outcome thesis: enhances experiences and relationships within communities to simplify the lives of residents, board members, and associates — ultimately driving trust and client success [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Name rationale per FirstService marketing copy: inspired by the clear blue of pure water; symbolizes the firm's pursuit of clarity and transparency, combining a calming hue (peace of mind for residents) with a vibrant color (energy and focus) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Public-facing product URL: fsresidential.com/corporate/cyan/ [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Source provides no architectural detail (tech stack, vendor partners, hosting model), no customer counts, no pricing, and no third-party integration enumeration beyond HODA™ [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
