# Alex G. Kim

## Summary

Researcher at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business and first-listed author of the 2024 paper "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models," which shows that GPT-4 Turbo with chain-of-thought prompting outperforms median financial analysts at predicting the direction of next-period earnings from anonymized balance sheets and income statements [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Chicago Booth School of Business; contact email alex.kim@chicagobooth.edu [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- First-listed author of "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models" (May 20, 2024 draft) with Maximilian Muhn and Valeri V. Nikolaev [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- The paper investigates whether an LLM can perform financial statement analysis in a way similar to a professional human analyst by passing standardized and anonymous financial statements to GPT-4 Turbo and instructing the model to determine the direction of future earnings [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- Reports that GPT-4 with a chain-of-thought prompt achieves 60% accuracy on direction-of-earnings prediction, exceeding the 53% achieved by first-month analyst consensus forecasts and on par with a state-of-the-art specialized ANN [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- Reports that trading strategies based on GPT predictions yield higher Sharpe ratios and alphas than strategies based on the other models in the paper's empirical comparison [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
