# Braveheart Trading

## Summary

Trading-education brand that distributes Inner Circle Trader (ICT) mentorship material in PDF form, including the 2022 ICT Mentorship Market Review on Balanced Price Ranges (August 13, 2022); Braveheart frames itself as "dedicated to freedom and for reaching your full trading potential" and directs readers back to the Inner Circle Trader YouTube channel as the original source of the material [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].

## Key facts

- Tagline: "Dedicated to Freedom and for Reaching Your Full Trading Potential" [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- Distributed the August 13, 2022 ICT Mentorship Market Review titled "Balanced Price Ranges" as a 5-page PDF [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- The distributed PDF explicitly attributes the underlying concepts to The Inner Circle Trader and points readers to the Inner Circle Trader YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/c/InnerCircleTrader) as the original source [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- Self-describes (via the PDF's introductory note) as a YouTuber who helps traders reach their full trading potential by serving as "the bridge between who Traders currently are and who they want to become" [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- Maintains Twitter, YouTube, and Newsletter channels, all linked from the distributed PDF's cover and back pages [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- The PDF carries a disclaimer that its contents should not be considered professional advice — "merely for entertainment purposes only" [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
