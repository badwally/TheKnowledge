# StrongFirst

## Summary

StrongFirst is the strength-education organization that publishes the original [[entities/dry-fighting-weight|Dry Fighting Weight]] kettlebell program at strongfirst.com — the upstream program that [[entities/bethskw|/u/Bethskw]]'s [[entities/dfw-kettleballs-remix|DFW Kettleballs Remix]] adapts into a calendar form [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key facts

- Hosts the original "Dry Fighting Weight" program at https://www.strongfirst.com/dry-fighting-weight/ [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Is referenced as the upstream authority by community-derived programs such as the DFW Kettleballs Remix, which credit StrongFirst's published version as the "Original" [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
