# Universa Investments

## Summary

Investment firm at which Nassim Nicholas Taleb lists an affiliation on the paper "Bitcoin, Currencies, and Fragility" (forthcoming, Quantitative Finance) [[sources/pdf-752c8824b750]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Taleb's primary institutional affiliation on the paper, alongside the Tandon School of Engineering at New York University [[sources/pdf-752c8824b750]].
- Mark Spitznagel — associated with Universa in the broader public record — appears among the discussants Taleb thanks in the paper's acknowledgments [[sources/pdf-752c8824b750]].
