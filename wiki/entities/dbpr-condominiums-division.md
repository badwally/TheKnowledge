# Florida DBPR Division of Florida Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes

## Summary

The Division of Florida Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes is the unit of the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) responsible for administering Florida's Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) reporting regime and operating the public SIRS Reporting Database [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]]. The Division receives SIRS Reporting Forms electronically from condominium associations within 45 days of SIRS completion (effective July 1, 2024), operates the association online account portal that became mandatory by October 1, 2025, publishes the annually adjusted reserve threshold used for SIRS "Other elements" scoping, and maintains the two public SIRS submission lists split between pre- and post-July 2025 cohorts [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].

### SIRS administration role

- Receives electronic submissions of the SIRS Reporting Form from condominium associations within 45 days of SIRS completion, effective July 1, 2024 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Operates the online account portal through which condominium and cooperative associations confirm SIRS completion [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- All condominium and cooperative associations were required to create an online account with the Division by October 1, 2025 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Publishes the Reserve Threshold Page documenting the annually adjusted reserve threshold used to scope which "Other elements" are included in the SIRS [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Operates the public SIRS Reporting Database, with online-account submissions reflected within one business day of completion [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Displays SIRS information in the Reporting Database exactly as submitted, with only complete submissions of the SIRS Reporting Form displayed [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
- Publishes two public submission lists: condominiums and cooperatives that submitted SIRS prior to July 2025, and those that submitted in July 2025 or later [[sources/web-2026-01-01-818]].
