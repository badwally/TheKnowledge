# LexisNexis Risk Solutions

## Summary

LexisNexis Risk Solutions is the operator of the LexisNexis® Telematics Exchange, a source-agnostic platform that ingests, processes, and normalizes vehicle telematics data from multiple automotive OEMs and telematics service providers for delivery to auto insurance carriers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]]. The company positions decades of experience in data processing and normalization as the foundation on which the Telematics Exchange is built [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].

## Key facts

- LexisNexis Risk Solutions operates the LexisNexis® Telematics Exchange [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- LexisNexis Risk Solutions operates LexisNexis® Telematics OnDemand, described by the company as its flagship, award-winning telematics-for-insurance solution [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- The company claims decades of experience in data processing and normalization that underpins the Telematics Exchange platform [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- LexisNexis Risk Solutions states that its intelligent driving data solutions are intended to help reduce the total cost of vehicle ownership for the consumer while improving the customer experience [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- The company positions its Exchange-based solutions as enabling insurers to assess risk more accurately at point of quote, underwriting, renewal, and beyond [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
