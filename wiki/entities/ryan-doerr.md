# Ryan Doerr

## Summary

Ryan Doerr is President and CEO of Strategic Club Solutions, the club-management consulting firm that operates the Navigator platform [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]]. He is the named SCS principal in the April 2025 strategic partnership announcement with SmartProperty extending the Living Reserve Study® into the Large Scale Clubs segment [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].

## Key facts

- Ryan Doerr is President and CEO of Strategic Club Solutions per the April 7, 2025 SmartProperty partnership announcement [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- He characterized the SmartProperty partnership as "a game-changer for clubs and communities," framing it as a combined financial planning and strategic management solution for data-driven decision-making [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
