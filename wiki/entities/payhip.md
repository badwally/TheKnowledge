# PayHip

## Summary

E-commerce platform used by SwingAlgo+ to sell its WEEKLY BIAS TradingView indicator; purchase verification with SwingAlgo+ requires the buyer to retain the PayHip receipt and provide the PayHip receipt number when requesting indicator access [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].

## Key facts

- Issues a purchase receipt by email upon a SwingAlgo+ WEEKLY BIAS order, which the buyer must keep on hand as proof of purchase [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- The PayHip receipt number is one of three required fields in the SwingAlgo+ access-request email (alongside full name and TradingView username) [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
- Buyers who do not see a PayHip receipt email are instructed to check their spam/junk folder before contacting the vendor [[sources/pdf-f073cccdbfe6]].
