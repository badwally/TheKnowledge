# Benjamin Bruder

## Summary

Quantitative researcher in the Research & Development group at Lyxor Asset Management, Paris; first-listed author of the December 2011 Lyxor working paper "Trend Filtering Methods for Momentum Strategies" [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].

## Key facts

- Listed affiliation on the paper: Research & Development, Lyxor Asset Management, Paris [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- Listed contact email: benjamin.bruder@lyxor.com [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- First-listed author of "Trend Filtering Methods for Momentum Strategies" (December 2011), co-authored with Tung-Lam Dao, Jean-Charles Richard, and Thierry Roncalli [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- The paper situates moving-average filtering as the most commonly used model in trading strategies because it is intuitive and easy to implement, and reviews both linear and nonlinear filtering classes [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
