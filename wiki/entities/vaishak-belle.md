# Vaishak Belle

## Summary

AI researcher at the University of Edinburgh and co-author of the AAAI-26 paper "The Future Is Neuro-Symbolic: Where Has It Been, and Where Is It Going?", which traces the evolution of neuro-symbolic AI and argues against the "scaling is all you need" hypothesis [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Edinburgh on the paper [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- Co-authored "The Future Is Neuro-Symbolic: Where Has It Been, and Where Is It Going?" with Gary Marcus, presented at the Fortieth AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI-26) [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- Cited in the paper as the author of "Belle 2017" on weighted model counting, the foundation invoked for a well-defined and scoped view of neuro-symbolic AI as the neural extension of weighted model counting [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- Cited as a co-author of "Belle and Bueff 2023" and "Bueff and Belle 2024" on extending differential program induction to dynamic settings such as reinforcement learning [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
