# Mitsuyoshi Akiyama

## Summary

Mitsuyoshi Akiyama is a co-author of the 2026 review paper on life-cycle performance, design, maintenance, optimization, and decision-making of asphalt pavement under uncertainty.

## Key facts

- Akiyama is the third listed author of 'Life-cycle performance, design, maintenance, optimization, and decision-making of asphalt pavement under uncertainty: a review,' published on 1 February 2026 with DOI 10.1080/15732479.2025.2552239 [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The paper is co-authored with Jiyu Xin, Dan M Frangopol, Lianzhen Zhang, and Jianzhong Pei [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
