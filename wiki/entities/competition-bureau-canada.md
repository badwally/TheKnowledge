# Competition Bureau of Canada

## Summary

The Competition Bureau is an independent Canadian law enforcement agency responsible for administering and enforcing the Competition Act and for protecting and promoting competitive markets and informed consumer choice [[sources/web-2024-06-27-57a]] [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].

## Key facts

- Headed by the Commissioner of Competition; final prosecutorial decisions in criminal matters rest with the Director of Public Prosecutions (DPP) rather than the Bureau itself [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- Final interpretation of the Competition Act is the responsibility of the Competition Tribunal and the courts, not the Bureau [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- Publishes enforcement guidelines describing its approach to particular provisions of the Act, and updates them through public consultation — the 2020 draft Competitor Collaboration Guidelines were open for public consultation between July 29, 2020 and September 28, 2020 [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- May use formal information-gathering powers under sections 11 and 15 of the Act to obtain information or records relevant to determining whether an arrangement is a merger, a competitor collaboration, or otherwise [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- May issue binding written opinions on the applicability of provisions of the Act to proposed future conduct under section 124.1 of the Act [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
- Sits administratively within Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED); located at 50 Victoria Street, Gatineau QC [[sources/web-2022-01-19-c5a]].
