# Rent Manager

## Summary

Rent Manager is a property-management software product named as one of 17 accounting integration partners in BuildingLink's resident-engagement platform [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. The Rent Manager–BuildingLink integration runs on a typically-overnight 24-hour sync and surfaces Rent Manager account balance and transaction history to unit owners on the BuildingLink resident site [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].

## Key facts

- Named as an accounting integration partner in BuildingLink's accounting-integration catalog [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Syncs with BuildingLink every 24 hours, typically overnight [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- After the sync, unit owners can view their Rent Manager account balance and transaction history on the BuildingLink resident site [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
