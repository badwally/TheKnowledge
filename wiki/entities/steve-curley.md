# Steve Curley

## Summary

Steve Curley is the named President of Alliance Association Bank (AAB), the Western Alliance Bank division that operates a community-association banking franchise across all 50 states [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]]. Curley's role was identified in the May 2022 CAI Keystone Chapter sponsored vendor profile of AAB, alongside the parent-bank leadership disclosure.

## Key facts

- Title: President, Alliance Association Bank, as of May 2022 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Identified alongside Kenneth Vecchione (President and CEO, Western Alliance Bank) in the AAB principal-of-company disclosure, establishing the division-to-parent leadership hierarchy [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
