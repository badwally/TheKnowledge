# Environmental Working Group (EWG)

## Summary

U.S. nonprofit (EWG.org) that publishes the "Dirty Dozen" and "Clean 15" lists of pesticide-residue rankings for produce; recommended in Dr. Carla Kuon's UCSF Long COVID Group Medical Visit handout as a practical tool for choosing which conventional vs. organic produce to prioritize on an anti-inflammatory diet [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].

## Key facts

- Web reference cited in the handout: EWG.org [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- The handout pairs the EWG "Dirty Dozen and Clean 15" with the UCSF nutrition reference page (osher.ucsf.edu/putting-nutrition-practice-tips-making-dietary-changes) as joint guidance for putting an anti-inflammatory diet into practice [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
