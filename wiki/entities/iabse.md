# IABSE (International Association for Bridge and Structural Engineering)

## Summary

IABSE is the International Association for Bridge and Structural Engineering, organizer and publisher of the IABSE Symposium Tokyo 2025 proceedings and copyright holder of the papers presented there.

## Key facts

- IABSE organized the IABSE Symposium Tokyo 2025 on 'Environmentally Friendly Technologies and Structures: Focusing on Sustainable Approaches,' held in Tokyo, Japan, 18-21 May 2025 [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
- IABSE publishes the *IABSE Symposium Tokyo 2025* proceedings and holds copyright over the papers contained therein [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
