# Vantaca

## Summary

Vantaca is the leading provider of community association management (CAM) software, headquartered in Wilmington, North Carolina [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. The company is focused exclusively on community management and positions its product as business operating software for owners and operators, community management teams, accounting teams, and association boards [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. On November 19, 2024, at its annual customer conference Vantaca Vision, Vantaca announced the acquisition of HOAi, a pioneering AI technology provider in the HOA management space; the acquisition was publicized via PRNewswire on November 20, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. The acquisition introduced a two-tiered AI approach combining Scout (an Everyday AI research assistant integrated within Vantaca) and HOAi (a standalone Transformational AI solution) [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

## Key facts

### Corporate profile

- Headquartered in Wilmington, North Carolina [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Self-described as the leading provider of community association management software [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Focused exclusively on community management [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Positioned as community management performance software that enables owners and operators, community management teams, accounting teams, and association boards to improve business performance [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Positioned as business operating software that helps increase revenue, efficiency, flexibility, and control [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Self-described as the trusted and proven technology leader in the community association management industry [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Public-facing website: www.vantaca.com [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Ben Currin is identified as Chief Executive Officer as of November 20, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

### Vantaca Vision annual customer conference

- Hosts an annual customer conference called Vantaca Vision [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Announced the HOAi acquisition at Vantaca Vision on November 19, 2024 (the day before the November 20, 2024 PRNewswire release) [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

### HOAi acquisition (November 19/20, 2024)

- Acquired HOAi, a pioneering AI technology provider in the HOA management space [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Characterized in company communications as reinforcing Vantaca's commitment to advancing the future of community management through innovative, AI-powered solutions [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Introduced a two-tiered AI product taxonomy with the acquisition: Scout (Everyday AI research assistant integrated within Vantaca) and HOAi (standalone Transformational AI solution) [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- HOAi continues to operate as an independent product post-acquisition with a dedicated focus on AI innovation [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Independent-product structure intended to allow HOAi to maintain maximum speed and flexibility in development while providing seamless integration to Vantaca customers and remaining available to other association management companies that do not use the Vantaca platform [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Acquisition rationale articulated by CEO Ben Currin centered on integrating HOAi's advanced AI capabilities while preserving operational independence to ensure rapid development and deployment of next-generation solutions [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
