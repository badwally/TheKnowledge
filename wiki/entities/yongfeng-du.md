# Yongfeng Du

## Summary

Yongfeng Du is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2024 *Advances in Structural Engineering* paper proposing a multivariate Bayesian dynamic linear method for bridge performance degradation modelling.

## Key facts

- Du is the fifth (final) listed author of "Bridge performance degradation model based on the multi-variate bayesian dynamic linear method," first published online on 23 July 2024 in *Advances in Structural Engineering* (DOI 10.1177/13694332241266541) [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- The paper is co-authored with Guojun Yang, Li Tian, Jianbo Mao, and Guangwu Tang [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
