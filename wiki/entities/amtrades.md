# AMtrades

## Summary

Trading-education brand (handle `_amtrades`) and author of the 2023 PDF "High-Probability Conditions: rules and guidelines," a six-page personal-rules document that codifies pre-news blackouts, daily consolidation-profile classification, overnight range expansion, and inside-day discipline as filters for NY-session intraday participation [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].

## Key facts

- Branded `_amtrades` on every diagram of "High-Probability Conditions: rules and guidelines" (2023) [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Frames the document as "rules and guidelines" rather than a strategy, organizing trading discretion around four filters: economic calendar, daily consolidation profile, overnight range expansion, and inside day [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Treats the economic calendar as a primary input, directing traders to forexfactory.com/calendar and naming CPI, NFP, FOMC, PCE, GDP, and PPI as the high-impact events that drive the blackout windows [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Operationalizes "high-probability" as engaging external-range liquidity rather than internal consolidation, after manipulation timed to high-impact economic events [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
- Concludes the document with a "personal rules" page enumerating the conditions under which the trader will and will not participate intraday — including no trading outside London or New York session, no trading on bank holidays, and no trading on half days [[sources/pdf-46c6dd54d41e]].
