# Ghislain Verdier

## Summary

Ghislain Verdier is the third-listed (senior) co-author of the 2021 *Journal of Applied Probability* paper on stochastic comparisons and ageing properties of an extended gamma process.

## Key facts

- Verdier is the third listed author of 'Stochastic comparisons and ageing properties of an extended gamma process,' published in the *Journal of Applied Probability*, Volume 58, Issue 1, on 20 March 2021 [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
- The paper is co-authored with Zeina Al Masry and Sophie Mercier [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
