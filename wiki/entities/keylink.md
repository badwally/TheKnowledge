# KeyLink

## Summary

KeyLink is BuildingLink's in-house electronic key management product, marketed as the integrated solution for storing, tracking, and managing access to unit and common-area keys at properties using the BuildingLink CAM platform [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]]. The product maintains a digital log documenting which residents and vendors took which keys and when, framed as a replacement for ad-hoc front-desk key-handoff processes that produce misplaced keys and front-desk confusion [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].

## Key facts

- Operated by BuildingLink as an integrated module of the BuildingLink CAM platform [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Securely stores, tracks, and manages access to unit and common-area keys [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Maintains a digital log documenting which user took which key and at what time, providing front-desk auditability [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Marketed against the operational problem of property managers spending time tracking down keys at resident or vendor request [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
