# Credit Suisse

## Summary

Global investment bank whose Investment Strategy Department within Investment Solutions & Sustainability published Zoltan Pozsar's May 13, 2022 dispatch "Ride of the 'Volkyries'" arguing that the Fed is pursuing demand destruction through negative wealth effects [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].

## Key facts

- The Investment Strategy Department within Investment Solutions & Sustainability is the institutional home of Zoltan Pozsar's "Ride of the 'Volkyries'" (May 13, 2022) [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Includes a "THIS IS NOT RESEARCH" disclosure on the dispatch, noting the report represents views of the Investment Strategy Department and was not prepared in accordance with the legal requirements designed to promote the independence of investment research [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Maintains policies in place to promote the independence of its Research Departments from its Investment Strategy and other departments and to manage conflicts of interest, including policies relating to dealing ahead of the dissemination of investment research [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- The dispatch notes that views of the Investment Strategy Department may differ materially from the views of the Credit Suisse Research Department and other divisions, even when the report references published research recommendations [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
