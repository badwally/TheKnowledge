# National Corn Growers Association (NCGA)

## Summary

The National Corn Growers Association (NCGA) is a U.S. producer association that has partnered with Ag Data Transparent (ADT) to extend ADT's certification-based data-governance model into the carbon-contracts space [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].

## Key facts

- Co-released Transparency Principles for Ag Carbon together with ADT [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
