# Olivier Lopez

## Summary

Olivier Lopez is a co-author of the 2025 arXiv preprint introducing Weibull-Tailored Neural Networks (WTNN) for survival analysis under right censoring.

## Key facts

- Lopez is the second listed co-author of "WTNN: Weibull-Tailored Neural Networks for survival analysis," arXiv 2512.09163, posted 9 December 2025 [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
- The paper is co-authored with Gabrielle Rives and Nicolas Bousquet [[sources/arxiv-2512.09163]].
