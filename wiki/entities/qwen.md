# Qwen

## Summary

Open-weight large language model family developed by Alibaba; reported by the March 2026 USCC "Two Loops" paper as the largest model ecosystem on Hugging Face, with over 100,000 derivatives [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].

## Key facts

- Accounted for the largest model ecosystem on Hugging Face with over 100,000 derivatives as of March 2026 [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Qwen3-Max — the flagship model in the family at the time of the paper — is reported with approximately 1 trillion parameters, 36 trillion training tokens, and 1.5 × 10^25 FLOPs of training compute, making it the leading Chinese model in 2025 by training compute, training data size, and model size [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Updates to the Qwen family became "widely used internationally" in 2025, contributing to the global uptake of Chinese open-weight AI [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
