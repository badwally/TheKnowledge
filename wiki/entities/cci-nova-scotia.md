# CCI-Nova Scotia

## Summary

CCI-Nova Scotia is the Nova Scotia chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI), the national industry body for condominium communities in Canada; it publishes chapter news including the June 5, 2023 announcement "Changes to the Condominium Act have been Proclaimed," reporting on the May 23, 2023 proclamation of amendments to the Nova Scotia Condominium Act [[sources/web-2026-01-01-577]]. It is the NS sibling of the CCI New Brunswick Chapter, which the CMHC New Brunswick provincial condominium fact sheet identifies as the independent industry body representing participants in the NB condominium community [[sources/web-2018-03-31-c63]].

## Key facts

- Nova Scotia chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI), the national industry body for condominium communities in Canada [[sources/web-2026-01-01-577]].
- Publishes chapter news to its `ccinovascotia.ca` website; the chapter news feed includes the June 5, 2023 piece "Changes to the Condominium Act have been Proclaimed," reporting on the May 23, 2023 proclamation of Nova Scotia Condominium Act amendments [[sources/web-2026-01-01-577]].
- Acts as a Nova Scotia industry-side reporting and education channel for changes to the Nova Scotia Condominium Act, including the 2023 amendments to reserve-fund-study cadence, developer-to-elected-board transition rules, voting modernization, and bare-land-condominium disclosure [[sources/web-2026-01-01-577]].
- The CMHC New Brunswick provincial condominium fact sheet identifies the CCI New Brunswick Chapter — CCI-Nova Scotia's Atlantic-Canada sibling chapter — as "the independent industry body representing participants in the NB condominium community," establishing the CCI chapter network as the structural Atlantic-Canada industry-association layer paralleling provincial statutory regimes [[sources/web-2018-03-31-c63]].
