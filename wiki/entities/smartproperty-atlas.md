# SmartProperty Atlas

## Summary

SmartProperty Atlas is SmartProperty's named reserve-study intelligence engine, launched publicly via the product landing page on June 3, 2026 and marketed as the platform purpose-built to document and defend an association's reserve position for lender, board, and regulator review. SmartProperty publicly positions Atlas and the parent company as the compliance partner for the Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac condominium-project standards announced March 18, 2026 — specifically the Jan 4, 2027 minimum 15% annual-budget reserve allocation, the Aug 3, 2026 elimination of the Limited Review process, and the Jul 1, 2026 $50,000 per-unit insurance deductible cap [[sources/web-2026-03-23-6fd]].

## Key facts

- SmartProperty's March 23, 2026 blog post is the company's primary public statement framing SmartProperty (and by parent designation Atlas) as the compliance instrument for Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 and Freddie Mac Bulletin 2026; per Finding-0043, no other primary source documents this positioning claim [[sources/web-2026-03-23-6fd]].
- The compliance narrative covers four dated obligations: (i) Aug 3, 2026 — Limited Review elimination (historically used in ~65% of condo loans); (ii) Jul 1, 2026 — $50,000 per-unit maximum insurance deductible; (iii) Jan 1, 2027 — annual servicer insurance verification and coverage-reduction monitoring; (iv) Jan 4, 2027 — 15% of annual budget allocated to reserves [[sources/web-2026-03-23-6fd]].
- SmartProperty markets its services as providing "reserve funding analysis, capital planning, and the financial documentation boards need to demonstrate long-term stability to lenders, homeowners, and regulators" — explicitly framing the company as the documentation/compliance vehicle boards engage to avoid a compressed late-2026 timeline [[sources/web-2026-03-23-6fd]].
- SmartProperty's blog positions the company as the substitute for baseline-funded reserve studies under the new rule: if an association funds below 15%, the rule requires submission of a professional reserve study using the "highest recommended funding level," and baseline funding will not be accepted as justification [[sources/web-2026-03-23-6fd]].
- The blog post acknowledges potential waivers for smaller associations (10 units or fewer) under the expanded Waiver of Project Review, but frames the majority of associations as subject to the full project review burden [[sources/web-2026-03-23-6fd]].
