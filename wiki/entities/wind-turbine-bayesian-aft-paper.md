# Bayesian Survival Models Reveal Wind-Driven Reliability Patterns in Turbines

## Summary

A 2026 paper by Clément Jacquet, Liliana Haus, Nicolas Marx Hermoso, and Rajasekhar Pulikollu proposing a Bayesian hierarchical Accelerated Failure Time (AFT) model that disentangles intrinsic manufacturer design variability from wind-driven environmental effects on wind turbine gearbox reliability, applied to a fleet-scale dataset of over 8,000 gearboxes and 64,000 cumulative operating years.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Clément Jacquet, Liliana Haus, Nicolas Marx Hermoso, and Rajasekhar Pulikollu and was published on 1 May 2026 with DOI 10.1088/1742-6596/3232/1/012018 [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The motivating premise is that wind turbine major components failures represent a disproportionate share of wind farm operation and maintenance costs [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The authors argue that robust statistical inference on wind turbine reliability is challenging because large-scale datasets aggregate turbines exposed to heterogeneous environmental conditions, creating confounding between intrinsic design variability and wind-driven effects [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The paper proposes a Bayesian hierarchical Accelerated Failure Time model to disentangle intrinsic and environmental contributions to failure [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The framework was applied to a dataset of over 8,000 gearboxes and 64,000 cumulative operating years [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The framework explicitly captures manufacturer-specific baseline reliability while estimating the influence of environmental covariates [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The authors report that intrinsic design differences dominate the failure signal in their analysis [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- Capacity factor emerges as the most robust environmental driver of lifetime reduction, which the authors interpret as consistent with fatigue-based degradation mechanisms [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The authors position the proposed framework as a rigorous basis for fleet-scale reliability assessment, maintenance planning, and long-term asset management in wind energy systems [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The article is published under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
