# Andrew Constan

## Summary

Market strategist and author of The Damped Spring Report; career spanning 38+ years across Salomon Brothers (corporate finance → convertible bond trading → equity derivatives), an assignment to the Presidential Task Force on Market Mechanisms (Brady Commission) after the 1987 crash, a visit to LTCM during the 1998 bailout on behalf of Sandy Weill, a stint at Bridgewater Associates starting May 3, 2010, and ultimately the founding of his own advisory practice [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].

## Key facts

- Started at Salomon Brothers as a corporate finance analyst approximately 15 months before the October 1987 crash [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- Soon after the 1987 crash, was assigned to the Presidential Task Force on Market Mechanisms (the "Brady Commission"), whose job was to determine why the stock market had crashed and recommend regulatory changes [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- During the 1989–1991 S&L/Drexel deleveraging, worked as a convertible bond trader at Salomon; participated in competitive bidding for Drexel Burnham's inventory after the firm went bankrupt, describing it as "my first experience of a fire sale of assets by a major end user and liquidity provider" [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- During the 1994 duration-led deleveraging, managed the U.S. convertible trading business at Salomon; proprietary inventory was marked down as hedge fund clients were forced to sell to meet redemptions [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- During the 1997 Asian Financial Crisis, his team lost $10 million short volatility on the Hang Seng Index and was forced to cover by Sandy Weill directly at 100 implied volatility [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- During the 1998 LTCM crisis, was responsible for the equity derivatives business at Salomon and held 5 million vega of 4-year straddles purchased from LTCM as a hedge to other customer positions [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- Sent to LTCM by Sandy Weill with two colleagues and Weill's son to attempt to buy the book of positions; was "rebuffed nicely" by Victor Haghani [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- Left Salomon to start his own hedge fund during or after the 2000–2002 tech bubble era [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- Joined Bridgewater Associates on May 3, 2010, the week of the Flash Crash [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- Publishes The Damped Spring Report (DSR), whose tagline is "Shifts in growth, inflation, risk premium and positioning all lead to opportunities in markets" [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- Developed a deleveraging framework based on 38 years of firsthand experience, studying deleveraging events, and working with others who lived through them [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
- In the August 11, 2024 DSR, applied the framework to 13 historical deleveraging episodes from 1987 to 2023 to contextualize the post-FOMC/BOJ/refunding deleveraging of August 2024, concluding the episode most closely resembles 1987 — broad but not systemic, banks healthy, no expected real economy impact, no expected policymaker response — but with circuit breakers making a crash unlikely and a 20% drawdown plausible [[sources/pdf-f139d7bd1924]].
