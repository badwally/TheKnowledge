# International Data Spaces Association (IDSA)

## Summary

The International Data Spaces Association (IDSA) is a non-profit organisation based in Dortmund, Germany that develops open standards and governance frameworks for sovereign data sharing [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]]. Its reference architecture underpins the Catena-X data space and numerous other industrial data ecosystems worldwide [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].

## Key facts

- Non-profit organisation headquartered in Dortmund, Germany [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Develops open standards and governance frameworks for sovereign data sharing [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Its reference architecture underpins the Catena-X data space and other industrial data ecosystems worldwide [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Lars Nagel is CEO [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Per CEO Nagel, IDSA has been developing the reference architecture for over ten years (i.e., since circa 2016) [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Co-conducts the €23 million Data Space Accelerator study in partnership with Catena-X and Cofinity-X [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Per Nagel, the value of industrial data spaces has historically been based on theoretical models and isolated pilot projects, and the Data Space Accelerator is positioned as a shift toward robust empirical evidence on how network effects, data maturity, and economic value interact in open data spaces [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
