# Strategic Inspections

## Summary

Strategic Inspections is a reserve study preparation firm active in the NYC condo/co-op market. Its president and CEO, Orest Tomaselli, is the principal industry-expert source in Habitat Magazine's April 2026 Bricks & Bucks coverage of the Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac March 2026 GSE policy update [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]]. The firm appears in the industry coverage as both a market-pricing reference point ($7,500–$9,500 reserve-study cost) and as the channel through which the demand-generation mechanism of the 15% reserve-funding floor and the Limited Review retirement is articulated to NYC boards [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].

## Key facts

- Identified in Habitat Magazine's April 2026 coverage as a reserve study preparation firm [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
- President and CEO is Orest Tomaselli [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
- Cited as the market-pricing reference for reserve studies in the NYC condo/co-op market at $7,500 to $9,500 [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
- Quoted via Tomaselli on the demand-generation mechanism created by the GSE 15% reserve floor and the retirement of Limited Review, and on the cascade of GSE-ineligibility designations into jumbo and commercial lending markets [[sources/web-2026-04-21-260]].
