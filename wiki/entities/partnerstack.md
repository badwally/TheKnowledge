# PartnerStack

## Summary

PartnerStack is the partner-management platform in Orita's internal stack, covering the partner program, referral tracking, and payouts [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]]. It is also a planned MCP connector for the Competitive Intelligence agent, providing competitive referral patterns as a partner/ecosystem signal source (S7.4) [[sources/docx-7ed3b7965067]].

## Key facts

- PartnerStack covers Orita's partner program, referral tracking, and payouts [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
- The Competitive Intelligence agent's source taxonomy assigns PartnerStack competitive referral patterns to provenance category S7.4 (Ecosystem and Partner Signals) [[sources/docx-7ed3b7965067]].
- PartnerStack integration is accessed via a platform MCP connector (Category 3, pending evaluation) [[sources/docx-7ed3b7965067]].
