# Daniel le Maire

## Summary

Economist in the Department of Economics at the University of Copenhagen; co-author of the 2022 NBER working paper "Eclipse of Rent-Sharing: The Effects of Managers' Business Education on Wages and the Labor Share in the US and Denmark," and the author whose access to Danish administrative data anchors the paper's mechanism analysis (rent-sharing under export demand shocks and the role-model IV for college-major choice) [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Copenhagen, Department of Economics, Copenhagen, Denmark 1719V on the paper [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- Contact email Daniel.le.Maire@econ.ku.dk on the paper [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- Co-author of "Eclipse of Rent-Sharing" (NBER Working Paper No. 29874, March 2022) with Daron Acemoglu and Alex He [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- The Danish data analyzed in the paper enable the rent-sharing mechanism analysis using firm-specific export demand shocks (the Hummels et al. 2014 strategy) and the role-model IV for college-major choice [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
- The paper acknowledges Jakob Munch for sharing the data on trade shocks used in the Danish analysis [[sources/pdf-4dbe002c1c7a]].
