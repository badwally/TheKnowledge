# CINC Capital

## Summary

CINC Capital is the sister-company acquisition-finance fund of CINC Systems, created by CINC Founder and Chairman Bill Blanton and publicly launched the week of June 10, 2020 concurrent with the announcement of Spectrum Equity's first institutional investment in CINC Systems [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]]. The fund was capitalized in part with a portion of the proceeds from Spectrum Equity's investment in CINC Systems and provides capital and strategic counsel for community association management companies to acquire other small- to medium-sized community association management companies across the United States [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]]. CINC Capital has a unique blend of community association and banking expertise; at its public unveiling in June 2020, the fund expected to fund 15 to 20 transactions a year and had already completed several before the public launch [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]]. Per project memory, CINC Capital is the operational predecessor to CINC's TresRE banking product, with the June 2020 launch establishing the load-bearing earliest primary-source data point on CINC's banking-product strategy and the operational extension of Bill Blanton's stated "closes the gap between banking and accounting" competitive-positioning frame [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].

## Key facts

- Sister company to CINC Systems, created by CINC Founder and Chairman Bill Blanton and publicly launched the week of June 10, 2020 [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Capitalized in part with a portion of the proceeds from Spectrum Equity's June 2020 growth investment in CINC Systems — surfacing CINC Capital as a structural use-of-proceeds artifact directly downstream of the Spectrum cap-table entry [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Stated mission at the June 2020 launch: provides capital and strategic counsel for community association management companies to acquire other small- to medium-sized community association management companies across the United States — positioning the fund as a customer-side acquisition-finance vehicle rather than a CINC-Systems-side PE vehicle [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Vendor-framed competitive moat at June 2020 launch: "a unique blend of community association and banking expertise" — surfacing the dual-domain framing (community association + banking) that mirrors CINC Systems' own "closes the gap between banking and accounting" competitive-positioning frame [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Transaction-volume target at June 2020 public unveiling: expected to fund 15 to 20 transactions per year [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Pre-launch operating history: had already completed "several" transactions before its public unveiling in June 2020 — surfacing CINC Capital as having an operating record predating its public launch, rather than being a greenfield announcement [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Founder framing of the launch (Bill Blanton, June 2020): publicly framed as "another mechanism to enable the growth of community association management companies seeking to expand" — operationalizing CINC Capital as a strategic extension of CINC Systems' customer-growth thesis rather than a standalone PE vehicle [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Per project memory, CINC Capital is the operational predecessor to CINC's TresRE banking product, surfacing the June 2020 launch as the load-bearing earliest primary-source anchor for the multi-year arc that yielded the $25B-payments-processed scale point in CINC's April 2026 Cephai+ framing [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
