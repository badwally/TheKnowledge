# Edward O. Thorp

## Summary

Mathematician and Professor of Mathematics at the University of California at Irvine; co-author with Sheen T. Kassouf of the 1967 book "Beat the Market: A Scientific Stock Market System," which presents a hedging-based "basic system" for trading warrants against their associated common stocks and reports a 25%/year five-year cash record alongside a 25%/year average over a seventeen-year (1946-1966) historical backtest [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].

## Key facts

- Identified on the title page of "Beat the Market" (1967) as Edward O. Thorp, Ph.D., Professor of Mathematics at the University of California at Irvine [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
- Co-authored "Beat the Market: A Scientific Stock Market System" (Random House, 1967) with Sheen T. Kassouf [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
- Listed as the author of two prior books on the title page: "Elementary Probability" and "Beat the Dealer" [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
- Chapter 2 of "Beat the Market" frames Thorp's contribution as the "Rediscovery of the system: Ed Thorp under a tree" — the book's narrative shorthand for the moment Thorp arrived independently at the warrant-stock relationships Kassouf had been developing [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
- The introduction reports that "we have used this method in the market for the past five years to earn 25% a year" and that the authors "have made profits during two of the sharpest stock market drops of this century," "when the stock market soared," and "in stationary and churning markets" [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
- The book attributes some of the underlying research to mathematical work supported in part by Air Force grant AF-AFOSR 1113-66 [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
- The introduction describes the method as making use of "mathematics, economics, and electronic computers" to prove and perfect the theory, and frames it as "the first scientifically proven method for consistent stock market profits" [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
