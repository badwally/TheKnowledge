# UpperBee Bonjour

## Summary

Bonjour is UpperBee's named smart visitor log product for building receptions, marketed as a digital register that keeps a real-time record of all guest, technician, and vendor entries at residential or commercial properties [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]]. UpperBee positions Bonjour's stated outcomes as enhanced security, improved tracking, and streamlined visitor management [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].

## Key facts

- Smart visitor-log product operated by UpperBee, distinct from the UpperBee core all-in-one accounting & property management platform and from the Track&Fix inspection product [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Target deployment surface: building receptions at residential or commercial properties [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Functional scope: real-time record of guest, technician, and vendor entries [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Stated value propositions: enhances security, improves tracking, streamlines visitor management [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Marketed as a "simple digital register," consistent with a lightweight, single-purpose front-desk tool rather than a full PMS module [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
