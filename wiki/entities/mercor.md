# Mercor

**Archetype:** AI-native marketplace (recruiting) / AI-native services firm

AI-powered talent marketplace. AI models conduct automated video interviews, assess skills, and autonomously match candidates to roles.

## Why it's in this domain

Engineered a data flywheel by choosing AI labs as a strategic initial wedge customer; the rapid feedback loop from demanding clients forced Mercor to automate vetting, ensuring every successful placement continuously improves the underlying matching algorithm. Cited as the canonical AI-native recruiting marketplace AND as a services-firm-economics exemplar.
