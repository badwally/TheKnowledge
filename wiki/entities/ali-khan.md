# Ali Khan

## Summary

Trader and former UK pharmacist; author of "The ICT Bible" (2023, Volume 1, Foundation Level), a 37-page e-book that systematizes the foundational ICT concepts originated by his mentor Michael J. Huddleston (ICT), covering IPDA, AMD, fair value gaps (BISI/SIBI), killzones, the 3-bar swing, SMT divergence, Power of 3, PD Arrays, order blocks, breaker blocks, mitigation blocks, the dealing range, and premium vs discount [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key facts

- Known by the handle AK and the social media handle @Ali_Khan_ICT [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Prior to becoming a full-time trader, spent a decade working as a Pharmacist in the UK [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Discovered ICT in early 2018 after being introduced by a friend who was already enrolled in Huddleston's private mentorship [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Describes himself as "ICT Chartered" on the title page of The ICT Bible [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- States he was "privately mentored by Michael" and has spent many years studying and using ICT concepts in personal trading [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Explicitly disclaims ownership of the concepts: "I am not claiming these concepts as my own" and notes that SMC (Smart Money Concepts) and ICT concepts are the same, from the same author Michael J. Huddleston [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Operates the website www.theinnercircledragons.com [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Created the e-book in response to "hundreds of messages" with recurring questions from newer traders [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Warns that "Trading is NOT easy and ICT concepts require time and experience to be used effectively" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
