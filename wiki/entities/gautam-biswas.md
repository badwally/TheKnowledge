# Gautam Biswas

## Summary

Gautam Biswas is the senior co-author of the 2025 DX conference paper introducing a data-driven particle filter approach for system-level Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction, and also serves as an editor of the DX 2025 proceedings volume in which the paper appears.

## Key facts

- Biswas is the fourth (final) listed author of "A Data-Driven Particle Filter Approach for System-Level Prediction of Remaining Useful Life," published 10 November 2025 in the DX 2025 proceedings (OASIcs vol. 136, pp. 11:1–11:13, DOI 10.4230/OASIcs.DX.2025.11) [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- Biswas is listed as an editor of OASIcs volume 136 — the 36th International Conference on Principles of Diagnosis and Resilient Systems (DX 2025) proceedings — alongside Marcos Quinones-Grueiro and Ingo Pill [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- The paper is co-authored with Abel Diaz-Gonzalez, Austin Coursey, and Marcos Quinones-Grueiro [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
