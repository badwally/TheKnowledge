# Guojun Yang

## Summary

Guojun Yang is the first-listed author of the 2024 *Advances in Structural Engineering* paper introducing a multivariate Bayesian dynamic linear method for modelling bridge performance degradation.

## Key facts

- Yang is the first listed author of "Bridge performance degradation model based on the multi-variate bayesian dynamic linear method," first published online on 23 July 2024 in *Advances in Structural Engineering* (DOI 10.1177/13694332241266541) [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
- The paper is co-authored with Li Tian, Jianbo Mao, Guangwu Tang, and Yongfeng Du [[sources/web-2024-10-20-950]].
