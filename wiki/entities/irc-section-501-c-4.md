# IRC Section 501(c)(4) (Social Welfare Organizations)

## Summary

IRC Section 501(c)(4) is the section of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code that provides federal tax exemption for social welfare organizations. A homeowners association may, in principle, qualify for exemption under this section; most associations do not qualify, but Gary Porter, CPA argues that as IRS restricts its interpretation of Rev. Rul. 70-604, the §501(c)(4) path should be reconsidered by many associations that previously assumed Rev. Rul. 70-604 would protect them from net-membership-income taxation [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]]. A §501(c)(4)-exempt HOA escapes tax on both interest income and net membership income, but remains subject to tax on unrelated business income (less related deductions) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].

## Key facts

- A section of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code providing federal income-tax exemption for social welfare organizations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Most homeowners associations will not qualify for exemption under this section [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- For an HOA that does qualify, two tax advantages apply: (1) interest income is not subject to taxation; (2) net membership income avoids taxation [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- A §501(c)(4)-exempt HOA may continue to operate with net operating profits from membership activities forever without being subject to tax on that net income [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- A §501(c)(4)-exempt HOA remains subject to the same tax on unrelated business income, less any related deductions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Historically, many HOAs felt no need to consider §501(c)(4) status because they assumed they could remain non-exempt membership organizations under IRC §277 with Rev. Rul. 70-604 protecting them from taxation on net membership income [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Gary Porter, CPA argues §501(c)(4) should be reconsidered by many associations now that the IRS interpretation of Rev. Rul. 70-604 is increasingly restrictive and the assumption of protection from net-membership-income taxation under the §277 / 70-604 regime is no longer reliable [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
