# Omar Khattab

## Summary

AI researcher at MIT CSAIL and co-author / corresponding author of the 2026 preprint "Recursive Language Models" with Alex L. Zhang and Tim Kraska; an earlier paper of his (Khattab et al., 2021) is cited as a foundational reference on context condensation and compaction [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with MIT CSAIL as co-author and corresponding author of "Recursive Language Models" (preprint, January 29, 2026) [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
- Listed correspondence address is okhattab@mit.edu, jointly with lead author Alex L. Zhang at altzhang@mit.edu [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
- Co-authored an earlier paper (Khattab et al., 2021) cited by the RLM authors as one of the seminal references on context condensation and compaction as a general inference-time approach for long context [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
