# ReserveWise

## Summary

ReserveWise is a DIY reserve-study SaaS with computer-vision component identification, identified in the condo-capital-infra synthesis as the cheapest modern-UX option but lacking a licensed reserve analyst in the loop — which the synthesis flags as risky for SIRS-jurisdiction work [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]]. REcollab Technologies (recollab.ai) joined the AI-positioned DIY/SaaS reserve-planning competitive cohort as of the 2026-05-18 vendor watch ingest, with a structurally similar AI-driven component-mapping ingest model applied at the document-extraction layer rather than at ReserveWise's site-walk image layer [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]]. As of the 2026-05-18 patent disambiguation ingest, USPTO patent US12462305B2 — circulated in some third-party framings as a REcollab patent — is instead assigned to Trete Inc and covers generic fintech / digital-securities asset-onboarding infrastructure, providing negative-evidence disambiguation against any claim that REcollab holds a reserve-study-methodology patent under that number [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].

## Key facts

- Self-service reserve-study SaaS with AI-vision for component identification and remaining-useful-life estimation [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Forecasting is deterministic [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- No licensed reserve analyst (RA) in the loop; the synthesis explicitly flags this as risky under Florida SIRS [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Recommended fit per the synthesis short list: small (<50 unit) self-managed condos that need to satisfy state reserve-study requirements at low cost [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- REcollab Technologies (recollab.ai), an AI-positioned reserve-planning vendor marketing to property managers across North America with an AI-driven "drop any file format" document-ingest model that extracts and maps data to building components, occupies the same DIY/SaaS-with-AI-component-identification competitive cohort as ReserveWise but applies the AI layer at document ingest rather than at site-walk imagery; like ReserveWise, REcollab discloses no licensed reserve analyst in the loop and no probabilistic methodology — placing both in the deterministic-DIY tier of the vendor watch set [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]] [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- Patent disambiguation (REcollab Technologies): US Patent 12462305B2 ("Data retrieval and validation for asset onboarding and deriving asset characteristics," USPTO, published 2025-07-03, active status) is assigned to Trete Inc — NOT REcollab Technologies — and covers generic fintech / digital-securities / asset-onboarding infrastructure for secondary-market platforms trading across real estate, planes, yachts, antique cars, art, jewelry, insurance policies, racehorse-backed structured products, franchises, natural-resource ventures, private credit, and intangible assets; the patent contains no reserve-study methodology, no probabilistic component-deterioration modeling, no condominium-specific content, and no engagement with any in-scope reserve-study regulatory regime (FL SIRS, CA Davis-Stirling, BC SPA, ON Condo Act 1998, NS Condominium Act, NB CPA c16.05, NL NLR 80/11, QC Civil Code Bill 16), making it negative-evidence disambiguation against any third-party claim that REcollab holds a reserve-study-methodology patent under this number [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].
- Implication for vendor-watch defensibility framing: deterministic-DIY-tier vendor patent claims in this competitive cohort require independent primary-source verification rather than reliance on third-party attribution to US12462305B2, which is unambiguously a Trete Inc fintech patent rather than a condo-reserve-study patent [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].
