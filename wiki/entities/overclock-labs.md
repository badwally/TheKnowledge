# Overclock Labs

## Summary

Company that authored the May 13, 2018 position paper "Akash Network: Decentralized Cloud Infrastructure Marketplace" (Version 0.0.3), which lays out the design of a decentralized cloud computing marketplace, the AKASH token economics, a peer-to-peer manifest-distribution and overlay-network protocol, and a roadmap to launch [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the author of "Akash Network: Decentralized Cloud Infrastructure Marketplace" (May 13, 2018, Version 0.0.3) [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Frames Akash as "an active research project" with new versions of the paper to be published at akash.network [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Public contact for comments and suggestions: research@akash.network [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Argues cloud computing is "inherently broken" — usable but limited by inefficiencies that advancements in container technology and a powerful token economy can solve [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Frames Akash as "the worlds first global spot market for cloud computing" in the abstract of the position paper [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
- Argues a future in which the global cloud infrastructure of the world is decentralized and distributed between all cloud service providers [[sources/pdf-c9b8f466ea39]].
