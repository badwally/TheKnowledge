# Real Estate 360

## Summary

Real Estate 360 is a Dartmouth, Nova Scotia-based property management firm that manages Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130 (Summer Gardens, 1470 Summer Street, Halifax) — a 21-storey, 118-suite, 1987-vintage downtown Halifax condominium — with Andrew Buchanan as the named board contact at the time of the June 11, 2025 Sense Engineering Reserve Fund Study [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]]. The firm sits in the HRM property-management channel relevant to the condo-capital-infra Halifax engagement (Brief-0004) under the ADR-0004 Canada-first GTM as a documented Halifax-area PM channel counterparty managing at least one large-scale legacy concrete-highrise corporation [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].

## Key facts

- Property management firm headquartered at 650-33 Alderney Drive, Dartmouth, NS B2Y 2N4 [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- Andrew Buchanan is the named board contact at the time of the June 11, 2025 Sense Engineering Reserve Fund Study; his specific role (property manager assigned to the account, principal, etc.) is not disclosed in the source [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- Manages Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130 (Summer Gardens, 1470 Summer Street, Halifax) — a 21-storey, 118-suite, 1987-vintage downtown Halifax concrete-highrise condominium with single-level underground parking garage and indoor pool / fitness / sauna amenities [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- The source does not disclose Real Estate 360's broader managed-corporation portfolio, principals, founding year, or affiliations with larger CAM franchises [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
