# SRAM Hydraulic Road Disc (HRD)

## Summary

SRAM's Hydraulic Road Disc (HRD) is a road-bike [[concepts/hydraulic-disc-brake|hydraulic disc brake]] system from [[entities/sram|SRAM]], serviced via a published procedure for hose shortening and [[concepts/brake-bleed|bleeding]] documented in SRAM manual 95-5018-024-000 Rev H (2023) [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].

## Key facts

- Identified by the abbreviation "HRD" in SRAM service documentation [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Documented by SRAM service manual 95-5018-024-000, currently at revision H, dated 2023 [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Service procedures cover shortening the brake hose, bleeding the system, resetting disc-brake pad gap, resetting pad retraction (Monoblock caliper only), and bedding in pads [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Uses [[concepts/stealth-a-majig|Stealth-a-majig]] hose-connection hardware (hose barb plus compression-nut fittings at the lever and caliper) [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Some HRD calipers use [[concepts/bleeding-edge|Bleeding Edge]] bleed ports while others do not, leading to two distinct bleed procedures in the manual [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Some HRD calipers are [[concepts/monoblock-caliper|Monoblock]] (one-piece) — a distinction that determines whether pad-retraction reset applies [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
- Service requires specialised tools and supplies — including 2.5/4/5 mm hex keys, T8/T10 Torx, brake fluid, grease, and bleed syringes — and SRAM recommends installation by a qualified bicycle mechanic [[sources/pdf-b51938faf250]].
