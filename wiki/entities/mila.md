# Mila — Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute

## Summary

Mila is the Montréal-based national AI institute and one of three CIFAR partner institutes delivering the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy alongside Amii and the Vector Institute [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].

## Key facts

- Mila is named as one of Canada's three national AI institutes (with Amii in Edmonton and the Vector Institute in Toronto) under the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- CIFAR led PCAIS Phase 1 in partnership with Amii, Mila and the Vector Institute beginning in 2017 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Under PCAIS Phase 2 (Budget 2021), Mila shares in a C$60M allocation across the three national AI institutes to translate AI research into commercial applications and grow business capacity to adopt those technologies [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- As of the June 2022 Phase 2 announcement, the national AI institutes (including Mila) had collectively trained over 1,500 graduate students and post-doctoral fellows since 2017 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
