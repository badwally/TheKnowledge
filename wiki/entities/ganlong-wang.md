# Ganlong Wang

## Summary

Ganlong Wang is a co-author of the 2025 paper proposing an optimal predictive maintenance policy for nonstationary gamma degradation processes.

## Key facts

- Wang is the second listed author of 'Optimal predictive maintenance for a nonstationary gamma process,' published on 7 April 2025 with DOI 10.17531/ein/203543 [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The paper is co-authored with Yue Wang, Yanxia Wu, Guoyin Zhang, and Rui Zheng [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
