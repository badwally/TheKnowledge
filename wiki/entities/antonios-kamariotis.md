# Antonios Kamariotis

## Summary

Antonios Kamariotis is the first-listed author of the 2022/2023 paper reviewing and comparing off-line and on-line Bayesian filters for uncertainty quantification of structural deterioration model parameters.

## Key facts

- Kamariotis is the first listed author of 'On off-line and on-line Bayesian filtering for uncertainty quantification of structural deterioration' [[sources/arxiv-2205.03478]].
- The paper was posted as arXiv 2205.03478 on 6 May 2022 and published in *Data-Centric Engineering*, volume 4, 2023, article e17 (DOI 10.1017/dce.2023.13) [[sources/arxiv-2205.03478]].
- The paper is co-authored with Luca Sardi, Iason Papaioannou, Eleni Chatzi, and Daniel Straub [[sources/arxiv-2205.03478]].
