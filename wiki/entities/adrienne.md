# Adrienne

## Summary

Adrienne is the Chief Marketing Officer at Orita, a seed-stage AI customer segmentation startup [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]]. In a 2026-05-27 strategic planning session with advisors, Adrienne is the named owner of the marketing automation buildout captured in Orita's internal CLAUDE.md — the project that pairs a single marketing executive with AI agents to operate the full marketing function with limited engineering demand [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].

## Key facts

- Adrienne's stated working-relationship norm requires critical evaluation of any proposed framework or architecture: surface structural problems before building on them, propose alternative structures rather than just objections, and seek consultation before committing to a direction [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
- The agent decomposition for Orita's marketing function was corrected during Adrienne's planning sessions from a User→Function→Agent→Features hierarchy to a two-tier workflow-agent + resource-agent model on the rationale that the original conflated organizational and architectural axes [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
- Adrienne's near-term deliverables include the CMO proposal (orita_marketing_automation_proposal.md), competitive analyses of Clay, Relevance AI, and HubSpot Breeze, and Phase 1 implementation of HubSpot MCP, Apollo API, and Google Sheets sync [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
