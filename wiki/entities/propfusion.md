# PropFusion

## Summary

PropFusion is a U.S. reserve-study and association-management SaaS vendor whose product is positioned as an all-in-one platform for Board Members, Association Managers, and Reserve Professionals, centralizing financial planning, compliance tracking, and asset management in support of multi-association reserve-study management [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]. It is one of the four named CI-tracked reserve-study software vendors in the condo-capital-infra synthesis (alongside SmartProperty, Solume, and ReserveWise) [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]]. PropFusion publishes a multi-state law-guide jurisdiction library covering FL/CA/TX/GA/MI/MD/NJ with no Canadian-jurisdiction coverage, framing its competitive perimeter as U.S.-only relative to the condo-capital-infra Canada-first GTM sequence under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]]. SaaSWorthy's third-party aggregation page (dated 2026-05-18, with pricing last refreshed 11/12/2024) provides independent corroboration of PropFusion's pricing tiers and documents the explicit absence of a public API as of the data refresh — meaning no CMMS or accounting integrations are available through a vendor-supported API surface [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].

## Key facts

### Corporate identity

- Vendor location of record: Dover, Delaware (per SaaSWorthy vendor-details surface as of the 11/12/2024 refresh) [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].
- Self-positioned as an all-in-one platform for reserve study management, designed to centralize tools used by Board Members, Association Managers, and Reserve Professionals — replacing spreadsheets and paperwork with a single dashboard for financial planning, compliance tracking, and asset management [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]. [[sources/web-2026-01-01-47b]]
- Product-line stack covers reserve study tools, multi-association management, client-portfolio scaling for reserve professionals, comprehensive report generation, dashboard-based collaboration, and assistance with planning future expenses and avoiding special assessments [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].

### Pricing tiers (per SaaSWorthy refresh 11/12/2024)

- Six pricing tiers segmented by user-role audience [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]:
  - Basic (Association Board Members): Free [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]
  - Advanced (Association Board Members): $29.00/month [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]
  - Pro (Association Managers): Free [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]
  - LITE (Reserve Professionals): Free [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]
  - PLUS (Reserve Professionals): $85.00/month [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]
  - PRO (Reserve Professionals): $395.00/month [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]]
- Free trial: not available per SaaSWorthy's structured-pricing schema [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].
- Pricing data last refreshed by SaaSWorthy on 11/12/2024 from the vendor website; SaaSWorthy disclaims that actual pricing may differ and directs prospective buyers to confirm with the vendor website before purchasing [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].

### Integration surface (no public API)

- SaaSWorthy explicitly documents "No, PropFusion does not provide API" as of the 11/12/2024 refresh — third-party independent corroboration that PropFusion does not expose a public API for integration with external systems [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].
- Implication: PropFusion's integration posture as of late 2025 is closed — no CMMS, accounting, or work-order-system integrations are documented as available through a vendor-supported API surface, materially differentiating PropFusion from API-first reserve-study and CAM platforms [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].

### Regulatory-interpretation content channel (FL SIRS)

- PropFusion publishes a vendor-curated April 2026 Florida Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) 2026 guide as part of its multi-state law-guide jurisdiction library, articulating HB 913 qualified-preparer categories, board-affidavit and unit-owner disclosure cascade, conflict-of-interest disclosure rules for SIRS preparers, and DBPR reporting registry mechanics [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]].
- PropFusion's law-guide jurisdiction library covers FL, CA, TX, GA, MI, MD, and NJ as of April 2026, with no Canadian-jurisdiction coverage — framing PropFusion's competitive perimeter as U.S.-multi-state and surfacing zero direct overlap with the ADR-0004 Canada-first GTM sequence (NS → Atlantic Canada → Ontario → US Y2) until the Year-2 FL channel-partnership ramp [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]].
- The April 2026 PropFusion guide reports wait times for qualified SIRS providers in Florida of three to six months in some markets — a supply-side capacity-constraint data point that flows downstream from the HB 913 reporting mandate [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]].

### Methodology framing (per condo-capital-infra synthesis)

- Identified in the engagement synthesis as one of four CI-tracked named reserve-study software vendors alongside SmartProperty, Solume, and ReserveWise [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].
- The synthesis frames all four vendors as operating on deterministic-methodology baselines consistent with the CAI Reserve Study Standards (2023 and 2025 editions), with no vendor in this competitive set publishing a probabilistic reserve engine — establishing the methodology gap that the condo-capital-infra engine v0.5 targets as its differentiation moat [[sources/docx-bf4965d0d33a]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- The Dover, Delaware vendor location combined with the U.S.-only multi-state law-guide footprint and the absence of a public API surface frames PropFusion as a U.S.-centric, closed-integration reserve-study vendor — a competitive posture that reduces overlap risk with the ADR-0004 Canada-first GTM sequence in Years 0-1 (NS and Atlantic Canada) and surfaces PropFusion primarily as a Year-2 FL-ramp comparator rather than a near-term competitive threat [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]] [[sources/web-2026-04-27-33b]].
- The $85/mo PLUS and $395/mo PRO pricing tiers for Reserve Professionals — combined with three Free tiers (Board Members Basic, Association Managers Pro, Reserve Professionals LITE) — establish a freemium-to-low-end-paid SaaS price-anchor for the U.S. DIY reserve-study segment, materially below the engagement-scale pricing typical of services-led reserve-study delivery [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]].
- The documented no-API posture is the load-bearing competitive-intelligence finding: it confirms that PropFusion's reserve-study workflow is bounded by its own platform rather than designed to plug into the CAM-platform ecosystem (CINC Systems, Yardi Voyager, FirstService Residential-managed properties), surfacing API-first integration as a meaningful differentiation lever for an AI-native reserve-study firm targeting acquirers in that ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-05-18-f80]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]].
