# Paygami

## Summary

Paygami is the CINC Systems digital-and-check payment platform launched in 2018, designed to make CINC's payment platform and online banking platform available to real estate management companies regardless of whether they used CINC's accounting software [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]]. Paygami is the direct predecessor product to TresRE; the June 2023 TresRE launch is positioned as expanding Paygami by adding the integration middleware that connects CINC's payments and banking platform to third-party accounting software [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

- Launched by CINC Systems in 2018 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Provided real estate management companies access to CINC's digital and check payment platform [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Provided access to CINC's online banking platform [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Made CINC's payments and banking infrastructure available regardless of whether the management company used CINC's accounting software [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Functions as the foundation that TresRE expanded by adding integration middleware to additional accounting platforms in June 2023 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- The Paygami-era CINC payments platform processed over $6 billion in payments volume in the twelve months preceding the June 2023 TresRE launch and included 14 lockbox locations with a real-estate-focused process matching payments to residents at a 99.74% rate [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
