# Vantaca Revenue Manager

## Summary

Vantaca Revenue Manager is the revenue-capture product within the Vantaca community association management platform, identified on the Vantaca features page as the named offering under the "Revenue Capture" feature category [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]]. The product is positioned by vendor marketing as an alternative to spreadsheet-based billing for CAM operators, promising accurate and automated billing for every service the management company provides [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key facts

- Named product within the Vantaca platform; identified on the Vantaca features page as the offering under the "Revenue Capture" feature category [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Marketed as a replacement for spreadsheet-based billing workflows, with the value proposition of getting paid "accurately and automatically" for every service the CAM operator provides [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Sits alongside five other top-level feature categories on Vantaca's product surface: Full Accounting Suite, Business Intelligence, Integrated Payments (Vantaca Pay), Resident Experience, and Partner Integrations [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The features-page copy does not describe the underlying billing-engine architecture, service-catalog model, or pricing-rule mechanism [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
