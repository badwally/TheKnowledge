# Ken French

## Summary

Finance researcher and longtime collaborator with Eugene Fama; maintainer of the Ken French data library used by Cliff Asness in "The Less-Efficient Market Hypothesis" to compute price-to-book and price-to-cashflow value spreads going back to 1950 [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].

## Key facts

- Maintains the Ken French data library, which Asness uses to compute the price-to-book value spread (using French's 3×2 sort on price-to-book × market equity) for the expensive 30% of stocks divided by the cheap 30% of stocks among large caps [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].
- The same exercise using price-to-cashflow from the same website produces a graph with essentially the same pattern — though in that case the dot-com bubble peak just edges out the COVID peak [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].
- Co-author of Fama and French (2005), cited by Asness as formalizing how investor non-risk-return tastes affect prices and expected returns [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].
