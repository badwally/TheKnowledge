# SiteCompli

## Summary

SiteCompli is a regulatory-compliance product within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio focused on helping property teams understand and resolve compliance violations across their portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- SiteCompli operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- At OpTech 2025 (Nov 17, 2025), SiteCompli announced Metro AI, an AI compliance assistant focused on responding to New York City regulatory compliance questions [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Metro AI is positioned as Hybrid Intelligence — AI with human compliance-expert judgment and oversight "to keep the technology on the rails" [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Metro AI's stated scope is NYC regulatory compliance; no condo/HOA-specific or non-NYC jurisdictions are referenced in the Nov 2025 announcement [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
