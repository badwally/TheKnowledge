# Degradation Model Correction of Miter Gates Through Synthesis of Hybrid Modeling with Recursive Bayesian State Estimation

## Summary

A 2025 conference paper by Zhao Zhao, Yichao Zeng, Michael D. Todd, and Zhen Hu, appearing as Article 5 in *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3* — the proceedings of the 43rd IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics. The paper addresses degradation model correction for miter gates through a synthesis of hybrid modeling with recursive Bayesian state estimation.
