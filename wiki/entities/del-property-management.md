# Del Property Management Inc.

## Summary

Del Property Management Inc. is an Ontario condominium management firm whose practitioner views were cited by ACMO in framing the workload that a single general licensee can handle [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key facts

- Catherine Murdock, district manager for Del Property Management Inc., stated that a solitary manager of a stand-alone building can comfortably manage about 270 high-rise units, preparing monthly meetings with one board, one annual budget meeting and one AGM [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
