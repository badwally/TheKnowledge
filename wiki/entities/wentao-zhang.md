# Wentao Zhang

## Summary

AI researcher at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, and sole listed author of the 2026 paper "Autogenesis: A Self-Evolving Agent Protocol," which introduces a two-layer protocol architecture (AGP) for governing self-evolution in LLM-based agent systems [[sources/pdf-wentao-zhang-2026-autogenesis-a-self]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, on the paper, with correspondence email zhangwent963@gmail.com [[sources/pdf-wentao-zhang-2026-autogenesis-a-self]].
- Listed as the sole author of "Autogenesis: A Self-Evolving Agent Protocol" (2026) [[sources/pdf-wentao-zhang-2026-autogenesis-a-self]].
- The paper introduces AGP, which decouples "what evolves" (the substrate of resources) from "how evolution occurs" (the operator interface), and AGS, a self-evolving multi-agent system built on AGP and evaluated on GPQA, AIME, GAIA, and LeetCode [[sources/pdf-wentao-zhang-2026-autogenesis-a-self]].
