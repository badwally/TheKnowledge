# Association of Condominium Managers of Ontario (ACMO)

## Summary

The Association of Condominium Managers of Ontario (ACMO) is the Ontario industry association representing condominium managers and management firms, which publishes statements on industry conditions and advocates for regulatory and workforce reform [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key facts

- ACMO issued a May 29, 2023 statement warning of a severe shortfall of licensed condo managers in Ontario, citing the combined pressure of the condo construction boom and the looming retirement of existing managers [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- An ACMO membership survey found 57% of responding managers were aged 55 or higher, 13% were aged 65 and nearing retirement, and only 5% were under 34 [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- ACMO recommends government action, including working with the CMRAO to recruit, educate and license more managers; promoting the career path through Ontario colleges and government retraining programs such as the Ontario Bridge Training Program; and keeping licensing education and licensing fees as low as possible [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- ACMO further recommends generating public awareness of the manager shortfall jointly with the Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI) and the Community Associations Institute (CAI) [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- ACMO frames the unprecedented demand for managers as an opportunity for a recession-proof career path with growth potential, while pressing for reduced barriers to entry [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
