# Retail Payment Activities Act (Canada)

## Summary

The Retail Payment Activities Act is the Canadian federal statute under which the Bank of Canada supervises registered payment service providers [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]]. Its pre-existing supervisory architecture is the structural template that the consumer-driven banking framework's governance design "leverages" — i.e., reuses the same Bank of Canada supervisory role to administer the Consumer-Driven Banking Act [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].

## Key facts

- The Bank of Canada's supervisory role under the Retail Payment Activities Act covers registered payment service providers [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].
- The Government cited alignment with this existing oversight as a reason to delegate consumer-driven banking implementation and oversight to the Bank of Canada [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].
- Registered payment service providers are among the entity classes eligible to opt-in to the consumer-driven banking framework [[sources/web-2025-11-06-ff1]].
