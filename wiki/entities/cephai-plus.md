# Cephai+

## Summary

Cephai+ is CINC Systems' AI-native automation suite for community association management (CAM), publicly framed by CINC CEO Ryan Davis as "a direct answer to the administrative and financial burden that has long constrained our industry" and positioned by the company as the load-bearing AI product line under continued Hg Capital + Spectrum Equity dual-sponsor governance [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]]. As of the August 5, 2025 PRNewswire announcement, 100+ management companies had signed up for Cephai+ since its recent launch, early adopters had processed more than 31,000 invoices using intelligent agents, and two additional agents were slated to launch in Q3 2025 with their unveiling planned for CINC's user conference in Austin, Texas, September 3-5, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]]. Cephai+ is the operational substrate of the CINC competitive perimeter against an AI-native reserve-study tuck-under acquisition target under the condo-capital-infra Year-3 exit thesis (ADR-0004), framing CINC's Cephai+ ecosystem as the operative integration target for any AI-native reserve-study product positioned for CINC acquisition [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

## Key facts

### Adoption scale (August 5, 2025)

- 100+ management companies signed up for Cephai+ since launch — the load-bearing primary-source adoption-scale metric as of August 5, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Early adopters have processed more than 31,000 invoices using Cephai+ intelligent agents [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Dedicated Client Growth Managers are now onboarding clients nationwide [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Framed in the August 2025 press release as "a new era of intelligent automation in a $38 billion industry long overdue for transformation" — surfacing the $38B CAM-industry TAM as the marketing-frame anchor for Cephai+ positioning [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

### Intelligent agent capability stack (per August 2025 announcement)

- **Invoice ingest agent**: ingests, populates, and codes invoices with AI-powered accuracy — the primary agent driving the 31,000+ invoice early-adopter metric [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- **EFT-matching agent**: automatically matches recurring EFT transactions (e.g., utilities) to bank records [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- **Board-packet agent**: generates board packets with executive summaries and agendas — the customer-validation anchor in RISE Management Group's framing ("managers reclaim nearly a full week of work every month") [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- **Budget agent**: builds budgets using real-time and historical data, with auto-generated line-item explanations [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- **Violation-detection agent**: detects violations from uploaded photos, auto-generates letters, and initiates workflows [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Two additional agents slated to launch Q3 2025, with announcements planned at CINC's user conference September 3-5, 2025 in Austin, Texas — establishing the CINC user conference as the operative product-roadmap-unveiling cadence for Cephai+ [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

### Customer validation (RISE Management Group)

- RISE Management Group is named as an early Cephai+ adopter, with Jason Delgado (Managing Partner at RISE per the August 2025 framing) surfaced as the named customer-reference voice [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Delgado's stated pre-Cephai+ baseline: "Before Cephai+, our managers spent hours compiling board packets — downloading reports, formatting them, and adding commentary" [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Delgado's stated post-Cephai+ outcome: "Now, our board packets are generated automatically, complete with AI-written executive summaries and agendas. We've seen our managers reclaim nearly a full week of work every month" — a load-bearing primary-source customer-productivity metric (one-week-per-month reclaimed) for the board-packet agent [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Delgado's framing positions Cephai+ as "more than automation — it's empowerment" enabling managers to focus on "boards, residents, and the health of our business" [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

### Executive framing

- Ryan Davis, CEO of CINC Systems, frames Cephai+ rationale as: "Cephai+ is a direct answer to the administrative and financial burden that has long constrained our industry. We didn't build AI for the sake of innovation. We built it because our managers and management companies deserve to thrive. We can shed their tedious tasks and allow them to spend time on what's most meaningful" [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Ashley Berenson, Chief Product Officer at CINC Systems, frames the agent impact as: "These agents are already changing how work gets done. We're not just improving productivity — we're revolutionizing the manager's role and reinforcing the value of professional management" [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Farouk Hussein, CINC Systems Board Member and Partner at Hg Capital, frames investor-side validation: "Hg has partnered with dozens of AI-enabled companies over the past two years. The customer demand and product-market fit for CINC's Cephai+ are among the strongest we've seen. Cephai+ isn't just automating tasks — it's transforming businesses. And we believe this is yet another extraordinary value creation and growth vector for CINC and its clients" — establishing Hg's stated cross-portfolio comparative framing of Cephai+ product-market fit [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- The Cephai+ adoption trajectory (100+ management companies, 31,000+ invoices processed, two additional Q3 2025 agents queued) confirms CINC is actively expanding its AI-agent product perimeter under continued Hg + Spectrum sponsorship — the operative product-line backdrop against which any AI-native reserve-study tuck-under acquisition would be evaluated as a Cephai+ adjacent-agent extension [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Hussein's "yet another extraordinary value creation and growth vector" framing signals Hg's appetite for additional Cephai+-style adjacent-capability investments at CINC, strengthening the strategic case for AI-native reserve-study acquisitions through the project's Year-3 exit window [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- The absence of a reserve-study or capital-planning agent in the announced Cephai+ agent set (invoice ingest, EFT matching, board packet, budget, violation detection) surfaces capital-planning as unfilled adjacent-agent territory within the Cephai+ roadmap — a load-bearing white-space data point for the condo-capital-infra exit thesis [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
