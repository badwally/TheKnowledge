# Peter Belcak

## Summary

AI researcher at NVIDIA Research and lead author of the 2025 position paper "Small Language Models are the Future of Agentic AI," which argues that small language models are sufficiently powerful, inherently more suitable, and more economical than LLMs for the majority of language-model invocations in agentic systems [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with NVIDIA Research as lead author of "Small Language Models are the Future of Agentic AI" (preprint, under review, September 2025) [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Listed as first author with co-authors Greg Heinrich, Shizhe Diao, Yonggan Fu, Xin Dong, Saurav Muralidharan, Yingyan Celine Lin (NVIDIA Research; Georgia Institute of Technology), and Pavlo Molchanov [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Frames the paper as a value statement — that SLMs are the future of agentic AI as a necessary consequence of the differences between SLMs and LLMs when natural priorities are followed — rather than a recommendation or obligation [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Group correspondence address for the paper is agents-research@nvidia.com, with ongoing critique and contributions to be published at research.nvidia.com/labs/lpr/slm-agents [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
