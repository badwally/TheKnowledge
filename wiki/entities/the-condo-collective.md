# The Condo Collective

## Summary

The Condo Collective is a condo management firm and a published UpperBee customer; firm spokesperson Mark Blanchette positions UpperBee as "the only software that fully supports our mission" of delivering modern, technology-enabled condo management [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Condo management firm and published UpperBee customer [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Named spokesperson in UpperBee's published testimonial set: Mark Blanchette [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Self-positioning: leverages "the most modern tools and processes to deliver efficient, high-quality condo management" [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Blanchette characterizes UpperBee as comprehensive, intuitive, and built for the realities of today's management firms, with marketing emphasis on transparency for boards, seamless access for owners, and smart-automation time savings for managers [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Strong vendor-loyalty positioning: "the only software that fully supports our mission" [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
