# Consumer Privacy Protection Act (Canada)

## Summary

The Consumer Privacy Protection Act was a proposed Canadian federal statute bundled as the first act in Bill C-27, the Digital Charter Implementation Act, intended (together with the Personal Information and Data Protection Tribunal Act) to modernize Canada's consumer data protection and privacy laws [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]]. It died alongside the rest of Bill C-27 after Parliament was prorogued [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- Was the first of three acts in Bill C-27 (Digital Charter Implementation Act), tabled in June 2022 [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Paired with the Personal Information and Data Protection Tribunal Act to modernize Canada's consumer data-protection and privacy regime [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Failed to become law when Bill C-27 died in the INDU committee after the prorogation of Parliament [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
