# Ronnie Sadka

## Summary

Senior associate dean for faculty, chairperson, and Haub Family Professor of Finance at Boston College's Carroll School of Management, and founder and managing partner of MKT MediaStats; corresponding author of the 2022 paper "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Senior associate dean for faculty, chairperson and professor of finance, and Haub Family Professor at Boston College's Carroll School of Management on the paper [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Also listed as founder and managing partner of MKT MediaStats [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Designated corresponding author of "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" (July 15, 2022) [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Boston College address given as 140 Commonwealth Avenue, Chestnut Hill, MA [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
