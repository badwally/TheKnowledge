# Alex (TraderEdge)

## Summary

Founder and CEO of TraderEdge; author of the 2023 marketing email "I'm profitable, now what?" that frames trading as a business and an "infinity game" while interleaving sleep-optimization advice and a $199 promotional bundle of TraderEdge's QuantumEdge, NexusEdge, and DeltaEdge indicators [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key facts

- Signs off the email as "Alex, Founder & CEO, TraderEdge" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Argues that knowing what to do once a trader actually makes money is "insanely important and doesn't get talked about AT ALL" by trading gurus [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Argues trading is strictly a business — with profitable days, losing days, and even losing months — and that "what matters is what's left over at the end of the quarter and the end of the year" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- States as the email's single most important takeaway: "trading is an infinity game, not a finite game" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Strongly recommends Morgan Housel's "The Psychology of Money" as foundational reading for traders building a personal-finance mindset on top of consistent profits [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Personal-finance beliefs include: pay yourself 50% of profits at the end of each month; save 6–12 months of expenses (vs the standard 3–6) because trading is "one of the riskiest jobs in the world (financially speaking)"; apply the 80/20 rule to focus on the 20% of categories — house, car, food, insurance — that drive 80% of monthly expenses [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Wore a Whoop Band sleep tracker for one month to optimize sleep, and reports that "alcohol is the worst thing in the world for sleep quality" — "if you wanna do one thing in life to improve your health just get rid of alcohol. It's useless and doesn't serve you at all" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Frames sleep as more important than "any supplement, workout, pill, or drug" for both trading performance and quality of life, and prescribes five science-backed habits: a consistent sleep schedule, a dark/quiet/cool bedroom, eliminating alcohol, limiting screens for at least an hour before bed, and mindful relaxation [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
