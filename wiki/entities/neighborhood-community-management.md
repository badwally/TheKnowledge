# Neighborhood Community Management

## Summary

Neighborhood Community Management is a community association management (CAM) company named as a Payables+ reference customer in CINC Systems' February 26, 2026 official launch announcement of the Payables+ AP automation solution, with Bryan Chan providing the customer testimonial framing the end-to-end-workflow benefits of the CINC-AvidXchange integration [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]]. Neighborhood Community Management is the load-bearing named CAM-customer reference for CINC's Feb 2026 Payables+ launch, surfacing it as evidence of pre-GA Payables+ adoption within the CINC client base and operationalizing the manual-to-automated AP workflow transition as the Payables+ value proposition for the broader CAM segment [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].

## Key facts

- Community association management (CAM) company referenced in CINC's February 26, 2026 official Payables+ launch announcement [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Bryan Chan is identified as the named user representing Neighborhood Community Management in the Payables+ customer testimonial [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Pre-GA Payables+ adopter: the company's documented usage of Payables+ predates the February 26, 2026 official launch, surfacing Neighborhood Community Management as a CINC customer that operated on Payables+ during the period between the October 2025 initial Payables+ launch and the February 2026 general-availability announcement [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Operational practice prior to Payables+ adoption per Chan's customer framing: manual invoice review and coding followed by paper check disbursement to vendors — superseded under Payables+ by AI-assisted invoice coding verification and one-click electronic payment to vendors, framing the structural workflow shift Payables+ enables for CAM operators of similar scale and complexity [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Operates as a CINC software-platform customer with Payables+ + AvidXchange integration enabled — establishing the company within the CINC client base whose AP workflow is the operational unit on which Payables+'s revenue-sharing economics are computed [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
