# JournalEngine (CommunityPay)

## Summary

JournalEngine is the named accounting engine inside the CommunityPay HOA accounting and governance platform [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]]. It is the shared substrate across CommunityPay's multiple product surfaces — every specialized surface (for boards, professional managers, attorneys, escrow officers, title officers, CDFIs, and lenders) runs against the same JournalEngine, enforcement guards, and bank reconciliation under a single subscription [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]]. JournalEngine is positioned by the vendor as the substrate that makes the platform a "system of record" rather than a workflow tool: every transaction is recorded directly to the ledger, with payments, fund allocations, and supporting records permanently linked [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]].

## Key facts

- Named component of the CommunityPay platform [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]].
- Shared accounting engine across all CommunityPay product surfaces; vendor positions JournalEngine, enforcement guards, and bank reconciliation as the three shared infrastructure layers across specialized surfaces under one subscription [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]].
- Every transaction is recorded directly to the JournalEngine ledger — dues, fees, rents, income, expenses, and fund allocations are all posted to the same source of truth [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]].
- Payments and records are permanently linked within the engine, framed by the vendor as preserving auditability and financial control [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]].
- Statements, reports, and board packets are generated from the JournalEngine ledger as the same source of truth, in the vendor's framing [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]].
- Operates in conjunction with enforcement guards: every transaction is evaluated by enforcement guards before it posts to the JournalEngine ledger [[sources/web-2026-01-01-fea]].
