# Elisa Dominguez Sotelino

## Summary

Elisa Dominguez Sotelino is a Ph.D. Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at PUC-Rio, and the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2025 ITcon paper proposing an integrated BIM/SHM/ANN framework for viaduct management.

## Key facts

- Dominguez Sotelino is the second (and final) listed author of 'Infrastructure management via BIM model: integration of structural health monitoring and ANN-based damage assessment,' published in *Journal of Information Technology in Construction* (ITcon), Vol. 30, pp. 1637-1658, October 2025 (DOI 10.36680/j.itcon.2025.067) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- She holds a Ph.D. and is a Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at PUC-Rio [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Her ORCID identifier is 0000-0002-2863-7498 [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Her institutional email address is sotelino@puc-rio.br [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The paper is co-authored with José Guilherme Porto Oliveira [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
