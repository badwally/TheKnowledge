# Nicolas Marx Hermoso

## Summary

Nicolas Marx Hermoso is a co-author of the 2026 paper proposing a Bayesian hierarchical Accelerated Failure Time model for wind turbine gearbox reliability.

## Key facts

- Hermoso is the third listed author of 'Bayesian Survival Models Reveal Wind-Driven Reliability Patterns in Turbines,' published on 1 May 2026 with DOI 10.1088/1742-6596/3232/1/012018 [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The paper is co-authored with Clément Jacquet, Liliana Haus, and Rajasekhar Pulikollu [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
