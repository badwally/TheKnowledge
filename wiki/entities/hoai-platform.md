# HOAi

## Summary

HOAi is a pioneering provider of AI technology for the HOA and community management industry, offering tools that streamline financial analysis, automate document management, and enhance communication efficiency [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. HOAi was acquired by Vantaca, the leading CAM software provider, in an acquisition announced at Vantaca Vision on November 19, 2024 and publicized via PRNewswire on November 20, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. Under the acquisition structure, HOAi continues to operate as an independent product with a dedicated focus on AI innovation, available to Vantaca customers via seamless platform integration and to other association management companies independently of the Vantaca platform [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. Vantaca positions HOAi as the "Transformational AI" tier of its two-tiered AI approach, complementary to Scout, the "Everyday AI" research assistant integrated within the Vantaca platform [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

## Key facts

### Product positioning

- Pioneering provider of AI technology for the HOA and community management industry [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Self-described as an industry leader in AI innovation, committed to transforming the way communities are managed through state-of-the-art technology and continuous advancements [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Positioned as a standalone "Transformational AI" solution within Vantaca's two-tiered AI approach following the November 2024 acquisition [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Public-facing website: myhoai.com [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

### Stated capabilities

- Streamlines financial analysis [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Automates document management [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Enhances communication efficiency [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Stated purpose: empower community managers and boards by reducing friction in daily operations and enabling them to focus on building thriving communities [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

### Vantaca acquisition (November 19/20, 2024)

- Acquired by Vantaca, announced at the Vantaca Vision annual customer conference on November 19, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Publicized via PRNewswire on November 20, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Continues to operate as an independent product post-acquisition [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Independent-product structure intended to allow HOAi to maintain maximum speed and flexibility in development [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Available to Vantaca customers via seamless integration with the Vantaca platform [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Available to other association management companies independently of the Vantaca platform [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Co-founder Haoyu Zha articulated the post-acquisition mission as "redefining community management through AI" and accelerating innovation in partnership with Vantaca [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
