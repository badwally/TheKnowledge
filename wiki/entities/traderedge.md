# TraderEdge

## Summary

Trading-education brand, headed by Founder & CEO Alex, that publishes a marketing email mixing trader-mindset commentary with promotional offers; the 2023 email "I'm profitable, now what?" answers a Reddit question about handling consistent trading profits with a framework that combines Simon Sinek's "Infinite Game," personal-finance heuristics drawn from Morgan Housel's "The Psychology of Money," and sleep-optimization stats — interleaved with an upsell of three proprietary indicators (QuantumEdge, NexusEdge, DeltaEdge) and a paid signal group [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

## Key facts

- Authored the 2023 email "I'm profitable, now what?" framing trading as a business and an "infinity game" rather than a finite game, and answering a Reddit question about how to use trading profits when the income cannot be applied to mortgage DTI [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Sells three proprietary trading indicators — QuantumEdge, NexusEdge, and DeltaEdge — as a bundle, normally priced at $699, discounted to $199, with a 7-Day 100% Money Back Guarantee [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Frames its indicator pitch around the claim that "the only way to truly build a system that beats everyone else is to use indicators that the average trader isn't using" — "How can you expect to be profitable if you're using the SAME thing everyone else is!" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- Operates a paid Exclusive Signal Group, marketed inline with a screenshot of a $277.26 single-trade profit [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
- The marketing email is signed off by "Alex, Founder & CEO, TraderEdge" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
