# MaintainX

## Summary

MaintainX is a San Francisco-headquartered maintenance and asset management software vendor that operates an AI-powered CMMS (computerized maintenance management system) and EAM (enterprise asset management) platform serving manufacturing, facilities management, food and beverage, and distribution-center customers [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. The company was founded in 2018 and is led by CEO and Co-Founder Chris Turlica [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. On July 9, 2025 the company announced a $150 million Series D round led by Bessemer Venture Partners, bringing total capital raised to $254 million at a $2.5 billion valuation [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. As of the funding announcement, MaintainX serves over 11,000 companies worldwide and manages 11 million-plus assets, processing 27 million-plus work orders and 370,000-plus safety procedures annually [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

- Serves over 11,000 companies worldwide as of the November 25, 2025 announcement [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Manages 11 million-plus assets across customer base [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Processes over 27 million work orders annually [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Processes over 370,000 safety procedures annually [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Customer-reported outcomes cited in MaintainX marketing: reducing unplanned downtime by 34%, increasing production capacity by 15%, and achieving up to 32% savings in monthly maintenance costs [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

- Industries served: manufacturing, facilities management, food and beverage, distribution centers [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

### Product positioning

- Positions the platform as a next-generation approach to AI-powered maintenance and asset management [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Articulates a human-AI collaboration thesis: AI amplifies human capability rather than replacing it, putting AI-driven insights in the hands of both the technician on the shop floor and the executive in the boardroom [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Leverages AI and IoT to connect asset and work intelligence data, providing real-time insights for proactive maintenance [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Operates a sensor-agnostic approach for machine health monitoring that works with any industrial sensor or control system [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Stated use of Series D proceeds: advance AI-powered asset and work intelligence; expand machine health monitoring capabilities and sensor-partner ecosystem; accelerate market expansion across industries and geographies; attract top talent and address the industry-wide skilled labor shortage [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
