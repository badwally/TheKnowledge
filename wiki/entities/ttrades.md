# TTrades

## Summary

TTrades (Twitter handle @TTrades_edu) is a trader and educator who publishes ICT-style smart-money trading materials; the framework first appeared in a 2022 5-page PDF "Entry Models and Checklist" cataloguing four integrated trade-entry models with Failed Swing and SMT variations [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] before being expanded in a 2023 6-page revision that adds a fifth "Box Setup" model on top of the same HTF-premise / LTF-trigger scaffolding [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].

## Key facts

- Public handle on the reference card is @TTrades_edu, watermarked on each model diagram in both the 2022 [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] and 2023 [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]] versions.
- Authored "Entry Models and Checklist" — the original 5-page 2022 PDF titled "ENTRY MODELS AND CHECKLIST / HOPE THEY HELP / -TTrades" with four models [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] — and a 2023 6-page expanded version that adds a fifth "Box Setup" model [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- Models 1–4 share a common scaffold present in both versions: an HTF POI as the directional premise, an HTF liquidity grab into FVG/POI, an LTF liquidity grab, then BOS + displacement + FVG as the entry trigger, with the trade taken in discount (long) or premium (short) [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- Model 1 is HTF + BOS + FVG; Model 2 adds Internal Liquidity (IDM); Model 3 adds the OTE .62–.79 fib zone to Model 1; Model 4 adds both IDM and OTE [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- In the 2022 version, Models 3 and 4 are explicitly labeled "Model 1 + OTE" and "Model 2 + OTE" respectively, with their Failed-swing and SMT variations carrying the parent-model labels rather than the OTE-extended labels — documenting the additive design intent [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]].
- Each of Models 1–4 has two named variations — "Failed swing" and "SMT" — that substitute the standard liquidity-grab leg with a failed-swing structure or SMT divergence confluence respectively, while keeping the rest of the sequence intact [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- Model 5 ("Box Setup") was added only in the 2023 expansion and is structurally distinct: an original consolidation range is broken aggressively in one direction, then aggressively reversed in the other direction, with entry on the retest of the original consolidation low (bullish) or high (bearish) [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- Model 5 has an explicit "No POI" experimental variation, indicating that the consolidation/retest geometry can be traded without an HTF point-of-interest premise [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
- Every model is checked against a "Good Risk : Reward" criterion as a final gate, framing R:R as the deciding filter once structural ingredients are present [[sources/pdf-c57b9a32c399]] [[sources/pdf-3a510f58854c]].
