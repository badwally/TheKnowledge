# Conservative Party of Canada

## Summary

The Conservative Party of Canada is a Canadian federal political party that, as of the source's January 2025 op-ed, was assessed as likely to form the next federal government following an upcoming non-confidence vote and federal election triggered by the prorogation of Parliament [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]]. The op-ed notes that a Conservative government may take a lighter-handed approach to AI regulation than the comprehensive cross-sectoral AIDA framework, focusing instead on innovation and targeted intervention in specific high-risk AI use cases [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].

## Key facts

- Assessed by the source as likely to form the next federal government after the post-prorogation non-confidence vote and election [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- The Conservative Party had not released a definitive official statement on its approach to regulating AI as of the op-ed's January 2025 publication [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- Public remarks from Conservative MPs Rick Perkins and Michelle Rempel Garner suggested a lighter-handed regulatory posture, focused on innovation and targeted high-risk-use intervention rather than AIDA's cross-sectoral framework [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
- The source links this likely Canadian posture to deregulatory pressure from the new Trump administration in the United States [[sources/web-2025-01-17-451]].
