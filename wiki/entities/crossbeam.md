# Crossbeam

## Summary

Crossbeam is the ecosystem-data platform in Orita's internal stack, providing account-overlap detection with partners and prospects [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]]. It is also a planned MCP connector for the Competitive Intelligence agent, supplying Crossbeam overlap shifts as a partner/ecosystem signal source (S7.3) [[sources/docx-7ed3b7965067]].

## Key facts

- Crossbeam provides account-overlap detection with partners and prospects in Orita's stack [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
- The Competitive Intelligence agent's source taxonomy assigns Crossbeam overlap shifts to provenance category S7.3 (Ecosystem and Partner Signals) [[sources/docx-7ed3b7965067]].
- Crossbeam integration is accessed via a platform MCP connector (Category 3, pending evaluation) rather than RSS or web scrape [[sources/docx-7ed3b7965067]].
