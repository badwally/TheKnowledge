# BC Housing Maintenance Matters Bulletin Series

## Summary

The "Maintenance Matters" series is a set of residential building-maintenance bulletins with companion videos published by BC Housing, providing practical maintenance, inspection, and remediation guidance prepared by a consortium of building envelope experts [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]]. Industry collaborators vary by bulletin — MM-06 lists Polygon and Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] while MM-02 lists Polygon and the Condominium Home Owners' Association (CHOA) [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]]. The series is the authoritative Canadian provincial source for prior calibration of the roof, building envelope, and parking deck / podium components in the condo-capital-infra probabilistic reserve engine [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].

## Key facts

- Series of building-maintenance bulletins with companion videos, designed to provide practical information on maintaining residential buildings [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Published by BC Housing; bulletin content is prepared by a consortium of building envelope experts [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]] [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Industry collaborators vary by bulletin: MM-06 (Decks and Balconies) lists Polygon and Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]]; MM-02 (Maintaining Your Roof) lists Polygon and the Condominium Home Owners' Association (CHOA) [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletin No. 2 ("Maintaining Your Roof") distinguishes low-slope roofing (most multi-unit residential buildings) from steep-slope roofing (townhouses and single detached houses); some buildings combine both types [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletin No. 2 addresses membrane systems, ballast/granules, flashings and drainage components, ponding, moss and algae growth, attic ventilation, and replacement planning [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletin No. 2 provides roof service-life range (10 to over 30 years depending on design, exposure, construction, and materials), inspection cadence (twice yearly: spring + fall, plus post-storm), and a structured failure-mode / inspection-item taxonomy used for low-slope and steep-slope roof prior calibration [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletin No. 6 covers Decks and Balconies — distinguishing decks (over enclosed space, therefore also roofs) from balconies (projecting from the building, not over enclosed living space) and addressing membranes, guardrails / guardwalls, drainage, soffits, and vents [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]].
- Bulletin No. 6 provides explicit service-life ranges (liquid-applied urethane ~10 years; sheet-applied vinyl PVC 10-15 years; SBS bitumen modified torch-on as a stronger two-ply alternative), inspection cadence (biennial for membranes and guardrails; annual for scupper attachments; twice-yearly surface cleaning), and a step-up-inspection trigger at 15+ years of membrane age [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]].
- Bulletins are cross-referenced — e.g., MM-06 references Builder Insight No. 8 (Compatibility of Fasteners and Connectors with Pressure Treated Wood) when discussing replacement of wood decking boards [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]].
- MM-06 distinguishes resident-performed maintenance (surface cleaning, debris removal) from professional inspection work (membrane condition assessment, guardrail integrity), explicitly directing residents and maintenance managers away from performing membrane inspections themselves [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-06]].
- MM-02 similarly directs roof inspection and maintenance to qualified roofing inspectors or contractors, with residents limited to visual identification of indirect signs (ceiling/wall staining) that they should report to the maintenance manager rather than diagnose [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletins reference the BC Strata Property Act Contingency Reserve Fund (CRF) regime as the funding mechanism for common-expense replacement of building components such as roofs [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletins direct readers to professional associations including the Roofing Contractors Association of British Columbia (RCABC) and the Roof Consultants Institute (RCI; now the International Institute of Building Enclosure Consultants, IIBEC) for selecting qualified professionals [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
- Bulletins cite the Homeowner Protection Act regulations, which require owners to mitigate damage and permit warranty providers to exclude coverage for damage caused or made worse by negligent or improper maintenance — a legal incentive for residents to retain qualified contractors [[sources/pdf-bc-housing-2020-maintenance-matters-02]].
