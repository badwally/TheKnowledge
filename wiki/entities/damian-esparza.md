# Damian J. Esparza

## Summary

Damian J. Esparza is the Founder of SmartProperty, the vendor of The Living Reserve Study® product [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]]. He is the named SmartProperty principal in the April 2025 announcement of the Strategic Club Solutions partnership extending SmartProperty's reserve-study product into the Large Scale Clubs segment [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].

## Key facts

- Damian J. Esparza is identified as Founder of SmartProperty® in the April 7, 2025 partnership announcement with Strategic Club Solutions [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
- He framed the SCS partnership as integrating SmartProperty's Living Reserve Study® with SCS's strategic expertise to equip club decision-makers with insights and technology for long-term operational success [[sources/web-2025-04-07-817]].
