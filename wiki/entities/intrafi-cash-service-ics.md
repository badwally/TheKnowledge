# IntraFi Cash Service (ICS)

## Summary

The IntraFi Cash Service (ICS) is an IntraFi-operated program that allows homeowners associations to remain FDIC-insured on deposits exceeding the $250,000 per-depositor / per-bank reserve-funds FDIC limit, using Demand Deposit Accounts (DDAs) placed into savings accounts at multiple banks within the ICS network [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]]. Unlike CDARS, ICS does not distribute the HOA's funds across certificates of deposit; the underlying instrument is a DDA-backed savings account, which is marketed as bearing interest at a higher rate than traditional savings accounts while remaining FDIC-insured [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].

## Key facts

- Full name: IntraFi Cash Service [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Operated by IntraFi [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Allows associations to remain FDIC-insured on deposits exceeding the $250,000 reserve-funds FDIC limit [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Underlying instrument: Demand Deposit Accounts (DDAs) placed into savings accounts at multiple banks within the ICS network [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- The ICS savings accounts bear interest at a higher rate than traditional savings accounts [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- The ICS savings accounts remain FDIC-insured [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Differs structurally from CDARS in that ICS uses DDA-backed savings accounts rather than CDs as the underlying deposit instrument [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
