# FIA Principal Traders Group

## Summary

The Futures Industry Association Principal Traders Group (FIA PTG); issued a statement during the "Fed Robbery" controversy saying that the rules governing the release of FOMC news were presumably not violated and that the simultaneous release of the September 18, 2013 FOMC announcement at 2:00 pm ET in both Chicago and New York was to be expected because the Federal Reserve had changed the release mechanism from lockup to embargo in March 2013 [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].

## Key facts

- Issued a formal statement in response to the controversy started by Nanex's "Einstein and The Great Fed Robbery" paper [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
- Stated that the rules governing the release of news were "presumably, not violated" [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
- Explained that the method of releasing FOMC information by the Federal Reserve had been changed from lockup to embargo in March 2013, and that simultaneous release at 2:00 pm ET in Chicago and New York was therefore to be expected [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
- The FIA PTG statement is the primary public source confirming the March 2013 lockup-to-embargo policy change [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
