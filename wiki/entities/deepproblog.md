# DeepProbLog

## Summary

A neuro-symbolic system (Manhaeve et al. 2018) that interfaces neural predicates as external artefacts in probabilistic logic programs; cited by Belle and Marcus (AAAI-26) as a representative example of the "neuro-symbolic programs" line of research that extends knowledge graphs with non-trivial axioms specified in logic programs [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].

## Key facts

- DeepProbLog interfaces neural predicates as external artefacts inside probabilistic logic programs (Manhaeve et al. 2018) [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- DeepProbLog is named as a canonical example in the "neuro-symbolic programs" line of research, which the paper describes as extending knowledge graphs with non-trivial axioms specified in logic programs [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- In this design, the neural training is influenced by the logical structure of the program — a probabilistic interpretation of the symbolic constraints [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- DeepProbLog and semantic loss are reported to perform essentially similar functions when computing gradients, despite their distinct origins [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
