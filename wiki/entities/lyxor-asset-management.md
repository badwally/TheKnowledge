# Lyxor Asset Management

## Summary

Paris-based asset manager whose Research & Development group published the December 2011 working paper "Trend Filtering Methods for Momentum Strategies" — a survey of econometric trend estimators (linear and nonlinear, univariate and multivariate) and their application to momentum strategies, illustrated on the S&P 500 [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].

## Key facts

- Research & Development group based in Paris; institutional home of all four authors of the paper — Benjamin Bruder, Tung-Lam Dao, Jean-Charles Richard, and Thierry Roncalli [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- The paper acknowledges Guillaume Jamet and Hoang-Phong Nguyen for helpful comments [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- The paper frames trend filtering as fundamental to most momentum strategies developed in asset management and hedge fund sectors in order to improve performance and limit portfolio risks [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- Identifies the CTA strategy used by hedge funds as one of the best-known momentum strategies [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
