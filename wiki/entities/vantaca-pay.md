# Vantaca Pay

## Summary

Vantaca Pay is the integrated-payments product within the Vantaca community association management platform, sitting under the platform's "Integrated Payments" feature category alongside the Full Accounting Suite, Business Intelligence, Revenue Capture, Resident Experience, and Partner Integrations capability groups [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]]. The product bundles digital payments, lockbox processing, and invoice-to-pay automation, and is positioned by vendor marketing as connecting directly to Vantaca's accounting and workflow modules so that payments post accurately and exceptions are clear [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key facts

- Named product within the Vantaca platform; identified on the Vantaca features page as the offering under the "Integrated Payments" feature category [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Bundles three capability sets: digital payments, lockbox processing, and invoice-to-pay automation [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Marketed as connecting directly to accounting and workflows so that payments post accurately and exceptions are clear [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Sits alongside five other top-level feature categories on Vantaca's product surface: Full Accounting Suite, Business Intelligence, Revenue Capture (Revenue Manager), Resident Experience, and Partner Integrations [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The features-page copy does not name any specific banking partner, payment processor, or third-party integration underlying Vantaca Pay [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
