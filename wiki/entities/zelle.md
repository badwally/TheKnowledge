# Zelle® Network

## Summary

Zelle is a peer-to-peer payments network operated by Early Warning Services that supports direct payments between people through their financial institutions [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]]. Launched by Early Warning in the 2013–2018 period, the Zelle network helped American consumers and small businesses send over $1 trillion in 2024 [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].

## Key facts

- Zelle was launched by Early Warning Services in the 2013–2018 period to support fast, direct payments between people through their financial institutions [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- In 2024, the Zelle network helped American consumers and small businesses send over $1 trillion [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
- Between 2019 and 2024, Early Warning enhanced the Zelle network with additional experiences and capabilities [[sources/web-2026-04-03-04f]].
