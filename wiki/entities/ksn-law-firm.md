# Kovitz Shifrin Nesbit (KSN)

## Summary

Kovitz Shifrin Nesbit (KSN) is a multi-state community-association law firm founded in 1983 that represents condominium, homeowner, and townhome associations, with offices in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, and Wisconsin. KSN's blog publishes commentary translating federal regulatory developments — including the March 18, 2026 Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 reserve-funding update — into board-level implementation guidance, and the firm also represents clients in real estate transactions, collections, landlord/tenant matters, and property tax appeals [[sources/web-2026-03-24-bb6]].
