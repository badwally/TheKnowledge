# Association Prime

## Summary

Association Prime is the community association banking division of SouthState Bank, N.A., specializing in the unique banking and lending needs of community association management firms and the communities they manage [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. Association Prime started its association banking division in 1986 and serves more than 450 management companies in 35 states, holding just under $2 billion in association deposits [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. The division is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
