# REcollab

## Summary

REcollab (recollab.ai) is a North American reserve-fund study and maintenance-planning SaaS vendor whose flagship product, RECOStudy™, delivers a live, interactive reserve fund dashboard built from uploaded documents. [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]] RECOStudy operates on a deterministic adequacy framing — presenting a percent-funded gauge and a special-assessment risk year flag — without probabilistic confidence intervals (P10/P50/P90) or Monte Carlo simulation. [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]

## Key facts

- **Product name**: RECOStudy™ [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Core positioning**: "Turn your Reserve Fund Study into a live, interactive roadmap" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Methodology class**: Deterministic adequacy; primary outputs are a percent-funded gauge (documented example: 17% = "low") and a special-assessment risk year flag (documented example: 2029, "High Risk") [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **No probabilistic outputs**: No P10/P50/P90 confidence intervals; no Monte Carlo simulation [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Ingest model**: No manual site inspection required; extracts inventory and costs from uploaded unorganized files and photos, supplemented by an expert-built dataset [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Compliance module**: Scans bylaws, contracts, and inspection records to flag risks across financial health, physical condition, and compliance before they become penalties [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Asset tracking**: Tracks Remaining Useful Life (RUL) for every component, from boilers to hallway carpets [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Multi-stakeholder platform**: Boards, owners, lenders, insurers, contractors, and vendors each receive role-appropriate access on a single platform [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Automated record-keeping**: Every repair and inspection is logged automatically for insurer and city-inspector access [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
- **Market**: Property managers across North America [[sources/web-2026-01-01-84c]]
