# REcollab Technologies

## Summary

REcollab Technologies (recollab.ai) is an AI-positioned reserve-planning and property-management software vendor marketing to property managers across North America under a "Financial Operating System for Asset Managers" positioning that frames its product as replacing static-PDF reserve studies with a live financial dashboard updated as building conditions change [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]]. The marketing-site self-description characterizes the team as "a group of civil engineers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, and capital markets experts" and the product as an AI document-ingest pipeline that extracts, categorizes, and maps uploaded files to building components — placing REcollab in the DIY/SaaS-with-AI-component-identification competitive cohort alongside ReserveWise, but with the AI layer applied at the document-extraction stage rather than at ReserveWise's site-walk imagery stage [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]]. As of the 2026-05-18 vendor-watch ingest, the marketing site discloses no methodology specifics (deterministic vs probabilistic), no pricing, no jurisdiction-specific regulatory positioning, no named team members or leadership, no licensed-reserve-analyst credential, no integration partners, and no verifiable customer case data — placing the vendor in the "marketing-only" tier of the condo-capital-infra competitive vendor set [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

## Key facts

### Corporate identity and team framing

- Marketing-site domain: recollab.ai [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Self-described as "Built by Civil Engineers. For the people who fund, insure, and run buildings." [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Team composition per the "About REcollab" section: civil engineers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, and capital markets experts who "understand buildings from the foundation up" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- No named founders, leadership, board members, or advisors disclosed on the marketing site as of the 2026-05-18 ingest [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Target customer set per the closing call-to-action: "property managers across North America" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### Top-level product positioning

- Top-line headline: "AI-Powered Reserve Planning" — "The Financial OperatingSystem for Asset Managers" — a horizontal "operating system" frame rather than a reserve-study-specific product frame [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Stated value proposition: "Every stakeholder, every action, at your fingertips. Reserve studies, RFPs, and financial plans that think ahead and keep your whole team moving." [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Three named product surfaces in the "Use Cases" section: Reserve Study, Reports (compliance / deadlines / certificates), and Contractors (RFP / bid workflow) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### RECOStudy™ (core reserve-study product)

- Branded name for the core reserve-study product: RECOStudy™ [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Positioned as "the reserve study, reborn as a live financial dashboard" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Stated outputs: contribution plans, special-assessment risk, and 30-year projections — all updating as the building changes [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Reserve-study product framing emphasizes "spot the risk before it becomes a special assessment" and "always live. Updated as your building changes." [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### AI document-ingest model

- Product framing of document ingest: "Drop your documents. Your building brain takes over. Drop any file format. The platform extracts, categorizes, and maps your data to the exact building component it belongs to." [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- The ingest model places the AI layer at the document-extraction stage — contrasting with ReserveWise's AI-vision component identification applied to site-walk imagery [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- No disclosure of underlying ML architecture, training data, accuracy benchmarks, or jurisdictional component-mapping conventions on the marketing site [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### "Old Way vs New Way" competitive framing

- Reserve studies framed as moving from "a 3-month consulting engagement, delivered as a static PDF that's outdated the day it prints" to "a live dashboard, updated as conditions change, defensible to any lender, insurer, or board" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Compliance framed as moving from "manual checks, missed deadlines, expired certificates slipping through" to "automated scans flag every gap before it becomes a penalty" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Board visibility framed as moving from "boards making decisions on outdated reports and partial information" to "boards see every detail in real time and approve with full context" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Board approvals framed as moving from "difficult conversations about sudden, massive fee hikes" to "incremental contribution paths the board can see and plan around" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Contractors framed as moving from "informal process, no price guarantee, no green loan eligibility" to "verified contractors, automated RFPs, and competitive bids that qualify your building for green financing" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Insurance framed as moving from "no proof of maintenance history to negotiate with" to "verified maintenance logs unlock insurance savings and preferred rates automatically" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### Contractor / RFP workflow

- Positions contractor procurement as "From repair to winning bid. Automatically." with "six steps, zero phone calls. Verified contractors compete for your work." [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Marketing claim that automated RFPs and competitive bids qualify buildings for green financing — a green-loan-eligibility positioning angle distinguishing REcollab's framing from purely-reserve-study DIY competitors [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### Compliance / reporting workflow

- Positions reporting as "Every deadline. Every certificate. Every jurisdiction." with reports going out automatically to the right party on time [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- The "every jurisdiction" framing asserts multi-jurisdiction compliance coverage but the marketing site does not enumerate jurisdictions, name statutes, or describe regulatory-interpretation depth — leaving the geographic and statutory perimeter undefined as of the 2026-05-18 ingest [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### Marketing-site transparency gaps (CI-relevant absences)

- No methodology disclosure: the marketing site does not state whether forecasting is deterministic or probabilistic, does not describe Monte Carlo or Bayesian modeling, and does not address regime-switching cost escalation [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- No pricing disclosure on the marketing site [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- No jurisdiction-specific regulatory positioning: FL SIRS, BC SPA, ON Condo Act 1998, NS Condominium Act, NB CPA c16.05, NL NLR 80/11, and California Davis-Stirling § 5550 are not named on the marketing site [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- No named licensed reserve analyst, P.Eng, RA, RS, PRA, AACI, or CRP credential disclosed in the team description [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

- No integration partners or accounting/CAM-SaaS integrations named (no CINC, Yardi, AppFolio, or Buildium named) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].

### Competitive cohort placement

- REcollab occupies the AI-positioned DIY/SaaS reserve-planning competitive cohort alongside ReserveWise (deterministic, no licensed RA, AI-vision component ID at site-walk imagery layer), with the key technical differentiator being REcollab's application of AI at the document-extraction layer rather than at the imagery-analysis layer [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- Like ReserveWise, REcollab discloses no licensed reserve analyst in the loop and no probabilistic methodology, placing both in the deterministic-DIY tier of the vendor watch set [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
- REcollab's "operating system" + contractor-RFP + insurance-records framing extends beyond ReserveWise's reserve-study-only DIY positioning, putting REcollab structurally closer to broader property-management-platform plays than to pure reserve-study SaaS [[sources/web-2026-01-01-d10]].
