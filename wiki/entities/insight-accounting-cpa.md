# Insight Accounting CPA

## Summary

Insight Accounting CPA is a Mississauga, Ontario CPA firm offering specialized condominium corporation accounting services — audit and review engagements, reserve fund accounting, financial reporting, and budget preparation — to condominium corporations throughout Mississauga, Toronto, Brampton, and the broader Greater Toronto Area (GTA) [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]]. The firm publishes practitioner-facing guidance on Ontario condominium financial reporting obligations under the Condominium Act, 1998, Ontario Regulation 48/01, CPA Canada Handbook (NPO standards), and CAO Guidelines [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].

## Key facts

- Mississauga, Ontario-based CPA firm focused on condominium corporation accounting [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Service-area scope explicitly framed as Mississauga, Toronto, Brampton, and the broader GTA [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Offers audit and review engagement services tailored to condominium corporations subject to Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 §66 financial-statement obligations and the 80% owner-waiver mechanism [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Offers consulting on reserve fund accounting (Condominium Act §93), budget preparation under §94, and reserve fund investment compliance under §115 [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Publishes a guide titled "Financial Reporting for Condominium Corporations in Ontario: A Complete CPA Guide" authored by principal Bader A. Chowdry, CPA, CA, LPA (March 5, 2026) [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Public contact number listed as (905) 270-1873 [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Frames the Ontario condo accounting market via the claim that "over 12,000 registered condominium corporations" exist in the GTA — a 2026-vintage practitioner figure that should be compared against earlier CCI June 2016 statistics showing approximately 9,000 corporations across all of Ontario [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
