# Zhi Da

## Summary

Finance researcher at the University of Notre Dame, Mendoza College of Business; co-author of the 2020 paper "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum," which uses 1974–2020 intraday futures data across more than 60 contracts to show that the last-half-hour return is robustly predicted by the rest-of-day return and that gamma-hedging demand is an important economic driver [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Notre Dame, 239 Mendoza College of Business, Notre Dame, IN 46556, USA, on the paper [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- Co-author of "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" (26 August 2020; SSRN abstract 3760365) with Guido Baltussen, Sten Lammers, and Martin Martens [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- The paper extends earlier intraday-predictability work — including Gao et al. (2018), which found first-half-hour returns to predict last-half-hour returns for ten US ETFs — by showing that rest-of-day returns are a stronger predictor than first-half-hour returns out of sample, and that the pattern generalizes across equities, bonds, commodities, and currencies [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- Provides empirical identification of the hedging channel via S&P 500 index option market makers' negative gamma exposure and leveraged-ETF rebalancing demand [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
