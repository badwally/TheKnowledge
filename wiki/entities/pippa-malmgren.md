# Pippa Malmgren

## Summary

Geopolitical analyst whose framing of "hot wars in cold places" — cyberspace, space, and deep underwater — Zoltan Pozsar adopts as the opening conceptual move in his August 1, 2022 Credit Suisse dispatch "War and Interest Rates," and whose stated belief that "we have already entered WWIII" is invoked at the close of the dispatch to argue that today's economic conflicts must be understood as wartime macro [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].

## Key facts

- Credited by Pozsar with the term "hot wars in cold places" — wars fought in cyberspace, space, and deep underwater — which Pozsar extends to include the "corridors of power" in Washington, Beijing, and Moscow where great powers are waging "hot economic wars" involving the flow of technologies, goods, and commodities [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
- Quoted as observing that "Peter Drucker delayed publishing his book, The End of Economic Man: The Origins of Totalitarianism, until 1939 because he was frightened to say that WWII was about to begin" [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
- States that she now feels Drucker's angst as she writes of her belief "that we have already entered WWIII" — but cautions that "war is changing: the confrontations and conflicts of our time may be different from what we experienced before" [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
- Frames the new theaters of conflict as "no longer exclusively about physical territory and humans on a front line, but about cyberspace, space and the high seas," which Pozsar extends to include pipelines, interbank messaging systems (SWIFT), ASML machines, currencies, commodities, and inflation [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
