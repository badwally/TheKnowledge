# Matija Franklin

## Summary

Researcher at Google DeepMind and co-author of the 2026 paper "Intelligent AI Delegation," which builds on his earlier work on AI-human delegation and algorithmic management [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Google DeepMind on "Intelligent AI Delegation," co-authored with Nenad Tomašev (lead) and Simon Osindero [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
- Cited in the paper as co-author of Ashton and Franklin (2022), the source for the framing that AI-human delegation is one of three core scenarios alongside human-to-AI and AI-to-AI delegation [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
