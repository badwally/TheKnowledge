# CIBC Wood Gundy

## Summary

CIBC Wood Gundy is a division of CIBC World Markets Inc. operating under the CIBC Private Wealth brand, providing investment advisory and portfolio management services. The firm services Canadian condominium boards through individual Investment Advisor practices (e.g., The MacKay Financial Group in Mississauga), and is one of the brokerage channels through which Ontario condominium corporations can purchase GICs spanning multiple CDIC member institutions to stack deposit insurance coverage above the per-institution limits [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].

## Key facts

- Division of CIBC World Markets Inc. [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Operates under the CIBC Private Wealth umbrella, which consists of services provided by CIBC and certain of its subsidiaries [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The CIBC logo and "CIBC Private Wealth" are trademarks of CIBC, used under license [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- "Wood Gundy" is a registered trademark of CIBC World Markets Inc. [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Provides investment advisory services to Canadian condominium boards through individual Investment Advisor practices, including The MacKay Financial Group in Mississauga, Ontario [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Can provide a single investor with GICs from several different CDIC member institutions, enabling reserve-fund investment programs to remain fully insured while exceeding the $100,000 per-institution CDIC ceiling [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Brokerage-purchased GICs are positioned by CIBC Wood Gundy as carrying higher interest rates than those purchased directly at the branch [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- For GIC terms of one year or less, simple interest is paid at maturity; for GIC terms greater than one year, simple interest is paid annually or compound interest is calculated annually and paid at maturity [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- CIBC and CIBC World Markets Inc., their affiliates, directors, officers and employees may buy, sell, or hold positions in securities mentioned in CIBC Wood Gundy publications, may perform financial advisory and investment banking services, and may have lending or other credit relationships with the same issuers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- CIBC World Markets Inc. and its representatives receive sales commissions and/or a bid-ask spread when retail clients purchase, sell, or hold securities [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
