# Dacheng Xiu

## Summary

Finance researcher at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business; co-author with Bryan Kelly of the 2023 SSRN survey "Financial Machine Learning," which argues that machine learning is well-suited to financial markets because of large conditioning information sets and ambiguous functional forms, and adopts a three-part definition of machine learning (high-dimensional models, regularization, and efficient model search) [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].

## Key facts

- Listed institutional affiliation on the paper: University of Chicago Booth School of Business [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
- Listed contact email: dacheng.xiu@chicagobooth.edu [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
- Second-listed author of "Financial Machine Learning" (2023, SSRN abstract id 4501707), co-authored with Bryan Kelly of Yale and AQR Capital Management [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
- Per the paper's abstract, the survey is designed to highlight the best examples of what machine learning has to offer financial-market research and to recommend promising directions for future research [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
- The paper argues that the more expansive a model can be in considering large conditioning sets, the more realistic the model will be — and that the same logic applies to functional form, since investors use information in ways researchers cannot exhaustively specify in a parametric statistical model [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
