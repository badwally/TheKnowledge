# Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA)

## Summary

The Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA) operates a deposit insurance program that protects insurable deposits held with Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. FSRA administers the Deposit Insurance Reserve Fund (DIRF), which is pre-funded by Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires through premiums paid to FSRA at no cost to depositors [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. The deposit insurance program is part of a comprehensive depositor protection program that ensures the safety and soundness of credit unions and caisses populaires, with FSRA's liability to insure deposits at Ontario credit unions limited to the assets of the DIRF [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

## Key facts

- Operates a deposit insurance program that protects insurable deposits held with Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Administers the Deposit Insurance Reserve Fund (DIRF), pre-funded by Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires through premiums paid to FSRA at no cost to depositors [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- FSRA's liability to insure deposits held at Ontario credit unions is limited to the assets of the Deposit Insurance Reserve Fund [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Requires all credit unions and caisses populaires in Ontario to prominently display an official FSRA decal at each of their locations [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Provides separate deposit insurance coverage categories for joint accounts, trust accounts, registered accounts, and eligible business accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Eligibility for deposit insurance coverage requires the depositor to be a member of the credit union or caisse populaire [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- When a credit union or caisse populaire goes out of business, FSRA payouts include principal and interest up to $250,000 for insurable deposits held in non-registered accounts (aggregated together) and unlimited for insurable deposits held in registered accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Borrowers from an insolvent credit union or caisse populaire remain responsible for repayment of outstanding debt until paid in full; FSRA may exercise a right of set-off against deposits or deposit insurance payments to assist in such repayment [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Maintains a Consumer Advisory Panel that provides a consumer perspective on proposed FSRA policy changes [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Publishes enforcement, monitoring, supervision, and administration information for credit unions and caisses populaires placed under supervision [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Public contact: 416-250-7250 / 1-800-668-0128 [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
