# Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

## Summary

Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) is a venture capital firm whose enterprise-software partners have published influential commentary on the strategic and competitive economics of data, including the 2019 essay "The Empty Promise of Data Moats" by partners Martin Casado and Peter Lauten arguing that data is rarely a durable competitive moat for enterprise startups [[sources/web-2019-05-09-487]].

## Key facts

- "The Empty Promise of Data Moats" was authored by a16z enterprise-focused partners Martin Casado and Peter Lauten and reflects pattern observations across the firm's enterprise-software portfolio [[sources/web-2019-05-09-487]].
- The firm had previously written at length endorsing "data network effects"; the 2019 essay is in part a self-revision of that earlier narrative [[sources/web-2019-05-09-487]].
