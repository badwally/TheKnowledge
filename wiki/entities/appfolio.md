# AppFolio

## Summary

AppFolio is a property management software vendor that markets AppFolio Property Manager as a single-platform solution covering core property-management workflows, and operates AppFolio Stack™ as the partner integration framework through which third-party proptech products plug into the AppFolio platform [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]]. SmartProperty's asset and project management software is one of the named Stack partners, integrating reserve-study data, replacement costs, financial health reporting, and capital project management directly into the AppFolio environment [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].

## Key facts

- Markets AppFolio Property Manager as a single property-management platform [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- Operates AppFolio Stack™, framed as a customization framework that allows customers to match workflows to how their business operates while remaining within the AppFolio platform [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- Positions the Stack as a partnership framework with "top proptech solutions on the market," intended to give customers a competitive edge, enhance productivity, and improve the resident experience [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- Lists SmartProperty as a named Stack partner integrating reserve-study data, replacement costs, monthly financial health reporting, and capital project management into AppFolio [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- Markets the SmartProperty integration's outcomes under four named pillars: boost financial transparency, attract and retain more customers, add additional revenue streams, and capitalize on real-time insights [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- Public-facing partner page for SmartProperty: appfolio.com/partners/smartproperty [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
- Offers a free product demo with a product expert as the marketed conversion path from the partner page [[sources/web-2024-09-13-69c]].
