# AppFolio

## Summary

AppFolio is a property management software vendor that markets AppFolio Property Manager as a single-platform solution covering core property-management workflows, and operates as one of three CAM platforms integrated with SmartProperty's Living Reserve Study [[sources/web-2025-01-01-329]].

## Key facts

- Identified by SmartProperty as one of three integrated CAM platforms (alongside CINC and Vantaca) bridging bank-account data and capital reserve plans [[sources/web-2025-01-01-329]].
- Integration scope per SmartProperty's positioning: connect operating-account balances to the Living Reserve Study's portfolio capital plan and real-time cash-flow view [[sources/web-2025-01-01-329]].
