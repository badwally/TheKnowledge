# BuildingLink

## Summary

BuildingLink is a resident-engagement platform for condominium and apartment buildings whose accounting-integration strategy is to act as the resident-facing surface for account data sourced from third-party CAM/accounting/property-management systems, rather than to operate its own accounting general ledger [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. As of its public accounting-integrations catalog, BuildingLink publishes 17 named accounting integration partners; most surface resident balance and transaction history through 24-hour batch syncs, and Yardi uniquely provides real-time work-order synchronization back from BuildingLink into the accounting system [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. BuildingLink frames its broader integration portfolio as spanning "three dozen partners and counting" across the property-management stack, with verticals beyond accounting including IoT sensors via the Aware Buildings partnership [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]] [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].

## Key facts

### Accounting integration portfolio

- Publishes 17 named accounting integration partners: Yardi, Hopem, FSRConnect, AMSI, Jenark, Caliber, TOPS, Remote Landlord, Vantaca, RealPage OneSite, CINC, Entrata, MDS, Rent Manager, VMS, Condo Manager, and C3 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Standard integration cadence is a 24-hour overnight sync that pushes resident move-in/move-out information, contact information, and account balances from the accounting system into BuildingLink [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Resident-portal surfacing: unit owners can view account balance and transaction history from the integrated accounting system on the BuildingLink resident site [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Yardi is the only named accounting partner with a real-time work-order sync from BuildingLink back into the accounting system; all other named partners use 24-hour or daily syncs [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- FSRConnect supports an optional reverse sync that pushes resident contact-information changes from BuildingLink back into Connect; FSRConnect also allows the Connect document library to be framed within BuildingLink [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Jenark and MDS each charge an integration fee distinct from the BuildingLink platform fee [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].

### Integration portfolio scale

- Markets integration options with "three dozen partners and counting" across the property-management stack [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- The accounting subset is one of multiple integration verticals; the IoT-sensor vertical is named Aware Buildings as of March 2025 [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
