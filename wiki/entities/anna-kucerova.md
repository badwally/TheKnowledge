# Anna Kucerova

## Summary

Anna Kucerova is the first-listed author of the 2011 arXiv paper applying Bayesian inference to combine sources of information for the description of coupled heat and moisture transport in heterogeneous materials.

## Key facts

- Kucerova is the first listed author of "Uncertainty Updating in the Description of Coupled Heat and Moisture Transport in Heterogeneous Materials" [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The paper was posted as arXiv 1102.5239 on 25 February 2011 and subsequently published in *Applied Mathematics and Computation*, volume 219, issue 13, pages 7252-7261, 2013 (DOI 10.1016/j.amc.2011.02.078) [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The arXiv preprint is classified under math.NA (primary) with cross-listing in physics.data-an [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
- The paper is co-authored with Jan Sykora [[sources/arxiv-1102.5239]].
