# Ben Bernanke

## Summary

Former Federal Reserve chair under whose stewardship the "art" of Quantitative Easing was forged; earned the moniker "Helicopter Ben" via the helicopter-drops-of-money theme, and is positioned by Zoltan Pozsar as Jay Powell's cyclical "anti-hero" because the QE that Bernanke architected to lean against deflation must now be reversed via negative wealth effects to lean against inflation [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].

## Key facts

- Earned the helicopter-themed moniker "Helicopter Ben" [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- The "art" of Quantitative Easing was forged under his stewardship at the Fed [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- The original aim of QE under Bernanke was to reflate in order to avoid deflation: reflating house prices, reflating stocks, and reflating the price level [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Asset price growth and positive wealth effects were explicit targets of QE to generate growth and jobs [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Per Pozsar, QE was essentially monetary policy for the asset rich, with trickle-down benefits for the less wealthy [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Treated by Pozsar as Powell's professional anti-hero in the cyclical sense — paired against Volcker as the cyclical hero — because leaning against inflation now requires generating the asset price deflation that Bernanke's QE was designed to prevent [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
