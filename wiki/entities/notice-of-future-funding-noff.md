# Notice of Future Funding of the Reserve Fund (NOFF)

## Summary

The Notice of Future Funding of the Reserve Fund (NOFF) is a prescribed form under the Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 that a condominium corporation must send to owners after the Board has proposed a plan for future funding of the reserve fund. The NOFF is the bridge between the Reserve Fund Study (RFS) and the implementation of the funding plan, and is the legal vehicle for disclosing any variances between the Board's adopted plan and the RFS recommendation [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]]. The underlying funding plan that the NOFF discloses is the same artifact that O. Reg. 48/01 enumerates as Category 6 of the "core record" definition — the "current plan proposed by the board under subsection 94(8) of the Act for future funding of the reserve fund" — making the plan itself producible under the request-for-records regime even after the NOFF has been distributed [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key facts

- The NOFF is a prescribed form sent to owners from the corporation once a funding plan has been proposed [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The Board must propose a future-funding plan within 120 days of preparing the RFS; within 15 days of proposing the plan, the corporation must send the NOFF [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The proposed plan can be implemented starting 30 days from the date the NOFF was issued [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]] [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- If the Board adopts an alternative funding model that varies from the RFS's recommended plan, the variances must be specifically disclosed in the NOFF [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The NOFF contains a contribution table that allows readers to quickly determine required contributions to the Reserve Fund for budgetary purposes [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The NOFF contains a cash flow table that allows readers to monitor the balance by comparing expected cash flows to the financial statements [[sources/pdf-aa9367a8a4a9]].
- The NOFF workflow is treated by the CAO Best Practices Guide as the principal owner-disclosure channel for reserve-fund decisions, sitting downstream of the RFS and upstream of the contribution implementation [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- The board's future-funding plan that the NOFF discloses is proposed under subsection 94(8) of the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- That s. 94(8) plan is explicitly enumerated as paragraph 6 of the "core record" definition in O. Reg. 48/01, meaning the plan itself must be producible under the request-for-records regime (subsection 55(3) of the Act) independent of the NOFF distribution [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Only the "current" plan qualifies as a core record under O. Reg. 48/01; once the board proposes a new plan, prior plans are superseded for core-record purposes [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
