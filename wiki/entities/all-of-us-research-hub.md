# All of Us Research Hub (AoURH)

## Summary

The All of Us Research Hub (AoURH) is a cloud-based biomedical data platform funded by the NIH Office of the Director that holds participant-contributed genomic, environmental, and linked phenotypic data from the All of Us Research Project [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]]. Among the five NIH cloud platforms surveyed by Dahlquist et al. (2023), AoURH is distinctive for using a user-based 'data passport' authorization model rather than project-based Data Access Committees [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].

## Key facts

- Primary funder is the NIH Office of the Director [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- All data are provided through the All of Us Research Project [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Data are harmonized using the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model (OMOP CDM) and further cleaned to protect participant privacy [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Three access tiers: public, registered, and controlled [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- No login is required for the public tier; the registered tier requires eRA Commons authentication; the controlled tier additionally requires more stringent access requirements including appropriate accreditation and separate authorization [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Access is authorized via a user-based 'data passport' model rather than project-based Data Access Committees (DACs) [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- An institutional Data Use and Registration Agreement (DURA) is required, alongside Responsible Conduct of Research Training, identity verification, contact and affiliation disclosure, and consent to release that information [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- AoURH's Resource Access Board (RAB) reviews Data Use and Code of Conduct (DUCC) compliance and recommends corrective action; any researcher or member of the public can ask the RAB to review a study [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- User obligations include no data screenshots, no publication or download of participant-level data, and no shared logins; analysis of registered or controlled data is permitted only on the platform [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Platform notes that the label 'de-identified' may not be fully accurate for its data [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Sanctions include account termination, public posting of name and affiliation, notification to the user's institution and NIH or other federal agencies, and possible financial or legal repercussions [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
