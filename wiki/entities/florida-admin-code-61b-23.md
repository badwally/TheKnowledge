# Florida Administrative Code Chapter 61B-23

## Summary

Florida Administrative Code Chapter 61B-23 is the rule chapter promulgated by the Florida Division of Condominiums (within the Department of Business and Professional Regulation) that governs the operational and governance obligations of condominium associations, covering board fiduciary duty, association operations, elections, recalls, developer transition, and contract bidding [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]]. Unlike Florida's SIRS regime (HB 913 / SB 4-D), which targets structural reserve funding, Chapter 61B-23 is the operational-governance layer of Florida condo regulation [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].

## Key facts

- Rule 61B-23.001 governs the Board of Administration and Committees and codifies the board's fiduciary duty (last amended 4/14/1999) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.002 governs the Operation of the Association (last amended 9/1/2015) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.0021 covers Regular Elections, vacancies caused by expiration of term, resignations, death, and election monitors (last amended 3/21/2016) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.00211 governs Electronic Voting (last amended 3/21/2016) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.00215 defines the Ombudsman role and Election Monitoring scope and extent (last amended 8/7/2005) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.0022 governs Contracts for Bid and Employees (last amended 4/1/1992), establishing competitive-bid requirements for association contracts [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.0026 governs the Right to Recall and Replace a Board Member, including developer, unit-owner, and class-voting recall mechanics (last amended 12/20/1995) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.0027 governs Recall of board members at a unit-owner meeting and vacancy filling (last amended 1/18/2018) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.0028 governs Recall by Written Agreement of Voting Interests and vacancy filling (last amended 1/18/2018) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.0029 governs Electronic Transmission of Notices (last amended 7/27/2006) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.003 governs Transition from Developer Control (last amended 1/19/1997) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
- Rule 61B-23.0051 governs Cable Television Service contracts at associations (last amended 11/23/1993) [[sources/web-2010-01-01-d36]].
