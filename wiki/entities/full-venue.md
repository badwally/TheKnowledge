# Full Venue

## Summary

Full Venue is a Porto, Portugal-based software vendor and the developer of Clustie: AI Marketing Segments, a Shopify-distributed AI audience-intelligence app that syncs predictive Shopify-first-party audiences to Meta Ads [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]]. Full Venue launched Clustie on the Shopify App Store on 2025-04-01 [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].

## Key facts

- Headquarters: Torre das Antas, 3° andar, fracção BL, Av. Fernão Magalhães, 4350-158 Porto, Portugal [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Known product: Clustie: AI Marketing Segments — a Shopify app for predictive AI audience segmentation and Meta Ads sync, launched 2025-04-01 [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Customer-facing support languages: English, Spanish, and Portuguese, implying at minimum a tri-lingual support function [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Distribution and billing model: vendor of record on the Shopify App Store, with recurring USD charges billed every 30 days through Shopify's app-billing rails [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Public traction signal at time of capture: 4.8 average rating across 19 Clustie reviews on the Shopify App Store [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
