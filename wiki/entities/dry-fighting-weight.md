# Dry Fighting Weight (DFW)

## Summary

"Dry Fighting Weight" (DFW) is a paired-kettlebell strength program published by [[entities/strongfirst|StrongFirst]] at strongfirst.com/dry-fighting-weight — the upstream program that [[entities/bethskw|/u/Bethskw]]'s [[entities/dfw-kettleballs-remix|DFW Kettleballs Remix]] reorganises into a 5-week, 3-day-per-week calendar [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].

## Key facts

- Published by StrongFirst at https://www.strongfirst.com/dry-fighting-weight/, identified on the Remix calendar as the "Original 'Dry Fighting Weight' program" [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Built around paired [[concepts/kettlebell|kettlebells]] used at the lifter's 5RM press weight, with [[concepts/clean-and-press|clean & press]] and [[concepts/front-squat|front squat]] as the central lifts [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
- Has been adapted into community-authored calendars such as the DFW Kettleballs Remix, which maps the program onto a fixed 5-week schedule [[sources/pdf-8dee6c5cd58d]].
