# University of California, Irvine

## Summary

Public research university in Irvine, California; institutional home of both Edward O. Thorp (Professor of Mathematics) and Sheen T. Kassouf (Assistant Professor of Economics) at the time of the 1967 publication of "Beat the Market: A Scientific Stock Market System" [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].

## Key facts

- Listed on the title page of "Beat the Market" (1967) as the institutional affiliation of Edward O. Thorp, identified there as Professor of Mathematics at the University of California at Irvine [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
- Listed on the same title page as the institutional affiliation of Sheen T. Kassouf, identified there as Assistant Professor of Economics at the University of California at Irvine [[sources/pdf-9da6062f0265]].
