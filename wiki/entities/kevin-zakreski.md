# Kevin Zakreski

## Summary

Kevin Zakreski authored the British Columbia Law Institute's March 7, 2019 commentary on Order in Council 101/2019, the Strata Property Regulation amendment that clarified deposit-insurance requirements for strata-corporation contingency reserve fund and special-levy investments [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].

## Key facts

- Authored 'Strata Property Regulation amendment clarifies deposit-insurance requirements,' published by BCLI on March 7, 2019 [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
- Wrote on behalf of the British Columbia Law Institute [[sources/web-2019-03-07-e42]].
