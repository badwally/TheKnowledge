# RISE Association Management Group

## Summary

RISE Association Management Group is a Houston, Texas-based community association management company that operates as one of CINC Systems' named early-adopter customers across both the August 5, 2025 Cephai+ adoption announcement (where the company is referenced as "RISE Management Group" and Jason Delgado is identified as Managing Partner) and the September 16, 2025 CINC Connect launch announcement (where Delgado is identified as CEO of "RISE Association Management Group") [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]] [[sources/web-2025-09-16-709]].
