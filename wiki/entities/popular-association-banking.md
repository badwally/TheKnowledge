# Popular Association Banking (PAB)

## Summary

Popular Association Banking (PAB) is the community association banking division of Popular Bank, a subsidiary of Popular, Inc. (NASDAQ: BPOP) [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. Popular Bank is headquartered in New York and operates branches and offices across New York, New Jersey, and Florida; Popular, Inc. was founded in 1893 and ranks among the top 50 U.S. bank holding companies by assets [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. PAB is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
