# State Street Associates

## Summary

Investment-research arm of State Street; institutional home of two of the five co-authors of the 2022 paper "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" — Rajeev Bhargava (Managing Director and head of investor behavior research) and Travis Whitmore (Vice President, Senior Quantitative Researcher) [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key facts

- Listed addresses on the paper: 140 Mt. Auburn St, Cambridge, MA (Bhargava) and 18 Stephanstrasse, Frankfurt am Main, Germany (Whitmore) [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Houses an investor behavior research function led by Rajeev Bhargava [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
