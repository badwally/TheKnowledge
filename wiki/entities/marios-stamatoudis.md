# Marios Stamatoudis

## Summary

Discretionary trader and U.S. Investing Championship Top Performer 2023; co-author of the 2024 SSRN paper "The Power Of Price Action Reading," in which he serves as the experienced trader whose chart-pattern judgment is evaluated against a baseline algorithmic gap-trading strategy in an anonymized setting [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated as "U.S. Investing Championship — Top Performer 2023" on the paper [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Listed contact email info.stamatoudis@gmail.com and Twitter handle @stamatoudism [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Co-authored "The Power Of Price Action Reading" (June 28, 2024) with Carlo Zarattini [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Plays the role of "experienced technical trader" inside the paper's anonymized-chart experimental setup, tasked with restricting the algorithm to trade only those stocks whose daily charts appear more promising [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
- Also performs the micromanagement layer in the same bias-free environment — analyzing daily and intraday price action following the overnight gap to set precise entries, stop losses, and partial exits at predetermined intervals [[sources/pdf-1662a4368954]].
