# US Patent 12462305B2

## Summary

US Patent 12462305B2, titled "Data retrieval and validation for asset onboarding and deriving asset characteristics" (publication date 2025-07-03, active status, US authority), is a generic fintech / digital-securities asset-onboarding patent assigned to Trete Inc that covers data-retrieval, identifier-creation, and data-validation infrastructure for secondary-market platforms trading digital securities across diverse asset classes including real estate, private planes, yachts, antique cars, art, jewelry, insurance policies, racehorse-backed structured products, franchises, dividend-generating businesses, natural-resource ventures (uranium, timber, commodities), private credit, private debt, and intangible assets [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]]. The patent contains no reserve-study methodology, no condominium-specific content, no probabilistic component-deterioration modeling, no infrastructure-forecasting substance, and no engagement with any in-scope reserve-study regulatory regime (FL SIRS, CA Davis-Stirling § 5550, BC SPA, ON Condo Act 1998, NS Condominium Act, NB CPA c16.05, NL Condominium Act 2009 / NLR 80/11, QC Civil Code Bill 16, AB CPA, MB Condominium Act) [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]]. The patent is held in the condo-capital-infra vendor-watch corpus as negative-evidence disambiguation: any third-party assertion that US12462305B2 is a REcollab Technologies patent is contradicted by the record — the assignee is Trete Inc, and the subject matter sits in the digital-securities / capitalization-table / blockchain-payments fintech-IP territory rather than the building-systems / reserve-study / probabilistic-deterioration territory occupied by the condo-capital-infra engine [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].

### Subject matter scope (out of scope for condo-capital-infra)

- Self-described scope: "methods, systems, and computer program products for data retrieval and validation for asset onboarding and deriving asset characteristics. A first set of data associated with an asset is collected. Identifiers associated with the first set of data are created. A second set of data associated with the asset is collected based on the identifiers" — a generic data-pipeline-with-identifier-bridging description with no building-systems specificity [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].
- Asset-class scope is explicitly broad and not limited to real estate: the patent enumerates a non-exhaustive list including "investments in franchises, investments in business that generate dividends or returns based on performance of the business or underlying asset, investments in ventures that capture or mine natural resource such as, but not limited to uranium, timber, and other commodities, private credit, private debt, intangible assets, tradeable assets, and any other types of appropriate assets" [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].
- Enumerated "real asset" examples include office buildings, multi-family apartment buildings, car washes, private planes or yachts, antique cars, art, jewelry, insurance policies, and structured products based on underlying-asset performance (e.g., a racehorse) — confirming a deliberately heterogeneous asset-class scope that does not single out condominium common-element infrastructure [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].
- Framing: real-estate-as-secondary-market-platform / Financial Exchange — the patent positions real-estate digital-securities trading as a special case within a broader secondary-market-exchange framework rather than as a domain-specific specification [[sources/web-2025-07-03-d90]].
