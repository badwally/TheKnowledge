# RealPage OneSite

## Summary

RealPage OneSite is a property-management product from RealPage named as one of 17 accounting integration partners in BuildingLink's resident-engagement platform [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. The OneSite–BuildingLink integration runs on a 24-hour batch sync that propagates resident move-in/move-out information, contact information, and account balances from OneSite into BuildingLink [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].

## Key facts

- Named as an accounting integration partner in BuildingLink's accounting-integration catalog [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Syncs with BuildingLink every 24 hours [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- The 24-hour sync propagates resident move-ins/move-outs, contact information, and account balances from OneSite to BuildingLink [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- The integration eliminates the need for staff to add or remove residents on both platforms manually [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
