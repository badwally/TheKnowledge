# Adobe Research

## Summary

The corporate research arm of Adobe; host of the internship during which the 2025 NoLiMa long-context evaluation benchmark was developed and home of five of its seven co-authors [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].

## Key facts

- Hanieh Deilamsalehy, Franck Dernoncourt, Trung Bui, Ryan Rossi, and Seunghyun Yoon are listed as Adobe Research affiliates on the NoLiMa paper [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
- The NoLiMa work was performed during Ali Modarressi's internship at Adobe Research, in collaboration with LMU Munich [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
- The resulting paper, "NoLiMa: Long-Context Evaluation Beyond Literal Matching," was published at ICML 2025 [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
