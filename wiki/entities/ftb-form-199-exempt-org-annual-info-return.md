# FTB Form 199 — Exempt Organization Annual Information Return

## Summary

FTB Form 199, the *Exempt Organization Annual Information Return*, is the annual information return that a California tax-exempt homeowners' association with gross receipts above the small-organization threshold must file with the California Franchise Tax Board [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]]. The Form 199 obligation runs independent of the Form 100 corporate income-tax obligation — the amount and type of income received determine which forms are required [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]]. Smaller organizations (gross receipts normally ≤ $50,000 for tax years 2012 and later) file the electronic FTB 199N California e-Postcard in lieu of Form 199 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].

## Key facts

- Form number: FTB Form 199 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Title: *Exempt Organization Annual Information Return* [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Issued by the California Franchise Tax Board [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Filing requirement is independent of the Form 100 corporate income-tax filing requirement; the amount and type of income received are the determining factors [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Form 199 is required when gross receipts normally exceed $50,000 for tax years 2012 and later (the 2011-and-prior threshold was $25,000) [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Defines gross receipts as the total amounts received from all sources during the annual accounting period without subtracting any costs or expenses (including cost of goods sold, cost of operations, or expenses to earn/raise/collect such amounts) [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Gross-receipts averaging schedule (tax year 2012 and later, $50,000 threshold): one year or less → file if > $75,000; more than one year but less than three years → file if > $60,000 (average of current and prior tax year); three years or more → file if > $50,000 (average of current and immediate prior two tax years) [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Due date: 15th day of the 5th month after the close of the organization's tax year; for a December 31 year-end, May 15 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Automatic six-month extension granted if the organization is not suspended on the original due date and files the return on or before the extended due date [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- No filing fee required for returns due on or after 01/01/2021 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- For returns due before 01/01/2021, a $10 filing fee applies (rising to $25 if the fee is paid late) under R&TC §23772 [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Filing fee does not apply if average gross receipts are less than the filing threshold [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
- Delinquent Form 199 penalty: $5 per month or part of a month the return is late, maximum $40, imposed from the original due date regardless of whether the fee is paid (R&TC §23772) [[sources/web-2020-12-01-877]].
