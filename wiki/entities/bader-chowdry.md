# Bader A. Chowdry, CPA, CA, LPA

## Summary

Bader A. Chowdry is a Mississauga-area Ontario CPA whose credentials include CPA (Chartered Professional Accountant), CA (Chartered Accountant), and LPA (Licensed Public Accountant). He is the named author of the March 5, 2026 Insight Accounting CPA guide "Financial Reporting for Condominium Corporations in Ontario," a practitioner-facing consolidation of Ontario Condominium Act, 1998 financial-reporting obligations targeted at condominium boards, property managers, and professional corporations in the GTA [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].

## Key facts

- Credentialed CPA, CA, and LPA (Licensed Public Accountant) — the LPA designation is the Ontario credential authorizing public-accounting engagements [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Authored the March 5, 2026 guide "Financial Reporting for Condominium Corporations in Ontario: A Complete CPA Guide" published by Insight Accounting CPA [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Article scope spans the regulatory framework (Condominium Act 1998, O. Reg. 48/01, CPA Canada Handbook for NPOs, CAO Guidelines), financial-statement requirements under §66, audit-vs-review engagement decision under the 80% owner-waiver mechanism, reserve fund accounting under §93, dual-fund accounting structure, chart-of-accounts design, budget process under §94, investment restrictions under §115, status-certificate financial disclosures under §76, common reporting mistakes, and CMHC compliance criteria for insured properties [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Article also identifies CondoWorks as an Ontario-specific accounting software platform recommended alongside Yardi Voyager, Buildium, and AppFolio [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
