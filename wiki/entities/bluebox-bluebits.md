# BlueBox (BlueBits)

## Summary

BlueBox is a parcel locker product, operated by BlueBits (bluebits.ca), integrated with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental as one of three named smart-locker partners in the UpperBee integration portfolio [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]]. The product is positioned by UpperBee as a secure and efficient solution for receiving and managing deliveries [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Parcel locker product operated by BlueBits, integrated with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Marketed positioning: secure and efficient solution for receiving and managing deliveries [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- One of three named smart-locker partners in UpperBee's integration portfolio alongside Snaile Lockers and Expedibox [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Public-facing site (.ca TLD signals Canadian footprint): bluebits.ca [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
