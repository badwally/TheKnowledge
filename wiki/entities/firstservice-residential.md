# FirstService Residential

## Summary

FirstService Residential is a community association management (CAM) firm that operates its own proprietary, in-house technology stack — the cyan™ all-in-one association management platform — designed exclusively for use by its community managers, heart-of-house staff, and the board members and residents of the associations it manages [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]]. The cyan™ platform is positioned as a one-of-a-kind technology available only to FirstService Residential associates and customers, integrated with the HODA™ platform, and marketed as the channel through which FirstService delivers community association management services to its clients [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].

## Key facts

- Operates a proprietary, cloud-based digital platform — cyan™ — designed exclusively for FirstService Residential, with no third-party licensing channel disclosed [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Uses cyan™ as the technology backbone for delivering community association management services to its clients, with the platform consumed by community managers and heart-of-house staff [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Integrates cyan™ with the HODA™ platform; the cyan↔HODA integration is positioned in FirstService's marketing copy as making cyan a one-of-a-kind technology available only to FirstService Residential associates and customers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Stated user constituencies for the cyan™ platform: board members, residents, and associates (FirstService's term for its own employees) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Stated platform value proposition: enhance experiences and relationships within communities, simplify the lives of residents/board members/associates, and drive trust and client success [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
- Public-facing corporate URL for the cyan™ platform: fsresidential.com/corporate/cyan/ [[sources/web-2026-01-01-efb]].
