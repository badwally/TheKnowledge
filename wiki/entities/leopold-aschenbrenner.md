# Leopold Aschenbrenner

## Summary

Economist at Columbia University and the Global Priorities Institute (University of Oxford), and author of the September 2020 working paper "Existential Risk and Growth," which models existential risk as following a Kuznets-style inverted U-shape under directed technical change between a consumption sector and a safety sector [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Columbia University and the Global Priorities Institute, University of Oxford, at the time of writing the paper [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Authored "Existential Risk and Growth," Global Priorities Institute Working Paper No. 6-2020, dated September 30, 2020 (Version 0.6, Preliminary) [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Listed contact email on the paper is leopold.aschenbrenner@columbia.edu [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
- Acknowledges Philip Trammell for "generous mentorship and help with this project," and the Centre for Effective Altruism, the University of Oxford's Future of Humanity Institute, and Columbia ISERP for support [[sources/pdf-e7ff11e4ad9b]].
