# CCI Eastern Ontario Chapter (CCI-EO)

## Summary

The CCI Eastern Ontario Chapter (CCI-EO) is the Eastern Ontario chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute, hosting two named recurring events surfaced in UpperBee's 2026 conference-sponsorship marketing: the CCI Eastern Ontario Spring Conference in Kingston (attended by UpperBee for the fourth consecutive year as of 2026) and the CCI-EO Annual Golf Tournament (June 11, 2026) [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].

## Key facts

- Eastern Ontario chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- Hosts the CCI Eastern Ontario Spring Conference in Kingston, Ontario [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- Hosts the CCI-EO Annual Golf Tournament; the 2026 edition was scheduled for June 11, 2026 [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- UpperBee returned to the CCI Eastern Ontario Spring Conference for the fourth consecutive year as of 2026, indicating sustained vendor-marketing activity at the chapter level since at least 2023 [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
