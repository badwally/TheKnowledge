# Alibaba

## Summary

Chinese technology company whose AI lab develops the Qwen family of open-weight large language models; as of the March 2026 USCC "Two Loops" paper, Qwen accounted for the largest model ecosystem on Hugging Face with over 100,000 derivatives [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].

## Key facts

- Develops the Qwen open-weight LLM family, which had over 100,000 derivatives on Hugging Face as of March 2026 — the largest model ecosystem on the platform [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Released Qwen3-Max with a reported training compute of 1.5 × 10^25 FLOPs, training data of 36 trillion tokens, and approximately 1 trillion parameters — the leading Chinese model in 2025 by all three measures [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- One of the major Chinese cloud providers whose AI capital expenditure is benchmarked against U.S. hyperscalers; per Bloomberg Intelligence, combined 2025 AI capex for Alibaba, Tencent, Baidu, SenseTime, and Kingsoft Cloud was less than $40 billion, versus at least $350 billion for Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Google combined [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
