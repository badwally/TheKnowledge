# Chris Millner

## Summary

Chris Millner is Chief Product Officer at CINC Systems and the named CINC product executive who articulated the SmartProperty Report's functional thesis in the May 2023 integration announcement [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]]. Millner framed the SmartProperty Report as transforming how HOA and COA boards receive communication from their management companies and emphasized the real-time data synchronization between property information in CINC and SmartProperty as the core integrity guarantee for board decision-making [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].

## Key facts

- Title: Chief Product Officer at CINC Systems as of May 2023 [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- Articulated the SmartProperty Report's product thesis: "This report transforms the way HOA and COA boards receive communication from their management companies" [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- Emphasized real-time data synchronization between property information in CINC and SmartProperty as the load-bearing technical claim of the integration [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
- Framed the integration's value proposition as: "every decision that a board makes can be made with the confidence that the information provided to them is accurate and up-to-date" [[sources/web-2023-05-05-52f]].
