# Carlos Guzman

## Summary

Carlos Guzman is co-founder and CEO of ONR Applications, Inc., the resident engagement platform founded in 2018 that CINC Systems acquired on January 6, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]]. Guzman and ONR's full executive team joined the CINC team via the acquisition and became shareholders — a founders-retained-as-shareholders integration pattern that is the operative negotiation reference point for any AI-native reserve-study founder evaluating a future CINC-led acquisition under continued Hg Capital sponsorship [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]]. Guzman is the load-bearing named acquired-company counterparty whose public framing of the ONR-CINC combined platform — booking pickleball courts, hiring babysitters, receiving packages, seamlessly paying dues "with the simplicity and aesthetic expected in the digital age" — operationalizes the resident-experience-inward product positioning that CINC adopted post-acquisition [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].

## Key facts

- Co-founder and CEO of ONR Applications, Inc. at the time of the January 6, 2025 CINC Systems acquisition [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Named spokesperson on the January 6, 2025 CINC-ONR acquisition announcement [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Public framing of the unified ONR-CINC vision at acquisition: "We are thrilled to join CINC and share a unified vision for enhancing the resident experience. Imagine a platform where residents can book a pickleball court, hire a babysitter, receive a package, or seamlessly pay their dues — all with the simplicity and aesthetic expected in the digital age. This is the future we're creating together" [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
- Joined CINC as part of the founders-retained-as-shareholders integration pattern — "Its co-founders and executive team will all join the CINC team and become shareholders" — establishing Guzman's post-acquisition equity participation in CINC under Hg Capital sponsorship [[sources/web-2025-01-06-1e2]].
