# Riadh Zaatour

## Summary

Researcher at Quincy Data; co-author with Stéphane Tyč of the December 2013 paper "The Fed Robbery Revisited," which uses exchange timestamp forensics to demonstrate that the September 18, 2013 FOMC "no taper" announcement was released under embargo simultaneously at the CME and Nasdaq colocation centers rather than from the Washington DC lockup [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].

## Key facts

- Listed first of two authors on "The Fed Robbery Revisited" (Quincy Data, December 4, 2013) [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
- Affiliated with Quincy Data [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
- Devised a corrected-timestamp methodology combining CME millisecond-precision market timestamps with Quincy Data's microsecond-precision pcap timestamps to recover microsecond resolution in market clocks, enabling the paper's key forensic conclusions [[sources/pdf-fa83c61dfa2d]].
