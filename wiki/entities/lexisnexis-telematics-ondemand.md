# LexisNexis Telematics OnDemand

## Summary

LexisNexis® Telematics OnDemand is the flagship, award-winning insurance-facing product built on top of the LexisNexis Telematics Exchange platform; it gives insurers a single real-time access point to multi-sourced telematics data at point of quote, underwriting, and renewal for premium pricing [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].

## Key facts

- Telematics OnDemand is described by LexisNexis Risk Solutions as its flagship, award-winning telematics solution [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- The product gives insurers a single access point to multi-sourced telematics data in real time at point of quote, underwriting, and renewal [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- The seamless data flow it enables is positioned as supporting faster access to potential benefits and a more transparent relationship between insurer and end consumer [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- Telematics OnDemand depends on the LexisNexis Telematics Exchange platform for data ingestion and normalization across heterogeneous OEM and telematics-provider sources [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
- The product is positioned as a way for insurers to apply telematics-derived insights to better price premiums [[sources/web-2026-01-01-dec]].
