# Fangxin Wang

## Summary

Researcher at the School of Computing Science, Simon Fraser University, and lead author of MacoCache, the multi-agent deep reinforcement learning framework for cooperative mobile edge caching of video content [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the School of Computing Science at Simon Fraser University, Canada, on the MacoCache paper [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].
- Listed as the first author of "Intelligent Video Caching at Network Edge: A Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach," alongside Feng Wang (University of Mississippi), Jiangchuan Liu (SFU), Ryan Shea (SFU), and Lifeng Sun (Tsinghua University) [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].
