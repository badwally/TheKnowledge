# Will MacKay, CFP, CIM

## Summary

Will MacKay is an Investment Advisor and Portfolio Manager at The MacKay Financial Group, a practice within CIBC Wood Gundy based in Mississauga, Ontario. He authored the Summer 2022 CCI GHC Condo News Magazine article "Investing the Reserve Fund: How to Maximize Return and Minimize Risk," which translates Ontario Condominium Act §115(5) eligible-securities constraints into a practitioner-facing GIC laddering and liquidity-planning framework for condominium boards [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].

## Key facts

- Holds the CFP (Certified Financial Planner) and CIM (Chartered Investment Manager) designations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Title: Investment Advisor, Portfolio Manager [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Practice: The MacKay Financial Group [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Affiliated with CIBC Wood Gundy in Mississauga, Ontario [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Authored "Investing the Reserve Fund: How to Maximize Return and Minimize Risk," published June 22, 2022 in the Volume 12 / Summer 2022 issue of the CCI GHC Condo News Magazine [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Framed the reserve-fund investment problem around three priorities in stated order: (i) principal protection and adherence to the Condominium Act, (ii) liquidity and meeting expenditure requirements, and (iii) maximizing returns [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Characterized special assessments, fee increases, and improved investment returns as the only three ways to add money to the reserve fund, and explicitly prefers investment improvement as the starting point [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Recommends combining duration-matched GICs (or HISAs) against known capital projects with a 1-, 3-, or 5-year laddered portfolio of GICs against general reserves, with 3- or 5-year ladders identified as the most effective duration choices given minimal yield differences across 3-, 4-, and 5-year terms as of May 17, 2022 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Recommends that boards engage an investment partner to evaluate the cost of waiting for rate increases rather than trying to time interest-rate cycles directly [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Recommends using brokerage-purchased GICs to stack CDIC coverage across multiple member institutions while obtaining higher rates than direct branch offerings [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Disclosure: views expressed in the CCI GHC article do not necessarily reflect those of CIBC World Markets Inc. [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
