# Cifas

## Summary

Cifas is a UK not-for-profit organisation that operates the UK's largest cross-sector fraud-sharing databases, working to reduce and prevent fraud and financial crime in the UK [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]]. It is a competitor-pooling data consortium of 500+ member organisations — including direct banking competitors — that contribute proprietary fraud markers into a shared National Fraud Database [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].

## Key facts

- Jurisdiction: United Kingdom [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Legal form: Not-for-profit organisation [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Stated mission: Reduce and prevent fraud and financial crime in the UK [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Membership scale: 500+ member organisations across multiple sectors [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Membership composition includes direct banking competitors, satisfying the competitor-pooling inclusion criterion for the data-collectives domain rather than purely complementary data exchange between non-rivals [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Shared infrastructure: The National Fraud Database, into which members contribute proprietary fraud markers [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Database scope: Cross-sector rather than single-industry [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Application domain: Identity fraud prevention, financial-crime detection, and protective registration services [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
- Positioning in the taxonomy: A real-world instance of a third-party-intermediary data utility in which direct competitors pool proprietary data for a shared anti-fraud purpose, operating under UK fraud-prevention legal exemptions [[sources/web-2026-05-21-5db]].
