# Cycling Corsica Self-Guided Luxury (Europe Active)

## Summary

The Cycling Corsica Self-Guided Luxury route is [[entities/europe-active|Europe Active]]'s 7-day, 6-night self-guided road-cycling holiday on [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] — running point-to-point from Bastia in the northeast to Calvi in the northwest, with luggage transferred between hotels each day and routes delivered via the operator's smartphone app [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].

## Key facts

- 7 days / 6 nights, with five cycling days bookended by an arrival transfer (Day 1) and a breakfast-and-depart morning (Day 7) [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Departures run daily from the beginning of April to the end of October, with July and August flagged as 'really hot' and best ridden early in the day [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Stage sequence: Day 1 arrival in Bastia old town; Day 2 transfer to Erbalunga then ride up the east side of Cap Corse, over to the Moulin de Mattei windmill and down to Centuri (47 km / +559 m, or 61 km / +856 m); Day 3 Centuri → Saint-Florent via Nonza, Albo, and Patrimonio (58 km / +823 m); Day 4 Saint-Florent rest day with optional 50 km Nebbiu / Conca d'Oro loop (+816 m); Day 5 Saint-Florent → Ile Rousse via the Désert des Agriates and the Vezzu pass (48 / 69 / 80 km variants); Day 6 Ile Rousse → Calvi via the La Balagne hill villages — Pigna, Sant'Antonino, Aregno, Cateri (22 / 30 / 35 km variants); Day 7 disperse from Calvi after breakfast [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Average daily distance is around 50 km, with 2–3 route options each day to flex distance and climbing [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Sold as a [[concepts/self-guided-cycling-tour|self-guided cycling tour]] — Europe Active provides routes, GPS tracks, and a daily 08.00–20.00 hotline, but riders are not accompanied by a guide on-route [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Headline price starts at €2,250 per person; single-room supplement is €1,335; high-season departures (15 June – 15 September) carry a €575 supplement, rising to a €1,975 single-room supplement in high season [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Bike rental is sold as an add-on, separate from the tour price: hybrid €320, electric €460, aluminium road €510, carbon road €560 per person [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Included in the tour price: airport transfer from Bastia on Day 1, six nights in 3-, 4-, and 5-star hotels, six breakfasts, daily luggage transfers, and the route-notes / map / GPS-track app (smartphone required) [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Not included: bike rental, the transfer from the hotel in Calvi at trip end, and any transfer not enumerated in the program [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Marketed as accommodating mixed pace by offering 2–3 route options per day rather than a single fixed itinerary, similar in spirit to the [[concepts/flexible-route-cycling-tour|flexible-route cycling tour]] format used by guided operators [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Hotel character is curated tier-by-tier: Bastia 4* in the citadelle overlooking the harbour; Centuri 'Maison de Maître' style hotel by the harbour; Saint-Florent 4–5* for two nights; Ile Rousse central 4*; Calvi 5* near the citadelle [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Group minimum is 2 persons [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
