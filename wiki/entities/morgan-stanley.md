# Morgan Stanley

## Summary

Global financial-services firm and parent of Counterpoint Global, the research and asset-management group that publishes the Consilient Observer series; copyright holder of the June 4, 2024 Counterpoint Global Insights report "Stock Market Concentration: How Much Is Too Much?" [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].

## Key facts

- The June 4, 2024 Counterpoint Global Insights report is published with the legend "© 2024 Morgan Stanley. All rights reserved. 6652630 Exp 6/30/2025" [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Authors of that report (Michael J. Mauboussin and Dan Callahan, CFA) use morganstanley.com email addresses [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
