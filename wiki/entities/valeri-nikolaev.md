# Valeri V. Nikolaev

## Summary

Researcher at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business; co-author of the 2024 paper "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models," which probes the boundaries of LLM capability in numeric financial-statement analysis and shows that GPT-4 Turbo with chain-of-thought prompting outperforms median analyst forecasts of earnings direction [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Chicago Booth School of Business; contact email valeri.nikolaev@chicagobooth.edu [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- Co-author of "Financial Statement Analysis with Large Language Models" (May 20, 2024 draft) with Alex G. Kim and Maximilian Muhn [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- The paper's full sample spans 1968-2021 with 150,678 firm-year observations from 15,401 distinct firms; the analyst sample spans 1983-2021 with 39,533 observations from 3,152 distinct firms [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
- The paper concludes that LLMs may take a central role in financial decision-making — extending beyond textual tasks into numeric judgment [[sources/pdf-2329b8b436f2]].
