# CheckAlt

## Summary

CheckAlt is named by the AppFolio HOA Financials platform as one of five HOA-segment banking and check-processing partners that AppFolio integrates with to provide automated lockbox, bank statements, AR check images, auto reconciliation, and Positive Pay to community-association customers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]]. CheckAlt is the only non-bank entity in the AppFolio HOA partner roster, positioning it as a specialized lockbox/check-processing provider rather than a chartered depository.

## Key facts

- Listed by AppFolio as an HOA integration partner in the HOA Financials product narrative [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- The AppFolio integration is positioned as providing automated lockbox, bank statements, Accounts Receivable (AR) check images, auto reconciliation, and Positive Pay [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- Appears in AppFolio's narrative partner enumeration as "CheckAlt" and in the partner-icon list with a space ("Check Alt"); both refer to the same vendor [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
- Distinguished from the other four AppFolio HOA partners (Alliance Association Bank, Pacific Premier Bank, First Citizens Bank, SmartStreet/Banc of California) by being a check-processing/lockbox specialist rather than a chartered bank [[sources/web-2026-01-01-2fd]].
