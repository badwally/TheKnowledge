# Jerome Powell

## Summary

Federal Reserve Chair whose August 2022 effort to establish credibility as an inflation fighter Zoltan Pozsar characterizes as a "general mobilization" of Paul Volcker's legacy — and whose place in economic history Pozsar argues will depend on the course of the economic war between the U.S., China, and Russia, with Powell remembered "as Paul Volcker or Arthur Burns" depending on the outcome [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].

## Key facts

- Identified in Pozsar's August 1, 2022 dispatch as Chair of the Federal Reserve, with inflation as his "singular focus du jour" [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
- Pozsar describes Powell as "trying hard to establish his credibility as an inflation fighter — a 'general mobilization' of Paul Volcker's legacy" — and writes that he will "either succeed in doing that or cause long-term damage to the Fed's credibility" [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
- Whether Powell will be remembered by economic historians "as Paul Volcker or Arthur Burns depends on the course of the economic war," per Pozsar [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
- Has, per Pozsar, made inflation "public enemy number one" in line with President Biden's framing — a stance that orients the Fed away from past hesitations and toward unrestricted demand destruction [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
