# Qiji Jim Zhu

## Summary

Mathematician at the Department of Mathematics, Western Michigan University; co-author of the 2015 paper "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules," which proves the optimality of a trend-following all-in / all-out strategy in a bull-bear regime-switching market with partial information [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Department of Mathematics, Western Michigan University (Kalamazoo, MI), on the paper [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- Co-author of "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules" with Min Dai, Zhou Yang, and Qing Zhang [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
