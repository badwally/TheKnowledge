# Corsican Highlights (Marmot Tours)

## Summary

'Corsican Highlights' is [[entities/marmot-tours|Marmot Tours]]'s 6-day September road-cycling holiday on [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] — a flexible-route, vehicle-supported itinerary running clockwise from Bastia to Solenzara via St-Florent, Calvi, Porto, and Corte, with each day offering Classic, Challenge, and (on some days) Easier route options [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].

## Key facts

- 6 cycling days plus travel-in and travel-out, run in September, with Bastia airport (BIA) as the standard arrival and departure point [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Stage sequence: Day 1 Bastia → St-Florent (around Cap Corse and over the Col de la Serra); Day 2 St-Florent → Calvi (Désert des Agriates, Col de San-Colombano, balcony road); Day 3 Calvi → Porto (NW coast and Col de Palmarella); Day 4 Porto → Corte (over the [[entities/col-de-vergio|Col de Vergio]]); Day 5 Corte → Solenzara (Col de Bellagranajo and Col de Sorba); Day 6 day-ride from Solenzara to the [[entities/col-de-bavella|Col de Bavella]] [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Hotels are Calavita at 39 Route du Cap, 20200 Pietranera (Bastia) on the start side and Hôtel Maquis & Mer in Solenzara on the finish side; four of the trip's hotels have pools [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Riding the Challenge route every day totals 850 km+ with ~15,950 m of ascent over the week — Marmot itself notes this is comparable to the Raid Corsica with more flexibility [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Marmot rates the Challenge variant as similar in difficulty to its Picos & Angliru and Andalusia Challenge tours [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Day 1 Challenge tops out via Col de la Serra (365 m) and the San Stefano detour (155 km / +2,100 m) [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Day 2 Challenge adds Col de Bataille (1,096 m), Col de Salvi, and Bocca di Neraghia (138 km / +2,300 m); the Easier variant is 90 km / +900 m and the Classic is 105 km / +1,300 m on the same St-Florent → Calvi stage [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Day 3 Challenge adds Col de Lava (491 m), Piana, and Col de Sevi (1,105 m) (172 km / +3,000 m) [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Day 4 Classic pairs the Col de Vergio with the Col de Ominanda (654 m) for 84 km / +1,750 m; the Challenge variant detours via Col Santàntone (993 m) for 132 km / +2,450 m [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Day 5 Challenge tops out via the Col de Sorba (1,314 m) and the Nazza/Fiumorbo/Isolaccio loop for 133 km / +3,300 m [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Day 6 Challenge chains Bavella E + a Zonza loop into the mountainous interior + Bavella W for 121 km / +2,800 m [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- The route is two-guide, support-van supported and uses the Marmot Tours App plus RWGPS-based turn-by-turn directions for navigation [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Marmot positions it as accessible to 'healthy cyclists, regardless of experience in the mountains' via the Classic route, while the Challenge route exists for riders wanting to push themselves [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
