# John Liew

## Summary

Longtime collaborator with Cliff Asness; co-author with Asness of a 2014 Institutional Investor piece proposing to inject the word "reasonable" into EMH testing, and a co-introducer of the "value spread" in Asness, Friedman, Krail, and Liew (2000) [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].

## Key facts

- Co-author with Cliff Asness of a 2014 Institutional Investor piece on EMH (Asness and Liew, 2014), proposing the modest reform of injecting the word "reasonable" into the joint-hypothesis test of EMH [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].
- Co-author with Asness, Friedman, and Krail of the 2000 paper that introduced the "value spread" concept during the dot-com bubble [[sources/pdf-c25a6984aba0]].
