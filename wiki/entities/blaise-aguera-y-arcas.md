# Blaise Agüera y Arcas

## Summary

AI researcher at Google's Paradigms of Intelligence Team and the Santa Fe Institute; co-author of the 2026 essay "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion" and author of the MIT Press book "What Is Intelligence? Lessons from AI About Evolution, Computing, and Minds" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Paradigms of Intelligence Team at Google and the Santa Fe Institute on the paper [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Co-authored "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion" (2026) with James Evans and Benjamin Bratton [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Author of "What Is Intelligence? Lessons from AI About Evolution, Computing, and Minds" (MIT Press, 2025), which the paper invokes as a frame for treating AI development as a major evolutionary transition [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Listed as a co-author on "Reasoning Models Generate Societies of Thought" (Kim, Lai, Scherrer, Agüera y Arcas, Evans; arXiv:2601.10825, 2026), the empirical basis for the society-of-thought claim [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Co-author of the Antikythera Substack pieces "The Silicon Interior: What Do Agents Believe?" and "Agentic AI's Emergent Phenomenological Lexicon" (both 2026, with Bratton and Evans) [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
