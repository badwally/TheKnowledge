# OctoAI Technologies Corp

## Summary

OctoAI Technologies Corp is a Vancouver, British Columbia-based company that self-describes as "a leading provider of condo document review tools"; its flagship product is Eli Report, an AI-powered condo document review platform for real estate professionals, buyers and owners that uses a proprietary AI system enhanced with summaries from OpenAI's ChatGPT to transform hundreds of pages of condo documents into a single, clear report [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]]. On October 7, 2024, OctoAI announced the transition of Eli Report to a freemium pricing model across North America, with the company having delivered over 25,000 reports to thousands of users across the US and Canada as of that date [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]]. CEO Thomas Beattie framed the freemium model as a strategic move to commoditize the baseline document-review tier while preserving paid revenue from advanced capabilities and access to OctoAI's expert condo data review team [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]]. OctoAI also operates a second product line — the Annual Benchmark Report — serving property managers, condo boards, and strata councils with deeper insights for budgets, capital planning, and savings-opportunity identification, establishing a structural adjacency to the reserve-fund-study advisory channel relevant to the condo-capital-infra GTM under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]]. The October 19, 2023 launch of Eli Report's Special Levy Forecast tool — vendor-framed at the time as "its most powerful tool yet" — is the load-bearing earliest primary-source data point in the OctoAI product-development timeline, establishing approximately 12 months of operational history on the company's flagship special-assessment-forecasting capability before the October 2024 freemium-pricing transition and confirming a Realtor-anchored audience-targeting posture that the October 2024 freemium launch later expanded to include lawyers, lenders, and property managers [[sources/web-2023-10-19-a69]] [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].

## Key facts

### Corporate identity

- Headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada — the dateline of the company's October 7, 2024 PRWeb / PRNewswire freemium-launch press release [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Self-description: "a leading provider of condo document review tools" [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Stated mission framing from About section: "a leader in providing automated condo document review to Realtors, buyers, owners, and property managers" [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Phone number at the October 2024 press release: 1 604-800-9084 (Vancouver area code) [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Primary corporate web property: www.elireport.com — operates the company under the Eli Report consumer-facing brand rather than the OctoAI Technologies Corp legal name [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].

### Leadership (as of October 2024)

- Thomas Beattie serves as CEO; named in the October 7, 2024 freemium-launch press release as the company spokesperson [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Beattie is also the named Media Contact for the freemium-launch announcement [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].

### Products

- **Eli Report** (flagship): AI-powered condo document review platform for real estate professionals, buyers, and owners, enabling quick and efficient review of condo documents while serving as a client-informing resource [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- **Annual Benchmark Report** (institutional tier): a separate product line serving property managers, condo boards, and strata councils with deeper insights for budgets, capital planning, and identifying potential savings opportunities — distinct from the transactional per-document review flow [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- **Special Levy Forecast** (Eli Report feature, launched October 19, 2023): a vendor-framed deterministic-methodology special-assessment-forecasting tool that uses three named inputs (depreciation report / reserve study, CRF / reserve fund balance, annual budget CRF contributions) to project the timing, amount, and primary expenditure for upcoming special levies on a dollar-per-unit average basis — vendor-framed at launch as "its most powerful tool yet" within the Eli Report platform, predating the October 2024 freemium pivot by approximately one year and later incorporated into Section 6 of the October 31, 2024 canonical product page taxonomy [[sources/web-2023-10-19-a69]].

### AI stack architecture

- Hybrid AI stack combining a proprietary OctoAI AI system with summaries from OpenAI's ChatGPT — combining in-house extraction with third-party LLM-generated summarization rather than building entirely on either layer alone [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- All users (free and paid) receive access to the proprietary AI system, ChatGPT-enhanced summaries, and the side-by-side document viewer [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Paid tiers add advanced capabilities and access to the company's "expert condo data review team" — a human-in-the-loop quality layer over the AI extraction baseline [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- The Special Levy Forecast tool's October 2023 launch surface explicitly characterizes the methodology layered on top of the AI extraction as deterministic point-estimate forecasting ("While our extraction is powered by AI, this feature is not a guess, it's simple math using the best available information") — establishing that OctoAI's AI stack is positioned as an extraction-and-presentation layer, with the underlying forecasting logic explicitly framed as deterministic math rather than probabilistic modeling [[sources/web-2023-10-19-a69]].

### Product-development cadence (October 2023 → October 2024)

- October 19, 2023: Special Levy Forecast tool launched on dedicated product page (elireport.com/products/special-levy-forecast/), vendor-framed at the time as "its most powerful tool yet" within the Eli Report platform, with a Realtor-primary audience framing and secondary positioning for condo owners and buyers [[sources/web-2023-10-19-a69]].
- October 7, 2024: Eli Report transitioned to freemium pricing model across North America (PRWeb / PRNewswire), expanding the audience perimeter to include lawyers, lenders, and property managers alongside the original Realtor/owner/buyer base [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- October 31, 2024: Canonical product page published at elireport.com/products/condo-document-review/ consolidating the prior standalone Special Levy Forecast feature into Section 6 of a six-section automated-reporting feature taxonomy, surfacing the broader product scope than the freemium-launch press release [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- The 12-day gap between the October 7, 2024 freemium-launch press release and the October 31, 2024 canonical product page surfaces a sequenced launch architecture in which the press-release surface establishes the freemium pricing model and the canonical product page operationalizes the full feature taxonomy at the product-page level [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- The approximately 12-month gap between the October 19, 2023 Special Levy Forecast launch and the October 7, 2024 freemium pivot establishes a documented operating history on the flagship special-assessment-forecasting feature predating the freemium-pricing transition — surfacing OctoAI as a vendor that operated a paid-only special-assessment-forecasting capability for approximately one year before commoditizing it under the freemium tier [[sources/web-2023-10-19-a69]] [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].

### October 2024 freemium-model transition

- Effective October 7, 2024, OctoAI transitioned Eli Report to a freemium pricing model designed to offer greater accessibility and flexibility across Realtors, condo buyers, lawyers, owners, and property managers seeking quick and thorough document analysis and insights [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Geographic scope at freemium launch: "anyone across North America" can access Eli Report's essential condo document review tools for free [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Beattie's stated rationale for the freemium pivot: "We've built a powerful AI condo document review system that processes documents quickly and accurately without the time and cost of human review. Our new free tier empowers everyone in the condo market to quickly understand their community… Realtors, buyers, owners and managers now have the flexibility to choose the level of service that suits them, while still receiving the trusted and thorough reports that have become synonymous with Eli Report" [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].

### Scale and market footprint (as of October 2024)

- Delivered over 25,000 Eli Reports to thousands of users across the US and Canada as of the October 7, 2024 freemium launch [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- Operates with an explicit North America (US + Canada) addressable market positioning rather than Canada-only [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra GTM

- OctoAI is a Vancouver, BC-headquartered AI-native condo software company that has achieved North America freemium scale (25,000+ reports, thousands of users across US/Canada) operating under a single named-CEO founder profile — a direct comparator for the condo-capital-infra Canada-first GTM sequence under ADR-0004 and a precedent that a Canadian-headquartered AI-native condo software entrant can scale into the US market via a freemium consumer/realtor tier [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- The Annual Benchmark Report product line — explicitly serving property managers, condo boards, and strata councils with budgets, capital planning, and savings-opportunity insights — surfaces OctoAI as a structural competitive perimeter in the institutional capital-planning channel relevant to an AI-native reserve-study firm, even though OctoAI's flagship Eli Report is positioned for transactional document review rather than reserve-study production [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- The hybrid AI stack (proprietary system + ChatGPT-enhanced summaries) is a competitive-architecture data point: OctoAI operationally validates a build-plus-buy approach to LLM tooling rather than full in-house model development, framing a build-vs-buy reference point for the condo-capital-infra engine's own AI-component decisions [[sources/web-2024-10-07-033]].
- The October 2023 Special Levy Forecast launch's explicit "simple math, not a guess" deterministic-methodology framing — paired with OctoAI's hybrid AI stack architecture — surfaces the vendor's methodological boundary: AI is positioned as an extraction-and-presentation enabler, while the forecasting layer itself is deterministic point-estimate math, leaving probabilistic methodology as unoccupied competitive territory available to an AI-native reserve-study firm operating against the same Canadian-condo special-assessment-forecasting use case [[sources/web-2023-10-19-a69]].
