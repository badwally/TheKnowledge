# TopoLM

## Summary

TopoLM (2025) is a language model whose authors added a simple spatial smoothness loss to the standard next-token prediction objective and showed that this single training-objective tweak "predicts the emergence of a spatially organized cortical language system as well as the organization of functional clusters selective for fine-grained linguistic features empirically observed in human cortex" [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].

## Key facts

- Adds a simple spatial smoothness loss to the usual next-token objective; no other architectural change is required to elicit cortex-like spatial organization [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
- The trained model exhibits the emergence of a spatially organized cortical language system whose functional clusters are selective for fine-grained linguistic features observed in the human cortex [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
- Cited as the 2025-vintage anchor of a three-study arc (Goldstein et al. 2022 ECoG → 2024 LLM/Functional-Brain-Network study → TopoLM 2025) that progressively tightens the brain-LLM organizational-alignment claim [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
- Provides one of the strongest empirical demonstrations that brain-like organization in LLMs can be elicited by adding biologically-motivated loss terms rather than baked-in architecture, supporting the universality/compression account of convergence [[sources/web-2026-05-27-cee]].
