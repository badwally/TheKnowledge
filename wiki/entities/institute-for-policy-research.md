# Institute for Policy Research (IPR)

## Summary

Policy-research institute at Northwestern University; publisher of the Working Paper Series in which Galvin & Thurston's "The Limits of Policy Feedback as a Party-Building Tool" appears as WP-18-16 (Version: June 20, 2018) [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]].

## Key facts

- Publisher of the Working Paper Series in which the source paper is issued under the identifier WP-18-16 [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]].
- Daniel J. Galvin is identified on the paper as an IPR Fellow [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]].
- Chloe N. Thurston is identified on the paper as an IPR Associate [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]].
- The paper is marked DRAFT with the instruction "Please do not quote or distribute without permission," indicating IPR working papers in this series circulate for discussion before formal publication [[sources/pdf-5e6d63eac790]].
