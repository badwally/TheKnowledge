# David Greenberg

## Summary

David Greenberg is a Partner at JMI Equity, the growth equity software investor that has been a minority investor in Vantaca and continued in the cap table after the October 15, 2025 $300+ million growth round led by Cove Hill Partners at a $1.25 billion valuation [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. He serves as JMI's named partner-relationship lead on the Vantaca investment [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].

## Key facts

- Title: Partner at JMI Equity as of October 15, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Named relationship lead on JMI's Vantaca investment [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Characterized JMI's prior tenure with Vantaca as a privilege spanning "the past few years through a period of tremendous growth and execution" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Stated post-Cove Hill-round intent: continue partnering with the Vantaca team as they accelerate innovation and extend market share in the community association management industry [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
