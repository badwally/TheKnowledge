# UpperBee Track&Fix

## Summary

Track&Fix is UpperBee's named property inspection solution, marketed to real estate professionals, developers, homeowners, and customer service teams as a tool for improving collaborator communication and accelerating corrective actions [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]]. UpperBee positions Track&Fix as suitable for pre-delivery inspections, rental inspections, building visits, and mandatory inspections [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].

## Key facts

- Property inspection solution operated by UpperBee, distinct from the UpperBee core all-in-one accounting & property management platform [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Target user archetypes: real estate professionals, developers, homeowners, and customer service teams [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Stated value proposition: improve communication between collaborators and accelerate corrective actions [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Named use cases: pre-delivery inspections, rental inspection, building visits, and mandatory inspections [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
- Product is surfaced as a separate journey-continuation tile on the UpperBee homepage, indicating it is sold or adopted independently of the core platform [[sources/web-2025-07-16-ce4]].
