# HubSpot Excellence

## Summary

HubSpot Excellence is a YouTube channel and consultancy brand operated by Jasper, a self-described certified HubSpot consultant, publishing implementation-grade walkthroughs of HubSpot architecture and sales-process automation [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]]. The brand also fronts a newsletter that distributes written SOPs corresponding to the channel's video walkthroughs [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].

## Key facts

- YouTube channel: `youtube.com/@HubSpotExcellence` [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
- Run by Jasper, a certified HubSpot consultant [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
- Content focus: HubSpot architecture and workflow patterns — e.g. operationalizing the MEDDIC sales-qualification framework via association labels, segments, and deal tags [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
- Distribution model: YouTube videos paired with written SOPs delivered via the HubSpot Excellence newsletter [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
- Service offering: consulting engagements for organizations whose HubSpot portals are technically messy or whose sales teams are not following qualification process [[sources/yt-PmViuQw2fNs]].
