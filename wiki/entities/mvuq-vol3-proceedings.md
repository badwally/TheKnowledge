# Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3 (IMAC 2025 Proceedings)

## Summary

A 2025 conference proceedings volume — the third of twelve — from the 43rd IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics, published by River Publishers in the Society for Experimental Mechanics conference proceedings series. The volume collects contributions on fundamental and applied aspects of Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification.

## Key facts

- The volume is titled 'Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3: Proceedings of the 43rd IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics, 2025' [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- It is published in the River Publishers Series in Conference Proceedings of the Society for Experimental Mechanics Series [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The editors are Roland Platz, Garrison Flynn, Kyle Neal, and Scott Ouellette [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The print ISBN is 9788743801481 and the e-ISBN is 9788743801603 [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The volume is the third of twelve volumes from the 43rd IMAC conference [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- Article 5 of the volume is 'Degradation Model Correction of Miter Gates Through Synthesis of Hybrid Modeling with Recursive Bayesian State Estimation' by Zhao Zhao, Yichao Zeng, Michael D. Todd, and Zhen Hu, with DOI 10.13052/97887-438-0148-1_5 [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
