# Sugata Ray

## Summary

Finance researcher at the University of Alabama, Culverhouse College of Business; co-author of "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" (2020, SSRN id 3392130), which finds that hedge fund managers who win poker tournaments exhibit lower turnover and less susceptibility to the disposition effect, yet the alpha signal they represent is self-defeating because the investor inflows it attracts erode post-tournament fund performance by 40–70 bp per month [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Alabama, Culverhouse College of Business, Box 870224, Tuscaloosa, AL 34587 [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Contact email: sugata.ray@cba.ua.edu; phone: +1-205-348-5726 [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Co-author of "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" (2020) with Yan Lu and Sandra Mortal [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Co-author of Brown, Lu, Ray, and Teo (2018), cited in "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" for documenting the correlation between managers' personal indiscretions and excessive risk taking [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Co-author of Lu, Ray, and Teo (2016), cited in "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" for documenting distraction arising from fund managers' romantic relationships [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
