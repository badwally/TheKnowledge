# Umpqua Bank

## Summary

Umpqua Bank is a US chartered depository institution that merged with Pacific Premier Bank on August 31, 2025; the combined institution will subsequently be rebranded as Columbia Bank with over $70 billion in combined assets [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].

## Key facts

- Officially merged with Pacific Premier Bank on August 31, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The post-merger combined institution will be rebranded as Columbia Bank later in 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The combined institution will hold over $70 billion in combined assets [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
- The post-merger Columbia Bank entity is positioned to continue serving the HOA industry while enhancing technology and resources, having adopted Pacific Premier's Community Association Banking division leadership and staff [[sources/web-2025-08-28-b49]].
