# Michael Thaler

## Summary

Economist in the Department of Economics at University College London; co-author with Ned Augenblick and Eben Lazarus of "Overinference from Weak Signals and Underinference from Strong Signals" (September 2024), a paper that was circulated in an early version as Thaler (2021) and whose core hypothesis — that people overinfer from weak signals and underinfer from strong signals due to imprecise estimation of signal strength — is confirmed across abstract experiments, a naturalistic NBA basketball experiment, sports betting markets, and financial markets [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
