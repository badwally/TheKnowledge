# Osher Center for Integrative Health

## Summary

University of California, San Francisco integrative-health center whose free guided-imagery and meditation resources (osher.ucsf.edu/guided-imagery-meditation-resources) and 8-week MBSR public class are recommended in Dr. Carla Kuon's 2023 Long COVID Part 3 group medical visit as vagal-toning resources for Long COVID patients [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key facts

- Hosts a free "guided-imagery meditation resources" page at osher.ucsf.edu/guided-imagery-meditation-resources, listed as a recommended free vagal-toning resource [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Hosts free "public classes" at osher.ucsf.edu/public-classes, also listed as a recommended resource [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Offers an Osher MBSR Class — an 8-week stress reduction course costing $395 — based on John Kabat Zin's MBSR curriculum, recommended in Dr. Kuon's vagal-toning slide [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Listed alongside Belleruth Naparsteck guided imagery, Stasis.life, the Andrew Weil 4-7-8 breath video, CALM, INSIGHT TIMER, and Headspace as Dr. Kuon's recommended vagal-toning resources [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
