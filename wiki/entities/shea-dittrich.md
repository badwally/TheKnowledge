# Shea Dittrich

## Summary

Shea Dittrich is the Chief Revenue Officer of CINC Systems as of June 2023, named as CINC's principal executive spokesperson for the June 2023 TresRE launch and for the value proposition CINC markets to its existing accounting-software customers around the time savings of in-software banking [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

- Holds the title Chief Revenue Officer at CINC Systems as of June 2023 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Quoted in the June 2023 TresRE launch announcement framing the product's market opportunity and the "swivel chair" problem TresRE is designed to eliminate [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Cited the average CINC customer as saving "hundreds of hours per week" banking inside CINC's accounting software [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- Characterized the swivel-chair approach to reconciling numerous accounts at the end of the month as debilitating and as requiring teams of people across multiple departments [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
