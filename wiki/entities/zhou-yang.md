# Zhou Yang

## Summary

Mathematician at the School of Mathematical Sciences, South China Normal University; co-author of the 2015 paper "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules," which characterizes the optimal trend-following all-in / all-out strategy in a bull-bear regime-switching market through HJB equations [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the School of Mathematical Sciences, South China Normal University, Guangzhou, on the paper [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- Co-author of "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules" with Min Dai, Qing Zhang, and Qiji Jim Zhu [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
