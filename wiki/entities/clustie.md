# Clustie

## Summary

Clustie (full product name "Clustie: AI Marketing Segments") is a Shopify-distributed AI audience-intelligence app that converts Shopify first-party customer and purchase data into predictive audiences and auto-syncs them to Meta Ads, marketed as an engine that identifies "future high-value buyers" most likely to convert, return, and grow LTV [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]]. It is developed by Full Venue, a Porto, Portugal-based vendor, and was launched on the Shopify App Store on 2025-04-01 [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].

## Key facts

- Vendor: Full Venue, headquartered at Torre das Antas, 3° andar, fracção BL, Av. Fernão Magalhães, 4350-158 Porto, Portugal [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Launch date: 2025-04-01 on the Shopify App Store [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Distribution channel: Shopify App Store (the listing is the sole distribution surface named) [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Ad-channel integration: one-click sync of predictive audiences to Meta Ads; no other ad platforms are named in the listing [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Core feature claims: "Predictive AI audiences built from your real Shopify first-party data," "automatic audience refreshes to keep your Meta campaigns sharp," and a "performance insights" dashboard, with "no coding or manual configuration required" [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Stated buyer outcomes: boost ROAS, lower CPA, and grow customer LTV [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Pricing (USD, all tiers include a 30-day free trial and an annual option at ~17% discount) [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]]:
  - Basic — $79/month or $790/year — up to 5,000 active customers. [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]]
  - Pro — $299/month or $2,990/year — up to 100,000 active customers; "everything in Basic.". [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]]
  - Enterprise — $499/month or $4,990/year — more than 100,000 active customers; "everything in Pro.". [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]]
- Billing rails: USD recurring charges billed every 30 days via Shopify's app-billing system [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Shopify App Store categories: Customer behavior; Marketing and sales; Visuals and reports [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Public proof points at time of capture: 4.8 average rating across 19 Shopify App Store reviews; vendor-curated testimonials assert "CPA's that are 3 times lower" and "ROAS that is 2x (two points) higher than our best performing campaigns" — these are unverified customer claims, not independent benchmarks [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Languages supported: English, Spanish, and Portuguese for product UI and customer support [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Data access requested at install: customer name/email/phone/physical address; device and activity data (geolocation, IP, browser/OS); store-owner identity; full customer, product, order, and location data; and edit access to store-analytics web pixels [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
- Competitive positioning relative to Orita: same core proposition (first-party Shopify data → predictive purchase-intent audiences) but scoped narrowly to a single ad-channel surface (Meta Ads) and a single distribution channel (Shopify App Store), in contrast to Orita's Klaviyo-channel beachhead and broader downstream-vendor coverage [[sources/web-2025-04-01-266]].
