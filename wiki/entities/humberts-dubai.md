# Humberts Dubai

## Summary

Humberts Dubai is the UAE flagship office of Humberts, a UK real estate firm with over 180 years of history in the industry [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]]. Humberts Dubai is described as the first international firm to adopt SmartProperty's asset and financial planning platform, deploying The Living Reserve Study® in the UAE's Jointly Owned Property (JOP) sector [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].

## Key facts

- Humberts Dubai operates as the UAE flagship office of Humberts, a UK real estate firm with operations across 10 UK offices in addition to the Dubai flagship [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- Humberts as a parent firm has over 180 years of history in the real estate industry [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- Humberts Dubai offers Brokerage, Property Management, Valuations, Reserve Studies, and Advisory services in the UAE market [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- Humberts Dubai is the first international (i.e., non-US) firm to adopt SmartProperty's platform, announced April 7, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- The Humberts Dubai × SmartProperty partnership targets the UAE Jointly Owned Property (JOP) sector [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- Waqar Hasan CPM PCAM is the named Director of Humberts UAE [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
