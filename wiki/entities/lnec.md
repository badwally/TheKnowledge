# LNEC (Laboratório Nacional de Engenharia Civil)

## Summary

LNEC (Laboratório Nacional de Engenharia Civil, the Portuguese National Civil Engineering Laboratory) is a state research institution that co-publishes Portugal's foundational technical guides on infrastructure asset management for water and wastewater services.

## Key facts

- LNEC is co-publisher (with ERSAR and IST) of Technical Guide n.16, 'Infrastructure asset management of water services' (Alegre & Covas, 2010, Lisbon, 472 pp., ISBN 978-989-8360-04-5) [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- LNEC is co-publisher (with ERSAR and IST) of Technical Guide n.17, 'Infrastructure asset management of wastewater and stormwater services' (Almeida & Cardoso, 2010, Lisbon, ISBN 978-989-8360-05-2), the wastewater counterpart to Technical Guide n.16 [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
