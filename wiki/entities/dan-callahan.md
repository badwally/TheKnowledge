# Dan Callahan

## Summary

Researcher at Counterpoint Global (Morgan Stanley), CFA charterholder; co-author with Michael J. Mauboussin of the June 4, 2024 Consilient Observer report "Stock Market Concentration: How Much Is Too Much?" [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].

## Key facts

- Listed author of "Stock Market Concentration: How Much Is Too Much?" (Counterpoint Global Insights / Consilient Observer, June 4, 2024); contact email dan.callahan1@morganstanley.com [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Listed with the post-nominal "CFA" on the report [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Co-authored the report with Michael J. Mauboussin [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
