# Salesforce xLAM-2-8B

## Summary

xLAM-2-8B is an 8B-parameter Salesforce model cited by Belcak et al. (2025) as achieving state-of-the-art performance on tool calling — surpassing frontier models such as GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 despite its relatively modest size [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].

## Key facts

- 8B-parameter language model from Salesforce [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Achieves state-of-the-art performance on tool calling [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Surpasses frontier models including GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 on tool-calling benchmarks despite its modest size [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Cited by Belcak et al. as evidence that SLMs can match or exceed LLM performance on the model→tool/code interface that is central to agentic systems [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
