# Western Reporting

## Summary

Western Reporting is the screening and fraud-prevention product within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio, focused on documentless income verification and (in early 2026) identity verification for multifamily-rental applicants [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- Western Reporting operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- At OpTech 2025 (Nov 17, 2025), Western Reporting launched Digital Income Verification — sourcing and verifying applicant income through a multilayered third-party-data approach that validates most applicants instantly without requiring documents or applicant action [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Digital Income Verification automatically deploys real-time checks and fraud tools if additional information is required for a complete income picture, and integrates with most major property management systems beyond [[entities/inhabit]]'s own portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Digital Income Verification is available through [[entities/resman]] and [[entities/tenanttech]] and can be fully embedded in the screening workflow [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Western Reporting Identity Verification was announced for early-2026 release, leveraging third-party technology to stop identity fraud before a tour or application begins; will be available through [[entities/resman]] and [[entities/tenanttech]] [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Western Reporting's scope is exclusively rental-applicant screening; no condo/HOA owner verification or board-member identity workflows are announced [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
