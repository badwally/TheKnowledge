# BC Real Estate Association (BCREA)

## Summary

The BC Real Estate Association (BCREA) is a British Columbia real-estate sector association identified in UpperBee's February 19, 2026 thought-leadership article as one of two named organizations calling for a full review of the BC Strata Property Act, alongside the Association of Interior Realtors (AOIR) [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]]. UpperBee's article frames those calls — surfaced in a REMI Network article featuring CCI BC Chapter President Sean Ingraham — as having sparked "an important conversation across British Columbia" in the period leading up to February 2026 [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].

## Key facts

- British Columbia real-estate sector association [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- Called for a full review of the BC Strata Property Act, as reported in REMI Network and cited by UpperBee's February 19, 2026 article on BC Strata reform [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- Identified alongside the Association of Interior Realtors (AOIR) as a co-sponsor of those reform calls [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- Reform calls explicitly framed by UpperBee as having sparked "an important conversation across British Columbia" in the run-up to February 2026 [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
