# Paul Meinshausen

## Summary

Paul Meinshausen is a co-founder of Aampe, the agentic AI personalization platform founded in 2020 [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].

## Key facts

- Co-founded Aampe in 2020 alongside Schaun Wheeler and Sami Abboud [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Harvard graduate [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
- Applied data science and AI to military operations in Afghanistan prior to founding Aampe [[sources/web-2025-02-03-42a]].
