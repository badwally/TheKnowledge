# Y. Lempérière

## Summary

Quantitative-finance researcher at Capital Fund Management; first-listed author of the 2014 paper "Two centuries of trend following," which extends the empirical study of the trend-following anomaly to a 200-year horizon across commodities, currencies, stock indices, and bonds [[sources/pdf-be69dd282a8c]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Capital Fund Management, 23 rue de l'Université, 75007 Paris, France [[sources/pdf-be69dd282a8c]].
- First-listed author of "Two centuries of trend following" (April 15, 2014), co-authored with C. Deremble, P. Seager, M. Potters, and J. P. Bouchaud [[sources/pdf-be69dd282a8c]].
- The paper extends earlier 100-year horizon work (cited as ref. [23] in the paper) to a 200-year horizon and reports a t-stat of ≈10 for trend-following excess returns since 1800 [[sources/pdf-be69dd282a8c]].
