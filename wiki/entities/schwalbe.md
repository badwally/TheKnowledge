# Schwalbe

## Summary

Schwalbe is a bicycle tire manufacturer whose products — including the [[entities/schwalbe-g-one-speed|G-One Speed]] tubeless gravel/road tire — are stocked by dealers such as [[entities/studio-velo|Studio Velo]] [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].

## Key facts

- Manufactures the G-One Speed tubeless-easy tire, sold by [[entities/studio-velo|Studio Velo]] in a 700 x 30c folding-bead variant for $80.00 in September 2019 [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- The G-One Speed line is built on Schwalbe's Evolution Line platform with the OneStar Compound rubber, a MicroSkin casing, and V-Guard puncture protection [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Schwalbe markets specific casing technologies under named programs — including "Tubeless Easy" for tires designed for straightforward tubeless setup [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
