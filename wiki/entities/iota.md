# IOTA

## Summary

IOTA is a permissionless distributed ledger technology firm that has partnered with automotive manufacturers to develop blockchain-based vehicle data products [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].

## Key facts

- In 2018, IOTA partnered with Volkswagen to develop a Digital Car Pass for collecting and communicating vehicle data [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- IOTA partnered with Jaguar Land Rover on a smart wallet for vehicles, enabling drivers to earn money and pay for selected services while driving [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
- IOTA's technology is permissionless, distinguishing it from private permissioned blockchain deployments [[sources/web-2021-03-07-5c3]].
