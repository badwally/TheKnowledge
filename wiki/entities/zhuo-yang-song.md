# Zhuo-Yang Song

## Summary

Researcher at the School of Physics, Peking University; lead author of the 2026 Letter "Detailed balance in large language model-driven agents," which proposes a least-action-based method to estimate an underlying potential function for LLM-driven agents and reports the first observation of a macroscopic detailed-balance law in LLM generative dynamics [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the School of Physics, Peking University, Beijing 100871, China [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
- Listed as the first author of "Detailed balance in large language model-driven agents" (2026) [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
- Co-authored the paper with Qing-Hong Cao (Peking University), Ming-xing Luo (Beijing Computational Science Research Center), and Hua Xing Zhu (Peking University) [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
- The paper experimentally probes three frontier LLMs — GPT-5 Nano, Claude-4, and Gemini-2.5-flash — performing 20,000 generations per model on a Conditioned Word Generation task to estimate transition kernels between agent states [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
