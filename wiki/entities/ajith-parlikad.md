# Ajith K. Parlikad

## Summary

Ajith K. Parlikad is a co-author on the Bull et al. (2022) paper applying hierarchical Bayesian multitask learning to engineering fleet management, including a truck fleet survival analysis and wind-farm power prediction.

## Key facts

- Parlikad is listed as a co-author of "Hierarchical Bayesian Modelling for Knowledge Transfer Across Engineering Fleets via Multitask Learning," alongside L. A. Bull, D. Di Francesco, M. Dhada, O. Steinert, T. Lindgren, A. B. Duncan, and M. Girolami [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
- The paper was published in *Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering* in 2022, corresponding to arXiv 2204.12404 with DOI 10.1111/mice.12901 [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
- The application work covered in the paper includes survival analysis of a truck fleet and power prediction in a wind farm, both treated as fleet-level asset management problems [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
