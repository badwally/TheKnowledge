# Alex Pentland

## Summary

Computational social scientist with joint affiliations at Stanford University and MIT; co-author of the 2026 paper "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents" [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].

## Key facts

- Listed with joint Stanford University and MIT affiliations on "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents" (2026) [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
- Co-author alongside Shu Yang (KAUST), Jiaxin Pei (Stanford), and Michiel A. Bakker (MIT), among others, on the first systematic study of multi-user LLM agents [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
