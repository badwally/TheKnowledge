# Rabi Mishalani

## Summary

Rabi Mishalani is an infrastructure-systems researcher and the second (final) listed co-author of the 1998 *Journal of Infrastructure Systems* paper introducing a structured econometric approach for correcting selectivity bias in modelling highway pavement maintenance and rehabilitation (M&R) effectiveness.

## Key facts

- Mishalani is the second (final) listed author of "Selectivity Bias in Modeling Highway Pavement Maintenance Effectiveness," published in the *Journal of Infrastructure Systems*, vol. 4, no. 3, 20 September 1998 (DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)1076-0342(1998)4:3(134)) [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
- The paper is co-authored with Samer Madanat [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
- Mishalani is also co-author of the earlier related paper with Madanat and Wan Ibrahim, "Estimation of infrastructure transition probabilities from condition rating data," *Journal of Infrastructure Systems*, ASCE, 1(2), 120-125, 1995 [[sources/web-1998-09-20-413]].
