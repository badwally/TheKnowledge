# PRISM (Property Replacement Intelligence & Specification Model)

## Summary

PRISM — Property Replacement Intelligence & Specification Model — is SmartProperty's proprietary data model and the data foundation underlying the SmartProperty Atlas reserve study intelligence engine [[sources/web-2026-06-03-cc7]]. SmartProperty describes PRISM as "what makes Atlas intelligent" and as the cost backbone that differentiates Atlas from "averages-of-averages" traditional reserve studies [[sources/web-2026-06-03-cc7]].

## Key facts

- PRISM is built from SmartProperty's cost database, characterized by SmartProperty as "billions in replacement values across thousands of communities" [[sources/web-2026-06-03-cc7]].
- PRISM ingests local climate, construction quality, usage frequency, and property type as inputs to cost projections [[sources/web-2026-06-03-cc7]].
- PRISM is recursive — every reserve study completed by Atlas feeds back into PRISM, "refining cost models, sharpening predictions, and improving accuracy" [[sources/web-2026-06-03-cc7]].
- SmartProperty does not publicly disclose PRISM's internal architecture (distributional form, hierarchical structure, covariate weighting, calibration approach, or update cadence) on the Atlas launch page; disclosure is limited to inputs, behavior (recursive learning), and outputs (defensible cost projections) [[sources/web-2026-06-03-cc7]].
- PRISM's cost-projection traceability is marketed at the per-line-item level: "Every cost projection traces back to the real data behind it" [[sources/web-2026-06-03-cc7]].
