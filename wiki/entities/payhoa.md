# PayHOA

## Summary

PayHOA is a US community association management (CAM) software vendor that markets an all-in-one HOA management platform priced on a tiered per-unit subscription model, with self-management of small HOAs and oversight of multiple condo associations as named target use cases [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]]. The platform bundles online payments, unit management, requests-and-violations workflows, financial reporting, communications tools, accounting, and vendor/payables management into a single annual subscription, with payment-rail processing fees (ACH, card, lockbox) and several ancillary services (mailed letters/checks, bookkeeping, 1120-H filing, 1099 filing) priced separately on a per-transaction or starting-at basis [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]]. PayHOA's published pricing structure positions it at the small-to-mid-HOA tier of the CAM market: the entry price is $49/month (billed yearly) for HOAs with 0–25 units, scaling through nine tiers to $0.55 per unit per month for 500+ units with a $275 monthly minimum [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].

## Key facts

### Corporate / product positioning

- Operates the payhoa.com domain; the pricing page is published at payhoa.com/pricing/ [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Markets the platform under the tagline "Simple & Flexible Pricing" with "No credit card required. Cancel anytime." [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]]
- Named target market: associations that "self-manage a small HOA or oversee multiple condo associations" [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Frames the offering as "all-in-one solutions" delivering "over 100 features" through a single platform [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].

### Subscription pricing tiers (annual billing)

- 0–25 units: $54/month month-to-month; $49/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 26–50 units: $65/month month-to-month; $59/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 51–100 units: $109/month month-to-month; $99/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 101–150 units: $142/month month-to-month; $129/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 151–200 units: $186/month month-to-month; $169/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 201–300 units: $219/month month-to-month; $199/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 301–400 units: $252/month month-to-month; $229/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 401–500 units: $275/month month-to-month; $249/month billed yearly [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- 500+ units: $0.55 per unit per month, with a $275 monthly minimum [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- All tiers are described as including the same feature set; tier discrimination is unit-count only [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].

### Payment-rail processing fees

- Incoming ACH payments: $2.45 per transaction [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Incoming card payments: 3.5% + $0.50 per transaction [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Lockbox payments: $2.50 per payment [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Locally collected payments: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Recurring and one-time invoices: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Bank connection and syncing: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Bank reconciliation: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].

### Communications fees (USPS pass-through)

- USPS First Class printing and postage: $1.25 per letter [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- USPS Standard Class printing and postage: $1.05 per letter [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- USPS mailed checks: $2.00 per check [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Text, emails, and phone calls: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Message boards and forums: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Surveys: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
- Website builder and hosting: unlimited (included in subscription) [[sources/web-2026-03-11-3a6]].
