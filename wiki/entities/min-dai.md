# Min Dai

## Summary

Mathematical-finance researcher at the Department of Mathematics and Risk Management Institute, National University of Singapore; lead author of the 2015 paper "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules," which proves the optimality of a trend-following all-in / all-out strategy in a bull-bear regime-switching market with partial information [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Department of Mathematics and Risk Management Institute, National University of Singapore, on the paper [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- First-listed author of "Optimal Trend Following Trading Rules" (Mathematics of Operations Research; SSRN-id1630903) with Zhou Yang, Qing Zhang, and Qiji Jim Zhu [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- Lead author of the companion paper Dai et al. [5], which first proposed trend-following based on the conditional bull-market probability but imposed the technical restriction that only a single share could be traded — yielding a non-self-financing wealth process [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- The 2015 paper relaxes that single-share restriction, adopts an objective function emphasizing percentage gains, and provides a more thorough theoretical analysis (removal of a technical condition in the verification theorem, an approximation-based proof, and a finite-trades-almost-surely result) [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
- Subsequent work by Chen et al. [1] extends the framework to a Merton-style portfolio optimization problem in which the investor may choose an optimal fraction of wealth invested in the stock [[sources/pdf-05df32dcb03e]].
