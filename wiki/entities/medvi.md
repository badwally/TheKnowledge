# Medvi

**Archetype:** AI-native marketplace (health brokering)

Health-services brokering platform. AI parses patient intake forms and routes them to a network of human providers for review and prescription.

## Why it's in this domain

Cited as the extreme-solo-scale marketplace archetype — operates a brokering business at billion-dollar valuation scale with a very small team. The AI handles the matching/intake layer; humans handle the regulated-judgment layer.
