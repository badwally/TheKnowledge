# Haoyu Zha

## Summary

Haoyu Zha is a co-founder of HOAi, the pioneering AI technology provider in the HOA and community management space that Vantaca acquired in an announcement made at Vantaca Vision on November 19, 2024 and publicized via PRNewswire on November 20, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. Zha articulated HOAi's post-acquisition mission as continuing to redefine community management through AI while accelerating innovation in partnership with Vantaca [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

## Key facts

- Co-founder of HOAi [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Identified by name in the November 20, 2024 PRNewswire announcement of Vantaca's acquisition of HOAi [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Articulated HOAi's pre-acquisition product focus as AI-driven solutions that simplify operations and enhance productivity [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Attributed quotation on the acquisition: "We're thrilled to join forces with Vantaca and continue our mission of redefining community management through AI" [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Attributed quotation on the post-acquisition plan: "Together with Vantaca, we'll accelerate innovation, providing community management teams with the tools they need to operate seamlessly and effectively" [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
