# Vertica Capital

## Summary

Vertica Capital Partners is a growth equity firm focused exclusively on mission-critical vertical software and tech-enabled service businesses, managing over $1 billion in capital [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]]. The firm is headquartered in New York, NY and has built a concentrated portfolio in the property management software vertical, with Condo Control and Property Control as anchor assets [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]] [[sources/web-2026-03-01-17a]].
## Key facts

- Manages over $1 billion in capital across funds [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Focuses exclusively on mission-critical vertical software and tech-enabled service businesses [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Headquartered in New York, NY [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]]
- Co-founded and led by Vitaly Vorobeychik (Managing Director) and Philip Vorobeychik (Managing Director) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-e48]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Leadership team also includes Jesse Javna (Principal), Stephen Hoey (CFO), Noah Keil (EVP of Marketing) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-e48]] [[sources/web-2020-03-03-165]]
- Fund I: $205 million, closed March 2020; significantly oversubscribed; LP base of software entrepreneurs, university endowments, charitable foundations, family offices, pension funds, funds of funds [[sources/web-2020-03-03-165]]
- Fund II: $380 million, closed August 2025 in under a month; nearly doubled Fund I; raised significantly above target [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Fund II LP base: returning institutional investors including endowments, foundations, pension funds, and family offices [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Fund II check size: up to $100 million of equity per transaction [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Investment approach: flexible capital (primary growth capital, shareholder liquidity, management buyouts) plus hands-on operational support [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Team: 15+ professionals; Vertica Vision platform of 50+ specialized consultants [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
- Portfolio: 25+ software investments spanning public safety, property management, campground management, healthcare, tours and activities, identity governance [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-e48]]
- Acquired Condo Control in a Founder Sale that closed February 2024 [[sources/web-2025-08-01-829]] [[sources/web-2026-03-01-17a]]
- Announced a strategic investment in Property Control in June 2024, described as the firm's third acquisition in the property management software vertical [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]]
- Property Control portfolio (as of 2025) includes Condo Control and Condo Manager, both confirmed live products linked from propertycontrol.com [[sources/web-2025-07-02-a17]]
- Applies an operational playbook across its property tech portfolio spanning go-to-market acceleration, talent acquisition, product development, and strategic M&A [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c85]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-f2e]]
