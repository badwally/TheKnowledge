# Jenny Zhang

## Summary

AI researcher at the University of British Columbia and the Vector Institute, lead author of the 2026 "HyperAgents" paper that introduces self-referential agents combining a task agent and a meta agent into a single editable program for open-ended self-improvement [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of British Columbia and the Vector Institute on the HyperAgents paper [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Listed as a corresponding author on HyperAgents with email jennyzzt@cs.ubc.ca [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Performed the work during an internship at Meta [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Lead author of the prior Darwin Gödel Machine paper (Zhang et al., 2025b), which the HyperAgents work directly extends into the DGM-Hyperagents (DGM-H) system [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Co-authors on HyperAgents include Bingchen Zhao (University of Edinburgh), Wannan Yang (NYU), Jakob Foerster (FAIR at Meta), Jeff Clune (UBC, Vector Institute, Canada CIFAR AI Chair), Minqi Jiang, Sam Devlin (Meta Superintelligence Labs), and Tatiana Shavrina (FAIR at Meta) [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
