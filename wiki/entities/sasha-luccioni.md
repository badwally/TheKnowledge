# Sasha Luccioni

## Summary

AI researcher at Hugging Face whose work focuses on the environmental costs of machine learning; co-author of the 2025 paper "Video Killed the Energy Budget" and prior author of foundational studies on the energy and carbon costs of inference across ML tasks [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Hugging Face on the paper, with email sasha.luccioni@huggingface.co [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- Co-author of "Video Killed the Energy Budget: Characterizing the Latency and Power Regimes of Open Text-to-Video Models" with Julien Delavande and Regis Pierrard [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- Cited in the paper as lead author of Luccioni et al. (2024), the first large-scale study of energy and carbon costs across different ML tasks and approaches, including image generation [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- Cited in the paper as co-author of Luccioni et al. (2022), an earlier work on the carbon footprint of different ML model types and the factors that influence them [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
