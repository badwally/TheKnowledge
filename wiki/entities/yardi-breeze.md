# Yardi Breeze

## Summary

Yardi Breeze is a Yardi property-management product distinct from the enterprise-tier Voyager platform, positioned by Yardi as "refreshingly simple condo management and HOA software" and marketed to the Canadian condo/strata segment through a dedicated landing page at yardibreeze.ca/condo-strata-features/ [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]]. The product is sold as a base platform with two add-on tiers — a regular Add-Ons tier ("Power your business with optional tools") and a Breeze Premier Add-Ons tier ("Go further with add-ons for Breeze Premier") — under the marketing tagline "Are you ready to make managing your properties a breeze?" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]]. The Premier Add-Ons tier exposes four named modules: a professional business website, Investor Relations, Invoice Processing, and Vendor Payments [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].

## Key facts

### Product positioning

- Yardi product distinct from the enterprise-tier Voyager Condo, Co-op & HOA platform [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Marketed as "refreshingly simple condo management and HOA software" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Markets a dedicated Canadian condo/strata-segment landing page at yardibreeze.ca/condo-strata-features/ [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Positioned under the marketing tagline "Are you ready to make managing your properties a breeze?" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Frames the user experience under the testimonial line: "From setup to maintenance to reporting, Yardi Breeze is very user friendly and simple to work with" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].

### Tiered add-on architecture

- Sold as a base product with a regular Add-Ons tier and a Breeze Premier Add-Ons tier [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Add-Ons tier positioning: "Power your business with optional tools" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Breeze Premier Add-Ons tier positioning: "Go further with add-ons for Breeze Premier" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- The Premier Add-Ons tier requires Breeze Premier (the higher-tier base product) [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].

### Named Premier Add-On modules

- Professional business website module: marketed as creating an easy-to-use website for the property management company, optimized for mobile and intended to help the management company appear in search results [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Investor Relations module: tracks capital commitments, contributions, and distributions in one place; includes built-in CRM tools framed as improving investor relations, and investor portals positioned as giving investors instant access to key metrics and documents [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Invoice Processing module: outsourced invoice processing in which vendors submit invoices to Yardi for scanning and data entry into Breeze Premier for the customer's review and approval; marketed as automating the payables life cycle and reducing costs [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
- Vendor Payments module: simplifies vendor payments while retaining full customer control over accounts payable, covering both paper cheque and EFT payment processing [[sources/web-2025-10-15-25b]].
