# Sasan Jafarnejad

## Summary

Sasan Jafarnejad is a co-author of the 2026 PHM Society paper proposing LLM-based agents for cleaning equipment maintenance logs to improve Predictive Maintenance.

## Key facts

- Jafarnejad is the third listed author of "Cleaning Maintenance Logs with LLM Agents for Improved Predictive Maintenance," published in the PHM Society proceedings on 13 January 2026 [[sources/web-2026-01-13-6bf]].
- The paper is co-authored with Valeriu Ionut Dimidov, Faisal Hawlader, and Raphael Frank [[sources/web-2026-01-13-6bf]].
