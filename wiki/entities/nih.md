# U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH)

## Summary

The U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH) is the federal biomedical research agency that co-leads the NAIRR Secure focus area with the U.S. Department of Energy [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].

## Key facts

- NIH co-leads the NAIRR Secure pilot with DOE [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- NIH provides open and controlled-access NIH computing and data platforms and biomedical datasets to support healthcare-focused AI research through NAIRR [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- NAIRR Secure, which NIH co-leads, strengthens secure, privacy-preserving AI research and explores the use of privacy/security-preserving infrastructure with high-quality AI-ready controlled-access data [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- Datavant's privacy-preserving record linkage platform contribution to NAIRR was specifically directed at the NAIRR Secure NIH component [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- Palantir's Foundry and AIP platforms supporting NAIRR Secure are deployed at NIH [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
