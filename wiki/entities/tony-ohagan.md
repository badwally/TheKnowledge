# Tony O'Hagan

## Summary

Tony O'Hagan is a co-developer of the Sheffield Elicitation Framework (SHELF), originally working in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Sheffield.

## Key facts

- O'Hagan developed SHELF together with Jeremy Oakley, originally in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Sheffield [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
- O'Hagan's work on SHELF is described as arising out of a long-standing commitment to research and practice in elicitation [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
