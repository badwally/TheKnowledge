# NVIDIA Nemotron-H

## Summary

Nemotron-H is an NVIDIA family of hybrid Mamba-Transformer language models in 2B, 4.8B, and 9B parameter sizes, cited by Belcak et al. (NVIDIA Research, 2025) as evidence that small language models can match the agentic-task performance of dense 30B LLMs at an order-of-magnitude fraction of the inference FLOPs [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].

## Key facts

- Released by NVIDIA as a family of 2B/4.8B/9B hybrid Mamba-Transformer models [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Achieves instruction-following and code-generation accuracy comparable to dense 30B LLMs of the same generation at an order-of-magnitude fraction of the inference FLOPs [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Cited as one of the primary capability data points supporting the position that SLMs are sufficiently powerful for agentic systems [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
