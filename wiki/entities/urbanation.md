# Urbanation

## Summary

Urbanation is a research firm whose Toronto-area condominium-supply forecasts are cited by industry bodies including ACMO when sizing condo-manager labour demand [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key facts

- Urbanation reported that 125,000 new condo units are planned for completion in Toronto between 2023 and 2028, with a fifth on target for 2023 alone [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
