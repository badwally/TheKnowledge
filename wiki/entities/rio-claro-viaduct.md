# Rio Claro Viaduct

## Summary

The Rio Claro Viaduct is the real-world case-study structure used to demonstrate and validate the integrated BIM/SHM/ANN viaduct management framework proposed in the 2025 ITcon paper by Porto Oliveira and Dominguez Sotelino.

## Key facts

- The Rio Claro Viaduct is the case-study structure for 'Infrastructure management via BIM model: integration of structural health monitoring and ANN-based damage assessment' (ITcon Vol. 30, pp. 1637-1658, 2025) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Field tests including ambient vibration analysis were conducted on the Rio Claro Viaduct to capture its dynamic behaviour [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- The captured dynamic-behaviour data was used to calibrate a finite element model of the viaduct [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- A decade-separated dataset of the Rio Claro Viaduct was used to validate the framework's robustness and indicated negligible structural deterioration of the viaduct over that decade [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
