# German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE)

## Summary

The German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE) is the German federal ministry funding the €23 million Data Space Accelerator programme, which forms part of the broader Manufacturing-X framework for scaling industrial data ecosystems [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].

## Key facts

- Funder of the €23 million Data Space Accelerator (announced 8 June 2026) [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Sponsors the Manufacturing-X framework for industrial data ecosystems [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
