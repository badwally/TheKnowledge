# Brett M. Frischmann

## Summary

Brett M. Frischmann is a legal scholar and co-author of "Constructing Commons in the Cultural Environment," a 2010 Cornell Law Review article that, together with co-authors Michael J. Madison and Katherine J. Strandburg, establishes the Governing Knowledge Commons (GKC) framework — an adaptation of Elinor Ostrom's institutional commons-governance work to information, data, and other cultural-environment resources [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].

## Key facts

- Co-authored "Constructing Commons in the Cultural Environment," 95 Cornell L. Rev. 657 (2010), with Michael J. Madison and Katherine J. Strandburg [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].
- The article was published in volume 95, issue 4 of the Cornell Law Review [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].
- Through this article he is one of three canonical scholars associated with the Governing Knowledge Commons (GKC) framework [[sources/web-2014-08-29-013]].
