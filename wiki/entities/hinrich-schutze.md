# Hinrich Schütze

## Summary

Professor of computational linguistics at LMU Munich and co-author of the 2025 NoLiMa long-context evaluation paper [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Center for Information and Language Processing (CIS), LMU Munich, Germany, and with the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML) [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
- Co-author of "NoLiMa: Long-Context Evaluation Beyond Literal Matching," ICML 2025, with Ali Modarressi (lead) and the Adobe Research team [[sources/pdf-ali-modarressi-2025-nolima-long-context]].
