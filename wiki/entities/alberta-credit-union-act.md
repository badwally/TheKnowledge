# Alberta Credit Union Act

## Summary

The Alberta *Credit Union Act* is the provincial statute establishing the legal framework for Alberta credit unions, Alberta Central, and the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation (CUDGC) — the deposit guarantor and prudential regulator for the Alberta credit union sector [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]]. The Act creates a three-level system of deposit protection — credit union capital, the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Fund, and the Government of Alberta as ultimate backstop — and authorizes CUDGC's regulatory powers over credit unions and Alberta Central, including the establishment of loan-approval limits and the management of unclaimed deposit balances [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].

## Key facts

- Establishes the legislation that provides a three-level system of deposit protection for Alberta credit union depositors [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Level one of the statutory protection scheme: each credit union must build and maintain adequate levels of capital, comprised primarily of retained earnings (members' common and investment shares also count toward capital) [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Level two: Alberta credit unions fund the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Fund through regular assessments; the Fund is managed by CUDGC and invested in high quality, liquid investments [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Level three: the Government of Alberta ensures CUDGC's obligation to Alberta credit union depositors is carried out [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Authorizes CUDGC to regulate credit unions and Alberta Central and to enforce the Act [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Authorizes CUDGC to establish credit union loan approval limits and to adjudicate loans that exceed those limits [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Establishes the requirements for managing inactive deposit accounts transferred to CUDGC as unclaimed balances from Alberta credit unions [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Authorizes the Alberta Minister of Finance to prescribe the rate at which CUDGC accrues monthly interest on unclaimed balances under management [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
