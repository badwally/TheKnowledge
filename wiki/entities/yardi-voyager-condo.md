# Yardi Voyager Condo, Co-op & HOA

## Summary

Yardi Voyager Condo, Co-op & HOA is Yardi's enterprise-tier property management software platform designed to consolidate accounting, operations, and maintenance management for condominiums, co-operatives, and homeowner associations on a single platform with real-time analytics [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]]. The product is structured as the core module in a four-module product family within Yardi's condo/HOA stack — Voyager Condo plus CondoCafe (resident portal), Yardi Payment Processing, Yardi Procure to Pay, and Yardi Aspire — positioning the platform as a multi-module vertical-integration play [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

## Key facts

### Product positioning

- Marketed as comprehensive property management software designed to streamline operations for condominiums, co-ops, and homeowner associations [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Consolidates accounting, operations, and maintenance management on a single platform with real-time analytics [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Product page URL: yardi.com/product/voyager-condo-co-op-hoa/ [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

### Functional scope

- Provides an integrated database and automated workflows to ensure accuracy and drive efficiency [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Tracks association fees, ownership changes, maintenance items, and violations [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Supports management of both associations and rental units; an owner can request the manager to manage a rental or series of rentals by adding the unit(s) to a rental pool property, which automates the entire process [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Maintains critical board member and meeting records, along with sign-in sheets [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Supports scanning and attachment of meeting minutes to the integrated meeting record [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

### Customization and compliance

- Allows extensive customization of screens, menus, and reports to meet the unique needs of different organizations [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Marketed as streamlining compliance with regional regulations and accounting updates [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

### Product family / add-on modules

- CondoCafe (resident portal) is the named integration that provides online services to resident owners — online payments, paperless certificates, and secure 24/7 access to essential reports for board members [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Yardi positions three additional modules as extensions that can be added to Voyager Condo to build a comprehensive condo/co-op/HOA management solution: Yardi Payment Processing, Yardi Procure to Pay, and Yardi Aspire [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

### Security

- Marketed as ensuring data security through robust access controls [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Secure 24/7 access to essential reports for board members is delivered through the CondoCafe portal [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

### Support

- Yardi offers comprehensive support including live chat, phone support, and email assistance [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Users have access to a dedicated account manager during the setup process [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
