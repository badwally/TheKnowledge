# Federal Reserve Bank of New York

## Summary

Regional Federal Reserve bank and publisher of the Staff Reports series; institutional home of Allan M. Malz, whose June 2014 Staff Report No. 677 "A Simple and Reliable Way to Compute Option-Based Risk-Neutral Distributions" describes a cookbook-style technique used in the bank's own market monitoring [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].

## Key facts

- Publisher of the Staff Reports series, in which the Malz paper appears as Staff Report No. 677, June 2014 [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Frames its Staff Reports as preliminary findings distributed to economists and other interested readers solely to stimulate discussion and elicit comments [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Notes on its Staff Reports that the views expressed are those of the author and do not necessarily reflect the position of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York or the Federal Reserve System, with any errors or omissions the responsibility of the author [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Per Malz (2014), the option-based risk-neutral distribution technique described in Staff Report No. 677 is used in the Federal Reserve Bank of New York's market monitoring [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
- Hosts seminar participants for the paper at the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, who provided comments on the work [[sources/pdf-a25e1c0d5f08]].
