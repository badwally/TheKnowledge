# IntraFi Certificate of Deposit Account Registry Service (CDARS)

## Summary

CDARS — the IntraFi Certificate of Deposit Account Registry Service — is an IntraFi-operated program that allows homeowners associations to access FDIC insurance on certificates of deposit that, in aggregate, exceed the $250,000 FDIC per-depositor / per-bank reserve-funds limit [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]]. CDARS distributes an HOA's funds across CDs at multiple member banks while permitting the HOA to maintain a single banking relationship for all CDARS transactions; the program enforces the per-bank FDIC limit such that no single member-bank balance exceeds the cap [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]]. CDARS is one of two IntraFi network products documented for HOA reserve-fund use — the other being ICS, which differs structurally by using DDA-based savings accounts rather than CDs [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].

## Key facts

- Full name: IntraFi Certificate of Deposit Account Registry Service [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Operated by IntraFi [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Allows associations to access FDIC insurance on CDs exceeding the $250,000 reserve-funds FDIC limit [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Distributes the HOA's funds across several CDs at various banks within the IntraFi member-bank network [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- HOA needs to work with only one bank for all CDARS transactions, despite the multi-bank distribution [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Ensures the HOA's money does not exceed the FDIC limit per bank [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Marketed as enabling HOAs to deposit multi-million-dollar amounts with FDIC protection [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
- Differs structurally from ICS in that the underlying deposit instrument is a certificate of deposit (CD), not a demand-deposit-account-backed savings account [[sources/web-2024-03-06-aca]].
