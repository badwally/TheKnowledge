# Diogo Vitorino

## Summary

Diogo Vitorino is the listed author of the 2012 paper introducing AWARE-P, the open-source software system for urban water infrastructure asset management.

## Key facts

- Vitorino is the listed author of the 2012 paper 'AWARE-P: A software for urban water infrastructure planning,' which presents the methodology, design requirements, and technology options of the AWARE-P platform [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- The paper describes AWARE-P as an open-source software system implementing system-centric asset management methods, framing Vitorino's authorship within the AWARE-P initiative associated with the Baseform organisation [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
