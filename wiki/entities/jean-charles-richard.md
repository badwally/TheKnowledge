# Jean-Charles Richard

## Summary

Quantitative researcher in the Research & Development group at Lyxor Asset Management, Paris; co-author of the December 2011 Lyxor working paper "Trend Filtering Methods for Momentum Strategies" [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].

## Key facts

- Listed affiliation on the paper: Research & Development, Lyxor Asset Management, Paris [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- Listed contact email: jean-charles.richard@lyxor.com [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
- Co-author of "Trend Filtering Methods for Momentum Strategies" (December 2011) with Benjamin Bruder, Tung-Lam Dao, and Thierry Roncalli [[sources/pdf-5ef2018823c8]].
