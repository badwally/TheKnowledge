# Lu Gao

## Summary

Lu Gao is a pavement-engineering researcher who applies machine-learning and explainable-AI techniques to pavement deterioration modelling using Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT) pavement condition data. He is the first-listed author of a 2024 *Transportation Research Record* paper applying a graph neural network to pavement deterioration modelling, and a co-author of a 2025 *Buildings* paper using explainable AI to evaluate flood-driven pavement deterioration rates.

## Key facts

- Gao is the first listed author of "Considering Spatial Structure of the Road Network in Pavement Deterioration Modeling" [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- That paper was published in *Transportation Research Record* 2678.5 (2024): 153-161, DOI 10.1177/03611981231188373 [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- The arXiv preprint (2508.02749) is classified under cs.LG (primary) [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- The graph-neural-network paper is co-authored with Ke Yu and Pan Lu [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- Gao is the second listed author of "Evaluating Pavement Deterioration Rates Due to Flooding Events Using Explainable AI" [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- That paper was published in *Buildings* 15.9 (2025): 1452, DOI 10.3390/buildings15091452, with arXiv preprint 2507.01056 classified under cs.LG [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- The flooding-deterioration paper is co-authored with Lidan Peng (first author), Feng Hong, and Jingran Sun [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- Both of Gao's papers draw on 20+ years of TxDOT pavement condition data, indicating a sustained research programme around TxDOT's Pavement Management Information System (PMIS) [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
