# Eric Benhamou

## Summary

Researcher and instructor at Université Paris-Dauphine; author of the 2021 SSRN-published course material "Algorithmic and advanced Programming in Python" (SSRN abstract 3953589), a masterclass covering algorithm analysis, data structures, and algorithm design in Python [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].

## Key facts

- Contact email eric.benhamou@dauphine.eu listed on the course material [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
- First-listed instructor of "Algorithmic and advanced Programming in Python" (2021), a masterclass co-taught with Remy Belmonte who led the lab sessions [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
- The course covers eleven topics: linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, priority queues and heaps, disjoint sets, graph algorithms, sorting, searching and selection, symbol tables and hashing, string algorithms, greedy algorithms, divide and conquer, and dynamic programming [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
- Course published on SSRN at https://ssrn.com/abstract=3953589 [[sources/pdf-eric-benhamou-2021-powerpoint-presentation]].
