# Kenneth Vecchione

## Summary

Kenneth Vecchione is the named President and CEO of Western Alliance Bank, the parent depository institution under which Alliance Association Bank (AAB) operates as the community-association banking division [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]]. Vecchione's role was identified in the May 2022 CAI Keystone Chapter sponsored vendor profile of AAB.

## Key facts

- Title: President and CEO, Western Alliance Bank, as of May 2022 [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Identified as the parent-bank executive in the AAB principal-of-company disclosure, alongside Steve Curley as President of the AAB division [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
