# Jeff Barnett

## Summary

Jeff Barnett is the Director of Community Association Banking Services at First Citizens Bank and the named expert featured in First Citizens's April 14, 2026 board-facing HOA financing guide "How to pay for an upcoming HOA project" [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]]. The guide features three video segments in which Barnett articulates First Citizens's view on (i) financing as an alternative to phased construction, (ii) bank assessment of community financial readiness for an HOA loan, and (iii) the value of early conversations with a financing partner [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].

## Key facts

- Title: Director of Community Association Banking Services at First Citizens [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Named as the principal expert voice in First Citizens's April 14, 2026 HOA financing guide on firstcitizens.com [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Frames bank financing as a path for HOAs to complete all phases of a capital project at once, maintaining consistent community appearance and avoiding the pitfalls of phased construction [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Articulates First Citizens's view on bank assessment of a community's financial readiness for an HOA loan in an embedded video segment in the guide [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
- Articulates First Citizens's view that early conversations with a financing partner can make a difference in identifying effective financing options for a given HOA situation [[sources/web-2026-04-14-940]].
