# Journal of Applied Probability

## Summary

The *Journal of Applied Probability* is a Cambridge University Press scholarly journal hosted on Cambridge Core, publishing research in applied probability including work on stochastic processes used in degradation and reliability modelling.

## Key facts

- The *Journal of Applied Probability* is hosted on Cambridge Core (cambridge.org) and published by Cambridge University Press [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
- The journal published Al Masry, Mercier, and Verdier's 'Stochastic comparisons and ageing properties of an extended gamma process' in Volume 58, Issue 1, on 20 March 2021 [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
