# California AB 1101 (Statutes of 2021, Chapter 270)

## Summary

Assembly Bill 1101 (Statutes of 2021, Chapter 270) is the California legislative enactment that amended California Civil Code § 5380, the statute governing management of common interest development association funds by managing agents. The amendment took effect January 1, 2022 and is the legislative vehicle that produced the current operative text of § 5380, including the FDIC/NCUA/guaranty-corporation insurance requirements, principal-protection limits, and the $5,000/$10,000 transfer-authorization thresholds [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

## Key facts

- Chaptered as Statutes of 2021, Chapter 270 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Bill number: AB 1101 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Amended California Civil Code § 5380 at Section 1 of the chaptered bill [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Effective date: January 1, 2022 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- The post-amendment § 5380 text includes the managing-agent trust-fund deposit obligation, federal/guaranty-corporation deposit-insurance requirements, principal-protection investment restriction (prohibiting stocks and high-risk investment options for association funds), the §§ 5380(b)(6)(A)–(B) transfer-authorization thresholds, anti-commingling rules, and prevailing-party legal-fee recovery [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
