# Barclays

## Summary

UK-headquartered global bank whose Prime Services Capital Solutions group published a July 2022 hedge-fund performance survey covering 290 hedge funds; cited by ClearAlpha as the empirical foundation for the claim that multi-manager funds with full-pass-through expense models nearly doubled net returns versus traditional 2/20 hedge fund structures [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].

## Key facts

- Published "Performance by Fee Type and Level" (Barclays Prime Services Capital Solutions, July 2022), surveying 290 hedge funds [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The survey reported multi-manager funds with "full pass-through" expense models delivered 11.8% net returns versus 6.3% for traditional 2/20 hedge fund structures [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The survey reported alpha of 10.8% (multi-manager) versus 4.1% (traditional 2/20) — more than twice the alpha [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The survey reported net Sharpe Ratio of 2.1 (multi-manager) versus 0.6 (traditional 2/20) — over three times the net Sharpe [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The outperformance occurred despite the higher fund expenses incurred under the full-pass-through model [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
