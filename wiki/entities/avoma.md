# Avoma

## Summary

Avoma is the meeting-recording and transcription platform Orita uses to centrally track every customer and sales call, retaining both transcript and video [[sources/pdf-4931157e130a]]. In Orita's internal stack inventory it is categorized as "Revenue intelligence" covering call recording and conversation analytics [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].

## Key facts

- Avoma — together with Granola — provides the call-intelligence layer that feeds Orita's deal-activity logging (workflow 5.6) and meeting-prep (workflow 2.6) processes [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
- The Engagement Agent and Pipeline Agent are the workflow agents that consume Avoma's structured call data [[sources/docx-92ec692fb0f8]].
