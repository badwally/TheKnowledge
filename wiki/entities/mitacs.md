# Mitacs

## Summary

Mitacs is a Canadian national non-profit that brings academic researchers and businesses together to collaborate on industrially relevant research [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].

## Key facts

- Sen (2026) recommends that the federal government further fund Mitacs specifically for research proposals that use confidential Statistics Canada data, as part of a coordinated Canadian AI data-supply-chain strategy [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
