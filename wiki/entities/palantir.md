# Palantir Technologies

## Summary

Palantir Technologies is a private-sector partner that contributed its Foundry and AIP platforms — deployed at the National Institutes of Health — to support the NAIRR Secure pilot and the expansion of the National Clinical Cohort Collaborative [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].

## Key facts

- Palantir supports the NAIRR Secure pilot and the expansion of the National Clinical Cohort Collaborative through access to Foundry and AIP platforms deployed at NIH, including compute hours and platform support resources [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
