# Scale AI Cluster

## Summary

The Scale AI Cluster is one of Canada's Global Innovation Clusters, headquartered in Quebec and focused on AI-powered supply chains [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]]. It brings together the retail, manufacturing, transportation, infrastructure, healthcare, and information and communications technology (ICT) sectors to build intelligent supply chains through artificial intelligence and robotics [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]]. As presented on the ISED program page, the Cluster operates as a commercialization and ecosystem-support program — funding industry-led AI projects, matching startups with first commercial buyers, and running workforce-development training — rather than as an inter-firm data-pooling or data-trust mechanism [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].

## Key facts

- Federal funding allocation of up to $284 million [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Headquartered in Quebec [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Technology focus is stated as artificial intelligence for supply-chain resilience [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Convenes retail, manufacturing, transportation, infrastructure, healthcare, and ICT sectors to develop intelligent supply chains through AI and robotics [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Operates four program streams: (1) Industry-led projects — drive adaptation and adoption of AI-powered supply-chain solutions; (2) Acceleration — scale-up support for startups and SMEs, including matching to commercial partners and investors; (3) Workforce Development — customized and upskilling training for AI/digital literacy; (4) Ecosystem Showcase and Global Presence — positioning Canada as a global hub for intelligent supply chains [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Beyond financial support, the Cluster matches startups with their first commercial buyer to accelerate market adoption [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Maintains a cross-country membership network and runs collaborative initiatives, networking, and capacity-building activities [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Stated mission frames Canada as a global export leader in AI-enabled supply chains [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Contact channels: scaleai.ca website; info@scaleai.ca [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- The ISED program page does not describe any mechanism for cross-firm data pooling, federated learning, differential privacy, or data-trust governance — the program is framed as funding, matchmaking, and training, not as a data-sharing infrastructure [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
