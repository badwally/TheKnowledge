# Arthur Burns

## Summary

Former Federal Reserve Chair invoked by Zoltan Pozsar in his August 1, 2022 Credit Suisse dispatch as the negative pole in the binary historical comparison framing Jerome Powell's tenure — Powell will be remembered "as Paul Volcker or Arthur Burns" depending on whether he succeeds in slaying inflation or instead causes long-term damage to the Fed's credibility [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].

## Key facts

- Named alongside Paul Volcker by Pozsar as the two reference points for evaluating Powell, with Burns serving as the cautionary parallel — "whether Jerome Powell will be remembered by economic historians as Paul Volcker or Arthur Burns depends on the course of the economic war" [[sources/pdf-6ae97b9c27c7]].
