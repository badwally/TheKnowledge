# Tanja Miketic

## Summary

Trader and educator (Twitter handle @TanjaTrades) who compiled "ICT 2022 Mentorship Key Points" (2023, 7 pages) — a structured note dump from Inner Circle Trader's 2022 mentorship covering technicals, trade management, bias and narrative, trading lunch and afternoon sessions, and psychology [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].

## Key facts

- Authored "ICT 2022 Mentorship Key Points," 7 pages, published 2023, sourced from her notes on @I_Am_The_ICT's 2022 mentorship and his YouTube material [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Twitter handle given on the document: @TanjaTrades [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Document is organized into five sections — Technicals, Trade Management, Bias and Narrative, Trading Lunch & Trading the Afternoon Session, and Psychology — and is presented as a personal study aid rather than a formal publication [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Cross-references specific ICT mentorship episode numbers (e.g., Episode 15 for FVG re-entry, Episode 21 for the midnight/8:30 ET opening-price bias rule) when documenting setups [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Frames ICT's psychology lessons in her own voice, emphasizing "longevity + controlled risk + impeccable risk management = consistent profitability" and the need to embrace the gray area of the market [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
