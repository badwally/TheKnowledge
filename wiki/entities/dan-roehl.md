# Dan Roehl

## Summary

Dan Roehl is EVP/Managing Director of [[entities/inhabit]]'s Residential Division, identified in the company's OpTech 2025 product launch announcement as the executive responsible for the residential-side product portfolio strategy [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- Holds the title EVP/Managing Director of [[entities/inhabit]]'s Residential Division as of Nov 17, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Framed Inhabit's OpTech 2025 product slate around "Hybrid Intelligence" — combining AI with human empathy, understanding, and oversight to mitigate the side effects of "first-generation AI tools" [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Positioned Inhabit's fraud-prevention and compliance roadmap as "purpose-built to overcome challenges that other products have seemingly overlooked or left unaddressed" [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
