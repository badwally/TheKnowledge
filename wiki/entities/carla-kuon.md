# Carla Kuon

## Summary

Integrative physician and Associate Professor of Medicine; led the 2023 group-medical-visit series "Long COVID Care with Dr. Carla Kuon," a 3-visit program providing patients with a holistic plan to promote recovery from Long COVID, with Part 3 ("Beyond Traditional Medicine") devoted to stress reduction, vagal toning exercises, and the concept of pacing in graduated exercise [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].

## Key facts

- Identified on the cover slide as "Carla Kuon, MD — Associate Professor of Medicine, Integrative Physician" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Convened a 3-visit Group Medical Visits series for post-viral recovery: Part 1 — The Anti-Inflammatory Diet, Lectins, and Histamine; Part 2 — Using Nature and Nutrients to Heal; Part 3 — Beyond Traditional Medicine [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Frames Part 3 around when patients should consider complementary and alternative therapies and mind/body interventions, with focus on stress reduction, vagal toning exercises, and pacing in graduated exercise [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Argues that COVID-driven inflammation propagates into brain inflammation, which then increases stress physiology on body and brain — further increasing neuroinflammation in a "looped, self reinforcing mechanism" [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Identifies the Vagus Nerve (CN-X) as a regulator of fight/flight responses and an anti-inflammatory pacemaker for organs that is negatively impacted by COVID, producing high stress responses and perpetual inflammation [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Recommends patients try several vagal-toning practices and adopt 1-2 daily, framed as "Pilates for your Vagus" — including a 10-minute daily mindfulness practice and the 4-7-8 breath [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Names "exercise intolerance" and fatigue as the two most common symptoms of Long COVID, prescribes "pacing" with periods of rest after activity (e.g., 20 minutes after cooking a meal), and warns that pushing through fatigue causes crashes [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Calls "no pain, no gain" a harmful mindset in Long COVID and tells patients to unlearn it; recommends building strength slowly before cardiovascular exercise; refers POTS patients to the modified Dallas POTS protocol (Children's Hospital of Philadelphia) [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
- Recommends specific resources: free apps CALM, INSIGHT TIMER, and Headspace; Belleruth Naparsteck guided imagery ($10, healthjourneys.com); Stasis.life (free); Osher guided imagery (free, osher.ucsf.edu); the 4-7-8 breath video at drweil.com (free); and the Osher MBSR Class ($395, 8-week stress reduction course based on John Kabat Zin) [[sources/pdf-e8351bcf5615]].
