# Form CO-17.SP — Information and Income Tax Return for Non-Profit Corporations (Quebec)

## Summary

Form CO-17.SP is the Revenu Québec annual information and income tax return for non-profit corporations, including most condominium syndicates (syndicats de copropriété) registered in Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. It is the provincial counterpart to the federal T2 and T1044 returns, applying to non-profit organizations that manage shared property for the benefit of co-owners without profit [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. The return reports provincial financial and administrative information, pays annual REQ registration fees, and keeps the syndicate's official records up to date and in compliance [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. For fiscal years starting in 2024, electronic filing is mandatory for all corporate returns in Québec (CO-17, CO-17.SP) with an exception only for registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Officially titled "Information and Income Tax Return for Non-Profit Corporations" [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Issued by Revenu Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Functions as the provincial counterpart to the federal T2 and T1044 returns for NPOs that manage shared property for co-owners without profit [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Through CO-17.SP, the syndicate reports its provincial financial and administrative information, pays annual REQ registration fees, and keeps its official records up to date and in compliance [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Revenu Québec publishes the CO-17.SP.G income tax guide as the detailed filing-instruction companion to the form [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Filing deadline: within 6 months following the end of the fiscal year (e.g., a December 31 fiscal year-end requires filing by June 30 of the following year) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Late filing penalty: $25 per day (minimum $100, maximum $2,500) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Late filing may also trigger reminder notices from Revenu Québec and delays in REQ updates or future assessments [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Failure to file may delay REQ record updates, create obstacles during property transactions or resale of condo units, and result in loss of credibility / non-compliance status with tax authorities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Board members (typically the president or treasurer) are responsible for ensuring the syndicate's information return is filed within the prescribed deadline [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Signatory: the authorized representative (usually the president or treasurer); a CPA accountant or tax specialist may be authorized to complete and transmit it electronically on behalf of the syndicate [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Required attachments: financial statements for the fiscal year and an updated list of directors and officers; federal forms (T2, T1044) are filed separately with the CRA and not attached to the CO-17.SP [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Supporting documentation (minutes, budgets, interest statements) should be kept along with proof of transmission in case of a future audit [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Mandatory electronic filing

- For fiscal years starting in 2024, electronic filing is mandatory in Québec for all corporate returns (CO-17, CO-17.SP), with an exception only for registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Failure to comply with the mandatory electronic filing requirements may lead to processing delays and, in some cases, administrative penalties [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Box 39 — REQ Annual Updating Declaration integration

- If the CO-17.SP is filed within six months following the fiscal year-end and "YES" is checked in box 39, the REQ Annual Updating Declaration is automatically considered completed, though the REQ annual fee still applies [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- This integration combines tax filing and registry update into a single compliance step [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Section 3 — federal-threshold parallel disclosure

- Some syndicates must also complete Section 3 of the CO-17.SP, which mirrors the federal T1044 Non-Profit Organization Information Return obligations when CRA thresholds are exceeded: investment or property income (interest, rents, royalties, dividends) over $10,000; total assets exceeding $200,000 at the end of the previous fiscal year; or a previous obligation to send the T1044 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Distinction from CO-17

- CO-17 is the corresponding return for taxable corporations, or for any corporation that claims a provincial tax credit or has taxable income to report [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- CO-17.SP applies to non-profit organizations (NPOs), including most condominium syndicates, and is used to report information, pay REQ fees, and maintain compliance [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Federal parallel returns

- A registered Québec syndicate (with NEQ starting with "11") must also submit a federal T2 Corporate Income Tax Return with the CRA, with attached schedules 100, 125, and 141 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- A T1044 Non-Profit Information Return must also be submitted to the CRA if federal thresholds apply (assets > $200,000 during the fiscal year, investment/property income > $10,000, or previous T1044 obligation) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- All resident corporations must produce a T2 return each year per the CRA, even if no GST, QST, or income tax is payable [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Example — small vs. large syndicate

- Small building (12 units) with contingency fund $60,000 and annual interest income $3,000 must file CO-17.SP and T2 but not T1044 (thresholds not met) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Large complex (100 units) with contingency fund $800,000 and annual interest income $15,000 must file CO-17.SP, T2, and T1044 because federal thresholds are exceeded [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
