# Danny Hernandez

## Summary

AI researcher at OpenAI and lead author of "Measuring the Algorithmic Efficiency of Neural Networks," which establishes that the compute needed to reach AlexNet-level ImageNet performance fell 44x between 2012 and 2019 — an algorithmic-efficiency doubling every 16 months that outpaces Moore's Law over the same period [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with OpenAI on the paper, with email danny@openai.com [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
- Per the contribution footnote, Hernandez led the research, while Tom Brown paired on initial experiments, scoping, and debugging [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
- Co-authored the related Amodei & Hernandez 2018 result that compute used by the largest AI training runs grew 300,000x between 2012 and 2018, which the paper builds on to estimate effective training compute [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
