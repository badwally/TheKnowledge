# NVIDIA Research

## Summary

Research division of NVIDIA whose authors published the 2025 position paper "Small Language Models are the Future of Agentic AI," arguing for an SLM-first paradigm in agentic AI and providing both the SLM model families (Nemotron-H, Hymba) and the inference infrastructure (Dynamo) cited as evidence [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].

## Key facts

- Institutional affiliation of all eight authors of "Small Language Models are the Future of Agentic AI," with co-author Yingyan Celine Lin jointly affiliated with the Georgia Institute of Technology [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Develops the Nemotron-H family — 2B/4.8B/9B hybrid Mamba-Transformer models that achieve instruction-following and code-generation accuracy comparable to dense 30B LLMs of the same generation at an order-of-magnitude fraction of the inference FLOPs [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Develops Hymba-1.5B, a Mamba-attention hybrid-head SLM that demonstrates best instruction accuracy and 3.5× greater token throughput than comparably-sized transformer models, outperforming larger 13B models on instruction following [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Develops NVIDIA Dynamo, an inference operating system that explicitly supports high-throughput, low-latency SLM inference in both cloud and edge deployments [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Commits to publishing critique of and contributions to the paper's position at research.nvidia.com/labs/lpr/slm-agents [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
