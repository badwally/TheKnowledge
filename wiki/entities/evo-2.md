# Evo 2

## Summary

A "DNA foundation model" trained in a self-supervised way on genomic sequences, cited in the June 2025 Innovation Endeavors "State of Foundation Models" report as evidence that foundation-model training paradigms generalize beyond text and images into the life sciences [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].

## Key facts

- Trained in a self-supervised manner on genomic (A/G/C/T) sequences using next-token-style prediction on input nucleotide sequences [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Identified as a candidate substrate for mutation-effect prediction, in which sequence likelihood is used to flag damaging mutations [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Identified as a candidate substrate for biological feature discovery via sparse autoencoders trained on Evo 2 to surface biologically-relevant concepts [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Identified as a candidate substrate for guided genome design when combined with biological-function prediction models such as Enformer [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Innovation Endeavors qualifies foundation models in this category as "nascent" and lacking broad industry adoption as of mid-2025; the biggest barrier to real adoption is data availability, since high-quality data in these domains is scarce [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
