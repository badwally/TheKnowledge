# Unified Modelling of Infrastructure Asset Performance Deterioration — a bounded gamma process approach

## Summary

A 2025 arXiv preprint by Wang Chen and Arnold X.-X. Yuan proposing a new bounded transformed gamma process (BTGP) model for infrastructure asset performance deterioration. The paper situates BTGP within the traditional regression modelling tradition of infrastructure asset management and benchmarks it against a bounded nonstationary gamma process (BNGP) and six other BTGP alternatives on real-world bridge condition data.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Wang Chen and Arnold X.-X. Yuan, posted on arXiv on 18 August 2025 with identifier 2508.13359 [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- Its primary subject classification is stat.AP, with cross-listings in cs.CE and math.PR [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- The motivation given is that infrastructure asset management systems require a flexible deterioration model capable of handling various degradation patterns in a unified way, and that prior bounded transformed gamma process (BTGP) alternatives lacked sufficient flexibility to characterise different deterioration patterns [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- The paper proposes a new BTGP model deeply grounded in the traditional regression modelling tradition of infrastructure asset management systems, and carries out qualitative and quantitative comparisons against a bounded nonstationary gamma process (BNGP) model from both deterioration modelling and asset management decision-making perspectives [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- An empirical study using real-world historical bridge condition data is reported, comparing the proposed BTGP against the BNGP and six other BTGP alternatives; the authors state the results confirmed the flexibility and significance of the proposed BTGP model for infrastructure systems [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
