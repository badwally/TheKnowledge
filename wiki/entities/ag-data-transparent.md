# Ag Data Transparent (ADT)

## Summary

Ag Data Transparent (ADT) is a farm-industry project that brings transparency to ag-data contracts by certifying agtech companies against a published set of principles for how they handle farm data [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]]. ADT functions as a sector-level data-governance and transparency standard for farm data shared among agtech firms — an industry self-regulation precedent in the agricultural data-sharing space [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].

## Key facts

- ADT brands itself around three values: Transparency, Simplicity, and Trust [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- Todd Janzen serves as ADT Administrator [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- ADT certifies companies for compliance with its principles; ADT-certified companies are described as committed to farmers controlling their own data [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- John Deere is publicly identified as an ADT-certified company [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- ADT and the National Corn Growers Association (NCGA) jointly released Transparency Principles for Ag Carbon, extending the certification model to carbon contracts [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- ADT operates a sub-program, Ag Carbon Transparent, framed as a resource for farmers evaluating carbon contracts [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- ADT announced its 2026 Officer Team, with the organization framing the structure as reflecting "farmer voice" [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
- ADT members are visibly branded with the ADT logo at industry events such as Commodity Classic 2026 ("Classic26") [[sources/web-2026-05-05-b07]].
