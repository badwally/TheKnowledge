# Scout (Vantaca AI Research Assistant)

## Summary

Scout is Vantaca's "Everyday AI" research assistant, seamlessly integrated within the Vantaca community association management platform for streamlined, efficient daily operations [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]]. Scout was introduced as part of Vantaca's two-tiered AI approach announced alongside the November 19, 2024 HOAi acquisition at Vantaca Vision; in this taxonomy, Scout is the integrated "Everyday AI" tier and HOAi is the standalone "Transformational AI" tier [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].

## Key facts

- Vantaca product positioned as the "Everyday AI" research assistant [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Seamlessly integrated within the Vantaca community association management platform [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Marketed for streamlined, efficient daily operations [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
- Introduced alongside the November 19, 2024 HOAi acquisition announcement at Vantaca Vision as one half of Vantaca's two-tiered AI approach (the other being HOAi, the "Transformational AI" tier) [[sources/web-2024-11-20-cd8]].
