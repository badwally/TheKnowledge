# CCI BC Chapter

## Summary

The CCI BC Chapter is the British Columbia chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute, identified by name in UpperBee's February 19, 2026 article on B.C. Strata reform as a load-bearing voice in the provincial conversation triggered by calls from the BC Real Estate Association (BCREA) and the Association of Interior Realtors (AOIR) for a full review of the Strata Property Act [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]]. Sean Ingraham serves as Chapter President as of that February 2026 date [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].

## Key facts

- British Columbia chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].

- Featured in REMI Network coverage of BC Strata reform proposals advanced by BCREA and AOIR, as cited in UpperBee's February 19, 2026 thought-leadership article [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
