# Jiangchuan Liu

## Summary

Researcher at the School of Computing Science, Simon Fraser University, and co-author of MacoCache, the multi-agent deep reinforcement learning framework for cooperative mobile edge caching [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the School of Computing Science at Simon Fraser University, Canada, on the MacoCache paper [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].
- Listed as a co-author of "Intelligent Video Caching at Network Edge: A Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach," together with Fangxin Wang and Ryan Shea (both SFU), Feng Wang (University of Mississippi), and Lifeng Sun (Tsinghua University) [[sources/pdf-f4016087ee51]].
