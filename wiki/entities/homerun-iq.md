# HomeRun IQ

## Summary

HomeRun IQ is a professional-grade reserve study, property condition assessment (PCA), structural integrity reserve study (SIRS), and capital planning software platform built for engineering firms, marketed as delivering assessments 5X faster than legacy Word/Excel/rigid-software workflows [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]]. The platform was co-developed with reserve specialists and engineers and centers on a single-platform workflow where changes flow automatically through every element of the assessment, side-by-side comparison of up to 8 funding plans, and live editing during client meetings [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]]. Named customers featured in published testimonials include Epic Forensics & Engineering, Inc. (Samie Hatim, P.E.) and Charles Taylor (Ryan Jessop, Project Manager), plus an unnamed "Leading Florida Provider" whose Reserve Study Department Head reports moving from 2-3 reports per day to 10-15 per day post-adoption [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].

## Key facts

### Product positioning

- Marketed as a "professional-grade platform" for reserve studies, structural integrity reserve studies (SIRS), and property condition assessments (PCAs) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Positioned against legacy workflows in which "best people spend too much time working in Word and Excel or rigid software, instead of applying their expertise" [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Headline value proposition: deliver reserve studies and PCAs to firm specifications 5X faster [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Self-described as built with reserve specialists and engineers to amplify expertise rather than get in the way [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Public-facing features URL: homeruniq.com/features [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].

### Functional feature surface

- Single-platform workflow in which changes flow automatically through every element of an assessment [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Side-by-side comparison of up to 8 funding plans with visuals positioned to empower client decisions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Live edits and real-time report finalization during client meetings [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Fully branded, customizable reports with templates aligned to firm brand standards; changes flow automatically through every element [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Sophisticated financial modeling supporting the full range of funding strategies for client and regulatory requirements [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Centralized component cost management — institutional knowledge that outlasts staff turnover; component costs stay consistent across projects while datasets grow more valuable [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Flexible component modeling — robust component settings to detail estimations, inform funding strategies, and cover more scenarios; activities can be broken into phases with different schedules for more accurate planning [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Calculations and reporting that align with state-specific and market regulations while maintaining flexibility to structure reports to firm standards [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Built-in progress tracking and real-time data positioned to eliminate status meetings; in-report collaboration tools [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Real-time client interaction — clients view studies on the platform and can drill down on data to make decisions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Photo management is identified as a streamlined workflow component within the integrated digital process [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].

### Florida regulatory compliance

- Specialized capabilities specifically designed for Florida Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) and SB 4-D compliance [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Built-in guidance for state-mandated calculations and the specific component tracking required by Florida statutes, framed as ensuring legal reporting standards are met directly within the interface [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].

### Customer outcomes — quantified testimonials

- Samie Hatim, P.E. (Epic Forensics & Engineering, Inc.): 20% reduction in man-hours per study, enabling 80% net revenue increase in the first year [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Ryan Jessop (Project Manager, Charles Taylor): 30–40% fee impact attributed to templating features baked into HomeRun IQ [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Unnamed Reserve Study Department Head at a "Leading Florida Provider": output increase from 2-3 reports per day to 10-15 per day post-adoption [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].

### Implementation model

- White-glove implementation including hands-on training and delivery of branded templates customized to firm specifications [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Implementation team configures the platform to match firm methodologies and workflows [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
- Ongoing support positioned as ensuring the platform continues to meet evolving firm needs [[sources/web-2026-01-01-936]].
