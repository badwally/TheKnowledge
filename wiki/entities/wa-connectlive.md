# WA ConnectLive

## Summary

WA ConnectLive is Western Alliance Bank's API-based HOA banking and treasury platform, positioned as a single intuitive digital tool through which community association management (CAM) operators manage association finances. The platform was originally branded "ConnectLive" at the Alliance Association Bank (AAB) division level — characterized in 2022 by AAB as an "industry-leading" platform that interfaces with the community-association industry's leading software providers and surfaces key banking information in one place [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]]. The "WA ConnectLive" parent-brand naming reflected on the 2026 Western Alliance product page postdates the 2022 AAB-division-era "ConnectLive" branding [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]]. The product is differentiated by the breadth of its CAM-software integration surface — 11 named integration partners — and by exposing three treasury services (Lockbox, Positive Pay, and Integrated Payables) directly to associations and their managing agents [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].

## Key facts

- Operated by Western Alliance Bank under the Western Alliance Bancorporation marketing surface [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Originated as "ConnectLive" at the Alliance Association Bank division level; AAB characterized ConnectLive in 2022 as an "industry-leading" platform that interfaces with the community-association industry's leading software providers and surfaces key banking information in a single platform [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- The "WA ConnectLive" parent-brand naming reflected on the 2026 Western Alliance product page postdates the 2022 AAB-division-era "ConnectLive" branding [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]] [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Positioned by vendor marketing as a "robust" API enabling association financial management through one intuitive digital tool [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Integrates with 11 named CAM software platforms: AppFolio, Buildium, C3, Caliber by Frontsteps, CINC, Enumerate, eUnify, Smartwebs, SPI, Vantaca, and VMS [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Integration availability and feature parity may depend on the accounting software used; services and integration tools may differ by software per the product's published footnote [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Exposes three named treasury services: Lockbox, Positive Pay, and Integrated Payables [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Lockbox-deposited funds follow Western Alliance Bank's funds-availability policy as outlined in the Deposit Account Agreement Disclosure [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Positive Pay enrollment requires a checking account and an updated Treasury Management Services agreement [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Integrated Payables service carries explicit usage charges [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- The AAB-division-era bundle also included a homeowner payment and management portal that provides management companies a single, secure tool to access, analyze, and retrieve online payment information for homeowners, while providing homeowners self-service access to manage payment methods and view electronic payment history [[sources/web-2022-05-20-5f1]].
- Customer testimonial from Dennis Abbott (President, Carolinas FirstService Residential) describes 200+ accounts on the platform enabling monthly reconciliations to be completed accurately by the second business day of the month [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- The reconciliation-cycle compression is positioned by the customer as enabling earlier financial-report delivery to client associations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
