# Alex L. Zhang

## Summary

AI researcher at MIT CSAIL and lead author of the 2026 preprint "Recursive Language Models," which proposes an inference paradigm that treats arbitrarily long prompts as part of an external environment programmatically navigated and recursively processed by the LLM itself [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with MIT CSAIL as lead author of "Recursive Language Models" (preprint, January 29, 2026) [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
- Co-authors on the paper are Tim Kraska and Omar Khattab, both at MIT CSAIL [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
- Listed correspondence address is altzhang@mit.edu, jointly with Omar Khattab at okhattab@mit.edu [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
- Released the RLM codebase at https://github.com/alexzhang13/rlm [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
