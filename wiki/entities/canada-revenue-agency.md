# Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)

## Summary

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is the federal Canadian tax administration. CRA issues Interpretation Bulletins — non-binding administrative statements of the agency's position on the application of the *Income Tax Act* — that practitioners and taxpayers rely on as the primary federal-tax guidance on how Canadian condominium corporations are treated for income tax purposes [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. CRA's primary statement on condominium corporations is Interpretation Bulletin IT-304R2, *Condominiums* (June 2, 2000), which establishes that most residential condominium corporations qualify as non-profit organizations under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) of the *Income Tax Act* and are exempt from Part I tax even though they remain obligated to file a T2 corporate income tax return annually [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].

## Key facts

- Issues Interpretation Bulletins as administrative-position statements; the bulletins do not have the force of law but reflect CRA's interpretation of the *Income Tax Act* in effect at the time of publication [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Published IT-304R2 *Condominiums* on June 2, 2000, which cancelled and replaced IT-304R (*Capital Cost Allowance — Condominiums*, May 13, 1991) [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Announced its revised position on condominium corporations in the last article of Income Tax Technical News No. 4, dated February 20, 1995 — the basis for the IT-304R2 revision [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Cancelled Information Circular 79-7, *The Condominium Corporation and Its Members*, via Income Tax Technical News No. 4 (February 20, 1995) [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Was formerly named the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency at the time of IT-304R2's June 2000 publication; the bulletin reflects the CCRA-era branding [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Administers paragraph 150(1)(*a*) of the *Income Tax Act*, which requires all corporations — including condominium corporations — to file an income tax return each year, even if they are exempt from paying tax under Part I [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Administers paragraph 149(1)(*l*) of the *Income Tax Act*, the NPO exemption under which most residential condominium corporations qualify [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Publishes IT-496, *Non-Profit Organizations*, as the cross-referenced authority on the conditions necessary to qualify as a tax-exempt non-profit organization under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
