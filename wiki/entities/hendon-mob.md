# Hendon Mob

## Summary

Global Poker Index's comprehensive poker tournament results database; the data source for Lu, Mortal, and Ray's "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" (2020), which cross-references Hendon Mob tournament cash records with hedge fund manager identities to establish the poker-skill/fund-performance correlation [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].

## Key facts

- Operated by Global Poker Index; records results for all players receiving payouts ("cashes") from the tournaments it tracks [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- On average, poker tournaments pay out the top 10% of finishers; a player who wins a payout is said to have "cashed" in that tournament [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- In the Lu, Mortal, and Ray sample, hedge fund managers who cashed in tournaments finished on average in the top 6.2% of entrants for the tournaments they played in [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- The database was cross-referenced with hedge fund manager identities to construct the empirical sample for "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
