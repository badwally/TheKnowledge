# Condominium Authority of Ontario (CAO)

## Summary

The Condominium Authority of Ontario (CAO) is the Ontario regulator that supports condominium corporations, owners, directors, and purchasers under the Condominium Act, 1998. CAO operates a director education program centered on Advanced Director Training Modules and publishes supplementary topical guides — including the CAO Guide on Condo Finances (April 11, 2024) — that deepen director knowledge of financial statements, bank accounts, budgets, reserve funds and reserve fund studies, audits and auditors, and taxes in the context of legal obligations under the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]]. CAO also publishes the CAO Best Practices Guide: Ensuring Healthy Reserve Funds (December 2023), which operationalizes the Notice of Future Funding of the Reserve Fund (NOFF) workflow under §94(8) of the Act [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]]. CAO is accessible by phone (Local 416-901-9356, Toll Free 844-880-5341) Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m., and via the Contact Us portal at condoauthorityontario.ca [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].

## Key facts

- Ontario regulator supporting condominium corporations, owners, directors, and purchasers under the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Operates a director education program centered on Advanced Director Training Modules, supplemented by topical guides intended to deepen participants' knowledge of issues affecting condominium living [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Published the CAO Guide on Condo Finances (April 11, 2024), a supplementary Advanced Director Training reference covering financial statements, bank accounts, annual budgets, reserve funds and reserve fund studies, audits and auditors, and taxes — all framed in the context of the Condominium Act, 1998 [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- The Guide on Condo Finances acknowledges Yale PGC and an Advisory Panel as contributors of significant Ontario-based expertise [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- CAO publishes the CAO Best Practices Guide: Ensuring Healthy Reserve Funds (December 2023), which sits alongside the Condo Finances guide in the director-facing reference library [[sources/pdf-c974b5dbd563]].
- Website: condoauthorityontario.ca; CAO updates its director-facing guides from time to time and directs users to the website for the most up-to-date version [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Contact channels: Contact Us portal on the CAO website; CAO Local number 416-901-9356; CAO Toll Free number 844-880-5341; TTY service via Bell Relay Service [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
- Office hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. [[sources/pdf-994c881efb49]].
