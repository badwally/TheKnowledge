# Stochastic comparisons and ageing properties of an extended gamma process

## Summary

A 2021 paper by Zeina Al Masry, Sophie Mercier, and Ghislain Verdier, published in the *Journal of Applied Probability*, Volume 58 Issue 1, addressing stochastic comparisons and ageing properties of an extended gamma process.

## Key facts

- The paper is authored by Zeina Al Masry, Sophie Mercier, and Ghislain Verdier [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
- The paper appeared in the *Journal of Applied Probability*, Volume 58, Issue 1, published online on 20 March 2021 by Cambridge University Press on Cambridge Core [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
- The paper addresses stochastic comparisons and ageing properties of an extended gamma process [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
- The paper has been cited by subsequent publications on construction-companies business acceptance capability evaluation using Gaussian mixture models (*International Journal of Management Science and Engineering Management*, Vol. 17, Issue 2, p. 112), optimal periodic inspection for multi-auxiliary component systems subject to load-sharing dependence (p. 363), a summarization of remaining life prediction methods for special power plants (*Applied Sciences*, Vol. 13, Issue 16, p. 9365), and a method for evaluating material performance parameters in long-life structural design under random degradation from freeze–thaw cycles (*Journal of Cold Regions Engineering*, Vol. 39, Issue 1) [[sources/web-2021-03-20-b9c]].
