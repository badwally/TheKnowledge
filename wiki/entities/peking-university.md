# Peking University

## Summary

Major research university based in Beijing, China; institutional home of three of the four authors of the 2026 Letter "Detailed balance in large language model-driven agents," through both its School of Physics and its Center for High Energy Physics [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].

## Key facts

- Hosts the School of Physics in Beijing 100871, China [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
- Hosts the Center for High Energy Physics in Beijing 100871, China [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
- Three of the four authors of "Detailed balance in large language model-driven agents" — Zhuo-Yang Song, Qing-Hong Cao, and Hua Xing Zhu — list Peking University as their primary affiliation; the work is physics-department-led research applying equilibrium-statistical-physics tools to LLM-driven agents [[sources/pdf-zhuo-yang-2026-detailed-balance-in]].
