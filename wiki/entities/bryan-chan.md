# Bryan Chan

## Summary

Bryan Chan is a named user of CINC Systems' Payables+ AP automation solution at Neighborhood Community Management, identified as the customer-reference voice in CINC's February 26, 2026 official launch announcement of Payables+ [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]]. Chan is the load-bearing named customer testimony surfaced by CINC to validate Payables+'s end-to-end-workflow value proposition combining CINC's software platform with AvidXchange's payments platform — establishing him as the operational counterparty an acquirer-side outreach would reach for Payables+ customer-validation diligence in the condo-capital-infra Year-3 exit window [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Neighborhood Community Management as of February 26, 2026 [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Named user of CINC's Payables+ AP automation product at Neighborhood Community Management [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Pre-GA Payables+ adopter: his documented usage of Payables+ predates the February 26, 2026 official launch, surfacing Chan and Neighborhood Community Management as part of the customer cohort that operated on Payables+ during the period between the October 2025 initial Payables+ launch and the February 2026 general-availability announcement [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
- Public framing of Payables+ value proposition: "Payables+ seamless integration with the rest of CINC's software platform and AvidXchange's industry leading payments platform means end-to-end workflow benefits. We used to review and code each of our invoices manually and then send checks to vendors. Now all we do is verify that each invoice has been coded accurately and pay our vendors electronically with a click of a button, saving us so much time" — operationalizing the manual-to-automated AP workflow transition Payables+ enables, with explicit endorsement of both the CINC software-platform layer and the AvidXchange payments-platform layer as integral to the value proposition [[sources/web-2026-02-26-870]].
