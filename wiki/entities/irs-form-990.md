# IRS Form 990 (Return of Organization Exempt From Income Tax)

## Summary

IRS Form 990 is the U.S. federal return of organization exempt from income tax, available as a filing option for the rare homeowners association that qualifies as an exempt organization [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]]. The exempt-organization designation is difficult to obtain, and very few larger associations may be exempt from taxation under this regime [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]]. Exempt-organization status applies to less than 1% of U.S. associations, and is limited to very large communities consisting entirely of single family homes [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].

## Key facts

- The U.S. federal return form for organizations exempt from income tax [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Available as a filing option for the rare homeowners association that qualifies as an exempt organization [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Used by associations that are "communities among themselves" and exempt from taxation [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- The exempt-organization designation is difficult to obtain, though some associations have been successful in obtaining this tax status [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Less than 1% of U.S. associations qualify as exempt organizations [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Exempt associations are very large communities consisting entirely of single family homes [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
- Even associations with very minimal interest income may be exempt from the reserve-interest-taxable rule [[sources/web-2026-05-15-040]].
