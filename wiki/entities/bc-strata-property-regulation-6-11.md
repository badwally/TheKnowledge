# BC Strata Property Regulation §6.11 (Eligible Investments)

## Summary

BC Strata Property Regulation §6.11 (made under the Strata Property Act, B.C. Reg. 43/2000) defines the universe of investments in which a BC strata corporation may invest money held in the contingency reserve fund (CRF) or money collected on a special levy. The current text of §6.11 was substituted by B.C. Reg. 68/2014, effective July 16, 2014, which repealed the prior version of §6.11 and the related sections 6.12 and 17.5 of the regulation [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]]. The regulation operates in addition to investments otherwise permitted under the Strata Property Act and is the eligible-investment universe referenced by §95(2)(a) of the Act (CRF) and §108(4)(b)(i) of the Act (special levies) [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]] [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].

## Key facts

- The current text of §6.11 was substituted by B.C. Reg. 68/2014, effective July 16, 2014; the same amending regulation repealed §6.12 and §17.5 of the Strata Property Regulation [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
- §6.11 applies in addition to investments otherwise permitted under the Strata Property Act, for the purposes of §95(2)(a) or §108(4)(b)(i) of the Act, as applicable [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
- §6.11(a) permits investment in a savings account or chequing account with a financial institution outside of British Columbia, insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC) [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
- §6.11(b) permits investment in a term deposit or guaranteed investment certificate (GIC) if the deposit or certificate (i) is insured by CDIC or by the Credit Union Deposit Insurance Corporation of British Columbia (CUDIC) and (ii) has a predetermined rate or predetermined rates of interest [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]] [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- §6.11(c) permits investment in a treasury bill issued by the government of Canada [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
- §6.11(d) permits investment in a bond, debenture, or other evidence of indebtedness issued or guaranteed by the government of Canada or a province, or issued by a corporation incorporated under the laws of Canada or a province, subject to three conditions at time of purchase: (i) remaining term to maturity of 5 years or less, (ii) interest and principal payable in Canadian dollars, and (iii) rating of A or higher from DBRS Limited [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]] [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- §6.11(e) permits investment in a fixed-income exchange-traded fund (ETF) traded on an exchange in Canada, subject to four conditions at time of purchase: (i) the fund's portfolio contains only bonds, debentures, and other evidence of indebtedness, (ii) holdings are denominated in Canadian dollars, (iii) the average remaining term to maturity of the holdings is 5 years or less, and (iv) 98% or more of the value of holdings have a rating of BBB or higher as reported by the issuer of that fund [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]] [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The version of §6.11 published in the new source is from an archived regulation consolidation; later amendments to the Strata Property Regulation may not be reflected and should be checked against the current consolidation [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
- The regulation is published by the Queen's Printer of British Columbia, with a separate Licence and Disclaimer applicable to the published text [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
