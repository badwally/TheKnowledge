# Guido Baltussen

## Summary

Finance researcher jointly affiliated with Erasmus School of Economics (Erasmus University Rotterdam) and Robeco Quantitative Investing; corresponding author of the 2020 paper "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum," which documents a robust positive predictive relationship between rest-of-day returns and last-half-hour returns across more than 60 futures contracts on equities, bonds, commodities, and currencies between 1974 and 2020 [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Erasmus School of Economics, Burgemeester Oudlaan 50, Rotterdam 3000 DR, Netherlands, and with Robeco Quantitative Investing, Weena 850, 3014 DA Rotterdam, on the paper [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- Listed as the corresponding author with email baltussen@ese.eur.nl on the 26 August 2020 draft of "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" (SSRN abstract 3760365) co-authored with Zhi Da, Sten Lammers, and Martin Martens [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- The paper documents that the return during the last 30 minutes before market close (LH) is positively predicted by the return during the rest of the day (ROD = ON + FH + M + SLH), with the predictive power economically and statistically highly significant and reverting over the next days [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- A simple market-intraday-momentum trading strategy built on this signal produces consistent returns over time, translating into annualized Sharpe ratios between 0.87 and 1.73 at the asset-class level [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- The paper provides novel evidence that links market intraday momentum to the gamma-hedging demand of market makers in options and leveraged ETFs, using S&P 500 negative gamma exposure (NGE) and LETF cross-sectional and time-series hedging-demand variation as identification [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
