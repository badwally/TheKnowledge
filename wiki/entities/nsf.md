# U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)

## Summary

The U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) is the federal science-funding agency that leads the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR), the U.S.'s shared national AI research infrastructure [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].

## Key facts

- NSF leads NAIRR, a scalable national infrastructure providing access to computing, software, data, models, educational resources, and expertise for the U.S. research and education communities [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- NSF leads three of NAIRR's four operational focus areas: NAIRR for Research, NAIRR Data and Models, and NAIRR Classroom [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- NSF released an opportunity to establish the NAIRR Operations Center to transition NAIRR from a pilot to a sustained national capability, consistent with the White House AI Action Plan [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
- NSF coordinates NAIRR's coalition of 13 federal agencies and 28 nongovernmental partners [[sources/web-2021-07-29-89d]].
