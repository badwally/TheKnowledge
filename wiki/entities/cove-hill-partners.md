# Cove Hill Partners

## Summary

Cove Hill Partners is a Boston-area investment firm focused on partnering with management teams building category-defining software companies, with deep expertise in vertical software markets [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. On October 15, 2025 the firm led a $300+ million minority growth investment in Vantaca at a $1.25 billion valuation, marking the firm as the lead investor in the dominant AI-first community association management platform [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. The firm operates under an innovative structure that provides flexibility to enable a patient, concentrated, and value-add approach across a small portfolio of long-term investments [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].

## Key facts

- Self-described as a leading investment firm focused on partnering with exceptional management teams building category-defining software companies [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Brings deep expertise in vertical software markets and a track record of supporting companies through transformational growth phases [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Operates an innovative structure providing flexibility for a patient, concentrated, and value-add approach across a small portfolio of long-term investments [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Public-facing website: www.covehillpartners.com [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Jane Levy Vance is identified as Co-Founder and Partner at Cove Hill Partners as of the October 15, 2025 announcement [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Investor Relations contact for the Vantaca transaction: Jane Levy Vance, 1-857-245-6060 (area code 857 indicates Boston-area presence) [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].

### Vantaca investment (October 15, 2025)

- Led a $300+ million minority growth investment in Vantaca at a $1.25 billion valuation [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Joined existing minority investor JMI Equity in the Vantaca cap table [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Investment thesis articulated by Co-Founder Jane Levy Vance characterized Vantaca as the rare combination of market leadership, technological innovation, and exceptional execution that defines category-winning companies [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Vance's stated belief: Vantaca's AI-first approach has sustainable competitive advantages and delivers significant value to customers [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Stated post-investment intent: partner with the Vantaca team as they scale the platform, introduce new capabilities, and expand into new markets [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
