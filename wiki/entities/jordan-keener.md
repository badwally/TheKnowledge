# Jordan Keener

## Summary

Economics researcher at the University of Michigan; co-author of NBER Working Paper No. 27734 "Who Profits From Amateurism? Rent-Sharing in Modern College Sports" (August 2020, revised October 2020), which estimates rent-sharing elasticities in NCAA college athletics and documents the regressive distributional consequences of amateurism rules [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].

## Key facts

- Institutional affiliation: University of Michigan, Department of Economics [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Contact email: jordkeen@umich.edu [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Co-author of "Who Profits From Amateurism?" with Craig Garthwaite, Matthew J. Notowidigdo, and Nicole F. Ozminkowski [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- The paper acknowledges financial support from the NSF GRFP under award number DGE-1842165 [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
