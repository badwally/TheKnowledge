# Pan Lu

## Summary

Pan Lu is the third-listed co-author of the 2024 *Transportation Research Record* paper applying a graph neural network to pavement deterioration modeling using the Texas Department of Transportation's pavement condition dataset.

## Key facts

- Lu is the third (final) listed author of "Considering Spatial Structure of the Road Network in Pavement Deterioration Modeling" [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- The paper was published in *Transportation Research Record* 2678.5 (2024): 153-161, DOI 10.1177/03611981231188373 [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- The paper is co-authored with Lu Gao and Ke Yu [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
