# Standards Council of Canada (SCC)

## Summary

The Standards Council of Canada (SCC) is a named federal partner in Phase 2 of the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy, responsible for advancing AI standards and conformity assessment [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].

## Key facts

- Identified as a Phase 2 implementation partner alongside CIFAR, the national AI institutes, and Canada's Global Innovation Clusters [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Receives C$8.6M under Phase 2 (Budget 2021) to advance the development and adoption of AI standards and a conformity assessment program [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- Operates an accreditation pilot for AI Management Systems, cited as an associated initiative at the Phase 2 launch [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
