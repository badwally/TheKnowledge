# Datavault AI Inc.

## Summary

Datavault AI Inc. (Nasdaq: DVLT) is a U.S. public company headquartered in Beaverton, Oregon that markets a cloud-based platform for data tokenization, valuation, and monetization in what the company describes as a "Web 3.0" environment [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]]. The company operates two divisions — an Acoustic Science Division and a Data Science Division — and on 27 October 2025 announced two "data union" ventures targeting the U.S. insurance and accounting sectors [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].

## Key facts

- Trades on Nasdaq under ticker DVLT [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Headquartered in Beaverton, Oregon [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Nathaniel Bradley is identified as Chief Executive Officer as of October 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Tim Varone, described as a Health Insurance Benefits Sales Director with over 25 years of industry experience, is identified as a longtime advisor to the company [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The Acoustic Science Division markets WiSA, ADIO, and Sumerian audio-transmission technologies covering audio timing, synchronization, and multi-channel interference cancellation [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The Data Science Division markets the Information Data Exchange (IDE), described as a platform for digital twins and for licensing of name, image and likeness (NIL) data by attaching physical objects to immutable metadata [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Patented data-management components include DataScore (data-quality scoring), DataValue (asset valuation), and Data Vault Bank (smart-contract custody and settlement) [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The company asserts ownership of U.S. Patent No. 12,198,201 ("Platform and Method for Preparing a Tax Return"), U.S. Patent No. 11,315,150 ("Portfolio Driven Targeted Advertising Network, System, and Method"), and U.S. Patent No. 11,593,515 ("Platform for Management of User Data") [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Pending patent applications cited in October 2025 communications include 17/941,550 ("Platform and Method for Tokenization of Corporate Data") and 17/842,139 ("System and Method for Tokenized Minting, Authentication, and Utilization of Assets") [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- On 27 October 2025 the company announced the Insurance Data Union, in partnership with the Independent Insurance Agents & Brokers of America ("Big I"), and the Accounting Data Union, in collaboration with what it describes as the top two private accounting firms from each U.S. state [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The October 2025 release states the strategic objective is to generate Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) through tokenized data sales, targeted analytics, and smart-contract transactions executed on the Information Data Exchange [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The same release's forward-looking-statements section flags the company's "current liquidity position and the need to obtain additional financing to support ongoing operations" and its "ability to continue as a going concern" as material risks [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
