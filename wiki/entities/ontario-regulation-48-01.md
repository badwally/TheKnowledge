# Ontario Regulation 48/01 (General)

## Summary

Ontario Regulation 48/01 (General) is the primary regulation made under the Condominium Act, 1998 that defines the operational substance of the Act — including the definition of "core record," the three-tier classification of reserve fund studies, the board's future-funding plan for the reserve fund (s. 94(8) of the Act), the periodic information certificate, declaration registration requirements, and the statutory definition of electronic communication. The current consolidation has been in force since October 1, 2023, with the most recent amendment being O. Reg. 191/23 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key facts

- Made under the Condominium Act, 1998 (referred to throughout the regulation as "the Act") [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Current consolidation has been in force since October 1, 2023 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Last amendment: O. Reg. 191/23 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Bilingual regulation; the captured version is the English text [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Part I governs Definitions and Application [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Part II governs Declaration and Description [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Enumerates 10 categories of "core record" that condominium corporations must maintain and produce on request [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Defines a three-tier reserve fund study classification: comprehensive study, updated study based on a site inspection, and updated study not based on a site inspection [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Defines "standard condominium corporation" as a freehold condominium corporation that is not a common elements condominium corporation or a vacant land condominium corporation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 3 classifies corporations created before Part II of the Condominium Act, 1998 came into force, and continued as corporations under subsection 178(1) of the Act, as standard condominium corporations [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Has been amended multiple times since original enactment, including by O. Reg. 442/11, O. Reg. 383/12, O. Reg. 180/17, O. Reg. 379/17, O. Reg. 397/17, O. Reg. 114/18, and O. Reg. 191/23 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Many of its provisions are tied paragraph-by-paragraph to the coming-into-force of specific sections of Schedule 1 of the Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015, with explicit revocation/substitution triggers [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Defines the "warranty corporation" by reference to the corporation designated under section 2 of the Ontario New Home Warranties Plan Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Defines "land registrar," "easement," "phase," "insurer," "policy," "deposit receipt," "information certificate update," and "new owner information certificate" for purposes of the Act and the regulation [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 1.1 establishes interpretive rules mapping references to "a portion of the units in a corporation" to specific subsets of voting units across numerous Act and regulation provisions [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Part II requires declarations to contain Schedules A through G; Schedule A includes the property/easement description with a solicitor's certification, Schedule B contains mortgagee consents, and Schedule C specifies unit boundaries by reference to buildings or monuments with surveyor certification [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
