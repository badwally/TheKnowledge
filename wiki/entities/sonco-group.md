# Sonco Group Inc.

## Summary

Sonco Group Inc. is a Halifax, Nova Scotia-based property management firm that expanded into condominium management in 2019 and is identified by Condo Control as the first company to implement the Condo Control platform in Atlantic Canada [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].

## Key facts

- Headquartered in Halifax, Nova Scotia [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Expanded its property management division in 2019 to add condominium management as a service line [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Manages a mixed portfolio across Atlantic Canada ranging from small townhouse communities to large multi-unit condominiums [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Designated by Condo Control as the first adopter of the Condo Control platform in Atlantic Canada [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Selection rationale per the case study: streamlining communication, improving board and resident transparency, and centralizing management tasks across heterogeneous property types [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Deployed five named Condo Control modules across its managed properties: the resident portal, the service request module, the library module, the survey feature, and customizable reporting [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Reported a 70–80% overall resident-portal adoption rate across its managed properties after rolling out Condo Control [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Reported a 75% survey response rate in one managed community using the Condo Control survey feature, characterized as notable given a high share of out-of-town owners in that community [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Drove resident and board adoption via live demos, short webinars, and one-on-one sessions, with specifically named outreach to the 75+ demographic [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Pre-Condo-Control operating state per the case study: communication relied on email chains across multiple stakeholders; documents, bylaws, and service requests were scattered across systems and inboxes; staff and resident confidence in the new platform was initially low [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
- Reported outcomes from the Condo Control rollout: reduced dependence on email for tracking, faster resident access to documents and service updates, manager time shifted toward proactive management, and analytics from surveys and service requests feeding board decisions [[sources/web-2026-04-24-ee4]].
