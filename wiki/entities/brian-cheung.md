# Brian Cheung

## Summary

Brian Cheung is an MIT researcher and equal-contribution co-author of the 2024 ICML paper "The Platonic Representation Hypothesis" [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].

## Key facts

- Equal-contribution author with Minyoung Huh, Tongzhou Wang, and Phillip Isola on the PRH paper [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
- MIT affiliation per the paper's author block [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
