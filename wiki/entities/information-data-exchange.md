# Information Data Exchange (IDE)

## Summary

The Information Data Exchange (IDE) is Datavault AI Inc.'s data-tokenization and monetization platform, described in company communications as enabling "Digital Twins" and the licensing of "name, image and likeness (NIL)" by attaching physical real-world objects to immutable metadata records [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]]. The IDE is presented as the operational substrate for Datavault AI's October 2025 Insurance Data Union and Accounting Data Union ventures [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].

## Key facts

- Operated by Datavault AI Inc. (Nasdaq: DVLT) as the data-monetization platform within the company's Data Science Division [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Described in October 2025 corporate communications as enabling digital twins and licensing of name, image and likeness (NIL) data through immutable metadata objects attached to physical assets [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Acts as the smart-contract execution layer for the Insurance Data Union and Accounting Data Union announced on 27 October 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- Integrates Datavault AI's patented DataScore, DataValue, and Data Vault Bank components for asset scoring, valuation, and tokenized settlement [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
- The press release frames IDE-mediated tokenization as customizable across sectors including "sports & entertainment, events & venues, biotech, education, fintech, real estate, healthcare, energy and more" [[sources/web-2025-10-27-5da]].
