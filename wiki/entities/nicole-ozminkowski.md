# Nicole F. Ozminkowski

## Summary

Researcher at Northwestern University; co-author of NBER Working Paper No. 27734 "Who Profits From Amateurism? Rent-Sharing in Modern College Sports" (August 2020, revised October 2020), which characterizes the economic rents from NCAA amateurism rules and estimates their distributional consequences [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].

## Key facts

- Institutional affiliation: Northwestern University [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Contact email: ozminkowskin@gmail.com [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
- Co-author of "Who Profits From Amateurism?" with Craig Garthwaite, Jordan Keener, and Matthew J. Notowidigdo [[sources/pdf-matthew-notowidigdo-2020-gkno-manuscript-1025]].
