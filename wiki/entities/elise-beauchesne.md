# Elise Beauchesne

## Summary

Elise Beauchesne is identified by name in UpperBee's published integrations-page testimonial set as the spokesperson for SolutionCondo's UpperBee adoption narrative, including the load-bearing claim that SolutionCondo grew from 0 to 15,000 doors in 10 years using the software [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with SolutionCondo as the named spokesperson in UpperBee's published testimonial set; title not stated in the source [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Attributed quotation reports that SolutionCondo has used UpperBee "since the beginning" of the firm's existence [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Reported the firm's growth trajectory: "from 0 to 15k doors in 10 years" [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Credits the UpperBee software development and support/training teams with listening and adjusting the software and support tools to SolutionCondo's needs in the field [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
