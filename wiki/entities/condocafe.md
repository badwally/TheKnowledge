# CondoCafe

## Summary

CondoCafe is the Yardi-branded resident and board portal module that integrates with Yardi Voyager Condo, Co-op & HOA to provide online services to resident owners and secure 24/7 access to essential reports for board members [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]]. CondoCafe is one of four named product modules within Yardi's condo/HOA stack, alongside Voyager Condo (the core platform), Yardi Payment Processing, Yardi Procure to Pay, and Yardi Aspire [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

## Key facts

- Branded as the Yardi resident-owner portal layer that sits on top of Yardi Voyager Condo, Co-op & HOA [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Provides online payment options to resident owners [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Provides paperless certificate processing [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Gives board members secure 24/7 access to essential reports [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Yardi marketing copy frames CondoCafe as the module that enables a "seamless experience" for resident owners on the Voyager Condo platform [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- Positioned by Yardi as the channel through which Voyager Condo "enhances services" for residents and boards [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
