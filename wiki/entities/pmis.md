# Pavement Management Information System (PMIS)

## Summary

The Pavement Management Information System (PMIS) is the large pavement condition dataset maintained by the Texas Department of Transportation; it served as the empirical basis for the 2024 *Transportation Research Record* study applying a graph neural network to pavement deterioration prediction.

## Key facts

- PMIS is maintained by the Texas Department of Transportation [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- The PMIS data used in the Gao, Yu, and Lu study comprised more than a half million pavement condition observations [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
- PMIS records were used to test whether considering the spatial structure of the road network improves the prediction performance of pavement deterioration models [[sources/arxiv-2508.02749]].
