# DBRS Limited

## Summary

DBRS Limited is the credit-rating agency referenced by name in BC Strata Property Regulation §6.11(d)(iii) as the source of credit ratings for bonds, debentures, and other evidence of indebtedness eligible for investment by BC strata corporations' contingency reserve funds and special levies [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]] [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]]. The DBRS-specific rating requirement was introduced by B.C. Reg. 68/2014, the amendment that substituted §6.11 of the Strata Property Regulation effective July 16, 2014 [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].

## Key facts

- BC Strata Property Regulation §6.11(d)(iii) requires that bonds, debentures, or other evidence of indebtedness must have a rating of A or higher from DBRS Limited at the time of purchase to be an eligible investment for a strata corporation's CRF or special levy [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]] [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The DBRS-specific rating-threshold language was introduced by B.C. Reg. 68/2014, effective July 16, 2014 [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
- The rating standard for fixed-income ETFs eligible under §6.11(e)(iv) is "BBB or higher as reported by the issuer of that fund," not DBRS-specific, reflecting that ETF holdings may be rated by any recognized agency [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]] [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- The §6.11(d) bond/debenture eligibility regime additionally requires Canadian-dollar denomination of interest and principal and a maximum 5-year remaining term to maturity at the time of purchase, alongside the DBRS A-or-higher rating [[sources/web-2014-07-16-7c3]].
