# Revenu Québec

## Summary

Revenu Québec is the Québec provincial tax authority, responsible for administering Québec's corporate income tax system — including the CO-17 return for taxable corporations and the CO-17.SP return for non-profit corporations (most condominium syndicates) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. Revenu Québec publishes detailed filing-instruction guides (e.g., the CO-17.SP.G guide), assesses late-filing penalties, and issues reminder notices for non-compliance [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]]. For fiscal years starting in 2024, Revenu Québec mandates electronic filing of all corporate returns (CO-17, CO-17.SP) with an exception only for registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Québec provincial tax authority [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Administers Form CO-17 (taxable corporate return) and Form CO-17.SP (NPO information and income tax return) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Publishes the CO-17.SP.G income tax guide as detailed filing instructions for CO-17.SP [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Publishes Form TP-997.1, the Information Return for Tax-Exempt Entities, which some Québec tax-exempt entities may need to file depending on activities and assessment category [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Issues late-filing penalty for CO-17.SP of $25 per day (minimum $100, maximum $2,500) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Mandates electronic filing of all Québec corporate returns (CO-17, CO-17.SP) for fiscal years starting in 2024, with a charity-only exception [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- A Québec corporation must obtain a Revenu Québec identification number to file properly [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Permits an authorized CPA accountant or tax specialist to complete and electronically transmit the CO-17.SP on behalf of a syndicate [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Operates parallel-but-separate from the CRA: federal T2/T1044 obligations are filed with CRA, not attached to the CO-17.SP filed with Revenu Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
