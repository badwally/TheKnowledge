# Alberta Condominium Property Act (RSA 2000, c C-22)

## Summary

The Alberta Condominium Property Act, codified at Revised Statutes of Alberta 2000, Chapter C-22, is the provincial statute governing the creation, registration, governance, and capital-reserve obligations of condominium corporations in Alberta [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]]. The Act is operationalized by the Alberta Condominium Property Regulation (Alta Reg 168/2000), which adds detail on plan registration mechanics, board administration, and the capital replacement reserve fund regime anchored at §38 of the Act [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].

## Key facts

- Codified at Revised Statutes of Alberta 2000, Chapter C-22 [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §14 governs developer obligations including cost-consultant requirements; §14(1)(b) requires the cost consultant to meet criteria established by regulation, and §14(14) provides for court appointment of an administrator, receiver, or receiver and manager [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §38 establishes the obligation for a condominium corporation to maintain a capital replacement reserve fund; §38(1) defines the scope of "depreciating property" subject to the reserve-fund requirement, and the regulation's Part 2 trilogy of reserve fund study, report, and plan implements §38 [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §49, §51, §52, and §63 govern instruments executed by the corporation that are registered against the condominium plan; §49(4) and §52(5) and §63(4) provide for certificates given by the corporation, and §63 governs transfer of a parcel by a corporation [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §58 provides a separate court appointment mechanism for an administrator [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §32 governs changes to the by-laws of a corporation, with notice prescribed by the regulation in Form 3 [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §73 requires the corporation to file a notice of change of address (regulation Form 7) [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §28(5) requires a notice in regulation Form 8 to be filed [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- §8 and §10 govern the contents of the first sheet, diagram sheets, and Schedule (Form 1) of a condominium plan presented for registration; §8(1)(g), (h), and (j) provide the substantive Schedule contents the regulation's §7 operationalizes [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
- The Act is implemented in conjunction with the Land Titles Act for terms not defined in the Act or the regulation [[sources/web-2011-06-02-7ff]].
