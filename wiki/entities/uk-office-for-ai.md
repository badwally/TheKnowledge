# UK Office for Artificial Intelligence

## Summary

The UK Office for Artificial Intelligence is a UK government office that, in 2018, partnered with the Open Data Institute and Innovate UK to assess data trusts as a potential approach to increasing trust and access to data [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].

## Key facts

- Co-sponsored the ODI's 2018 data-trust research programme alongside Innovate UK [[sources/web-2019-04-13-653]].
