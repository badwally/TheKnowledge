# Cursor

## Summary

AI-powered code editor company that Innovation Endeavors' June 2025 "State of Foundation Models" report characterizes as the fastest-growth SaaS ever, with revenue and user growth that exemplify the impact of AI code generation on developer tools [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].

## Key facts

- Innovation Endeavors (June 2025) describes Cursor as "fastest growth SaaS ever" and reports the company at approximately $1B ARR at the time of writing [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- The same report includes an earlier-snapshot table listing Cursor at approximately $100M ARR with 360,000 active users, achieved in approximately 1 year, with a team of approximately 20 employees [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Cited alongside GitHub Copilot and Midjourney as one of the AI-native products with the fastest revenue ramps of all time [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Used by Innovation Endeavors as evidence that AI software-engineering copilots had grown into a roughly $2B/year market in 2–3 years [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
