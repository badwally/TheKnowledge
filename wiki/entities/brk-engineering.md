# BRK Engineering Inc.

## Summary

BRK Engineering Inc. is a Nova Scotia-area engineering firm with documented practice in reserve fund studies and annual roof anchor inspections for Halifax-area condominium corporations; the firm authored the February 27, 2021 Reserve Fund Study for Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130 (Summer Gardens) — the predecessor study to the June 2025 Sense Engineering RFS — and the February 13, 2024 annual roof anchor inspection report for the same corporation [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]]. BRK is a load-bearing competitive-intelligence anchor for the Halifax engagement (Brief-0004) under ADR-0004 as a documented incumbent NS reserve-study firm whose engagement was replaced (or supplemented) by Sense Engineering on the most recent 5-year cycle [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].

## Key facts

- Nova Scotia-area engineering firm with documented practice in reserve fund studies and roof anchor inspections for Halifax-area condominium corporations [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- Authored the February 27, 2021 Reserve Fund Study for Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130 (Summer Gardens, 1470 Summer Street, Halifax) — the predecessor study to the June 11, 2025 Sense Engineering RFS [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- Authored the February 13, 2024 annual roof anchor inspection report for the same corporation — a recurring inspection-cadence practice separate from the 5-year reserve fund study cycle [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- The 5-year-and-2-month interval between the 2021 BRK study and the 2025 Sense study is consistent with the NS Condominium Act's 5-year reserve-fund-study cadence (study report issued at the end of a renewal cycle) [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- The source does not disclose BRK's office address, principal engineers, NS P.Eng licensure details, or broader corporate scope beyond the documented Summer Gardens engagements [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
