# CINC Systems

## Summary

CINC Systems is a community association management (CAM) software platform that operates an open-API partner ecosystem connecting industry-leading software providers across accounting, payments, communications, and reserve planning [[sources/web-2025-01-01-329]]. CINC is one of three CAM platforms integrated with SmartProperty's Living Reserve Study, allowing operating-bank balances to flow into the reserve capital plan [[sources/web-2025-01-01-329]].

## Key facts

- Identified by SmartProperty as one of three integrated CAM platforms (alongside Vantaca and AppFolio) bridging bank-account data and capital reserve plans [[sources/web-2025-01-01-329]].
- Integration consumes operating-account balances and emits them into the Living Reserve Study's real-time cash-flow view per community [[sources/web-2025-01-01-329]].
