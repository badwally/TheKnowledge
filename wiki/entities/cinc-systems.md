# CINC Systems

## Summary

CINC Systems is a community association management (CAM) software platform that operates an open-API partner ecosystem connecting industry-leading software providers across ten functional categories of association management — collections, resale documents, print and mail services, accounts payable, reserve planning, property inspections, vendor screening, staffing, e-filing, and payments [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]]. CINC's partner-ecosystem positioning is built around three named pillars: an open API allowing approved partners to securely connect their solutions, an application and approval process operated by CINC's partnerships team, and a large network of community association management companies that partners gain access to through the integration [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]]. CINC's 2019 investment from Spectrum Equity is credited by company materials as the trigger for the accelerated growth that funded both the core CINC accounting platform and the subsequent build-out of TresRE as the banking-and-payments middleware product [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].

## Key facts

### Partner ecosystem architecture

- Operates an open API allowing approved partners to securely connect their solutions, positioned as ensuring seamless data flow across key property management functions like collections, payments, and vendor management [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Partners with industry-leading providers across ten functional categories: collections, resale documents, print and mail services, accounts payable, reserve planning, property inspections, vendor screening, staffing, e-filing, and payments [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Operates a partnerships team that runs the application and approval process for prospective partner solutions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Stated value proposition to partners: access to a large network of community association management companies, increased exposure through the partner ecosystem, and the opportunity to deliver integrated solutions that improve client operations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Partnership eligibility criterion stated on the public page: the prospective partner's solution must support property management operations such as collections, document management, payments, or vendor services [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- States that CINC welcomes client feedback and regularly evaluates opportunities to expand the network of integrated software solutions based on customer needs [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].

### Named integration partners (2026)

- Collections: Axela, TechCollect [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Resale documents: HomeWise, CondoCerts [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Print and mail services: HOA Mailers, Letter Stream [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Vendor management: VIVE, VendorSmart [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Staffing: Gordian, RocketStation [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Payments: ClickPay [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Four categories appear in CINC's partner-ecosystem framing without named partners on the 2026 page: accounts payable, reserve planning, property inspections, and e-filing [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].

### Investor history

- Spectrum Equity joined CINC Systems as an investor in 2019 [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
- CINC company materials attribute the company's accelerated growth — and the continued innovation provided through both the CINC core platform and the TresRE banking-and-payments solution — to Spectrum Equity's 2019 investment [[sources/web-2023-06-06-844]].
