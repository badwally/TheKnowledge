# Steven (Daisy AI Agent)

## Summary

"Steven" is an AI agent in the Daisy property-management platform whose ownership area is the takeover-onboarding workflow when Daisy assumes management of a new building [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. Steven handles all communication with the prior management company during the transition — chasing down required documents, following up when something is missing, and escalating to humans at decision points [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. Daisy frames Steven as an example of AI agents being built as "actual teammates" with full ownership of a repeatable workflow rather than as an assistant to a human owner [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].

## Key facts

- Component of the Daisy AI agent family, framed by Daisy as "actual teammates" with full workflow ownership rather than assistants to a human owner [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Ownership area: takeover-onboarding workflow when Daisy assumes management of a new building [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Handles all communication with the prior management company during the transition [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Chases down required documents from the prior management company [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Follows up when something is missing from the document set [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Checks in with the Daisy human team when a decision point is reached, rather than autonomously deciding [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Cited by Cohen as the canonical example of how Daisy designs roles around what humans do best while giving agents full ownership of repeatable workflows, allowing the company to scale faster without sacrificing service [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
