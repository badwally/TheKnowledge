# Evan Solomon

## Summary

Evan Solomon is Canada's Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation; in fall 2025 he established an AI Strategy Task Force to define Canada's approach to AI [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].

## Key facts

- Established the AI Strategy Task Force in fall 2025 [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Task-force priorities under Solomon include research and talent; AI adoption across industry and governments; AI commercialization; scaling of startups; safe AI systems and public trust; facilitating skills acquisition; and building public infrastructure [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Has publicly emphasized scaling the AI industry, driving adoption and trust in the technology, and Canadian sovereignty over AI [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Sen (2026) critiques that Solomon's articulated priorities did not explicitly include establishing strong data supply chains for firms [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
