# IRC Section 277 (Membership Organizations)

## Summary

IRC Section 277 is the section of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code that governs the federal income tax treatment of non-exempt membership organizations — including homeowners associations and condominium associations that elect to file Form 1120 (rather than Form 1120-H under IRC §528) and have not obtained exemption under IRC §501(c)(4) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]]. Section 277 is the regime under which Revenue Ruling 70-604 operates as the rollover mechanism for excess member assessment income; absent a proper Rev. Rul. 70-604 election, a §277 association is taxed on its net membership income [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].

## Key facts

- A section of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code that subjects non-exempt membership organizations — including non-exempt HOAs and condominium associations filing Form 1120 — to federal income tax on net membership income [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Revenue Ruling 70-604 is the IRS-promulgated rollover mechanism allowing a §277 association to defer immediate tax on excess member assessment income by applying the excess to the following year's assessments [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Revenue Rulings 75-370 and 75-371 must be considered alongside §277 in determining net membership income; many associations have discovered during IRS audit that they had net membership income they did not know existed because their tax preparer failed to apply the 75-370/75-371 framework [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- An association subject to §277 that fails to properly elect under Rev. Rul. 70-604, or that fails to absorb the rollover in the subsequent year, is taxed on its excess net membership income; in many cases the resulting tax is far greater than would have been owed under Form 1120-H [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- §277 is the default tax regime for an HOA that has not (a) elected Form 1120-H under IRC §528, or (b) obtained exemption under IRC §501(c)(4) [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
