# ClearAlpha

## Summary

Investment firm that publishes thought leadership on multi-manager hedge fund construction; authored the 2023 paper "The Math of the Multi-Manager: Fad or Better Mousetrap?" by Brian Hurst, which argues that the structural advantage of multi-manager funds over single-strategy funds and traditional hedge fund portfolios comes from low-correlation strategy diversification and cash-efficient implementation [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].

## Key facts

- Published "The Math of the Multi-Manager: Fad or Better Mousetrap?" (2023), authored by Brian Hurst, branded "Thought leadership from ClearAlpha™" [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- Maintains additional whitepapers available upon request, including "The Evolution of Alpha" (other reasons for the multi-manager fund structure's success) and "Building a Diversified Alpha Portfolio - Why do Correlations all go to 1.0?" [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- The "Math of the Multi-Manager" paper offers a spreadsheet model — used to generate Figure 1 (Sharpe vs strategy count) and Figure 2 (Sharpe and volatility vs correlation) — available upon request [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
- Position summarized in the paper: the "special sauce" in multi-manager fund portfolios is portfolio construction math — finding and allocating to enough lowly correlated strategies and implementing them in a cash-efficient manner [[sources/pdf-7663c24d3159]].
