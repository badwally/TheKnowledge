# Snaile Lockers

## Summary

Snaile Lockers is a parcel locker vendor for condominiums and apartments, integrated with both UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental as one of three named smart-locker partners in the UpperBee integration portfolio [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]]. The product is positioned as supporting acceptance of deliveries from any carrier for all owners and tenants from day one [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Parcel locker solution for condominiums and apartments [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Integrated with UpperBee Condo and UpperBee Rental property management systems [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Carrier-agnostic positioning: accepts deliveries from any carrier for all owners and tenants from day one [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- One of three named smart-locker partners in UpperBee's integration portfolio alongside Expedibox and BlueBox [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Public-facing site: snailelockers.com [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
