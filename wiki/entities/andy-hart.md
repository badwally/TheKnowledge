# Andy Hart

## Summary

Andy Hart, formerly at the Food and Environment Research Agency (Fera), provided particular encouragement during the 'Bayesian analysis in microbial risk assessment' project that contributed to the development of the Sheffield Elicitation Framework (SHELF).

## Key facts

- Hart was formerly at the Food and Environment Research Agency (Fera) [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
- Hart's particular encouragement, in the context of the 'Bayesian analysis in microbial risk assessment' project, is cited as part of the principal spur for the development of SHELF [[sources/web-2026-05-13-b67]].
