# Brivo

## Summary

Brivo is a building access system vendor identified by BuildingLink as one of three named "top providers" integrated with the BuildingLink CAM platform for automated entry, key fob management, and visitor access tracking, alongside ButterflyMX and HID [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].

## Key facts

- Identified as one of three named "top providers" of building access systems integrated with BuildingLink [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Integration capabilities span automated entry, key fob management, and visitor access tracking from a single platform [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Marketed integration outcomes per BuildingLink: simplified security, reduced manual data entry, enhanced resident experience, and a clear record of who is coming and going [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
- Listed in BuildingLink's Canadian-facing (en-ca) integration portfolio [[sources/web-2025-03-13-d73]].
