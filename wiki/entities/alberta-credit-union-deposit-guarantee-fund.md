# Alberta Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Fund

## Summary

The Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Fund (the "Fund") is the second of the three statutory levels of deposit protection for Alberta credit union depositors established under the Alberta *Credit Union Act*. The Fund is funded by Alberta credit unions through regular assessments, managed by the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation (CUDGC), and invested in high quality, liquid investments [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]]. The Fund's role in the protection hierarchy is to satisfy depositor claims after the failing credit union's own capital (level one) is exhausted but before any draw on the Government of Alberta backstop (level three) [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].

## Key facts

- Established as the second-level protection mechanism in the three-level deposit protection system created by the Alberta *Credit Union Act* [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Funded by Alberta credit unions through regular assessments [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Managed by the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Invested in high quality, liquid investments [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Functions as the intermediate layer between credit-union-level capital (level one) and the Government of Alberta backstop (level three) [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
- Structurally analogous to FSRA's Deposit Insurance Reserve Fund (Ontario) — pre-funded by member institutions and managed by the provincial deposit-insurer body [[sources/web-2000-01-01-611]].
