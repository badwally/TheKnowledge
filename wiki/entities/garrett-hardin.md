# Garrett Hardin

## Summary

Garrett Hardin is the author associated with "The Tragedy of the Commons," the framework holding that common-pool resources will inevitably be depleted by rational, self-interested actors; the source under citation spells his first name as "Garret" [[sources/web-2022-09-01-460]].

## Key facts

- Authored "The Tragedy of the Commons," the dominant pre-Ostrom interpretation of common-pool resource dynamics [[sources/web-2022-09-01-460]].
- His model was the primary intellectual foil for Elinor Ostrom's 1990 *Governing the Commons*, which argued that Hardin's framework was too abstract and ignored real commoning institutions [[sources/web-2022-09-01-460]].
