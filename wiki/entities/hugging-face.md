# Hugging Face

## Summary

AI platform company that hosts the Hugging Face Hub for open-source models, datasets, and the Diffusers inference library; institutional home of the authors of the 2025 paper "Video Killed the Energy Budget" [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].

## Key facts

- All three authors of "Video Killed the Energy Budget" — Julien Delavande, Regis Pierrard, and Sasha Luccioni — list Hugging Face affiliations on the paper [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- The paper relies on Hugging Face's Diffusers library (von Platen et al., 2022) for inference benchmarks of open text-to-video models, using its default generation parameters [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- The paper notes that WAN2.1 was, at the time of writing, the most-downloaded text-to-video model on the Hugging Face Hub, motivating its selection as the reference model [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- The paper releases all generated videos publicly on the Hugging Face Hub as supplementary material, alongside an anonymized GitHub repository for code, prompts, and configurations [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
