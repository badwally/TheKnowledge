# Yotpo

## Summary

Yotpo is an ecommerce marketing platform centered on user-generated content (UGC) — ratings, reviews, photo reviews, and social-proof widgets — that has expanded into customer loyalty, SMS marketing, and email under one integrated platform [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]]. On Capterra it is positioned across five categories (Customer Engagement, Customer Experience, Customer Loyalty, Review Management, SMS Marketing) and held a 4.5-star average across 376 verified reviews as of June 2026 [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].

For the orita-cmo competitive map, Yotpo is a martech-consolidation exemplar: a single vendor selling reviews + loyalty + SMS + email into the same Shopify-skewed SMB retail buyer Orita targets, overlapping with Klaviyo, Postscript, Attentive, Omnisend, and Okendo across different surfaces of the stack [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].

## Key facts

- Yotpo's stated value proposition is end-to-end ownership of customer content — automated or AI-moderated collection, customizable widget display at conversion points, and integrated marketing on UGC — in one platform rather than stitched-together point tools [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Capterra-surfaced category placement: review management, customer experience, customer loyalty, customer engagement, and SMS marketing [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Pricing as of June 2026: Pro plan starts at $199/month flat rate; Premium plan at $1,198/month; SMS and email usage are billed separately on top of the base plan [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Plan limits: Pro bundles loyalty, reviews, SMS and email and supports up to 500 orders/month; Premium raises the cap to 1,000 orders/month and adds onboarding and support [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- No free trial and no free version are offered as of June 2026 [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Capterra aggregate ratings (n=376, June 2026): Overall 4.5, Ease of Use 4.4, Features 4.4, Customer Service 4.5, Value for Money 3.9, Likelihood to Recommend 7.2/10, with review sentiment 91% positive / 6% neutral / 3% negative [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Customer mix from Capterra reviewer profiles: 93% small business, 5% midsize, 2% enterprise; top industries Retail 30–37%, Consumer Goods 12–25%, Apparel & Fashion 10–15%, Cosmetics 13% [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Top reviewer job functions: Marketing 30%, Entrepreneurs/Owners 30%, Administrative 13%, Creative & Design 7% [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Integrations: Shopify and HubSpot Marketing Hub are the reviewer-confirmed primary integrations, with 50+ additional integrations including BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Gorgias, Instagram, and AdRoll covering storefront, customer service, and campaign workflows [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- 2025 Capterra Shortlist recognition in five categories: Customer Engagement, Customer Experience, Customer Loyalty, Review Management, and SMS Marketing [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Most-cited pro on Capterra: responsive and knowledgeable support team (90% positive across 125 mentions); most-cited con: high and restrictive pricing structure with key features locked behind costly tiers and unclear plan details (42% negative across 84 mentions) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Quick and simple setup is the second-most-cited pro (96% positive across 23 mentions), particularly for major ecommerce platform integrations [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Support channels offered include email/help desk, FAQ/forum, knowledge base, phone support, 24/7 live representatives, and chat; training options include in-person training, live online sessions, webinars, and documentation [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Capterra-listed alternatives to Yotpo: Birdeye, Okendo, ReputationStacker, Weave, and TrueReview, plus the featured comparison product TrueReview ($49/month, 5.0 from 1,204 reviews) [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
- Reviewer-identified problem set: low review volume, weak product-page trust, and manual post-purchase follow-up — Yotpo targets marketing, ecommerce, and customer service teams turning customer photos, ratings, and feedback into social proof, repeat purchases, SEO visibility, and faster product improvement [[sources/web-2026-06-15-809]].
