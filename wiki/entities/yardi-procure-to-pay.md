# Yardi Procure to Pay

## Summary

Yardi Procure to Pay is one of three named add-on modules that Yardi positions as extensions to its Voyager Condo, Co-op & HOA core platform, alongside Yardi Payment Processing and Yardi Aspire [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]]. Yardi's marketing copy frames the module as part of building a "comprehensive condo, co-op and HOA management solution" when combined with Voyager Condo and the CondoCafe resident portal [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].

## Key facts

- Identified by Yardi as an add-on module that extends Voyager Condo, Co-op & HOA [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- One of three named modules (Yardi Payment Processing, Yardi Procure to Pay, Yardi Aspire) marketed as the extensions that build a comprehensive condo/co-op/HOA management solution [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
- The Voyager Condo product page does not enumerate the specific functional capabilities, pricing, or deployment model of Yardi Procure to Pay beyond the module-name positioning [[sources/web-2026-02-18-d33]].
