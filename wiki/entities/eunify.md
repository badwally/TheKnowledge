# eUnify

## Summary

eUnify is a community association management (CAM) software product named on Western Alliance Bank's WA ConnectLive product page as one of 11 industry software programs the bank's HOA API platform integrates with [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].

## Key facts

- Named as an integration partner on Western Alliance Bank's WA ConnectLive product page [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Sits in the WA ConnectLive integration roster alongside AppFolio, Buildium, C3, Caliber by Frontsteps, CINC, Enumerate, Smartwebs, SPI, Vantaca, and VMS [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
- Per the WA ConnectLive product caveat, integration availability and feature parity may depend on the accounting software used [[sources/web-2026-01-01-962]].
