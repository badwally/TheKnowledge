# Gordian

## Summary

Gordian is a staffing vendor named by CINC Systems as one of two staffing-category software partners (alongside RocketStation) in CINC's published software partner ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]]. CINC's open API enables Gordian to securely connect to the CINC platform for data flow across staffing workflows in community association management operations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].

## Key facts

- Named by CINC Systems as one of two staffing-category integration partners in CINC's published software partner list [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- The other named staffing partner in the CINC ecosystem is RocketStation [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Connects to CINC via CINC's open API, which allows approved partners to securely connect their solutions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
- Integration positioned by CINC as ensuring seamless data flow across key property management functions [[sources/web-2026-01-01-c87]].
