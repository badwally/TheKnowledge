# CCI Golden Horseshoe Chapter (CCI GHC)

## Summary

The CCI Golden Horseshoe Chapter (CCI GHC) is a regional chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute serving the Golden Horseshoe region of southern Ontario. CCI GHC publishes the CCI GHC Condo News Magazine, a practitioner-facing quarterly carrying articles on condominium governance, finance, and Ontario regulatory developments contributed by lawyers, accountants, investment advisors, and management professionals [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].

## Key facts

- Regional chapter of the Canadian Condominium Institute serving the Golden Horseshoe region of southern Ontario [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Publishes the CCI GHC Condo News Magazine; Volume 12 was the Summer 2022 issue [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The Summer 2022 issue included "Investing the Reserve Fund: How to Maximize Return and Minimize Risk" by Will MacKay, CFP, CIM (CIBC Wood Gundy, Mississauga), an Ontario §115(5)-focused investment-strategy piece for condominium boards [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- The June 22, 2022 chapter publication date for the MacKay article confirms an active practitioner-publishing cadence as of mid-2022 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
- Chapter articles are cataloged on CCI National's resource centre at cci.ca (e.g., the MacKay piece at cci.ca/resource-centre/view/1268), confirming cross-chapter editorial syndication through the national portal [[sources/web-2026-01-01-040]].
