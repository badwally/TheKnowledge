# Percel Inc.

## Summary

Percel Inc. is an Ontario condominium management firm whose president and CEO Robert Weinberg commented on manager compensation in the May 2023 ACMO statement [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key facts

- Robert Weinberg is president and CEO of Percel Inc. [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- Weinberg stated that property managers manage multi-million dollar corporations and protect what is likely the largest financial investment made by owners, and should be paid commensurate to their expertise, experience and importance [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Sources

- [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]] — "Who will manage all the future condos in Ontario?" REMI Network, May 31, 2023.
