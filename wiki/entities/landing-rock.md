# Landing Rock

## Summary

Landing Rock is an online cash management provider that offers an Insured Deposit Account (IDA) product designed to help community associations earn higher returns and safeguard their Reserve Funds, comparable in posture to a Savings or Money Market account [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. The IDA distributes deposits across federally insured credit unions and well-capitalized banks and savings institutions to provide up to $50 million of FDIC and NCUA insurance per association, with next-day liquidity and no withdrawal limits or fees [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. Landing Rock is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].

## Key facts

- Online cash management provider [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Core product: Insured Deposit Account (IDA), positioned as comparable to a Savings or Money Market account [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Marketed product features: highly competitive interest rates; up to $50 million of FDIC and NCUA insurance; next-day liquidity; no withdrawal limits or fees; high earnings credits for association managers [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Operational architecture: links to the association's existing operating bank account; Reserve funds are transferred in and out via ACH whenever necessary [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Deposits and interest in the IDA are federally insured and distributed among credit unions and well-capitalized banks and savings institutions as defined by FDIC and NCUA regulations [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Provides 24/7 access to account information via the website [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Named banking partner in the Vantaca partner directory [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
