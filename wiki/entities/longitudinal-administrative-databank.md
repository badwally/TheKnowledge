# Longitudinal Administrative Databank (LAD)

## Summary

The Longitudinal Administrative Databank (LAD) is a Statistics Canada administrative dataset: a 20% sample of all Canadian tax filers with annual records from 1982 onward, linked over time at the individual level [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].

## Key facts

- 20% sample of all Canadian tax filers, with annual data from 1982 onward [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Links individuals through tax files and other sources; individual records are linked across years [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Includes demographic details (age, gender, marital status) [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Captures income sources including wages, self-employment, social assistance, registered retirement savings plans, investments, pensions, and child tax benefits [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Includes geographic location at the level of provinces and census divisions [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Contains immigration-status details: landing year, immigration category, and education level [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
- Sen (2026) cites the LAD as an example of a Statistics Canada dataset that would be invaluable for training ML models for credit-risk and default prediction, fraud-detection pre-training, labour-market forecasting, and customer segmentation, and which private-sector firms cannot replicate [[sources/web-2026-03-26-f82]].
