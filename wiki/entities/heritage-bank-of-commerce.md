# Heritage Bank of Commerce

## Summary

Heritage Bank of Commerce is a Silicon Valley-based relationship business bank, FDIC member, that operates a dedicated HOA banking practice marketed as providing a banking experience associated with larger institutions through continual investment in technology, products, and services [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]]. Heritage Bank of Commerce is enrolled as a named banking partner in Vantaca's CAM integration ecosystem [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].

## Key facts

- Self-described as the premier relationship-based business bank based in Silicon Valley [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Member FDIC [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Operates a dedicated HOA specialist practice providing a suite of banking products and services to improve management company and association operating efficiencies [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Marketed delivery model: personal service, customized products, and access to key decision makers [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Stated investment posture: continually invests in technology, products, and services to deliver the type of banking experience associated with larger institutions [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
- Named banking partner in the Vantaca partner directory [[sources/web-2026-04-02-20c]].
