# Matthew Ryan

## Summary

Discretionary day trader and author of "The Study: Using the '2-Day AVWAP Rule + Momentum Signal' in Day Trading to become Consistently Profitable" (April 2023), a 14-page trading manual that proposes a three-pillar framework — market context (2-day anchored VWAP plus Tradytics Market Net Flow), execution (a re-parameterized Stochastic indicator he renames the Momentum Signal), and risk management (bracket orders, hard 20% stop on options, never-lower-stop discipline) — and reports backtest results of 72% bullish and 76% bearish accuracy [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].

## Key facts

- Authored "The Study: Using the '2-Day AVWAP Rule + Momentum Signal' in Day Trading to become Consistently Profitable" (April 2023) [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- Frames consistent profitability as requiring a repeatable, systematic process with a win rate of 70% or higher, achieved by mastering three aspects he labels Market Context, Execution, and Risk Management [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- Argues that new traders lack consistency because of a limited understanding of these three key aspects, and that none should be favored over the others [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- Specifies the toolset: multiple Anchored VWAP for trend, a re-parameterized Stochastic for execution (renamed the Momentum Signal), bracket orders with automatic stop-loss and take-profit, and Tradytics Market Net Flow as an additional tool [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- Reports backtest results: 36 wins / 14 losses bullish (72% accuracy) and 38 wins / 12 losses bearish (76% accuracy), and concludes the strategy is effective but requires patience — "no setup, no trade" [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- Trading-psychology stance: "do not focus on outcome, focus on process," and uses tick-based targets rather than dollar amounts to teach his trading brain to focus on process [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- Implements his own understanding of Gamma/Vanna levels for every trade and recommends an advanced understanding of dealer hedging to readers, though the document itself does not cover those topics [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
