# Regis Pierrard

## Summary

AI researcher at Hugging Face and co-author of the 2025 paper "Video Killed the Energy Budget," which develops a compute-bound analytical model of latency and energy for open text-to-video diffusion systems and validates it on WAN2.1-T2V-1.3B [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Hugging Face on the paper, with email regis.pierrard@huggingface.co [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
- Co-authored "Video Killed the Energy Budget" with Julien Delavande (lead) and Sasha Luccioni [[sources/pdf-julien-delavande-2025-video-killed-the]].
