# Trico Case

## Summary

A "Trico case" is a bicycle travel case used by [[entities/studio-velo|Studio Velo]] when packing customer bikes for shipping under its paid "Pack Bike for Shipping" labor service [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].

## Key facts

- Studio Velo's "Pack Bike for Shipping" service uses a Trico case as the destination container for the customer's bike [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- A spare tire can be packed alongside the bike inside the case [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
- Studio Velo records on the work order which tools the customer will need on the receiving end to reverse the pack-out (e.g., a BB wrench to re-install rotors), implying that some components are removed before the bike is placed in the case [[sources/pdf-a5ea98378b0a]].
