# Ellen R. Alemany

## Summary

Ellen R. Alemany served as Chairwoman and Chief Executive Officer of CIT Group Inc. at the time CIT Bank, N.A. completed its $1 billion acquisition of Mutual of Omaha Bank on January 1, 2020 — the transaction that added $4.5 billion of community association deposits to CIT and consolidated the market-leading HOA banking franchise into CIT's commercial banking segment [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].

## Key facts

- Held the dual title Chairwoman and Chief Executive Officer of CIT Group Inc. as of January 2, 2020 [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Named as the official CIT spokesperson on the January 2, 2020 press release announcing close of the Mutual of Omaha Bank acquisition [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Characterized the Mutual of Omaha Bank acquisition as accelerating CIT's strategic plan to further enhance its capability as a leading national bank and create additional long-term shareholder value [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Stated that the addition of the homeowner association deposit channel had significant growth potential and would reduce CIT's overall cost of funds [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Stated that the middle market banking franchise added by the acquisition would expand CIT's footprint and customer base [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Stated post-close objective: welcome teammates and clients of Mutual of Omaha Bank, strengthen existing relationships, build new ones, and continue delivering value for customers, colleagues, shareholders and communities [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
