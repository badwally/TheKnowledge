# Lovable

**Archetype:** AI-native indie SaaS (app builder)

AI-native app builder; users describe an app and AI generates it.

## Why it's in this domain

**$50M ARR with <30 employees; burned only $2M to reach the first $30M ARR.** Explicitly rejected Y Combinator to maintain strict lean focus. Cited as the canonical capital-efficiency exemplar of the AI-native indie SaaS archetype — and as a counter-narrative to "AI startups must raise mega-rounds."
