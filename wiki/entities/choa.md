# Condominium Home Owners' Association of British Columbia (CHOA)

## Summary

The Condominium Home Owners' Association of British Columbia (CHOA) is a BC-based advocacy and educational organization that provides leadership, education, and resources for strata owners across British Columbia. CHOA publishes a numbered Bulletin series — including Bulletin 600-003 on investing contingency reserve fund and special levy money — that operationalizes the Strata Property Act and its regulations for board members, owners, and strata managers [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].

## Key facts

- CHOA's stated mission is providing leadership, education, and resources for strata owners across British Columbia [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Operates a public website at www.choa.bc.ca and a toll-free contact line at 1.877.353.2462 [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Publishes a numbered Bulletin series; Bulletin 600-003 covers investing of the contingency reserve fund or special levy and was most recently updated December 2022 [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Bulletins explicitly disclaim that they contain general information only and are not intended as legal advice; CHOA, its authors, and related entities disclaim liability for losses arising from use of the publication [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
- Bulletins explicitly restrict reproduction, blogging, or distribution without prior consent of the writer [[sources/pdf-dheide-2022-microsoft-word-600]].
