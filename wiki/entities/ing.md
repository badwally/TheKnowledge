# ING

## Summary

Dutch global financial-services group whose THINK economic and financial analysis team published the December 8, 2022 "ING global economic outlook 2023" titled "May he live in interesting times," a 46-page macro outlook framing 2023 as a year of "different shades of recession" across the US, eurozone, UK, and China and offering base-case forecasts alongside upside and downside scenarios for energy prices, growth, inflation, and central-bank policy [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].

## Key facts

- Publishes economic and financial analysis under the THINK brand at www.ing.com/THINK [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Authored "ING global economic outlook 2023" (December 8, 2022), led by Carsten Brzeski (Global Head of Macro and Chief Economist, Eurozone, Germany, Austria) [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- ING's three calls for 2023 are framed as "Recession, inflation and central banks" — Brzeski's base case [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Contributing team for the outlook includes Rob Carnell (Regional Head of Research, Asia-Pacific), Padhraic Garvey (Head of Global Debt and Rates Strategy / Regional Head of Research, Americas), James Knightley (Chief International Economist, Americas), Iris Pang (Chief Economist, Greater China), James Smith (Economist, Developed Markets), Chris Turner (Global Head of Markets and Regional Head of Research, UK & CEE), and Frantisek Taborsky (EMEA FX & FI Strategist) [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Frames the 2023 forecasts as offering "the widest range of possible outcomes and forecasts in years," explicitly excluding new black, grey, or pink swans like another pandemic or a war that markets did not have on their radar [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Continues a tradition of stress-testing assumptions and central forecasts by publishing upside and downside scenarios alongside its base case for energy prices and for the US, eurozone, UK, and Chinese economies [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
