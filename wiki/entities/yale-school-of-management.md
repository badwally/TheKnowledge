# Yale School of Management

## Summary

Graduate business school at Yale University; institutional home of Bryan Kelly, first-listed author (jointly with AQR Capital Management and NBER) of the 2023 SSRN survey "Financial Machine Learning" with Dacheng Xiu of the University of Chicago Booth School of Business [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the first of three institutional affiliations for Bryan Kelly on "Financial Machine Learning" (2023), alongside AQR Capital Management and NBER [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
- Bryan Kelly's contact address on the paper resolves to the school via the email bryan.kelly@yale.edu [[sources/pdf-e2068d89c2a7]].
