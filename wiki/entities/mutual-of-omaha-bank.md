# Mutual of Omaha Bank

## Summary

Mutual of Omaha Bank was the bank subsidiary of Mutual of Omaha Insurance Co. that operated the market-leading community association banking business in the United States prior to its January 1, 2020 acquisition by CIT Bank, N.A. [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]]. The acquired franchise included $6.8 billion in deposits, $4.5 billion of which were community association (HOA) deposits, plus $8.3 billion of total assets including $3.9 billion of middle-market commercial loans (figures as of September 30, 2019) [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]]. Following close, the bank's retail branch locations transitioned to the CIT Bank brand [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].

## Key facts

### Pre-acquisition profile

- Operated the market-leading community association banking business in the United States as of the January 2020 acquisition announcement [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Owned by Mutual of Omaha Insurance Co., which received approximately 3.1 million shares of CIT stock as part of the acquisition consideration [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Operated retail branch locations under the Mutual of Omaha Bank brand prior to acquisition [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Operated relationship banking teams that CIT identified as a strategic asset extending CIT's commercial banking capabilities [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].

### Deposit and loan franchise at close (Sept. 30, 2019 pro forma)

- Total deposits: $6.8 billion [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Community association deposits: $4.5 billion (approximately 66% of total deposits) [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Total assets: $8.3 billion [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Middle-market commercial loans: $3.9 billion [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Deposit and asset figures based on unaudited CIT and Mutual of Omaha Bank pro forma data as of September 30, 2019 [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].

### Acquisition by CIT Bank, N.A.

- Acquired by CIT Bank, N.A. on January 1, 2020 [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- Total transaction value: approximately $1 billion ($850 million cash + ~3.1 million shares of CIT stock) [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- CIT's stated rationale for the acquisition: add a stable, lower-cost homeowner association deposit channel from the market-leading community association banking business [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- CIT also identified relationship banking teams and expanded product and technology solutions as deal value drivers [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].

### Brand and customer transition

- Scheduled to begin transitioning to the CIT brand following close, with retail branch locations adopting the CIT Bank brand over the coming months [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- At close, customer accounts remained unchanged and continued to be accessible through Mutual of Omaha Bank branches, website, mobile apps, and relationship managers [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
- CIT CEO Ellen R. Alemany described the post-close objective as strengthening existing relationships, building new ones, and continuing to deliver value for customers, colleagues, shareholders and communities [[sources/web-2020-01-02-b60]].
