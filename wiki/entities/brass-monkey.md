# Brass Monkey

## Summary

UK-based cold-water-immersion company (brassmonkey.co.uk) founded in late 2020 by a Wim Hof Method practitioner; publisher of a free 52-page DCE/CWI guide that synthesizes cold-exposure science from Wim Hof, Daniel Kluken, Dr Rhonda Patrick, Prof. Huberman, and Tim Ferriss into a customer-facing handbook on building a cold-water-immersion practice [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].

## Key facts

- Sells ice baths that can be set between 0–10°c [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Founded in late 2020 by an author who first encountered the cold via the Wim Hof Method in late 2018, including in-person training in Poland with Wim Hof himself [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- The author explicitly disclaims being a scientist or "cold expert," framing the guide as a curation of credible sources rather than original research [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Defines "cold" operationally as anywhere below 16°c (per the studies the guide references) and a "typical ice bath" as 5–10°c [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Treats the rule "uncomfortably cold" as personal and time-varying — depending on mental state, tiredness, post-workout/sauna context, or recent illness [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Operates a feedback channel (dce@brassmonkey.co.uk) for reader corrections and updates the guide several times per year [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
- Names its primary sources for mapping the science as Wim Hof and the Wim Hof team (especially Daniel Kluken), Dr Rhonda Patrick, Prof. Huberman, and Tim Ferriss [[sources/pdf-291109666e00]].
