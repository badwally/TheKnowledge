# Condo Control AI Property Manager Assistant

## Summary

The Condo Control AI Property Manager Assistant is an AI feature suite within the Condo Control CAM platform (a Property Control sister product) marketed to property managers as a way to deflect routine resident inquiries, accelerate first-draft communications, and produce board-ready summaries of long financial and governance documents [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]]. It is positioned by the vendor as a productivity layer for property managers handling portfolio workloads, not as a quantitative analytics or reserve-adequacy engine [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].

## Key facts

- The product is marketed under the umbrella label "Condo Control AI" and groups five capabilities: resident self-serve Q&A (24/7), Manager Communication Co-Pilot, instant document and financial summaries, operational suggestions, and multilingual communication [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- The resident self-serve Q&A answers questions about community rules, amenities, and processes with the stated goal of deflecting routine inquiries from property managers [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- The Manager Communication Co-Pilot drafts announcements, building notices, emails, and FAQs, with the marketing claim of producing first drafts "in seconds" and maintaining consistent tone across communities [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- The document-summary capability accepts reserve fund studies, budgets, financial reports, and meeting materials as inputs and returns "highlights, key takeaways, and clear next steps"; the marketed function is document Q&A and summarization, not reserve adequacy analysis or probabilistic forecasting [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- A specific document-summary tier is branded "Condo Control Certified PM Advanced" and is positioned around uploading "any reserve study, budget, financial report, or lengthy document" for instant highlights [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- The multilingual capability is advertised as supporting communication and document simplification in "150+ languages" [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- The marketing copy frames the AI as augmenting rather than replacing the property manager, with managers "in control of what gets shared" [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- The product is positioned for portfolio-scale property managers, with vendor messaging emphasizing manager-to-door ratio expansion without proportional headcount growth [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
- The source page makes no claims about underlying model provider, training corpus, integration architecture, customer adoption counts, pricing, or regulatory posture [[sources/web-2026-02-20-7dd]].
