# California Civil Code §5570 (Reserve Funding Disclosure Form)

## Summary

Civil Code §5570 is the section of California's Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act that prescribes the Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary — a standardized seven-item form that every California common interest development association must summarize and deliver annually alongside the budget report required by §5300 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]]. The section also supplies the operative definitions for "estimated remaining useful life" and "major component" (the latter by reference to §5550), the optional treatment of components with more than 30 years of remaining useful life, and the component-method formula for calculating the reserve amount needed for a given component at a given time [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]]. §5570 was amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 349, Sec. 1, effective January 1, 2016 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].

## Key facts

- §5570(a) requires the disclosures required by the article to be summarized on the prescribed Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary form [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- The form is organized around seven numbered items: (1) regular assessment per ownership interest; (2) additional regular or special assessments already scheduled to be imposed; (3) whether projected reserve account balances will be sufficient at the end of each year to meet repair/replacement obligations for major components during the next 30 years (yes/no); (4) if no, what additional assessments or contributions would be necessary to ensure sufficient reserves each year over the next 30 years that have not yet been approved; (5) confirmation that all major components are included in the reserve study and its calculations; (6) the estimated amount required in the reserve fund at the end of the current fiscal year (using the §5570(b)(4) calculation), the projected reserve fund cash balance at year end, and the resulting percent-funded figure; (7) the corresponding five-year forward projection of required reserves, projected cash balances, and percent-funded figures both before and after the approved funding plan [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- The form must also disclose the assumed long-term before-tax interest rate earned on reserve funds and the assumed long-term inflation rate applied to major component repair and replacement costs [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- The financial representations in the summary are based on the preparer's best estimates at that time and are subject to change [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- §5570(b)(1) defines "Estimated remaining useful life" as the time reasonably calculated to remain before a major component will require replacement [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- §5570(b)(2) defines "Major component" by reference to Civil Code §5550 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- §5570(b)(2) permits components with an estimated remaining useful life of more than 30 years to be included in a reserve study as a capital asset or disregarded from the reserve calculation, so long as the decision is revealed in the reserve study report and reported in the Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- §5570(b)(3) requires the form to accompany each annual budget report or summary delivered pursuant to §5300, and permits supplementation or modification provided the minimum information in §5570(a) is provided [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- §5570(b)(4) specifies the component-method calculation: the amount of reserves needed to be accumulated for a component at a given time is computed as the current cost of replacement or repair multiplied by the number of years the component has been in service divided by the useful life of the component [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- §5570(b)(4) explicitly states the calculation is not to be construed to require the board to fund reserves in accordance with this calculation [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Section 7(2) of the form supports two reserve-amount calculations in parallel: the §5570(b)(4) statutory calculation and an alternate, generally accepted method of calculation, with an explanation attached when both are provided [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 349, Sec. 1, effective January 1, 2016 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
