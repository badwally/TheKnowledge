# FirstOntario Credit Union

## Summary

FirstOntario Credit Union is an Ontario credit union that operates a dedicated Condominium Services product line for condominium boards and property managers, covering both the investment and lending sides of a condominium community's balance sheet [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]]. The product line is positioned as a tailored program developed with input from industry leaders, property managers, boards, and residents, and is built around four named components: a Reserve Fund Investment Planning Tool, custom deposit rates, consultative lending solutions, and FirstOntario membership benefits for residents of client communities [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].

## Key facts

- Operates a dedicated Condominium Services product line for condominium boards and property managers, positioned as covering both the investment and lending sides of a condominium community's balance sheet [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].
- Frames the Condominium Services program as developed in collaboration with industry leaders, property managers, boards, and residents [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].
- Offers a Reserve Fund Investment Planning Tool: a custom 10-year investment plan developed in collaboration with the client condominium's board and property manager, marketed as maximizing returns while supporting long-term financial stability [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].
- Markets "custom rates" for condominium deposits, framed as a blend of top market rates with flexibility to access funds during unforeseen circumstances [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].
- Offers a consultative lending approach to condominium communities, marketed as safeguarding residents and facilities while supporting long-term financial sustainability as community needs evolve [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].
- Extends FirstOntario membership benefits to residents of client condominium communities, including investment bonuses, lending discounts, and educational financial literacy seminars [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].
- Product-page URL: firstontario.com/business/condominium-services [[sources/web-2026-04-26-bf8]].
