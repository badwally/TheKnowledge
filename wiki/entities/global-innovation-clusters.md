# Canada's Global Innovation Clusters

## Summary

Canada's Global Innovation Clusters are a federal industrial-innovation program, administered by Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED), that funds regional innovation ecosystems organized around specified technology focus areas [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]]. The Scale AI Cluster — focused on AI-powered supply chains, based in Quebec, with up to $284 million in federal funding — is one of these clusters [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].

## Key facts

- The Scale AI Cluster is the Global Innovation Cluster focused on AI-powered supply chains; it is headquartered in Quebec and convenes the retail, manufacturing, transportation, infrastructure, healthcare, and ICT sectors [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Scale AI's federal funding allocation is up to $284 million [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Cluster activity (per the Scale AI example) is organized around four program streams: industry-led projects, acceleration (startup/SME support with commercial-partner matching), workforce development, and ecosystem showcase / global presence [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
- Cluster service offerings, as described for Scale AI, are positioned as end-to-end commercialization and ecosystem support — funding, matchmaking, training, and global-presence activity — rather than as data-sharing infrastructure [[sources/web-2018-02-15-c35]].
