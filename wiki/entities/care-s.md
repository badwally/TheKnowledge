# CARE-S (Computer Aided Rehabilitation for Sewer and Stormwater Networks)

## Summary

CARE-S (Computer Aided Rehabilitation for Sewer and Stormwater Networks) is a research project whose methodologies were integrated by the subsequent AWARE-P platform, extending CARE-W's water-network focus to sewer and stormwater infrastructure.

## Key facts

- CARE-S (Computer Aided Rehabilitation for Sewer and Stormwater Networks) was published by IWA Publishing in 2006 as a 140-page volume edited by S. Saegrov (ISBN 1843391155) [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
- CARE-S is cited alongside CARE-W as one of the prior R&D projects whose methodologies AWARE-P integrates, providing analytical foundations for rehabilitation planning of sewer and stormwater networks [[sources/web-2012-01-01-57d]].
