# Hg Capital

## Summary

Hg Capital is the lead PE sponsor of CINC Systems and the named primary acquirer-target sponsor in the condo-capital-infra exit thesis under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]]. As of the August 5, 2025 Cephai+ adoption announcement, Hg's stated cross-portfolio comparative framing — articulated by Hg Partner and CINC Board Member Farouk Hussein — positions CINC's Cephai+ as having product-market fit "among the strongest we've seen" across the dozens of AI-enabled companies Hg has partnered with over the prior two years, surfacing Hg as the load-bearing investor-side validator of CINC's AI-first growth strategy [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]]. Hg's continued co-sponsorship of CINC alongside Spectrum Equity is confirmed in the August 2025 CINC boilerplate ("Backed by Hg Capital and Spectrum Equity") and is consistent with the December 14, 2023 Hg strategic-growth-investment entry and the September 16, 2025 CINC Connect launch boilerplate, establishing a continuous Hg co-sponsor tenure across the December 2023 → August 2025 → September 2025 window [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

## Key facts

### Cross-portfolio AI investment posture (August 2025 framing)

- Hg Partner Farouk Hussein states (August 5, 2025): "Hg has partnered with dozens of AI-enabled companies over the past two years" — establishing dozens of AI-enabled portfolio companies as the Hg-side comparative baseline for evaluating CINC's Cephai+ product-market fit [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Hussein characterizes CINC's Cephai+ customer demand and product-market fit as "among the strongest we've seen" across Hg's two-year AI-portfolio cohort — a load-bearing primary-source cross-portfolio ranking that elevates Cephai+ to a top-tier position within Hg's AI-investment thesis [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Hussein frames Cephai+ as "yet another extraordinary value creation and growth vector for CINC and its clients" — signaling Hg's appetite for additional Cephai+-style adjacent-capability investments at CINC and supporting the strategic case for AI-native reserve-study acquisitions through the project's Year-3 exit window [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

### CINC Systems sponsorship continuity

- Hg is identified in the August 5, 2025 CINC Cephai+ announcement boilerplate as a co-sponsor of CINC alongside Spectrum Equity ("Backed by Hg Capital and Spectrum Equity") — confirming continued co-sponsorship as of August 2025 [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Farouk Hussein holds a board seat at CINC Systems as a representative of Hg Capital — surfacing Hussein as the named Hg-side relationship anchor on CINC's board [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- CINC's August 2025 scale snapshot under Hg + Spectrum co-sponsorship: more than 50,000 communities powered through CINC's single, integrated, AI-powered platform unifying accounting, banking, operations, and resident engagement across all community types from single-family homes and condominiums to high-rises and lifestyle communities [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].

### Implication for condo-capital-infra exit thesis

- Hg's stated cross-portfolio comparative framing positioning Cephai+ as having product-market fit "among the strongest we've seen" across dozens of recent AI-enabled investments is the load-bearing primary-source evidence that Hg views CINC as a high-conviction growth vehicle — supporting CINC's identification as the primary acquirer target in the condo-capital-infra exit thesis under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
- Hussein is the operational counterparty an acquirer-side outreach would need to reach for any Hg-mediated CINC acquisition discussion within the condo-capital-infra Year-3 exit window — a load-bearing relationship-routing data point for the acquirer-engagement strategy [[sources/web-2025-08-05-4b5]].
