# database of Genotypes and Phenotypes (dbGaP)

## Summary

The database of Genotypes and Phenotypes (dbGaP) is a pre-existing genomic data sharing mechanism operated by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) that long pre-dates the emergence of the NIH cloud-based biomedical data commons [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]]. Dahlquist et al. (2023) included dbGaP in their landscape analysis to situate the new cloud platforms against an established baseline, and noted that several of the cloud platforms continue to rely on dbGaP for data access request and review [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].

## Key facts

- Operated by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- A pre-existing data sharing mechanism distinct from cloud-based platforms, in which users typically download data to store and analyze locally rather than analyzing in a centralized cloud [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- AnVIL, BDC, and GDC require studies to be registered in dbGaP for controlled-access submissions [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- AnVIL and BDC Data Access Committees determine access using dbGaP consent codes and Data Use Limitations (DULs) [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- Dahlquist et al. reviewed 17 dbGaP documents (June–July 2021) as part of their cross-platform governance analysis [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
