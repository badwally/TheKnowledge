# MaintainX CoPilot

## Summary

MaintainX CoPilot is the AI-assistant feature embedded in the MaintainX maintenance and asset management platform, marketed as enabling technicians to get accurate, real-time answers the moment they need them, in place of digging through lengthy manuals and historical data [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]]. The feature is positioned within MaintainX's broader human-AI collaboration thesis — AI amplifies frontline-technician capability rather than replacing it — and is cited in customer testimonial copy as supporting technician training and onboarding [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].

## Key facts

- Named product feature within the MaintainX platform [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Marketed as enabling technicians to get accurate, real-time answers the moment they need them, instead of having to dig through lengthy manuals and data [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Positioned by customer testimonial copy as "like having an expert on hand 24/7 to guide our team through any task" [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Marketed use case: digitizing preventive maintenance workflows and supporting technician training and onboarding [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Customer reference: Jeremiah Dotson, Facility Maintenance Manager at Amfab Steel, Inc., cited as the source of the CoPilot trust-and-impact testimonial in the November 25, 2025 funding announcement [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- Sits within the broader "MaintainX AI" feature family [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
- One of the targets of Series D proceeds, which MaintainX stated would be used to advance AI-powered asset and work intelligence capabilities [[sources/web-2025-11-25-72b]].
