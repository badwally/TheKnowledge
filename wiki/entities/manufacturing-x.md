# Manufacturing-X

## Summary

Manufacturing-X is the German government's framework for scaling industrial data ecosystems across sectors; Catena-X (automotive) is its initial sectoral implementation and is positioned as a model for Manufacturing-X initiatives in other industries [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].

## Key facts

- A German government-led framework for scaling industrial data ecosystems [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Catena-X is its initial automotive-sector implementation [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- The €23 million Data Space Accelerator (announced 8 June 2026) forms part of the Manufacturing-X initiative [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Intended sectors for transferable insights from the Data Space Accelerator study include chemicals and semiconductors [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
