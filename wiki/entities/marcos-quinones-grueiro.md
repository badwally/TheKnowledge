# Marcos Quinones-Grueiro

## Summary

Marcos Quinones-Grueiro is both a co-author of the 2025 DX conference paper introducing a data-driven particle filter approach for system-level Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction and an editor of the DX 2025 proceedings volume in which that paper appears.

## Key facts

- Quinones-Grueiro is the third listed author of "A Data-Driven Particle Filter Approach for System-Level Prediction of Remaining Useful Life," published 10 November 2025 in the DX 2025 proceedings (OASIcs vol. 136, pp. 11:1–11:13, DOI 10.4230/OASIcs.DX.2025.11) [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- Quinones-Grueiro is listed as an editor of the same OASIcs volume 136 — the 36th International Conference on Principles of Diagnosis and Resilient Systems (DX 2025) proceedings — alongside Gautam Biswas and Ingo Pill [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- The paper is co-authored with Abel Diaz-Gonzalez, Austin Coursey, and Gautam Biswas [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
