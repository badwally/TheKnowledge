# Aidan Toner-Rodgers

## Summary

Economist at MIT studying the impact of artificial intelligence on innovation, productivity, and scientific discovery [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the MIT Department of Economics [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Authored the November 2024 paper "Artificial Intelligence, Scientific Discovery, and Product Innovation," which uses the randomized rollout of an AI materials-discovery tool to 1,018 scientists at the R&D lab of a large U.S. firm to identify causal effects of AI on R&D [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Acknowledges guidance from Daron Acemoglu, David Autor, Jacob Moscona, and Nina Rousille [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
- Work supported by the George and Obie Shultz Fund and the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (Grant No. 2141064) [[sources/pdf-22a560609b73]].
