# Anyone Home

## Summary

Anyone Home is the AI-powered multichannel leasing assistant within the [[entities/inhabit]] residential portfolio, focused on "Hybrid Intelligence" — combining AI with live-agent oversight for inbound voice, SMS, and chat inquiries [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].

## Key facts

- Anyone Home operates as a brand within the [[entities/inhabit]] residential portfolio [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- The Anyone Home AI Leasing Assistant manages inbound voice, SMS (coming soon as of Nov 2025), and chat inquiries for leasing teams, with seamless transition to live agents when appropriate [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Anyone Home's positioning emphasizes "Hybrid Intelligence" — AI plus human oversight — rather than fully autonomous AI agents [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Anyone Home's technology powers the next generation of [[entities/resman]]'s Leasing Assistant [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
- Anyone Home is positioned exclusively for multifamily rental leasing workflows; no condo/HOA owner-engagement use cases are announced [[sources/web-2025-11-17-cf4]].
