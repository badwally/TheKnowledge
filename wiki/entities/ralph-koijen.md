# Ralph S. J. Koijen

## Summary

Finance researcher at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business; co-author with Xavier Gabaix of the June 2021 NBER Working Paper No. 28967 "In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis," which uses a new "granular instrumental variables" methodology to estimate that flows of $1 into the U.S. equity market raise aggregate market value by approximately $5 [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, 5807 S Woodlawn Ave, Chicago, IL 60637, and NBER on the paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Contact email Ralph.koijen@chicagobooth.edu on the paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Co-authored "In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis" (NBER Working Paper No. 28967, June 2021) with Xavier Gabaix [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Acknowledges financial support from the Center for Research in Security Prices at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business on the paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
- Co-authored the stand-alone Gabaix and Koijen (2020) paper that worked out the "granular instrumental variables" methodology used as the empirical core of the inelastic-markets paper [[sources/pdf-b8cec9d1ec0c]].
