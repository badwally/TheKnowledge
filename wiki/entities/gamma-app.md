# Gamma

**Archetype:** AI-native indie SaaS (presentations)

AI-native presentation and document tool; PLG with embedded distribution.

## Why it's in this domain

Three load-bearing signals in the playbook:
- **"Player-coach" org model:** leaders actively code and design; one-third of the team were designers at peak — kept the company at $50M ARR with ~30 employees.
- **Product-led self-distribution:** every free presentation embeds a "Made with Gamma" badge, turning user output into an organic acquisition loop.
- **Credit-based pricing:** 400 free credits, $10/month for 400 more — keeps compute costs predictable.
