# Form T2 — Corporation Income Tax Return

## Summary

Form T2, the *Corporation Income Tax Return*, is the federal corporate income tax return that every Canadian corporation — including every condominium corporation — must file each year under paragraph 150(1)(*a*) of the *Income Tax Act*, regardless of whether the corporation has any taxable income or is exempt from Part I tax [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. The T2 obligation is unconditional: a residential condominium corporation that qualifies as a non-profit organization under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) and is exempt from Part I tax must still file T2 (alongside Form T1044) every year [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]]. CRA publishes the T2 in two formats — a 9-page long form and a 2-page T2 *Short Return* — with eligibility for the short form documented on page 13 of the Corporation Income Tax Guide [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]]. Residential strata corporations file using NPO Code 10 ("Residential condominium corporation") [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]]. In Québec, the federal T2 obligation runs in parallel with a provincial CO-17 (taxable) or CO-17.SP (NPO) obligation administered by Revenu Québec; the federal forms are filed separately with the CRA and not attached to the provincial return [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

## Key facts

- Must be filed annually by all corporations under paragraph 150(1)(*a*) of the *Income Tax Act*, including condominium corporations [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- Filing obligation applies even when the corporation is exempt from paying tax under Part I of the Act [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- A residential condominium corporation that qualifies as a non-profit organization under paragraph 149(1)(*l*) must file Form T1044 alongside its T2 return [[sources/web-2002-09-05-ce9]].
- All resident corporations must produce a T2 return each year per the CRA, even if no GST, QST, or income tax is payable [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Form versions

- CRA publishes two versions of the T2: a 9-page long form and a 2-page T2 *Short Return* [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Short-return eligibility is documented on page 13 of the Corporation Income Tax Guide [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].

### NPO Code

- Residential strata and condominium corporations file using NPO Code 10 ("Residential condominium corporation") on the T2 [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].

### Business number prerequisite

- A strata or condominium corporation needs a CRA Business Number (BN) to file a T2 [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- Stratas without a BN must request one using CRA form RC-1 E (23), *Request for a Business Number and Certain Program Accounts* — the Business Registration Online channel does not include fields appropriate to strata corporations and is not the correct registration path [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].
- If the CRA requests incorporation documents during BN registration, the strata should clarify that it is a strata corporation rather than a business corporation; the strata's legal-formation document is the strata plan deposited in the Land Title Office, not an incorporation document [[sources/web-2025-10-20-a96]].

### Required attached schedules

- A registered Québec syndicate (NEQ starting with "11") filing T2 must attach schedules 100, 125, and 141 [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Mandatory electronic filing (fiscal years starting in 2024)

- For fiscal years starting in 2024, the federal T2 must be submitted electronically, except for certain tax-exempt entities under subsection 149 of the Income Tax Act, including many NPOs [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Failure to comply with the electronic filing requirements may lead to processing delays and, in some cases, administrative penalties [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].

### Quebec parallel provincial filing

- A Québec corporation must file a parallel provincial return with Revenu Québec: CO-17 for taxable corporations or CO-17.SP for NPOs (most condominium syndicates) [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- Federal T2 and T1044 returns are filed separately with the CRA and are NOT attached to the provincial CO-17.SP filed with Revenu Québec [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
- For fiscal years starting in 2024, the Québec provincial CO-17 and CO-17.SP returns must also be filed electronically, with an exception only for registered charities [[sources/web-2025-11-06-d1c]].
