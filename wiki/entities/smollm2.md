# Hugging Face SmolLM2

## Summary

SmolLM2 is a Hugging Face family of compact language models ranging from 125M to 1.7B parameters, cited by Belcak et al. (2025) as evidence that modern SLMs can match the language-understanding, tool-calling, and instruction-following performance of 14B contemporaries — and the performance of 70B models from two years prior [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].

## Key facts

- A Hugging Face family of compact language models with sizes ranging from 125M to 1.7B parameters [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Each member of the family runs up in language-understanding, tool-calling, and instruction-following performance to 14B contemporaries [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Matches 70B models of two years prior on the same capability axes [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
