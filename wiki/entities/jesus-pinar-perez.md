# Jesús M. Pinar-Pérez

## Summary

Jesús M. Pinar-Pérez is a co-author of the 2024 *Reliability Engineering & System Safety* paper introducing a Double Deep Q-Network reinforcement-learning agent for maintenance of deteriorating systems with increasingly imperfect repairs.

## Key facts

- Pinar-Pérez is the second listed author of "A reinforcement learning agent for maintenance of deteriorating systems with increasingly imperfect repairs" [[sources/arxiv-2505.20725]].
- The paper was published in *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*, volume 252, article 110466, December 2024 (DOI 10.1016/j.ress.2024.110466) [[sources/arxiv-2505.20725]].
- The work is co-authored with Alberto Pliego Marugán and Fausto Pedro García Márquez [[sources/arxiv-2505.20725]].
