# First Nations Data Governance Strategy (FNDGS)

## Summary

The First Nations Data Governance Strategy (FNDGS) is a First Nations–led plan, developed by FNIGC and its regional partners and delivered to the Government of Canada in spring 2020, that lays out an incremental path to First Nations data sovereignty designed so that "no First Nation is left behind" [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].

## Key facts

- The FNDGS positions itself as a response to a complex and evolving digital environment, offering what FNIGC describes as a "plausible incremental plan" to achieve First Nations data sovereignty [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- Budget 2018 funded development of the FNDGS over three years; FNIGC delivered it to the Government of Canada in spring 2020 rather than 2021, completing the work in two years [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- The strategy represents a collective vision articulated by First Nations leadership, rights holders, and data sovereignty experts through multi-year engagements [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- The strategy's central deliverable is a national network of fully functioning, interconnected Regional Information Governance Centres (RIGCs) with the capacities needed to serve the data and statistical needs of First Nations [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- The FNDGS frames data sovereignty as plural rather than uniform: each First Nation is to achieve data sovereignty "in alignment with its distinct world view" [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
- FNIGC's communicated outcomes from the strategy include First Nations' ownership and control of their data informing decisions, improving outcomes, and enabling cross-Nation collaboration on common priorities [[sources/web-2025-11-28-252]].
