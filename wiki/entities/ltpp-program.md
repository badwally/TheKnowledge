# Long-Term Pavement Performance (LTPP) program

## Summary

The Long-Term Pavement Performance (LTPP) program provides a publicly accessible pavement performance database used as a foundational data resource for asphalt pavement life-cycle research, operating under the leadership of the U.S. Federal Highway Administration.

## Key facts

- The LTPP program provides a publicly accessible database that is used as a data source for asphalt pavement life-cycle research [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The LTPP program operates under the leadership of the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The LTPP database is acknowledged as a foundational resource in the 2026 review on asphalt pavement life-cycle performance by Xin, Frangopol, Akiyama, Zhang, and Pei [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
