# UCSF Osher Center for Integrative Health

## Summary

University of California, San Francisco's integrative-medicine center; institutional home of Dr. Carla Kuon and host of her three-visit Long COVID Group Medical Visit series, which combines anti-inflammatory diet, botanical and nutrient therapies, and mind/body interventions for post-viral recovery [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the host institution on Dr. Carla Kuon's Long COVID Group Medical Visit handout [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Office contact for the program: 415-353-7700 (phone) and 415-353-7358 (fax) [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Patient handouts cite osher.ucsf.edu/putting-nutrition-practice-tips-making-dietary-changes as the canonical UCSF page for putting the recommended nutrition guidance into practice [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Requires a defined intake bloodwork panel (Vitamin B2, Ferritin, Histamine, Tryptase, hsCRP, ESR, Vitamin D, Vitamin A, Zinc, Copper) for evaluation and clearance into the medical group visits [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
- Uses ICD-10 code U09.9 (Post COVID-19 condition) on the group-visit referral [[sources/pdf-5bef1fceb745]].
