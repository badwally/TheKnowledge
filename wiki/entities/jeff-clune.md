# Jeff Clune

## Summary

AI researcher at the University of British Columbia and the Vector Institute, holder of a Canada CIFAR AI Chair, and a long-standing advocate of open-ended AI systems; co-author of the 2026 HyperAgents paper and author of the "AI-Generating Algorithms" agenda invoked as motivation for that work [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of British Columbia, the Vector Institute, and the Canada CIFAR AI Chair program on the HyperAgents paper [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Co-author of "HyperAgents" (2026) with Jenny Zhang (lead) and others [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
- Author of "AI-Generating Algorithms" (Clune, 2019), which the HyperAgents paper cites as motivation for open-ended systems that improve not only the artifacts they generate but also the mechanisms by which novelty and progress are produced [[sources/pdf-jenny-zhang-2026-hyperagents]].
