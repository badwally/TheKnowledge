# GitHub Copilot

## Summary

AI pair-programming product reported in the June 2025 Innovation Endeavors "State of Foundation Models" report at approximately $400M ARR with 1,500,000 users, accumulated over a 3-year timeframe — one of the fastest revenue ramps of all time [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].

## Key facts

- Reported at approximately $400M ARR with 1,500,000 users, achieved over a 3-year timeframe [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Cited alongside Cursor and Midjourney as one of the AI-native products with the fastest revenue ramps of all time [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Used by Innovation Endeavors as evidence that AI software-engineering copilots had grown into a ~$2B/year market in roughly 2–3 years [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Innovation Endeavors argues that AI is fundamentally disrupting software engineering and that all developer-tool products are likely to be rethought in a world of AI code generation [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
