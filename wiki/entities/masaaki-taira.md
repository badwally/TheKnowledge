# Masaaki Taira

## Summary

Japanese Liberal Democratic Party politician who chaired the LDP Project Team on the Evolution and Implementation of AI that produced Japan's April 2023 AI Whitepaper [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].

## Key facts

- Served as Chairperson of the LDP Project Team on the Evolution and Implementation of AI, established February 3, 2023 [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
- Led the team that drafted "The AI White Paper: Japan's National Strategy in the New Era of AI" (April 2023) [[sources/pdf-822d5467c24e]].
