# Sense Engineering (Atlantic) Ltd.

## Summary

Sense Engineering (Atlantic) Ltd. is a Halifax-area engineering consultancy that prepares reserve fund studies under the Nova Scotia Condominium Act; the firm authored the June 11, 2025 Reserve Fund Study for Halifax Condominium Corporation No. 130 (Summer Gardens, 1470 Summer Street, Halifax) under Sense Project No. 24hA007A, prepared by Vaughn Keeble [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]]. The "(Atlantic)" suffix in the corporate name suggests Sense Engineering operates as a regional subsidiary structure with a parent Sense Engineering elsewhere in Canada [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]]. As a NS-licensed engineering firm with active reserve-fund-study practice, Sense Engineering is a load-bearing candidate counterparty for the condo-capital-infra Halifax engagement (Brief-0004) under the ADR-0004 Canada-first GTM, both as a P.Eng partnership candidate for sealing NS reserve fund studies and as a competitive intelligence anchor for understanding the operative NS RFS practice baseline [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].

### Operational study methodology (per Summer Gardens 2025 RFS)

- Reserve fund expenditures projected over a 25-year period for the financial analysis (exceeding the 20-year statutory minimum physical-analysis horizon) [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- Itemized projects with anticipated costs greater than ~$5,000; minor repairs, routine maintenance, and replacements costing less than ~$5,000 are assumed to be funded from the operating budget [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- Cost estimates are inflated and include contingencies (typically 5 to 15%) and allowances for design/project management (5 to 15%), where appropriate; HST (15%) is included [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- Methodology is deterministic point-estimate — cost, useful life, remaining useful life, and inflation rate are treated as scalar inputs rather than distributions [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
- The funding plan presents an Immediately Adequately Funded scenario as the approved cash-flow analysis option, alongside (implicitly) other unselected funding-plan scenarios — consistent with the CAI funding-plan-option structure [[sources/pdf-3c6b4345c8c4]].
