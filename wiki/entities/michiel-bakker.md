# Michiel A. Bakker

## Summary

AI researcher at MIT and co-author of the 2026 paper "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents"; the paper invokes his prior work (Bakker et al., 2022) on social welfare objectives as a precedent for treating multi-user LLM coordination as a form of utility aggregation [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with MIT on "Multi-User Large Language Model Agents" (2026), the first systematic study of multi-user LLM interactions [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
- The paper cites Bakker et al. (2022) as a basis for treating multi-user LLM coordination as a form of utility aggregation potentially resembling a social welfare objective, alongside Keeney and Kirkwood (1975) [[sources/pdf-shu-yang-2026-multi-user-large]].
