# Monocle

## Summary

Monocle is a lifecycle marketing startup that builds autonomous tools to dynamically adjust messaging, timing and promotional offers across email, SMS and web channels [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]]. The company was acquired by OuterSignal on 2026-05-28; terms were not disclosed, and existing customers continue using the platform without disruption while integration with OuterSignal expands over time [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].

## Key facts

- Co-founder and CEO: Noam Szpiro [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].
- Product category: lifecycle marketing automation across email, SMS and web channels [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].
- Platform replaces rules-based marketing flows with AI-driven customer journeys that respond to individual purchase intent, discount sensitivity and engagement timing in real time [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].
- Designed to reduce reliance on static campaign logic and manual segmentation processes commonly used in ecommerce marketing [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].
- Acquired by OuterSignal in a deal announced 2026-05-28; terms not disclosed [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].
- Per Monocle CEO Noam Szpiro, the acquisition combines upstream customer intelligence with downstream engagement automation into a more complete personalization system [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].
- Channel overlap with Klaviyo: both serve email/SMS lifecycle automation for ecommerce, though Monocle's positioning is AI-driven customer journeys replacing rules-based flows [[sources/web-2026-05-28-0cb]].
