# Waqar Hasan

## Summary

Waqar Hasan is Director of Humberts UAE (the Dubai flagship office of UK real estate firm Humberts) and holds CPM and PCAM professional designations [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]]. He is the named Humberts executive who articulated the firm's rationale for adopting SmartProperty's platform as part of Humberts Dubai's UAE service expansion [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].

## Key facts

- Waqar Hasan holds the title Director of Humberts UAE as of the April 7, 2025 SmartProperty partnership announcement [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- He holds the CPM (Certified Property Manager, IREM) and PCAM (Professional Community Association Manager, CAI) professional designations [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- He framed the SmartProperty collaboration as a "natural extension" of Humberts' commitment to bringing technology and expertise to the UAE market [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
- He cited the partnership's intent to ensure clients benefit from "the highest standards in asset and financial planning" — language tied to the Living Reserve Study deployment [[sources/web-2025-04-07-b53]].
