# Col de Vergio

## Summary

The Col de Vergio is a 1,477 m mountain pass on [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] — the highest road pass on the island — and the central spine crossing on the Day 4 Porto → Corte stage of [[entities/marmot-tours|Marmot Tours]]'s [[entities/corsican-highlights|Corsican Highlights]] holiday [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].

## Key facts

- 1,477 m summit elevation — the highest road pass on Corsica [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Crosses the central rib of the island, separating Porto on the west coast from Corte in the interior [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- Although low compared with the major Alps and Pyrenees passes, it is climbed from sea level — Marmot describes that as making it a 'significant challenge' despite generally kind gradients [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- The eastern descent drops down the Gorge de la Ruda, which Marmot describes as 'spectacular' [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- The Classic-route Day 4 of the Corsican Highlights holiday pairs Vergio with the Col de Ominanda (654 m) for 84 km / +1,750 m [[sources/pdf-f03d709c7ff7]].
- The col is identified on the 2019 GT 20 promotional kakemono under its Corsican name "Bocca a Verghju," sitting between Portu and Corti on the route's central spine [[sources/pdf-c985b0372d8a]].
