# Sten Lammers

## Summary

Researcher at the Erasmus School of Economics, Erasmus University Rotterdam; co-author of the 2020 paper "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum," which documents that intraday momentum appears "everywhere" across asset classes and links it to gamma-hedging demand from option market makers and leveraged-ETF issuers [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Erasmus School of Economics, Burgemeester Oudlaan 50, Rotterdam 3000 DR, Netherlands, on the paper [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- Co-author of "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" (26 August 2020; SSRN abstract 3760365) with Guido Baltussen, Zhi Da, and Martin Martens [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
- The paper's underlying dataset spans 17 developed-market equity index futures, 16 developed-market bond futures, 21 commodity futures, and 8 currency futures over a 45-year window from December 1974 to May 2020 [[sources/pdf-2742492120a8]].
