# Abel Diaz-Gonzalez

## Summary

Abel Diaz-Gonzalez is the first-listed author of the 2025 DX conference paper introducing a data-driven particle filter approach for system-level prediction of Remaining Useful Life (RUL).

## Key facts

- Diaz-Gonzalez is the first listed author of "A Data-Driven Particle Filter Approach for System-Level Prediction of Remaining Useful Life," published 10 November 2025 in the proceedings of the 36th International Conference on Principles of Diagnosis and Resilient Systems (DX 2025) [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- The paper appears in Open Access Series in Informatics (OASIcs) volume 136, pages 11:1–11:13, with DOI 10.4230/OASIcs.DX.2025.11 [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- The work is co-authored with Austin Coursey, Marcos Quinones-Grueiro, and Gautam Biswas [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
