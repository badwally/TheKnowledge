# Iris Pang

## Summary

Chief Economist for Greater China at ING; contributing author of the December 8, 2022 "ING global economic outlook 2023," responsible for the China chapter "Still dire from rising number of Covid cases" [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].

## Key facts

- Listed title at ING: Chief Economist, Greater China [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Listed contact email: iris.pang@asia.ing.com [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Diagnoses China's housing market as quiet because uncompleted home projects are yet to be finished — most are left in the hands of local governments to find a private company to finish the construction work, which takes time [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Reports more local governments have subtly changed to slightly softer Covid practices, but the higher number of Covid cases means there is a limit on how fine-tuning can benefit the economy and that sporadic lockdowns would continue to affect retail sales and production adversely [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Identifies fiscal spending in advanced technology and new energy as the only support to the Chinese economy in late 2022 [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Reports retail sales fell into yearly contraction in October 2022 with PMIs suggesting that pattern could easily repeat for the rest of Q4 2022 [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
- Reads exports as continuing to show weaknesses due to high inflation in the US and Europe [[sources/pdf-87087198976b]].
