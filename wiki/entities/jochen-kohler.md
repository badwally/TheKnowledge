# Jochen Köhler

## Summary

Jochen Köhler is a structural reliability researcher who serves as lead editor of the preface and contributes to multiple articles in the March 2025 JCSS-themed special issue of *Structural Safety*.

## Key facts

- Köhler is the first listed editor of "Preface of the Special Issue: The Joint Committee of Structural Safety: past, present and a perspective on the future" (article 102542), co-edited with Vrouwenvelder, Faber, and Limongelli [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Köhler is the first listed author of "Codes and standards for structural design - developments and future potential" (article 102495), co-authored with Sørensen and Ellingwood [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Köhler is a co-author of "Interpretation of probability in structural safety – A philosophical conundrum" (article 102473), led by Vrouwenvelder [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Köhler is a co-author of "Recent developments in mechanical and uncertainty modelling of concrete" (article 102526), led by Chen [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Köhler is a co-author of "On information value and decision analyses" (article 102481), led by Thöns [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
