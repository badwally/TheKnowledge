# NIH Genomic Data Sharing (GDS) Policy

## Summary

The NIH Genomic Data Sharing (GDS) Policy is the NIH-wide policy framework governing the submission, access, and use of large-scale genomic data generated by NIH-funded research [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]]. It functions as a compliance backbone across the NIH cloud-based biomedical data commons, with platforms such as AnVIL and the Genomic Data Commons (GDC) requiring submitted data to conform to it [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].

## Key facts

- AnVIL data must conform to the NIH GDS Policy as a submission requirement [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- GDC data submission adheres to both NIH and NCI GDS policies [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- GDC users granted controlled access must agree to a Data Use Agreement and the NIH GDS Policy and submit a data sharing plan [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
- AnVIL's security regime is consistent with NIH Security Best Practices and the GDS Policy [[sources/web-2023-04-12-931]].
