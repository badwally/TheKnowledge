# Marc Cadieux

## Summary

President of Silicon Valley Bank's commercial banking business; co-lead author (with Mark Gallagher) of the H2 2025 "State of the Markets" report (30th edition), which covers VC fundraising, AI investment dominance, IPO dynamics, and macro headwinds across the innovation economy [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].

## Key facts

- Title: President, SVB Commercial Bank; contact: mcadieux@svb.com [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Focuses on the needs of innovation companies at all stages of development, including the investors who back them [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Together with Mark Gallagher, brings over a half-century of industry knowledge and experience working with many of the top companies and investors across the innovation economy [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
- Characterizes the current venture landscape: "As we saw with the dot-com era, early adopters of platform shifts are often not the market winners. Today's valuations are starting to stretch rationality. It is likely many companies won't achieve high returns, but the best companies could get so big they make up for the failures" [[sources/pdf-silicon-valley-2025-state-of-the]].
