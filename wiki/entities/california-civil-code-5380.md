# California Civil Code § 5380 (Management of Association Funds)

## Summary

California Civil Code § 5380 governs the management of funds belonging to a common interest development (CID) association by a managing agent in California. The statute requires a managing agent who receives association funds — and does not place them in escrow or in an account under the association's direct control — to deposit those funds in a trust fund account at a California bank, savings association, or credit union that is federally insured or insured by a guaranty corporation subject to § 14858 of the California Financial Code [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]]. Section 5380 also defines transfer-authorization thresholds (the lesser of $5,000 or 5% of estimated income for associations with ≤50 separate interests; the lesser of $10,000 or 5% for associations with ≥51 separate interests), prohibits commingling, prohibits managing-agent benefit from interest earned on association funds, restricts investments to principal-protected accounts (no stocks or high-risk investments), and provides for prevailing-party legal-fee recovery in enforcement actions [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]]. The current version was amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 270, Sec. 1 (AB 1101), effective January 1, 2022 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

## Key facts

### Managing-agent trust-fund deposit obligation — § 5380(a)

- A managing agent of a common interest development who accepts or receives funds belonging to the association must deposit those funds — if not placed into an escrow account or an account under the control of the association — into a trust fund account maintained by the managing agent in a bank, savings association, or credit union in California [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- All funds deposited by the managing agent in the trust fund account must be kept in California in a financial institution as defined in § 31041 of the California Financial Code, that is insured by the federal government or is a guaranty corporation subject to § 14858 of the Financial Code [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- Funds must be maintained in the trust fund account until disbursed in accordance with written instructions from the association entitled to the funds [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

### Board-directed deposit option — § 5380(b)

- At the written request of the board, the funds the managing agent accepts or receives on behalf of the association may instead be deposited into an account in a California bank, savings association, or credit union that is insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, the National Credit Union Administration Insurance Fund, or a guaranty corporation subject to § 14858 of the Financial Code, provided all of the conditions in § 5380(b)(1)–(6) are met [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(1) requires the account to be in the name of the managing agent as trustee for the association or in the name of the association [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(2) requires all funds in the account to be covered by insurance provided by an agency of the federal government or a guaranty corporation subject to § 14858 of the Financial Code; funds may only be deposited in accounts that protect the principal; in no event may those funds be invested in stocks or high-risk investment options [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(3) requires the funds in the account to be kept separate, distinct, and apart from the funds belonging to the managing agent or to any other person for whom the managing agent holds funds in trust [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(4) requires the managing agent to disclose to the board the nature of the account, how interest will be calculated and paid, whether service charges will be paid to the depository and by whom, and any notice requirements or penalties for withdrawal of funds from the account [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(5) prohibits any interest earned on funds in the account from inuring directly or indirectly to the benefit of the managing agent or the managing agent's employees [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

### Transfer-authorization thresholds — § 5380(b)(6)

- Transfers of funds out of the association's reserve or operating accounts may not be authorized without prior written approval from the board of the association unless the amount of the transfer falls below the applicable threshold [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(6)(A): the threshold is the lesser of $5,000 or 5% of the estimated income in the annual operating budget, for associations with 50 or less separate interests [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(b)(6)(B): the threshold is the lesser of $10,000 or 5% of estimated income in the annual operating budget, for associations with 51 or more separate interests [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

### Recordkeeping, anti-commingling, and enforcement — § 5380(c)–(f)

- § 5380(c) requires the managing agent to maintain a separate record of the receipt and disposition of all funds described in the section, including any interest earned on the funds [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(d) prohibits the managing agent from commingling the funds of the association with the managing agent's own money or with the money of others that the managing agent receives or accepts [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(e) provides that the prevailing party in an action to enforce § 5380 is entitled to recover reasonable legal fees and court costs [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
- § 5380(f) defines "completed payment" as funds received that clearly identify the account to which the funds are to be credited [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].

### Legislative history

- The current text of § 5380 was amended by Statutes of 2021, Chapter 270, Section 1 (AB 1101), effective January 1, 2022 [[sources/web-2022-01-01-a78]].
