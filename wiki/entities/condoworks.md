# CondoWorks

## Summary

CondoWorks is identified in CPA-practitioner guidance as an Ontario-specific condominium accounting software platform, listed alongside three U.S.-origin CAM accounting platforms (Yardi Voyager, Buildium, AppFolio) as recommended platforms for condominium corporations in Ontario [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]]. The product is positioned within the Ontario CAM-accounting market as the only jurisdictionally-tailored option in a roster otherwise dominated by U.S.-headquartered platforms [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].

## Key facts

- Identified as an Ontario-specific condominium accounting software platform in CPA-practitioner guidance authored from Mississauga, Ontario [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- Listed in a four-platform recommended roster alongside Yardi Voyager, Buildium, and AppFolio for Ontario condominium corporations [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- The only platform in the recommended four explicitly tagged "Ontario-specific" by the CPA-authored guide; the other three are U.S.-origin general-purpose CAM/PM accounting platforms [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- The guide frames recommended features for condo accounting platforms as: dual-fund accounting (operating + reserve), unit owner account tracking, automated late fee calculations, online owner portals, and integration with property management systems [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
- CondoWorks is presented in the context of Ontario regulatory requirements — Condominium Act, 1998; Ontario Regulation 48/01; CPA Canada Handbook for NPOs; CAO Guidelines — suggesting the platform is positioned to handle Ontario-specific dual-fund reporting and 80% audit-waiver compliance workflows [[sources/web-2026-03-05-dc5]].
