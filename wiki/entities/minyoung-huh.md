# Minyoung Huh

## Summary

Minyoung Huh is an MIT researcher and the corresponding, first-listed equal-contribution author of "The Platonic Representation Hypothesis" (ICML 2024) [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the corresponding author; contact email minhuh@mit.edu [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
- Co-authored the PRH paper with Brian Cheung, Tongzhou Wang, and Phillip Isola; all four contributed equally [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
- Maintains the PRH code repository at github.com/minyoungg/platonic-rep [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
