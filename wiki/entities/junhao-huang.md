# Junhao Huang

## Summary

Junhao Huang is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2025 paper proposing a comprehensive Gamma process-based approach for the time-dependent reliability of reinforced concrete structures considering non-stationary resistance degradation.

## Key facts

- Huang is the fifth (final) listed author of 'An Evaluation on the Time-Dependent Reliability of Reinforced Concrete Structures Considering Non-Stationary Resistance Degradation: A Comprehensive Gamma Process-Based Approach' [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- The paper was published on 31 January 2025 with DOI 10.17531/ein/200284 [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
- The paper is co-authored with Conghe Jin, Tao Yang, Wangxi Xu, and Yongjiu Qian [[sources/web-2025-01-31-943]].
