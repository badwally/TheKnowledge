# Liliana Haus

## Summary

Liliana Haus is a co-author of the 2026 paper proposing a Bayesian hierarchical Accelerated Failure Time model for fleet-scale wind turbine gearbox reliability assessment.

## Key facts

- Haus is the second listed author of 'Bayesian Survival Models Reveal Wind-Driven Reliability Patterns in Turbines,' published on 1 May 2026 with DOI 10.1088/1742-6596/3232/1/012018 [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
- The paper is co-authored with Clément Jacquet, Nicolas Marx Hermoso, and Rajasekhar Pulikollu [[sources/web-2026-05-01-6b7]].
