# Arnold X.-X. Yuan

## Summary

Arnold X.-X. Yuan is the senior co-author of the 2025 arXiv preprint proposing a new bounded transformed gamma process (BTGP) model for unified infrastructure asset performance deterioration modelling.

## Key facts

- Yuan is the second listed author of "Unified Modelling of Infrastructure Asset Performance Deterioration — a bounded gamma process approach," arXiv 2508.13359, published 18 August 2025, co-authored with Wang Chen [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- The paper is classified under stat.AP (primary), cs.CE, and math.PR, situating Yuan's work at the intersection of applied statistics, computational engineering, and probability theory as applied to infrastructure asset management [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
