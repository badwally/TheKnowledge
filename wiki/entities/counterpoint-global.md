# Counterpoint Global

## Summary

Investment research and asset-management group within Morgan Stanley; publisher of the Counterpoint Global Insights / Consilient Observer research series, including the June 4, 2024 report "Stock Market Concentration: How Much Is Too Much?" by Michael J. Mauboussin and Dan Callahan [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].

## Key facts

- Publishes the "Counterpoint Global Insights" research series under the "Consilient Observer" banner [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- The June 4, 2024 issue, "Stock Market Concentration: How Much Is Too Much?", is © 2024 Morgan Stanley with disclosure ID 6652630 (Exp 6/30/2025) [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Mauboussin and Callahan are listed as the authors and provide morganstanley.com email addresses, situating Counterpoint Global as part of the Morgan Stanley research apparatus [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
- Report draws on FactSet, Compustat, SEC annual reports, and Counterpoint Global's own analyses for its concentration and economic-profit calculations [[sources/pdf-7acc9c92267c]].
