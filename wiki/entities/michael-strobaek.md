# Michael Strobaek

## Summary

Global Chief Investment Officer at Credit Suisse; co-author of the editorial 'A time for prudence' in the Credit Suisse Investment Outlook 2023, in which he argues that 2023 will be challenging given tight financial conditions and the fundamental reset of macroeconomics and geopolitics, and frames CS's 'House View' as a valuable compass for navigating it [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].

## Key facts

- Listed title at Credit Suisse: Global Chief Investment Officer [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Co-authored the editorial in the Investment Outlook 2023 ('A time for prudence' / 'Reset is the new reality') with Nannette Hechler-Fayd'herbe and Philipp Lisibach [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Argues 2023 is likely to be challenging because financial conditions are all but certain to remain tight and the fundamental reset of macroeconomics and geopolitics is continuing [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Recommends investors 'adhere to a robust investment process and diversify investments broadly, particularly as the transition out of negative rates is behind us' [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Characterizes 2022 as a particularly difficult environment in which the war in Ukraine drove price levels up further and central banks led by the Federal Reserve brought forward rate hikes and tightened monetary policy aggressively [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- Argues central banks 'will not be able to slow the pace of rate hikes before realized inflation falls persistently' [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- States 2022 saw equities and bonds firmly in negative territory, with bonds unable to act as effective diversification due to a stronger correlation between the two asset classes [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
- States 'preserving wealth is our singular focus' at Credit Suisse [[sources/pdf-8f021296560c]].
