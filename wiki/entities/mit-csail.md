The MIT Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (Cambridge, MA, USA), institutional home of all three authors of the 2026 "Recursive Language Models" preprint — Alex L. Zhang, Tim Kraska, and Omar Khattab [[sources/pdf-5c2f94fd33cd]].
