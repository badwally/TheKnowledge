# UpperBee Pay

## Summary

UpperBee Pay is the electronic payment processing product offered by UpperBee, marketed alongside UpperBee's integrated property management platform as a paired solution that "helps managers streamline operations and payments" [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]]. UpperBee Pay is one of three named pillars in UpperBee's corporate self-description as "a leading provider of property management, accounting, and electronic payment solutions" [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].

## Key facts

- Electronic payment product line operated by UpperBee [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- Marketed in pair with the UpperBee integrated property management platform as the combined offering that "helps managers streamline operations and payments" [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- One of three named pillars in UpperBee's corporate self-description as "a leading provider of property management, accounting, and electronic payment solutions" [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
- Featured by name in UpperBee's 2026 CAI Canada Conference booth (Booth #1, Toronto) marketing copy [[sources/web-2020-09-02-290]].
