# Roland Platz

## Summary

Roland Platz is the first-listed editor of *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3*, the proceedings of the 43rd IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics, 2025.

## Key facts

- Platz is the first listed editor of *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3: Proceedings of the 43rd IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics, 2025*, published in the River Publishers Series in Conference Proceedings of the Society for Experimental Mechanics Series (ISBN 9788743801481, e-ISBN 9788743801603) [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- He co-edited the volume with Garrison Flynn, Kyle Neal, and Scott Ouellette [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
