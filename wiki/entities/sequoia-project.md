# The Sequoia Project

## Summary

The Sequoia Project serves as TEFCA's Recognized Coordinating Entity (RCE), responsible for developing and maintaining the Common Agreement and for administering the Qualified Health Information Network (QHIN) designation process. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]

## Key facts

- The Sequoia Project currently serves as TEFCA's Recognized Coordinating Entity (RCE) under a 5-year contract with ONC awarded in August 2023. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- As RCE, The Sequoia Project develops, updates, implements, and maintains the Common Agreement that binds all QHINs. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- As RCE, The Sequoia Project solicits and reviews applications from health information networks (HINs) seeking QHIN status and administers the QHIN designation and ongoing monitoring processes. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- As RCE, The Sequoia Project updates the QHIN Technical Framework and engages TEFCA stakeholders through routine public information sessions, ad hoc presentations, and other channels. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
