# Phillip Isola

## Summary

Phillip Isola is an MIT researcher and senior co-author of the 2024 ICML paper introducing the Platonic Representation Hypothesis [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].

## Key facts

- Co-authored "The Platonic Representation Hypothesis" with Minyoung Huh, Brian Cheung, and Tongzhou Wang; all four listed as equal-contribution authors affiliated with MIT [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
- The paper appeared in PMLR 235, Proceedings of the 41st International Conference on Machine Learning, Vienna, Austria (2024) [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
- Hosts the PRH project page at phillipi.github.io/prh [[sources/pdf-minyoung-huh-2024-the-platonic-representation]].
