# daytradingrauf

## Summary

Discretionary trader and author of the 2023 PDF "Draw On Liquidity (DOL)" — a six-page primer that organizes draw-on-liquidity targeting into three layers (what liquidity is, market-structure liquidity, and external-to-internal range liquidity) explicitly built on top of Inner Circle Trader (ICT) teachings [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].

## Key facts

- Authored "Draw On Liquidity (DOL)" (2023, six pages), branded with the @Daytradingrauf handle on every diagram [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Frames the document around five sections: What is Liquidity, Market Structure Liquidity, External to Internal Range, Study Videos/Charts, and Socials [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- States explicitly that "the concepts I spoke about today come from ICT's teachings" and links readers to an "ICT LIQUIDITY RUN VIDEO" on his YouTube channel for a video explanation [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Defines the trading edge as identifying "reference points where a high percentage of [liquidity] is resting," specifically buy stops above old highs and sell stops below lows, rather than reading patterns [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Argues for trading the opposite of what the crowd is doing with harmonic patterns, bull and bear flags, failure swings (double tops and bottoms), and trend line liquidity — but only when there is higher-time-frame order flow to back up why the patterns are being faded [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Names "External Range Liquidity to Internal Range Liquidity" as his "favorite way to identify draws on liquidity because it is the most forgiving concept; when you are wrong you can be profitable" [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Operationalizes the external-to-internal framework by identifying external liquidity at swing highs and lows in price action (illustrated on a DXY Monthly Chart), waiting for a stop hunt into them, and then targeting internal liquidity at fair value gaps (FVGs) or other imbalances such as volume imbalances or gaps [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Lists his social channels in the document — Twitter (X), Instagram, YouTube, and Telegram — and recommends a prop firm via the discount code "Rauf" for any challenge account size [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
- Recommends Vela_exchange as his preferred crypto exchange in the same document [[sources/pdf-cce4b539ce1c]].
