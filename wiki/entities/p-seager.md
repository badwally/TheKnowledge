# P. Seager

## Summary

Quantitative-finance researcher at Capital Fund Management; co-author of the 2014 paper "Two centuries of trend following" [[sources/pdf-be69dd282a8c]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Capital Fund Management, 23 rue de l'Université, 75007 Paris, France [[sources/pdf-be69dd282a8c]].
- Listed third of five authors on "Two centuries of trend following" (April 15, 2014), alongside Y. Lempérière, C. Deremble, M. Potters, and J. P. Bouchaud [[sources/pdf-be69dd282a8c]].
