# Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology (ONC)

## Summary

ONC is the US Department of Health & Human Services agency that serves as the lead federal authority for health IT policy and that created TEFCA to enable nationwide electronic health information exchange. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]

## Key facts

- ONC is the lead US federal agency for health IT policy and sits within the US Department of Health & Human Services (HHS). [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- ONC created TEFCA to remove barriers to sharing health records electronically among healthcare providers, patients, public health agencies, and payers. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- ONC's TEFCA responsibilities include setting TEFCA policy, supporting and guiding network governance, collaborating with the Recognized Coordinating Entity (RCE) to expand TEFCA, and coordinating with federal agency partners whose regulatory responsibilities intersect with health IT. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- In August 2023, ONC awarded a 5-year contract to The Sequoia Project to serve as TEFCA's Recognized Coordinating Entity. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
- ONC and the RCE jointly expand TEFCA's permissible Exchange Purposes over time, informed by market needs raised by the QHIN Governing Council. [[sources/web-2026-06-09-e4c]]
