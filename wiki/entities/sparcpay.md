# SparcPay

## Summary

SparcPay is a digital vendor-payments provider partnered with [[concepts/shiftsuite]] to deliver the Digital Vendor Payables application within the Shiftsuite condo management tech stack [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a88]].

## Key facts

- SparcPay's partnership with Shiftsuite powers the Digital Vendor Payables application, positioned by Shiftsuite as an industry-leading innovation in property management payments [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a88]].
- The SparcPay-Shiftsuite integration enables property managers and condo boards to jointly approve bills and issue payments to vendors through a fully customizable approval workflow [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a88]].
- SparcPay is named by Shiftsuite as a single, specific payments partner — Shiftsuite's homepage discloses no alternative payments integration partners alongside SparcPay for the Digital Vendor Payables surface [[sources/web-2026-06-04-a88]].
