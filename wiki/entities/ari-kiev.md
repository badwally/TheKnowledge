# Ari Kiev

## Summary

Practicing psychiatrist and motivational consultant to a major New York trading company; author of "Centered Trading" (Stocks & Commodities V.13:2, 1995), which introduces centering as the critical psychological skill enabling traders to distance themselves from automatic negative reactions and maintain discipline [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]], and author of "Trading to Win" (1998), in which he codifies the proactive trading-psychology program he co-developed with Steve Cohen at SAC Capital Management over six years of weekly trader meetings [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].

## Key facts

- Identified as a practicing psychiatrist and motivational consultant to a major New York trading company in the 1995 Stocks & Commodities article [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Argues successful trading requires three capabilities: willingness to commit to specific objectives, skill to manage emotion and fear, and discipline to adhere to trading strategy [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Defines centering as learning to distance oneself from the sequence of emotional responses and negative interpretations of stressful and unsuccessful trades that trigger panic, denial, rationalization, and avoidance — all of which perpetuate the circle of failure [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- States the centered trader accepts responsibility for his trading results and recognizes that his greatest asset is his ability to control his own actions [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Argues uncertainty is more an internal factor than a factor in the marketplace [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Describes centering as enabling the trader to become a self-starter pursuing his own concepts rather than a passive, reactive player controlled by market movements [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Previously authored "Using visual imagery in trading" (Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities, Volume 12: November 1994) [[sources/pdf-ari-kiev-2005-stocks-commodities-v]].
- Worked with SAC Capital Management traders in weekly meetings for six years, helping them identify and modify limiting thoughts and bad habits and proactively incorporate successful changes into their trading strategies [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Per Steve Cohen's foreword to "Trading to Win" (1998), some SAC traders' profits went up 100% since implementing the program [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
- Under the program, SAC's horizons expanded from equity trading alone into foreign exchange, convertibles, options, junk bonds, risk arbitrage, and systems trading [[sources/pdf-869163b2f118]].
