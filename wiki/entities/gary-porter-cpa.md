# Gary A. Porter, CPA

## Summary

Gary A. Porter, CPA is a US public accountant whose practice is restricted exclusively to Common Interest Realty Associations (CIRAs), including homeowners associations, condominium associations, property owners associations, timeshare associations, fractional associations, condo-hotels, commercial associations, and other associations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]]. Porter is the creator and coauthor of the Practitioners Publishing Company (PPC) Guide to Homeowners Associations and other Common Interest Realty Associations, and the Homeowners Association Tax Library [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]]. He served as national president of the Community Associations Institute (CAI) in 1998–1999, served as Editor of CAI's Ledger Quarterly from 1989 through 2004, and is the author of more than 300 published articles on HOA tax and accounting; he has been quoted or published in The Wall Street Journal, Money Magazine, Kiplinger's Personal Finance, and many major newspapers [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].

## Key facts

- Licensed by the California Board of Accountancy and the Nevada Board of Accountancy [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Began his accounting career with the national CPA firm Touche Ross in 1971 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Restricted his practice to work only with Common Interest Realty Associations (CIRAs): homeowners associations, condominium associations, property owners associations, timeshare associations, fractional associations, condo-hotels, commercial associations, and other associations [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Creator and coauthor of Practitioners Publishing Company (PPC) Guide to Homeowners Associations and other Common Interest Realty Associations, and Homeowners Association Tax Library [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Served as Editor of CAI's Ledger Quarterly from 1989 through 2004 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Author of more than 300 published articles, with appearances in The Ledger Quarterly, The Practical Accountant, Common Ground, and numerous CAI Chapter newsletters [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Member of Community Associations Institute (CAI); served as national president of CAI in 1998–1999 [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Author of "Revenue Ruling 70-604 – The Complete Guide," described in his own follow-up article as the first comprehensive article written on the subject [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- His follow-up article "Is Revenue Ruling 70-604 Still Alive?" reports that a conversation with the IRS Special Industry Group Liaison validated his original Complete Guide's restrictive conclusions, even though some readers had felt those conclusions were too restrictive at the time of original publication [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
- Operates his practice through garyportercpa.com [[sources/web-2026-01-01-a9a]].
