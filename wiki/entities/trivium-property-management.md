# Trivium Property Management

## Summary

Trivium Property Management is a condominium management firm and a published UpperBee customer; firm representative Chris Andrews reports approximately one year of UpperBee usage as of the testimonial, with positive feedback on the accounting tools and platform-driven centralization of the firm's management operation [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Condominium management firm with a published condominium management portfolio on the UpperBee platform [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- UpperBee usage tenure at time of testimonial: approximately one year [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Named spokesperson in UpperBee's published testimonial set: Chris Andrews [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Andrews characterizes the platform as intuitive and user-friendly and credits it with centralizing the firm's management operation [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Andrews singles out the accounting tools as particularly impressive [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
