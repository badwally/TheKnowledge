# Mike Farrell

## Summary

Mike Farrell is a Managing Director at Spectrum Equity and the named Spectrum sponsor on the CINC Systems investment, serving as the Spectrum-side spokesperson on both the June 10, 2020 announcement of Spectrum's first institutional investment in CINC and the December 14, 2023 announcement of Hg joining as co-investor alongside Spectrum [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]]. Farrell joined CINC Systems' board of directors in conjunction with the June 2020 Spectrum investment, making him the longest-tenured Spectrum board representative at CINC across the ~3.5-year Spectrum-and-Hg co-sponsorship arc [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]]. Farrell is the operational counterparty an acquirer-side outreach would need to reach for any Spectrum-side engagement on CINC under the condo-capital-infra Year-3 exit thesis [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]].

## Key facts

- Managing Director at Spectrum Equity at both the June 10, 2020 first-institutional-investment announcement and the December 14, 2023 Hg co-investor announcement — establishing a continuous ~3.5-year MD tenure on the CINC account [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]] [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]].
- Joined CINC Systems' board of directors in conjunction with Spectrum Equity's June 10, 2020 growth investment in CINC [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Joined the CINC board alongside Robert Petrie (CEO of Origami Risk — a fellow Spectrum portfolio company) and Chris Trower (founder of Electric Law) at the June 2020 deal close, surfacing Farrell as the Spectrum-direct board appointment within a three-person new-board cohort [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Public framing of the June 2020 Spectrum-CINC thesis: "Ultimately, clients serve as the foundation for all of CINC Systems' business initiatives. The company's earliest clients provided it with the feedback and collaboration to develop software that solved their specific challenges. Since then, CINC Systems' client base has continued to be a critical source of new product ideas. We are thrilled to support the team as the market opportunity for CINC and its customers only continues to grow" — establishing Farrell's stated client-feedback-loop framing as the load-bearing 2020-era Spectrum investment thesis on CINC [[sources/web-2020-06-10-363]].
- Named MD spokesperson on the December 14, 2023 announcement of Hg joining as co-investor in CINC alongside Spectrum and the founder/management team, framing the dual sponsorship as positioning CINC for its "next phase of growth" [[sources/web-2023-12-14-8ec]].
