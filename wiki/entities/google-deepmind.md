# Google DeepMind

## Summary

Google's AI research division; institutional home of all three authors of the 2026 paper "Intelligent AI Delegation," which proposes an adaptive framework for task allocation, authority transfer, accountability, and trust between human and AI delegators and delegatees in complex multi-agent networks [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].

## Key facts

- Listed as the sole institutional affiliation for Nenad Tomašev, Matija Franklin, and Simon Osindero on "Intelligent AI Delegation" (arXiv:2602.11865v1, 2026-02-12) [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
- The paper situates current Google DeepMind work in the context of an emerging "agentic web" requiring delegation protocols beyond simple heuristic multi-agent frameworks [[sources/pdf-nenad-toma-2026-intelligent-ai-delegation]].
