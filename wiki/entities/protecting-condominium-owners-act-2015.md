# Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 (PCOA)

## Summary

The Protecting Condominium Owners Act, 2015 (PCOA) is the Ontario amending act that introduced extensive substantive changes to the Condominium Act, 1998 and triggered corresponding amendments to Ontario Regulation 48/01. Many provisions of O. Reg. 48/01 are explicitly conditioned on the coming-into-force of specific sections of Schedule 1 to the PCOA, with paragraph-level revocation/substitution rules that take effect on the dates designated provisions are proclaimed [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].

## Key facts

- Schedule 1 of the PCOA contains the substantive amendments to the Condominium Act, 1998 that, when proclaimed in force, trigger corresponding revocations or substitutions within O. Reg. 48/01 [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 51 of Schedule 1 establishes the effective-date trigger for the "core record" requirement that minutes of owner and board meetings (held within the 12-month window before a records request) be included as core records [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Subsection 13(2) of Schedule 1 enacts clause 11(8)(b) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 23 of Schedule 1 enacts clause 26.1(2)(b) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 24 of Schedule 1 enacts clause 26.2(2)(b) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 40 of Schedule 1 re-enacts subsection 46(1) of the Condominium Act, triggering revocation of paragraph 10 of subsection 1.1(2) of O. Reg. 48/01 on its in-force date [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Subsection 45(1) of Schedule 1 enacts subsections 50(1), (1.1), and (1.2) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Subsection 46(2) of Schedule 1 re-enacts subsection 51(6) of the Condominium Act, triggering revocation of paragraph 14 of subsection 1.1(2) of O. Reg. 48/01 on its in-force date [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Subsection 52(4) of Schedule 1 re-enacts clause 56(1)(c) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Subsection 52(13) of Schedule 1 re-enacts clause 56(10)(a) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 74 of Schedule 1 enacts subsection 82.1(1) of the Condominium Act [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Section 88 of Schedule 1 re-enacts subsection 97(7) of the Condominium Act, triggering revocation of paragraph 21 of subsection 1.1(2) of O. Reg. 48/01 on its in-force date [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- Subsection 106(2) of Schedule 1 re-enacts subsection 123(7) of the Condominium Act, triggering revocation of paragraph 24 of subsection 1.1(2) of O. Reg. 48/01 on its in-force date [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
- The PCOA's staged proclamation model is the mechanism by which O. Reg. 48/01 maintains parallel "as it read immediately before" / "as enacted by" text for the same statutory subsections during transitional periods [[sources/web-2023-10-01-d55]].
