# Society for Experimental Mechanics (SEM)

## Summary

The Society for Experimental Mechanics (SEM) is the host organization whose conference proceedings series — published by River Publishers — contains the *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3* volume from the 43rd IMAC conference on Structural Dynamics, 2025.

## Key facts

- SEM lends its name to the River Publishers Series in Conference Proceedings of the Society for Experimental Mechanics Series, in which *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3* is published [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
- The SEM-affiliated 43rd IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics, was held in 2025 and produced twelve proceedings volumes, of which *Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Vol. 3* is the third [[sources/web-2025-08-21-7a6]].
