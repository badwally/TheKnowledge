# Noam Shazeer

## Summary

AI researcher at Google Brain and co-first author of "Attention Is All You Need" (NIPS 2017), credited with proposing several of the Transformer's defining mechanisms including scaled dot-product attention and multi-head attention [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Google Brain, with email noam@google.com on the paper [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
- Listed as an equal-contribution co-first author of "Attention Is All You Need" [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
- The paper's contribution footnote credits Shazeer with proposing scaled dot-product attention, multi-head attention, and the parameter-free position representation, and notes he became the other person involved in nearly every detail [[sources/pdf-ashish-vaswani-2025-attention-is-all]].
