# Jaron Lanier

## Summary

Jaron Lanier is a co-author of "Should We Treat Data as Labor? Moving beyond 'Free'," a 2018 AEA Papers and Proceedings article arguing for treating user data as labor rather than as capital created by corporations [[sources/web-2018-05-10-3b3]].

## Key facts

- Co-author of Arrieta-Ibarra, Goff, Jiménez-Hernández, Lanier, and Weyl (2018), "Should We Treat Data as Labor? Moving beyond 'Free'," AEA Papers and Proceedings 108: 38–42 [[sources/web-2018-05-10-3b3]].
- Co-authors on that paper include Imanol Arrieta-Ibarra, Leonard Goff, Diego Jiménez-Hernández, and E. Glen Weyl [[sources/web-2018-05-10-3b3]].
