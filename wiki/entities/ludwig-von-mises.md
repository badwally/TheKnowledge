# Ludwig von Mises

## Summary

Austrian economist; namesake of the Mises Institute and co-dedicatee (with Carl Menger) of Per Bylund's "How to Think about the Economy: A Primer" (2022), which positions Mises's school of thought — alongside Murray N. Rothbard, Henry Hazlitt, and F.A. Hayek — as the intellectual tradition the Mises Institute exists to support [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].

## Key facts

- Named alongside Carl Menger in the dedication of Bylund's 2022 primer as one of "the giants upon whose shoulders I have the great honor of standing" [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
- The Mises Institute, founded in 1982, is described as supporting "the school of thought represented by Ludwig von Mises, Murray N. Rothbard, Henry Hazlitt, and F.A. Hayek" [[sources/pdf-judy-2022-primer-adjusted-a]].
