# Speedplay

## Summary

Speedplay is a clipless-pedal brand whose Zero Aero Walkable cleats — sold by dealers such as [[entities/studio-velo|Studio Velo]] — are installed during professional cycling bike fits [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].

## Key facts

- Makes the "Zero Aero Walkable Cleat Set" in colour-coded variants (e.g., yellow) [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- The yellow Zero Aero Walkable cleat set retailed for $55.00 at [[entities/studio-velo|Studio Velo]] in August 2019 [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- Cleats are installed during the customer's bike-fit session, indicating Speedplay cleats fit into the fit-and-install workflow at high-end shops [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
