# AMSI

## Summary

AMSI is a property-management accounting software vendor named as one of 17 accounting integration partners in BuildingLink's resident-engagement platform [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. The AMSI–BuildingLink integration runs on a daily sync that keeps resident information current in BuildingLink and exposes AMSI account information — balance and transaction history — to unit owners on the BuildingLink resident site [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].

## Key facts

- Named as an accounting integration partner in BuildingLink's accounting-integration catalog [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Syncs with BuildingLink daily, ensuring resident information stays current without manual BuildingLink updates [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Unit owners can view AMSI account information (balance and transaction history) on the BuildingLink resident site [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
