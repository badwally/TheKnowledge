# Archegos Capital Management

## Summary

Family office whose March 2021 collapse — described in the WSJ piece "Inside Archegos Epic Meltdown" (2021) — is invoked by van der Beck, Bouchaud, and Villamaina (2024) as the high-profile illustration that when investment funds trade concentrated positions, portfolio returns can be driven by the funds' own price impact rather than fundamentals [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].

## Key facts

- The 2024 "Ponzi Funds" paper opens by citing the Archegos collapse as its motivating real-world example that concentrated funds' returns can come from their own price impact [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
- The paper presents the Archegos episode alongside the GameStop saga (Greenwood et al. 2023) as evidence of how important flow-driven returns can be at the individual stock level [[sources/pdf-806192f007eb]].
