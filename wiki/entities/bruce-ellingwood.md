# Bruce Ellingwood

## Summary

Bruce Ellingwood is a structural reliability researcher whose contributions appear in the March 2025 JCSS special issue of *Structural Safety* both as a co-author of a code-and-standards review and as the lead-listed author of a multi-author review of the development of structural reliability methods.

## Key facts

- Ellingwood is the first listed author of "Development of methods of structural reliability" (article 102474) in the March 2025 issue of *Structural Safety*, co-authored with Maes, Bartlett, Beck, Caprani, Der Kiureghian, Dueñas-Osorio, Galvão, Gilbert, Li, Matos, Mori, Papaioannou, Parades, Straub, and Sudret [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
- Ellingwood is the third listed author of "Codes and standards for structural design - developments and future potential" (article 102495) in the same issue, co-authored with Köhler and Sørensen [[sources/web-2025-01-07-4bf]].
