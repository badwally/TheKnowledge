# Wang Chen

## Summary

Wang Chen is the first-listed author of the 2025 arXiv preprint proposing a new bounded transformed gamma process (BTGP) model for unified infrastructure asset performance deterioration modelling.

## Key facts

- Chen is the first listed author of "Unified Modelling of Infrastructure Asset Performance Deterioration — a bounded gamma process approach," arXiv 2508.13359, published 18 August 2025 [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
- The paper is co-authored with Arnold X.-X. Yuan and is classified under stat.AP (primary), cs.CE, and math.PR [[sources/arxiv-2508.13359]].
