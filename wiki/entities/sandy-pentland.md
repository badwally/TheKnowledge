# Alex 'Sandy' Pentland

## Summary

Alex 'Sandy' Pentland is an MIT researcher associated with the development of the MIT Open Algorithms (OPAL) approach and influential framings of data cooperatives as a vehicle for citizen-controlled, privacy-preserving data sharing [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key facts

- Co-author with Thomas Hardjono of foundational work on data cooperatives and the OPAL access model cited in the glossary entry (Hardjono & Pentland, 2019) [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Cited (Pentland et al., 2019) for arguments that data cooperatives need tools, information system architectures, and governance models specific to data capture/creation, management, analysis, and use [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
