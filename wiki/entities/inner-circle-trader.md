# Inner Circle Trader (ICT)

## Summary

Inner Circle Trader (ICT), Twitter handle @I_Am_The_ICT, is the original creator of the ICT mentorship — a multi-month course on smart-money concepts, liquidity, and price-delivery models — whose Month 2 [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]] and Month 4 [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]] modules are summarized by Trader Theory threads in this corpus, whose 2022 Mentorship Market Review on Balanced Price Ranges is distributed by Braveheart Trading [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]], whose terminology is the foundation of Hopiplaka's "Demystifying ICT" book in both Version 1.0.9 [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] and Version 1.1.3 [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]], whose 2022 mentorship technicals, trade management, bias/narrative, lunch/afternoon, and psychology lessons are compiled by Tanja Miketic in "ICT 2022 Mentorship Key Points" (2023) [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]], whose umbrella Market Maker Models (MMXM) framework — the union of the Market Maker Sell Model (MMSM) and Market Maker Buy Model (MMBM), the Draw on Liquidity (DoL), the Optimal Trade Entry (OTE), the silver bullet 2nd entry, and IPDA 20/40/60 day look back periods — is laid out in the 2022 PDF "MMXM: Market Maker Models" [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]], and whose foundational concepts are systematized at the "Foundation Level" by Ali Khan in "The ICT Bible" (2023, Volume 1), a 37-page e-book covering IPDA, AMD, FVG (BISI/SIBI), killzones, the 3-bar swing, SMT, Power of 3, PD Arrays, order blocks, breaker blocks, mitigation blocks, the dealing range, and premium vs discount [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].

## Key facts

- Real name is Michael J. Huddleston, aka The Inner Circle Trader, aka ICT [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Ali Khan refers to Huddleston as "ICT — The engineer of the algorithm," identifying him as the creator of the IPDA (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm) concept [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Twitter handle: @I_Am_The_ICT, credited as the original creator of the mentorship material [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]] [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- Authored the ICT Mentorship Core Content Month 4 module covered in pdf-08bcb2d549ba, which establishes the interest rate triad as a smart-money confirmation tool and reinforces liquidity, orderblock, and price-delivery concepts [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Authored the ICT Mentorship Core Content Month 2 module, which lays the risk-management foundation: compounding small accounts, reward-to-risk discipline, top-down higher-timeframe analysis, process-oriented trading, and market-maker traps (false flags and false breakouts) [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- The Month 2 module organizes trade analysis into three perspectives — Big Picture (macro, interest rates, intermarket, seasonal), Intermediate (top-down analysis, COT data, sentiment), and Short-Term (correlation, time & price, IPDA) [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]].
- Mentorship modules are published as YouTube playlists; Month 2 [[sources/pdf-13d51286c4cc]] and Month 4 [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]] each link to their respective playlist URLs.
- The Month 4 ICT Action Plan instructs traders to consult the interest rate triad and the DXY at any focus point (orderblock, liquidity pool, fair value gap) and to pass on trade ideas where smart-money participation is not evident [[sources/pdf-08bcb2d549ba]].
- Originator of the AMD cycle (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution) terminology that Hopiplaka builds his Power-of-3 dealing range framework around [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Per Ali Khan, ICT teaches that price is not moved by buying and selling pressure but by a computer algorithm (IPDA), and that the claim markets are driven by supply and demand is "false" [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per ICT via Ali Khan, IPDA's job is to "manipulate price in order to engineer liquidity into the market place, and to offer fair value," allowing smart money to capitalize on price movement [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Ali Khan, ICT's library of concepts can be found on his YouTube channel (The Inner Circle Trader) [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Ali Khan notes that SMC (Smart Money Concepts) and ICT concepts are the same and from the same author, Michael J. Huddleston [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
- Per Hopiplaka, ICT teaches that banks trade from the Weekly and Daily charts, which is why higher-timeframe algorithms (Tesla Vortex variants) are emphasized in longer-term workflows [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Originator of foundational ICT vocabulary that Hopiplaka treats as canonical: order block, fair value gap, AMD cycle, MMxM (Market Maker buy/sell model), and IPDA [[sources/pdf-930894c4fbad]] [[sources/pdf-1ab14c833d75]].
- Source of the Balanced Price Range concept covered in the 2022 ICT Mentorship Market Review titled "Balanced Price Ranges" (August 13, 2022), distributed in PDF form by Braveheart Trading and explicitly cross-referenced to the Inner Circle Trader YouTube channel as the original source of the same material [[sources/pdf-40482428d656]].
- Teaches that a valid Fair Value Gap must run above a single high or multiple highs — "random" FVGs that don't sweep liquidity are not actionable [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Teaches that a bearish market structure shift requires price to first rally above old highs and then quickly shift lower with significant displacement, with energetic candles being the visual signature of the shift [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Treats order blocks as "bookmarks for algos" that price returns to when filling imbalances/FVGs, and recommends trailing stops below the previous down-closed candle / bullish order block low so traders aren't stopped out early during retracements [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- For the New York Open, instructs marking the midnight opening price and the 8:30 ET opening price, then looking for a Judas swing + FVG + orderblock as the canonical "great entry" setup [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Teaches that 8:30 ET should always be marked because news typically prints at that time, and the first swing high to the left of 8:30 should be marked horizontally as buy-side liquidity (BSL) [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Caps day-trading frequency at no more than 2 trades in the morning and 2 in the afternoon, since the open is most volatile and tricky and the initial move first qualifies what to expect [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Uses fib retracements on the bodies of swing-high and swing-low candles, explicitly excluding wicks and tails which he labels "distractions" [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Teaches "Purge and Revert" — a separate ICT video setup in which price action purges sell stops and then reverts back into the high of the last 3 daily candles [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Distinguishes bias (a 5-day directional view) from narrative (the reasoning for what price should do and what it must encounter to confirm the assumed path); when a bullish stance is easily communicated and a bearish stance is a hard stretch, the trade is high-probability, but if it could go either way, entering is gambling [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Teaches that, when bullish, traders should buy at or close to the midnight opening price; if price has not rallied above the midnight or 8:30 ET opens and the bias is bearish, the day is "extremely bearish" [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Treats 12–1pm ET as New York Lunch, when the market typically clears stops; prefers waiting until 1:30 ET for cleaner afternoon price action and uses a break of the lunch consolidation low as a long trigger when bullish [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Recommends combining multiple confirmations — daily directional bias, presence of a daily order block, multiple equal highs/lows as liquidity, and fib confluence — rather than relying on any single ingredient [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- Teaches that anytime the market trades above an old high, that price is at a "premium" because it is reaching for liquidity; anytime it trades into an old low, it is at a "discount" — and that things can be at a premium and still go higher, or at a discount and still go lower, which is why narrative-within-market-structure determines actionable bias [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- SMT (Smart Money Technique) divergence is a confirmation tool only — by itself it is meaningless and ICT reports having blown accounts looking only for divergences without an underlying narrative [[sources/pdf-3a3f6480ad77]].
- The MMXM PDF treats Market Maker Models as the umbrella under which MMSM and MMBM sit, framing them as the two daily-recurring models a trader is primarily interested in and stating that price action is delivered via algorithms on a daily basis — "thats all that really matters" [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF expands IPDA as "interbank price delivery array" and prescribes look back periods of 20, 40, and 60 days for HTF analysis of liquidity [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF defines a Low Resistance Liquidity Run as a setup where structure supports the direction of price and does not oppose it (does not provide resistance to the narrative), tying the term to the MMXM model context [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF teaches that the initial impulse move at the start of an MMXM is a "failure to displace" that grabs liquidity, and that a subsequent displacement plus market structure shift is the true MMXM signal [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF identifies an OTE plus a "silver bullet" 2nd entry as the two canonical entries during the re-accumulation (MMBM) or re-distribution (MMSM) leg of the model [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- The MMXM PDF introduces the concept of "offset-distribution" — the terminus where longs exit above old highs (where shorts are positioned with their stops) in an MMBM, with the symmetric concept of offset-distribution at old lows in an MMSM where buyers absorb sellers [[sources/pdf-d7094631fbf1]].
- Per Ali Khan, ICT often uses the analogy of an elephant stepping into a children's pool to describe displacement — since the size and mass of the elephant's foot is so large, we see a displacement of water — and draws a comparison to larger institutions with "deep pockets" entering the marketplace [[sources/pdf-ali-khan-2023-the-ict-bible]].
