# NYC Local Law 97 (Carbon Emissions Limits)

## Summary

New York City's Local Law 97 sets strict carbon emissions limits for buildings, with steep financial penalties for non-compliance [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. LL97 limits are calculated in part using data reported by buildings under NYC Local Law 84, which tracks each building's annual energy and water usage [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]]. Inaccurate LL84 filings can therefore inflate a building's projected LL97 penalty exposure; correcting the upstream LL84 data and resubmitting accurate reports can reduce projected penalties materially — in one Upper West Side case documented by Daisy in 2025, a corrected resubmission reduced approximately $58,000 in projected LL97 penalties to zero [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].

## Key facts

- New York City statute imposing strict carbon emissions limits on buildings [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Provides steep financial penalties for non-compliance [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Penalty calculations are based in part on data reported under NYC Local Law 84 (annual energy and water usage benchmarking) [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- The LL84→LL97 calculation chain creates a meaningful CAM operational risk: a prior management company's inaccurate LL84 estimates can directly inflate a building's projected LL97 penalty exposure [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
- Documented case (Upper West Side condo, Daisy takeover, 2025): inaccurate LL84 filings from the prior management company produced ~$58,000 in projected LL97 penalties; correcting and resubmitting LL84 data reduced projected penalties to zero [[sources/web-2025-09-29-f2f]].
