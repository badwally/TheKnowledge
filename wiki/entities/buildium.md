# Buildium

## Summary

Buildium is a property management software platform that operates a three-tier subscription pricing structure — Essential, Growth, and Premium — with Lumina AI as its named AI product suite and Open API access positioned as a tier-gated customization feature [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]]. The published self-service tier structure is explicitly bounded: operators with more than 5,000 units or those seeking pricing for community associations are directed to a separate sales channel (1-877-396-7876) rather than to the public pricing page [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]]. The pricing page enumerates ten feature categories — Leasing, Resident Experience, Maintenance, Accounting, Business Operations, Revenue Generation & Growth, Lumina AI, Automation, Customization, and Support — plus a separately-priced add-on services menu spanning payment processing, tenant screening, renters insurance, 1099 generation, eSignature, retail-cash rent collection, and outsourced check/document mailing [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].

## Key facts

### Pricing-tier structure

- Operates three named subscription plans: Essential, Growth, and Premium [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Customers can switch plans through the Account and Billing page within their Buildium account [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Free 14-day trial requires no credit card and is positioned as taking ~30 seconds to set up; trials run with sample data and can convert to any of the three plans [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].

### Self-service tier cutoff and community-association sales channel

- Operators with more than 5,000 units or those seeking pricing for community associations are directed to call 1-877-396-7876 rather than purchase through the published self-service tier structure [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- The community-association channel is named separately from the published Essential/Growth/Premium tier menu, indicating that condominium/HOA pricing is not exposed on the public pricing page [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].

### Feature categories enumerated on the pricing page

- Leasing: Rental Listing Syndication, Prospect Management, Applicant Management, EFT Residential Refunds, Marketing Website, Tenant Screening, Tenant Application Grouping, Rental History Verification, eSignature [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Resident Experience: Resident Portal, Buildium Moving Services, Payment Alternatives (Obligo, Livble, Retail Cash), Rent Reporting, Amenity Booking, Community Calendar, Online Payments & Autopay [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Maintenance: Task and Work Order Management, Maintenance Projects and Project Templates, Maintenance Reports, Maintenance Billing, Staff Calendar, Staff Time Tracking, Unit Turn Automation, Property Inspections, Maintenance Contact Center [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Accounting: Bank Account Setup, EFT Approvals, Bank Reconciliation, Financial Reporting & Standard Reports, Batched Reports [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Business Operations: Owners Portal, Integrated Communications, Two-Way Email and SMS Threads, Performance Analytics and Insights, Business Analytics and Insights [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Revenue Generation & Growth: Buildium Rewards, Buildium Property Insurance, All Property Management lead service, HomeWiseDocs [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Lumina AI: Buildium Assistant (Help Only), Write with Lumina, Lumina AI Workforce, AI Bill Scan [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Automation: Resident Payment Reminders, Automated Tenant Screening, Returned Payment Automation, Preferred Approvers Automation (Bill Approvals), Bank Reconciliation Rules, Reporting Automation, Workflow Automations [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Customization: Custom Dashboard, Customized User Roles and Permissions, Customized Reports, Marketplace Integrations, Custom Fields, Open API [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].

### Support tier structure

- Self-reported 95% customer support satisfaction rating [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Ticket Support and access to Buildium Academy (self-guided, on-demand training) are available to all Buildium customers regardless of plan [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Live Phone Support — staffed Monday–Friday 9 AM to 8 PM EST, with 85% of customer support calls resolved on the first call — is provided to customers on the Growth or Premium plan [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Priority Support — designed as a fast-track queue with average phone wait time under 1 minute and average ticket wait time under 30 minutes, staffed by Buildium's most senior support professionals — is available for purchase on the Growth plan and is included as part of the Premium plan [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Customized Onboarding (data import plus tailored training plan) is required for all new Buildium customers purchasing the Growth or Premium plan [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].

### Additional fee-based services

- Automated EFT and credit card payment processing for rent, fees, and bills [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Tenant screening service for filling vacancies [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Renters insurance product offered to protect properties and residents [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Online 1099 generation for property owners and vendors [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Prospect management automation covering scheduling, follow-ups, and prospective tenant management [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Field inspection app with one-step data sync [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- eSignature collection for leases and addenda from any resident device [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Cash rent payment acceptance at more than 20,000 trusted retail locations nationwide [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Outsourced vendor and owner check printing and mailing [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- Outsourced resident and owner document printing and mailing [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
- All Property Management lead service that delivers leads from property owners and board members seeking property management services in a designated market area; eligibility for the $500 All Property Management lead credit is contingent on the purchase of the customer's first lead, with the credit applied within 30 days of that purchase and usable toward additional lead purchases [[sources/web-2026-05-08-cf8]].
