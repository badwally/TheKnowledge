# Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary

## Summary

The Assessment and Reserve Funding Disclosure Summary is the standardized seven-item disclosure form prescribed by California Civil Code §5570(a) that every common interest development association must deliver to owners annually together with the budget report required by §5300 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]]. The form serves as the owner-facing summary of the association's reserve-adequacy position, combining the regular assessment per ownership interest, all scheduled and unapproved additional assessments, a binary yes/no answer on 30-year reserve sufficiency, a five-year forward-looking projection of required reserves and percent-funded status, and the long-term interest and inflation assumptions used to prepare the summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]]. The form may be supplemented or modified to clarify the information delivered, so long as the minimum information set out in §5570(a) is provided [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].

## Key facts

- Prescribed by Civil Code §5570(a) of the Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Must accompany each annual budget report or summary delivered pursuant to Civil Code §5300 [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Organized around the fiscal year ending date, used as the header value at the top of the form [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Item (1): the regular assessment per ownership interest, with a note that if assessments vary by size or type of ownership interest, the assessment applicable to the recipient's interest may be found on a referenced page of the attached summary [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Item (2): a multi-row table of additional regular or special assessments already scheduled to be imposed or charged that have been approved by the board and/or members, including due date, amount per ownership interest per month or year, and purpose [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Item (3): a yes/no question — based upon the most recent reserve study and other information available to the board, will currently projected reserve account balances be sufficient at the end of each year to meet the association's obligation for repair and/or replacement of major components during the next 30 years [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Item (4): if the answer to (3) is no, a table of additional assessments or other contributions to reserves that would be necessary to ensure sufficient reserve funds will be available each year during the next 30 years, that have not yet been approved by the board or members [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Item (5): a single-line declaration that all major components are included in the reserve study and are included in its calculations [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Item (6): the §5570(b)(4)-computed estimated amount required in the reserve fund at the end of the current fiscal year, based on the most recent reserve study or update (preparer named, dated by month and year); the projected reserve fund cash balance at year end; and the resulting percent-funded figure at that date; with an optional alternate-method calculation supported by an attached explanation [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Item (7): the §5570(b)(4)-computed required amounts for each of the next five budget years, the projected cash balance for each year taking into account only approved assessments and other known revenues (and the resulting percent-funded figures), and the corresponding projected cash balances and percent-funded figures if the approved funding plan is implemented [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Assumption note: must state the assumed long-term before-tax interest rate earned on reserve funds and the assumed long-term inflation rate applied to major component repair and replacement costs [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Carries the prescribed caveat that the financial representations are based on the best estimates of the preparer at the time and are subject to change [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
- Permits supplementation or modification to clarify the information delivered, provided the minimum information set out in §5570(a) is included [[sources/web-2025-01-01-946]].
