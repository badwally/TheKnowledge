# Grower Information Services Cooperative (GISC)

## Summary

GISC is cited in the Internet Policy Review glossary entry on data cooperatives as a working agricultural data cooperative — a producer-owned cooperative whose members are farmers contributing operational data [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].

## Key facts

- Listed alongside SAOS as a farming-sector data cooperative in the glossary's enumeration of non-health examples [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Operates on a membership-fee monetisation model, in common with MIDATA and SAOS [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
- Used in the source as evidence that the data-cooperative form extends to organisational and producer-data contexts beyond personal data [[sources/web-2024-04-04-ad6]].
