# Morgan Housel

## Summary

Author of "The Psychology of Money"; recommended by TraderEdge's Alex in the 2023 email "I'm profitable, now what?" as the basis for a strong fundamental personal-finance mindset for traders who have become consistently profitable [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].

- Author of the book "The Psychology of Money" [[sources/pdf-b72c5d424466]].
