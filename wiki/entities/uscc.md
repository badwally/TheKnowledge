# U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission

## Summary

U.S. congressional commission that publishes Research Working Group papers and an Annual Report on U.S.-China economic and security competition; publisher of Ngor Luong's March 2026 paper "Two Loops" on China's open AI strategy [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].

## Key facts

- Operates under the domain USCC.gov and publishes Research Working Group Papers, of which "Two Loops: How China's Open AI Strategy Reinforces Its Industrial Dominance" (March 23, 2026) is one [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Its 2024 Annual Report defined Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) as systems "as good as or better than human capabilities across all cognitive domains" that "would surpass the sharpest human minds at every task" [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
- Its 2025 Annual Report documented that China's industrial base generates "interlocking innovation flywheels" across adjacent sectors — a framing the 2026 "Two Loops" paper extends to AI [[sources/pdf-ngor-luong-2026-two-loops-how]].
