# HOACPA

## Summary

HOACPA is a CPA firm specializing in homeowners-association financial work that publishes practitioner-oriented FAQs explaining state-level Review and Audit requirements for HOAs, including California's Davis-Stirling Act §5305 threshold and the firm's own enumerated circumstances under which a Review or Audit should be performed even when not statutorily required [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].

## Key facts

- Publishes practitioner-oriented FAQs on state-level Review and Audit requirements for HOAs, hosted at hoacpa.com [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Maintains a published list of ten particular circumstances under which the firm strongly suggests a Review or Audit be performed even when not statutorily required [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Positions itself as a CPA practitioner-of-record interpreting California Civil Code §5305 (Davis-Stirling Act) for HOA boards and managers [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Frames its recommendations as engagement-scoping guidance for boards considering whether to commission a CPA Review or Audit beyond the §5305 statutory baseline [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
