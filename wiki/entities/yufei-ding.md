# Yufei Ding

## Summary

Computer science researcher at the University of California, San Diego, working on systems for large-scale machine learning, and a senior co-author of the 2026 paper "Pancake: Hierarchical Memory System for Multi-Agent LLM Serving" [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Computer Science and Engineering, University of California, San Diego, on the Pancake paper [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].
- Listed as a co-author on "Pancake: Hierarchical Memory System for Multi-Agent LLM Serving" alongside lead author Zhengding Hu and senior co-author Steven Swanson [[sources/pdf-zhengding-hu-2026-pancake-hierarchical-memory]].
