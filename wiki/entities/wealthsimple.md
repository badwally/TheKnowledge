# Wealthsimple

## Summary

Wealthsimple is a Canadian fintech that operates personal and business banking products distributed via web and mobile applications. Wealthsimple's business banking offering, the Wealthsimple Business chequing account, is structured as an incorporated-business-only product with explicit categorical eligibility exclusions, the most consequential of which (for purposes of Canadian condominium treasury operations) is that strata or condominium corporations are not permitted to open Wealthsimple Business accounts [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]]. Wealthsimple operates customer support documentation at help.wealthsimple.com that describes account features, eligibility, and operational mechanics for each product [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].

## Key facts

- Canadian fintech distributing personal and business banking products via web and mobile apps; the customer support knowledge base is hosted at help.wealthsimple.com [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Operates a Business chequing account product targeted at incorporated Canadian businesses [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Wealthsimple deposit accounts carry CDIC coverage up to $100,000 CAD [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Wealthsimple Business does not support corporate accounts for sole proprietorship or partnership businesses [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Wealthsimple Business categorically excludes strata or condominium corporations from product eligibility — this is one of seven enumerated eligibility criteria for opening a Business chequing account [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Wealthsimple Business eligibility additionally requires that the entity be registered in Canada, be a Canadian-controlled private corporation or a CRA-registered charity/association, have a single layer of ownership for incorporated entities, be an active or passive non-financial entity (cannot be a financial institution), and have a 9-digit federal business number [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Wealthsimple Business chequing supports CRA payments (Corporate Income Tax, GST/HST, Payroll Remittances), bulk-scheduled recurring CRA payments for corporate income tax and payroll source deductions (GST/HST bulk scheduling not yet supported), Bill Pay, Interac e-Transfer (autodeposit-only for receiving), CAD wire transfers (no USD wire support inbound or outbound), monthly statements in PDF and CSV, and integration to QuickBooks and other accounting software [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
- Prepaid Mastercard and credit card products are not currently available for Wealthsimple Business chequing accounts [[sources/web-2026-05-23-34b]].
