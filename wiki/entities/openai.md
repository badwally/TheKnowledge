# OpenAI

## Summary

AI research organization; institutional home of Danny Hernandez and Tom B. Brown, the authors of "Measuring the Algorithmic Efficiency of Neural Networks," which quantifies algorithmic progress in deep learning by holding capability constant and measuring the compute reduction over time [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]]. Innovation Endeavors' June 2025 "State of Foundation Models" report characterizes OpenAI, alongside Anthropic, as showing "truly unprecedented growth, accelerating at $B+ of annual revenue" [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].

## Key facts

- Both authors of the algorithmic-efficiency paper — Danny Hernandez and Tom B. Brown — list OpenAI affiliations on the paper [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
- The paper builds on the prior OpenAI analysis [Amodei & Hernandez, 2018] showing that compute used by the largest AI training runs grew 300,000x between 2012 and 2018 [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
- The paper estimates a 7.5 million-times increase in the effective training compute available to the largest AI experiments between 2012 and 2018, combining hardware, spending, and algorithmic efficiency [[sources/pdf-danny-hernandez-2025-measuring-the-algorithmic]].
- Innovation Endeavors (June 2025) reports OpenAI's spend at approximately $3B/year on training and data, with an illustrative leading-model breakdown of $150–300M pre-training, $50–150M post-training (including RL), and $50–150M data [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Innovation Endeavors (June 2025) reports OpenAI as paying $2–3k per individual reasoning trace, evidence that high-end reasoning traces are a meaningful component of post-training data costs [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Innovation Endeavors (June 2025) characterizes GPT-4 as having cost more than $100M to train and as released in March 2023 [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Innovation Endeavors (June 2025) describes o1-pro as "likely 'best of n o1'": multiple o1 responses are sampled and a verifier picks the best [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
- Innovation Endeavors (June 2025) cites OpenAI alongside Anthropic as exhibiting "truly unprecedented growth, accelerating at $B+ of annual revenue" [[sources/pdf-5bec4feeb233]].
