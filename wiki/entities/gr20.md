# GR20

## Summary

The GR20 is a long-distance walking trail across [[entities/corsica|Corsica]], depicted on the 2019 GT 20 promotional kakemono running alongside the [[entities/gt-20|GT 20]] cycling itinerary at multiple points and indicating where the island's flagship hiking and cycling routes share geographic corridors [[sources/pdf-c985b0372d8a]].

## Key facts

- The GR20 is a long-distance walking/hiking trail that traverses Corsica end-to-end and is distinct from the GT 20 cycling route, though both are depicted on the same Corsican tourism map [[sources/pdf-c985b0372d8a]].
- On the 2019 kakemono the GR20 label appears in three separate segments — in the northwest between Calinzana and Galeria, in the central interior near Bocca a Verghju and Corti, and in the south near Zicavu and Zonza — marking the zones where it overlaps with the GT 20 [[sources/pdf-c985b0372d8a]].
