# Webull

## Summary

US-based commission-free retail brokerage, headquartered in New York; designated by Nikonomics as the brokerage of choice for a beginner options day-trading curriculum on the strength of zero commissions, in-app advanced charting, and a paper-trading mode that supports options [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].

## Key facts

- Described as a US-only brokerage; Nikonomics tells international readers to use Interactive Brokers as a fallback [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Headquartered in New York and characterized as "a very trusted and secure brokerage" [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Charges no commissions on trades, an attribute Nikonomics flags as especially important for traders just starting out [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Offers "paper trading with options" — the source claims that, "if I'm not mistaken, no other brokerage has" options paper trading at the time of writing [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Paper trading on Webull provides real-time market data and lets traders place buy and sell orders as if using real money [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Advanced charting capabilities are built into the app, eliminating the need for an external charting tool — Nikonomics calls this his favorite feature of the platform [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- A user who opens a "cash account" at signup can make unlimited day trades under $25,000, which Nikonomics calls essential for traders with smaller account sizes [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Promotional offer cited in the book: signing up via the partnership link https://a.webull.com/i/nikonomics yields up to $30,000 in free stocks after a deposit of any amount, with a $1 deposit unlocking 12 free stocks [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- For users under 18, Webull requires a parent or guardian to set up the account jointly [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
- Webull's chart UI supports multiple chart types — line, bar, candlestick — with candlestick recommended for day trading; the platform supports time frames ranging from 1-minute to 1-month intervals [[sources/pdf-5a5936689043]].
