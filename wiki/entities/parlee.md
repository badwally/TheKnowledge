# Parlee Cycles

## Summary

Parlee Cycles is a high-end bicycle manufacturer that builds custom-geometry frames, including the [[entities/parlee-z-zero-xd|Z-Zero XD]] sold through dealers such as [[entities/studio-velo|Studio Velo]] in Mill Valley, California [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].

## Key facts

- Builds the Z-Zero XD as a custom-geometry frame, configurable with groupsets such as SRAM [[entities/sram-etap-axs|eTap AXS]] [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- Sells branded G23 wheels alongside its frames; dealers can return them to Parlee when a customer specs different wheels (per Studio Velo's work-order note that "G23's are going back to Parlee") [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
- Build process accommodates customer review of the bare frame — the Studio Velo work order records that the customer wanted to see the frame "in its rawness" prior to final assembly [[sources/pdf-95753e942ede]].
