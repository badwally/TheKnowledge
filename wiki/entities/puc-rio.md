# Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio)

## Summary

PUC-Rio is a Brazilian university whose Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering hosts the research program behind the 2025 ITcon paper integrating BIM, SHM, and ANNs for viaduct management.

## Key facts

- The Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at PUC-Rio is the institutional affiliation of both authors of 'Infrastructure management via BIM model: integration of structural health monitoring and ANN-based damage assessment' (ITcon Vol. 30, pp. 1637-1658, 2025) [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- José Guilherme Porto Oliveira is a D.Sc. Researcher at PUC-Rio's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
- Elisa Dominguez Sotelino is a Ph.D. Professor at PUC-Rio's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering [[sources/web-2025-10-14-bcb]].
