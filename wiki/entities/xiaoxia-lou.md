# Xiaoxia Lou

## Summary

Professor of Finance at the University of Delaware and head of research at MKT MediaStats; co-author of the 2022 paper "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Delaware (42 Amstel Ave, Newark, DE) and MKT MediaStats on the paper [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Co-author of "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" (July 15, 2022) with Rajeev Bhargava, Gideon Ozik, Ronnie Sadka, and Travis Whitmore [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Title on the paper is Professor of Finance at the University of Delaware, and head of research at MKT MediaStats [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
