# Peter Fallon

## Summary

Peter Fallon is a general partner at Elephant Ventures and the named partner-counterparty on PayHOA's $27.5 million Series A round announced May 15, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]]. Fallon provided the investor-side framing of the investment thesis, positioning the self-managed-HOA segment as a structurally underaddressed market gap that PayHOA's purpose-built platform fills [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].

## Key facts

- General partner at Elephant Ventures as of May 15, 2024 [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Named spokesperson on Elephant Ventures' lead investment in PayHOA's $27.5 million Series A round [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
- Public framing of the investment thesis: "Many HOAs manage their communities themselves, and for too long, their needs haven't been fully addressed. PayHOA recognizes this gap and provides a comprehensive platform designed specifically for self-managed HOAs. This empowers them to access powerful tools typically reserved for larger communities" [[sources/web-2024-05-15-1b2]].
