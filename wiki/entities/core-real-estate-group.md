# Core Real Estate Group

## Summary

Core Real Estate Group is an Edmonton, Alberta-based residential property management company founded in 2011 that manages more than 15,000 residential units in Edmonton; on May 8, 2025, FirstService Residential announced its acquisition of the company, with founder David Brown and his team joining FirstService and the transaction expanding FirstService Residential's footprint in Edmonton, Alberta, and Western Canada [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]]. The acquisition pushed FirstService Residential's Canadian portfolio past 250,000 residential units under management, making Core a load-bearing competitive-intelligence data point on Canadian property-management consolidation by the named secondary acquirer target in the condo-capital-infra exit thesis under ADR-0004 [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].

## Key facts

- Edmonton, Alberta-based residential property management company [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- Has served residential properties in Edmonton since 2011 [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- Manages more than 15,000 residential units in Edmonton at the time of the FirstService acquisition [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- Founder: David Brown [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- Acquired by FirstService Residential on May 8, 2025, with the acquisition announcement issued from Edmonton via CNW [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- David Brown and his team join the FirstService Residential organization as part of the partnership; Brown framed the transaction as joining "a Great Place To Work® and a company listed as one of Canada's Best Workplaces™" with customer benefits from combined expertise, industry-leading best practices, and property management resources [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- The acquisition expanded FirstService Residential's footprint in Edmonton, Alberta, and Western Canada [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- Post-acquisition, FirstService Residential's Canadian residential-unit-under-management count exceeded 250,000 [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
- The transaction was framed by FirstService Residential CEO David Diestel as central to FirstService's continued investment and expansion in Western Canada, and by Glenne Manlig, president of FirstService's operations in Alberta, as enabling the integration of FirstService's Resident Support Services team and technology into the acquired Edmonton communities [[sources/web-2025-05-08-91b]].
