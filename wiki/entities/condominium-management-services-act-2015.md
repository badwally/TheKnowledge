# Condominium Management Services Act, 2015 (Ontario)

## Summary

The Condominium Management Services Act, 2015 is the Ontario statute that requires condo corporations to be managed by a holder of a valid general licence issued by the Condominium Management Regulatory Authority of Ontario (CMRAO), with a carve-out for corporations self-managed by a volunteer board [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].

## Key facts

- Under the Condominium Management Services Act, 2015, condo corporations in Ontario must be managed by someone holding a valid general licence issued by the CMRAO, except when the condo corporation is self-managed by a volunteer board [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
- The Act establishes the licensing regime administered by the CMRAO, including the general and limited licence categories used to credential condo managers in Ontario [[sources/web-2023-05-31-64e]].
