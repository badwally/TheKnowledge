# Hopem

## Summary

Hopem is a property-management accounting software vendor named as one of 17 accounting integration partners in BuildingLink's resident-engagement platform [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]]. The Hopem–BuildingLink integration runs on a 24-hour batch sync that updates resident information — including move-ins/move-outs, contact information, and account balances — from Hopem into BuildingLink, eliminating manual dual-platform resident maintenance [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].

## Key facts

- Named as an accounting integration partner in BuildingLink's accounting-integration catalog [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- Syncs with BuildingLink every 24 hours [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- The 24-hour sync propagates resident move-ins/move-outs, contact information, and account balances from Hopem to BuildingLink [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
- The integration eliminates the need for staff to add or remove residents on both platforms manually [[sources/web-2022-01-01-3fa]].
