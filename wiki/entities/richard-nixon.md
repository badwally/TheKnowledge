# Richard Nixon

## Summary

37th President of the United States; per Kai Volatility's Q2 2022 commentary, the laissez-faire Republican who continued LBJ's two-front fiscal spending and ordered a 90-day nationwide freeze on all prices and wages in 1971 — a soft transfer payment from corporations to consumers that, once lifted, "only served to make matters worse, fueling the fires of even worse, double-digit inflation" [[sources/pdf-ed56b074fb11]].

## Key facts

- Despite being a laissez-faire Republican, continued LBJ's war on two fronts and passed new laws to increase social welfare spending, owing to the continued popularity of populist policies to address inequality [[sources/pdf-ed56b074fb11]].
- Like LBJ, "pushed the Federal Reserve front and center and mandated that they reign in prices" — and the Fed obliged [[sources/pdf-ed56b074fb11]].
- Faced calls from critical economists and politicians in 1971 to intervene directly and freeze prices and wages, as the economy declined into stagflation following the William McChesney Martin Moment [[sources/pdf-ed56b074fb11]].
- Absent a viable alternative, ordered a freeze on all prices and wages throughout the United States for a period of ninety days in 1971 [[sources/pdf-ed56b074fb11]].
- Per Kai Volatility, the price controls "amounted to soft transfer payments from corporations to consumers and, once lifted, only served to make matters worse, fueling the fires of even worse, double-digit inflation" [[sources/pdf-ed56b074fb11]].
