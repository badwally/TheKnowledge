# Gary Marcus

## Summary

Cognitive scientist and AI researcher at New York University (Emeritus); long-standing critic of the "scaling is all you need" hypothesis and co-author of the AAAI-26 paper "The Future Is Neuro-Symbolic" with Vaishak Belle [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with New York University (Emeritus) on the paper [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- Co-authored "The Future Is Neuro-Symbolic: Where Has It Been, and Where Is It Going?" with Vaishak Belle, presented at AAAI-26 [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- The paper cites "Marcus 1998" on the inability of statistical learners to generalize beyond their training distribution, framing distribution drift as a core limitation of pure-neural systems [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- The paper cites "Marcus 2001" and "Marcus and Davis 2019" on the need to integrate algebraic processing with neural networks, alongside "Marcus 2018" and "Marcus 2022" on the question of whether scaling neural models will "hit a wall" [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- The paper invokes "Lenat and Marcus 2023" for the argument that ideas from earlier large-scale expert-driven knowledge sources such as CYC could play a pivotal role in making generative models trustworthy [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
- The paper invokes "Marcus 2025b" as arguing that the use of code interpreters within LLM-based systems is a neuro-symbolic training regime that is not always acknowledged, leading the wider community to overestimate LLM reasoning capabilities [[sources/pdf-vaishak-belle-2026-the-future-is]].
