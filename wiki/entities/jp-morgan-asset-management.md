# J.P. Morgan Asset Management

## Summary

Asset-management arm of J.P. Morgan; publisher of the November 2022 "Investment Outlook 2023: A bad year for the economy, a better year for markets," whose core scenario is a mild developed-market recession with stocks and bonds having already priced in much of the macro damage [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].

## Key facts

- Issued the 2023 Investment Outlook in November 2022 under the byline of seven Global Market Strategists and two Global Market Analysts, led by Chief Market Strategist for EMEA Karen Ward [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
- House view: developed economies fall into a mild recession in 2023, but both stocks and bonds have pre-empted the macro troubles, and the firm is more excited about bonds than it has been in over a decade [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
- Forecasts US interest rates rising to 4.5%–5.0% in Q1 2023 and stopping there; ECB pausing at 2.5%–3.0% in Q1 2023; UK Bank Rate peaking at 4.0%–4.5% in Q2 2023 [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
- Expects central banks to be modest in their balance-sheet reduction ambitions, with no significant disruption from quantitative tightening [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
- Recommends climate-related stocks, emerging markets, and cheaper stocks offering dependable dividends, arguing the broad-based equity sell-off has left strong-earnings names at very low valuations [[sources/pdf-2b2d34f2b0ff]].
