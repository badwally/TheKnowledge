# Europe Active

## Summary

Europe Active is a France-based outdoor-tourism operator headquartered in Borgo, [[entities/corsica|Corsica]] that runs self-guided cycling holidays on the island under the cycling-corsica.com brand — including the [[entities/cycling-corsica-self-guided-luxury|7-day self-guided luxury Corsica route]] from Bastia to Calvi [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].

## Key facts

- Headquartered at Strada Vecchia n°7, 20290 Borgo, France — the Corsican base from which it operates [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- French business identifiers: Siret 80172306500038 and Licence IM02B140003 [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Reachable at +33 495 444 967, with a daily local hotline available 08.00–20.00 during a customer's holiday [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Operates the consumer brand cycling-corsica.com for its Corsican cycling holidays [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Holds copyright on its tour brochures — the 3 September 2024 luxury Corsica brochure is © Europe Active [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Sells holidays in a [[concepts/self-guided-cycling-tour|self-guided cycling tour]] format with luggage transfers, GPS tracks delivered via app, and on-call hotline support — no on-route guide [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
- Offers bike rentals across multiple tiers — hybrid (€320), electric (€460), aluminium road (€510), and carbon road (€560) per person — sold separately from the basic tour price [[sources/pdf-a78b30f3c6c3]].
