# Lidan Peng

## Summary

Lidan Peng is the first-listed author of the 2025 *Buildings* paper applying explainable AI to evaluate pavement deterioration rates driven by flooding events using two decades of TxDOT pavement condition data.

## Key facts

- Peng is the first listed author of "Evaluating Pavement Deterioration Rates Due to Flooding Events Using Explainable AI" [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- The paper was published in *Buildings* 15.9 (2025): 1452, DOI 10.3390/buildings15091452 [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- The corresponding arXiv preprint identifier is 2507.01056, classified under cs.LG (primary) [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
- The paper is co-authored with Lu Gao, Feng Hong, and Jingran Sun [[sources/arxiv-2507.01056]].
