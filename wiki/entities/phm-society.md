# PHM Society

## Summary

The PHM Society is the organization that operates the PHM Society Data Repository at data.phmsociety.org, which hosts free downloadable datasets relevant to prognostics and health management research.

## Key facts

- The PHM Society operates the PHM Society Data Repository, accessible at data.phmsociety.org/phm-datasets/ [[sources/web-2023-01-05-939]].
- The repository hosts several free downloadable datasets [[sources/web-2023-01-05-939]].
- The PHM Society Data Repository page also links out to the NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence Dataset Repository [[sources/web-2023-01-05-939]].
