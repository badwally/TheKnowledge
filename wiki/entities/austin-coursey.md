# Austin Coursey

## Summary

Austin Coursey is a co-author of the 2025 DX conference paper introducing a data-driven Bayesian particle filter approach for system-level Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction.

## Key facts

- Coursey is the second listed author of "A Data-Driven Particle Filter Approach for System-Level Prediction of Remaining Useful Life," published 10 November 2025 in the DX 2025 proceedings (OASIcs vol. 136, pp. 11:1–11:13, DOI 10.4230/OASIcs.DX.2025.11) [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
- The paper is co-authored with Abel Diaz-Gonzalez, Marcos Quinones-Grueiro, and Gautam Biswas [[sources/web-2025-11-10-fd9]].
