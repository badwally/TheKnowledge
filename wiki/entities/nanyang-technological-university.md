# Nanyang Technological University

## Summary

Research university in Singapore; listed as the institutional affiliation of Wentao Zhang, sole author of the 2026 paper "Autogenesis: A Self-Evolving Agent Protocol" [[sources/pdf-wentao-zhang-2026-autogenesis-a-self]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Wentao Zhang's affiliation on "Autogenesis: A Self-Evolving Agent Protocol" (2026) [[sources/pdf-wentao-zhang-2026-autogenesis-a-self]].
- The Autogenesis work proposes the AGP two-layer self-evolution protocol and the AGS multi-agent system, evaluated on GPQA, AIME, GAIA, and LeetCode [[sources/pdf-wentao-zhang-2026-autogenesis-a-self]].
