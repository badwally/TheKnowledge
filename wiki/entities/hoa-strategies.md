# HOA Strategies

## Summary

HOA Strategies is a community association management (CAM) operator identified as a Vantaca customer on the Vantaca features page, with Trever Midgely named in the testimonial copy and attributed as the source of the operational-impact quotation about Vantaca's work queue [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].

## Key facts

- Identified as a CAM operator and Vantaca customer in published Vantaca marketing copy [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- Trever Midgely is named in the Vantaca testimonial as the spokesperson representing HOA Strategies [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The operator's published testimonial attributes Vantaca's work queue with the biggest day-to-day operational impact, citing improved task visibility ("team knows exactly what's showing up on their desktop, what's in their queue, and what needs to get done") and right-job-to-right-desk workflow routing [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
- The Vantaca features page does not specify HOA Strategies' jurisdiction, association portfolio size, headcount, or service-line scope [[sources/web-2025-11-04-f77]].
