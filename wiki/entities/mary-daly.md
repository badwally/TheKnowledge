# Mary Daly

## Summary

Federal Reserve President referenced in Zoltan Pozsar's May 13, 2022 dispatch via a screen-tape comment — "WANT TO SEE MORE TIGHTENING OF FINANCIAL CONDITIONS" — that Pozsar uses as evidence that the Fed is not done shaping market expectations even after the recent stock market correction and rise in mortgage rates [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].

## Key facts

- Identified by Pozsar simply as "President Daly" with no Reserve Bank specified in the May 13, 2022 dispatch [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Quoted screen tape: "DALY: WANT TO SEE MORE TIGHTENING OF FINANCIAL CONDITIONS" [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Per Pozsar, Daly's comment that the recent stock market correction and rise in mortgage rates is "great" but not enough ("want to see more") implies the Fed will not stop shaping expectations until more damage is done to stocks and bonds [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
