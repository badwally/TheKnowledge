# Rui Zheng

## Summary

Rui Zheng is the senior (final-listed) co-author of the 2025 paper proposing an optimal predictive maintenance policy for systems following a nonstationary gamma degradation process, solved as a semi-Markov decision problem.

## Key facts

- Zheng is the fifth (final) listed author of 'Optimal predictive maintenance for a nonstationary gamma process,' published on 7 April 2025 with DOI 10.17531/ein/203543 [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
- The paper is co-authored with Yue Wang, Ganlong Wang, Yanxia Wu, and Guoyin Zhang [[sources/web-2025-04-07-e6e]].
