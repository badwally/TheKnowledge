# Mark Girolami

## Summary

Mark Girolami is the senior co-author on the Bull et al. (2022) paper introducing a hierarchical Bayesian multitask learning framework for engineering fleets via population-level inference.

## Key facts

- Girolami is the final listed author of "Hierarchical Bayesian Modelling for Knowledge Transfer Across Engineering Fleets via Multitask Learning," co-authored with L. A. Bull, D. Di Francesco, M. Dhada, O. Steinert, T. Lindgren, A. K. Parlikad, and A. B. Duncan [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
- The paper was published in *Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering* in 2022 and is available as arXiv 2204.12404 with DOI 10.1111/mice.12901 [[sources/arxiv-2204.12404]].
