# Federal Highway Administration (FHWA)

## Summary

The Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) is the U.S. federal agency that leads the Long-Term Pavement Performance (LTPP) program, providing the publicly accessible pavement performance database underpinning asphalt pavement life-cycle research.

## Key facts

- The FHWA leads the Long-Term Pavement Performance (LTPP) program, which provides a publicly accessible pavement performance database [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
- The FHWA's leadership of the LTPP program is acknowledged in the 2026 review on asphalt pavement life-cycle performance by Xin, Frangopol, Akiyama, Zhang, and Pei [[sources/web-2026-02-01-5c9]].
