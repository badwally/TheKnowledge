# Jane Levy Vance

## Summary

Jane Levy Vance is Co-Founder and Partner at Cove Hill Partners, the Boston-area vertical-software investment firm that led the $300+ million minority growth investment in Vantaca at a $1.25 billion valuation announced October 15, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]]. She serves as the named Investor Relations contact for the Vantaca transaction [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].

## Key facts

- Title: Co-Founder and Partner at Cove Hill Partners as of October 15, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Identified as the Investor Relations contact for the Vantaca transaction (phone 1-857-245-6060, Boston-area exchange) [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Articulated Cove Hill's investment thesis on Vantaca: "Vantaca represents the rare combination of market leadership, technological innovation, and exceptional execution that we believe defines category-winning companies" [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Stated belief: Vantaca's AI-first approach has sustainable competitive advantages and delivers significant value to customers [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
- Stated post-investment intent: partner with the Vantaca team as they scale the platform, introduce new capabilities, and expand into new markets [[sources/web-2025-10-15-7aa]].
