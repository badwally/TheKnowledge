# Deposit Insurance Reserve Fund (DIRF)

## Summary

The Deposit Insurance Reserve Fund (DIRF) is the fund administered by the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA) that provides deposit insurance coverage for Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. The DIRF is pre-funded by Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires through premiums paid to FSRA, at no cost to depositors [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]]. FSRA's liability to insure deposits held at Ontario credit unions is limited to the assets of the DIRF [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].

## Key facts

- Administered by the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA) [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Pre-funded by Ontario credit unions and caisses populaires through premiums paid to FSRA [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Provided at no cost to depositors [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Caps FSRA's deposit insurance liability at Ontario credit unions to the assets of the fund [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Funds the deposit insurance coverage of up to $250,000 (principal and interest combined) for insurable deposits in non-registered accounts and unlimited coverage for insurable deposits in registered accounts [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
- Funds payouts to depositors when an Ontario credit union or caisse populaire goes out of business, on an as-soon-as-possible basis [[sources/web-2022-02-01-597]].
