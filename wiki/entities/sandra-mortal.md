# Sandra Mortal

## Summary

Finance researcher at the University of Alabama, Culverhouse College of Business; co-author of "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" (2020, SSRN id 3392130), which establishes that hedge fund managers with poker tournament success outperform their peers by approximately 10 basis points per month [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the University of Alabama, Culverhouse College of Business, Box 870224, Tuscaloosa, AL 34587 [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Contact email: scmortal@cba.ua.edu; phone: +1-205-348-7076 [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
- Co-author of "Hedge Fund Hold 'em" (2020) with Yan Lu and Sugata Ray [[sources/pdf-sugsu-2020-microsoft-word-hfholdem]].
