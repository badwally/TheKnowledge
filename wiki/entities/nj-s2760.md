# New Jersey S2760 (Structural Integrity and Reserve Funding Act)

## Summary

S2760 is the New Jersey Structural Integrity and Reserve Funding Act signed into law in January 2024, establishing capital reserve study and funding obligations for planned real estate developments. The statute originally required covered building owners to obtain a capital reserve study at minimum once every five years (conducted by a licensed architect, engineer, or credentialed reserve specialist) and to maintain a 30-year funding plan; it used the terms "adequate" and "adequacy" without defining them, prohibited funding reserves at 85% of the study plan, and granted associations authority to impose special assessments or obtain loans when a capital asset reached the end of its useful life sooner than projected. S2760 was subsequently amended and clarified by S3992 in August 2025 [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].

## Key facts

- Signed into law in January 2024 [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- The January 8, 2024 date functions as the grandfathering anchor under S3992 for which associations qualify for the 85% transitional funding option [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Originally required "covered building owners" to obtain a capital reserve study conducted by a licensed architect, engineer, or credentialed reserve specialist at a minimum of once every five years [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Originally required a 30-year capital reserve funding plan [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Originally used the terms "adequate" and "adequacy" without defining them [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Originally prohibited funding reserves at 85% of the study plan [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Originally provided that an association had authority to impose a special assessment or obtain a loan if a capital asset reached the end of its useful life sooner than projected in the reserve study [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Originally did not mandate notice requirements; sellers in planned real estate developments did not have to provide disclosures related to capital reserve funding [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
- Amended by S3992, signed into law on August 21, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-06-10-ddd]].
