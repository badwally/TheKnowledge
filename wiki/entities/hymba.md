# NVIDIA Hymba-1.5B

## Summary

Hymba-1.5B is a Mamba-attention hybrid-head small language model from NVIDIA Research, cited by Belcak et al. (2025) as demonstrating best-in-class instruction accuracy and 3.5× greater token throughput than comparably-sized transformer models — and outperforming larger 13B models on instruction following [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].

## Key facts

- Architecture is a Mamba-attention hybrid-head SLM at the 1.5B parameter scale [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Demonstrates best-in-class instruction accuracy among comparably-sized models [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- Achieves 3.5× greater token throughput than comparably-sized transformer models [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
- On instruction-following benchmarks, outperforms larger 13B models [[sources/pdf-peter-belcak-2025-small-language-models]].
