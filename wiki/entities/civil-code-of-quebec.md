# Civil Code of Québec (CCQ-1991)

## Summary

The Civil Code of Québec (CCQ-1991) is the foundational civil-law statute of Quebec, codifying the substantive rules of private law including the divided co-ownership (copropriété divise) framework that corresponds to condominium ownership in common-law jurisdictions [[sources/web-2022-09-02-033]]. As of November 1, 2022, the Code was accompanied by 19 named regulations, only one of which (CCQ, r. 4.1 — Regulation respecting various measures in matters of divided co-ownership insurance) was directed at the divided co-ownership domain [[sources/web-2022-09-02-033]]. The Bill 16 framework — enhanced by Bill 31 and operationalized by the regulation published in the *Gazette officielle du Québec* on July 30, 2025 — was incorporated into the Civil Code of Québec, materially expanding the Code's substantive treatment of divided co-ownership beyond insurance to include mandatory maintenance logbooks, contingency fund studies, buyer certificates, and trustee-deposit rules for new project deposits [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].

## Key facts

- Chapter designation: CCQ-1991 [[sources/web-2022-09-02-033]].
- As of November 1, 2022, accompanied by 19 named regulations covering subjects such as bodily-injury damages discounting, civil marriage/union, civil status, land registration, register of personal and movable real rights, and liability of carriers by water [[sources/web-2022-09-02-033]].
- CCQ, r. 4.1 (Regulation to establish various measures in matters of divided co-ownership insurance) was the only one of those 19 regulations directed at the divided co-ownership domain as of November 1, 2022 [[sources/web-2022-09-02-033]].
- The Bill 16 framework — as enhanced by Bill 31 — was incorporated into the Civil Code of Québec following enactment, with the implementing regulation published in the *Gazette officielle du Québec* on July 30, 2025 and in force August 14, 2025 [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
- Post-Bill 16, the Code's treatment of divided co-ownership extends beyond insurance to include mandatory standardized maintenance logbooks, periodic contingency fund studies, buyer certificates, and trustee-deposit rules for new project deposits [[sources/web-2025-07-30-00d]].
