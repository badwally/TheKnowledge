# Andrew Aziz

## Summary

Day trader and trading educator affiliated with Peak Capital Trading and Bear Bull Traders in Vancouver, BC; co-author of the April 10, 2023 paper "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?" with Carlo Zarattini, which establishes empirical support for the 5-minute Opening Range Breakout (ORB) strategy on QQQ and demonstrates that pairing it with the leveraged ETF TQQQ would have produced a total return of 1,484% from January 1, 2016 to February 17, 2023 [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with Peak Capital Trading and Bear Bull Traders, both at 744 West Hastings Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada V6C 1A5, on the paper [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- Listed corresponding author email: andrew@peakcapitaltrading.com [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- Co-author with Carlo Zarattini of the 2023 ORB paper, which compares the active ORB QQQ portfolio to a passive QQQ buy-and-hold benchmark over January 1, 2016 to February 17, 2023 [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- The paper attributes the n-minute ORB strategy concept to Aziz and notes that Aziz et al. have released several publications on using the ORB strategy in the US stock market (cited as references [9], [11], [13]) [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
- The paper's share-sizing formula (Shares = integer(min(A·0.01/$R, 4·A/P))) is described as elaborated in more detail by Elder and Aziz, indicating Aziz's prior work on retail-account position sizing under FINRA leverage rules [[sources/pdf-e63407c2b4f4]].
