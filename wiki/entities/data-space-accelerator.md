# Data Space Accelerator

## Summary

The Data Space Accelerator is a €23 million programme/study funded by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE), conducted by the International Data Spaces Association (IDSA) in partnership with Catena-X and Cofinity-X, designed to fast-track SME integration into the Catena-X automotive data space [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]]. Announced on 8 June 2026 in Berlin, it forms part of the German government's Manufacturing-X initiative to scale industrial data ecosystems [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].

## Key facts

- €23 million programme funded by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE) [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Targets small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in the automotive industry [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Compensation is tied to verifiable productive data exchange rather than cost subsidy [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Participating companies can receive up to €15,000 for successful onboarding and productive implementation of Certificate Management, plus up to an additional €15,000 for implementation of a second predefined Catena-X use case — up to €30,000 total [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Participating companies are required to take part in an accompanying survey [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Applications and full implementation must be completed within calendar year 2026 [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Certificate Management is the primary use case: it replaces the inefficient process of suppliers uploading the same compliance certificates (e.g. ISO 9001, IATF 16949) to multiple customer portals; under Catena-X certificates are uploaded once via a standardised interface and shared with all relevant partners [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Second-use-case options include Product Carbon Footprint, Digital Product Passport, traceability, quality management, demand and capacity management, short-term supply management, and circular economy [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Forms part of the German government's Manufacturing-X framework for scaling industrial data ecosystems; insights are intended to be transferable to other sectors including chemicals and semiconductors [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Open to automotive companies, particularly European SMEs; registration at data-space-accelerator.com [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Cites an Accenture study reporting that 67% of companies expect new collaboration opportunities through data spaces, 54% anticipate new revenue potential, and 50% expect reduced costs and risks [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
- Positioned by Catena-X (Hanno Focken) as "not a pilot" but "a structured, funded pathway into productive participation in the data space" [[sources/web-2026-06-08-406]].
