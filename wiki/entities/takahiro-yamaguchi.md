# Takahiro Yamaguchi

## Summary

Takahiro Yamaguchi is a researcher at the University of Tokyo and the sole author of the 2025 IABSE Symposium Tokyo paper proposing a physics-informed neural network for post-earthquake damage identification of reinforced concrete bridge piers.

## Key facts

- Yamaguchi is affiliated with the University of Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
- Yamaguchi is the sole listed author of "Damage Identification of a Reinforced Concrete Bridge Pier after an Earthquake based on a Physics-informed Neural Network," presented at the IABSE Symposium Tokyo 2025, 18-21 May 2025 [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
- The paper was published in the *IABSE Symposium Tokyo 2025* proceedings, pages 1902-1909, with DOI 10.2749/tokyo.2025.1902 [[sources/web-2025-06-07-869]].
