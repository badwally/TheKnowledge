# Paul Volcker

## Summary

Former Federal Reserve chair whose tenure is invoked by Zoltan Pozsar as the cyclical archetype the current Fed needs to emulate; Pozsar argues that Jay Powell's professional hero is Volcker, and that the deliberate inflation-slaying shock the Fed needs is a "Volcker Moment" — paired against Ben Bernanke as the cyclical anti-hero [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].

## Key facts

- Identified by Pozsar as Jay Powell's professional hero in the cyclical sense [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- The "Volcker Moment" concept introduced in Pozsar's February 16, 2022 dispatch is named after Volcker and frames the Fed's required posture as injecting volatility into markets to control inflation through asset prices [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
- Treated as the cyclical hero opposite Ben Bernanke, who is treated as the cyclical anti-hero given his association with the original aim of QE — reflating asset prices to avoid deflation [[sources/pdf-3d3d42a0717a]].
