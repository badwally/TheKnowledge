# Eben Lazarus

## Summary

Finance researcher at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley; co-author with Ned Augenblick and Michael Thaler of "Overinference from Weak Signals and Underinference from Strong Signals" (September 2024), which demonstrates that insensitivity to objective signal strength causes people to overreact to weak information and underreact to strong information across four empirical environments [[sources/pdf-ned-augenblick-2025-overinference-from-weak]].
