# Trustii

## Summary

Trustii is a pre-rental screening service that integrates with the UpperBee property management platform as UpperBee's named tenant-screening partner [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]]. The product is differentiated by its bank-authentication-based identity and financial-reliability verification, which Trustii markets as a fully automated process that delivers comprehensive applicant reports in minutes [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]]. Trustii reports flow directly into UpperBee and trigger automatic e-lease generation using pre-entered information [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].

## Key facts

- Pre-rental screening tool accessible to UpperBee users through the UpperBee platform [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Differentiating technology: bank authentication for applicant identity validation and financial-reliability assessment [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Marketed delivery: fully automated process, comprehensive results, output in minutes [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Integration depth with UpperBee: Trustii reports integrate directly with UpperBee, enabling automatic e-lease generation using pre-entered information [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
- Public-facing site: trustii.com [[sources/web-2025-01-31-f4e]].
