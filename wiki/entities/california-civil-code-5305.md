# California Civil Code §5305 (Review of Financial Statement)

## Summary

California Civil Code §5305, within the Davis-Stirling Act, requires a California HOA whose gross income exceeds $75,000 in a fiscal year to obtain a Review of the financial statement prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) by a licensee of the California Board of Accountancy, and to distribute that Review to members within 120 days after the close of the fiscal year via the individual-delivery method specified in Civil Code §4040. Governing documents may impose more stringent standards [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].

## Key facts

- Applies to fiscal years in which the association's gross income exceeds $75,000 [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Requires the Review to be prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- The Review must be prepared by a licensee of the California Board of Accountancy [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- A copy of the Review of the financial statement must be distributed to members within 120 days after the close of each fiscal year [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Distribution must be by individual delivery pursuant to Civil Code §4040 [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- The statute permits an exception to the Review requirement when gross income is $75,000 or less [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- The governing documents of the association may impose more stringent standards than §5305's statutory baseline [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
- Section 5305 is silent on a statutory audit requirement; audits in California arise from governing-document mandates or voluntary engagement, not from §5305 itself [[sources/web-2018-08-01-f87]].
