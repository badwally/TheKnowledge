# Lehman Brothers

## Summary

Global investment bank (Lehman Brothers Holdings, Inc.) whose internal "Foreign Exchange Training Manual" — preserved as document LBEX-LL 3356480-3356609 in the Barclays-custodied Lehman Live archive — is a foundational training workbook covering FX spot, forwards, swaps, and options, organized as a teaching text with definitions, conventions, worked examples, and review problems for new desk hires [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].

## Key facts

- Authored an internal "Foreign Exchange Training Manual" preserved as document LBEX-LL 3356480-3356609 in the Barclays-custodied Lehman Live archive [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Manual is organized into four major sections: FX Spot, FX Forwards, FX Swaps, and FX Options [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Each section progresses from definitions and conventions through worked examples and review problems, indicating the document is a teaching workbook rather than a desk reference [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- The FX Options section walks from vanilla payoffs through the Greeks (with a worked "Trading Gamma on a Lehman Call Option" P&L example) to second-order Greeks, exotic options, trading conventions, and trading strategies [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Manual carries the marking "Confidential Treatment Requested By Lehman Brothers Holdings, Inc." on every page [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
- Manual was custodied by Barclays after Lehman's 2008 bankruptcy and Barclays' acquisition of the broker-dealer business; the document carries "CONFIDENTIAL TREATMENT REQUESTED BY BARCLAYS SOURCE: LEHMAN LIVE" headers [[sources/pdf-4b87a8059b38]].
