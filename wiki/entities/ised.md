# Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED)

## Summary

Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) is the federal department, led by the Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry, that published the Phase 2 launch of the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy in June 2022 [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].

## Key facts

- ISED issued the June 22, 2022 news release announcing Phase 2 of the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- The minister responsible at the time of the announcement was the Honourable François-Philippe Champagne, Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- ISED is the home department for the implementation of Phase 2 funding flows to CIFAR, the national AI institutes, the Standards Council of Canada, and Canada's Global Innovation Clusters [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
- The department also positions the proposed Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA) as the governance framework accompanying the Phase 2 investments [[sources/web-2022-06-22-4be]].
