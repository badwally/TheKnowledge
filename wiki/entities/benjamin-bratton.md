# Benjamin Bratton

## Summary

Philosopher and theorist whose work bridges Google's Paradigms of Intelligence Team, the Antikythera program at the Berggruen Institute, and the University of California, San Diego; co-author of the 2026 essay "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion" [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].

## Key facts

- Affiliated with the Paradigms of Intelligence Team at Google, with Antikythera at the Berggruen Institute, and with the University of California, San Diego on the paper [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Co-authored "Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion" (2026) with James Evans and Blaise Agüera y Arcas [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Cited as co-author of "The Silicon Interior: What Do Agents Believe?" (Agüera y Arcas, Bratton, Evans; Antikythera Substack, 2026), described as offering embryonic glimpses of agent-platform futures [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
- Cited as co-author of "Agentic AI's Emergent Phenomenological Lexicon" (Agüera y Arcas, Bratton, Evans; Antikythera Substack, 2026), discussing emergent multi-perspective behaviors in reinforcement-learned reasoning models [[sources/pdf-james-evans-2026-agentic-ai-and]].
