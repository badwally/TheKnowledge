# Gideon Ozik

## Summary

Founder and managing partner of MKT MediaStats and Affiliate Professor of Finance at EDHEC Business School; co-author of the 2022 paper "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].

## Key facts

- Listed as Founder and managing partner of MKT MediaStats and Affiliate Professor of Finance at EDHEC Business School on the paper [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- MKT MediaStats office address given as 140 Mt. Auburn St, Cambridge, MA [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
- Co-author of "Quantifying Narratives and their Impact on Financial Markets" (July 15, 2022) with Rajeev Bhargava, Xiaoxia Lou, Ronnie Sadka, and Travis Whitmore [[sources/pdf-19445750a197]].
