# Tradytics

## Summary

Trading-analytics platform whose Market Net Flow (MNF) tool — a visualization of how option participants are affecting dealer hedging via call-line and put-line plots relative to a zero line — is used by Matthew Ryan as the trend-confluence overlay paired with his 2-Day Anchored VWAP rule [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].

## Key facts

- Developer of the Market Net Flow tool, which Ryan adopts as a confluence indicator for determining trend direction by visualizing how option participants force dealers to hedge [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- The MNF tool is described as showing how option participants are affecting dealers and forcing them to hedge — a topic Ryan calls too extensive to cover in his manual but recommends readers understand at an advanced level [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
- Tradytics publishes a YouTube tutorial on the basics of using MNF, which Ryan links to (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R8-eJ7IJhco&t=1s) [[sources/pdf-2248d6cdc39f]] [[sources/pdf-6ba2dc608ac8]].
